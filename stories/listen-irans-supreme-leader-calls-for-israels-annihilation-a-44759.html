LISTEN: Iran’s supreme leader calls for Israel’s annihilation, arming of West Bank (Second Coming Watch Update #531 with Daniel Whyte III)

November 11, 2014 Second Coming Watch

Daniel Whyte III

Let’s take a quick look at today’s prophecy-related headlines which point towards the second coming of the Lord Jesus Christ and the end of the world as we know it.

First, today, under the Sign category of THE TURMOIL IN ISRAEL & THE MIDDLE EAST — According to Israel National News, Iran’s Supreme Leader, Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, has once again called for Israel’s destruction. Khamenei’s latest provocation came in a series of tweets in which he launched a tirade against the Jewish state and called to arm Palestinian Arabs in Judea and Samaria. He wrote, “This barbaric, wolflike & infanticidal regime of Israel which spares no crime has no cure but to be annihilated.” In another message he said, “The West Bank should be armed just like Gaza. Friends of Palestine should do their best to arm people in the West Bank.”

Second, today, under the Sign category of THE TURMOIL IN ISRAEL & THE MIDDLE EAST — According to Israel National News, Jordan’s Prime Minister, Abdullah Nsur, criticized Israel over its actions on the Temple Mount, but ruled out the possibility that his country would cancel the peace deal with Israel. He said that severing the deal will cause political, security and military consequences. He added, however, that the ongoing tension over the Al-Aqsa mosque compound is inflicting a “stab wound” on the peace treaty between the two countries.

Third, today, under the Sign category of THE TURMOIL IN ISRAEL & THE MIDDLE EAST — According to Agence France-Presse (AFP), the European Union’s top diplomat Federica Mogherini said on Saturday that Jerusalem “should be the capital of two states”, as tensions gripped the holy city hit by Israeli-Palestinian violence. Touching on a sensitive issue that has blocked peace efforts for decades, Mogherini told reporters in the Israeli-occupied West Bank city of Ramallah, “I think Jerusalem can be and should be the capital of two states.” Her appeal, at a joint news conference with Palestinian prime minister Rami Hamdallah, came hours after the killing of an Arab-Israeli by police fanned tensions between Israel and the Palestinians.

Fourth, today, under the Sign category of DISTRESS AMONG NATIONS — According to Reuters, U.N. atomic inspectors say Iran’s stockpile of low-enriched uranium gas has grown by 8 percent to nearly 8.4 tonnes in about two months, an amount world powers probably will want to see cut under any nuclear deal with Tehran. Iran’s holding of refined uranium gas is one of the factors that could determine how much time it would need for any attempt to assemble nuclear weapons. Iran says it has no such goal but the West wants verifiable action by the Islamic Republic to make sure it cannot produce an atomic bomb any time soon.

Fifth, today, under the Sign category of WARS & RUMORS OF WARS — According to the Jerusalem Post, Hamas declared the formation of a “popular army” in the northern Gaza Strip on Friday. A spokesman for the al-Qassam Brigades — Hamas’s military branch — said 2,500 recruits would form “the first section of the popular army for the liberation of al-Aksa and of Palestine.” A Hamas official urged those older than 20 to sign up “to be prepared for any confrontation” with Israel.

The prophetic passage of Scripture we are looking at today is Genesis 17:1-2 which reads: “And when Abram was ninety years old and nine, the Lord appeared to Abram, and said unto him, I am the Almighty God; walk before me, and be thou perfect. And I will make my covenant between me and thee, and will multiply thee exceedingly.”

Allow me to share with you some commentary on this passage from the Popular Bible Prophecy Commentary, edited by Tim LaHaye and Ed Hindson.

The fourth expansion of the Abrahamic Covenant’s promises is contained in Genesis 17:1-21. God again appears to Abraham, identifying Himself as God Almighty, “the one who grants fertility and life.” This encounter adds the requirement of circumcision, a physical sign of the covenant, on the eight day for Abraham and his male descendants. This is to serve as a vivid reminder, in blood, to every descendant of Abraham of God’s “everlasting covenant.”

At this time, in conjunction with the reiteration of the promise of innumerable descendants, the Lord changes Abraham’s name from Abram (“exalted father”) to Abraham (“father of multitudes”). The covenant blessing is expanded to incorporate not just a single nation to issue forth from Abraham, but “nations,” as well as kings. Reiterated, as well, is the promise of Abraham’s and his descendants’ eternal possession of the land. And another promise is added to the covenant complex — that of a personal relationship with Israel.

If the Lord tarries His coming and we live, we will continue looking at this prophecy in our next broadcast / podcast.

Our second coming quote for today is from George Eldon Ladd. He said, “Nowhere is salvation conceived of as a flight from history as in Greek thought; it is always the coming of God to man in history. Man does not ascend to God; God descends to man.”

If you are not ready for the return of Jesus Christ, may I encourage you to get ready today by receiving Him as your Savior. John 3:16 says, “For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.” Just believe in your heart that Jesus Christ died for your sins, was buried, and rose from the dead by the power of God for you so that you can live eternally with Him. Pray and ask Him to come into your heart today, and He will. Romans 10:13 says, “For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.”

Keep looking up for your redemption draweth nigh. Let us join in the prayer of John the Revelator — “Even so, come, Lord Jesus.” God bless you.
