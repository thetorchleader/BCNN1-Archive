Daily Look at Tim Tebow In Jets Training Camp

August 23, 2012 Uncategorized

A look at Tim Tebow’s day at New York Jets training camp:
TEBOW TIME: The Jets were back at their practice facility Wednesday after working out at MetLife Stadium on Tuesday night. Tebow ran the second-team offense, as he has done for almost all of training camp.
HOW’D HE DO: It was a ho-hum practice for Tebow, who finished 2 of 6 passing in team drills, with four “sacks.”
TEBOWMANIA: ESPN, which camped out at Jets camp in Cortland, N.Y., for nearly a week and covered Tebow’s every move, poked fun at itself and its coverage of New York’s popular backup quarterback. On the cover of its NFL preview issue, ESPN the Magazine has a picture of Lions receiver Calvin Johnson crushing a football with a note next to his right arm that reads: “What? Because we’re ESPN you were expecting Tebow?”
TALKING TEBOW: “I think what we’ll do is we’ll go with Mark (Sanchez), and then when it’s time, we’ll put in Tebow to close out the game. Do I see them out there together? No, I don’t see that happening.” — coach Rex Ryan on the Jets’ game plan for Sanchez and Tebow against Carolina on Saturday night.
SOURCE: The Associated Press
