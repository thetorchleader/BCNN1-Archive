Ahead of Obama's Second Inauguration, "A Day to Pray" for Country's Leaders Planned for this Sunday

January 17, 2013 Uncategorized

Make this Sunday, Jan. 20, a day of repentance as well as a day to pray. 
Thousands have signed up for this Sunday’s “day to pray”–encouraged by Gov. Mike Huckabee and prayer intercessor Cindy Jacobs (ADayToPray.com). The purpose of the spiritual event is to bring us together to ask God for forgiveness and to heal our land, to grant our leaders wisdom from local school houses to the White House. 
Another Christian ministry, the national day of repentance, dayofrepentance.org, is urging believers to take time that day not just for prayer, but specifically to focus on repentance. 
It’s repentance from His believers that triggers God’s heart to heal nations. Second Chronicles 7:14 says: “If My people who are called by My name will humble themselves, and pray and seek My face, and turn from their wicked ways, then I will hear from heaven, and will forgive their sin and heal their land.”
The phrase “wicked ways” is misunderstood, as is the concept of repentance. In Hebrew the phrase “wicked ways” may be translated as “inadequacies.” We all have inadequacies; we all fall short of the glory of God. But what a privilege He gives us: if we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness. Repentance thus is a positive, healthy process that the Lord wants to share with us [Revelation 3:19-20] to help us leave our old ways and turn towards His Purity as His Bride. Intimacy, joy with Him follows repentance!
