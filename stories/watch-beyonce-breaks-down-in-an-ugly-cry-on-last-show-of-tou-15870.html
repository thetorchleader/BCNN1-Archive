WATCH: Beyonce Breaks Down in an ‘Ugly Cry’ on Last Show of Tour; Lifts Hands to Heaven as if Thanking God

March 28, 2014 Entertainment

Even Queen Bey gets teary now and then.

The “XO” singer, 32, got weepy on her last stop on the Mrs. Carter Show world tour in Lisbon, Portugal, on Thursday.

Beyonce gave a little speech thanking the audience and mentioning her daughter, Blue Ivy.

“If you all don’t know, tonight makes 132 shows. Tonight is the last show of the Mrs. Carter Show. We started a year ago and I want to say this has been such a journey,” she began. “In the past year, we’ve been through so much together.”

“When I first started the tour, my baby was not even walking yet,” she said. “I just want you to know that I am so lucky.”

When the tears started, Beyonce stopped to acknowledge her emotional state.

“I’m giving y’all an ugly cry. Thank y’all again. Thank you for allowing me to have a career. And when I fall, you lift me up. When I’m hungry, you feed me. And I just want to give you my light.”
