Penn State Report on Whether Paterno and Others Covered Up Sex Scandal to be Released Today

July 12, 2012 Uncategorized

A report by former FBI director Louis Freeh into whether head coach Joe Paterno and other Penn State officials covered up a sex scandal involving former assistant Jerry Sandusky will be released Thursday morning.

Freeh’s report is expected to discuss how Sandusky was treated after Paterno and other administrators received complaints about his forced sexual advances on young boys.
Sandusky was arrested in November and convicted in June on 45 counts of abuse involving 10 boys.
Penn State’s board of trustees ousted Paterno and president Graham Spanier Nov. 9 after Sandusky’s arrest. Paterno died of lung cancer Jan. 22 without speaking with Freeh.
