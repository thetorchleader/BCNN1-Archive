Detroit Bishop Eric Lloyd Opens New Christian Light Club for Teens

November 2, 2014 Christian

The next generation is our future s we have to invest in them! We have to do things differently to get their attention. But once we do that, we can get them either introduced or excited agin about Christ! Bishop Eric Lloyd of Rhema International Church in Metro Detroit is doing just that by opening The Edge, Metro Detroit’s Premier Christian Light Club.

Grand opening night of The Edge is this Friday, October 31st at 9pm until 1am and will be open every Friday Night. A couple weeks ago we introduced you all to The Rotunda for young adults 25 and up, well The Edge is for our teens ages 13 & up with only a $4.00 admission.

Expect Poetry, Gospel Rappers, Comedy, Games, Karaoke, Food, Clean Fun, And Much More!!! The edge is located at Rhema International Church, 6782 E. 9 Mile Rd., Warren, MI 48091.

For more information, Office: 586.806.0603, Toll-Free: 1.888.967.6777, http://www.rhemaiconline.org, (Enter through the rear entrance).
