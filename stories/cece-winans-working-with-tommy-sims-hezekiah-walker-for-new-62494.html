CeCe Winans Working With Tommy Sims, Hezekiah Walker for New Album?

March 19, 2015 Entertainment

Singer CeCe Winans attends Black Girls Rock! 2011 at the Paradise Theater on October 15, 2011 in New York City. (Credit: GETTY)

Since signing with Motown Gospel in 2013, the public hasn’t heard much about CeCe Winans’ forthcoming music.

But Bishop Hezekiah Walker leaked a few details on Instagram to the excitement of eager fans.

“It was great working with CeCe in the studio can’t wait to hear it,” the “Every Praise” singer captioned a photo posted on Instagram Tuesday, March 17.

He added, “It’s going to be Amazing!!!!”

While there is no word yet about specifics, Walker’s posting did confirm that multiple GRAMMY® award-winning producer and songwriter Tommy Sims is involved in 50-year-old Winans’ project.
