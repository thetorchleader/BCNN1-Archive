What Happened to the Hundreds of Bodies in Florida’s Old Colored Cemetery?

February 26, 2015 Black

The “old colored cemetery” today
GOOGLE MAPS/TWITTER

As the Deerfield Beach City Commission in Florida debated whether or not to allow a black cemetery to become a townhouse development during a special meeting on Tuesday night, a few residents had a different question: Where are the bodies?

The Sun Sentinel reports that family members of those buried at the cemetery have raised questions after several studies have determined that there are no longer any bodies at the cemetery which may date as far back as the 19th century. No one knows how or where the bodies were moved.

 “I saw it with my own eyes, I am a living eyewitness,” Benjamin Miller, who had family and friends buried there, said. The 83-year-old who now lives elsewhere, although he was born and raised in the community said that if the bodies were moved, “They didn’t notify me, and they didn’t notify my mother and they didn’t notify my daddy. They were living, they were next of kin. Where did they put the bodies?”

According to the Sentinel some estimates believe that up to 400 bodies were buried at the now vacant cemetery. This is also not the first attempt to build over the burial grounds, with a 1974 pitch being thwarted by angry residents.

Deerfield Beach City Mayor Jean Robb said that she believes the bodies were moved to Pineview Cemetery, owned by the city, but wasn’t able to confirm that suspicion.
