Cossy Pachares Named New President of LifeWay Christian Stores

February 11, 2015 Books

Cossy Pachares

A 14-year veteran LifeWay employee, Cossy Pachares has been chosen as the new president of LifeWay Christian Stores, the company announced this week.

He most recently served as associate vice president of B&H Publishing Group, where he led daily operations, including giving direct oversight to publishing and strategic planning. Pachares’ professional experience prior to coming to LifeWay includes various operational, financial, technology and human resources roles with companies in the travel, staffing, legal and oil and gas industries.

Pachares holds a bachelor’s degree from Baylor University and is a certified public accountant (CPA).

“I’m humbled to be asked to serve in this role,” Pachares said. “From the time I accepted Christ, God has been using Christian bookstores in my life. They are so important to people and communities.

“I love this ministry. My hope is that my experience and background can be used to further our ministry in communities. There are so many exciting opportunities ahead for us.”
