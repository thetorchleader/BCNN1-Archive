Selena Gomez In Tears at the Potter’s House Church; Singer Thanks Bishop T.D. Jakes for ‘Unbelievable Message’

October 13, 2014 Entertainment

Selena Gomez and T. D. Jakes / Instagram

Selena Gomez has thrown herself into faith, family and the community following another reported split from her on-off boyfriend Justin Bieber.

The singer shared a picture on her Instagram on Sunday with TD Jakes, pastor of The Potter’s House Church, in Dallas, Texas.

She appeared in a simple black outfit and appeared to be barefaced as she smiled warmly for the picture with Jakes.

In the caption, the 22-year-old thanked the pastor for his sermon, saying: “Unbelievable message. Had me in tears. Thank you Bishop for reminding me why I should always keep going.”

Gomez has been spotted at church a few times this year and was said to have been moved to tears by the preaching at Judah Smith’s Jesus Is conference in LA during the spring.

One person at the conference, Taylor Hodge, tweeted that she had seen Gomez “crying and praying in the spirit”.
