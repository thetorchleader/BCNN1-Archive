Death of Virginia High School Football Player, Jamond Salley, Is 8th of 2014 Season

October 23, 2014 Sports

A typical Friday night football game in Virginia turned tragic quickly when Park View High junior lineman Jamond Salley was injured during a game against Brunswick County High, came to the sidelines and collapsed unconscious. Moments later he fell into a bout of seizures and he was later pronounced dead at a nearby hospital. The death is the eighth this year to occur immediately after a player collapsed at a football game or practice, either on the field or sidelines and follows just weeks after three players died in the span of one week.

The eight player deaths in 2014 match the number who died in 2013, with more than a month still to play counting state playoff games. Like 2013, it is also the most player deaths in a single season since 1976, a stark statistic that underlines the growing concern shown by parents about the safety of youth football at various levels.

Friday’s death unfolded similarly to many of the others that have taken place this year. Salley collapsed in the second half of the Virginia contest and the game was eventually halted with four minutes remaining and Brunswick leading 26-0, with both teams coming together at midfield to form a prayer circle for Salley.

As students and faculty returned to campus in Mecklenburg County following a tough weekend digesting the shocking news of Salley’s death, all struggled to come to grips with the loss. According to Richmond ABC affiliate WRIC, Park View flew all flags at half mast on Monday, while grief counselors and priests were made available all day to meet with students.
