Illinois Senator Donne Trotter Ends House Race After Gun Charges Are Released

December 31, 2012 Uncategorized

An Illinois state lawmaker who was a frontrunner to replace former U.S. Rep. Jesse Jackson Jr. says he’s ending his candidacy because he doesn’t want the felony gun charges he faces to detract from the district’s important issues.


Illinois State State Sen. Donne Trotter leaves the Cook County Jail complex after posting bond Thursday, Dec. 6, 2012 in Chicago. Trotter was charged Wednesday, Dec. 6, 2012 with a felony count for allegedly trying to board a flight at O’Hare International Airport with a weapon and ammunition in a carry-on bag. (AP Photo/M. Spencer Green)
Donne Trotter was arrested Dec. 5 when security screeners at O’Hare International Airport found an unloaded .25-caliber Beretta handgun in his bag.
He told officers he has the gun for a job he works with a security firm and forgot it was in his bag before he went to the airport.
Trotter announced Saturday he will not seek the 2nd congressional district seat, explaining that solving the area’s economic and other problems was too important to allow his legal “situation to detract from what needs to be front and center.”
Source: The AP
