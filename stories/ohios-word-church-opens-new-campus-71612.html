Ohio’s Word Church Opens New Campus

May 3, 2015 Christian

Dr. R. A. Vernon (left), pastor at The Word Church Akron/Canton, and his wife, Victory, share a celebratory hug during the grand opening of the building on Saturday in Akron. (Ed Suba Jr./Akron Beacon Journal)

Parishioners at The Word Church Akron/Canton campus will gather today to worship for the first time in their new home — a freshly renovated 75,000-square-foot space at 1700 Brittain Road.

“I’m probably more excited about the kitchen and pantry area than the sanctuary because that area goes to the heart of who we are. We are an outreach ministry and we’re here to bless the city of Akron,” said the Rev. R.A. Vernon, founder and senior pastor of the Warrensville Heights-based megachurch. “I am so excited to be here. We consider ourselves the community’s church, and we are here to have a positive impact on our community, as we stand with the great pastors who are already in Akron.”

On Saturday, about 1,500 people got a look at the new building, during a grand-opening celebration. The event included a ribbon-cutting, praise and worship and a dedication of the building by Bishop F. Josephus Johnson II, founder and senior pastor at The House of the Lord in Akron and Vernon’s bishop.

During the tours of the campus, a resounding “Wow!” could be heard as the crowd filed through the front entrance into the lobby area, where The Word’s motto — “Come As You Are, You Won’t Stay As You Are”— is positioned to be the first thing seen.

“This is amazing. It’s unbelievable to see how this building has been transformed, from what looked like warehouse space,” said Charles Grant of Akron. “This is truly a day of celebration. While we appreciate our time at North High School, this is going to really open doors to outreach during the week.”

Grant, who serves in the prayer and altar ministries, joined the Akron/Canton campus ministry in 2010, shortly after The Word expanded into the area and began meeting at North High School. The move was predicated on a demographic study that showed about 550 people were driving from the Akron/Canton area to the Warrensville Heights campus for worship services. Since then, the local campus has grown to attract more than 1,000 people to its Sunday and Wednesday services.

The Akron/Canton campus is one of four that make up The Word Church, a contemporary, nondenominational church that is known for its giveaways of food, shoes, clothing, cars, houses and other items and for attracting a significant number of youth and unchurched young adults.
