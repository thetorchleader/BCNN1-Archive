The Tax Man Came Knocking at Robert de Niro’s Door, and de Niro Wrote a Check for $6.4 Million

February 27, 2015 Entertainment

Robert DeNiro

Robert De Niro is a deadbeat no more.

The two-time Academy Award-winning actor quickly cut a check for $6.4 million Thursday afternoon after learning he had been slapped with a lien for that staggering amount by the IRS, his lawyer said.

“If they sent the bill to the right address, it would have gotten paid sooner,” said the lawyer, Tom Harvey.

De Niro spokesman Stan Rosenfield said the feds delivered it “to an old address, currently an unoccupied commercial building.”

“Once Mr. De Niro learned of this tax delinquency, he had a check for the full amount hand-delivered to the IRS this morning,” Rosenfield said.

There was no immediate confirmation from the feds that the bill had been paid.

The IRS announced it was dunning De Niro in a Feb. 3 notice to New York City’s Department of Finance that was obtained Thursday by The Smoking Gun website.

It said De Niro had been notified three months ago that the government would go after his Tribeca condominium if “you neglect or fail to pay a tax debt.”

The bill for a whopping $6,410,449.20 was from his personal tax filing from 2013, according to the papers.
