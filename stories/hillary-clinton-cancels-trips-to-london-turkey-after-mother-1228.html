Hillary Clinton Cancels Trips to London, Turkey After Mother Becomes Ill

November 1, 2011 Uncategorized

Secretary of State Hillary Rodham Clinton
has canceled a trip to London and Turkey for international conferences
on cyber security and Afghanistan because of her mother’s illness.

Clinton had been
scheduled to depart Washington late Monday but decided against the trip
when her mother, 92-year-old Dorothy Rodham, became ill.

A State Department official says Clinton will remain in Washington. The nature of her mother’s illness was not released.

Wednesday’s
conference in Istanbul is focused on creating a regional strategy for
improving security and economic development in Afghanistan.

Source: The Associated Press
