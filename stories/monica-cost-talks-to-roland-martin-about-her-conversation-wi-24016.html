Monica Cost Talks to Roland Martin About her Conversation with Mark Cuban

May 27, 2014 African-American

Roland Martin & Monica Cost

What was supposed to be simply a professional development conference turned into an national conversation on prejudice and race.

Pathfinder, Monica Cost, a columnist for EURweb, spoke directly with Mark Cuban regarding his comments on being a bigot. Find out what she said, how he responded and her thoughts on his confession this Tuesday morning at 9am EST on TV One with Roland Martin.

It looks to be an interesting conversation that impacts us all.
