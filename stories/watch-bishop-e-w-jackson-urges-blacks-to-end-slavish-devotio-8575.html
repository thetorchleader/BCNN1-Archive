WATCH: Bishop E. W. Jackson Urges Blacks to End ‘Slavish Devotion’ to the Democratic Party

October 6, 2012 Uncategorized


African-American Christians have traditionally aligned themselves with the Democrat party.

But these days, a growing number of black ministers are calling on their church communities to take a closer look at the issues and support the candidates that best reflect their values.
Bishop E. W. Jackson, founder of the Chesapeake, Va.-based Exodus Faith Ministries is one of those pastors. Bishop Jackson is also the chairman of Ministers Taking a Stand.
