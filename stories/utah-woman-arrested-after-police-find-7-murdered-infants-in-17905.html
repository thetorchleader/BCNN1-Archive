Utah Woman Arrested After Police Find 7 Murdered Infants in her Home

April 13, 2014 National

Pleasant Grove Police investigate the scene where seven infant bodies were discovered and packaged in separate containers at a home in Pleasant Grove Sunday, April 13, 2014. (Photo: KSL)

Police have placed a 39-year-old woman in custody after finding seven deceased infants in a home Saturday.

According to NBC affiliate KSL in Salt Lake City, Megan Huntsman gave birth to at least 6 children before killing them. This all happened over a 10-year span from 1996 to 2006.

KSL reports the police found a newborn infant’s body on Saturday April 12 when they responded to the home after received a report about a deceased infant.

The infant was discovered by Huntsman’s husband. The two have been separated for some time but are legally married.

Huntsman is in jail on 6 counts of murder.
