Family Research Council Warns Pastors That If They Stay Silent, Nation Will ‘Fall Off Cliff’

October 13, 2014 Christian

The Family Research Council is urging Christians to get involved in next month’s midterm elections.

The conservative group says pastors and their congregations can’t afford to sit this one out.

In a Sunday evening webcast to churches around the country, FRC President Tony Perkins urged Christians to vote for candidates who reflect biblical values on abortion, marriage and religious freedom.

Rev. Rick Scarborough of Vision America urged pastors to use their pulpits to address moral issues.

“If the pastors remain silent, the country is going to fall off the cliff. We’re close now,” Scarborough said.
