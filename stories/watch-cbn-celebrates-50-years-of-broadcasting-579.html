WATCH: CBN Celebrates 50 Years of Broadcasting

October 4, 2011 Uncategorized

The Christian Broadcasting Network celebrated its 50th anniversary over the weekend.
Thousands attended events on the CBN campus in Virginia Beach, Va., which included carnival games, vendors, concerts, and fireworks.
Evangelist Reinhard Bonnke was the keynote speaker Saturday evening. MercyMe also performed, along with other musical guests.
