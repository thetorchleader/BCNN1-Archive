Iyanla Vanzant’s Special ‘Healing in Ferguson’ to Bring the Races Together

August 22, 2014 Black

Iyanla Vanzant meets with Ferguson police chief Thomas Jackson and Michael Brown’s uncle, pastor Charles Ewing

OWN has released more details about its upcoming “Iyanla: Fix My Life” special from Ferguson, Missouri.

The episode, titled “Iyanla: Fix My Life – Special Report: Healing in Ferguson,” will air on Tuesday, August 26 at 9 p.m. ET/PT – ahead of its official season 3 premiere on Sept. 6.

As previously reported, the episode follows Vanzant as she embeds herself in the local community and works to empower citizens by helping them formulate a plan for peace and healing in the wake of the shooting death of Michael Brown.

Iyanla visits the protest area and memorial, spending hours reaching out to individuals affected by the event to hear their stories and empowering them with ways to move past their anger.

She also brings face-to-face Ferguson police chief Thomas Jackson and Michael Brown’s uncle, pastor Charles Ewing. Together they discuss the need for peace in order to give the system time to work.
