10 False Ideas About Intercessory Prayer

November 13, 2011 Uncategorized

  • It is only for little old ladies.
  • It can only be done when we are feeling super godly.
  • It doesn’t really change situations (God’s going to do what he’s going to do anyway, right?)
  • It doesn’t really change us.
  • It is only for pastors.
  • Contrary to 1 Timothy 2:1-4 it can’t reach and rescue the lost.
  • Contrary to Colossians 4:12 it doesn’t really deepen the spiritual maturity of those we pray for regularly.
