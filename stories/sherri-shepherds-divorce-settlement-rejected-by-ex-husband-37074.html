Sherri Shepherd’s Divorce Settlement Rejected by Ex-Husband

September 4, 2014 Black

Sherri Shepherd is still going through drama with her ex-husband Lamar Sally. 

After the two filed separate divorce papers, Lamar has reportedly rejected the settlement that offered him a $100,000 one-time lump sum payment and $3,000 in monthly child support for his son that was delivered by a surrogate a few weeks ago.  Instead of the payment, Lamar reportedly wants $10,000 in child support, even though the original $100,000 payout was more than what the original prenup gave him. The prenup also didn’t require Sherri to pay any child support to Lamar at all.

This proposed settlement comes as a surprise because it was originally reported that the former host of “The View” refused to pay child support for her then unborn son.  If you remember, the two went through a chaotic split with details being aired out to the public. Lamar accused Sherri of being a bad mom and even filed for custody of their son.
