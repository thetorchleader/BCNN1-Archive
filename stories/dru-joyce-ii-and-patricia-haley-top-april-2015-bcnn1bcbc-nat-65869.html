Dru Joyce II and Patricia Haley Top April 2015 BCNN1/BCBC National Bestsellers List

April 7, 2015 Books

ATLANTA, GA, (April 7, 2015) — Bestselling authors, Dru Joyce II (Beyond Championships: A Playbook for Winning at Life — Nonfiction) and Patricia Haley (Unforgiving — Fiction) lead the bestsellers list of Black Christian authors on the April 2015 edition of the Black Christian News/Black Christian Book Company National Bestsellers List.

BCNN1.com has also released the April 2015 edition of the BCNN1/Amazon.com Kindle E-book Bestsellers List. This list ranks African-American Christian e-books based upon their sales rankings in the Amazon Kindle e-book store. Also, BCNN1 is releasing the April 2015 edition of the BCNN1/Barnes & Noble Nook E-book Bestsellers List. This list ranks African-American Christian e-books based upon their sales rankings in the B&N Nook e-book store.
