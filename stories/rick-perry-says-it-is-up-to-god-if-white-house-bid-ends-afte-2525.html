Rick Perry says It Is Up to God If White House Bid Ends After Iowa

December 29, 2011 Uncategorized

Texas Gov. Rick Perry says it’ll be up to God if his White House bid ends after the Iowa caucuses on Tuesday.

“That’s God’s will,” Perry told Ken Herman of the Austin American-Statesman when asked if there was any outcome on Tuesday that would cause him to end his presidential bid. “There might be an outcome that He decides that I wouldn’t go on.”
Perry, who upended the GOP presidential race when he joined the field in August, has been trailing in public opinion polls here in Iowa and nationally. He’s now running fourth in the field in Iowa, more than 10 percentage points behind leader Ron Paul, according to the recent surveys compiled by RealClearPolitics.
Perry has openly talked about his faith in the campaign.
