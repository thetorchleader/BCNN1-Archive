Cora Jakes Coleman Launches GoFundMe Page for Ministry Movement

January 4, 2015 Christian

Cora Jakes Coleman

Hi! My name is Cora Jakes Coleman and it’s been on my heart to speak life and give purpose to the broken hurting people of all generations.

To not only show God but give relationship to all people and to give fertility (the ability to produce a thing) faith to those who have allowed their fear to consume them and keep them from producing their hopes and dreams. Cora Jakes Coleman Ministries is dedicated to bringing purpose back to life. My hope is to be praying, leading, cheering and building a now generation of chosen leaders that will be faith fighters and producers in relationship and full consumption of Christ. CJC would love to have a office facility, conferences, seminars, prayer services, and also would love to be able to give back to children in our community and all people that are trying to pursue their ultimate purpose. I can’t do this without you. Help me to help the world. Here’s your opportunity to bless a ministry whose ultimate goal is to bless you!! God bless and may God speak life and fertility into every area of your life. Love you and thank you for your blessing and contribution to CJC Ministries!! Cora Jakes Coleman
