Divorce Between Beyonce’s Parents Is Final

December 19, 2011 Uncategorized

It is now completely over for Beyonce Knowles’ mother Tina and father Mathew.
The couple – who were married for 31 years – were issued with a final decree of divorce last month in Texas two years after she filed for separation.  At the time, the matter was dropped after neither party turned up for court.
Tina re-filed the papers on Aug. 30, and stated the marriage was “insupportable because of discord or conflict of personalities” which “prevents any reasonable expectation of reconciliation.”
Tina first filed papers a month after Mathew was hit with a paternity suit by actress Alexsandra White, who gave birth to a son, Nixon, in February in February 2010. Although he initially denied he was the father, a DNA test later proved Mathew was the child’s dad.
