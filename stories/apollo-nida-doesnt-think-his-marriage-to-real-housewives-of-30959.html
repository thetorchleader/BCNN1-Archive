Apollo Nida Doesn’t Think His Marriage to ‘Real Housewives of Atlanta’ Star, Phaedra Parks, Will Survive Prison Stint

July 19, 2014 Entertainment

‘Real Housewives of Atlanta’ cast member Phaedra Parks (right) and Apollo Nida attend Cirque du Soleil TOTEM Premiere at Atlantic Station on October 26, 2012, in Atlanta, Georgia. (Photo by Rick Diamond/Getty Images for Cirque du Soleil)

Apollo Nida is speaking out publicly for the first time since being sentenced July 8 to eight years in prison for cashing stolen checks and laundering more than $2.3 million through phony bank accounts.

Yesterday, Apollo sat down for an interview with Atlanta radio host Rodney Perry from BE100′s STFU! and revealed that he’s remorseful about his criminal actions

“I’m still human, but at the end of the day I am remorseful,” Nida said. “I love my kids, and coming from a non-father, non-mother background, at the end of the day, I feel most sad and disappointed that I let my little man down.”

Nida also confessed that his marriage to Real Housewives of Atlanta star Phaedra Parks is on the rocks.
