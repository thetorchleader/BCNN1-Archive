Great Boxer Manny Pacquiao Hints at Retiring from Boxing to Be Ambassador for God

February 15, 2012 Uncategorized

There was a time when a Manny Pacquiao-Floyd Mayweather Jr.
fight seemed inevitable, just because of the record gate it would
generate. But now it seems more and more likely that the match won’t
ever happen, with Pacquiao hinting that his retirement is on the
horizon.

In comments translated by MindaNews.com
in the Philippines, Pacquiao indicated that continuing in the ring puts
him at odds with his new-found dedication to the Catholic religion.

“It’s
not right that I share God’s word and at the same time hurt other
people,” Pacquiao told reporters at a military ceremony in the
Philippines.

“I’ve gained more than enough blessings these past years so I think it’s time for me to return the favor,” Pacquiao said.

So might his June 9 fight against Timothy Bradley in Las Vegas be his last?

Pacquiao recently accepted an invitation to become an ambassador for the Catholic Church. He also announced he was “no longer in the vice business,” and would sell his interest in a casino while also getting rid of his collection of about 1,000 fighting roosters.
