Halle Berry Wants Judge to Cut Child Support because Ex Gabriel Aubry Is Living Off the Money

October 16, 2014 Entertainment

Halle Berry poses in the press room during the 66th Annual Prime-Time Emmy Awards at Nokia Theatre L.A. Live on Aug. 25, 2014, in Los Angeles.
JASON MERRITT/GETTY IMAGES

The Oscar-winning actress wants Gabriel Aubry to get a job and stop using the system.

It seems as though Halle Berry is fed up with her ex Gabriel Aubry living off the child support she’s required to pay him. According to TMZ, Berry pays Aubry $16,000 a month for their daughter, Nahla, even though they have joint custody.

Berry recently filed court documents asking a judge to reduce the monthly payment to $3,000, saying that Aubry isn’t working and is comfortable living off child support. According to the report, Berry feels that Aubry shouldn’t have any problem finding a job and is abusing the system.

Berry has been paying Aubry the $16,000-a-month payment since June, when a Los Angeles judge also signed off on the custody dispute over the 6-year-old. In addition to the monthly payment, Berry was also required to pay a retroactive amount of $115,000 and $300,000 in Aubry’s attorney fees.
