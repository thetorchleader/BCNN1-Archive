Man Kills Woman then Kills Himself in Nahshua, New Hampshire

November 30, 2014 National

Authorities say a man apparently killed a woman in Nashua, then killed himself.

Attorney General Joseph Foster says the Nashua Police Department received a call late Saturday when a woman’s body was found on the Baldwin Street Bridge. He says she appeared to be a homicide victim.

Foster says that shortly after the body was discovered, police responded to a residence where a man was holed up with multiple firearms and refusing to come out. Foster says police evacuated adjacent homes, while other nearby residents were told to stay inside.

A SWAT team was activated. Foster says the team entered the home Sunday morning and found the man dead of an apparent self-inflicted gunshot wound.

Autopsies are planned for Monday to confirm the man’s and woman’s cause and manner of death. Their names haven’t been released.
