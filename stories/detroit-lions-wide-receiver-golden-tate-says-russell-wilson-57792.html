Detroit Lions Wide Receiver Golden Tate Says Russell Wilson Should Put End to Cheating Wife Rumors

February 20, 2015 Sports

Ashton Meem and Russell Wilson in happier times at the 2013 ESPYs. (Getty)

Don’t expect Golden Tate and Russell Wilson to reminisce about Seattle’s glory days anytime soon.

The Lions star wideout, who is rumored to have had an affair with the Super Bowl winning and losing QB’s now-ex-wife Ashton Meem, isn’t thrilled that his former teammate has yet to jump to his defense.

“It’s a bunch of bull****,” Tate tells TMZ in a video of the scandalous claims. “It’s sad that he’s letting this go on. ”

Wilson’s radio silence regarding the supposed tryst speaks volume to Tate.

“I don’t know what he believes,” said Tate, who signed with the Lions last off-season after winning a Super Bowl two seasons ago as one of Wilson’s top targets for the Seahawks.
