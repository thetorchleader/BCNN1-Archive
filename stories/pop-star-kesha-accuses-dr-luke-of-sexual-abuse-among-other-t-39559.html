Pop Star Kesha Accuses Dr. Luke of Sexual Abuse Among Other Things

October 14, 2014 Entertainment

Kesha arrives at the MTV Video Music Awards on Aug. 24 in Inglewood, Calif.
(Photo: Jordan Strauss, Invision/AP)

Pop star Kesha is embroiled in a nasty, legal war of words with her longtime music producer, Dr. Luke.

In a lawsuit filed Tuesday (and first reported by TMZ), Kesha, 27, says she was “sexually, physically, verbally and emotionally abused” for nearly 10 years by him, starting when she signed on with him at 18.

She says Luke would force her to use drugs and alcohol, and that he made repeated sexual advances toward her.

Dr. Luke, 41, whose real name is Lukasz Sebastian “Luke” Gottwald, has filed a counter complaint, denying and denouncing the accusations.

Luke’s lawyer, Christine Lepera, says in a statement to USA TODAY that the singer’s claims of abuse are part of a “campaign of publishing outrageous and untrue statements” engineered by Kesha’s mom and her new management company. She says the statements are “scurrilous” and “false.”
