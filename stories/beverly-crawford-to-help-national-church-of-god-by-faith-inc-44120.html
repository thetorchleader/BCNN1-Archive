Beverly Crawford to Help National Church of God by Faith Inc. Celebrate 100 Years

November 7, 2014 Christian

Beverly Crawford

She hails from the city of Gainesville, Florida. She’s travelled the world yet remains humble. God certainly has his hands on her life. To some she’s pastor, others an evangelist and to even some a friend, but to us she’s family and she’s coming to help us celebrate 100 years. You don’t want to miss this great event in history!

Oh, can’t you feel the excitement in the air? The preaching! The singing! The Gala! The fellowship! Oh, what a time! My, my, my…this convention will truly be special, hope to see you there.”
