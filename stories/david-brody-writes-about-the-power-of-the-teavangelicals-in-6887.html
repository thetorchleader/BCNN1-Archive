David Brody Writes About the Power of “The Teavangelicals” In New Book

July 3, 2012 Uncategorized

A noted political journalist has authored a new book that investigates the connection between members of the tea party movement and the Christian faith.
In his book The Teavangelicals: The Inside Story of How Evangelicals and the Tea Party are Taking Back America, David Brody, who is chief political correspondent of CBN News, does not just explore the emergence of the tea party movement as a powerful voting bloc, but he also unravels its path to power and its roots as a group in which 50 to 60 percent of the participants call themselves “evangelical Christians.”
“Conservative Christians who agree with the tea party agenda have picked up their bayonet, if you will, and their don’t tread on me flag and are breaking bread with libertarians,” he offers. “But they see the world from a biblical worldview, not necessarily from a libertarian worldview. And the book goes on to explain exactly the reasons why the two sides reverse simpatico on many issues.”
Brody says the book in many ways is a “myth-buster” of the perceptions people have of both the tea party and evangelical Christians.
