Beyonce’s Dad Says Elevator Fight Was Faked

August 28, 2014 Entertainment

Mathew Knowles and Beyonce in 2004. (Mark J. Terrill, AP)

Of course sometimes stuff goes down when it’s a billion dollars on an elevator.

That’s what Beyoncé had to say on her Flawless remix about the now-infamous elevator fight in May between husband Jay Z and sister Solange.

But their father, Mathew Knowles, says it may not be that simple. Bey’s former manager told Houston radio’s The Roula & Ryan Show that it was all a “Jedi mind trick” to sell tickets for Beyoncé and Jay Z’s On the Run tour, which began soon after.

The elevator incident was to “ignite that tour. It’s called a Jedi mind trick. A Jedi mind trick fools you a lot of times. Things you see, sometimes are (makes *poof* sound and hand gesture).”
