WATCH: Broncos Lose, but Focus on the Family’s John 3:16 Ad Still Scores

January 15, 2012 Uncategorized

“Sweet cheese and crackers!” That’s a Tim Tebow curse. Seriously.

Pictured: A still shot for the Focus on the Family ad,
which run during the Broncos vs. Patriots game on Saturday, Jan. 14.
(Image provided by Focus on the Family)

The clean-living Christian QB might be muttering that now, since the Patriot’s Tom Brady has cleaned the Broncos’ clock.

But even with the pious player’s gridiron loss, the big QB in the Sky still scored, thanks to an ad from Focus on the Family.

A
gaggle of darling tots recited John 3:16 — Tebow’s favorite verse
proclaiming salvation through Jesus — during a second quarter ad.

The
idea played on the excitement last week after Tebow threw 316 yards to
beat the Steelers and John 3:16 became the most searched term on the
Internet.

Focus President Jim Daly said Saturday night, “Our chief development officer had just read the USA Today Tuesday cover story,
and sort of tossed out, ‘What if we did an ad in Saturday’s game
focused on John 3:16?’ It was a tight deadline — we conceived, cast,
shot and delivered the ad in just over three days — but then we were
working with an excellent, inspired script.”
