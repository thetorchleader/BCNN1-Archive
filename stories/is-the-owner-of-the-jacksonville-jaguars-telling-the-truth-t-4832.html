Is the Owner of the Jacksonville Jaguars Telling the Truth that Tim Tebow Didn’t Want to Go to Jacksonville?

March 26, 2012 Uncategorized

When it became apparent Tim Tebow‘s days with the
Denver Broncos were numbered, the Jaguars immediately popped up as a
logical team to take him on. Tebow is from Jacksonville, and its NFL
team has not only struggled to win in recent years but filling EverBank
Stadium has also become a challenging task.

But apparently Tebow wasn’t interested in a homecoming.

“He didn’t want to come,” new Jaguars owner Shahid Khan tells The Florida Times-Union.
“Any one of the 53 players we have, have to be committed to
Jacksonville, making us be the best team we are. The question was: Did
he want to come? And if the decision had been taken out (of his hands)
and we got him, that would not be good for him, or for us.”

The Jets and Jags seemed to be the finalists to obtain Tebow last week when New York’s deal with the Broncos got hung up. The Times-Union story suggests Jacksonville would have had to part with a third-rounder to swing a trade in their favor.

“I think this is like any negotiation,” Khan said. “Our offer kept increasing. … Obviously we were serious.”

Khan
had polled the people in Jacksonville since he assumed control of the
team in January, and there was still plenty of local interest in the
hometown hero Jaguars GM Gene Smith opted not to draft in 2010.
