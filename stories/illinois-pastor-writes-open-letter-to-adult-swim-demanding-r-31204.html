Illinois Pastor Writes Open Letter to Adult Swim Demanding Retraction of ‘Disrespectful, Degrading’ “Black Jesus” Show

July 23, 2014 Christian

To the OWNERS, PRODUCERS, WRITERS and ADVERTISERS of ADULT SWIM and the BLACK JESUS SHOW,

I need all of the SPIRITUAL LEADERS that are tagged to this letter, to lend your WISDOM to drafting this LETTER that needs to be sent to the ADULT SWIM CHANNEL and their ADVERTISERS. If you are not familiar with this show, please go to my PAGE and view the TRAILER. It is utter foolishness and disrespectful!! We must work TOGETHER to keep this show from being AIRED! Please COPY this letter and have the members of your church to SIGN IT and to MAIL IT TO:

TURNER BROADCASTING
One CNN Center
Atlanta, Georgia 30303
Phone: 404-827-1700

ADULT SWIM is a subsidiary of TURNER BROADCASTING. If you are willing to help us please COMMENT to this NOTE so that other LEADERS will see that they are NOT ALONE in our discontent with this situation. Thank you for taking the time to defend the integrity of the IMAGES of our LORD and SAVIOR begin depicted on television. If you have any additional points that need to be made, please email them to me at: DVFIORG@aol.com. I will draft the FINAL LETTER tomorrow and will send it out by WEDNESDAY. Thank you again!

We, the Christian Community are vehemently opposed and violently offended at this upcoming program soon to air on your channel called BLACK JESUS. The level of disrespect aimed at the depiction of our Lord and Savior is inexcusable. We are hard pressed to find any value in this program, its message or its purpose. As a group of believers, we are prepared to launch a mass campaign and boycott of your channel and any advertisers that are financially profiting from this program. These continued attacks against our Christian Values and the degrading imagery of the Lord Jesus Christ, will be met with social media awareness to the entire Christian Community and a targeted boycott of your advertisers.

There is no way that this show would be aimed at the Jewish or Muslim community. We are demanding an IMMEDIATE retraction of this show and a PUBLIC APOLOGY to the Christian Community at large. We will continue to make our community aware of your blatant disrespect until there is a redemptive shift in the culture of your channel and programming. Please understand, that we will not take this lying down. We will fight this and any other program that seeks to demean our values, our Savior and our Beliefs. A draft of this letter is being distributed to every church in America.

Until All Have Heard,

Apostle David A. Rodgers and the Christian Community of America

Church Phone: (773)568-5403
Email: DVFIORG@aol.com
