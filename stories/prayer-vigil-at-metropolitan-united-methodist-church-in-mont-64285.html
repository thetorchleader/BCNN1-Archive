Prayer Vigil at Metropolitan United Methodist Church in Montgomery Remembers Children Lost or Killed by Crime

March 29, 2015 Black

People hold hands in prayer during a Prayer Vigil for youth victims of violent crime and the missing held at Metropolitan United Methodist Church in Montgomery, Ala., on Saturday March 28, 2015.
(Photo: Mickey Welsh / Advertiser)

LaQuanta Riley was 19 when she was abducted 11 years ago and never seen again. Aiden Howard, 6, died after an SUV crashed through an apartment and hit him in February. Tymothy Jackson Jr., 3, and 1-month-old Tyleah Jackson were killed in an accidental house fire, also in February. Trevor Shotack, 5, was killed in a house fire this month.

A dozen concerned community members gathered in front of Metropolitan United Methodist Church off Rosa L. Parks Avenue on a chilly Saturday afternoon to pray for those children and others killed by violent crime or tragedies, and those who have gone missing.

Riley’s mother, Pam Riley Boldin, created the Riley Relief Foundation and families of missing children support groups to help others, bring awareness and make sure no one goes through the pain of losing a child, like she has.

LaQuanta’s disappearance has been called by police, a strange abduction, after she left with someone she thought she knew Dec. 7, 2003, in the Fairview Avenue area in Montgomery.

“It doesn’t matter if you’re a child or you’re an adult, you better tell someone where you’re going because these people are not just taking small kids, those missing are from 80 years old down to newborns. Everybody is at risk,” Boldin said.

“We know my daughter went with someone she thought she trusted, but she didn’t know their intentions. … They were not trustworthy because 11 years later they have not allowed her to come home,” Boldin added.

Community prayer vigils are held regularly around the city as a way to remember victims, pray for the city and make a stand against violent crime. The vigils are organized by Ministers of the River Region and Tri-Success, a resource service for women.
