“Red Tails” Out on DVD Today, May 22nd

May 22, 2012 Uncategorized

As “Red Tails” prepares for its May 22nd release on Blu-ray, DVD and digital download, we caught up with one of its stars to look back at the film’s impact since its Jan. 20th release in theaters.
“The theater release was amazing, with people turning out in record numbers and showings that were sold out all over the country,” said Eliijah Kelley , who played pilot Samuel “Joker” George. “That was one of the most emotional happenings in my life thus far – just being part of a movement that people want to be a part of.”
Produced by George Lucas and helmed by Anthony Hemingway, “Red Tails” also stars Cuba Gooding Jr., Terrence Howard and Nate Parker in the story of the Tuskegee Airmen, the first African-American combat unit to serve in World War II.
Special features on the Blu-ray includes the documentary “Double Victory” along with profiles of the filmmakers and behind-the-scenes featurettes.
