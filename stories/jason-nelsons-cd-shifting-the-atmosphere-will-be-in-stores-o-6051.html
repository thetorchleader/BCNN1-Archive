Jason Nelson’s CD ‘Shifting the Atmosphere’ Will be In Stores on May 22

May 19, 2012 Uncategorized

Jason Nelson’s major label debut “Shifting the Atmosphere,” one of the most anticipated Gospel CDs of the year, will be in stores May 22.

Before the CD has even been released, Nelson finds himself in the unique position of having a Top-10 single at radio.  He was also featured last fall on Kirk Franklin’s Fearless Tour; received acclaim from The New York Times, and delivered a powerful appearance on the internationally broadcast TBN.
Jason Nelson’s major label debut on Verity Gospel Music Group elevates contemporary urban praise and worship to new heights.
“We worked hard to put together a CD with no fillers,” says Nelson.  “By the time people get to the last song, I want listeners to feel like their atmosphere has really shifted.  I hope that the CD helps people to redefine themselves and their relationships with God, and to worship God in an intense way.  I want people to see God, hear God and experience Him in a profound way.”
The title track, currently a Top-10 radio single, was called “an excellent first single…a religious quiet-storm ballad” by The New York Times.   Nelson says that, with “Shifting the Atmosphere,” he wants people to understand that when they get in trouble, when things get rough, they can worship their way into a better circumstance.  “Worship invites God into your space, and wherever God is, things have to change.  That’s where we get ‘shifting the atmosphere.'”
