5 Tips to Minimize Stress, Stay Happy Throughout This Holiday Season

October 23, 2014 Health, Lifestyle

A Thanksgiving turkey.
(Photo: Larry Crowe, AP)

Keep cortisol levels low and spirits bright — even if (gasp) the turkey catches on fire — with these five tips this holiday season.

1. Budget your spending

Make a plan for how much you’re willing to spend on what during the holidays, and stick to it. This should relieve the stress of overspending.

“Everyone spends more than usual over the holidays, and if you have debt this makes that worse,” said Amit Sood, an internal medicine physician, author of The Mayo Clinic Guide to Stress-Free Living. “It’s been found that debt goes up around Christmas and the New Year.”

2. How to deal with travel stress? One tip: Don’t overpack

“A key to stress-free living is keeping things simple,” Sood said. When traveling, he advises packing lightly to avoid unnecessary anxiety.

3. Stay healthy, stay healthy, stay healthy

Maintain your fitness and eating habits throughout the holiday season to avoid the added stress of extra pounds. Exercise reduces levels of stress hormones, including adrenaline and cortisol. It also stimulates endorphins, the body’s natural mood elevators.

“Make sure you don’t fall off the health bandwagon,” Sood adds.
