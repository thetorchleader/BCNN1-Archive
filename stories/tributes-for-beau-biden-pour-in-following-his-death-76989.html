Tributes for Beau Biden Pour in Following his Death

May 31, 2015 National, zTransfer-UCN

After winning the attorney general race in 2006, Beau Biden makes his acceptance speech with his father, Joe Biden, in his room at a Wilmington hotel on Nov. 7, 2006. (Photo: Fred Comegys, The News Journal)

Tributes poured in for former Delaware attorney general Beau Biden, who died Saturday of brain cancer at 46.

President Obama said his family was grieving with the Bidens, adding that Beau took after his father Vice President Joe Biden by devoting his life to public service.

“In the words of the Biden family: Beau Biden was, quite simply, the finest man any of us have ever known” —@VP Biden pic.twitter.com/wayhHq78hx

— The White House (@WhiteHouse) May 31, 2015

“For all that Beau Biden achieved in his life, nothing made him prouder; nothing made him happier; nothing claimed a fuller focus of his love and devotion than his family,” Obama said. “Just like his dad.”

“Michelle and I humbly pray for the good Lord to watch over Beau Biden, and to protect & comfort his family” —@POTUS pic.twitter.com/KilnFEDbN4

— The White House (@WhiteHouse) May 31, 2015

Delaware Governor Jack Markell extended his sympathies to the family of Joseph Robinette “Beau” Biden III.

My entire family is shattered by his death.​ http://t.co/NBmh0E6oXJ

— Gov. Jack Markell (@GovernorMarkell) May 31, 2015

“Carla and I are heartbroken for Hallie, his children, his parents, his siblings, and the entire Biden family. Beau spent his entire career in the service of his country and his state. He was an outstanding Attorney General, lawyer, soldier, and father. I feel privileged to have served with him and Delaware is a better place thanks to him, Markell said in a statement. “Beau was also a friend. He was unfailingly interested in the well being of Carla, my kids and my extended family. My entire family is shattered by his death. ”

Markell directed that the U.S. and Delaware flags be flown at half-staff to honor Beau Biden.

Others in the political sphere, from the Clintons to Senate Minority Leader Harry Reid, D-Nev., expressed their sympathies.

My heart is broken for the family of Beau Biden—a wonderful man who served his country with devotion and lived his life with courage. -H

Hillary & I mourn the loss of Beau Biden – so full of life, love, honor, and service – and we pray for the strength of his wonderful family.

It is with great sorrow and heavy hearts that we share the news of the loss of a great leader, Maj. Beau Biden. http://t.co/E77RPIHlbQ

— Del National Guard (@DelawareNG) May 31, 2015

Beau was a wonderful, strong and courageous man. I thought the world of him. Our deepest condolences to the Biden family in this tragic time

From the entire Cheney family – our deepest sympathy and heartfelt prayers to the@VP Biden family.

— Liz Cheney (@Liz_Cheney) May 31, 2015

Beau Biden was a throughly admirable man. Great husband, father, son, veteran and public servant. My heart aches for the Biden family.

— David Axelrod (@davidaxelrod) May 31, 2015

Our deepest condolences go out to @VP Joe Biden and his family as they and the nation mourn the loss of Beau Biden.

— NAACP (@NAACP) May 31, 2015

My heart goes out to the Biden family – we honor Beau Biden’s faithful service in our nation’s military and as Attorney General of Delaware
