Some Accuse Ray Lewis of the Baltimore Ravens of Not Being What he Used to Be; he Agrees and says ‘That’s Life’

October 12, 2012 Uncategorized

The buzz in Baltimore is that Ray Lewis isn’t what he used to be.

A former teammate offered a blunt and unforgiving critique via Aaron Wilson of the Baltimore Sun.
“He’s outmanned at the point of attack,” retired Ravens offensive lineman Wally Williams told the Sun. “He’s not as physical. You see a lot of Ray on the ground, on his back. He gets overpowered and stuck to blocks.
“You can’t expect a guy to not lose a couple of steps with all the wear and tear on his body. You’re not accustomed to seeing Ray have so many problems. It’s glaring, it looks really bad.”
Williams adds that Lewis isn’t getting enough help from his defensive line.
Baltimore ranks 24th in total defense and 20th against the run, though they are sitting at 4-1 thanks in part to an edge in the turnover battle.
On Sunday, the front seven was overwhelmed in Kansas City to the tune of 140 yards for Chiefs running back Jamaal Charles. Yet the Ravens still won, 9-6.
So what does the 37-year-old, 13-time Pro Bowler have to say about all of this? A reporter asked him this week if he still has “it,” and Lewis laughed.
