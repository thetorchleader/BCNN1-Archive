WATCH: T. D. Jakes Responds to Deitrick Haddon’s Challenge to “Open the Doors” of the Church and Let Reality Show Cameras In

November 22, 2014 Christian

Courtesy T.D. Jakes Enterprises

Deitrick Haddon, one of the ministers featured on Oxygen network’s “Preacher of L.A.” recently called on T.D. Jakes, Creflo Dollar, and Paul Morton to let reality TV cameras into their churches and into their lives.

HipHollywood.com asked T.D. Jakes for his response to Haddon’s challenge. He said, “I’m a little bit busy. I’m doing movies; I’m doing television; I’ve done talk shows. I’m pastoring 30,000 people. I think my plate is full.”
