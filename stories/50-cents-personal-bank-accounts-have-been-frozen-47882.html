50 Cent’s Personal Bank Accounts Have Been Frozen

December 2, 2014 Entertainment
Rapper 50 Cent’s personal bank accounts have reportedly been frozen following an expensive legal battle.The 21 Questions hitmaker allegedly failed to pay out $17 million (£11 million) after he was accused of copying the design of headphones by a company called Sleek Audio. Bosses at the technology firm claimed the hip-hop star marketed the design as his own, and judges sided with Sleek Audio in the legal battle.

Editors at TMZ.com report that because the judgement wasn’t paid, 50 Cent’s personal bank account has since been frozen.

An attorney for the star tells the website they are appealing the Sleek Audio judgement. Sleek bosses have allegedly been “desperately attempting” to hold money from any accounts it can locate and they insist 50 Cent’s money is “appropriately protected”. A source added that all the rapper’s business accounts are safe and only his personal account is in jeopardy.
