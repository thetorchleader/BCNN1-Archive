Bebe Winans on a 7-Day Tour of Kuwait, Serving the Troops

September 7, 2012 Uncategorized

R&B and Gospel music favorite Bebe Winans is on a 7-day tour of Kuwait as he tweeted today.
According to the Winans’ tweets, during the trip Winnas will serve the troops by performing for them, he will visit several bases in Kuwait including Camp Virginia, Camp Arifjan, Camp LSA, Camp Buering, and Camp Patriot.
Winans tweeted, “In addition to performing for the servicemen and women, I’m also participating in base activities including Command visits, walking tours and autograph/photographs sessions.”
