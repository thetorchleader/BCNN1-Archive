“Tia Mowry At Home” Is Coming to the Cooking Channel

March 26, 2015 Entertainment

Source: Michael Tran / Getty

Tia Mowry-Hardrict is going to be whipping up something tasty for viewers of The Cooking Channel starting next month!

The “Instant Mom” star announced via Instagram that she’s heading to the kitchen for her next big project, “Tia Mowry At Home.” She didn’t release many details about the show—just that it debuts April 29 at 9 p.m. EST.

She shared a screen capture from the set, excitedly revealing, “I HAVE A COOKING SHOW!!! TIA MOWRY AT HOME!”
