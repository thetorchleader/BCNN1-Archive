BMI Honors Edwin Hawkins, Lady Tramaine Hawkins, and Kurt Carr at Luncheon

January 23, 2013 Uncategorized

Broadcast Music, Inc. (BMI) honored Edwin Hawkins, Lady Tramaine Hawkins, and Kurt Carr at the 14th annual Trailblazers of Gospel Music Awards Luncheon on Friday, January 18 at Rocketown in Nashville, TN.
Hosted by CeCe Winans and Donnie McClurkin, along with Del Bryant, BMI President & CEO, and Catherine Brewton, BMI Vice President, Writer/Publisher Relations, the event also recognized BMI’s Most-Performed Gospel Song of the Year: Kirk Franklin’s “I Smile.”
