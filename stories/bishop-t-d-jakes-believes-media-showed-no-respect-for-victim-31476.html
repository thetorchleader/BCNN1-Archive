Bishop T. D. Jakes Believes Media Showed No Respect for Victims of Malaysia Flight M17 Crash

July 25, 2014 Christian

Bishop TD Jakes ministers at The Potter’s House in Dallas, TX June 2013 (Credit: TD Jakes Ministries)

Did the media go too far with its coverage of the Malaysia Flight M17 crash? Bishop TD Jakes thinks so.

During a Sunday morning sermon at the Potter’s House in Dallas on July 20, the mega pastor expressed his disapproval of the handling of the tragic story, calling it disrespectful.

“Normally, out of respect for the dead and the people that love them, there’s certain dignity that ought to go with any site where there are human remains,” said the leader who turned 57 last month.

“In the news that we’ve been seeing lately, there has been no respect for the dead, no respect for their remains.”

298 passengers and crewmembers—including dozens of children—died while aboard the Malaysia Airlines Boeing 777 en route from Amsterdam to Kuala Lumpur.

The commercial plane was shot out of the sky by a missile in eastern Ukraine Thursday, July 17.

Body parts were strewn all over the open crash site leaving victims’ mangled frames decomposing for days under the scorching sun.

“I know the news media has to cover it and they have to talk about the various human parts being burned and being connected with the metal until you’re stepping over human organs and remains,” Jakes continued. “But every time they talk about it, I think, Shhh.”
