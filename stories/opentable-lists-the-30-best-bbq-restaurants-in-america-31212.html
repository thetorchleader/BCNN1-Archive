OpenTable Lists the 30 Best BBQ Restaurants In America

July 23, 2014 God & Food

Chuck’s Southern Comforts Café – Burbank, Illinois

OpenTable Diners’ Choice

2014 Best Barbecue Restaurants in America

As voted by OpenTable Diners

Apple pie aside, there is, perhaps, no fare more quintessentially American than barbecue. The Top 30 Barbecue Restaurants awards honor the eateries that bring us together over plates of lovingly slow-cooked, smoky dishes of deliciousness. The list of winners is determined after analyzing more than five million reviews of nearly 19,000 restaurants across the country – all submitted by verified diners.
