Cain Finds Himself Fighting Back Against Affair Claims Again

November 29, 2011 Uncategorized

An Atlanta businesswoman said Monday she had a 13-year extramarital affair with Herman Cain, prompting an extensive and harsh denial from the former Godfather’s Pizza CEO.
But in an extraordinary step for a presidential candidate, Cain preempted the claims on national television.
In an attempt to diffuse the latest grenade rolled into his campaign, Cain said that while he’s familiar with the Atlanta businesswoman who’s produced records of phone calls and text messages that she says substantiate her claims, the two did not have a sexual relationship.
It’s another instance of flatly contradicting the account of a woman from Cain, who surged briefly to the top of polls on the strength of his appeal to the party’s social conservative base. Confronted with multiple claims that he’d sexually harassed women while the head of the National Restaurant Association, Cain initially claimed not to know of the women, their allegations or the settlements they received from the group.
But Cain waited 10 days to directly respond to those allegations — a stark contrast to his decision to address the report of the affair before it aired, followed by a statement from his campaign lumping in the latest claims with the sexual harassment allegations as what he called “dirty politics and smear tactics.”
Ginger White, though, says there’s no denying what happened between them.
“It was pretty simple. It wasn’t complicated. I was aware that he was married and I was also aware I was involved in a very inappropriate situation, relationship,” White said in an interview with Atlanta’s Fox 5 News that the station that aired Monday afternoon.
White provided the station with bills that include 61 phone calls or text messages from a number that she says is Cain’s private cell phone number.
