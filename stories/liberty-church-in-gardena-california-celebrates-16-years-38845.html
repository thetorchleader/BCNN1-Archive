Liberty Church in Gardena, California Celebrates 16 Years

October 12, 2014 Black

Pastor David Cross

The Liberty Church Family will celebrate its ‘Sweet 16th’ Church Anniversary on Sunday, October 12, at 10 a.m., in the edifice located at 14725 South Gramercy Place in Gardena.

The theme is ‘Always Abounding’ with the scripture reference of 2 Corinthians 9:8.  The guest preacher will be Bishop Miquail Broadus, pastor of Grace Temple Baptist Church in Los Angeles.

“Mere words are inadequate to enumerate the manifold blessings that God has bestowed upon The Liberty Church,” said a member.

“Since our first worship service in October 1998 and by God’s grace through faith, we have expanded our sanctuary, more than doubled our attendance, built a new baptismal pool, fellowship hall and kitchen, and remodeled many areas of our administrative offices.”

Pastor Cross said, “The Lord shared with me, in no uncertain terms, that He would continue to powerfully bless and grow the ministry.  God has done just that and so much more.”
