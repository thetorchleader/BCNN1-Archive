LISTEN: Karen Ehman, Tiffany L. Warren, and Regina Mixon on BC Book Review Show #261

February 22, 2015 Books

The authors that we are featuring today are: Karen Ehman, author of “Keep It Shut: What to Say, How to Say It, and When to Say Nothing at All”; Tiffany L. Warren, author of “The Favorite Son”; and Regina Mixon, author of “Have I Told You Lately That I Love You?”.

The purpose of this show is to highlight wholesome Christian books, promote them to our audience, encourage you to buy these books for yourself and for others, and most importantly, to see people come to Christ and lives changed for the glory of God.

MUSICAL SELECTION: “Your Great Name” by Krissy Nordhoff, “Love the Lord” by Lincoln Brewster, and “Your Grace is Enough” by Matt Maher
