Man Shot to Death Outside of New Orleans Church

December 30, 2012 Uncategorized

A man was found shot to death outside of a church in the 9th Ward on Sunday morning, police said. The New Orleans Police Department said the man was found inside of a blue Chevy Malibu near the 3200 block of Pleasure Street, in front of the Greater Carver Missionary Baptist church. 
He was identified by the Orleans Parish Coroner’s Office as Ronald Washington, 32, the coroner’s chief investigator, John Gagliano, said. 
Washington was found dead around 9 a.m. by a citizen from the area, police said. He had been shot once.
Parishioners attending the church’s 9 a.m. Sunday service were instructed to enter through the building’s back door by police, as Washington was found directly in front of the side entrance, a deacon at the church, who did not wish to give his name, said.
