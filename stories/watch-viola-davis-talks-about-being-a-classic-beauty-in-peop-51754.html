WATCH: Viola Davis Talks About Being a ‘Classic Beauty’ in People’s Choice Award Acceptance Speech

January 9, 2015 Entertainment

One of our fave actresses, Viola Davis, received an award at the People’s Choice Awards 2015 on Wednesday night for ‘Favorite Actress in a New TV Series’ for her stellar performance in How to Get Away with Murder.

The gorgeous actress received her award with a lovely speech. In her acceptance speech, Viola Davis referenced the New York Times article that called her “less classically beautiful” than lighter-skinned actresses.
