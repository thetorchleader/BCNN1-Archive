Michelle J. Howard, First Woman and First Black Admiral, Visits Commissions Cadets at Tuskegee

May 10, 2015 Black

Admiral Michelle J. Howard, Vice Chief of Naval Operations, speaks. (Source: WSFA 12 News)

Donovan Coleman, 22, was commissioned as Second Lt. Friday in the U.S. Army, commissioned by Admiral Michelle J. Howard, Vice Chief of Naval Operations. “Oh, it was a pleasure, a very big pleasure. We heard her talk before,” Coleman explained. 

The commander started her speech at Tuskegee University with a little humor and then turned serious. Howard issued a direct challenge to the very cadets sitting in front. Howard commanded, ‘light the match.’

“This is your moment of truth. Find your wood. Stack up your best efforts. You get one match. One match. There are consequences to failure just as there is satisfaction when you succeed. I often learn you don’t get to pick your moments of success. You must be ready when it’s thrust upon you,” Howard proclaimed.

Michelle J. Howard has been in the fire many times before. It was Howard who drew up plans to rescue Capt. Richard Phillips six years ago in the Gulf of Aden. You may recall Somali pirates captured Phillips’ cargo ship.

Howard is not only the first female to hold a military rank this high. She’s also the first African-American.
