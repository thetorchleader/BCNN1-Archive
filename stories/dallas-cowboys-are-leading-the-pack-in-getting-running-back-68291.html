Dallas Cowboys Are Leading the Pack In Getting Running Back Peterson

April 18, 2015 Sports

Ronald C. Modra/Getty Images
Adrian Peterson told USA Today: “I take full responsibility, because I spanked my child, and no matter what my intentions were, I end up leaving those marks on his legs. That’s the bottom line.”

Adrian Peterson and the Vikings are that awkward couple arguing at the bar, and other NFL teams are the single men watching; waiting to take advantage of a potential breakup.

Peterson, under contract with Minnesota for three more years and owed $13 million next season after he was reinstated by the NFL this week, has expressed interest in playing for Dallas. At least one report indicates the Cowboys could indeed trade for the 30-year-old running back and become the home wreckers.

“The Cowboys are the front-runners,” one general manager told Bleacher Report’s Mike Freeman on Thursday. “But I actually believe the Raiders are right there.”

A trade for Peterson won’t be easy. The Vikings have said they have no interest in dealing their star player, and another scout told Freeman that the high price tag is considered “an anchor around Peterson’s neck.”

The Cardinals were named with the Cowboys and Raiders as possible trade partners, according to the report.

Peterson felt slighted by Minnesota when the team separated itself from the running back amid child abuse allegations last year. He’s now allowed to participate in Vikings offseason activities, but Peterson seems to have no interest in playing for the organization that drafted him in 2007.
