“The Book of Negroes” is Coming February 16th at 8 pm/7pmC on BET

February 13, 2015 Entertainment

“The Book of Negroes”
From L-R: Aminata Diallo (Aunjanue Ellis) & Sam Fraunces (Guba Gooding Jr.)— Photo Credit: Entertainment One/BET

BET marks Black History Month with its first miniseries, “The Book of Negroes” — a six-part historical drama akin to “Roots” and based on Lawrence Hill’s novel Someone Knows My Name.

The story tells, in flashback, the extraordinary journey of Aminata Diallo, played as an adult by Aunjanue Ellis (“Ray,” “The Help”). Aminata’s story begins, slowly, in a peaceful village in West Africa, where she is captured by slave traders and shipped to South Carolina. The adult Aminata emerges at the end of the grueling first hour; she will go on to make her way through New York during the American Revolution, land in Nova Scotia, then pass through the jungles of Sierra Leone, finally securing her freedom in England at the start of the 19th century.

Cuba Gooding Jr. (“Jerry Maguire,” “A Few Good Men”), Lou Gossett Jr. (“An Officer and a Gentleman,” “Roots”) and Jane Alexander (“The Cider House Rules”) also star. Graphic cruelty, not to mention violence, makes for difficult viewing in this lavishly produced miniseries. But it’s worthwhile, especially as director Clement Virgo has opened a new window on the experience of blacks in Canada.

The Book of Negroes, an historical document recording the names and descriptions of 3,000 African-American slaves who had to work for the British during the American Revolution, is at the core of the work.
