Starbucks Brings Out the Frappuccino Cookie Straws

April 30, 2015 God & Food

Starbucks

You know what pairs nicely with Starbucks’ new S’mores Frappuccino? A cookie straw, of course.

In conjunction with the limited-time S’mores Frappuccino release on Tuesday, April 28, Starbucks announced that it will offer customers free Frappuccino Cookie Straws while supplies last. Described as a “rolled sweet wafer biscuit lined with rich chocolate ganache,” the cookie straw will be available for purchase for 95 cents once stores run out of their free supplies.

The cookie straw is reminiscent of Dominique Ansel’s brilliant “Chocolate Chip Cookie Milk Shots,” which sparked a nationwide cookie trend — and these edible coffee cups — earlier this year.

The new S’mores Frap, by the way, is made with marshmallow whipped cream, milk chocolate sauce, graham crackers, coffee, milk and ice — and then topped off with even more marshmallow whipped cream and bits of graham cracker. Bring on summer!
