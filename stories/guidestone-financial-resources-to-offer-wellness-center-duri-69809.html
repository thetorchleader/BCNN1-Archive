GuideStone Financial Resources to Offer Wellness Center During 2015 SBC Meeting

April 25, 2015 Christian

GuideStone Financial Resources will again offer its Wellness Center during the Southern Baptist Convention annual meeting. Messengers can visit the exhibit hall in Columbus, Ohio, to take advantage of the Wellness Center’s free services, valued at up to $150.

The wellness screening offers blood-based screenings; for best results, fast at least the previous 12 hours. Participants will receive a personalized report from physicians and health care professionals suitable to take to their family doctors. GuideStone’s booth will also offer retirement and insurance consultations during the SBC annual meeting and Pastors’ Conference June 15–17.

GuideStone will offer three special seminars. The “Retirement Income Solutions” seminar is designed for participants in GuideStone’s retirement plans who are within five years of their intended retirement date and offers information on retirement income options. “Compensation Planning for Smaller Churches” offers insights on how smaller churches can make the most of limited budgets while providing adequately for their pastors’ needs. A third seminar, “Health Care Reform Impacts Your Church, Too,” will discuss new reporting requirements, new changes to premium reimbursement arrangements, penalties for non-compliance and additional regulations affecting churches and ministries of all sizes.

All seminars are free, but as space is limited, preregistration is recommended. Visit GuideStone.org/SBC2015 after May 1 to register. Participants also can download a health questionnaire on the website and take it to the Wellness Center.
