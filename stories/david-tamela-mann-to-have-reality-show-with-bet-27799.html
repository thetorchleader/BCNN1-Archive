David & Tamela Mann to Have Reality Show with BET

June 23, 2014 Black

TV viewers met David and Tamela Mann through their popular characters Leroy Brown and Cora on “Meet the Browns,” but the couple is getting ready to introduce you to a new pair: themselves via their new BET reality show, “Meet The Manns.”

AlwaysAlist reports the series will follow their lives as well as their five children, eight grandchildren and extended relatives who all live nearby.

The alliance with BET represents a natural progression for the Manns.

“We’re excited to announce our new television home is with BET,” the couple said in a joint statement. “BET is a great fit for the Mann brand and the perfect place for this new show. There’ll also be some news and reveals we’re saving to share on the show – you’ll have to tune in to find out what’s in store.”
