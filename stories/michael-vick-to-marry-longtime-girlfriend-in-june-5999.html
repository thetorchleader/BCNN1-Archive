Michael Vick to Marry Longtime Girlfriend in June

May 17, 2012 Uncategorized

He has failed to pilot the Philadelphia Eagles to a Super Bowl championship, so let’s hope Michael Vick has better luck on the way to the altar.

Vick will marry his longtime fiancee Kijafa Frink in a celebrity-filled wedding this summer in South Florida. The New York Post reports the Eagles QB will tie the knot at the legendary Fontainebleau Miami Beach on June 30.
Aside from supporting her future husband’s NFL career, Frink co-owns PINK Elephant fashion jewelry store in Philadelphia. The store sells affordable jewelry and women’s fashion accessories.
Vick’s wedding reportedly will include a handful of important people that helped him rehab his life after a felony conviction for running a dogfighting operation. Vick, 31, originally proposed to his fiancee before going off to jail for two years.
Former Colts coach Tony Dungy, who served as Vick’s mentor, Eagles coach Andy Reid and the quarterback’s agent Joel Segal are just a few of the bigger names on the wedding guest list.
