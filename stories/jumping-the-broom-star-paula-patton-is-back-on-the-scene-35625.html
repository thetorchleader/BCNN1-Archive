‘Jumping the Broom’ Star Paula Patton Is Back on the Scene

August 26, 2014 Entertainment

Paula Patton at HBO’s Post Emmy Awards party on August 25. Photo: AP

After a lengthy hiatus from the limelight, Paula Patton is back!

The 38-year-old actress made a surprising appearance at HBO’s Post Emmy Awards reception Monday night.

It was her first appearance since flying solo at the 2014 Vanity Fair Oscar Party on March 2, days after her splitting from her husband Robin Thicke.

Patton was all smiles as she wore a see-through black mini dress and black pumps.

E! News reports the “Baggage Claim” actress was seen smiling and laughing with other Hollywood elites at the party.

In July, the 37-year-old “Blurred Lines” singer confessed to having not seen his wife in four months during a Hot 97 interview. He was on a promotional (apologetic) tour for his latest album appropriately titled “Paula.”
