WATCH: Billy Graham’s Age or Health Hasn’t Stopped Him from Spreading the Gospel

September 24, 2011 Christian

Rev. Billy Graham will turn 93 in November. And although the popular evangelist has a few health issues, they haven’t stopped him from spreading the gospel.
Graham’s new book Nearing Home: Life, Faith, and Finishing Well is set to release in October.
He also has a new exhibit at the Billy Graham Library in Charlotte, N.C. The exhibit will showcase Graham’s personal writings and well as those belonging to his late wife, Ruth.
The Billy Graham Evangelistic Association is working hard to connect with younger generations too.
The ministry is using social media to reach out. It has an active Twitter page and a Facebook page with more than 300,000 fans.
