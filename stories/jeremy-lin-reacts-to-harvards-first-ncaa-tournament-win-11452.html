Jeremy Lin Reacts to Harvard’s First NCAA Tournament Win

March 25, 2013 Uncategorized

Houston Rocket Jeremy Lin graduated from Harvard in 2010, so he was particularly excited to watch the 14-seed Crimson play 3-seed New Mexico Thursday night.

Behind 18 points from Wesley Saunders, the Crimson upset the Lobos and notched their first NCAA tournament victory in school history. Lin celebrated accordingly, with three goggles.
