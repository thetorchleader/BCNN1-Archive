Bestselling Thanksgiving E-books Share Ways to Celebrate and Enjoy the Thanksgiving Holiday More Meaningfully

November 22, 2011 Christian

Bestselling Authors Share How to Get in a “Thanksgiving State of Mind” Through Four Thanksgiving E-Books: Money Under the Car Seat, 365 Days of Thanking God, and for the Children, The Thanksgiving That Almost Wasn’t and A Miraculous Thanksgiving


National bestselling author, Daniel Whyte III, along with his wife and children, have collaborated to help readers make their Thanksgiving celebrations more meaningful. Daniel Whyte III, along with family and friends, has written a light and fresh celebration of Thanksgiving in his book, Money Under the Car Seat & Other Things to Thank God For. 

A perennial bestseller on Amazon Kindle in the “Holidays” category, this book includes the story of Whyte’s journey from being a “literal Scrooge” and how the warm spirit of Thanksgiving drew him out of the “non-holiday cold.” Powerful Thanksgiving quotations, poems, Scripture verses, songs, activities, serious things to be thankful for, humourous things to be thankful for, and traditional and non-traditional Thanksgiving recipes are included in the pages of Money Under the Car Seat, along with various writings on the meaning of Thanksgiving from different perspectives.

As Whyte writes, this book is more than just another traditional celebration of the holiday Thanksgiving, rather, this book was written to “rekindle an excitement for Thanksgiving in the hearts of some who have allowed that fire to dim a little over the years, and to inspire the Thanksgiving lovers to love this holiday even more and to celebrate it better.”
Money Under the Car Seat (which is available on Amazon Kindle for just $2.99) will truly be a blessing and benefit to readers throughout the holiday season. It not only helps readers to look at the Thanksgiving holiday through fresh eyes, but it encourages them to be thankful to God every day of the year.

In 365 Days of Thanking God, Daniella Whyte challenges readers to “cultivate a heart of thankfulness to God and to the people He has placed in your life.” Daniella Whyte expresses that too many times, people forget to be thankful outside of the Thanksgiving holiday season. However in her book, which is available on Amazon Kindle for just $2.99, she reminds readers to be thankful for the simple, everyday blessings and express gratitude everyday.

In the latest book from Torch Legacy Publications, A Miraculous Thanksgiving (which is available on Amazon Kindle for only $2.99), Daniel Whyte III, along with his wife, Meriqua Whyte, tell a beautiful story of how God worked a miracle for one family around the Thanksgiving holiday.
Eight-year-old Adriena Atkinson is very concerned about Thanksgiving this year–will they have a turkey with all the trimmings and will they have her favorite Green Giant Golden Sweet Kernel Corn? Things are not looking good as there are only eight days left until Thanksgiving. But as she makes her daily countdown to Thanksgiving, she learns the true meaning of the holiday and the importance of prayer and faith in God.

In The Thanksgiving That Almost Wasn’t, Daniella Whyte, along with her six siblings, share a touching story of faith, love, hope, forgiveness, and prayer, as the Johnson family goes through a tough separation before Thanksgiving. As parents read this story to their children, families will see how God worked a miracle to bring this family back together on their favorite holiday–Thanksgiving. This book is available on Amazon Kindle for just $2.99.
Daniel Whyte III has spoken in meetings across the United States and in twenty-three foreign countries. He is the author of sixteen books. He is also the president of Gospel Light Society International, a worldwide evangelistic ministry that reaches thousands with the Gospel each week, as well as president of Torch Ministries International, a Christian literature ministry which publishes a monthly magazine called The Torch Leader. He is heard by thousands each week on his radio broadcasts, The Prayer Motivator Devotional and the Prayer Motivator Minute, as well as Gospel Light Minute X, the Gospel Light Minute, and the Sunday Evening Evangelistic Message. He holds a Bachelor’s Degree in Theology from Bethany Divinity College, a Bachelor’s degree in Religion from Texas Wesleyan University, and a Master’s degree in Religion from Liberty Baptist Theological Seminary. He has been married to the former Meriqua Althea Dixon, of Christiana, Jamaica for twenty-five years. God has blessed their union with seven children.
Daniella Whyte is the daughter of national bestselling author, Daniel Whyte III. She is the author of two books and co-author of two books. She is also a college student, studying psychology, religion, and counseling at Liberty University in Lynchburg, Virginia. She is a member of the American Association of Christian Counselors. She also assists her father in the work of the ministry. She is heard each week on her radio broadcast, YBW Focus. Her main mission in life is to tell everyone she can about the grace and love of God, and to help everyone she can be all they can be for God’s glory. Her passion is helping families and children mature and flourish in life.
