Lonnie Hunter’s New #GETITDONE Album Is Available Now

May 2, 2015 Entertainment

Lonnie Hunter and Structure will be releasing their new album entitled #GETITDONE on Saturday May 2nd for iTunes. His hit song “Forever I Will” can also be downloaded from iTunes.

Gospel singer and organist Lonnie Hunter III was born and raised in Harvey, Illinois; he was inspired to begin performing by his mother, herself a professional gospel vocalist, and after graduating high school, served five years as lead singer for the U.S. Air Force Jazz Band. In 1980, Hunter accepted the position of Minister of Music with the St. Mark Missionary Baptist Church Cathedral in Harvey; nine years later, the church’s choir took top honors in the Singsation National Gospel Choir Competition. Their debut album, Lonnie Hunter III & the Voices of St. Mark, followed on the Muscle Shoals label in 1998, with a second release, Next Level, again with the Voices of St. Mark, appearing from the imprint in 2003. It would be eight years before the next Hunter album hit shelves. I’m Back, this time on the Black Smoke Music label, was released in 2011, and featured Structure, Hunter’s own hand-picked choir.
