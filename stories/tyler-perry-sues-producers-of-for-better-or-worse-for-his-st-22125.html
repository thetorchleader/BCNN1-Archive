Tyler Perry Sues Producers of “For Better or Worse” for his Studio Fire

May 14, 2014 Entertainment

Perry blames flames on bad lighting, negligence.

Tyler Perry is suing the producers of his television series For Better or Worse following an accident that set fire to his Tyler Perry Studios in May 2012. The lawsuit asserts the fire happened after a technician put a hot light too close to a wooden façade on the set.

Perry claims the fire occured as a result of negligence on the part of the producers, who should have hired more qualified technicians to avoid such accidents. He is seeking $1.8 million in damages.
