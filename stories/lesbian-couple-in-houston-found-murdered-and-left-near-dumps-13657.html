Lesbian Couple in Houston Found Murdered and Left Near Dumpster

March 12, 2014 Uncategorized

Police are investigating the death of a Houston lesbian couple found murdered next to a dumpster in Port Bolivar, Texas, according to the Galveston County Sheriff’s Office.

A beer delivery man discovered the bodies  of Britney Cosby and Crystal Jackson, both 24, outside of the Fisherman’s Cove store while taking out some trash Friday morning.

Police officials believe the women were killed somewhere else and dumped at the store. Early examinations for the causes of death determined the women were killed in different ways. There is not enough evidence to determine the motive for the murder or if the murders were a hate crime.
