Son of Robert H. Schuller, Robert Anthony Schuller, Starts ‘The Gathering’ Church In Mexico

June 1, 2014 Christian

Robert A. Schuller and his wife Donna

It’s a long way from the Crystal Cathedral in Garden Grove, California, to a small church for ex-pats in Los Cabos, Baja California — 1,123.4 miles to be exact – but that’s the move that Dr. Robert A. Schuller and his wife, Donna have made.

And they say they couldn’t be happier.

Robert Anthony Schuller, the only son of Crystal Cathedral founders Robert H. Schuller and Arvella Schuller, was senior pastor for nearly three years — from January 2006 to November 2008 — of the famous Garden Grove church, and was seen by millions on the “Hour of Power” weekly television program broadcast from the glittering church.

He was often joined by his lovely wife, Donna, who is a regular columnist with the ASSIST News Service.

But now the couple have a new and surprising ministry called “The Gathering,” which is a small church for mainly American (and other) expats in Los Cabos, Los Cabos, a municipality located at the southern tip of Mexico’s Baja California Peninsula, in the state of Baja California Sur. It encompasses the towns of Cabo San Lucas and San José del Cabo (the municipal seat), as well as the Resort Corridor that lies between the two.

The new church, evolved after the couple left the Crystal Cathedral, says Donna.

“My husband Robert and I have discovered that there are many new and different ways to serve God,” she told ANS. “Besides traveling and speaking for the interfaith group, The Global Peace Foundation, we have two other new ministries since we left the ‘big church’ in 2008. And everything we do is under the umbrella of a church with no boundaries and with no walls.

“In November of 2012 we started a monthly telephone meeting named ‘The Call’ which meets on the 15th of each month at 6pm Pacific Time. In order to access The Call you simply need a phone. At 6pm on the 15th of each month dial: 530-881-1300 and using the code 642848# you can anonymously join with others who are looking for some mid-month encouragement and direction.

“My husband Robert and direct and lead The Call by reading scripture, praying together, sharing a brief message of hope and healing, and by taking your questions if you so desire to participate. We do this every month, no matter where we are in the world. You too can participate anyplace you find yourself on the 15th of each month.”
