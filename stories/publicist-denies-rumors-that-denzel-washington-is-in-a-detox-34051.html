Publicist Denies Rumors that Denzel Washington Is In a Detox Program

August 16, 2014 Entertainment

Rumors that Denzel Washington had a wild month of July that required him to detox from alcohol are being addressed. The detox reportedly occurred in his Orange County residence for more than two weeks.
