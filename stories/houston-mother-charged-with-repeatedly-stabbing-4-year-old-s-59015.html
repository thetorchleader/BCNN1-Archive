Houston Mother Charged with Repeatedly Stabbing 4-Year-Old Son; Boy Was Also Sexually Mutilated

February 27, 2015 National

Unidentified relatives react after learning of a mother who police say repeatedly stabbed her 4-year-old son Feb. 26, 2015, in Houston. (Photo: KHOU-TV, Houston)

A woman has been charged in connection to a brutal attack on her 4-year-old son, authorities said Thursday.

A witness called 911 to report the mother was outside striking the boy with an object. She tried unsuccessfully to intervene.

Harris County Sheriff’s deputies say the mother was still attacking the little boy when they arrived at the scene around 1:15 p.m. They tackled her and handcuffed her.

The child suffered multiple stab wounds and investigators found blood inside and outside the house. Investigators say he had also been sexually mutilated.

“The scene itself was a terrible one for deputies,” said Thomas Gilliland with the Harris County Sheriff’s Office. “These are the type of scenes that weigh very heavily on us.”

Life Flight was called to the two-story home in northwest Harris County. The boy was last reported in grave, critical condition at Memorial Hermann Hospital.

CPS workers met with police and hospital staff Thursday night. A CPS official said there’s no prior history with the family and they don’t believe any other children lived in the house.

The boy’s 23-year-old mother was taken into custody. Gilliland said she was partially clothed and was not wearing a top when they arrived. The exact charges the woman faces have not been made public yet.

He said a mental health evaluation will be performed as part of her standard booking process into the Harris County Jail.

“I’ve lived in this area for 20 years,” said Carrie Smith. “All of the neighbors are in shock that this could possibly happen on our street.”
