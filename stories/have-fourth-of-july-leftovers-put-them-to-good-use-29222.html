Have Fourth of July Leftovers? Put Them to Good Use

Date not recorded in the original archive God & Food

Baked Potato Bread (www.thefreshloaf.com)

If your Independence Day celebration has left you with some extra food and fixins’, you there are plenty of ways to continue the party with a twist on your favorite treats.

Whether you’ve enjoyed your annual barbecue bash or found yourself baking up a storm in Fourth of July revelry, here are some simple ways to use up those Fourth of July leftovers:

Fourth of July Leftovers: Mashed Potatoes
Leftover Recipe: Mashed Potato Bread
It can be hard to make use of extra mashed potatoes from that Fourth of July dinner, especially during those hot days ahead. Mix up mashed potatoes with sour cream, cooked bacon and fresh chives in this Mashed Potato Bread recipe for the perfect sandwich bread or loaf you can freeze with ease.

Fourth of July Leftovers: Hamburger Buns
Leftover Recipe: Bread Pudding
It’s easy to make up this comforting dessert with leftover hamburger (or hot dog) buns; just let the bread dry out slightly for a day and then use it in place of French bread in your favorite bread pudding recipe. Hamburger and hot dog buns soak up the egg and cinnamon mixture just as well as French or ciabatta bread, and the dessert can be served hot or cold for your next gathering.

Fourth of July Leftovers: Coleslaw
Leftover Recipe: Chicken Coleslaw Salad
As tasty as coleslaw can be, it’s a side dish that can quickly lose its appeal at the backyard barbecue or picnic. Keep this one a fresh and exciting addition to the summer table by mixing in pieces of grilled chicken, fresh vegetables and a touch of BBQ sauce and you’ll have something new to look forward to at lunch.
