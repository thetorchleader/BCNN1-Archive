Oprah Does her Favorite Thing Thing on OWN

October 13, 2012 Uncategorized

For years, Oprah Winfrey wowed viewers every year with her “Favorite Things” show, as she handed out one fabulous prize after another to lucky audience members.


Oprah Winfrey speaks in New York on April 14, 2011. (Photo: George Burns, OWN/AP)
She went out with a bang in 2010, when, in a two-part episode, she gave away items that included a hot new thing called an iPad, Ugg sequined boots and a 2012 VW beetle.
Now, Oprah has announced she has revived the franchise and will air Oprah’s Favorite Things: 2012 on Nov. 18 as a two-hour special from 8 to 10 p.m. ET on OWN.
As always, she will unveil her must-haves for the holiday season and that includes a “top-secret giveaway” for an unsuspecting group of military spouses from each branch of the Armed Forces. (So that’s why she was spotted on Thursday in downtown D.C.)
With help from her BFF and O Magazine’s editor-at-large, Gayle King, and creative director Adam Glassman, Oprah enlisted designers Michael Kors and Tory Burch and celebrity chef Curtis Stone to help find the hottest, newest, tastiest items of the year for the magazine’s O List (on stands Nov. 6).
