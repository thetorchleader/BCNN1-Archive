MiKaila Sapp, Marvin Sapp’s Daughter, Launches Nonprofit Organization to Serve Youth In Need

January 27, 2015 Christian

MiKaila Sapp has launched a nonprofit organization called “Kai’s Big Payback” to serve youth in need (Photo Credit: FACEBOOK)

GRAMMY® nominated gospel recording artist Marvin Sapp has donated both time and resources to helping those in need. His daughter, MiKaila Sapp, is following in his footsteps.

The 17-year-old has launched “Kai’s Big Payback,” a nonprofit organization to help youth in need.

MiKaila, who is both founder and acting chief executive officer wants to expose the talents of young people with “low self-esteem, poor self-image, health conditions and other challenges life brings.”

To accomplish this, Kai’s Big Payback, Inc. will sponsor events to generate funds to support causes that enhance their quality of life.

Currently, MiKaila’s influential dad is assisting his ambitious daughter in raising funds to support her charitable vision.

“Help my daughter to grant dreams to a deserving child,” the Michigan-based pastor requested in a Jan. 25 social media post, sharing a link to MiKaila’s Go Fund Me Page.
