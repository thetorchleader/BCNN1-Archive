Campus Preacher Says Immodestly Dressed Women Are Asking to Be Raped

May 8, 2014 Christian

Brother Dean Saxton (VICE)

Brother Dean Saxton is a campus preacher who caught attention last August when he preached at the University of Arizona with a sign that read, “YOU DESERVE RAPE.”

Saxton believes that immodestly dressed women are asking to be raped and are partially responsible for their rape.

Vice.com did a profile of Saxton, catching up with him as he was preparing to protest the screening of Brave Miss World, a documentary about Miss Israel Linor Abargil, who was stabbed and raped just six weeks before winning the Miss World crown in 1998.
