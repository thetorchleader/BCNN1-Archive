Man Wearing a Devilish ‘Scream’ Mask Attempts to Steal Young Girl in the Middle of the Day

March 24, 2014 What is this World Coming To?

Philadelphia police are investigating the attempted abduction of a teenage girl by a man wearing a mask made famous by a series of horror movies.

The incident happened at about 7:30 a.m. on March 21st in the 2100 block of Granite Street in the city’s Frankford section.

A 14-year-old told police she was walking to school when a male, wearing a mask, exited a vehicle, grabbed her from behind and attempted to cover her mouth.

The teen told police she was able to break free and ran to school where she reported the incident.
