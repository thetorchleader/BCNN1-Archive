Delusional Man Who Calls Himself the ‘Son of God’ Tries to Get Money from Jerry Jones by Releasing Compromising Photos of the Married Billionaire Dallas Cowboys Owner with Young Blondes

August 7, 2014 Sports

Photos allegedly showing Dallas Cowboys owner Jerry Jones in compromising poses with young women have been posted online. (AP Photo/Gus Ruelas)

The photos appear to show the married 71-year-old grabbing one woman’s breasts and having another lean her head against his groin. They appear in a 20-page manifesto ‘Uncovering the Truth,’ in which the author refers to himself as the ‘Son of God.’

Compromising pictures purportedly showing Dallas Cowboys owner Jerry Jones cavorting with two young women have been released online as part of an alleged extortion plot.

One of the photographs appears to show the married 71-year-old grabbing a blond woman’s breasts, while in the second a brunette crouches down and leans her head against his groin. The existence of the pictures was first reported by TerezOwens.com.

They appeared in a rambling 20-page manifesto – entitled “Uncovering the Truth.”

It was reportedly uploaded by a mysterious Dallas-based businessman, calling himself Frank Hoover, over the weekend.

With the title “For: Jerry Jones,” it claims that the snaps were taken by another person in a bid to blackmail the billionaire.
