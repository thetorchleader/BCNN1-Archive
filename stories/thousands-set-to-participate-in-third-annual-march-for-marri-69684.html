Thousands Set to Participate In Third Annual March for Marriage This Saturday In Washington, DC

April 24, 2015 National

Supporters can participate in Saturday’s Annual March for Marriage in Washington, DC — even if they can’t physically be there.

This year’s is the third March for Marriage, and Brian Brown, president of the National Organization for Marriage (NOM), tells OneNewsNow tens of thousands of people are expected to be on hand at the Capitol to march to the U.S. Supreme Court.

“We’re going to tell the Supreme Court, ‘Do not redefine marriage; do not launch another Roe v. Wade-type decision on this country. If you do, it will be illegitimate.’ The people of this country are not going to stand for marriage being redefined, and it’s critical that we have as many people as possible gathering before the Capitol,” Brown states.

He says it is also critical that the message be sent to the courts that marriage between one man and one woman is God’s intent for humanity, so it should be America’s.
