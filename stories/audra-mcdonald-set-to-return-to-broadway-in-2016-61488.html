Audra McDonald Set to Return to Broadway In 2016

March 13, 2015 Entertainment

Audra McDonald attends the American Theatre Wing’s 68th Annual Tony Awards at Radio City Music Hall on June 8, 2014, in New York City.
(Photo: D Dipasupil, FilmMagic)

Will Audra McDonald win her seventh Tony Award next year?

That’s the question posed by news Thursday that the celebrated singer/actress — who already holds the record for most Tonys won by an actor, with six — will return to the Broadway stage in spring of 2016. And she’ll bring a powerhouse team with her, including director George C. Wolfe, who’s also writing the book, and choreographer Savion Glover.

McDonald will star in Shuffle Along, Or, The Making of the Musical Sensation of 1921 and All That Followed, a new musical telling a story behind an old one. 1921’s Shuffle Along, featuring an all-black cast, featured music and lyrics by Eubie Blake and Noble Sissle. Against all odds, it was a success, helping to propel such names as Josephine Baker and Paul Robeson.

McDonald — who won her most recent Tony in 2014, for Lady Day at Emerson’s Bar and Grill — will play the performer Lottie Gee in the show, which is set to open April 16, 20016 at the Music Box Theatre.

SOURCE: USA Today
