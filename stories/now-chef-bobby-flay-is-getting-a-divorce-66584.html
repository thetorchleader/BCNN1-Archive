Now, Chef Bobby Flay is Getting a Divorce

April 10, 2015 Entertainment

Stephanie March and Bobby Flay

After 10 years of marriage, Food Network chef Bobby Flay has filed for divorce from “Law & Order: SVU” actress Stephanie March.

On Thursday, E! News reported that the couple had separated three weeks ago and that Flay had moved out of their New York City home.

On Friday, Flay filed for divorce.

In court documents, the celebrity chef reportedly wrote that the marriage had “broken down irretrievably.”

A source told E! News that March had “tried everything she could do to save the marriage.”

Flay, 50, and March, 40, have no children. Multiple reports suggest that the two are fighting over the prenup agreement they signed before getting married on Feb. 20, 2005.

The divorce news comes after gossip linked the workaholic restaurateur to both “Mad Men” actress January Jones and Food Network colleague Giada De Laurentiis. He denied being involved with either.

“Bobby Flay’s reputation as a Casanova didn’t escape his soon-to-be-ex wife,” writes The New York Daily News.
