LISTEN: Roland Martin on Overzealous Teachers or Out-of-Control Children

March 29, 2014 Uncategorized

Roland S. Martin

The “NewsOne Now” panel took on school discipline policies that Attorney General Eric Holder says create the “school-to-prison pipeline.” Dr. Steve Perry, who was a part of the panel Wednesday says it’s unsafe to be a black male at any age, especially in our schools, but that some students are just plain disruptive. If you’re a parent of a child in public school, you’ll definitely want to hear this debate over whether black students are over-disciplined.

Be sure to listen to “NewsOne Now” with Roland Martin, weekdays at 7 a.m. EST and watch at 9 a.m. EST on TV One.
