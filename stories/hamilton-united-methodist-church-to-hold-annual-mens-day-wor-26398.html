Hamilton United Methodist Church to Hold Annual Men’s Day Worship on Sunday

June 13, 2014 Black

Michael Lawson

Displaying their commitment to Christ, the male members of Hamilton United Methodist Church will present, ‘Men Stepping into Faith: Men of Character, Purpose and Worth,’ based on Romans 5:1-5.

The Annual Men’s Day Worship is set for Sunday, June 15 at 11 a.m., in the edifice at 6400 South Figueroa Street in Los Angeles where the Rev. Gary Bernard Williams serves as pastor.

Inviting the public to attend, Pastor Williams said, “You will be refreshed and inspired by fellowshipping with us at this spirit-filled service.  Our speaker will be Ambassador Michael Lawson, a Hamilton member and dynamic orator.  Also, the legendary vocalist, Kenneth Glover, will share songs of praise.”

Ambassador Lawson, formerly president of the Los Angeles Board of Airport Commissioners, represents the United States on the International Civil Aviation Organization based in Montreal, Canada.
