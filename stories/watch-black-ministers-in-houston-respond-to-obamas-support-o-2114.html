WATCH: Black Ministers in Houston Respond to Obama’s Support of Same-Sex Marriage

May 11, 2012 Uncategorized

Will Houston’s African-American ministers stand behind President Barack Obama’s decision to support same sex marriages? It is a President whose portrait actually hangs in the very church where I met with local religious leaders.

One of those ministers we spoke with was Leroy Joseph. He says, “As being a pastor and a Christian, it’s wrong, it’s definitely wrong. I preach it’s wrong now.”
Pastor Paul E. Giddings says, “That is a sin according to the bible we can not disagree with the fact same sex marriage that’s not the way God planned it.”
They’re referring to Leviticus 18:22. It reads: “You shall not lie with a male as one lies with a female; it is an abomination.”
If the president condones what is considered morally wrong in the bible, will these African American ministers who have supported him in the past remain by Mr. Obama’s side?
James Nash is pastor at St. Paul Missionary Baptist Church. He says, “He’s a President that’s done a lot for people who are oppressed people who have been disenfranchised. That’s what I’m looking at.”
