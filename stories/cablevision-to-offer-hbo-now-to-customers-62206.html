Cablevision to Offer HBO Now to Customers

March 18, 2015 Technology

Cablevision, the cable company that offers broadband Internet and pay-TV packages in the New York City area, said Monday it will offer HBO’s new steaming-only service, HBO Now, to its customers in the coming weeks.

In striking the deal with HBO, Cablevision, whose service is marketed under the Optimum brand, said it becomes the first cable company to announce plans to distribute the service. Cablevision didn’t disclose the deal’s terms or how much it’ll charge its customers.

As it introduced HBO Now last week at an Apple event, HBO, a subsidiary of Time Warner, said it will launch the service early next month — in time for the fifth season of Game of Thrones — initially on Apple TV, a device that allows viewers to stream video content directly without a pay-TV service. Priced at $14.99 per month, HBO Now on Apple TV would allow customers to access to all of HBO’s content, including the shows that have already aired.
