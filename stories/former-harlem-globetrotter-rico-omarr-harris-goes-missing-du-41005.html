Former Harlem Globetrotter, Rico Omarr Harris, Goes Missing During Trip from California to Seattle

October 22, 2014 Black

Rico Omar Harris
FACEBOOK

Police are asking for the public’s help in finding 37-year-old Rico Omarr Harris who went missing Oct. 10 during a planned road trip from California to Seattle to visit his longtime girlfriend. Former Harlem Globetrotter who was making the 1,100-mile trek from California to Seattle to be with his longtime girlfriend has been missing for more than a week and police are turning to the public for help, news station KTLA  reports.

37-year-old Rico Omarr Harris called his mother October 10 to tell her that he was passing through Sacramento and that he was going to grab a bite to eat and take a nap. An hour later, he called his girlfriend an hour later and left a voicemail. That was the last time anyone heard from Harris.

Harris’ black Nissan was found 3 days later 60 miles northwest of Sacramento in a remote park off State Route 16, according to KTLA. The 6-foot-9, 280-pound former Globetrotter was nowhere to be found. After running the plates, police found that the car belonged to Harris. A team searched the area near the car and found a Harris’ cell phone and a backpack with jumper cables. Deputies reportedly told the family that Harris’ car battery had died.

On Monday, Harris’ mother, Margaret Fernandez, held a press conference to ask the public for help.

“I miss my son,” a visibly upset Fernandez told reporters. “I’m very, very sad.”

According to police, Harris set out on his journey from Alhambra, California, to Seattle on Oct. 10 around 1 am. Harris’ family told the news station that Harris was in the process of moving to be with his longtime girlfriend, Jennifer Song, and that he looking forward to starting a new job in sales.
