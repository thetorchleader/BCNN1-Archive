WATCH: Al Sharpton Preaches at Charity Missionary Baptist Church in South Carolina, Praises Mayor for Firing Police Officer

April 12, 2015 Black

The mayor and police chief of North Charleston drew kind words from activist Al Sharpton at a service Sunday in a South Carolina city still grappling with racially charged fallout from a police shooting eight days ago.

The healing service came one day after the funeral for Walter Scott, 50, the unarmed black man who was fatally shot multiple times in the back by white police officer Michael Slager. Cellphone video of the April 4 shooting went viral days later, and by Tuesday Mayor Keith Summey had fired Slager — and he was charged with murder.

“He (Summey) did not do this for the black community,” Sharpton told the raucous crowd at the Charity Missionary Baptist Church. “He did this to uphold his oath of office. He said it best when he said ‘wrong is wrong.'”

Sharpton alluded to previous cases where white police officers killed unarmed black men and no charges were filed. Recent cases in Ferguson, Mo., and New York have prompted sometimes violent protests.

“It is not the job of the mayor or police chief to try a case,” Sharpton said. “It’s their job, if there is enough evidence to proceed, to charge the man and let the jury decide.”

Sharpton said that in some similar cases even video evidence was insufficient to result in charges.
