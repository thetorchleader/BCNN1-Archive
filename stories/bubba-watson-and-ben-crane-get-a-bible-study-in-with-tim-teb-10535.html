Bubba Watson and Ben Crane Get a Bible Study In with Tim Tebow

January 31, 2013 Uncategorized

Ben Crane and Bubba Watson are admitted Tim Tebow fans. On Tuesday in Phoenix, they got to spend a little time with their backup QB buddy.
Crane tweeted out a photo of him, Watson and Tebow gathered for a Bible study presumably near TPC Scottsdale, where the WM Phoenix Open will be played this weekend.
Watson actually gave Tebow a piece of his Masters win last year.
Not much more to say here other than — oh, yeah — one time, Crane and Watson, along with Rickie Fowler and Hunter Mahan, inadvertently parodied Tebow by opposite-hand throwing a football in a warehouse.
