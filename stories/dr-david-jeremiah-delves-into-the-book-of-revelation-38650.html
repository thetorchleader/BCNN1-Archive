Dr. David Jeremiah Delves Into the Book of Revelation

October 10, 2014 Books

Dr. Jeremiah, who pastors Shadow Mountain Community Church in California, was a recent guest on the “Today’s Issues” radio show to discuss “Agents of the Apocalypse.”

The purpose of the book, the pastor and author said, is to help Christians understand the mysterious New Testament book, which often scares and confuses people with its signs and symbols.

“And so I decided to do this in a little different way,” Dr. Jeremiah said in the radio interview, explaining that he wrote a chapter on the “key players” in Revelation, beginning with the apostle John, who is the book’s author.

Jeremiah, Dr. David”And then the martyrs, and the two witnesses, and the 144,000, and then the dragon, the beast from the sea, the beast from the earth, the victor, the king and the judge,” he said.
