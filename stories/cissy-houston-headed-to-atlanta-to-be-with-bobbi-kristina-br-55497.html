Cissy Houston Headed to Atlanta to Be With Bobbi Kristina Brown

February 6, 2015 Entertainment

© Shareif Ziyadat/FilmMagic
Whitney Houston’s mother flying to Atlanta to be with Bobbi Kristina Brown

Bobbi Kristina Brown‘s grandmother Cissy Houston is traveling to Georgia to join her family at Emory University Hospital in Atlanta, ET has learned. Cissy, who is 81 years old, will be there through the weekend.

A source close to the family shot down rumors that the Houston and Brown clans had gathered at the 21-year-old’s bedside to say goodbye, and denied reports in the Daily Mail that life support measures had been ended.

The family has also denied rumors of any in-fighting, and have reportedly agreed that father Bobby Brown — who turned 46 on Thursday — will be the one to make any decision regarding his daughter.

Bobbi Kristina was discovered face down in a bathtub of water on Jan. 31, just days shy of the third anniversary of the death of her mother, Whitney Houston.
