WATCH: How Shiloh Church and Ridgewood Baptist Church Became One

November 24, 2014 Christian

On November 23, 2014, Shiloh Church and Ridgewood Baptist Church (soon to be Shiloh of Orange Park) had it’s first UNITY SERVICE to help the members of each church get to know each other, pray with one another and take the Lord’s Supper (Communion) together.

To kick-off the service a short video was played to show how these two ministries began the courtship of becoming one.
