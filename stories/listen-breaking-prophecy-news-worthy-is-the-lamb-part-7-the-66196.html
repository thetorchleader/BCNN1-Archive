LISTEN: Breaking Prophecy News; Worthy is the Lamb! Part 7 (The Prophet Daniel’s Report #546 with Daniel Whyte III)

April 8, 2015 Christian, Second Coming Watch

Daniel Whyte III

PROPHECY HEADLINES: U.S. increasing involvement in Yemen fight, sends weapons to support Saudis | After victory in Tikrit, Iraq and allies turn attention to next, more difficult battle | Delegates from Middle East, Arab world convene in Israel for nuclear conference | ‘Electronic Holocaust’ against Israel falls flat | Iranian foreign minister expected to pressure Pakistan not to join coalition against rebels in Yemen

The Bible says in Luke 9:26: “For whosoever shall be ashamed of me and of my words, of him shall the Son of man be ashamed, when he shall come in his own glory, and in his Father’s, and of the holy angels.”

PROPHECY BOOTCAMP: Our topic for today is titled “Worthy is the Lamb!” (Part 7) from Dr. Ed Hindson’s book “Revelation: Unlocking the Future”.

Matthew 24:14 says, “And this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world for a witness unto all nations; and then shall the end come.” We must assume, therefore, that this process has been completed by the time we get to Revelation 4-5.

While some have suggested that the “gospel of the kingdom” is different than the gospel of the Church Age, this is highly unlikely! The New Testament emphasizes that there is but one gospel and that any other gospel is a perversion of the ‘good news.’ The task of world evangelization is part of our Lord’s Great Commission. The fact that it would take time to accomplish this task did not eliminate the sense of the imminent return of Christ that the early church expressed. Nor should it eliminate that same sense of imminency that we should experience either.
