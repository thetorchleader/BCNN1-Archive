Even King Felipe and Queen Letizia, President Obama and His Family, and Other Well-Known People Had to Give Honor to the King of kings and Lord of lords

April 6, 2015 Entertainment

From church services and feasts to costumes and candy, see how your favorite stars celebrated Easter 2015.

Queen Letizia, King Felipe, Princess Leonor, Princess Sofia, and Queen Sofia of Spain arrive at Palma Cathedral in Mallorca, Spain, on April 5, 2015.

“Obama family portrait on Easter Sunday” –White House photographer Pete Souza, who posted this photo of Malia Obama, Michelle Obama, President Barack Obama and Sasha Obama on April 5, 2015.

The reality-TV family — including Kim Kardashian West, Kourtney Kardashian, Khloe Kardashian, Kris Jenner, Kendall Jenner and Kylie Jenner — as well as their kids and significant others (we’re talking about you, Kanye West, Corey Gamble and Tyga!) attended Easter Sunday religious services in Calabasas, Calif., on April 5, 2015.
