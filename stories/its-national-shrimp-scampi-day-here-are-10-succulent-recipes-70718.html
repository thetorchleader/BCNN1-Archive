It’s National Shrimp Scampi Day! Here Are 10 Succulent Recipes

April 29, 2015 God & Food

Shrimp Scampi with Lemon Cream
Very Culinary

In the United States, when you hear the words “shrimp scampi” you immediately think of shrimp sauteed in a garlic butter sauce, right? Well, not necessarily always, although it’s fabulous that way, too! There are many great ways to make this dish, and I’m going to share a few of them with you today!

April 29 is National Shrimp Scampi Day, and I’ve decided to celebrate by putting together a collection of shrimp scampi recipes. Some are traditional, others are great new twists to the dish (hello Shrimp Scampi Quesadillas!), some a little more indulgent and some made a bit healthier. There is something for everyone here!
