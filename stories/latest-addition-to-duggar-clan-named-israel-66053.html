Latest Addition to Duggar Clan Named Israel

April 8, 2015 Entertainment

“Congrats to my sister @jillmdillard, and brother-in-law, @derickdillard, on the birth of their beautiful baby boy, Israel David Dillard! Born last night at 11:49pm! 9lbs 10oz & 23in. long!” (Instagram / @jessaseewald)

Jill Dillard, formerly Jill Duggar of the “19 Kids and Counting” clan, had her first child with husband Derick on April 6. The baby boy is named Israel. In the family’s first photo — published on People.com on Tuesday – Derick made sure everybody knows how he feels about Israel by wearing what appears to be an Israel Defense Forces T-shirt. Baby Israel wore an “Israel” onesie. Jill wore a headband that says, “Jill.”

“We are so thankful for a healthy Mom and baby,” the Duggar family wrote on their official Facebook page. “Love this sweet baby Israel! We are happy grandparents and we promise more pictures soon!”

The new parents are part of Jill Dillard’s family’s TLC reality show, “19 Kids and Counting,” which follows the life (and, in many seasons, growth) of the Christian “quiverfull” family. This season will cover Israel’s arrival in early May, according to the cable network.

The show returned in the spring following an unsuccessful campaign to cancel the program over the family’s conservative stances on issues such as gay marriage and LGBT anti-discrimination laws. In response to that campaign, family patriarch Jim Bob Duggar said that the scrutiny has only made their family stronger.

“All it has done is give us more exposure. We’ve gained 50,000 Facebook fans last week,” Jim Bob Duggar said in December.

Although it’s too early to tell whether the Dillards will adopt the Duggar family tradition of picking alliterative names for their children — Jill Dillard’s siblings, for instance, all have “J” names — Jill Dillard has said that she hopes to have a large family. Earlier in her pregnancy, Jill Dillard told People that she and Derick were “open to as many children as God will give us,” adding, “We believe children are a blessing from the Lord and a gift from him.”
