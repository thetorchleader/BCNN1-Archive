Roland Martin and TV One Are Becoming National Leaders in Black Political Opinion

October 12, 2012 Uncategorized

Roland Martin has the ratings rolling in for TV One.
Earlier this week, the network reported a 35 percent ratings growth of season four of “Washington Watch,” a Sunday morning (11am Eastern / 8am Pacific) news program hosted by the political analyst.
“Washington Watch has allowed TV One to keep our loyal and expanding viewership informed and up-to-date about key issues and current affairs on a regularly basis,” said TV One’s president, Wonya Lucas. “Roland Martin deserves much of the credit for the leap in viewership and we’re pleased to have become such an important touchstone for Black audiences during this important Presidential election year.”
Martin commented that the show is successful due to its raw nature and focus on the Black community.
“Black Americans have made it clear that they want their perspective heard, and too often mainstream Sunday shows have ignored us,” Martin said.
This Week’s Guests on “Washington Watch”:
Dr. Paul Butler, Law Professor at Georgetown University
Dr. Chris Metzler, Political Scientist, Georgetown University
Dr. Juliane Malveaux, Founder/Thought Leader, Last Word Productions, Inc.
Rodell Mollineau, President, American Bridge
Angela Rye, Executive Director/General Counsel, Congressional Black Caucus
Armstrong Williams, Political Commentator/Host, The Right Side
Sunday, October 14, 2012 at 11 AM ET / 8 AM PT on TV One.
