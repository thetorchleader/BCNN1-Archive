T. D. Jakes Announces MegaFest Will Return to Dallas, Texas, In August

January 23, 2015 Christian

Bishop T.D. Jakes announces MegaFest will return to Dallas in August 2015.
(Photo: WFAA)

It brought Oprah, Jennifer Hudson, Joel Osteen, and a long list of other stars to Dallas in 2013. On Thursday, we learned MegaFest will be back in our backyard this August.

It’s our nation’s largest multi-day family festival, and MegaFest is living up to the saying, “everything is bigger in Texas.”

“Just the name alone implies that it’s a big event,” said Dallas Convention & Visitors Bureau President and CEO Phillip Jones.

MegaFest packs faith, inspiration, music, comedy, and film into a three-day event that draws big business.

Oprah kicked off the last MegaFest at the American Airlines Center in 2013. Kerry Washington and Steve Harvey came, too.

“The world needs to see our city,” said MegaFest host Bishop T.D. Jakes at this year’s kickoff event. “Don’t you agree?” he asked before an excited crowd broke out in cheers.

Organizers are staying mum on which big names will arrive this go-around, but Bishop Jakes promises we won’t be disappointed.

“We’ve got something for every age level,” Jakes said. “We’ve got something for every interest, every type of woman [and] every type of man.”
