Pastor William Murphy Hosts Live Album Recording In Atlanta Today

May 28, 2015 Entertainment

GRAMMY-nominated singer, songwriter and pastor William Murphy will lead an unforgettable worship experience when he hosts his live album recording this Thursday, May 28, 2015 in Atlanta, GA.

The gifted songwriter and vocalist behind the Shekinah Glory praise and worship anthem “Praise Is What I Do”, and the recent hit “It’s Working” from his 2013 GRAMMY-nominated album, God Chaser, will bring alive his fifth album, The Overcomer’s Records, with a free live recording to take place at The Church at Chapel Hill in Douglasville, GA.

The live recording will kick-off Murphy’s three-day conference DEMONSTRATE, which runs May 28th – 30th in Atlanta, GA.

Doors open: 6:30 pm EST
The Church at Chapel Hill
5357 Chapel Hill Road
Douglasville, GA 30135
Live recording is FREE and open to the public!

For more details, visit demonstrate.williammurphy.org or email demonstrate@williammurphy.org!
