Christian Education Department of the AME Zion Church Sponsors 7th Annual Young, Gifted & Black Awards

April 6, 2014 Uncategorized

YOUNG GIFTED & BLACK—Above, not in order, are the honorees: Ruben Coleman, Devon Lyons, Erica Cooley, Brittany Dreher, Miles Hines, Brittny McGraw, Jada Smith, Mayor Dwan Walker and Jan Welch. (Photos by Chris Chapman)

The Christian Education Department of the Mid-Atlantic Episcopal District of the Allegheny Conference of the AMEZ Church recently held its 7th annual “Young, Gifted and Black” Awards Dinner. Bishop Louis Hunter is the presiding prelate.
