WATCH: Pastor Tony Evans Teams With Daughter, Chrystal Evans Hurst, to Release “Kingdom Woman” Book

June 6, 2013 Uncategorized

Tony Evans is teaming up with his daughter, Chrystal Evans Hurst, to release a book entitled “Kingdom Woman: Embracing Your Purpose, Power, and Possibilities” in July 2013.
The senior pastor of Dallas megachurch Oakcliff Bible Fellowship says that he and his daughter wrote the book to “challenge women to look higher than where you are to whose you are, and to be transformed by the truth of being a Kingdom Woman.”
The book encourages women to set their eyes on Christ and to pursue what God has created them to be. 
Evans says of the book, “When you operate in light of that, all of a sudden life changes – because you have a heavenly perspective, regardless of your earthly circumstances.”
Evans previously released “Kingdom Man: Every Man’s Destiny, Every Woman’s Dream” in 2012. 
“If a husband and wife don’t have a heavenly foundation, they’ll end up with a hellish relationship,” he said.
