Bill Cosby’s Comedy Show in Massachusetts Canceled

January 14, 2015 Black

Bill Cosby’s comedy tour, with dates slated through May 2, continues to soldier on, with each show facing the question: Is it going to be canceled? Or will it go on as planned?

One upcoming date has now been nixed. Officials at the Hanover Theatre in Worcester, Mass., came to an agreement with Cosby’s agent to cancel the Feb. 6 show.

Cosby has had a home in Shelburne, Mass., since the 1970s.

President and Chief Executive Officer Troy Siebels tells Telegram.com, “I do think we are relieved. It’s not a situation anybody wanted to be in, but our image in the community means a lot to us and I know that there are those who will be relieved not to see him appear here.”
