Rory McIlroy Returns to #1 Spot with Win at Bridgestone

August 3, 2014 Sports

Rory McIlroy celebrates his win at the Bridgestone Invitational.
(Photo: Sam Greenwood, Getty Images)

Rory McIlroy made sure there was no hangover after he won the Claret Jug two weeks ago.

Now he can celebrate again.

McIlroy moved to No. 1 in the official world golf rankings Sunday by winning the World Golf Championships Bridgestone Invitational on the South Course at Firestone Country Club. After steamrolling playing partner Sergio Garcia early with birdies on the first three holes to erase a three-shot deficit and take a one-shot lead when Garcia bogeyed the third, McIlroy held off the Spaniard down the stretch and surpassed Adam Scott in the rankings.

After a delay of 1 hour, 15 minutes due to storms, McIlroy, who celebrated his victory in the British Open for 48 hours and then hit the gym to begin preparing for the rest of the season, shot a 4-under-par 66 to finish at 15 under 265 and two shots clear of Garcia (71).

FINAL LEADERBOARD: WGC Bridgestone Invitational

Two weeks ago, McIlroy won the British Open at Royal Liverpool at 17 under and was clear of Garcia and Rickie Fowler by two.

Now McIlroy, 24, will try to win his third consecutive title in this week’s PGA Championship, the last major of the season.

McIlroy’s mindset was clear this week — don’t dwell on his win in the British Open, don’t become complacent, keep the pedal down.

“That’s what I’ll have to do after this win as well. I’ve just got to keep moving forward. I’ve got plenty of time at the end of the year to celebrate all these successes,” said McIlroy, who won his first WGC event and eighth PGA Tour title. “… I feel like, with the way I’m playing, there’s a few (wins) left in me this year.”
