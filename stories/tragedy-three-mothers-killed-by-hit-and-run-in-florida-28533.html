Tragedy: Three Mothers Killed in Hit-and-Run Incident in Florida

June 28, 2014 National

Jame’sia Lang, 21, was one of three women, all of them relatives and all single mothers, killed when a hit-and-run driver slammed into the women’s car in St. Petersburg, Fla. (Facebook)

Florida police have identified and issued an arrest warrant for a man who allegedly crashed into a car carrying three women, all of them related and all single mothers, causing their deaths.

Marquice Lamonz Anderson, 27, of St. Petersburg ran from the scene of Thursday’s fiery wreck and is believed to be hiding out, police said.

Grace Collier, 25, was the aunt of fellow passengers Briana Campbell, 23, and Jame’sia Lang, 21, police said.

Collier was partially ejected from the car and was airlifted to Tampa General Hospital with major burns and massive internal injuries. She died Friday after family members removed her from life support, the St. Petersburg Tribune reported.

Campbell and Lang were pronounced dead at the scene.
