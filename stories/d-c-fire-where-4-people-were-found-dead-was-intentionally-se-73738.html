D.C. Fire Where 4 People Were Found Dead Was Intentionally Set

May 16, 2015 National

Savvas and Amy Savopoulos (PHOTO CREDIT: Facebook)

A fire in which four people were found dead in a Northwest Washington home was intentional, according to District of Columbia Police Chief Cathy Lanier.

Two of the victims have been identified as Savvas Savopoulos, 46, and his wife, Amy Savopoulos, 47. Lanier said the other two victims had been tentatively identified as the couple’s 10-year-old son, Philip, and a housekeeper, Veralicia Figueroa, 57, of Silver Spring, Md.

Three of the four victims had blunt-force injuries or stab wounds, according to autopsy findings. Lanier said the injuries were sustained before the fire was set on Thursday afternoon.

Lanier said the investigation is in its early stages and evidence collection will continue for several days. There were no signs of forced entry at the home.

Police are seeking information about a Porsche belonging to the family that was found Thursday night in Prince George’s County, Md. The car had also been set on fire.

Savvas Savopolous was the president of American Iron Works, a building materials manufacturer based in Hyattsville, Md.

The family home, valued at $4.5 million, is located blocks away from Vice President Joe Biden’s official residence and from the Washington National Cathedral, which was preparing to host memorial services for the victims.

Lisa Lambert, who lives nearby but did not know the family well, said the slayings have residents frightened.

“Everyone in the neighborhood is incredibly unsettled about this,” Lambert said. “They were well-liked. It’s surprising to everyone.”

SOURCE: The Associated Press, WUSA-TV
