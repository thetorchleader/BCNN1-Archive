Listen to the Full Homegoing Service for Dr. Gardner C. Taylor

April 13, 2015 Christian

The homegoing service for Rev. Dr. Gardner Calvin Taylor took place at the Concord Baptist Church of Christ in Brooklyn, NY, on April 13, 2015. Rev. Dr. Gary V. Simpson preached the eulogy titled, “The Angel’s Final Word”.
