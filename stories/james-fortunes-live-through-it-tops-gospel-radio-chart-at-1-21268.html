James Fortune’s “Live Through It” Tops Gospel Radio Chart at #1

May 6, 2014 Entertainment

Award-winning Gospel star, songwriter, producer and nationally-syndicated radio host James Fortune continues to inspire listeners everywhere with the triumphant anthem “Live Through It,” as the hit single tops the Gospel Radio chart at #1, scoring his fifth #1 hit single.

Empowering listeners with a powerful message of achieving strength and prevailing through life struggles, to keep faith and healing with God’s love, the chart-topping single marks another #1 hit from acclaimed Gospel superstar Fortune, whose dynamic songwriting and powerful messages of faith have also made his latest album release Live Through It (FIYA World Music/Entertainment One Music), a #1 Gospel hit.

Carrying his signature style that have made him a driving force in Gospel since the start of his career, James Fortune & FIYA bring the live power of their spirited performances to fans worldwide, on their first-ever live-recorded new album. The 20-track, two-disc new album has been an immediate hit, with additional “must-hear” tracks that are all powerful performances in a diverse mix of rousing choruses and quiet melodies, from “Best Praise” and “Forever” to “We Give You Glory” (feat. Tasha Cobbs), “Light Your Way” (feat. Israel Houghton), “Let Your Power Fall” (feat. Zacardi Cortez), “Just Smile” and more.
