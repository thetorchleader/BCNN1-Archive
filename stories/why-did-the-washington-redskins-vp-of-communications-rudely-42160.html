Why Did the Washington Redskins VP of Communications Rudely Stop an ESPN Reporter from Interviewing Colt McCoy After Big Win Over Cowboys?

October 28, 2014 Sports

The Washington Redskins won a stunning overtime game over the Cowboys on Monday night, but the end of the game was marred when Redskins Senior VP of Communications Tony Wyllie forcibly stopped ESPN Deportes reporter John Sutcliffe from interviewing Washington QB Colt McCoy.

After yanking McCoy back, Wyllie pushed the QB past Sutcliffe toward the locker room, then turned and shouted back “No means no!”
