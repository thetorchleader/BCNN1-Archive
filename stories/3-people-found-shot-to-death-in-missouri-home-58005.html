3 People Found Shot to Death in Missouri Home

February 22, 2015 National

Christian County Sheriff Joey Kyle gives an update on the triple homicide near Ozark Friday night. (PHOTO CREDIT: Nathan Papes/News-Leader)

No one has been arrested in connection with a triple homicide that occurred Friday near Ozark, but deputies do have a person of interest, authorities say.

Speaking at a news conference Saturday afternoon, Green County Sheriff Joey Kyle said no one had been arrested, despite an update earlier in the day that there had been an arrest.

Kyle did not take any questions at the news conference.

Three people were found dead with gunshot wounds at a residence in Christian County south of Springfield on Friday evening, Kyle said.

Kyle said the bodies of two women and a man were discovered at the Starlight mobile home park.

He said the fourth occupant of the residence found the bodies when he returned home from work Friday evening, but the victims likely died Friday morning.
