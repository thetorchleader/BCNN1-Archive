Fairview Baptist Church in Oklahoma City to Celebrate Pastoral Anniversary of Senior Pastor John A. Reed, Jr.

March 6, 2015 Black

Fairview Baptist Church Celebrates Pastoral Anniversary of Senior Pastor John A. Reed, Jr. and Assistant Pastor D. E. Walter, Sr.

Dates & Times
Sunday, March 15, 10:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m.
Wednesday – Friday, March 18 – 20, 7:00 p.m.
Sunday, March 22, 10:00 a.m. and 3:00 p.m.

Event Description
The Fairview Church Family will observe the 52nd Anniversary of Senior Pastor Rev. Dr. John A. Reed, Jr. and 3rd Anniversary of Assistant Pastor Rev. Derrick Walter, Sr. and family during the month of March. The theme for the celebration is “What the Bible Says About Pastors,” Jeremiah 3:15.

March 15, 10:00 a.m. – Worship Celebration
March 15, 6:00 p.m. – Anniversary Musical presented by Rev. Joe Carter and the New Hope Baptist Church, Newark, New Jersey.
March 18 – 20, 7:00 p.m. – Worship Celebrations
March 22, 10:00 a.m. & 3:00 p.m. – Worship Celebrations

Details on all services are available at www.fairviewokc.com

Event Location
Fairview Missionary Baptist Church
1700 N. E. 7th Street
Oklahoma City, OK 73117
