Alveda King on Tragic Shooting Death of Michael Brown and Violent Outbreaks In St. Louis: ‘This Is Not the Time to Riot, This Is the Time to Pray’

August 12, 2014 Black

Alveda King

The niece of a civil rights icon has spoken out on the tragic shooting death of Michael Brown and violent outbreaks in a St. Louis County suburb.

Alveda King is niece of Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. and author of King Rules. She said that the shooting incident is a tragedy, but it should not be turned into violent outrage.

“In memory of my father and in honor to God, I urge nonviolence in the demand for justice of Michael’s death,” she said. “This is not the time to burn, this is the time to turn our prayers towards heaven and seek nonviolent and just solutions to what has happened to Michael. This must be done for his family, his neighbors, and our nation.”

NewsChannel 5’s Mike Bush spoke to King’s niece via Skype. To see what else she said during their interview, check out the above video.

Read her full statement to NewsChannel 5 below:

Praying to avoid yet another series of angry retaliation against injustice, King added: “Amidst reported angry protests of racial profiling, intended peaceful prayer vigils are turning violent as protestors of the youth’s death are taking to streets with torches, looting and stealing and rampaging local business establishments. This painful incident brings to mind my father Rev. A. D. King in Birmingham, Alabama in 1963 when our home was fire bombed by rabid racists. The people were angry and wanted to riot. Daddy stood on the hood of a car with a megaphone and calmed the people. This is not the time to riot. This is the time to pray. Please, protest, stand up for our rights, but do this with nonviolence!”
