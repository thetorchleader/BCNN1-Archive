Bishop Paul S. Morton Sr. to Retire at 2015 Full Gospel Baptist Church Fellowship International Conference

November 2, 2014 Christian

2015 Full Gospel Baptist Church Fellowship International Conference: #Changed
Full Gospel Baptist Church Fellowship International
Wednesday, July 15, 2015 at 12:00 PM – Friday, July 17, 2015 at 3:00 PM (EDT)
New Orleans, LA

Our 2015 International Conference is a historical time for our Presiding Bishop Founder Paul S. Morton, Sr. and the entire Full Gospel Baptist Church Fellowship, International. The conference will take place July 15-17, 2015 in New Orleans, LA.
