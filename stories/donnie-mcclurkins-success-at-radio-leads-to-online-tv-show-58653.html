Donnie McClurkin’s Success at Radio Leads to Online TV Show

February 25, 2015 Entertainment

GETTY

T&T CREATIVE produced award-winning DONNIE MCCLURKIN SHOW syndicated by OCEANIC TRADEWINDS/WESTWOOD ONE, is pulling impressive NIELSEN 25-54 ratings in NEW YORK and PHILADELPHIA.

MCLURKIN’s Syndicated Morning Show on EMMIS/NEW YORK Urban AC WBLS scored a #1 12.7 share, beating out the popular CBS Radio 1010 WINS ALL NEWS, ALL THE TIME, which landed behind them with an 11.4. At iHEARTMEDIA/PHILADELPHIA Urban AC WDAS, MCCLURKIN scored another #1 victory in DECEMBER with a 12.9 share. The Inspirational show airs 5-6a WEEKDAYS in both markets.

WBLS/NEW YORK OM SKIP DILLARD, “Pastor DONNIE MCCLURKIN has been truly a blessing to our airwaves. He is the #1 radio program in its time slot of 5-6a in the entire NEW YORK radio market. The feedback from our listeners is always positive. They are always asking for more content from him.”

iHEARTMEDIA/PHILADELPHIA Dir./Urban Programming DERRICK CORBETT, commented, “Pastor MCCLURKIN has been a big part of the WDAS morning presentation in preceding THE STEVE HARVEY MORNING SHOW for almost 7 years now. His numbers show the focus of faith and family isn’t lost with this audience, and is a testament to his positive message and the spirit of the music that’s played within that hour.”
