Reverend James H. Wall, Jr. to Celebrate 25 Years as Pastor of Shady Grove Missionary Baptist Church in Long Branch, Texas

March 6, 2015 Black

Reverend James H. Wall, Jr. will celebrate 25 years as pastor at the Shady Grove Missionary Baptist Church of Long Branch, Texas on Sunday March 15, 2015 at 10:30 a.m., commemorative celebration and at 3 p.m., silver anniversary celebration.

Rev. Wall became the 19th minister to Pastor this 132 year old church. He was installed and given the keys to the church April 8, 1990. Wall acknowledged his call to preach the Word of God, Sept. 24, 1989 and was ordained and licensed March 18, 1990 at the New Jerusalem Missionary Baptist Church of Longview. Shady Grove has been the only pastorate he has had since acknowledging his call to the Gospel Ministry.

Since arriving at the Shady Grove Missionary Baptist Church in 1990, Wall has led Shady Grove according to Matthew 28:17-20. Shady Grove under the leadership of Pastor Wall opened their Church to house over 100 individuals who were victims during the Hurricane Rita/Katrena crisis. The Church’s Mission Projects are: Haiti, Port of Houston Seafarers, Mission Carthage, Gideon’s and the Afterschool Youth Enrichment Program.

Rev. Wall served as Director of the Commission on Missions for the Texas State Missionary Baptist Convention, Inc. He serves as Dean of the East Texas Union Progressive District Association Congress of Christian Education; he serves on the Board of Directors for the Afterschool Youth Enrichment Program and on the Board of Directors of Succour Foundation of Southeast Texas (Haiti Missionary Support Group).

Shady Grove’s Religious Affiliations are: East Texas Union Progressive District Association; Texas State Missionary Baptist Convention; and the National Baptist Convention U.S.A., Inc.
