Yes, America Is Under the Judgment of God, But There Is Still Hope for Us

November 18, 2014 Christian

James Robison

Over 50 years ago God called me to be an evangelist, to proclaim the good news—the gospel. I have never ceased declaring it around the world in person and through television. Even while preaching and witnessing, God clearly revealed the importance of living as a yielded servant. First, to serve the Lord, loving Him with all my heart, and then to serve my neighbors, caring for them as I do myself and my own family. I have diligently sought to do this. I am joyfully your servant, the servant of the family of God and all those Jesus died to redeem.

“When the righteous are in authority, the people rejoice” (Prov. 29:2). And when they don’t, we all do and should mourn. No words can describe the depth of concern that I have for the church and our nation. As Jesus wept over Jerusalem, extending the glorious invitation to return to the shelter and shadow of the Almighty like chicks to the wings of a hen, I offer faithfully and continually that invitation from our Lord to every person with ears to hear. One of the ways God has impressed me to serve is by encouraging Christ’s body—each individual, unique part—to join the other parts, supplying one another according to each person’s need and all together submitted to the Head. This is the only way Christ’s body can reveal Him in all of His fullness, and the only way we can grow into the unity of the faith. We must recognize the importance of every member and seek with all our heart to be brought together in a bond of peace and commitment to the spiritual unity Christ prayed for.

We are clearly witnessing the judgment and wrath of God already revealed against all ungodliness as we see people given over to ravenous and demonic appetites and practices. The greatest horror occurs when God’s standard is discarded in idol worship and, as Romans 1:25 says, “They turned the truth of God into a lie.” Then people are given over to unnatural, vile affections. We are witnessing the most awful decisions on the part of the highest authorities in this land. There is great reason to mourn, if only with a broken heart for a nation gone mad. Hear me: God is ready and able to heal our land if we will humble ourselves, pray and seek His face, and turn from our wicked ways. The land will indeed be healed beginning with our own personal lives, then nationally.

Next year LIFE Outreach will continue offering help to everyone who needs to be lifted out of any pit of defeat, depression or unbearable debt bondage. If we return to God with our whole heart, He will return to us and make up for “the years the locusts have eaten.” In addition to media outreach through television and missions, we will launch an online presence called The Stream. This will be a free-flowing, truth-filled river of life, wisdom and divine guidance. The Stream will be fed by tributaries of wisdom from Spirit-filled individuals set in the church by God to build us up into the fullness of Christ’s stature. Too often the tributaries become reservoirs that no longer freely release the benefit and blessings of God, ultimately becoming like the Dead Sea. I am led by the Lord to seek sold-out believers and church leaders to release transforming truth and wisdom that comes only from above.

We still desperately need what only divine providence can give. His truth is marching on, even now flowing from the very throne of God into tributaries that joyfully receive it. When they receive it, the result is fruitfulness. Life springs up! The bountiful blessings of Almighty God become apparent. We will be giving you more information concerning The Stream in the next few weeks as we prepare for a January launch.

I am asking you to please meditate on these verses from Philippians 2:1-4 (NAS): “Therefore if there is any encouragement in Christ, if there is any consolation of love, if there is any fellowship of the Spirit, if any affection and compassion, make my joy complete by being of the same mind, maintaining the same love, united in spirit, intent on one purpose. Do nothing from selfishness or empty conceit, but with humility of mind regard one another as more important than yourselves; do not merely look out for your own personal interests, but also for the interests of others.”
