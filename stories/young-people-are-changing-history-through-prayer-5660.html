Young People Are Changing History Through Prayer

May 5, 2012 Uncategorized

Prominent Christian leader and TheCall co-fouder Lou Engle says now, more than ever, Americans need to unite in prayer for their nation.


And he believes today’s young people are committed and ready for the assignment.
“We’re in a prayer, worship, youth revolution all over the globe right now.  It’s stunning,” Engle told CBN News.
He talked more about the state of America and the world today, and how young people are responding. Engle says prayer can change the course of history, and younger generations are proving that.

Click play to watch all of Engle’s comments on how young people are taking on the Great Commission.  He also talks about his new book A House that Contends.
