Kelly Rowland Expecting First Child With New Husband, Tim Witherspoon

June 11, 2014 Entertainment

Source: http://blogs.fusionradio.fm/

Kelly Rowland is pregnant with her first child! The singer took to her Instagram on June 10 to share the happy news with her friends and fans, posting an adorable picture of an infant pair of Air Jordans — so sweet!

It’s been quite a year for Kelly! Just one month after the singer and her new hubby, Tim, tied the knot in an intimate ceremony in Costa Rica on May 9, Kelly, 33, announced that they’re expecting a little bundle of joy!

Kelly shared the news in the cutest way, posting a pic of Tim’s Air Jordans next to a pair of baby Airs, alongside the caption: “I’ll be stuntin like my daddy…” (The line is actually a reference to the Birdman and Lil Wayne song “Stuntin’ Like My Daddy.”)

We can’t wait to meet Kelly and Tim’s little #1 stunna — congrats to the happy couple!
