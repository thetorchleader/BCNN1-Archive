Nick Gordon Being Investigated in Drowning of Bobbi Kristina; Allegedly ‘Cleaned Up Blood’ Before Police Arrived

February 7, 2015 National

As the updates about Bobbi Kristina Brown’s condition are sought and reported on by the hour, so are the updates about Nick Gordon and any role he may have played in Bobbi Kristina ending up face down in the bathtub, reports TMZ.

Sources tell the publication that not only has Nick Gordon gotten lawyers to protect him, he is also under investigation in the Bobbi Kristina drowning tragedy. TMZ couldn’t reach Nick for comment, but the website states that cops have officially opened a criminal investigation of Gordon. Bobbi Kristina Brown had boasted on Twitter was Gordon was her husband, despite later reports which stated that the 21-year old Bobbi Kristina and Nick Gordon — a man who was taken under Whitney Houston’s wing when he was only 12-years-old — were never officially married.

The criminal investigation against Nick Gordon is due to injuries found on Bobbi Kristina Brown, says Us Magazine‎. Emerging reports also that state Gordon may have cleaned up blood and other matter around the couple’s home prior to police arriving and after Nick reportedly administered CPR to Bobbi Kristina once she was discovered face down in the bathtub. With all the eerie similarities of the drowning of Bobbi Kristina and Whitney Houston’s death, the mysterious circumstances are being looked into further to get clear answers as to what actually happened to Bobbi Kristina.
