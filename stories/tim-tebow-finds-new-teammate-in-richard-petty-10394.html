Tim Tebow Finds New Teammate In Richard Petty

January 26, 2013 Uncategorized

It’s easy to bust on poor Tim Tebow these days.
He is essentially an NFL orphan, looking for a team still willing to buy into the concept that he can have success as an NFL quarterback beyond the gig with the Denver Broncos.
But through it all, he remains a good guy. Witness his recent charitable act _  He has hired NASCAR icon Richard Petty and his crew to restore one of his cars for charity.
Tebow didn’t have much use for a 1995 Ford Thunderbird after he was selected in the first round of the 2010 NFL Draft by the Broncos, so he left it at his parents’ house in Florida. Nobody drove it for three years.
Instead of just ditching the car, Tebow decided to turn it over to his foundation for a fund-raiser,  which then turned to Petty and his famous restoration shop in Level Cross, N.C. to get it souped up properly.
The Sporting News reported that the restored car likely will be unveiled at Daytona International Speedway during Speedweeks in February, and then shown at various Tebow foundation events before it goes to auction at the end of this year. It could draw well into six figures at a car auction such as Barrett-Jackson.
But first, it’s needs fixin’ up.
