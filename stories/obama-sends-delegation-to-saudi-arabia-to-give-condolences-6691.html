Obama Sends Delegation to Saudi Arabia to Give Condolences

June 20, 2012 Uncategorized

President Obama sent a high-level delegation to Saudi Arabia today to offer condolences for the death of Crown Prince Nayif bin Abd al-Aziz Al Saud.

Leon Panetta is greeted by Khaled Al-Aabed, head of royal protocol during his arrival at King Abdul Aziz International Airport in Jeddah Saudi Arabia.
Defense Secretary Leon Panetta led the delegation that included some of the biggest names in U.S. foreign policy and counterterrorism.
Among them: White House adviser John Brennan and FBI Director Robert Mueller.
It is also a bipartisan delegation, including George W. Bush counterterrorism adviser Frances Fargo Townsend.
In a statement, Panetta said he was “deeply saddened” by the crown prince’s death last week.
