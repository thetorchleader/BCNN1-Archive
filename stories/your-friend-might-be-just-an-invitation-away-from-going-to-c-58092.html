Your Friend Might Be Just an Invitation Away from Going to Church

February 22, 2015 Christian

If you’ve been thinking about inviting a friend or family member to church, March 1 might be the perfect day to do that.

More than 3,000 churches across the nation are expected to participate in “Friend Sunday,” an initiative developed by Outreach, Inc. Outreach founder and CEO Scott Evans says that surveys show many people who attend church come because of an invitation from a friend. Yet, in the last year, only two percent of church members invited someone to come with them.

“I think people are afraid sometimes to make the invitation,” he says. “I think many times churches don’t do a good job of reminding their members to invite. So I think ‘Friend Sunday’ just provides that single day of focus once a year where churches say, We’re going to create a special day just for you to reach out to your friends and family.”
