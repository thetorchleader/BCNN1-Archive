Christians Urged to Stand With “Houston Five” Pastors on ‘I Stand Sunday’

October 31, 2014 Christian

An organization of church pastors is encouraging Christians to stand with the five Houston pastors who were subpoenaed for sermons that opposed homosexuality and a controversial city ordinance.

Sam Rohrer, who heads the American Pastors Network, is urging pastors to stand with their Houston counterparts, known as the “Houston Five.”

How? “By preaching from the pulpit what’s happening in America – that these kinds of things are not distant, they are occurring,” he tells OneNewsNow.

Houston Mayor Annise Parker publicly stated October 29 that the subpoenas have been withdrawn, a decision that was made amid public outcry and just days before a Nov. 2 rally is planned at Grace Community Church in Houston.

Grace pastor Steve Riggle is one of the subpoenaed pastors and is scheduled to speak at “I Stand Sunday” along with Mike Huckabee, “Duck Dynasty’s Phil Robertson, and many others.

Riggle and the other pastors supported a citizen-driven referendum to overturn a “non-discrimination” ordinance pushed by Parker, an open lesbian, and homosexual city council members. The “bathroom bill” was opposed by many in the Houston community, including area pastors.

A successful petition drive gathered more than 50,000 signatures, in less than 30 days, to put the issue on the ballot. But the city attorney for Houston rejected the petitions, alleging they failed a legal test, and a group of citizens – none of them the five pastors – have sued city government to allow the referendum.
