H. B. Charles Jr. Asks for 31 Days of Prayer Before Launch of Shiloh Church Orange Park

December 2, 2014 Christian

We believe it happens after prayer!

So as we approach the launch of Shiloh Church of Orange Park, we commit to 31 days of intercessory prayer for this new work. Each member of the Shiloh Church is asked to send time daily during the 31 days of December to cover Shiloh OP in believing prayer. This daily guide will enable us to pray together on one accord.

  • 12/1 – On-Mission: Pray that Shiloh OP will make more and better disciples for Jesus Christ in the community and through the nations (Matt. 28:18-20).
  • 12/2 – Believing Prayer: Pray our devotion to and dependence upon the Lord will be continually expressed through believing prayer.
  • 12/3 – Pastor-Teacher: Pray for H.B. Charles, Jr. and family. (Hebrews 13:17-18)
  • 12/4 – Shiloh OP Fund: Pray that we will reach our financial goal for launch-related expenses for Shiloh OP. (2 Cor. 9:6-8).
  • 12/5 – Connect: Pray that the Lord will use Shiloh OP to connect lost people to Christ and the church. (John 4:23-24).
  • 12/6 – Grow: Pray that the Lord will use Shiloh OP to grow our members to spiritual maturity in Christ (2 Peter 3:18).
  • 12/7 – Serve: Pray that the Lord will use Shiloh OP to serve one another, our community, and the nations (Mark 10:45).
  • 12/8 – Staff: Pray for all current and future staffs at both locations.
  • 12/9 – Pastor of Youth & Young Adults: Pray for Cameron Triggs and family as he leads the Youth & Young Adult ministries at both locations (Hebrews 13:17-18)
  • 12/10 – Children’s Ministry: Pray for the development of ministry to children and families at Shiloh OP (Luke 2:52).
  • 12/11 – Spirit & Truth: Pray that the worship services at Shiloh OP will glorify God in spirit and truth (John 4:23-24).
  • 12/12 – Financial Stewardship: Pray that we will be a fully-resourced church and faithful stewards of all the financial and material ministry resources the Lord has entrusted to us (Luke 16:10-13).
  • 12/13 – Volunteers: Pray for faithful, available, and teaching volunteers to serve in Shiloh OP (1 Peter 2:9-10).
  • 12/14 – Spiritual Wisdom: Pray that our pastors will be filled with the knowledge of God’s will as they lead the launch of Shiloh OP (Col. 1:9-11).
  • 12/15 – JAX Pastor of Christian Education: Pray for Michael Clifford and family as he transitions to his new role downtown. (Hebrews 13:17-18)
  • 12/16 – First Class Learning Center: Pray for the growth and success of the VPK and day care center at Shiloh OP. (Matt. 19:14).
  • 12/17 – Spiritual Awakening: Pray that many lost people will be won to saving-faith in Jesus Christ through Shiloh OP.
  • 12/18 – Shiloh OP Campus: Pray that we will be good stewards of the new ministry campus the Lord has provided.
  • 12/19 – Mercy Ministry: Pray for strategic opportunities to serve the poor and needy in Orange Park (Matt. 5:13).
  • 12/20 – Faithful Preaching: Pray that our pastors at Shiloh OP will faithfully preach the Word of God. (2 Timothy 2:15)
  • 12/21 – OP Campus Pastor: Pray for Daniel Beckwith and family as he leads Shiloh OP. (Hebrews 13:17-18)
  • 12/22 – Biblical Evangelism – Pray that the Lord will use us to share the gospel faithfully, clearly, and boldly throughout Orange Park (Romans 1:16).
  • 12/23 – Local Schools: Pray for gospel outreach at Ridgeview Elementary, Lakeside Junior High, Orange Park High School, and Ridgeview High School.
  • 12/24 – Worship & Arts: Pray for Joe Pace and family as he gives leadership to our worship services at both locations (Hebrews 13:17-18).
  • 12/25 – Jesus Christ: Pray that the good news of the Lord Jesus Christ will be faithfully proclaimed through Shiloh OP.
  • 12/26 – OP Pastor of Christian Education: Pray for Donald Lewis and family as he transitions to his new role in Orange Park (Hebrews 13:17-18).
  • 12/27 – Partnership in the Gospel: Pray that Shiloh OP will be a friend and neighbor to the Christian churches and ministry in Orange Park (Philippians 1:3-5).
  • 12/28 – Shiloh OP Leaders: Pray for all the servant-leaders who will give spiritual leadership at Shiloh OP. (Proverbs 11:14)
  • 12/29 – Ridgewood Transitions – Pray for Ridgewood B.C. staff, leaders, and members who are transitioning to other churches (Proverbs 3:5-6).
  • 12/30 – Ridgewood Baptist Church: Pray for the members of Ridgewood as they conclude their ministry as a congregation (Lam. 3:22-23).
  • 12/31 – Spiritual Unity: Pray for spiritual unity, as we become one church in two locations (Eph. 4:1-6).

H.B. CHARLES, JR. Is the Pastor-Teacher at the Shiloh Metropolitan Baptist Church of Jacksonville, Florida, where he has served since the fall of 2008. He is primarily responsible for preaching-teaching, vision casting, and leadership development. Prior to coming to Shiloh, he led the Mt. Sinai Missionary Baptist Church of Los Angeles for almost eighteen years. Succeeding his late father, he began his pastorate at Mt. Sinai at the age of seventeen.H.B. Charles regularly speaks at churches, conferences, and conventions around the country. He has contributed to several books and journals, and is the author of It Happens After Prayer. H.B. and his wife Crystal have three children: H.B. III, Natalie, and Hailey. Keep in touch with HB Charles at HBCharlesJr.com.
