Aretha Franklin Loses Weight Through Brisk Walks at Wal-Mart and K-Mart

July 25, 2014 Entertainment

Aretha Franklin

Aretha Franklin told a Metro Detroit TV station Wednesday she’s determined to never be “grossly overweight” again.

The 72-year-old “Queen of Soul” who grew up in Detroit told WDIV-TV Channel 4 she’s not afraid to work out in public and visits stores weekly to get brisk walks in.

Franklin’s favorite spots: Walmart, Kmart and Meijer stores. She didn’t disclose her current weight in the interview, but said she’s in much better shape now.

“The weight that I am at now, I said, ‘I am going to own this weight,’” Franklin said. “I will never, ever, be the size that I was again.”
