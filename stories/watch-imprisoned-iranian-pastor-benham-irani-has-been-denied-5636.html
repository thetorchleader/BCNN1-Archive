WATCH: Imprisoned Iranian Pastor Benham Irani Has Been Denied Medical Help

August 1, 2012 Uncategorized

An Iranian pastor imprisoned for his faith is being denied medical attention for his deteriorating health.

Several sources report Benham Irani is suffering from severe stomach ulcers and colon complications and he may have only months or even weeks to live.
Nevertheless, prison officials have refused to let him see a doctor. According to media sources, frequent beatings by prison guards and cell mates have inflicted injuries to his legs and eyes. In mid-July, he was unconscious for three days.
“As a result of injuries sustained during these assaults, he has difficulty walking,” Christian Solidarity Worldwide reported. “His health has further deteriorated due to the harsh prison conditions; he has suffered severe bleeding as a result of ulcers and complications with his colon, which led to his recent loss of consciousness.”
