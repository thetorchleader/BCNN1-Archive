Bishop Joseph Mattera says Get Ready for the New Normal – 8 Trends the Church Needs to Prepare For

January 12, 2013 Uncategorized

With the United States tilting towards the left, the church must prepare for a new normal in our lives and ministries. The body of Christ has to realize it is no longer business as usual.

Every conservative and liberal pundit I am reading say virtually the same thing: During the last election, our nation has experienced a dramatic cultural shift similar to the 1960s! (Check out the CNN online opinion article 2013: America on the Cusp of Social Change.)
Unless the Body of Christ is able to bring about a genuine countercultural shift, the following are some of the possible projections we will experience in the next decade.
Increased Limitations on Freedom of Religion

The present move towards secularism in our nation will produce a climate of hostility towards freedom of speech in both the religious and public sectors. Many cultural gatekeepers will attempt to censor what is preached from the pulpit and on the airwaves. This will be done under the name of hate speech that can (supposedly) incite bullying and domestic terrorism.
The result? A ban against exposition of Bible passages from pulpits and on the airwaves in which God condemns certain sexual practices and lifestyles (Leviticus 18; Romans 1) that our secular society not only tolerates but also celebrates. Can’t happen in America? Consider that in our neighboring Canada, preaching against certain sexual lifestyles is legally censored in local churches and over the airwaves.
More Scrutiny of Religious Leaders and Institutions
Congress and the IRS will continue to closely watch religious leaders and institutions they deem countercultural to the kind of neutered, syncretistic humanistic society they desire.
As Karl Marx deemed religion “the opium of the people” that should be eradicated, those that tend towards socialism deem traditional nuclear families and activist Bible-believing churches a threat to their desire for the secular state to regulate and rule every aspect of culture, human life and society.
Since the beginning of time there has always been a war between God and autonomous men who try to build their own towers and empires that reach to heaven so they can control their own destiny (Genesis 11:1-8; Isaiah 14:12-14; Daniel chapters 2, 4, 7; Obadiah vv. 2-3; Psalm 2).
Decreased Tax-Exempt Status for Religious Leaders, Churches and Ministries
Today, New York City churches must fill out a form every year just to maintain their tax-exempt status (as required by the NYC Department of Finance). Churches that somehow didn’t receive the application in the mail or who failed to fill it out in time must start the process of receiving tax-exempt status all over again.
I am sure hundreds or even thousands of churches miss the deadline every year; filling out this application gives the city more up-to-date information on every single church and is probably the first step towards gradually taking tax exemption status away from as many houses of worship as possible.
One person from the Department of Finance visited our church three times last year to investigate our tax exempt status. DOF demanded to know how our building was used every day, what we do with our property and if other people or organizations are renting our facilities. We were able to prove that we use our facilities every day for church non-profit use. You’ll see more of this activity approved by Congress and local municipalities in order to collect more tax monies to make up for the huge budget deficit.
