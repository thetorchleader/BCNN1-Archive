LISTEN: On the “The Roland Martin Show”, Ray Baker Asks Whether the Moment Has Passed In Ferguson?

March 5, 2015 Black

Ray Baker sits in for Roland Martin for this edition of “The Roland Martin Show” on the Empowerment Radio Network.

Law Professor at Saint Louis University and Human Rights Activist Justin Hansford talks Ferguson, the Ferguson Commission meetings and building a sustainable movement.

Congressional Black Caucus Chair, Congressman G.K. Butterfiled joined Baker on “The Roland Martin Show” to discuss the most important issues facing Black America today.

Richard Prince discusses the life and legacy of Dori Maynard.

Activist and minister Nyle Fort breaks down the role of the church in modern activism.

Phillip Agnew of the Dream Defenders discusses the 3rd anniversary of the murder of Trayvon Martin.

GenYNot’s Jamira Burley joins the conversation to discuss millennials and activism.
