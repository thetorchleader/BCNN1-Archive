Keke Palmer Lands Lead Role In ‘Scream Queens’

January 20, 2015 Entertainment

Our January cover star, Keke Palmer, stays on the grind. Just days after ending her historic run as Cinderella on Broadway, Palmer has landed a role on Fox’s upcoming series, Scream Queens.

Scream Queens is a new comedy-horror anthology series created by Glee’s Ryan Murphy, according to Hello Beautiful. Palmer will play the lead alongside a star-studded cast including Academy Award-nominee Abigail Breslin, Glee’s Lea Michelle, Joe Manganiello and Ariana Grande.

The first season will feature 15 hour-long episodes centering around a series of murders on a college campus. No word yet on Palmer’s specific role in the show which begins production this spring.
