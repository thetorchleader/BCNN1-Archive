Bernanke says the Economy Is Getting Worse

July 18, 2012 Uncategorized

U.S. stock index futures pointed to a lower open on Wednesday, after Federal Reserve Chairman Ben Bernanke disappointed investors on Tuesday by offering no clear indication of further stimulus measures in his semi-annual report to Congress.

U.S. stocks fell initially after Bernanke’s speech to the Senate Banking Committee on Wednesday, but closed higher on better-than-expected earnings from Goldman Sachs, [GS  97.98     0.30  (+0.31%)   ]Citigroup [C  27.38    0.57  (+2.13%)   ] and Coca-Cola.[CCE  26.96     -0.29  (-1.06%)   ]
European shares [.FTEU3  1043.91     2.38  (+0.23%)   ] inched up in early trade on Wednesday, boosted by U.S. earnings and by strong results from European companies including Nordic bank Nordea.
U.S. companies reporting during Wednesday’s trading session include Dow component Bank of America, [BAC  7.92     0.11  (+1.41%)   ] as well as Blackrock,[BLK  176.15     0.86  (+0.49%)   ] Bank of NY Mellon, Northern Trust [NTRS  47.07     0.04  (+0.09%)   ] and US Bancorp.[TBBK  9.54     0.24  (+2.58%)   ]
Also reporting is Abbott Laboratories.[ABT  66.46     0.98  (+1.5%)   ]
Dow components American Express [AXP  58.68     0.04  (+0.07%)   ] and IBM [IBM  183.65     -1.14  (-0.62%)   ] will report after the U.S. closing bell.
The Federal Reserve will be in focus again on Wednesday as Bernanke gives his monetary policy report to the Financial Services Committee.
