Convicted Sex Offender Arrested for Wal-Mart Toe Sucking Incident

March 22, 2014 What is this World Coming To?

LINCOLNTON POLICE DEPARTMENT
Cops say this man posed as a podiatry student before sucking a woman’s toes in the shoe department of a North Carolina Walmart.

They got their arch nemesis. Police said Michael A. Brown was the foot fetishist who allegedly persuaded a woman in Lincolnton, N.C., to try on several shoes before making his move. Cops said he’d weirded out a woman at a different Walmart earlier in the day.

Attention Walmart shoppers: this guy sucks!

A foot freak posing as a podiatry student in a North Carolina Walmart sucked on a shopper’s toes before hoofing it out of there, cops said.

Hours after police released a plea for the public to stay on its toes, a suspect and registered sex offender, Michael A. Brown, was arrested, WSOC in Charlotte reported.

Surveillance video captured the man entering the big box store in Lincolnton on Monday at 11:15 a.m., police said.

He then struck up a conversation with the victim, telling her he was studying to become a foot specialist.

He persuaded the shopper to swing by the shoe department and try on several pairs, cops said.

The game was afoot.
