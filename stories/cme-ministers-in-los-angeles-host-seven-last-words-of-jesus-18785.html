CME Ministers in Los Angeles Host ‘Seven Last Words of Jesus Christ’

April 20, 2014 African-American

Bishop James B. Walker

Seven dynamic preachers will deliver the ‘Seven Last Words of Jesus Christ’ during a special worship presented by the Southern California Ministerial Alliance of the C.M.E. Church.

The service is set for Friday, April 18, at 12 p.m. at Hays Tabernacle C.M.E. Church, 10121 South Central Avenue in Los Angeles, said the Rev. Joseph C. Gardner, Sr., Alliance president.  The host pastor is Presiding Elder Julio Andujo.

“We encourage the community-at-large to join us for a powerful, uplifting worship experience featuring our presiding prelate, Bishop James B. Walker, preaching the fourth word,” said President Gardner.
