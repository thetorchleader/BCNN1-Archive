WATCH: Trailer for “Preachers of LA” Season Two

June 26, 2014 Entertainment

 

Ministry and family!

Take a front pew on the return of Oxygen’s “Preachers of LA” premiering Wednesday, August 20 at 10:00PM ET/PT.

This successful docu-series made history with its freshman series averaging over one million viewers. Now they’re back to give viewers what they’ve asked for: MORE!

Yes, the people want more of the returning cast: Bishop Noel Jones, Minister Deitrick Haddon, Bishop Clarence McClendon, Pastor Jay Haizlip, Pastor Wayne Chaney and Bishop Ron Gibson. The audience also wanted more of the incredible women of strength beside them. Filled with self-discovery, change, growing families and ministry, season two is certainly going to deliver what got the viewers talking during debut.

Hot topics like Bishop Jones & Loretta’s relationship, Deitrick & Domonique being accused of deceit by a fellow Pastor and a life altering decision shakes the Chaney’s after Myesha sheds 40 pounds. This fiery season is guaranteed to keep you in anticipation!
