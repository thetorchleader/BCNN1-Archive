Pakistani Minister Offers Bounty for Death of Anti-Islam Film Maker

September 23, 2012 Uncategorized


Pakistani Railway Minister Ghulam Ahmed Bilour said at a news conference in Peshawar: “I invite the Taliban brothers and the al-Qaeda brothers that they should join me in this sacred mission. Along with others, they should also join in the good work. And God willing, whoever is successful (in killing him) I will present one lac dollars (100,000 U.S. dollars) to him.”


Pakistani Railway Minister Ghulam Ahmad Bilour says he’d give $100,000 to the person who kills the anti-Islam filmmaker.
Mr Bilour urged the Taliban and al Qaeda to hunt down the producer of the film.
A spokesman for Pakistan’s prime minister said the government disassociated itself from the minister’s statement.
Fifteen people were killed during protests on Friday in several Pakistani cities over the amateurish video, which enraged Muslims in several countries. It depicts the Prophet Mohammad as a womaniser and a fool.
Many Western and Muslim politicians and clerics have appealed for calm, denouncing those behind the mockery of the Prophet, but also condemning violent reactions to it.
