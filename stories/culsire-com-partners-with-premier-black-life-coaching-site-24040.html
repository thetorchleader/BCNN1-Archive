CulSire.com Partners With Premier Black Life Coaching Site

May 27, 2014 African-American

CulSire.com announced today its new collaboration with BlackLifeCoaches.net, the number one life coaching and personal development resource for the black community. This will help bring CulSire.com into a position where they can continue to expand comprehensive content from established professionals to help create well-balanced lives in the Black community.Today’s announcement highlights the Black Life Coaches Network as the newest contributors to CulSire. Black Life Coaches will offer articles on career and business advice.

“Teaming up with the Black Life Coaches Network, to engage the burgeoning Black community, is very exciting,” said LaNée Javet, CEO of CulSire, the ultimate online resource for Black culture and exchange. “With the introduction of career and business advice content from BlackLifeCoaches.net, together we can help create well-balanced lives for our people. I see this as an exciting development for our community,” said LaNée Javet.

This partnership allows the Black Life Coaches Network to continue to expand the benefits of life coaching within another online forum that focuses on people of color. While, allowing CulSire.com to distribute amazing content on how to live an extraordinary life. “Within the Black Life Coaches Network, we have career and business experts that will provide articles and professional support,” said Dr. Towanna Freeman, creator of Black Life Coaches Network regarding the mission to partner with Culsire.com.

The collaboration reflects the desire of two companies working towards the same common goal of motivating Black people. “I believe now is the time for companies like Black Life Coaches and CulSire to come together in a partnership to expand our efforts to educate, elevate, and empower the Black community,” said Dr. Towanna.

About CulSire.com
CulSire.com is the definitive lifestyle destination that promotes Black culture through comprehensive content that informs, entertains, and empower. CulSire provides relevant insight to the Black community as a whole from community, to business, to history, to education, to beauty, to finance and more. CulSire.com is The Voice in The Black Community, providing a collision between our cultural distinction and our desire for continuous excellence. For more information, visit www.CulSire.com

About BlackLifeCoaches.Net
Black Life Coaches Network was created and founded by Dr. Towanna Freeman in 2012, offering an online platform where accredited life coaches, coach-clinicians, trainers, and educators from a variety of professional backgrounds can come together with a common goal of changing Blacks lives for the better as well as advancing and strengthening the coaching profession, making it more visible to the Black community and helping functional people live extraordinary lives. For more information, visit www.BlackLifeCoaches.net
