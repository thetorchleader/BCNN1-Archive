Brandon Heath, Mandisa and Laura Story Team Up for '3 In 1 Tour'

December 27, 2012 Uncategorized

Food for the Hungry presents the ‘3 In 1 Tour’ featuring Brandon Heath, Mandisa and Laura Story. The ‘3 In 1 Tour’ stands for the three unique artists and one amazing live experience.
The tour will kick off at the end of February 2013 and will run throughout April 2013.
Mandisa, “Just saw the list of locations for the “3 in 1 Tour” with Brandon Heath, Laura Story, and I next spring! 28 concerts in 16 states (some are still being confirmed, so if you don’t see your state, stay tuned…): FL, SC, NC, MS, AR, GA, LA, WV, PA, NJ, NY, OH, IA, TX, IN & MO.”
