Silicon Valley Investor Charles Hudson Launches “The Searchlights Project” to Highlight Blacks in the Tech Industry

October 18, 2014 Black

Charles Hudson

As one of the few African American investors in Silicon Valley, I am often mistaken for someone else.

In my 18 years here, I have been confused with everyone from Google’s David Drummond to MC Hammer.

These sad — and sometimes comical — cases of mistaken identity were happening so frequently that I created a Pinterest board called “I Am Not This Guy”.

I realized that a lot of people in my network simply did not know many African Americans in technology.

That’s an even bigger deal now that the industry’s lack of diversity has become such a pressing issue in Silicon Valley.

I get one consistent request from entrepreneurs, executives and other investors: They all want to know how to find African American executives and entrepreneurs who can make a real impact on their companies. And they are honest enough to admit that these folks simply aren’t in their network.

That series of conversations led to my desire to co-found a project that would shine a bright light on African Americans doing great work but who may be tougher to find because they do not have a strong social media following or presence.

The result is The Searchlights Project which my friend Denmark West and I created.

Our goal with this series of interviews with African Americans in high tech is to give the next generation of African Americans more role models and real road maps for their lives and careers. We want people to know that it is possible to make it in an industry that is so notoriously insular.

We are not the first to undertake the effort to highlight the achievements of African Americans in high tech.

Yet, while some people have created lists of important or influential African Americans in technology, many of those lists are incomplete in two ways.

First, they tend to focus on people who already have a strong public profile.

Second, they do not give a sense of the individual’s personal journey and career path in technology.
