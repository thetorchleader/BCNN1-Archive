Lachlan Murdoch Named Co-Chairman of News Corp and 21st Century Fox

March 26, 2014 Media

Lachlan Murdoch, Rupert Murdoch and James Murdoch attend the Television Academy’s 23rd Hall Of Fame Induction Gala at Regent Beverly Wilshire Hotel on March 11 in Beverly Hills, Calif. Getty Images

News Corp NWSA -0.63% and 21st Century Fox Inc. FOXA +0.74% on Wednesday named Lachlan Murdoch as nonexecutive co-chairman, marking his re-emergence as a force at the businesses nine years after he resigned his executive positions at the old News Corp.Mr. Murdoch, 42, will share the role of chairman of both companies with his father, Rupert, currently chairman and chief executive of 21st Century Fox and executive chairman of News Corp.

“Lachlan is a strategic and talented executive with a rich knowledge of our businesses,”Rupert Murdoch said in a statement.

“I have no doubt that Lachlan’s strategic sense and his passion for media and for knowledge will greatly assist Robert [Thomson, News Corp chief executive] and me as we lead the company over the coming decade,” Rupert Murdoch said in a memo to News Corp staff on Wednesday.

The Wall Street Journal is owned by News Corp, which was a part of the same company as 21st Century Fox until June 2013.

21st Century Fox also said Wednesday that Lachlan Murdoch’s brother, James, 41, has been appointed co-chief operating officer of the company. He was previously 21st Century Fox’s deputy chief operating officer and chairman and CEO, international.

James Murdoch was seen by many as his father’s natural successor after Lachlan left his executive position at News Corp. in 2005 to begin a career in private equity. James Murdoch was promoted to deputy chief operating officer of the old News Corp. in 2011, a move that established him as the third-most senior executive at the company. He had previously overseen the company’s European and Asian operations from London.
