Rev. Alexander Hamilton to Be Honored for Trailblazing Work with Gospel Artists

May 31, 2015 Black

Rev. Alexander Hamilton

The faith community will gather on Sunday, May 31, at 5 p.m., to salute the pioneering ministry of the Rev. Alexander Hamilton, a quiet trailblazer who helped many gospel artists cross over into commercial success.

The tribute will be held at Faithful Central Bible Church (FCBC) in the Living Room, located at 400 West Florence Avenue in Inglewood. Bishop Kenneth C. Ulmer, pastor of FCBC, and Quaford Coleman will host the benefit service and the Rev. Xavier L. Thompson and the Baptist Minister’s Conference of Southern California are the co-hosts.

“Rev. Hamilton’s record speaks for itself and his expertise has touched many lives,” said Coleman.  “He has given dedicated service to many such as Mount Moriah Baptist Church, under both the pastorates of the late Dr. Earl A. Pleasant and Dr. Melvin V. Wade, Sr.; Double Rock Baptist Church under the late Dr. Joseph Holmes and Community Baptist Church under the late Dr. E. Boyd Esters.”
