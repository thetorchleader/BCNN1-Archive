3 People Shot In 2 Incidents Within 2 Hours in New Orleans

August 11, 2014 Black

(Photo by Ken Daley, NOLA.com | The Times-Picayune) (Ken Daley, NOLA.com | The Times-Picayune)

Three people were shot in two incidents about an hour apart on Saturday night (Aug. 9) in New Orleans.

Police responded shortly before midnight after two men were shot in the 3400 block of Erato Street. The victims “were in the area when shots were fired,” according to a police report. Police did not discuss the circumstances further.

The victims were privately transported to a hospital. Police did not report the victims’ ages or the extent of their injuries.

Police responded shortly after 11 p.m. to the 2400 block of Lavender Street, where a 25-year-old man said he was shot in the hand while sitting in the front room of a house. He was taken to a hospital and was reported in stable condition as of Sunday morning.
