Ebola-Free Nurse Amber Vinson Had COGIC Intercessors Praying for Her

October 23, 2014 Christian

Photo Credit: Lawrence Vinson

Prayer changes things. When 29-year-old health worker Amber Vinson contracted the Ebola virus after caring for an infected patient, while media talked, intercessors prayed. She is now Ebola-free.

The Dallas-based nurse, who is the granddaughter of a Church of God In Christ (COGIC) District Missionary, had a faith-filled support system.

In an October 15 Facebook post, a New Life Church of God In Christ representative wrote, “I need everyone reading this to intercede on her behalf.”

The Toledo, Ohio-based church, led by Bishop Edward and First Lady Sheila Cook, also prayed that Vinson’s “fiancé, mother and anyone else she had contact with not be affected and that Amber break forth healed delivered and set free.”

The message ended with, “Let this be a Miraculous healing in Jesus name. It is so, it is done…Amen!”

Indeed, it is done.

Official reports say doctors are unable to find any traces of the virus that killed Thomas Eric Duncan, one of Vinson’s patients.

The Ebola-free nurse’s mother, Debra Berry, shared the news with the press, adding that her daughter, who has been receiving treatment at Emory Hospital in Atlanta, will be transferred from isolation.

Her family said the quarantined patient’s spirits were high.

In an interview with CNN’s Don Lemon, Vinson’s pastor Earl McClellan of Shoreline Dallas said, Amber and Derrick, her fiancé, “did have some fear, which is natural,” but also, he added, they “put their trust and hope in God.”
