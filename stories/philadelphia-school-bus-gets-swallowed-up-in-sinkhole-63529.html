Philadelphia School Bus Gets Swallowed Up in Sinkhole

March 25, 2015 National

A school bus carrying Philadelphia students crashed into a sinkhole along a Kensington street Friday afternoon.

The wreck left the front wheels of the bus in the hole along E Ontario near C Street around 3:30 p.m.

  • Sinkhole Swallows Up School Bus

No students were hurt. They got off the Philadelphia School District bus and got onto another bus.

No word yet on what school the kids came from.
