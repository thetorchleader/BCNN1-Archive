Kanye West Turns himself in to the Police

March 29, 2014 Entertainment

Kanye West attends the Y-3 Menswear Fall/Winter 2014-2015 Show as part of Paris Fashion Week on Jan. 19, 2014, in Paris. RICHARD BORD/GETTY IMAGES

Kanye West reportedly turned himself into Los Angeles police custody Thursday over an assault case involving a photographer.

According to TMZ, the 36-year-old rapper was booked and released by the LAPD in under an hour.

Earlier this month, West pleaded no contest to a misdemeanor battery charge.

West had been accused of assaulting photographer Daniel Ramos and wrestling his camera to the ground in July at Los Angeles International Airport.

He was sentenced to two years of probation and required to complete 24 hours of anger management therapy sessions in addition to 250 hours of community service.

The rapper, who recently appeared on the cover of Vogue with fiancee Kim Kardashian, reported to the police station for booking Thursday as part of his plea.
