Oprah Shedding Tears Over Bobbi Kristina’s Sad Situation

February 6, 2015 Entertainment

The 21-year-old daughter of Whitney Houston and Bobby Brown remains in a medically induced coma after being found unconscious and face down in a bathtub last Saturday

Oprah Winfrey got emotional on Thursday when she learned that Whitney Houston’s daughter, Bobbi Kristina Brown, was on life support after being placed in a medically induced coma at an Atlanta Hospital last week.

“There aren’t any words right now,” Winfrey told a TMZ cameraman after she appeared to hold back tears. “But it’s a sad time.”

Despite grim media reports, the 21-year-old reality star’s father, Bobby Brown, has issued a statement saying she has not been removed from life support.

“This is false, just as is the vast majority of the other reporting that is currently taking place,” he said on Thursday.
