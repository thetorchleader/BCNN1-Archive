WATCH: Bishop Paul S. Morton Discusses Bridging the Gap Between Baptists and Pentecostals and Why He’s Stepping Down as the Head of His Church

June 10, 2014 Christian

Bishop Paul S. Morton

In this episode of Behind-The-Praise, Cheryl Jackson speaks with Bishop Paul S. Morton about his mission in bridging the gap between Baptists and Pentecostals, how he overcame a mental breakdown and why he’s stepping down as the head of his church. He also talks about how he councils his son P.J. Morton to deal with the scrutiny of the church as a secular music artist.
