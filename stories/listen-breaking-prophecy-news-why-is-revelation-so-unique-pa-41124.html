Looking at world events through the lens of the Word of God, let’s look at some headlines from today’s news that point to the second coming of the Lord Jesus Christ.

First, today, under the Sign category of INCREASE IN VIOLENCE — According to The Washington Post, the cost of turning against the Islamic State was made brutally apparent in the streets of a dusty backwater town in eastern Syria in early August. Over a three-day period, vengeful fighters shelled, beheaded, crucified and shot hundreds of residents after they dared to rise up against the extremists. By the time the killing stopped, 700 people were dead, making this the bloodiest single atrocity committed by the Islamic State in Syria since it declared its existence 18 months ago.

Second, today, under the Sign category of THE TURMOIL IN ISRAEL & THE MIDDLE EAST — According to the Jerusalem Post, Western governments will reluctantly consider an extension of negotiations with Iran over its nuclear program should world powers fail to clinch a comprehensive deal by November 24 – a move Israel would support. Informal discussion of an extension beyond the self-imposed deadline – the second since talks began in January, with the aim of ending international concerns over the nature, purpose, size and scope of the Iranian program – has already started despite five weeks being left on the clock.

Third, today, under the Sign category of WARS & RUMORS OF WARS — According to CNN, ISIS militants launched about 15 near-simultaneous attacks on Kurdish forces in northern Iraq on Monday in what Kurdish government officials said was a fierce and renewed push for territory. ISIS also launched attacks against Mosul Dam, a strategic prize, and also renewed its offensive on the Sinjar mountain range in northern Iraq. A senior official reported that ISIS fighters were also killed in the attacks, and that the Peshmerga successfully repelled most of the more than a dozen incidents on Monday.

Fourth, today, under the Sign category of THE INCREASE IN SEXUAL IMMORALITY & HOMOSEXUALITY — According to The New York Times, President Obama says he now believes that the Constitution guarantees a right to same-sex marriage in all 50 states. Mr. Obama opposed same-sex marriage until 2012, when he came out in favor of letting states decide the issue for themselves and urged them to embrace such unions. Now, in an interview with The New Yorker, he says same-sex marriage should be a right for all Americans regardless of where they lived.

Fifth, today, under the Sign category of INCREASED PERSECUTION — According to Worthy News, a Christian in Uzbekistan has been fined and threatened with further punishment after religious literature was seized from his home during a raid by Uzbek police. A judge said the literature should have been stored in a building belonging to a registered religious organization, but the homeowner is a member of a Christian denomination that refuses on principle to seek state registration. Uzbek authorities also raided the homes of four other believers and seized dozens of Bible and other Christian literature.

The Bible says in 2 Thessalonians 2:1-3: “Now we beseech you, brethren, by the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, and by our gathering together unto him, That ye be not soon shaken in mind, or be troubled, neither by spirit, nor by word, nor by letter as from us, as that the day of Christ is at hand. Let no man deceive you by any means: for [that day shall not come], except there come a falling away first, and that man of sin be revealed, the son of perdition.”

Now, it’s time for Prophecy Bootcamp.

Prophecy Bootcamp is where we deal with the basics of prophecy, the Second Coming of Christ, and what will happen in the future according to the Bible. Our aim here is not to make predictions but to help you get prepared by understanding how things will unfold in the end times.

Our topic for today is titled “Why is Revelation So Unique? (Part 1)” from Dr. Ed Hindson’s book “Revelation: Unlocking the Future”.

There are several elements that make Revelation the most unique book in the Bible. The basic structure of the book is woven around a series of threes and sevens. The overarching triplet reveals past, present, and future:

Past: “the things which you have seen” (chapter 1).
Present: “the things which are” (chapters 2-3).
Future: “the things which shall take place after these things (chapters 4-22).

The seven visions are as follows:

Seven churches
Seven seals
Seven trumpets
Seven symbolic figures
Seven bowls
Seven judgments
Seven triumphs

The use of symbolic numbers is found everywhere in the Apocalypse. These include: ½, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 10, 12, 24, 41, 144, 666, 1000, 1260, 1600, 7000, 12,000, 144,000, 100 million, and 200 million. The term “hour” is used ten times in Revelation, always referring to a brief period of time (e.g., “in one hour your judgment has come”).

Three, one of the most significant numbers in the Apocalypse, is the symbolic number of the Trinity. It is one of John’s favorite numbers, and his use of it dominates his writing style. He constantly expresses himself in triplets: “Blessed is he who reads…those who hear…and heed”; “Jesus Christ: faithful witness…first born of the dead…ruler of the kings of the Earth”; “[He] loved us…released us…made us to be a kingdom, priests to His God”; “says the Lord God, who is and who was and who is to come”; “the living one…was dead…am alive forevermore”; I know your tribulation…the poverty…the blasphemy”; “remember…keep it…repent”; “Buy…gold…white garments…eye salve.” There are scores of these triplets throughout Revelation.

The number three also figures prominently in several passages referring to judgment. There are three series of judgments: seals, trumpets, and bowls. Judgment consists of three elements: fire, smoke, and brimstone. These elements kill a third part of mankind. A third of the trees are burned up, and the sun is blacked out a third part of the day. There are three evil agencies (the satanic trinity): Dragon, beast, and False Prophet behind all opposition to the reign of Christ on earth. Finally, there is the threefold defeat of Satan: on earth, into the abyss, and into the lake of fire.

In closing, let’s consider what God wants you and me to do in light of His Second Coming.

Jesus Christ said in Luke 19:13 to “Occupy till I come.” In light of that, we are continuing our Bible study series on the books of First & Second Thessalonians which Paul wrote to believers who had concerns about the Second Coming of Jesus Christ, and what they should do as they waited. This section is titled “Comfort at the Grave (Part 5)” from the book “Waiting on the Second Coming” by Ray C. Stedman.

We can best understand the Thessalonians’ perspective if we remember five simple steps:

1. The Thessalonians had clearly been expecting Jesus to return before any of them died. His return was a moment-by-moment expectancy in the early church. First-century Christians believed the Lord was coming at any moment. In the first chapter of this letter Paul commends the Thessalonians for waiting for God’s Son from heaven. That is what they were looking for. Jesus’ own words suggest that this would be the case. All His statements about His return were addressed to people still alive, and He spoke as though they would still be alive when He returned. To His disciples He said, “Watch, because you do not know the day or the hour.”. He used such terms as “be not deceived,” and “the Son of Man will come at an hour when you do not expect him.” There is no mention of the impact of His coming upon those who had already died.

If the Lord tarries His coming and we live, we will continue looking at this topic in our next broadcast/podcast.

Pray and ask Him to come into your heart today, and He will. Romans 10:13 says, “For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.”
