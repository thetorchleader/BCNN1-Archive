Will the Saints Come Marching In for Tim Tebow?

May 3, 2013 Uncategorized

Following up the must-see ranking of every NFL team 1-32 based on their fit for Tim Tebow, a newly minted NFL free agent, Yahoo! Sports’ Jason Cole offered this tantalizing tweet:
Tiny bit #Tebow news: he’d like to go where he can sit, learn behind great QB & coach. New Orleans would be perfect, for example.
— Jason Cole (@JasonColeYahoo) May 2, 2013
Beyond the willingness to “sit and learn” — Cole’s phrase, not necessarily a direct quote from Tebow — let’s unpack the two criteria of “great QB” and “[great] coach.” Back to our list:

(1) Patriots: Tom Brady (check), Bill Belichick (check)
(2) 49ers: Colin Kaepernick (not yet), Jim Harbaugh (absolutely)
(3) Seahawks: Russell Wilson (basically, yes), Pete Carroll (yes)
(4) Eagles: Mike Vick (meh), Chip Kelly (almost certainly)
(5) Jaguars: Blaine Gabbert (nope), Gus Bradley (maybe?)
(6) Bucs: Josh Freeman (not really), Greg Schiano (sorta?)
(7) Bears: Jay Cutler (“great?” eh … ), Marc Trestman (TBD)
(8) Chargers: Phil Rivers (not still), Mike McCoy (not yet)
(9) Rams: Sam Bradford (nope), Jeff Fisher (yup)
(10) Cardinals: Carson Palmer (please), Bruce Arians (possibly!)
