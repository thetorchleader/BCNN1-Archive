Ralph Reed Says Christian Conservatives Will Outorganize Obama in North Carolina

August 29, 2012 Uncategorized

Religious conservatives have in recent days begun a major grass roots voter registration and get-out the vote effort in North Carolina and other key swing states that they believe can more than match those of President Barack Obama’s campaign.
Ralph Reed, the leader of the Faith and Freedom Coalition, said conservative religious households this week began receiving mailing urging them to register to vote. The mailings will be followed by automated telephone calls from former Arkansas Gov. Mike Huckabee and former Pennsylvania Sen. Rick Santorum.
The mailings say, “Get off the bench, and get in the game,” Reed told the N.C. Delegation breakfast at the Republican convention Tuesday.
“In 2008, there were 17 million born-again evangelical Christians who didn’t even bother to vote,” Reed said. His group has sent the mailing to 1.97 million conservative Christians in North Carolina, Colorado, Ohio, Iowa, Nevada, Florida and Virginia.
He said other conservative groups, such as National Right to Life and Focus in Family also have similar campaigns.
