Family Source says Brown and Houston Families Agree to Let Bobbi Kristina Go on the Same Date as Whitney Houston; Tyler Perry Helping Both Families Come to Peace and Make Funeral Arrangements

February 10, 2015 Black

BEVERLY HILLS, CA – FEBRUARY 12: Singer Whitney Houston (L) and Bobbi Kristina Brown arrives at the 2011 Pre-GRAMMY Gala and Salute To Industry Icons Honoring David Geffen at Beverly Hilton on February 12, 2011 in Beverly Hills, California.
PHOTO BY JASON MERRITT/GETTY IMAGES

After being in a medically induced coma for two weeks, Bobbi Kristina Brown’s family has agreed to wait until Wednesday to take her off so she can die on the same date as Whitney Houston, according to the NY Post.

A family source told the Post it was Bobbi Kristina’s grandmother, Cissy Houston, who came up with the idea and even though the Houston and Brown families have been in disagreement on several issues, they agreed on Feb. 11. The source says both families now realize that there’s nothing else they can do for Bobbi Kristina.

“This is the only thing they can agree on — that she’s gone [brain-dead], and there’s nothing more than can be done for her,” the source said.

The source also told the Post that Tyler Perrry took part in having both families coming to peace with each other and is helping them with funeral arrangements.

“You wouldn’t think of him as being someone who’d be a peacemaker, but he’s someone both sides really respect,” the Brown source said.
