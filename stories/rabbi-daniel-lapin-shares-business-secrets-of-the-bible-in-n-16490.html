Rabbi Daniel Lapin Shares Business Secrets of the Bible In New Book

April 3, 2014 Books

Business Secrets from the Bible

Rabbi Daniel Lapin says you’ll almost never find Jews tinkering with their cars or mowing their own lawns on the weekends.

He explains there’s a very good reason for that, and it lies within the business secrets of the Bible.

His business theory demonstrates how making money is actually a spiritual activity. In his book, Business Secrets from the Bible, he shares how profits are God’s reward for forming relationships with others and serving them.

His book suggests ways for readers to transform themselves and continue toward success even in the face of fear and uncertainty while focusing on others.

Rabbi Lapin shared how to understand the “power of specialization,” among other principles for success outlined in his book, on CBN News Today, March 2.
