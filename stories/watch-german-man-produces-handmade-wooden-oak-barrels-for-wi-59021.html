WATCH: German Man Produces Handmade Wooden Oak Barrels for Wine

February 27, 2015 God & Food

Ralf Mattern builds wooden barrels. He’s one of the last of his kind in Germany but his work is more popular than ever. More and more young winemakers are ordering their barrels from him. They know that high-quality wine matures better in barrels made of oak rather than steel.
