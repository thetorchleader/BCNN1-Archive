Tourism Campaign Aims to Bring More African Americans to San Antonio, Texas

September 4, 2014 Black

A promotional video produced by the Convention and Visitors Bureau has one specific purpose: attracting more African American tourists to San Antonio.

Tourism leaders say a promotional video aimed at attracting more African American tourists to San Antonio is not about race – it’s about marketing.

The Convention and Visitors Bureau says African American tourism is a $40 billion industry, and as diverse a city as we are, we want to be a part of that.

A trip to the Riverwalk is a walk down memory lane for Laverne and Troy Byrd.

“It’s a wonderful city,” Laverne says. “We love it.”

They lived here 30 years ago when they were stationed at JBSA-Randolph and are back for the first time to see what’s changed.

“It’s gotten bigger, larger,” Laverne says.

But they’ve found much of what they loved about the city has stayed the same.

“History, the culture,” Laverne says. “To go to the Alamo. Go to the Riverwalk. Just to recapture.”

Their visit coincides with a big push to bring more African American tourists to San Antonio.

“Arts, culture, heritage – it’s all very important to the black American traveler,” Richard Oliver with the Convention and Visitors Bureau says. “So when you talk about those kids of things, you’re talking about San Antonio.”
