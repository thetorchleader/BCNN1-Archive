Oklahoma City Thunder Guard, Russell Westbrook, Gets Engaged to Former UCLA Player, Nina Earl

September 6, 2014 Sports

NBA player Russell Westbrook has officially drafted a woman with whom he can spend the rest of his life! 

The Oklahoma City Thunder guard has just announced that he is getting married to his girlfriend Nina Earl. The baller revealed their engagement on Instagram in a shot of his wife-to-be showing off the ring he gave her, excitedly writing, “Nothing even matters..’ @ninamari3_ forever my lady! LOVE YOU!”

Guard your eyes, beauties, because the betrothal bling below is blinding!
