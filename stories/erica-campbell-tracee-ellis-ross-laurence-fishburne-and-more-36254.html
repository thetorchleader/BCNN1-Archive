Erica Campbell, Tracee Ellis Ross, Laurence Fishburne and More Celebs Talk Faith and Entertainment at 6th Annual Merge Summit

August 30, 2014 Entertainment

Faith, Love & Success Panel (L-R): Derek and Sofia Luke, Wayne and Myesha Chaney, Faune Chambers Watkins and Derek “Fonzworth Bentley” Watkins, Meagan Good and Devon Franklin

The Sixth Annual Merge Summit was held August 21-23, 2014 at the historic Millennium Biltmore Hotel in Downtown Los Angeles.

Hundreds came out to see the industry’s top entertainers and executives lead more than 20 interactive panel discussions, workshops and Master Class sessions covering music, television, branding and new media.

Celebs included the leads of ABC’s new show “Black-ish”: Anthony Anderson, Tracee Ellis Ross and Laurence Fishburne; hot gospel artists like Mali Music, Deitrick Haddon, Michelle Williams and Erica Campbell; and wild cards like Common, Mikki Taylor, Lala Anthony, Lynn Whitfield and cast members of “Preachers of LA.” There were close to 75 celebs & industry movers and shakers; too many to count! This one of a kind event integrates faith and entertainment providing an opportunity for men and women of faith to interface, learn and partner with leading executives and talent in the entertainment industry. The founders, Dr. Holly Carter and Robi Reed did a fantastic job organizing all the seminars, all while looking fabulous.

Our associate, LaCora Stephens, spoke to a few of the guests on the red carpet to get their take on the importance of faith in the industry.
