Faith Film “Where Hope Grows” Is First Major Motion Picture to Cast Actor With Down Syndrome In Lead Role

May 17, 2015 Entertainment

Hollywood’s recent trend of faith films is scoring another title at the box office this weekend.

“Where Hope Grows” hits theaters Friday, May 15, and it’s the first major motion picture to cast an actor with Down syndrome in a lead role.

David DeSanctis plays the character called “Produce,” nicknamed after his job in the produce department at a grocery store. The young man befriends Calvin Campbell, a retired baseball player whose panic attacks force him into early retirement and send his life in a downward spiral.

When “Produce” meets Campbell, played by Kristoffer Polaha, the two embark upon an unexpected journey and an unexpected friendship.

“When you meet these characters and see what he is going through, you want to go on that journey with them,” Chris Dowling, the film’s director, told CBN News.
