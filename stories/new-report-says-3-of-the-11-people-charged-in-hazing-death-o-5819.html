New Report says 3 of the 11 People Charged in Hazing Death of Robert Champion Were Not Students at FAMU

May 9, 2012 Uncategorized

Three of the 11 charged in the November hazing death of Florida A&M drum major Robert Champion were not FAMU students.

A list provided Tuesday to FAMU Board of Trustees of those arrested last week showed that Caleb Jackson, Bryan Jones and the lone female defendant, LaSherry Conder, were not enrolled at the university last fall when Champion was beaten on a band bus in Orlando following the Florida Classic football game.
The Tallahassee Democrat reported last week Jones was last enrolled at FAMU in spring 2011. The list provided to trustees said Jackson was not a FAMU student and was last enrolled at Tallahassee Community College in 2009.
Jackson is the only one of those charged with felony hazing who was denied bond. He violated his probation from a 2009 felony battery charge stemming from a fight he had on TCC’s campus.
Conder’s FAMU enrollment status for fall 2011 and spring 2012, the list notes, is “unknown.” She is cited as a TCC student, but no details are provided.
University officials previously have said all those participating in the band, some of whom were TCC and Florida State University students, were enrolled in FAMU’s band course. In August 2011, the band, which is indefinitely suspended, was comprised of 336 FAMU students, 35 TCC students and two FSU students.
Officials noted in a written response to questions earlier this year that band policy allowed “students not enrolled in the band course to participate in band-related activities.”
