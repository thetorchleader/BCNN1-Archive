Pastor Marjorie Esomowei of Great Britain says Women Should Be Beautiful Inside and Outside

May 31, 2014 Black

Pastor Marjorie Esomowei

Pastor  Marjorie Esomowei is the co- pastor, Triumphant International Church  in the UK. She is also the President of Wisdom International For Women.  She is in Nigeria for the forthcoming  Wise Women (Nigeria edition) coming up next month.  Married to  Pastor Clement Esomowei, Marjorie also have a niche in beauty therapy as a consultant to Mary Kay in the UK. So, her ministry entails giving  women the essentials of being  beautiful inside and outside. In this interview with CHRISTY ANYANWU, she talked about the Wise Women awards and other issues.

Could you tell us about this award?

It’s called the wise women award. It is 10 years now in the UK. It is one of the major events in the Christian calendar among the black Caribbean and African women.  We had the inaugural meeting at Sheraton Hotel, Nigeria  last year. This year’s edition is coming up on June 21. Our targets are women who are impacting society, impacting the church but really going beyond the walls of the church to impact society and community. The awards have three category: Woman in Media, Woman in Business, Woman in Nation, Woman in Ministry, Professional  Christian Woman  and Naomi Award. Naomi award  is for women who are over 60 and impacting their church and world in Nigeria. We also have  young exceptional women  between 18 and 25 who are doing exceptionally well; the award for them is called “Turn-around award”.

It is for Christian women of any  denomination. Turn-around is for women that have overcome what life challenges and she’s using it as a message. I will give you an example. Last year in the Uk, we got a young lady who left home at 13. She became a gang leader at 13 and almost completely ruined her family but her church and her mum stood by her and prayed. Today, she’s turned around and so we are looking for stories like that. We have reputable women across Nigeria who are on the panel of judges. Once nominations are done, we will send everything to them. Everything information  is online,  on our website.  It’s so competitive, they should read the criteria and tell us about their nominees.
