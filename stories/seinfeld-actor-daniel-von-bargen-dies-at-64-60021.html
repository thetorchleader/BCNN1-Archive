“Seinfeld” Actor Daniel von Bargen Dies at 64

March 5, 2015 Entertainment

Daniel von Bargen

A Cincinnati native whose acting career spanned more than 30 years has passed away.

Daniel von Bargen, 64, died over the weekend after a long illness, sources confirmed Tuesday.

The actor, active from 1976 to 2009, was best known for TV roles on “Seinfeld” and “Malcolm in the Middle,” and movie roles in “Broken Arrow,” “Super Troopers” and “O Brother, Where Art Thou.”

von Bargen suffered a self-inflicted gunshot wound at his Montgomery apartment in February 2012. He told a 911 operator he didn’t want to go to surgery to have toes removed due to diabetic complications.

The actor’s memorial service and burial will be private, sources said.
