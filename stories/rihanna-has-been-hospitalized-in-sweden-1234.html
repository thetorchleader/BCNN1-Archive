Rihanna Has Been Hospitalized in Sweden

November 1, 2011 Uncategorized

Sources say she wasn’t feeling well and her show in Malmo, Sweden was cancelled by order of a doctor who said she “was not capable of carrying out the concert.” As of now, organizers are trying to find a new date for the show, Uptown Magazine reports.
