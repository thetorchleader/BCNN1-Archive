LA Clippers Closer to Winning Playoff Series

May 8, 2012 Uncategorized

The Los Angeles Clippers showed a lot of character in coming back from that 27-point deficit in their playoff-opening miracle a week ago in Memphis.
And they showed some growing maturity in coming home and taking care of business, winning two nail biters to take a commanding 3-1 lead in their first playoff series in the Chris Paul era.
The second of those two games came Monday night, a gritty 101-97 overtime win led by forward Blake Griffin (30 points, a playoff high, along with seven assists) and point guard Chris Paul (27 points, nine rebounds and seven assists).
Now they must show a killer instinct and, yes, the fact that “Clippers” and “killer instinct” are being used in the same sentence does suggest that times have changed in the NBA.
For most of their history, the Clippers have been the victim, the downtrodden, the doormat.
But with Paul directing the show and Griffin supplying the oohs and aahs, the Clippers look like a sure thing to make the second round.
Paul doesn’t see why there has to be a two- or three-year plan for the Clippers to go deep into the playoffs. He’s ready now.
“I’m blessed to be healthy right now,” Paul says. “For me, it’s all about the right now and seizing the moment. This team right now, we’re trying to go all the way.
“The sky’s the limit. We have the pieces to be successful now.”
