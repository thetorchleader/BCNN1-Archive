T-Boz Says She Never Got Angry With God Over Sickle Cell Disease

March 4, 2015 Entertainment

GETTY

When people go through hardships, trauma and seemingly unfair things in life, they sometimes get angry with God.

But Tionne Tenese Watkins, 44, better known as T-Boz of the best-selling girl group TLC, said she was not upset about her sickle cell diagnosis.

“I never got angry with God,” she told BET’s “Lift Every Voice” host Fonzworth Bentley in an interview.

Sickle cell disease, an inherited disorder, causes abnormal shape in red blood cells, resulting in extremely painful episodes, serious infections, chronic anemia, and damage to body organs.

Watkins has battled the disease all her life.

At age 7, she said an ill-informed doctor misdiagnosed her. “He told me I would never live past 30 and I would never have children, and I would be disabled, and handicapped my whole life.”

According to Watkins, her mother, however, did not waver. “She just looked at me and she said, ‘God has the last say, so, don’t worry about it. We’ll talk when we get in the car and on the way home.’”

Her mother also told her that God “practices medicine. God can heal you” and “miracles happen every day.”
