Jeremy Lin Takes Some Sweet Kids Shopping for Christmas

December 24, 2012 Uncategorized

Intro:
Rockets point guard Jeremy Lin took some time out of the NBA season to help the Houston community on Friday, December 21st.
CLICK HERE TO WATCH VIDEO
Script:
Jeremy Lin gets around doesn’t he?
The Houston Rockets shot to fame during last season’s NBA with the New York Knicks last season but he has now moved to the Houston Rockets.
And he is a fan favourite in Texas, especially amongst the franchise’s younger supporters.
As it’s the festive season, Line gave 25 children disadvantaged families a Christmas gift by taking them shopping for toys. 
It looked like an expensive trip but Lin was full of Christmas spirit.
He should be – Houston have won four of their last five games and Lin scored 18 points during the win over Philadelphia on Wednesday.
The Rockets host the Memphis Grizzlies at the Toyota Center on Saturday .
