Explosion Strikes Nigerian City Attacked by Boko Haram

February 14, 2012 Uncategorized

Police are reporting an explosion in a central Nigeria city that has seen attacks claimed by a radical Islamist sect.

The Kaduna State
Police spokesman told The Associated Press an explosion occurred Tuesday
morning in a residential neighborhood in Kaduna.

Aminu Lawan said officers from the police’s anti-bomb squad said they had found other unexploded bombs in the area.

He said there have been no reports of casualties.

A radical Islamist sect known as Boko Haram claimed a similar attack earlier this month.

Boko
Haram is blamed for killing at least 286 people so far this year,
according to an AP count. At least 185 people died in the sect’s
coordinated assault in January on Kano, the largest city of Nigeria’s
north.

SOURCE: The Associated Press
