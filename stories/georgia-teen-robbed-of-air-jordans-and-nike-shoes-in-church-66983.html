Georgia Teen Robbed of Air Jordans and Nike Shoes in Church Parking Lot

April 12, 2015 National

An 18-year-old Columbus man reported to police that he was struck in the face and robbed Saturday afternoon.

He did not require medical treatment.

According the police report, the incident happened around 2 p.m. in the parking lot of McCartha Drive Church of Christ.

He said a pair of Air Jordan shoes valued at $180 and a pair of Nike shoes valued at $120 were taken.

No description of the thief was given in the report.

Anyone with information should contact Columbus police.

SOURCE: LARRY GIERER
The Columbus Ledger-Enquirer
