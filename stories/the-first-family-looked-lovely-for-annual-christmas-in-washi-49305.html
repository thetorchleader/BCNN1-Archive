The First Family Looked Lovely for Annual Christmas in Washington

December 16, 2014 Black

President Barack Obama, first lady Michelle Obama, and their daughters Sasha and Malia pose with “elves” prior to the taping of TNT’s Christmas in Washington program on Dec. 14, 2014, in Washington, D.C.
KRISTOFFER TRIPPLAAR-POOL/GETTY IMAGES

It was just a couple of weeks ago that President Barack Obama pardoned a turkey and his two teenage daughters were doing what teenagers do best—looking as if they had plenty of other things to do. I mean, do you blame them? Pardoning a turkey isn’t exactly the highlight of someone’s day. Sasha and Malia Obama received tons of scrutiny for what some people perceived as their bad attitudes and grimaces they donned. But no one came under fire more than a former GOP communications director, Elizabeth Lauten.

Lauten not only criticized the first daughters, but she pretty much said their attire looked more appropriate for the bar than turkey pardoning. Lauten received backlash online after her inappropriate Facebook post was tweeted, and she eventually resigned from her job.

Maybe Lauten had a chance to see Sasha and Malia in their Christmas best over the weekend and had a change of opinion (too little, too late, though). On Sunday the first family attended the annual Christmas in Washington concert and, as usual, took a photo while surrounded by little elves, all of them former patients of Washington, D.C.’s Children’s National Medical Center, the concert beneficiary. (The concert will air on TNT this Friday.)
