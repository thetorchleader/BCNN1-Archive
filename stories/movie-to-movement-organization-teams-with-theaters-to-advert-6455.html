Movie to Movement Organization Teams with Theaters to Advertise and Promote Faith Based Films

June 6, 2012 Uncategorized

“Not only can we support positive films, we can produce them. We can fulfill the Great Commission through film.” -Jason Jones

World Net Daily reports that over 1,200 theaters have signed up with an organization called Movie to Movement to advertise, promote and recruit ticket buyers to see films that affirm faith and family values.  
President and founder of the movement, Jason Jones, was quoted as saying the success of recent faith-based films is “creating amazing opportunities for people of faith in Hollywood to communicate the kinds of messages that can transform a nation–for the better.”
Said Jones, “When I started Movie to Movement just 2 years ago, I thought,’Wouldn’t it be great if we could get celebrities to speak out and share their values?’ I thought it was a stretch… I thought it was a dream, but it’s happening.”
