“Motown Christmas” Is #1 on Billboard’s Top Gospel Album Chart

December 11, 2014 Entertainment

And the sales are in! Check out what the TOP 5 Gospel Artists sold and see where their albums charted on Billboard’s Top Gospel Album chart below…

Various Artists – “Motown Christmas”
Chart position: #1
Weekly Sales: 6,270 / Total US Sales: 16,909

J Moss – “Grown Folks Gospel”
Chart position: #2
Weekly Sales: 3.382 / Total US Sales: 10,032

Fred Hammond – “I Will Trust″
Chart position: #3
Weekly Sales: 2,785 / Total US Sales: 16,970

Various Artists – “WOW Gospel 2014”
Chart position: #4
Weekly Sales: 1,487 / Total US Sales: 135,687

3 Winans Brothers – “Foreign Land″
