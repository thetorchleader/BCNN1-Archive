Ludacris Marries Longtime Girlfriend

January 7, 2015 Entertainment

© @Eudoxiee/Instagram Ludacris marries his longtime girlfriend in surprise wedding

First Cameron Diaz gets hitched and now Ludacris! Bring on the celeb newlyweds of 2015!

OK, so Chris “Ludacris” Bridges tied the knot on Christmas Eve 2014. But the big reveal of his nuptials came on Jan. 6 when the rapper posted photos of himself and his bride on Instagram.

“Why wait? Did the thing before 2015.#mrandmrsrbridges,” he captioned a photo of himself and his new wife Eudoxie, as they lovingly looked into each other’s eyes.

According to TMZ, Luda popped the question over the holidays, and the couple exchanged vows that same day. Following the proposal, the entertainer took his bride-to-be out for a nice meal in Georgia. When they returned home, a wedding dress, that Luda’s mom helped pick out, was waiting for Eudoxie.
