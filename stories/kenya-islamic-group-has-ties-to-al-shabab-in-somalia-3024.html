Kenya Islamic Group has Ties to al-Shabab in Somalia

January 15, 2012 Uncategorized

An increasingly vocal Islamist group says
its leader has been appointed to represent an al-Qaida-linked Somali
militia in Kenya, a development that underscores the dangers Kenya faces
from Somalia’s insurgency.

The statement this
week by the Kenya-based Muslim Youth Center came amid a flurry of
warnings from embassies about planned terror attacks in Kenya. The
Somali militant group al-Shabab has promised to attack Kenya for its
decision to send troops to Somalia in October.

The
Muslim Youth Center was named in a U.N. report last year for
recruiting, fundraising, and running training and orientation events for
al-Shabab. The Wednesday statement published on the center’s blog said
that Amiir Ahmad Iman Ali is the lead of al-Shabab inside Kenya.

SOURCE: The Associated Press
