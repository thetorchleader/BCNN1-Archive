Paula White to Take Over New Destiny Christian Center as Pastor, Riva Tims Plans to Address Controversy

December 29, 2011 Uncategorized

New Destiny Christian Center’s church board members officially named their choice for Pastor Zachary Tims’ replacement.
A news release was sent out Thursday morning announcing Rev. Paula White’s new position as the church’s senior pastor.
The official announcement comes on the same day Riva Tims plans to address the controversy surrounding the replacement.
Zachary Tims was found dead inside a New York City hotel room in August.
