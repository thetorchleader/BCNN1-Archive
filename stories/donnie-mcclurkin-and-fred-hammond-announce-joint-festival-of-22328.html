Donnie McClurkin and Fred Hammond Announce Joint “Festival of Praise” Tour

May 15, 2014 Entertainment

If you love seeing the gospel of Jesus Christ spread through music—and great music at that—this bit of news will excite you.

Two of gospel music’s most respected names, Grammy, Stellar and Dove Award-winning recording artists Donnie McClurkin and Fred Hammond, are headed out on tour this fall. And they just may be coming to a city near you.

September 22 through November 2, 2014, the “Festival of Praise” tour will converge upon cities across the nation.

EEW Magazine’s inside sources have confirmed the tour is still in its initial planning phases, with decisions yet to be made regarding which venues it will occupy.

News about gospel tours is always great news since the niche genre is feeling the impact of the changing music landscape.
