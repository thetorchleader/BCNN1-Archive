The Greatest Christmas Gift of All Time

December 4, 2011 Uncategorized

It is hard to believe it’s that time of the year again. From now until Christmas Eve, retailers are vying for the same prize, your hard-earned dollar. In the misguided and too often materialistic focus of the Christmas holiday, it has become too easy to forget the true meaning of Christmas.

Christmas is not about the endless search for the best gift to be opened on Christmas morning. In fact, the true gift of Christmas is not a material thing at all. The true gift of Christmas is a perfect, priceless, gift of love. 
WHAT IS THE TRUE GIFT OF CHRISTMAS?
God loved the world so much that He sent His only Son, Jesus Christ, to die on the cross to pay the penalty for our sins. 
The first Christmas was celebrated over two thousand years ago. A baby boy named Jesus was born and placed in a manger — a feeding trough for animals was his bed. God planned for Baby Jesus, His perfect Son, to be born into this world for a very special and wonderful reason.
Because God loved the world so much and wanted fellowship with us, He made the most selfless, loving and wonderful sacrifice on our behalf that the world will ever know. God sent His only Son, Jesus Christ, who was pure and blameless, to take the punishment that we deserved. Jesus died on the cross to pay the penalty for our sins. Three days later, Jesus was raised from the dead. He now offers all who believe in Him the gift of eternal life.
The Bible says, God demonstrates His own love for us in Romans 5:8: “While we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.”
God makes us a wonderful, hope-filled promise. God promises that whoever believes in Jesus will not die, but will receive the gift of eternal life.
The Bible says in John 3:16, “For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.”
This Christmas season will you receive the true and most wonderful gift of love by placing your trust in Jesus Christ as your Saviour?
If you will accept Jesus Christ as your Lord and Saviour, please pray this prayer with all of your heart: Heavenly Father, I realize that I am a sinner and that I have done some bad things in my life. For Jesus Christ sake, please forgive me of my sins. I now believe with all of my heart that Jesus Christ died for me, was buried, and rose again. Lord Jesus, please come into my heart and save my soul and change my life today. Amen.  
Remember, friend, Believe by faith. Share the faith. and Keep the faith!
