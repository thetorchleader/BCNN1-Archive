House Ends Standoff, Passes Homeland Security Funding Bill

March 3, 2015 National

The standoff over funding the Department of Homeland Security is nearing its end after House Speaker John Boehner, R-Ohio, told his GOP colleagues in a private meeting Tuesday morning that he would allow vote on the bill as early as today.

The bill, already approved by the Senate, would fund the department for the full fiscal year through September and would not include any restrictions on President Obama’s executive actions on immigration that led to the impasse between the Senate and the House.

“As you’ve heard me say a number of times, the House has done its job by passing legislation to fund DHS and block the president’s executive actions on immigration,” Boehner told GOP representatives, according to a GOP aide in the room who was not authorized to speak about private deliberations. “Unfortunately, the fight was never won in the other chamber.”
