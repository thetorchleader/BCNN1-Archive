Rapper Common and Oprah to Be on Season 2 of ‘Empire’

March 16, 2015 Black

Common and Oprah Winfrey attend the “Selma” and the Legends Who Paved the Way gala at Bacara Resort on Dec. 6, 2014, in Goleta, Calif.
JASON MERRITT/GETTY IMAGES

If you blinked, you probably would have missed Oprah Winfrey’s first appearance on Empire. Well, technically, it was just a photo of Oprah and Lucious Lyon, but it may have been a bit of foreshadowing of next season. According to series co-creator and director Lee Daniels, Winfrey will make a guest appearance on the show in season 2.

“Yes. She’s gonna be on it,” Daniels told Access Hollywood in an interview.

Daniels and Winfrey worked together on Lee Daniels’ The Butler, and rumor has it that she will join him on his Richard Pryor biopic starring Mike Epps.

In addition to Winfrey’s appearing on Empire next season, one of her co-stars from Selma will join the cast. Daniels revealed that Common would be featured.
