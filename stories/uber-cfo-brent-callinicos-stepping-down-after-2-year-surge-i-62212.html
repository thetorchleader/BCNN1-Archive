Uber CFO Brent Callinicos Stepping Down After 2 Year Surge in Growth

March 18, 2015 Technology

Uber will be getting a new CFO. (PHOTO CREDIT: Andrew Harrer, Bloomberg)

Uber CFO Brent Callinicos, who oversaw ballooning growth in the ride-hailing company over the last two years, is stepping down.

Uber CEO Travis Kalanick shared the news of Callinicos’ planned departure in an e-mail, saying Callinicos will stay on as a consultant, according to a copy of the letter obtained by USA TODAY.

Kalanick said Gautam Gupta, Callinicos’ “right hand” on strategic finance, will be acting head of the finance division. He did not say if Gupta will replace Callinicos.

Callinicos has seen the company’s valuation tied to investments grow to a whopping $41 billion — up from $3.5 billion, where it was before he joined Uber in September 2013.

“I cannot tell you how incredibly excited I am to work with such a great leader and one of the top financial minds in the technology industry,” Kalanick said of Callinicos at the time he was hired from Google, where he was treasurer & chief accountant.

“Though two years sounds short, Uber was a much smaller start-up then — about one-tenth the size we are today,” Kalanick said in his e-mail on Monday. Kalanick said Callinicos helped take Uber “to the next level,” including financial systems in 53 countries.

“Brent has done a wonderful job here at Uber but has decided that it is time for his next journey, one where his wife and daughter take the front seat,” Kalanick said.
