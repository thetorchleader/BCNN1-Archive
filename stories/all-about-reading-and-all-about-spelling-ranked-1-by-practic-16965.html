All About Reading and All About Spelling Ranked #1 by Practical Homeschooling Readers

April 7, 2014 Bring the Children Home

The All About Reading and All About Spelling programs recently won first place in the phonics and spelling categories in the 2013 Practical Homeschooling Reader Awards.

Practical Homeschooling magazine’s 2013 Reader Awards named All About Reading and All About Spelling as the top homeschool programs for phonics and spelling.

All About Learning Press, Inc. is pleased to announce that its popular All About Reading and All About Spelling programs recently won first place in the phonics and spelling categories in the 2013 Practical Homeschooling Reader Awards.

The annual Practical Homeschooling Reader Awards allow thousands of readers to rate their satisfaction with homeschooling programs in a wide range of categories. The results of the readers’ votes were released in the January/February/March 2014 issue of Practical Homeschooling magazine. All About Reading received the highest number of votes in the phonics category to achieve the first place ranking, while All About Spelling received the highest number of votes in the spelling category.

For over twenty years, Practical Homeschooling magazine has been a valued resource in the homeschooling community. The magazine is published by Home Life, Inc. under the direction of Mary Pride, founder of http://www.home-school.comand the author of homeschooling classics such as The Big Book of Home Learning (the first mass-market homeschool how-to book).

Created by author and curriculum developer Marie Rippel, the All About Reading and All About Spelling programs are based on the Orton-Gillingham instructional approach and are designed to take the struggle out of reading and spelling. These comprehensive programs use multisensory lessons to teach children the way they learn naturally—through sight, sound, and touch. Both programs come with lifetime support and a 100 percent money-back guarantee.

For more information about the All About Reading and All About Spelling programs, visithttp://www.allaboutlearningpress.com.
