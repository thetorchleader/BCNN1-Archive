Texas Schoolteacher’s Expletive-Laden Tweet About Ferguson Lands her in Firestorm

November 10, 2014 Black

The Duncanville Independent School District has promised “swift action” in response to Vinita Hegwood’s tweet. (Photo: Screen grab via WFAA-TV, Dallas-Fort Worth)

A tweet by a Texas teacher has landed the woman in the middle of a social media firestorm, with the school district promising “swift action.”

The tweet included a reference to Ferguson, Mo., where rioting occurred after the fatal shooting of unarmed black teen Michael Brown in August. The town is awaiting a decision by the grand jury into Darren Wilson, the white police officer who killed Brown.

On Friday night, a Twitter account belonging to Duncanville Independent School District teacher Vinita Hegwood tweeted the following:

“Who the (expletive) made you dumb duck (expletive) crackers think I give a squat (expletive) about your opinions about my opinions RE: Ferguson? Kill yourselves.”

Hegwood, who is African American, teaches English, according to the Duncanville High School website.

A man who identified himself as Hegwood’s husband at their home Sunday night said she wasn’t available but that there was more to the story. He wouldn’t elaborate.

Hegwood’s Twitter account has since been closed down, despite attempts by a growing number of people to tag her in tweets and posts. It isn’t clear whether there was a tweet aimed directly at her that prompted the outburst Friday night.

Many comments on Twitter have called on the school district to fire Hegwood.
