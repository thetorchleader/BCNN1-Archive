TobyMac Crowned "King" Australian Christian Music Charts

January 11, 2013 Uncategorized

Rapper TobyMac has added to his collection of awards this week after being crowned the “King” of the Australian Christian music charts.
In addition to already winning his first American Music Award late last year, the singer scored dual honors from Australia’s official national Christian music chart- The Rock Across Australia.
TobyMac garnered both Song of the Year and Artist of the Year, according to a press release Thursday.
While his song “Me Without You” maintained a Top 10 position on Australia’s Christian radio airwaves, TobyMac “stood out on the chart with multiple entries over the year- four as a soloist, and two as a featured act with other performers,” chart researcher Wes Jay explained to Woodlands Media.
The songs Jay referred to include “Changed Forever,” “Steal My Show,” “Start Somewhere,” “Good Morning” with Mandisa,” and “Hold Me” with Jamie Grace.
Furthermore, the chart analyst noted TobyMac’s incredible radio feat.
