N. Korean defector says country’s hackers are ‘capable of attacks that can kill people and destroy cities’

May 29, 2015 Second Coming Watch, zTransfer-UCN

Professor Kim Heung-Kwang says hackers in Korea are a potentially deadly threat (AFP)

Hackers in North Korea are capable of launching attacks that could kill people and destroy cities, a defector has warned.

Professor Kim Heung-Kwang, who escaped the country in 2004 after teaching computer science at Hamheung Computer Technology University for 20 years, claims the regime has trained more than 6,000 hackers.

While Heung-Kwang did not teach the lethal techniques himself, many of his former students have gone on to form North Korea’s notorious hacking unit Bureau 121.

And the team, credited with many attacks made against South Korean banks and power plants, could even have the ability to kill.

Professor Heung-Kwang told the BBC: “The size of the cyber-attack agency has increased significantly, and now has approximately 6,000 people.

“The reason North Korea has been harassing other countries is to demonstrate that North Korea has cyber war capacity.

“Their cyber-attacks could have similar impacts as military attacks, killing people and destroying cities.”
