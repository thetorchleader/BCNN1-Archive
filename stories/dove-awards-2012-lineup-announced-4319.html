Dove Awards 2012 Lineup Announced

March 9, 2012 Uncategorized

The 43rd Annual Gospel Music Association’s Dove Awards has announced this year’s performance lineup on Thursday.

Francesca Battistelli and Chris August win Female Vocalist of the Year and Male Vocalist of the Year at the 42nd annual Dove Awards, Wednesday, April 20, 2011, in Atlanta.
The star-studded event will see top musical artists such as Lecrae, Natalie Grant, and Donnie McClurkin take the stage.
Audiences will enjoy a performance by “Adore Him” singer and two-time Dove Award-winner Kari Jobe as well.
Also announced Thursday, Jason Crabb, Jamie-Grace, Kim Burrell, and The Isaacs were revealed as the first round of artists confirmed to perform live during the ceremony.
“American Idol” break-out stars Danny Gokey, Ruben Studdard, Mandisa, and Jason Castro will also contribute their voices to the event.
The event’s hosts have also been revealed as Daytime Emmy-nominated Chonda Pierce and comic writer David Mann.
Earlier this year, GMA and presenters such as Montell Jordan and Chonda Pierce announced the Dove Awards nominees of 2012.
Some of the awards and their nominees are as follows.
Nominated for Song of the Year are artists Natalie Grant, Laura Story, the Perrys, Casting Crowns, and Jamie Grace, among others.
Up for Female Vocalist of the Year are Francesca Battistelli, Kari Jobe, Laura Story, Mandisa, and Natalie Grant.
