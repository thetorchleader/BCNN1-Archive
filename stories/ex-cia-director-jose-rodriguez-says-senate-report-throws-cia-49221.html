Ex-CIA Director Jose Rodriguez Says Senate Report Throws CIA ‘Under the Bus’

December 15, 2014 National

Ex-CIA interrogation director Jose Rodriguez says the Senate Intelligence Committee’s report throws the CIA “under the bus.”

Rodriguez said it could create many problems for agents in the future, including distancing allies who cooperated in the past.

Former Vice President Dick Cheney told NBC’s “Meet the Press” there’s no comparison between CIA interrogation methods and the 9/11 terror attacks.

“Torture is what al Qaeda did,” Cheney said. “There is no comparison between that and what we did with respect to enhanced interrogation.”
