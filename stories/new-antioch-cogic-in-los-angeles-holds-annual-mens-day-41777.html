New Antioch COGIC in Los Angeles Holds Annual Men’s Day

October 26, 2014 Black

Pastor Jeffrey M. Lewis

The Men’s Fellowship of New Antioch Church of God in Christ will host its Annual Men’s Day worship services on Sunday, October 26, at the 8 a.m. and 11 a.m.

Under the leadership of President Charles Freeman, Men’s Day will celebrate from the theme, ‘Clear Vision, Clean Hands, Constant Faith.’  The theme scripture is Matthew 7:7.

The guest speaker is Elder Robert Booker, the Southern California Region Five President of the Evangelism Department of the Church of God in Christ and Director of Pentecost Ministries, Inc.

“Come prepared for a time of worship.  Annual Men’s Day promises to be an anointed time that empowers men to stand up and take their rightful place in the church, home and the community in order to be the leaders God intended for them to be,” said Superintendent Jeffrey M. Lewis, pastor.
