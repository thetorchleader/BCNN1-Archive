New Mount Calvary Church Honors Its Pastor

January 25, 2015 Black

The members of New Mount Calvary Baptist Church (NMC) will host four services as part of their 4th Pastoral Appreciation and Birthday Celebration for the Rev. Dr. Sonja R. Dawson.

A birthday concert on Friday, January 23, at 7 p.m., will kick-off the festivities.  Worship Pastor Jovita Sheppard of Transformation Church Greenwood in Tulsa, OK, will headline the program.

NMC Minister Dawnesha Beaver said, “As a Christian music artist, Pastor Sheppard’s music transcends across generations and cultures to create a unique sound that pushes listeners into the presence of God.”
