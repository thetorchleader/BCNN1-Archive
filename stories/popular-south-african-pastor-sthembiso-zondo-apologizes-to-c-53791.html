Popular South African Pastor, Sthembiso Zondo, Apologizes to Church After Naked Video Surfaces

January 27, 2015 Eye on Africa

Pastor Zondo. Picture: facebook

Pastor Zondo publicly apologised to his congregation on Sunday after a video of him walking around naked surfaced and went viral last week.

The pastor, who gives motivational talks on Ukhozi FM, was cheerfully welcomed by his congregation at his Zero to Hero church in Durban.

The service was also attended by Ukhozi FM presenters DJ Sgqemeza and Irvin Sihlophe .

“To those that have my video; keep it, do not delete it, so that when you experience something painful and difficult, you can remember that a man was shown naked to the whole country but did not lose hope and carried on with life,” Zondo said.

In the video, which was shared across social media, Zondo is seen walking around naked in the lounge area of a house and speaking on the phone while touching his genitals.

The video was taken by an unidentified female.

“I have forgiven the person who shot the video and all those involved. I apologise to Ukhozi FM, fellow pastors who listen to my show as well as all the people of South Africa for the video,” he said.
