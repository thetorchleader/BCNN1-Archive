See, We Told You So: A Female ESPN Reporter Who Meant No Harm Can’t Even do a Report on Homosexual Football Player, Michael Sam, Without the Entire ESPN Organization Apologizing. Wow!

August 29, 2014 Sports

A report (above) on SportsCenter Tuesday featured a discussion of Michael Sam’s showering habits; specifically a defensive player told Josina Anderson he believed Sam was purposely avoiding showering with other members of the team.

It went over about as well as you’d expect — Rams defensive end Chris Long fired a 140-character missile across the network’s bow — and on Wednesday ESPN apologized.

“ESPN regrets the manner in which we presented our report,” the network said in a statement. “Clearly yesterday we collectively failed to meet the standards we have set in reporting on LGBT-related topics in sports.”

Love the “standards we have set” line there. It’s like the back-handed compliment version of an apology; a very slick PR move.
