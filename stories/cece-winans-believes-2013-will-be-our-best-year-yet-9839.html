CeCe Winans Believes '2013 Will Be Our Best Year Yet!'

January 1, 2013 Uncategorized

It’s New Year’s Eve, which is always an exciting time because we’re on the brink of new beginnings. Award-winning gospel recording artist, CeCe Winans, is certainly approaching the incoming year with an optimistic outlook. “2013 will be our best year yet!” she tweeted.
Winans, who just returned home from ministering in Manchester, England, is also reflecting back on 2012, calling it “an awesome year,” though it had its challenges and came along with some major blows.
In February, Winans lost her longtime friend Whitney Houston and told the press, “I just feel bad I wasn’t there for her this time.”
