Arizona Mother Who Left Kids in Hot Car Pleads Guilty to Child Abuse

March 16, 2015 National

Shanesha Taylor pleaded guilty to one count of child abuse Monday, March 16, 2015, stemming from her arrest in March 2014 for leaving her two children in a hot car while she interviewed for a job. An initial deal was rescinded after Taylor failed to deposit money into a trust fund for her children. (PHOTO CREDIT: Charlie Leight, The Arizona Republic)

The Arizona woman who left her two young children in a parked hot car during a job interview last year, pleaded guilty to one count of child abuse Monday, a court official said.

Shanesha Taylor, 36, of Phoenix, had been set to begin trial March 31.

She pleaded guilty to one count of child abuse, a class 6 felony and domestic-violence offense, in exchange for prosecutors’ dismissal of a second count of child abuse, Maricopa County Attorney’s Office spokesman Jerry Cobb said.

In July, Taylor reached a plea deal in which prosecutors agreed to dismiss the child-abuse charges she faced if she successfully completed parenting and substance-abuse classes and establish education and child-care trusts for each of her three children.

The Maricopa County Attorney’s Office reinstated charges against Taylor in October after she failed to deposit $60,000 in a trust fund for her children as part of the agreement.

Her arrest last year and her widely publicized, tearful mugshot won the sympathy of 4,000 supporters who donated more than $114,000 via a viral Internet campaign to get Taylor back on her feet.

Taylor has since said the trust has been established and funded, but would not say how much money had been deposited.
