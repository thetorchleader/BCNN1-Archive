Mary J. Blige Pays Off Nearly $1 Million Tax Debt

May 26, 2014 Entertainment

Singer Mary J. Blige has settled a large portion of her huge tax debt.

Blige was hit with a series of money woes in 2013 including accusations she owed the state of New Jersey more than $900,000 in back taxes. However, new legal documents, seen by editors at TMZ, show the $901,770 tax debt has now been cleared.

The singer reportedly still owes $3.4 million Internal Revenue Service for income taxes she failed to pay on earnings between 2009 and 2011.

Last year, Blige was also sued over claims she had failed to pay back a number of loans.
