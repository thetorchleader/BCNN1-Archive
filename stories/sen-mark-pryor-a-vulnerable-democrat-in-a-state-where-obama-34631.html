Sen. Mark Pryor, a Vulnerable Democrat in a State Where Obama is Unpopular, Highlights his Vote for Obamacare

August 20, 2014 Politics

Sen. Mark Pryor, D-Ark. (The El Dorado Times via AP)

Sen. Mark Pryor’s latest ad in Arkansas touts his support for the Affordable Care Act, in a rare campaign spot on the topic by a vulnerable Democrat in a red state where President Obama is unpopular.

The commercial features Pryor’s father, former U.S. senator and Arkansas governor David Pryor, talking about how his son’s insurance company didn’t want to pay for treatment that Mark needed when he battled a rare cancer 18 years ago.

“No one should be fighting an insurance company while you’re fighting for your life,” Mark Pryor says in the 30-second spot. “That’s why I helped pass a law that prevents insurance companies from canceling your policy if you get sick, or deny coverage for pre-existing conditions.”

Sen. Pryor never mentions the Affordable Care Act by name or refers to it as Obamacare, its more commonly known moniker. But there’s no mistaking what health care law Pryor is talking about. The Democrat has been criticized by his GOP rival, Rep. Tom Cotton, and groups such as Americans for Prosperity for voting for the law in 2009.
