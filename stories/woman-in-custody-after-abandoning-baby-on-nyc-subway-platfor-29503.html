Woman in Custody After Abandoning Baby on NYC Subway Platform

Date not recorded in the original archive Black, National

The baby left on the subway platform at Columbus Circle around noon on Monday. (Credit: New York Police Department)

A woman believed to be the mother of a baby girl abandoned in a Manhattan subway station on Monday was found early Tuesday morning, the police said.

“A little after midnight, a good Samaritan recognized her based on the video footage we had released an hour earlier, and brought that to the attention of a police officer,” on Broadway near 72nd Street, a spokesman for the police said Tuesday morning.

The woman was captured on camera on Monday at around noon as she pushed a polka-dotted stroller onto the platform of the Columbus Circle station. Moments later, the police said, she stepped back into the train alone, leaving a 6- or 7-month-old girl behind. On Monday night, the police released pictures of the child and asked for the public’s help in identifying her. They later released a video of the woman.

After she was taken into custody, the woman, whose name has not been released, made statements that implicated her as the mother of the child, the police said. No charges had been filed against her Tuesday morning.

“She’s in custody. She is not arrested right now,” said the police spokesman.

The woman had not been taken to a hospital for any kind of medical or psychological evaluation, the spokesman added.

The baby was unharmed and in stable condition at St. Luke’s-Roosevelt Hospital Center on Monday night. She was in the custody of the city’s Administration for Children’s Services awaiting placement, a spokesman for the agency said.
