Jay Z and Beyonce Reportedly Buy Converted Church Building in New Orleans

May 10, 2015 Entertainment

BUYERS: (allegedly) Beyoncé and Jay Z
LOCATION: New Orleans, Louisiana
PRICE: (last listing) $2.6 million
SIZE: 13,292-square feet with multiple units

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie may want to dump their historic mansion in the Big Easy’s French Quarter, but the kids at Curbed hear that entertainment industry superstars Jay Z and Beyoncé just might have shelled out a few million clams for the so-called La Casa de Castille, a hulking, 13,292-plus-square-foot Spanish Baroque-style converted church mansion in the heart of New Orleans’ Garden District.

The former house of worship, built in the 1920s as the Westminster Presbyterian Church and later the home of Ballet Hysell, was first put on the open market in September 2013 at $2.5 million. The price jumped the following October to $2.6 million, and the sale price appears to have been suppressed or removed from every property record database we know to look. (For the record, the kids at Curbed discovered — as we later did — that the property was quietly sold in early January to a corporate entity that links back to a Los Angeles address associated with Parkwood Entertainment, Beyoncé’s management company.)

There are actually four self-contained apartments within the hulking structure, including a mansion-sized owner’s residence of more than 9,800 square feet that encompasses the long-ago desanctified sanctuary and has four bedrooms and four full and two half bathrooms. Ceilings soar to 26 feet in the main living space, where the walls are lined with towering arched windows. The altar is now the dining room, and the master suite, replete with sitting area, two-room bath and a dressing area, is located on the third level in what was once the choir loft. Three one bedrooms apartments of around 1,000 square feet apiece are located on the ground floor, have separate side-street access and retain some original architectural details. There does not appear to be a great deal of outdoor space besides a hedged-in front garden and a green roof with trellised deck and meandering pathways.
