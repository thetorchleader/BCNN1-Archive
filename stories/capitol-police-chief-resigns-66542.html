Capitol Police Chief Resigns

April 10, 2015 National

U.S. Capitol Police Chief Kim Dine address the media regarding a police chase and shooting, on Capitol Hill in Washington, Thursday, Oct. 3, 2013. (AP Photo/Molly Riley)

United States Capitol Police Chief Kim Dine has resigned after more than two years in the position.

Dine submitted his letter of resignation Friday afternoon. It is not yet clear when his resignation will take effect.

Dine was sworn in to the position in December 2012. Dine led the Frederick Police Department for 10 years before taking the helm with the Capitol Police.

The resignation comes amid mounting frustration within the Capitol Police force, reports WTOP’s Dave McConnell.

Capitol Police refused to get involved in a high-speed chase near the capitol from Prince George’s County the night of the State of the Union in February.

There was also blowback when officers  who were sent to the 2013 Navy Yard shooting were called back.

Neither Dine nor a spokesperson for the Capitol Police have commented on the resignation yet.
