WATCH: Yolanda Adams Gives Steller Performance of Lionel Richie’s “Jesus Is Love” at 2014 BET Awards

Date not recorded in the original archive Entertainment

The BET Awards has a not-so-great reputation for being wild, vile and in today’s lingo, straight up ratchet. The N-word, B-word, lots of other profanity and vulgarity is expected.

Enter Yolanda Adams. This year, God used this woman to shift the atmosphere and the conversation in the most unexpected way.

The 52-year-old Grammy-winning gospel singer was trending worldwide on Twitter for hours after her BET Awards performance.

Though the respected vocalist was paying tribute to singer-songwriter Lionel Richie, 65, the real star of the show—Jesus—stole the show during her rendition of “Jesus Is Love.”

The atmosphere was electric, leaving everyone stunned and turning many people’s attention toward the supernatural man Adams so beautifully sang about.
