Rev. Run and Tyrese Gibson Have a New Book on Men

January 2, 2013 Uncategorized

Oh yes. Ladies, the book we’ve all been waiting for. “Manology: Secrets of Your Man’s Mind Revealed.”
Though we sometimes think we’ve got the simple man figured out – you know, he’s like a puppy – feed him, pet him, give him commands, and he’s happy.
But Tyrese and Rev. Run say it’s a bit more complex than that.
In “Manology,” the artists and friends, who come from different relationship backgrounds – one is married, the other is a single dad looking for some action, answer all the burning questions women have about their man.
