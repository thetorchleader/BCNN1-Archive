WATCH: Duck Dynasty’s Phil Robertson Rebukes Boehner in CPAC Speech

March 1, 2015 National

“Duck Dynasty” patriarch Phil Robertson was at CPAC to accept a Defender of the First Amendment award, and during his acceptance speech took a few swipes at Republican Speaker of the House John Boehner.

“By the way, these are my church clothes,” said Robertson, who was dressed like, well, someone from “Duck Dynasty.” “I never got around to buying a suit, not yet. I’m 68.”
