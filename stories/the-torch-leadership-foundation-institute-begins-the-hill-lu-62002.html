The Torch Leadership Foundation Institute Begins the Hill-Lutzer-McKissic Lecture Series on Why the Church Must Stand Against Homosexuality and Homosexual Marriage

March 16, 2015 Christian

ATLANTA, GA, (March 16, 2015) – In response to the National Baptist Convention affiliated, American Baptist College, in Nashville, Tennessee, inviting a lesbian minister and two pro-homosexual pastors to speak at the Garnett-Nabrit Lecture Series, the Torch Leadership Foundation Institute has launched the “Hill-Lutzer-McKissic Lecture Series.” This Lecture Series is in honor of E.V. Hill, Erwin Lutzer, and Dwight McKissic for standing for God, truth and righteousness down through the years without compromise.  

Daniel Whyte III, president of Gospel Light Society International created this Lecture Series primarily to inform the Christian church about why it must stand against homosexuality, homosexual marriage, homosexual parenting, and the homosexual agenda. Whyte believes, “The normalization of homosexuality and everything that pertains to it is probably the greatest danger facing our world today. Thus, it is imperative that the body of Christ choose to stop ignoring this prevailing issue and stand up for what God says on this matter before it completely destroys our country.” The Hill-Lutzer-McKissic Lecture Series is designed to equip pastors, church leaders, and Christians everywhere to take a firm stand for God against homosexuality and the homosexual agenda, in the spirit of love, grace and truth, so that Christians will not be responsible for allowing this nation to implode on our watch.

The Theology on Homosexuality and The Hill-Lutzer-McKissic Lecture Series can be heard and seen online at www.TorchLeadershipFoundation.com at 1:00 PM, central time today, Monday, March 16th, tomorrow, Tuesday, March 17th, and on Wednesday, March 18th. It can also be heard every Tuesday going forward. The videos for each lecture will also be available on Youtube, Vimeo, and Godtube.

The Torch Leadership Foundation is a research and leadership think tank whose mission is to promote traditional values in the public square. Our views are informed by Scripture, the perspectives of the American founding fathers, and the outcome of reliable public surveys that reveal the opinions and expectations of the American public. Our goal is to inform and train leaders (particularly young leaders) who will move the country forward in a direction that is consistent with America being “a shining city upon a hill.”
