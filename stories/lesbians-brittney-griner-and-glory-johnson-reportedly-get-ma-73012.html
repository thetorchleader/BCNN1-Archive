Lesbians Brittney Griner and Glory Johnson Reportedly Get Married in Arizona

May 11, 2015 Sports

WNBA star Brittney Griner is officially off the market.

The two-time WNBA All-Star for the Phoenix Mercury tied the knot with Glory Johnson (a forward with the Tulsa Shock) Friday afternoon in Phoenix, the couple confirms to PEOPLE. The 24-year-old brides said their vows beneath an archway at the Pointe Hilton Tapatio Cliffs Resort in front of approximately 75 guests.

After the ceremony, the newlyweds celebrated with a dinner reception and enjoyed a tiered cake that combined Griner’s favorite, white cake, with Johnson’s favorite, red velvet. The couples first dance was to Ed Sheeran’s “Thinking Out Loud.”
