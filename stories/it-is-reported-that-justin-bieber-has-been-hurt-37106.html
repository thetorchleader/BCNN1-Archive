It Is Reported that Justin Bieber Has Been Hurt

September 4, 2014 Entertainment

Justin Bieber
(Photo by Ari Perilstein/Getty Images for Variety)

Troubled pop star Justin Bieber was reportedly hospitalized in his native Canada on Wednesday to treat a wrist injury. 

The singer was admitted to Stratford General Hospital in Stratford, Ontario, where doctors examined the star’s arm and diagnosed a sprain, according to TMZ.com.

The hospitalization comes just days after Bieber’s dramatic arrest in the area on Friday when he clashed with another driver following a car accident.

Bieber, who had been driving with his on-and-off girlfriend Selena Gomez at the time of the drama, was detained by police and charged with dangerous driving and assault.
