Missing Actress Misty Upham’s Body Reportedly Found

October 16, 2014 Entertainment

Actress Misty Upham, best known for her roles in “Frozen River” and “August: Osage County.”
(Photo: Jason LaVeris, FilmMagic)

Police in Washington state believe they have found the body of missing actress Misty Upham, according to multiple reports including The Seattle Times.

The Times quoted police spokesman Steve Stocker saying there was a “good chance” the body found near the White River in Auburn, Washington was Upham. It was found over a cliff in a wooded area.

No official identification or determination of gender has been made.

Upham, who is known for her roles in August: Osage County and Frozen River was reported missing by her parents on Oct. 6.

Upham’s father, Charles Upham, told KIRO-FM this week that his daughter was upset and erratic and had stopped taking medication for anxiety and bipolar disorder. He also told the station that he didn’t believe his daughter would kill herself.

The 32-year-old Native American actress has been listed as missing in the Washington Crime Information Center database.

The family said Upham had moved to the Seattle area to help care for her father, who’s recovering from a stroke.
