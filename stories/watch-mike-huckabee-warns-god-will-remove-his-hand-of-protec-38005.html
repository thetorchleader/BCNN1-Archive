WATCH: Mike Huckabee Warns: “God Will Remove His Hand” of Protection from America If We Don’t Stand With Israel

October 7, 2014 Christian

Mike Huckabee, former Arkansas governor and host of Fox News’ “Huckabee,” speaking at the Values Voters Summit at the Omni Shoreham Hotel in Washington, Friday, September 26, 2014.
(PHOTO: FAMILY RESEARCH COUNCIL/CARRIE RUSSELL)

During a rally in support of Israel in Washington DC on Sunday, Mike Huckabee warned that God will remove his hand of protection from America if the United States does not stand with Israel. “If America doesn’t stand with Israel when those who seek to attack it get close to destroying it, then I do believe this: God will remove His hand from us.” Huckabee made the comments at an event he sponsored along with Concerned Women For America.

Watch the video below along with a partial transcription.

http://www.cbn.com/media/player/embedplayer.aspx?bcid=3823742863001&width=320&height=180

Mike Huckabee at Stand With Israel rally: “If Israel falls, if we stand back and allow them to be attacked, to be destroyed, and by the way I don’t really worry that that is going to happen because I do believe that before that were to happen, God’s hand would intervene and the world will see that God will keep His promise to Israel, but if America doesn’t stand with Israel when those who seek to attack it get close to destroying it, then I do believe this: God will remove His hand from us.”
