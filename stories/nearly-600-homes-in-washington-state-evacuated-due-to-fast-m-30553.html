Nearly 600 Homes in Washington State Evacuated due to Fast-Moving Wildfire

July 17, 2014 National

In this July 9, 2014 photo, smoke and fire blow through the lower Entiat Valley near Entiat, Wash.
(Photo: AP/Don Seabrook, The Wenatchee World)

About 600 homes near Leavenworth have received a Level 3 evacuation notice, instructing residents to leave immediately, due to a fast-growing wildfire.

Residents were told to remember the six “P’s” — people, pets, prescriptions, important papers, photos and personal computer.

The Red Cross will open a shelter at the First Baptist Church in Leavenworth, Chelan County Emergency Management said.

U.S. Highway 2 was closed at Coles Corner to just west of Leavenworth on Wednesday afternoon. CCEM said it’s not known how long the road will be closed.

A massive plume of smoke from the Chiwaukum Creek Fire could be seen from Seattle on Wednesday.
