1-Year-Old Baby Dies in Hospital After Mother Throws him Off Bridge and Tries to Commit Suicide

May 10, 2015 National

Zymeir Perry (left) has died six days after his mother Johnesha Perry, 19, allegedly threw him into a river from a bridge in Allentown, Pennsylvania

A one-year-old boy who was allegedly thrown into a river by his young mother who then tried to kill herself moments later has died in hospital.

Zymeir Perry was found 700 yards downstream from the Hamilton Street Bridge in Allentown, Pennsylvania, last Sunday after the failed murder-suicide bid.

Johnesha Monae Perry, 19, kissed the boy, pushed him over the rail and then jumped in, authorities have said.

The pair both survived the initial fall, but young Zymeir passed away after fighting for his life in hospital for six days.

Perry was charged with attempted homicide, endangering the welfare of a child and aggravated assault on Monday. The charges have not been upgraded since the boy’s death.

She was arraigned from her hospital bed and didn’t have an attorney. The teenager is due in court on Monday for a preliminary hearing.

Allentown police Capt. William Reinik told local reporters that Perry was competent at the time of her arrest and knew what she was doing.

She remains under the guard of sheriff’s deputies at Lehigh Valley Hospital.

Her bail was set at $100,000.

Witnesses claim the woman took her child out of the stroller then threw him over the bridge. She then climbed over the railing and threw herself in as well.

The baby was found floating in the Lehigh River towards the town of Bethlehem. He had been in the water five minutes before officers jumped in to save him.

Capt. Renik commended the officers that went to help the baby.

Allentown police officer Joseph Ianetta pulled the child out and performed mouth-to-mouth resuscitation, while a colleague performed CPR, it was reported by the Morning Call of Allentown.

‘I commend these two officers for actually going into the river and pulling him out and doing CPR,’ Capt. Renik said.

Perry also survived the 52ft fall and was able to walk out of the water before collapsing under the bridge.

A number of suicides have been recorded at the Hamilton Street Bridge over the years.
