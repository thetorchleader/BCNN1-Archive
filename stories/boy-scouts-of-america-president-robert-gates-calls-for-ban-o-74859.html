Boy Scouts of America President, Robert Gates, Calls for Ban on Homosexual Leaders to End – What a Tragedy and a Travesty!

May 22, 2015 National

Former Defense Secretary Robert Gates addressing the Boy Scouts of America’s annual meeting in 2014 after being selected as the organization’s new president. (AP/Mark Zaleski, File)

The Boy Scouts of America will no longer seek to revoke the charters of chapters that accept gay adult leaders, the organization’s president, Robert Gates, told its national leadership on Thursday.

“The status quo in our movement’s membership standards cannot be sustained,” Gates said during the BSA’s annual national meeting in Atlanta.

The organization took no formal action on its current policy to ban gay adult leaders, but Gates said the group can no longer “ignore growing internal challenges to our current membership policy.”

“Nor can we ignore the social, political and juridical changes taking place in our country – changes taking place at a pace over this past year no one anticipated,” said Gates, a former U.S. secretary of defense. “We must deal with the world as it is, not as we might wish it to be.”

Gates’ remarks were welcome news to those who have been challenging the Scouts’ longstanding anti-gay policies.

“While our work won’t be done until we see a full end to their ban on gay adults once and for all, today’s announcement is a significant step in that direction,” said Scouts for Equality executive director Zach Wahls. “I’m proud to see Dr. Gates charting a course towards full equality in the BSA.”
