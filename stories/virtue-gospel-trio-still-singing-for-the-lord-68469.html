Virtue – Gospel Trio Still Singing for the Lord

April 19, 2015 Black

Virtue is comprised of sisters (from left) Ebony Holland, Karima Kibble and Heather Martin.

Dark times appear brighter after listening to the music of Virtue, three sisters who sing with power for Christ.

The contemporary gospel trio began in the industry 22 years ago and garnered a Dove award and Stellar and Grammy nominations during their journey.  After taking an eight-year hiatus to get married and raise families, they are back with the album, ‘Testimony Reloaded’ featuring two new tracks.

“We recorded ‘Testimony’ in 2006 and it was off the market for a long time.  Our new company got the rights and decided to re-release it with our new singles, ‘You Are’ and ‘You Deserve’” said Ebony Holland.

Encouraging music lovers to listen, Karima Kibble insisted, “We worked really hard on it.  The song, ‘You Are,’ when we first heard it, we knew that it blessed us.  If it blesses us first, then it’s going to bless others indeed.

“The song talks about how God is everything and you can’t do anything without Him. We just have to keep Him out front in our lives so we know that He is [everything].”

Heather Martin noted, “I think our music has a way of reaching people, even those who don’t go to church at all.  A lot of young people are looking to relate to something that is real.  There are so many songs about shaking your bottom, stuff that is probably fun. But, when times are hard and you need to know how to get to the bottom of your problem, I think the songs that we sing help you get through life.

“If you want something real, find answers and some solace and peace, I believe that the words and music that we sing, God has given it to us directly for a blessed people for such a time as this,” she said.

Their strong relationship with God had been present throughout their lives.  Growing up in New Orleans, Louisiana, the sisters’ mother was deeply spiritual and a vocalist as well.  She taught her daughters to sing and they often performed at local churches.

“We are three blessed sisters.  We grew up singing and it just never left us,” said Martin.

When they face challenging times, the sisters refer to their favorite scriptures to guide them through obstacles in life.
