Ex of Scandal’s Columbus Short, Agostina Laneri, Says He ‘Threatened to Kill Actor Jamie Foxx’ While at the Same Time She Is Getting a Restraining Order

February 3, 2015 Entertainment

Acrimonious: Columbus Short’s ex-girlfriend Agostina Laneri has said the Scandal actor ‘threatened to kill Jamie Foxx’

He’s had his fair share of problems of late and now Columbus Short’s ex-girlfriend Agostina Laneri has claimed the Scandal actor ‘threatened to kill Jamie Foxx.’

According to TMZ, Laneri got a restraining order against her ex-boyfriend last week, after claiming he was emotionally abusive to her and physically abusive to her child.

She also claims that Columbus told her, ‘If I choose to kill one of these n****s … I will do that. I’m that crazy n**** that will be on the news as the n**** that murked [murdered] Jamie Foxx.’

Agostina also claims Columbus hit her child, justifying it by saying the boy needed to learn, adding he once told her ‘Don’t forget who I am. I have a knife.’

The site reports that the star has been ordered to stay 100 yards clear of her and her son. MailOnline has contacted a spokesperson for Columbus Short for comment.

The actor recently revealed he has struggled with drug and alcohol use.
