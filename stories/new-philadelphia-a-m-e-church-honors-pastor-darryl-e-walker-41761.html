New Philadelphia A.M.E. Church Honors Pastor Darryl E. Walker for 40 Years of Ministry

October 26, 2014 Black

Pastor Darryl E. Walker

The congregation of New Philadelphia A.M.E. Church will celebrate the 60th birthday and the 40th year of ministry of Pastor Darryl E. Walker. 

The salute begins with a luncheon on Saturday, October 18, at the Marriott Hotel in Long Beach.  Ministers, family and friends from across the country will attend to join members at the milestone celebration.

Also, the public is invited to attend the worship on Sunday, October 19, at 9 a.m., in the edifice at New Philadelphia is located at 19100 South Susana Road in Rancho Dominguez.  The Rev. Benjamin Thomas, pastor of Tanner Chapel A.M.E. Church in Phoenix, AZ, will be the guest preacher.  Only one service will be held on this day.

The Rev. Walker was ordained in the A.M.E. Church as an Itinerant Deacon in 1974 and as an Itinerant Elder in 1977 in the A.M.E. Church.  Prior to current appointment, he served as pastor of Mount Olive A.M.E. in Emporia, KS; Allen Chapel A.M.E. in Cheyenne, WY; Ebenezer A.M.E. in Bremerton, WA; Parks Chapel A.M.E. in Los Angeles, St. John A.M.E. in Omaha, NE; Allen A.M.E. in Tacoma, WA; Ebenezer A.M.E. in Kansas City, MO; and Turner Memorial A.M.E. in Hyattsville, MD.
