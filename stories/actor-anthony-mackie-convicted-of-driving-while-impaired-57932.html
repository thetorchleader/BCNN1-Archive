Actor Anthony Mackie Convicted of Driving While Impaired

February 21, 2015 Entertainment

Convicted: Actor Anthony Mackie, who appears in Hurt Locker and the Captain America franchise, was found guilty of DWI.

Captain America: The Winter Soldier actor Anthony Mackie has been ordered to undertake a drunk driving program after he was convicted of Driving While Impaired on Friday.

The 36-year-old star was arrested back in November 2013 after he was initially stopped by police in Harlem in the early hours of the morning for having tinted windows, which is illegal in New York.

In addition Mackie, who also appeared in The Hurt Locker and 8 Mile, was fined $300 and banned from the road for 90 days in New York state for the violation.

Mackie was convicted of DWI after a two day long, non jury hearing at the Manhattan Criminal Court which took testimony from several law enforcement officers.

He was suspected of being under the influence because he smelled of alcohol and had bloodshot, watery eyes, police stated.

According to a report on the proceedings by the New York Daily News, the star refused to undergo a breathalyzer or co-ordination test once at the station.

He chose not to testify at the trial but his lawyer, Mike Miller,unsuccessfully argued that his client’s eyes were red after taking antihistamines for a cold.

Mr Miller argued that it was: ‘abundantly clear that the people have not met their burden’ of proof to convict Mackie of driving while impaired.

He pointed to police video which reportedly shows Mackie appearing sober after his arrest.
