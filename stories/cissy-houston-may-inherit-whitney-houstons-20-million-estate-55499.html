Cissy Houston May Inherit Whitney Houston’s $20 Million Estate if Bobbi Kristina Dies

February 6, 2015 Entertainment

Bobbi Kristina Brown was found unresponsive at home in Atlanta in a scenario close to her mother’s demise.
(Photo: Dan Steinberg, AP)

Whitney Houston left a $20 million fortune to her daughter, but if Bobbi Kristina doesn’t recover, family matriarch Cissy Houston and her sons are next in line, the Daily News has learned.

Bobbi Kristina Brown was her 48-year-old mother’s sole heir — but the bulk of the money is in a trust until she turns 30. She got 10%, roughly $2 million, this year when she turned 21.

Another 15% is to be distributed at age 25 and the remainder at 30.

But according to the will’s terms, Cissy Houston and Whitney Houston’s two brothers inherit the estate if Bobbi Kristina dies before coming into her majority.

Bobbi Kristina’s dad, Bobby Brown, 46, was also originally included as a beneficiary in the event of Whitney Houston’s early death. But his 2007 divorce from Houston nullified his claim, said Atlanta legal expert Bruce Gaynes.

But a showdown might loom between Brown and Nick Gordon, 25, who claims he and Bobbi Kristina are married.
