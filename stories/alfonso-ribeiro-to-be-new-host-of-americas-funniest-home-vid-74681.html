Alfonso Ribeiro to be New Host of ‘America’s Funniest Home Videos’

May 20, 2015 Entertainment

Alfonso Ribeiro is pictured on May 14, 2015 in Beverly Hills, Calif. (PHOTO CREDIT: Paul Archuleta/Getty Images)

Talk about keeping it in the family.

“America’s Funniest Home Videos” has chosen its new host and it’s “Dancing With the Stars” champ Alfonso Ribeiro. He’ll take over next season for ‘DWTS’ host Tom Beregron.

Ribeiro, 43, will begin as host in the fall, according to Entertainment Weekly. Beregron hosted the show for more than a decade.

“It won’t be very different,” the “Fresh Prince” icon told EW. “It’s really about assisting the videos not trying to overpower the videos with your comedy. But I have a different energy than Tom does and I’m very animated and energetic, and some of that will absolutely come through. I’m sure it will be dance-related in some form…. For me to able to come in and replace Tom, who’s my idol in the hosting world, will be a challenge but one that I feel excited to be a part of.”
