President Obama Calls Jimmie Johnson the ‘Michael Jordan of NASCAR’

June 26, 2014 Sports

(Photo: Geoff Burke, USA TODAY Sports)

Jimmie Johnson chases no less than the legends of stock car racing on the speedways and racetracks across America most weekends.

Second in the NASCAR Sprint Cup standings after winning three of the past five races, Johnson is in position to win his seventh Cup title in nine years. If he does, he will tie Richard Petty and Dale Earnhardt for the most Cup titles.

Among this esteemed company, Johnson has President Obama’s vote. “He’s the best there ever was,” Obama said Wednesday, when the six-time champion and his No. 48 Hendrick Motorsports team were honored at the White House for winning the 2013 title.

“Now, everybody knows I’m a Chicago guy,” Obama said, “and usually when we do these sports events I make some crack about how the football (team) is not as good as the ’85 Bears or the basketball team is not as good as the Bulls, but today I can’t really say anything, because Jimmie Johnson is pretty much the Michael Jordan of NASCAR.”
