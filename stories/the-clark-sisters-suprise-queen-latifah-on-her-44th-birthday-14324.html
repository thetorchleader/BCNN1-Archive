The Clark Sisters Suprise Queen Latifah on her 44th Birthday

March 17, 2014 Uncategorized

Queen Latifah and The Clark Sisters

Queen Latifah’s 44th birthday is tomorrow, and it will be celebrated with several surprises on her daytime talk show.

First, the show’s entire staff came on stage to surprise her with a birthday cake.

But that was topped when she was blindsided by gospel group The Clark Sisters, armed with one of her all-time favorite songs, “You Brought the Sunshine”.
