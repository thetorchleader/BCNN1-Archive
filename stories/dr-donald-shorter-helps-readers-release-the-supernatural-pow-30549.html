Dr. Donald Shorter Helps Readers Release the Supernatural Power of God in Their Lives in Book, The Power In You

July 17, 2014 Books

We all face difficult circumstances in life, adversities that we wish would pass quickly. Because we live in an imperfect world, such unfortunate things are inevitable. From time to time, we will be forced to work through what we wish we didn’t have to, deal with people that we don’t particularly like, and handle bad news when we least expect it. How do we face hard times? How do we overcome stumbling blocks in the road?In the pages of The Power In You, Dr. Donald Shorter shows readers how to release the supernatural power of God to work in, through, and for you. God is a victor and He created His people to be victorious no matter what circumstance or situation they face. Readers will discover:

  • The amount of God’s power available to you
  • Common hindrances to releasing the power of God
  • Steps to allowing the power of God to operate in your life
  • Your authority over the devil’s schemes
  • Much, much more

The Power In You will help Christians realize what they have inside them and take steps to activate it. In doing so, Christians can learn to take control over their lives and their circumstances to live powerfully and victoriously through Jesus Christ.

Donald Shorter Sr., is the author of several books including: The Power In You, Winning Against All Odds, Casting Down Imaginations, and Take Control Of Your Thoughts. He is also the owner of several businesses and a commercial airplane and helicopter pilot. He is known as a visionary leader of business and a passionate leader of ministry both regionally and worldwide. He is also the founder of several nonprofit (CDC agencies) including: Future Aviators Association, Northco Development Inc., as well as the Excellence In Business seminars. He and his wife Kathy hosts the weekly television program “Don And Kathy Live!” seen worldwide. He is also the Founding Pastor of Pacific Christian Center. Dr. Shorter is a pastor, teacher and apostle, ordained into the ministry by Apostle Frederick K. C. Price of Crenshaw Christian Center in Los Angeles, California. He and his wife Kathy are celebrating 40 years of marriage as of July 2014.
Donald Shorter has over 30 years of professional broadcasting and public speaking experience. As a seasoned broadcaster he worked for an ABC Network affiliates for Radio and Television, and over 20 other broadcasting stations. He has spoken to millions of people in various venues. Dr. Shorter has spoken to thousands of audiences for corporate training sessions at U.S. Military training facilities, international conferences, ministry meetings and state and local engagements involving varying subjects. Dr. Shorter is well known for delivering problem solving solutions on how to win against personal life challenges in marriages, professional life and with corporate challenges.
Dr. Shorter is a sought after speaker for ministry conferences, corporations, and for corporate business training seminars. He and his wife Dr. Kathy L. Shorter also ministers in marriage and family training seminars and conferences. They have a passion to help individuals and families of all backgrounds to learn how to win in every area of life.
For more information about Dr. Donald Shorter, visit www.DonAndKathyLive.com and www.PacChurch.org.
