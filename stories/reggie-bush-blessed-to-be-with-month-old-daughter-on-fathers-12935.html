Reggie Bush ‘Blessed’ to Be With Month-Old Daughter on Father’s Day

June 17, 2013 Uncategorized

While Kim Kardashian and Kanye West were welcoming their daughter into the world, Kardashian’s ex, NFL star Reggie Bush, was celebrating his own bundle of joy.
The 28-year-old Detroit Lions running back posted a cute Father’s Day photo of him holding his month-old daughter, Briseis, along with the words, “Enjoying my first Father’s Day with my family! #blessed”
The baby’s mother, dancer Lilit Avagyan, is of Armenian descent, just like Kardashian, and looks strikingly similar to the reality star. She and Bush were together for less than a year when he confirmed her pregnancy in October 2012 – just months before Kardashian, 32, announced her pregnancy.
Bush and Kardashian dated off and on for several years before calling it quits in March 2010.
