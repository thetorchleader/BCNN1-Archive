Join the Annual Sisters & Friends Getaway in Martha’s Vineyard This September

July 25, 2014 Lifestyle

Motivational speaker Vera Cornish leads discussion on Creating Your “A” Game to Live Life to the Fullest.

Haven’t made vacation plans yet? Join more than 75 women from seven states including Texas, Washington, Illinois, Ohio that are gathering in Martha’s Vineyard for the annual Sisters & Friends Getaway, September 7-14 or September 14-21.Can you imagine a place where there is no smog, no 18 wheelers pushing you to go faster, no temptation to visit a fast food franchise (because there are none), and a place where you can dress down all the time? Imagine being in the company of adults who seem like folks you’ve known all your life…women who have inspiring stories and even better advice! How about being in a place where your meals are delicious and prepared for you every day, a place where you can walk along the ocean or sit on the porch and relax all day for an affordable price for an entire week? You will experience this and more when you join Sisters & Friends Getaway to Oak Bluffs, on Martha’s Vineyard.

With times so tough and many women wanting to save and forgo vacations, this girlfriends retreat is a perfect way to experience a wonderful place and join with other women while not spending a lot of money. Citing “needing some downtime”, “looking forward…need stress relief”, “wanting to be with positive people” and “this is on my bucket list” are some reasons why guests say they are going on the Sisters and Friends Getaway to Martha’s Vineyard. They are waiting to exhale on this relaxing and rejuvenating week-long vacation taking place September 7-14 or September 14-21.

Many are connecting with friends from around the country some are venturing out on their own. Interesting and motivating discussions topics lead by accomplished speakers, island tours, shopping and day trips to Boston and Foxwood Casino are some of the activities planned. And, if you don’t want to join in you can do nothing, nothing but walking, dining, swimming and the other stuff that makes your time there relaxing. It’s whatever floats your boat on Sisters & Friends Getaway!

Visit www.sistersandfriends.com or call host Connie Portis at (412) 391-8208 for more information.
