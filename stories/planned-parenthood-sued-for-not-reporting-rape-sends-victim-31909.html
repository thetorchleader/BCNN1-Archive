Planned Parenthood Sued for Not Reporting Rape; Sends Victim Back to Abuser

July 29, 2014 Christian

Boston police officers stand in front of a Planned Parenthood clinic in Boston, Massachusetts, June 28, 2014. (PHOTO: REUTERS/DOMINICK REUTERS)

The Colorado Attorney General’s office is reviewing undercover videos showing Planned Parenthood staffers advising underage girls on violent sex, the National Catholic Register reports.

The videos, obtained by the Daily Caller in mid-July, show staffers advising investigators posing as 15-year-old girls about sadomasochistic sex practices, how to obtain sex toys while underage, and encouraging them to view pornography without parental knowledge or consent.

The videos are part of an ongoing investigation from pro-life group Live Action, who in June also released a video documenting similar behavior at a Planned Parenthood clinic in Indiana.

Live Action is concerned that Planned Parenthood is systematically ignoring laws designed to protect minors from sex abuse–and are using taxpayer funds to do it. They received over $500 million in government funds last year, accounting for 45 percent of its annual revenue. “We are the largest provider of sex education in the U.S.,” they boasted in their annual report, “with educators who will reach more than one million young people and adults this year.”

“We are relieved to learn that authorities in Colorado are taking these tapes, and their shocking and extremely troubling contents, seriously,” said Live Action’s president Lila Rose. “Parents of every political persuasion can come together and condemn an institutional ethos where sexual abuse of teens is portrayed as consensual sexual activity — where tax-funded counselors can recommend internet pornography and sex shops, and advise children on how to hide these things from their parents.”
