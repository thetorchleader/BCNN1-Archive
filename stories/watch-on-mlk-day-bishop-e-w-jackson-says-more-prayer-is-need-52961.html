WATCH: On MLK Day, Bishop E.W. Jackson Says More Prayer Is Needed Instead of Protests

January 20, 2015 Christian

E. W. Jackson is pictured. | AP Photo.

Groups across the nation are marking Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Day with large demonstrations protesting the police involved grand jury decisions in Ferguson, Missouri, and New York City.

But one group in Chesapeake, Virginia, is doing something different. They are protesting the violence and racial tensions with prayer.

At the annual S.T.A.N.D. prayer breakfast led by Bishop E.W. Jackson, the group prayed for and thanked members of the local police and sheriff’s department.

CBN News’ Efrem Graham spoke with Bishop Jackson about the event and why it’s time to come together. He explains ways Christians can help heal the national racial divide. Click play to watch.
