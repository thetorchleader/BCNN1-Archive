Oregon Outlasts Wisconsin to Win Rose Bowl

January 2, 2012 Uncategorized

Let New Orleans and the BCS title game have their snarling defenses, their three-yard runs, their field goals and a rematch of a 9-6 game.

The Rose Bowl will take dazzling speedsters, 91-yard scores, an avalanche of touchdowns and a pinball machine for a scoreboard.

Long
runs for touchdowns. Long passes for touchdowns. Record-setting
touchdowns. More than 1,100 yards between the two teams, and more points
scored than in any previous Rose Bowl.

Yep, the 98{+t}{+h} Granddaddy of Them All might have been the Great Granddaddy of Them All.

It
was day in which “P” stood for Pasadena and Points, and the Rose Bowl
produced a plethora of pretty plays and a predictably preposterous
product – a final score of Oregon 45, Wisconsin 38.

The victory Monday was Oregon coach Chip Kelly‘s
first in a BCS bowl game after having lost in the Rose Bowl and the BCS
title game in the two previous seasons, and it was Oregon’s first Rose
Bowl victory since 1917.
