Second A.M.E. Church in Los Angeles to Present ‘A Tribute to Maya Angelou’ at Black History Celebration

February 15, 2015 Black

Maya Angelou (huffingtonpost.com photo)

The Golden Glory Senior Club of Second  A.M.E. Church will present ‘A Tribute to Maya Angelou’ at its Black History Celebration in the C. D. Toliver Fellowship Hall.

The program takes place on Tuesday, February 17, at 10 a.m., at 5500 South Hoover Street in Los Angeles.  The $5 donation includes lunch.

The Rev. John E. Cager III is the host pastor and Dr. Edna Stafford and Carolyn Harvey are the program chairs.  For details, call (323) 753-2451.

Source: LA Sentinel
