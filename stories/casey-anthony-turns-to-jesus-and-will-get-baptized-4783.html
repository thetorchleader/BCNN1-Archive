Casey Anthony Turns to Jesus and Will Get Baptized

March 22, 2012 Uncategorized

Casey Anthony, the 26-year-old mother cleared in the murder of her 2-year-old daughter Caylee, has turned to the Lord, Radar Online reports.

The publication states an insider said she plans to get baptized some time in March and turn a new leaf in her life.
Anthony is reportedly doing this to ensure her salvation so that she can one day see her daughter again in heaven.
Anthony has been living in a hidden location in Florida since she was found not guilty for the 2008 murder of her daughter in July of last year.
The “tot-mom” made resurgence in the public eye after a video journal of her popped up in January, discussing her new found freedom and newly adopted dog.
Source: ABC Action News
