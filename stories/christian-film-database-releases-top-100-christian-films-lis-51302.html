Christian Film Database Releases ‘Top 100 Christian Films’ List for 2014

January 6, 2015 Entertainment

Christian Film Database, the most extensive database of Christian Movies,” has released its annual Top 100 Christian Films on CFDb for the year 2014.

The Christian film industry enjoyed a banner year in 2014, according to CFDb owner Annelie Rudlaff.

God’s Not Dead starring Kevin Sorbo, Shane Harper, David A.R. White and Dean Cain topped the list. Click here for the rest of the list of the Top 100 Christian Movies for 2014.

“What a great year for Christian film 2014 has been,” Rudlaff said. “We are looking forward to 2015 as an even better year for faith-based movies.”

Even though the Top 100 list is for the year 2014, these encompass the most looked at pages on Christian Film Database. As a result, popular films from the past, as well as films that haven’t been released yet are included.
