Sherri Shepherd Praises David and Tamela Mann’s “A Manns World”

January 30, 2015 Entertainment

GETTY

Some so-called faith-based reality shows are filled with vitriolic feuds and drama, but David and Tamela Mann’s “A Manns World” is a welcome change.

While watching a Wednesday, January 28 episode, Christian comedienne Sherri Shepherd praised the program.

“Great show about their family,” wrote the 47-year-old former co-host of ABC’s “The View.” She likes the fact that BET’s newest docu series shows “ups & downs,” and does it all with “love”.

It is no secret that Tamela and Sherri are friends. Last year, she performed her smash hit “Take Me To The King” on “The View” in honor of Sherri’s birthday.

Whether a friend or fan, most who view the Manns’ positive program, breathe a sigh of relief and say, “It’s about time.”

“There’s a stigma to reality shows,” David recently told The Dallas Morning News. “The emphasis is often on the negative. The producers can push you to be mean and to make it dirty. That’s not what we’re about.”

Tamela added that viewers “were saying how happy they were to see a show about a family where everyone loves and supports one another. This has just been a blessing.”
