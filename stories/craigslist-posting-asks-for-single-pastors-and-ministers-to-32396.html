Craigslist Posting Asks for Single Pastors and Ministers to Apply for New TV Show

August 3, 2014 Christian

Production company in Los Angeles is looking for single pastors, ministers, and preachers:

Pastors and ministers fulfill many roles within their community and their congregations, from teacher, counselor, event planner, wedding officiant, fundraiser, spiritual guide and so much more. It can be a thankless job, and being this busy doesn’t leave much time for oneself. It takes it’s toll on a family and relationships, for sure, but what happens when the pastor or minister hasn’t found their soulmate yet? 

What are the challenges a religious leader faces when they are single, divorced or widowed? In the quest to bring people to a closer relationship with God, how does the minister develop their own interpersonal relationships? 

We want to hear stories about the journey a single pastor takes to find that life-long commitment. Where are they trying to find him or her? How close have they come in the past, and what are their plans for the future?
