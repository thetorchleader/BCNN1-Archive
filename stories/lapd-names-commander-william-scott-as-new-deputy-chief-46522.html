LAPD Names Commander William Scott as New Deputy Chief

November 23, 2014 Black

(Commander William Scott hopes to take on his new position with a fresh approach.)

LAPD’s Commander William Scott will soon take on the title of deputy chief for the Southwest division. For more than 20 years Scott has served the city of Los Angeles as an officer and believes there is more work to be done.

“I’m extremely excited about this opportunity. I plan to make the best of it as much as I can and hopefully leave it better than I found it,” said Scott.

Raised as an army brat, Scott grew up on an army base and moved around a lot as a teenager. His family eventually settled in Birmingham, Alabama, where he soon attended the University of Alabama where he got his degree in accounting. He considered the military at one point of his life, but he always knew that it took great commitment to serve the local community.

He began working in LAPD’s department in October 1989 and has worked on assignments in patrol, detectives, gangs, Internal Affairs and several others. Scott was promoted the rank of Commander on January 1, 2012, and now 2 years later he is taking on a higher rank.

As the deputy chief, Scott will be over the South Bureau, which consists of the Southwest Police Station, Southeast Station, 77th Division, Harbor Division, South Traffic Division and Criminal Gang Homicide Division.  This will put him in charge of about 1,700 employees and his position will hold him accountable for all things in his bureau.

“The deputy chief role will be in the position to be more active with the community. I’m really excited about that part. That aspect of the position is the piece that makes us work. You have to have the right mindset of a great relationship with the people you serve. That’s what bring it all together,” he said.
