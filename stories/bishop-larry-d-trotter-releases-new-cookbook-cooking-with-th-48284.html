Bishop Larry D. Trotter Releases New Cookbook, “Cooking With the Bishop”

December 5, 2014 Books

J Hardy Agency

Bishop Larry D.Trotter, the best-selling author and Senior Pastor of the Sweet Holy Spirit Church of Chicago has finally lifted the lid on a sweet collection of his culinary secrets via his new cookbook, “Cooking With the Bishop,” available now.

The singing Bishop is also known for such songs as “Trouble Don’t Last” and “My Worship is For Real” tries his hand at culinary delights. In his first published cookbook, Bishop Trotter whips up an assortment of must-see recipes just in time for the holidays. No stranger to the kitchen, he is a certified chef trained at Wasburne Trade School and Chicago Cooking and Catering School.

To get a copy of “Cooking With the Bishop,” visit Cookingwithbishop.com, Sweetholyspirit.org, or call 773-721-6178 for more information.
