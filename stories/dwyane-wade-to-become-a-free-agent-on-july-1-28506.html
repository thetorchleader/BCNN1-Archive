Dwyane Wade to Become a Free Agent on July 1

Date not recorded in the original archive Sports

Dwyane Wade has told the Miami Heat he is opting out of the final two years of his contract.
(Photo: Steve Mitchell, USA TODAY Sports)

Dwyane Wade and Udonis Haslem will become free agents on July 1, the Miami Heat said Saturday, and now, the Heat are waiting to see what Chris Bosh does.

If Bosh opts out and becomes a free agent, the pieces are falling into place for the Heat to retain Wade, Bosh and LeBron James and give the Heat financial flexibility to make roster improvements.

Bosh has not made a decision, his agent, Henry Thomas, said, and Bosh has until midnight Monday to utilize the early termination option. Thomas also represents Wade and Haslem.

“Dwyane has been the cornerstone of our organization for over a decade, and we hope he remains a part of the Heat family for life,” Miami president Pat Riley said in a statement. “Udonis has been the heartbeat of this team for 11 years. He has sacrificed countless times to make this organization successful, and he is the epitome of what this organization stands for. We look forward to meeting with Dwyane and Udonis and their agent in the coming days to discuss our future together.”

James used the early termination option on his contract earlier in the week and will also be a free agent. James is keeping his options open, but it looks like the Heat have a great chance to bring the Big 3 plus Haslem back next season. Bosh, James and Wade met Wednesday in Miami to discuss the future.

Wade had two years and $41.8 million left on his deal with Miami, and it’s difficult to imagine him turning that down if he didn’t have a good idea what might happen with James and Bosh, who has two years and $42.7 million left on his deal with the Heat. It’s very likely that Wade and Bosh – both of whom indicated they want to return to the Heat – take deals worth less money than they made last year but for four, five years.
