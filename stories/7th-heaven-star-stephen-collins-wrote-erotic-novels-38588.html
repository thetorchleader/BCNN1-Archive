‘7th Heaven’ Star, Stephen Collins, Wrote Erotic Novels

October 10, 2014 Entertainment

Stephen Collins in 2008, Seth Wenig/AP

Foreshadowing?

Or just a hobby?

Gawker has uncovered yet another little-known side to actor Stephen Collins.

In addition to have roles on 7th Heaven and Revolution – and Scandal, until he was edited out of that, Collins wrote two erotic mystery novels.

Eye Contact was published in 1994 and Double Exposure came out in 1998.

Taken at face value, says Gawker, the books are “throwaway pulp fiction: sexualized without being sexy, using violence as a shorthand for drama. Smut.”

But now, in light of the molestation tape and estranged wife Faye Grant’s accusations of a “long-term pattern of sexually abusing minor children,” they’re uncomfortable and, well, creepy.

Eye Contact is told from the perspective of a woman, a struggling actress in New York named Nicolette “Nick” Stallings, who is constantly battling an internal struggle to expose her naked body to others.
