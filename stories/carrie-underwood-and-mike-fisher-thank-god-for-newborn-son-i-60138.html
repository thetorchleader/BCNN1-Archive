Carrie Underwood And Mike Fisher Thank God for Newborn Son – Isaiah Michael Fisher

March 5, 2015 Entertainment

Carrie Underwood is officially a new mommy!

The country singer and her Canadian-born hockey player husband Mike Fisher, both welcomed a baby boy, named Isaiah Michael Fisher. Underwood tweeted the news today with a glimpse of her newborn’s hands.

“Tiny hands and tiny feet…God has blessed us with an amazing gift! Isaiah Michael Fisher – born February 27,” wrote the 31-year-old star.
