LISTEN: Russia to give S-300 missile system to Syria (Second Coming Watch #548 with Daniel Whyte III)

December 4, 2014 Christian, Second Coming Watch

Daniel Whyte III

Let’s take a quick look at today’s prophecy-related headlines which point towards the second coming of the Lord Jesus Christ and the end of the world as we know it.

First, today, under the Sign category of WARS & RUMORS OF WARS — According to Ynet News, state-affiliated media in Syria have reported that during a recent visit to Moscow by representatives of the embattled regime, Damascus received promises of widespread Russian military support. Senior Russian officials reportedly “stressed their absolute commitment to continue their support for the Syrian regime in its military attempt to fight terror.” The report also claimed that Russia will give Syria the S-300 missile defense system. In the past, reports of such a transfer strained Western-Russia relations, and led to an all time low in Russia’s relations with the US.

Second, today, under the Sign category of THE TURMOIL IN ISRAEL & THE MIDDLE EAST — According to The Times of Israel, the Palestinian envoy to the United Nations said a draft resolution demanding an Israeli withdrawal to the 1967 lines by 2016, as part of a wider agreement to end the Israeli-Palestinian conflict, will be presented to the UN Security Council by the middle of December. Palestinian representative Riyad Mansour said the text, being shepherded by France, is expected to lay out a timeframe for negotiations on a final peace deal and possibly a second deadline for Palestinian statehood. The position of the United States, which has repeatedly vetoed UN resolutions seen as pressuring Israel, will be crucial in deciding if the latest push at the United Nations stands a chance.

Third, today, under the Sign category of INCREASED NATURAL DISASTERS — According to Yahoo News, molten rock from a thundering volcano threatens to engulf several villages in Cape Verde days after its initial eruption. The active volcano, Pico do Fogo, is the archipelago’s highest peak on its most prominent island. It burst open on November 23 for the first time since 1995 and has continued to erupt – prompting government officials to call for an emergency meeting with U.N. representatives and aid agencies. Along with the villages, a large fertile region of the country is at risk of being destroyed.

Fourth, today, under the Sign category of DISEASES & EPIDEMICS — According to IRIN, rates of new HIV infections and AIDS-related deaths are rising faster in the Middle East and North Africa than anywhere else globally. Experts warn that many patients are left in the shadows unable to access treatment and support, leading to unnecessary loss of life and increasing transmission risks. According to the latest data available from the Joint UN Programme on HIV and AIDS, there are 230,000 people living with HIV in the 21 countries that make up the organization’s definition of the Middle East and North Africa. In 2013 the region lost 15,000 people to the disease, a 66 percent increase on 2005, and despite new infections declining globally by 38 percent since 2001, in the Middle East and North Africa they have risen over the same period.

Fifth, today, under the Sign category of THE TURMOIL IN ISRAEL & THE MIDDLE EAST — According to The Jerusalem Post, the Jordanian government sent an official letter to Israel warning against making any changes to the status quo in Jerusalem. The letter called on the government of Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu to carry out its duty to prevent “attacks” by “the herds of extremists and settlers” on al-Aqsa Mosque. This message accompanied parallel contacts Jordan made with the US administration on the same subject.

The prophetic passage of Scripture we are looking at today is Genesis 49:1-2 which reads: “And Jacob called unto his sons, and said, Gather yourselves together, that I may tell you that which shall befall you in the last days. Gather yourselves together, and hear, ye sons of Jacob; and hearken unto Israel your father.”

Allow me to share with you some commentary on this passage from the Popular Bible Prophecy Commentary, edited by Tim LaHaye and Ed Hindson.

Judah, the fourth son, is dealt with next. It is Judah, the “lion,” to whom the mantle of tribal leadership is passed. Judah is recognized as being worthy to receive the privileges of the firstborn.

The tribe of Zebulun would be “a haven for ships,” enriched by maritime trade. Although the tribe of Issachar was naturally equipped for hard work, they would not live up to their potential. Dan would provide leadership to Israel. Gad would both be attacked and counterattack. Asher’s tribal territory would prove abundantly fertile. Naphtali would be a speedy doe, roaming free.

Joseph receives a superior, double blessing. The tribe of Joseph would be characterized by prosperity and military capability, broadly blessed in every way. Joseph is “the one distinguished among his brothers.”

Finally, Benjamin is characterized as a tribe of violent warriors.

The section concludes with the affirmation that every son/tribe was blessed “with the blessing appropriate to him.”
