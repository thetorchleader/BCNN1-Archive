Man Arguing with his Wife Throws Mug at her; Misses and Kills 2-Year-Old Son — Tragic!

February 11, 2015 National

Anthony Benjamin Grove, 33, of Canton, Ohio
(Photo: Stark County (Ohio) Sheriff’s Office)

A father faces a murder charge after police said he hit his 2-month-old with a coffee mug while arguing with his wife.

Anthony Benjamin Grove, 33, was arrested Tuesday after the boy died that morning at Akron Children’s Hospital of serious head injuries, and hospital workers notified police. Grove also was charged with child endangering and domestic violence and is being housed in Stark County Jail.

The Canton resident is accused of throwing a ceramic coffee mug at his wife during an argument, detectives said. Instead, he hit his son, Zeeland Grove.

Anthony Grove also was arrested on a warrant for failing to appear in an unrelated theft case, authorities said.
