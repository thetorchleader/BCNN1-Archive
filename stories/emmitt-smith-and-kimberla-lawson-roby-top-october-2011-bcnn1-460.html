Emmitt Smith and Kimberla Lawson Roby Top October 2011 BCNN1/BCBC National Bestsellers List

October 1, 2011 Uncategorized

Black Christian News: http://www.BCNN1.com / Black Christian Book Company: http://www.BlackCBC.com (Based upon Amazon.com rankings, BarnesandNoble.com rankings, and BlackCBC.com rankings.) 

Bestselling authors, Emmitt Smith (Game On — Nonfiction) and Kimberla Lawson Roby (Secret Obsession — Fiction) lead the bestsellers list of Black Christian authors on the October 2011 edition of the Black Christian News/Black Christian Book Company National Bestsellers List.
BCNN1.com has also released the 16th edition of the BCNN1/Amazon.com Kindle E-book Bestsellers List. This list ranks African-American Christian e-books based upon their sales rankings in the Amazon Kindle e-book store. Also, BCNN1 is releasing the 10th edition of the BCNN1/Barnes & Noble Nook E-book Bestsellers List. This list ranks African-American Christian e-books based upon their sales rankings in the B&N Nook e-book store.
The next BCNN1/BCBC Bestsellers List will be published on Tuesday, November 1, 2011.
