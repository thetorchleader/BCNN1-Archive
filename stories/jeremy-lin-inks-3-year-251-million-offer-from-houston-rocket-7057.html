Jeremy Lin Inks 3-Year, $25.1 Million Offer from Houston Rockets

July 14, 2012 Uncategorized

The pressure is on.
And the ball is now in the New York Knicks’ court.
Jeremy Lin, in Las Vegas to sign his latest offer as a free agent, hit the jackpot by cashing in on his sensational season with the New York Knicks. The point guard signed a three-year, $25.1 million offer sheet with the Houston Rockets on Friday, Newsday and ESPNNewYork reported.
The Knicks, who recently signed veteran floor general Jason Kidd, have three days to match the offer. Kidd was effervescent about mentoring Lin into a premier point guard in New York.
The Rockets have made it difficult on New York to match, “backloading” the third year of the deal, which reportedly gives Lin $5 million in the first year, $5.2 in the second and then $14.9 million in the third and final year of the contract. Initial reports had the Rockets offering a four-year deal in the $28 million range. Houston, in the hunt for Dwight Howard, recently amnestied Luis Scola. Reeling in an up-and-coming point guard seems to be a top priority.
