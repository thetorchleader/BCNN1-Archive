Answers In Genesis Sues Kentucky After State Pulls Plug on Tax Rebate for Ark Project

February 5, 2015 Christian

The Ark Encounter theme park is going to court in Kentucky, demanding that it be treated the same as other applicants for a state program.

The Ark, a project of Answers in Genesis, had been cleared for participating in the Kentucky rebate incentive program. But state government backed out after atheist groups complained of state involvement in a project with a religious theme.

Freedom Guard chief counsel Mike Johnson says Kentucky law requires religious organizations to receive the same consideration as non-religious ones and the state has approved the latter groups, such as museums and theme parks.

“Even a beer distillery museum,” Johnson tells OneNewsNow.

The problem, says the attorney, is that when Answers in Genesis showed up with the Ark Encounter project, “suddenly they say they have a problem with it.”

That’s not legal under both federal and state law, he claims, “and that’s why we’re going to have to go to court over it.”

The Courier-Journal newspaper reported that Answers in Genesis would be able to collect more than $18 million from sales tax receipts for 10 years as part of the state program.

The first phase of the project is $73 million to build the 510-foot replica of the ark, the story also states.

Answers in Genesis is pointing to comments made earlier by Gov. Steven Bashear, during a press conference, in which he called The Ark an economic development project that is permitted by Kentucky laws regardless of the subject matter, referring to its religious theme.
