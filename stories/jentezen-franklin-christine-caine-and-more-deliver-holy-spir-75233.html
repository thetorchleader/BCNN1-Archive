Jentezen Franklin, Christine Caine and More Deliver Holy Spirit-Based Messages at Empowered21

May 23, 2015 Christian

Jentezen Franklin takes the stage at Empowered21. (Mark Moore/Empowered21/Facebook)

Jack Hayford, Christine Caine and Jentezen Franklin opened up the morning general session of Empowered21 on Friday, May 22.

Hayford, founder of Jack Hayford Ministries, followed up his morning devotional from the previous day with a second message of hope and renewal. Caine, a native of Australia and co-founder of the A21 Campaign, spoke to the morning gathering of thousands in the Pais Arena, followed by Jentezen Franklin, senior pastor of Free Chapel in Gainesville, Georgia. Both centered their messages on renewing and refreshing the power of the Holy Spirit in our lives.

Empowered21’s second day of ministry track sessions took place in the afternoon in the Pais Arena. Friday’s tracks included “Word of the hour,” “Leading Effective Worship and Praise,” “Signs, Wonders and Healing,” “Communicating the Word: Publishing, Media and the Arts,” “How to Pray and Intercede Effectively,” “How Shall We Walk between Cultures?” “Leading, Planting & Prospering the Church,” “The Book of Acts Today,” “The Effective Evangelist,” “True Religion: Compassion” and “Scholar Presentations.”

The afternoon meeting was keynoted by Daniel Kolenda, president and CEO of Christ for All Nations. Previously scheduled for the message, Reinhard Bonnke was unable to attend the congress, but addressed the arena crowd by video.
