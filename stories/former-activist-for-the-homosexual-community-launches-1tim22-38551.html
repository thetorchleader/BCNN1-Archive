Former Activist for the Homosexual Community Launches ‘1Tim2:2’ Prayer Project

October 10, 2014 Christian

Mark Olson, a former LGBT activist, discusses the 1Tim2:2 Project. (Facebook)

Mark Olson, a former LGBT activist, announces the 1Tim2:2 Project, a grass-roots effort based on 1 Timothy 2:2, to mobilize prayer to bless our president and leaders in government.

The project’s website features 3,166 free prayer guides, one for every county in the nation. Each county prayer guide is customized to help Christians pray for their specific leaders in government by name.

First Timothy 2:2 states that, “first of all,” we are to give thanks for, and bless in prayer, all of our leaders in government.

“If we Christians can’t consistently follow one of the clearest commands of Scripture, we really don’t have any reason to be dabbling in any other political action,” says Olson, a pro-life political consultant. “Frankly, over the last six years, too many of the words spoken by Christian leaders and politicians over President Barack Obama have been outright embarrassing and certainly not consistent with Scripture. We, the church, have some serious repenting to do.

“The instructions of Scripture in 1 Timothy 2 or Romans 13 are not altered by whether or not America was founded as a Christian nation,” he adds. “As a Christian, my obedience is to the Word of God. His Word is eternally fixed and is certainly not altered by the foundations or current state of American politics.”
