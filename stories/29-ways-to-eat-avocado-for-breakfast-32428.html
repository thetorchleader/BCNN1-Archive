29 Ways to Eat Avocado for Breakfast

August 4, 2014 God & Food

Guacamole Omelet / Macheesmo

There’s not really a wrong way to eat avocado. But there’s definitely a right way, and that’s with breakfast.

The savory green fruit is an egg’s best friend. It creamifies an a.m. smoothie without hesitation and it transforms a bland piece of toast into a sought after delicacy.

Start your morning with avocado and you’ll be able to handle anything that comes at you for the rest of the day. You’ll be invincible. Here are 29 ways to wake up with avocado:

  • Poached Eggs Over Avocado & Smoked Salmon
  • Eggacado
  • Vegetarian Croissant Sandwich
  • Sweet Corn Cake Eggs Benedict With Avocado Hollandaise
  • Avocado Cream Cheese Roll-Ups
  • Black Bean & Avocado Breakfast Burritos
  • Tex Mex Egg Cups
  • Avocado, Banana, Pear & Chia Seed Smoothie
  • Turkey, Smashed Avocado, Cranberry, Brie & Mashed Potato Waffle Melts
  • Avocado Egg Salad
  • Slow Roasted Tomatoes With Avocado & Feta
  • Breakfast Corn Cakes With Avocado Cream
  • Mushroom Breakfast Torta With Avocado
  • Green Eggs & Ham Breakfast Bowl
  • Guacamole Omelet
