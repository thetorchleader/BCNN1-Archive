Halle Berry Suffered a Serious Injury on Tuesday

July 18, 2012 Uncategorized

An on-set injury sent Halle Berry to the hospital last night.


TMZ and X17 report that the actress was on the set of The Hive, a thriller starring Abigail Breslin and Jennifer Hudson’s fiance David Otunga, when she suffered an injury Tuesday night around 10 p.m. in Los Angeles.
TMZ says she “hit her head on the concrete after taking a nasty fall.”
