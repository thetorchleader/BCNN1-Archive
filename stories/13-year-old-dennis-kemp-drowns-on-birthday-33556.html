13-Year-Old Dennis Kemp Drowns on Birthday

August 12, 2014 Black

Dennis Kemp
DENNIS KEMP BURIAL FUND SCREENSHOT

Lifeguards tried to rescue Dennis Kemp to no avail after he went under while swimming in a deeper part of the lake. 

A young, Laurel, Md., boy lost his life on his birthday during what was supposed to be a fun day at camp, WJLA reports.

Dennis Kemp, 13, drowned while swimming in a lake at Oregon Ridge Park while out with his camping group, Camp Sonshine, based in Silver Spring, Md., officials said, according to the news station.

Onlookers watched the young boy go under the water a little after noon Friday. Lifeguards attempted to get to him, but did not succeed. Rescuers had to take over the mission, recovering the boy’s body more than two hours after he first went down.
