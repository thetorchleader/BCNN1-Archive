‘Rent Is Too Damn High’ Party Founder Is Unable to Pay the Rent

January 28, 2015 Black

Jimmy McMillan ran for governor of New York as the candidate of his self-created Rent is Too Damn High party, but failed to win the office despite support from other fringe lunatics and people who believed they should be paying less rent. I guess McMillan was right in his assessment of the highness of the rent, because he is now being evicted.

McMillan has reportedly filed suit to avoid being thrown out of his $872-a-month rent stabilized East Village apartment (via New York Daily News):

“They don’t have the authority to evict me,” McMillan told the Daily News on Monday.

McMillan’s two-page complaint says landlord Lisco Holdings LLC did not comply with an order from the Housing Court in 2009 to give him a new key after locking him out of the building .

“They never gave me a damn key,” he said, so he obtained a new one on his own.
