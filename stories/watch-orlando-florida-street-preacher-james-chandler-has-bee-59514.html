WATCH: Orlando, Florida, Street Preacher, James Chandler, Has Been Preaching the Gospel Every Saturday for 15 Years

March 2, 2015 Black
Street preacher James Chandler spreads the gospel of Jesus every Saturday in Parramore in downtown Orlando. Chandler and his wife Loretha have been preaching in the community for 15 years.
