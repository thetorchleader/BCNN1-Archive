The Association of Welcoming & Affirming Baptists (AWAB), Which Appears to Include Dr. Frederick Haynes, Responds to Rev. Dwight McKissic, Rev. Randy G. Vaughn, and the National Baptist Fellowship of Concerned Pastors With Statement of Support for Dr. Forrest Harris’ Invitation to a Lesbian Minister and Two Pro-Homosexual Pastors to Preach in the Garnett-Nabrit Lecture Series at American Baptist College

March 10, 2015 Christian

Special Note from BCNN1 Editors Regarding the Article Below

A dear pastor who is a part of the National Baptist Fellowship of Concerned Pastors sent us a kind email regarding the article below, because as far as he knows, the current president of the National Baptist Convention, Dr. Jerry Young, pastor of New Hope Baptist Church in Jackson, MS, and the immediate past president, Dr. Julius Scruggs, pastor of the First Missionary Baptist Church in Huntsville, AL, are firmly against homosexuality, homosexual marriage, and against the lesbian minister and two pro-homosexual pastors speaking at the American Baptist College. Here is our response to this dear pastor:

March 10, 2015

Thank you for your kind email.

We trust that you, your family, and church are doing well.

The way the Association of Welcoming & Affirming Baptists (AWAB) have the open letter posted on their site (awab.org) (click here), which they linked to BlackChristianNews.com, makes it appear as though the current president, Dr. Jerry Young, and the immediate past president, Dr. Julius Scruggs, support and agree with the invitation of the lesbian minister and the two pro-homosexual ministers to speak at the American Baptist College which the National Baptist Convention owns and operates. The letter that a group of ministers signed who are apparently associated with the AWAB, which included Dr. Freddy Haynes, is as follows:

Faith Leaders Issue Statement of Support for Dr. Forrest Harris and the American Baptist College

On March 5, 2015 a press release was issued by the National Baptist Fellowship of Concerned Pastors expressing deep concern for the American Baptist College* in Nashville, TN and its invitation to Bishop Yvette Flunder, Dr. Allan Aubrey Boesak and Pastor Delman Coates to preach as a part of this year’s Garnett-Nabrit Lecture Series.

This statement is in response to last week’s press release by the National Baptist Fellowship of Concerned Pastors and seeks to offer support to President Forrest Harris and the Board and Trustees of American Baptists College as well as Bishop Flunder, Dr. Boesak and Pastor Coates. The Association of Welcoming & Affirming Baptists, the Samuel Dewitt Proctor Conference and faith leaders from across the Baptist and other Christian traditions join together in the following statement.

*The American Baptist College is affiliated with the National Baptist Convention, USA and not the American Baptist Churches, USA.

We applaud the wisdom, prophetic courage, and commitment to intellectual honesty and academic integrity that Dr. Forrest Harris has demonstrated as President of American Baptist College by inviting Bishop Yvette Flunder of California to preach and lecture at American Baptist College later this month. A few vocal opponents to equality for persons who are gay, lesbian, bisexual, or transgender are trying to force Dr. Harris to “disinvite” Bishop Flunder because she is a lesbian who married her same-sex partner. Those critics also are displeased that Dr. Harris invited Dr. Allan Aubrey Boesak of South Africa and Pastor Delmon Coates of the United States to preach and/or lecture at American Baptist College because Dr. Boesak and Pastor Coates are effective advocates for equality and are equally effective in exposing the injustice of homophobia.

American Baptist College is dedicated to educating students to be strengthened in believing, practicing, and proclaiming the liberating gospel of Jesus. The students of American Baptist College deserve to know that the gospel of Jesus is believed, lived, and preached by faithful people who are in committed same-sex relationships in the same way the gospel of Jesus is believed, lived, and preached by faithful people who are in committed heterosexual relationships.

We thank God that President Harris has not allowed homophobia to shape and determine who can be an effective advocate for the gospel of Jesus Christ. In that spirit, we pray that the Holy Spirit will bless Bishop Flunder, Dr. Boesak, Pastor Coates, and other prophetic leaders to be invited, welcomed, affirmed, and encouraged by other courageous and principled Christian pastors, educators, and denominational leaders such as President Harris.

Again, the way this letter is formatted makes it appear as though Dr. Jerry Young and Dr. Julius Scruggs are in agreement with the letter because their names are affixed right under the letter. At the very least, it makes it seem as though they are aware of this letter and are prepared to receive letters of support from other pastors, churches, and Christians for Dr. Forrest Harris in support of the lesbian minister and pro-homosexual pastors coming. Normally, when an organization writes an open letter such as this one, the names of the people who sign the letter are affixed directly underneath the letter. That was not the case with this letter as you can see on both their site (awab.org) and on BlackChristianNews.com (BCNN1.com).

In the meantime, out of respect for your concerns for these two dear brothers, we will remove Dr. Young and Dr. Scruggs names from the title of the article, but we will leave Dr. Freddy Haynes’ name on there because he is one of the actual signers of the letter. In addition, it would be good for you, Dr. Young, and Dr. Scruggs to know that we used the words “Appears to Include” in the title as the letter format suggested. Also, we stated that Dr. Young’s church, Dr. Scruggs’ church, and Dr. Haynes’ church were not included in the Welcoming & Affirming Baptist church network.

If you have a personal connection or relationship with Dr. Young and Dr. Scruggs, please tell them that they need to hurry up and publicly denounce the invitations to the homosexual ministers, otherwise as the current president and the immediate past president, they will be seen as complicit with it. In addition to that, Dr. Jerry Young should tell Dr. Forrest Harris and the American Baptist College not to have the lesbian minister and the two pro-homosexual pastors as speakers. And that will put an end to this demonic fiasco.

Best regards,

Black Christian News Network One
BCNN1.com

Dr. Forrest Harris
