Survey Shows 92% of Teens Are Online Every Day, 24% Are Online ‘Almost Constantly’

April 10, 2015 Technology

Thanks to the proliferation of smartphones and other mobile devices, teens are going online more frequently than ever before.

A new survey from the Pew Research Center found that 92 percent of teens ages 13 to 17 go online every day. Moreover, 24 percent of the more than 1,060 teens who participated in the online survey said they’re online “almost constantly.”

In contrast, just 6 percent of teens go online weekly, and 2 percent do so less often.

“Much of this frenzy of access is facilitated by mobile devices,” the report notes. “Nearly three-quarters of teens have or have access to a smartphone and 30 percent have a basic phone.” Just 12 percent have no cell phone of any type.

In terms of demographics, African-American teens are most likely to have a smartphone, with 85 percent having access to one, compared to 71 percent of white and Hispanic teens.
