Karen Clark-Sheard Readies Release Of New Single ‘My Words Have Power’

March 20, 2015 Entertainment

Karen Clark-Sheard of the legendary Clark Sisters, releases to radio the next single from her forthcoming full project “Destined To Win” set for release later this year! The song entitled “My Words Have Power” is written by Rick Robinson, produced by Donald Lawrence and backed by Donald Lawrence & Co.  Donald Lawrence posted a snippet on the social networks and Karen Clark-Sheard also encourages you to pre-order from iTunes via instagram which will be available for download on Tuesday, March 24, 2015. You should be able to request the song from your favorite radio station over the next few weeks.
