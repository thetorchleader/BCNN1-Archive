Oprah Winfrey Is Back With New Book Club Pick

February 10, 2015 Books, Entertainment

It’s been more than a year since Oprah Winfrey recommended a good book.

But it’s time now.

“Wow. Wow,” says Oprah in a video on her Book Club site. “I’ve never READ a book like this before!”

She has chosen Cynthia Bond’s Ruby. It’s a debut novel that was published last year. On Bond’s site, the book is described as the “story of a man determined to protect the woman he loves from the town desperate to destroy her.”

Oprah tells AP that it was so good she had to sit back and let it sink in.

“I put it down and waited until I was in bed with the flu to start reading it. I found the language and descriptions so vividly compelling that sometimes I would have to take a breath and repeat the sentences out loud,” Oprah says.

The novel will be out in paperback on Tuesday, says AP, with a printing of 250,000 copies. The hardcover currently has 20,000 copies in print, according to Hogarth, an imprint of Penguin Random House, and is available as an e-book.

Oprah is so into the story that she has already acquired film and television rights for Ruby through her Harpo Films. She interviews Bond in the March issue of O magazine, which comes out Feb. 17.

“Nothing comes close to the experience of reading this book,” Oprah says in the mag. “When I found out that Cynthia Bond is 53 years old and hasn’t written a book before, I couldn’t fathom it. Ruby is a vivid, searing novel that penetrates straight through the page and into the reader’s heart.”
