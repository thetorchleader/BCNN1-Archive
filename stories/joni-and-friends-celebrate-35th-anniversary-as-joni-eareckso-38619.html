Joni and Friends Celebrate 35th Anniversary as Joni Eareckson Tada Turns 65

October 10, 2014 Christian

Joni Eareckson Tada

As the staff of Joni and Friends International Disability Center prepare to honor founder Joni Eareckson Tada’s 65th birthday later this month, they have much to celebrate: 2014 was the organization’s 35th anniversary, October marks another “Breast Cancer Awareness Month” that Tada is cancer free after her diagnosis in 2010, and they are finalizing plans for their first ever internationally-focused “Global Access” Conference on disability ministry in 2015.

“I figured by the time I reached my 65th birthday I would be slowing down, especially as a quadriplegic in a wheelchair for more than 47 years,” Tada said. “However, God continues to provide strength and energy, and amazing co-laborers who have come alongside in the past 35 years to share in our mission to accelerate ministry to those with disabilities.”

According to a news release from A. Larry Ross Communications (alarryross.com), during this 35th anniversary year, Joni and Friends invited individuals around the world to share how the organization has impacted them, and stories have come in by the thousands. Here are examples from four winners who were selected on Joni’s blog:

“I felt less alone in living with a disabling chronic illness.” (Rachel)

“With the presence of a disability understood, expected, and even celebrated, men from around the country become vulnerable. New lifelong friendships have been forged.” (Glen)

“I have a passion and it is ignited. The world of disability is in desperate need of HOPE and grace.” (Electra)

“(I realized) what if all those accessibility devices I needed weren’t there? I am thankful they help me and thankful to have them.” (Stephanie)
