Suge Knight Jailed On No-Bail Hold in Deadly Hit-and-Run

February 2, 2015 Entertainment

Marion ‘Suge’ Knight walking into Los Angeles County Sheriffs department on Jan. 30, for questioning in connection with a hit-and-run incident that left one man dead and another injured.
(Photo: Photo by AP)

Former rap mogul Marion “Suge” Knight is now jailed in Los Angeles on a no-bail hold following his arrest on suspicion of murder in the death of a man.

Knight was originally held in lieu of $2.2 million bail after his arrest Friday.

Los Angeles County Sheriff’s Department spokeswoman Nicole Nishida says detectives petitioned a bail commissioner on Monday to change his status to no-bail due to Knight being a possible flight risk, a three-strikes candidate, possible witness intimidation issues and his criminal past.

Authorities allege Knight killed one man and injured another with his pickup truck on Thursday in a restaurant parking lot near a film set in Compton, and then fled the scene.

Knight’s lawyer, James Blatt, has said Knight accidentally ran over the men as he tried to escape a vicious attack.

Blatt confirmed the bail change to the Los Angeles Times. Earlier, he had predicted the charges against Knight would be dropped.

Knight is scheduled to appear at the Compton courthouse Tuesday.
