Second Baptist Church of Doylestown, PA Breaks Ground on New Fellowship Hall

April 7, 2014 Uncategorized

The addition of a new fellowship hall will be added on to the back of the Second Baptist Church of Doylestown, Fountainville, pictured here. It held its groundbreaking of its new fellowship hall, Sunday. (Catherine Meredith/Staff Photographer)

The Second Baptist Church of Doylestown seeks to be a beacon of Christian light to the community, congregants say.

The Rev. Robert E. Hamlin Sr. and the church’s 300 members have faith that the light will shine brighter thanks to the new Fellowship Hall on which the congregation officially broke ground Sunday.

“It’s more than just a building,” Hamlin said during the groundbreaking ceremony. “It’s a place of worship, of outreach, of refuge…A beacon to those searching for purpose in life.”

Situated atop a hill on Route 313 near Stump Road where the suburbs meet the countryside, the Plumstead-based church’s membership has doubled over the last decade.

The hall will provide an additional 6,000 square feet of space – including classrooms, a commercial kitchen, a seminar space and 250-seat banquet hall – that will help Second Baptist better serve the growing congregation and become more of a center for the surrounding community.

“We’ve talked about hosting banquets, weddings – even having a day care,” said Lavinia Thompson, a Pipersville resident who is Second Baptist’s head usher. “We’re excited to invite the community in for all different kinds of activities.”

Construction crews will likely commence building within the next two to four weeks. The aim is to have work wrapped up by September.

“It’s a great feeling to see it coming to fruition,” Luretha Wilson, a Doylestown resident who has been a Second Baptist congregant for some 50 years, said of the new hall Sunday. “God has blessed us.”

First charted in 1874, Second Baptist has historically been home to a black congregation. But in recent years, membership has diversified to include people of other races and ethnicities.
