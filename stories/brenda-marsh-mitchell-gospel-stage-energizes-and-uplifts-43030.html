Brenda Marsh-Mitchell Gospel Stage Energizes and Uplifts

November 2, 2014 Black

Ricardo Mowatt & Rev. Kelvin Sauls, producers (photo by Nell Cunningham)

The Brenda Marsh-Mitchell Gospel Stage debuted with power at the 9th Annual Taste of Soul. Thanks to the huge stage backdrop featuring her image, the spirit of Brenda enveloped the air as artist after artist saluted her love of God and wisdom in creating a gospel stage.

“It’s a privilege for us to be in partnership to sponsor this stage celebrating Brenda’s spirit and mission in life to serve families and work for unity,” said Pastor Kelvin Sauls of Holman United Methodist, who joined Ricardo Mowatt of Mowatt Entertainment as producers.

“This is how we envision this stage to be a place where we can celebrate who we are in God with one another and to say to people, ‘God loves you, period.’  We are excited and we are happy with how it all turned out,” he added.

In addition to dynamic performances by a range of musical, jazz and spoken word artists, the production featured plenty of laughter triggered by the comedy of host, J-Red.  His gentle pokes at Christian behavior in and outside the church as well as audience interactions kept the program moving at a swift pace.
