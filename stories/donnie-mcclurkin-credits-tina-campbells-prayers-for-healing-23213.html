Donnie McClurkin Credits Tina Campbell’s Prayers for Healing “Diseases”

May 21, 2014 Entertainment

James 5:15 says, “And the prayer offered in faith will make the sick person well; the Lord will raise them up.” It seems that Pastor Donnie McClurkin is validating the truth of that scripture.

According to him, when Grammy-winning gospel artist Tina Campbell prayed for him during the process of recording his latest chart-topping Duets CD, some unspecified “diseases” disappeared.

In a social cam video uploaded to the Perfecting Church Pastor’s official Twitter page Wednesday, May 21, he said, “Tina Campbell, you came in the studio, talked to me and ministered to me for about 20 minutes, prayed for me, canceled out some diseases that I was dealing with and it hasn’t come back since.”

The revelation of this miraculous event came amid the 54-year-old’s thank you to media personality Steve Harvey for making a heartfelt appeal to The Steve Harvey Morning Show listeners to purchase McClurkin’s latest project.
