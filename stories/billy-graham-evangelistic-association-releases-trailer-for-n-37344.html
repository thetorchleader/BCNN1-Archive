Billy Graham Evangelistic Association Releases Trailer for New My Hope 2014 ‘Heaven’ Film

September 6, 2014 Christian

New evangelistic program will be released Nov. 7 in the United States and Canada as a tool for churches through My Hope 2014 with Billy Graham

Beginning in 2002, My Hope has been implemented in 59 countries in Africa, Asia, Europe, the Americas, and the Middle East.

More than 305,000 churches and 4 million homes have participated and more than 10 million salvation and rededication decisions for Jesus Christ have been recorded, according to a media release from the BGEA.

The news release explains that My Hope combines the impact of video programs with the power of personal relationships. Christians can share the Gospel message with friends, family, colleagues, and neighbors using one of several evangelistic video programs featuring life-changing testimonies and powerful messages from Billy Graham.

Following the successful impact of Billy Graham’s video message ‘The Cross’ in November, 2013, the Billy Graham Evangelistic Association has just released a trailer for ‘Heaven’, the next installment of evangelistic films available for use – free of charge – through the My Hope with Billy Graham ministry.

Releasing on Nov. 7 in the United States and Canada, the film features a never-before-released message from Billy Graham, alongside compelling stories of hope and faith, much like The Cross and the other impactful programs previously produced. You can watch the trailer for Heaven online at http://www.MyHopewithBillyGraham.org/programs.
