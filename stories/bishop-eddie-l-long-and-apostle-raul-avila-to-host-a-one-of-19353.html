Bishop Eddie L. Long and Apostle Raul Avila to Host a One of a Kind Bilingual Conference Uniting Latin America with North America on May 15-18, 2014

April 24, 2014 African-American
The Kingdom and The Power of One Conference, organized jointly by Bishop Eddie L. Long and Apostle Raul Avila will open with a call for unity between Latin America and North America, as these two great men of God connect The United States with the South American Country of Chile.
The unprecedented four-day unity event will be held May 15-18, 2014 at New Birth Missionary Baptist Church at 6400 Woodrow Rd. in Lithonia, GA. The conference is expected to draw approximately 2500 Latin American and American conference attendees from as far away as Venezuela, Chile, other Latin Nations, and throughout the United States, for the one of the most important conferences ever held that will bond Christians in Latin America with Christians in America, to mobilize and effect the culture of the world.
Keynote speakers include Apostle Raul Avila. Bishop Renny Mclean, Pastor Paula White, Apostle Marcelino Sojo, Prophet Rony Chavez, Apostolic Pastor Raul David Avila, and Bishop Eddie L. Long. Each speaker will minister about connecting the church as a global force to affect change. Apostle Raul Avila stated, “We will see that God is doing something great in the nations; we will see how God is opening doors.” Apostle Raul added, “As God open doors for the African American and Latin American people who live in the United States, this will bring an awakening of glory, honor, victory, growth, and unity.”
The conference will include experienced Spanish translators, who will translate live during each service. This creates a unique conference experience for the attendees, who will get to witness first hand how God has united lives, ministries, and churches. According to Bishop Eddie L. Long, “We know that this encounter will profoundly impact our lives, because we will have an apostolic, prophetic, directional, and revelation-filled word for these challenging, decisive, and accelerated times that we live in.” More details can be found at (www.KingdomConference2014.com)
 
About New Birth Missionary Baptist Church
International recognized as a premiere faith-based organization that leads extensive ministries, catering to children, families, community, business and public outreach initiatives, New Birth Missionary Baptist Church is located at 6400 Woodrow Road in Lithonia, Georgia, 30038. Under the leadership of Bishop Eddie L. Long, New Birth launched satellite locations across America. The mission of New Birth is to lead the world to worship God through serving, loving, evangelizing, and making disciples. The vision of New Birth is to Love, Live, and Lead like Christ. For more information visit: www.NewBirth.org.
