Kirk Franklin Calls Prosperity Gospel Theology ‘Wrong’

February 27, 2015 Christian

GETTY

Teachings that say it is God’s will for everyone to be healthy and wealthy, better known as prosperity theology, has millions of advocates.

But GRAMMY® award-winning gospel artist Kirk Franklin, 45, isn’t one of them.

In a Feb. 25 blog post titled Thank You, he slammed the gospel of success and riches.

“If your theology can’t be preached in a third world country, where a Bentley and a Lear jet probably won’t come to the guy who walked four miles to church for prayer, then your theology is wrong,” he wrote.

Well, he made that plain, didn’t he?

The original context of the post deals with the importance of expressing our thankfulness to God for His goodness. According to Franklin, Christians can often be self-centered.
