New York Churches Sponsor Tour of Historic Black Colleges for 80 Staten Island Students

May 14, 2013 Uncategorized

Close to 80 students took advantage of the 2013 Historical Black Colleges and Universities (HBCU) Tour this year. The trip, operated by a host of Staten Island churches and individuals, ran from March 24 to March 29 and included visits to five states and universities.

During the Historic Black Colleges and Universities tour earlier this spring, Staten Island students and their chaperones take a group picture outside Tuskegee (Ala.) University.
The five states were Tennessee, Alabama, North Carolina, George, Maryland, plus Washington, D.C. Among the colleges and universities were Fiske University, Tennessee State University (TSU), Alabama State University, Tuskegee University, Morehouse College, Clarke Atlanta University, Spellman, Shaw University, Winston Salem College and Howard University.
The tour was sponsored by the Fellowship Baptist Church, Mariners Harbor; First Central Baptist Church, Stapleton; St. Philips Baptist Church, Port Richmond; Silver Lake Temple Order of Eastern Stars; Young Talented & Gifted, Inc., based on the North Shore, and Moms on a Mission.
If you are interested in signing up for next year’s tour or have questions, reach out to Serena Brothers-Mohamed at 718-506-5198.
