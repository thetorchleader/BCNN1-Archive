RIM Launches Playbook OS 2 with Built-in Email

February 21, 2012 Uncategorized

RIM launched PlayBook OS 2.0 Tuesday, a major system update for its
much-maligned enterprise tablet. The update adds key features to the
tablet–specifically email, contacts, and calendar management apps–in
addition to a host of extras that provide for a more complete experience
on the PlayBook.

First up, the vitals. RIM explained that with PlayBook OS 2.0, owners
will no longer have to make use of the Bridge BlackBerry application to
access their email, contacts, and calendar on the tablet. These features
are now built in. PlayBook OS 2.0 offers a unified inbox that can
handle not only corporate and personal email, but can act as a social
network hub and control messages from Facebook, Twitter, and LinkedIn.

It also offers something called People View, which lets users see a
snapshot of all the contacts with whom they are going to interact
throughout each day. Additionally, the contact cards are populated by
social network updates from the likes of Facebook, Twitter, and LinkedIn
in real time, so users always know what their favorite people are up
to.

RIM baked other social networking powers into PlayBook OS 2.0, as well.
Not only does the calendar support corporate calendar tasks, it can
handle invitations from LinkedIn and Facebook. These additions make the
PlayBook a much more viable enterprise tablet.
