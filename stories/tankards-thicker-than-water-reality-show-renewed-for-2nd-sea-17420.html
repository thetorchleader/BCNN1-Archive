Tankards’ “Thicker Than Water” Reality Show Renewed for 2nd Season

April 10, 2014 Entertainment

The Tankards are ready for another season of their hit show, Thicker Than Water, now that Bravo has announced to renew the series for a second season.

We can’t wait to see what Ben Tankard and his wife, Jewel, have been up to since we last saw them on the small screen, but we’re guessing they will deliver even more drama on their second time around.

The show was a guilty pleasure that proved money doesn’t have to change people and highlighted the close-knit bond of a blended family.

While the Tankards faced their share of backlash for their seemingly extravagant lifestyle while preaching from the pulpit of their church, fans didn’t get the whole story on Season 1.
