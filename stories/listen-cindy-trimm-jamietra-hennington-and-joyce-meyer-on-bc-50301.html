LISTEN: Cindy Trimm, Jamietra Hennington, and Joyce Meyer on BC Book Review Show #252

December 28, 2014 Books

The authors that we are featuring today are: Cindy Trimm, author of “Heal Your Soul, Heal Our World”; Jamietra Hennington, author of “Trapped Inside of Me”; and Joyce Meyer, author of “Trusting God Day by Day”.

The purpose of this show is to highlight wholesome Christian books, promote them to our audience, encourage you to buy these books for yourself and for others, and most importantly, to see people come to Christ and lives changed for the glory of God.

First up is Cindy Trimm, author of “Heal Your Soul, Heal Our World”

Here’s a little information about Cindy Trimm

Dr. Cindy Trimm has dedicated her life to serving God and humanity. A best-selling author, high-impact teacher, and former senator, Dr. Trimm is a sought-after empowerment specialist, revolutionary thinker, and transformational leader. She has earned a distinguished reputation as a catalyst for change and voice of hope to the nations.

Here’s a short description of the book, “Heal Your Soul, Heal Our World”

The solutions to today’s greatest problems will not be found in a new technology, philanthropy, or social philosophy, but will be mined from the human soul. When Jesus came preaching a new kingdom, He was not proclaiming a new government as much as He was communicating a new paradigm that would liberate all who embraced it and empower them to live the authentic lives as world changers that God has planned for each of us.

Heal Your Soul, Heal Your World is about the impact the life of our soul has on the world around us, and the journey into healing the soul that frees it to be the seat of the God ideas we need to address today’s most troubling issues.

Now, Here’s a Brief Review of the book, “Heal Your Soul, Heal Our World”

The world is hurting and people are hurting. Everybody is trying to find a solution to their problem, a balm for their pain. But true healing on the inside comes from the soul. We may not be able to heal all the pain of the world, but we can heal the pain of our souls through God. Cindy Trimm lays out a biblically-based life-altering plan to heal the pain of our soul so that we can be in a godly position to help heal the world. Readers will be encouraged and empowered to change their own lives from the inside out and then address the pressing issues of today. We highly recommend this book.

Visit Cindy Trimm online at http://www.trimminternational.com.

Heal Your Soul, Heal Our World is published by Destiny Image. Be sure to get your copy from your local bookstore or from any online retailer such as Amazon.com, BarnesandNoble.com, and BlackCBC.com.

_______

Next up today is Joyce Meyer, author of “Trusting God Day by Day”

Here’s a little information about Joyce Meyer

Joyce Meyer is one of the world’s leading practical Bible teachers, with her TV and radio broadcast, Enjoying Everyday Life, airing on nearly 450 television networks and 400 radio stations worldwide, including ABC Family Channel, Trinity Broadcast Network, Daystar, and the Word Network. Her bestselling books include Living Beyond Your Feelings, Power Thoughts, Battlefield of the Mind, Look Great, Feel Great, The Confident Woman, I Dare You, and Never Give Up! Joyce holds conferences approximately 15 times each year, worldwide, speaking to thousands.

Now, here’s a short description of the book, “Trusting God Day by Day”

In her dynamic new devotional, TRUSTING GOD DAY BY DAY, international speaker and New York Times bestselling author Joyce Meyer provides you with powerful “starting points” for every day of the year. Each day’s devotion is filled with practical advice and help from Joyce along with life-changing promises from God’s Word that you can quickly and easily apply in your own life.

The world wants you to place your trust in your circumstances, your success, your talents and the opinions of others. But God’s called you to rise above the world, and put your full trust in Him – to believe and apply what He’s promised in His Word more than anything else.

Living this way won’t just happen – you have to be intentional. But where do you begin? We all need help to make good choices, battle worry, overcome anxiety and keep a positive attitude. Using this devotional, readers will learn to grab hold of life this way, day by day, with trust in God.

Now, here’s a brief review of the book, “Trusting God Day by Day”

We all want someone or something to trust. In a world full of uncertainty, it is easy to put our trust in things that we have or in people that we can see. But everything that we see, will eventually let us down. In her daily devotional book, Joyce Meyer shows readers how to fully and completely put their faith in God. Trusting God is a choice and Joyce helps Christians to make that choice each day through her powerful words of wisdom and biblical knowledge. Day by Day, we can claim the promises of God and live in the peaceful abundance of His truth and joy. We highly recommend this book.

Visit Joyce Meyer online at http://www.joycemeyer.org.

Trusting God Day by Day is published by FaithWords. Be sure to get your copy from your local bookstore or from any online retailer such as Amazon.com and BarnesandNoble.com.
