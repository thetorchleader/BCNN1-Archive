Jaden Smith Sparks Debate About Sin on Twitter

April 28, 2014 Entertainment

Jaden Smith (Jonathan Leibson/WireImage)

Jaden Smith may have sparked a debate about sin on Twitter.

Although Smith is only 15 years old, the son of entertainers Will and Jada Smith often shares some of his philosophies on the social networking website where he has over 5 million fans. His most recent post caused some serious conversation.

“Unawareness Is The Only Sin, And If You Were Aware You Would Know,” he tweeted.

A number of people responded to the posting, including one person who said, “But a sin isn’t a sin if you don’t know it’s a sin so if you’re unaware then you’re not sinning.”

Another person seemed to disagree with Jaden, saying, “That’s like saying killing someone isn’t a sin if u dont know about it huh.”

Yet another seemed sarcastic when they replied to the post by saying, “Is it ok if I kill 177 people? Thats not a sin right? Im pretty sure its not? I swear im not sinning. See you in heaven.”

Jaden often shares his insights on Twitter and spoke about commitment last month.

“Commit To Love Not To People, Commit To Happiness Because When You Commit To People The Love Leaves And You Are Forced To Stay, (SIC)” Smith tweeted.
