Adrian Peterson Sends Tweet Comparing his Child Abuse Case to Ferguson Ruling; Tells People to “Pray Hard” for Michael Brown Family

November 25, 2014 Sports

Adrian Peterson (Matthew Emmons, USA TODAY Sports)

The Minnesota Vikings’ Adrian Peterson tweeted out a response late Monday night after a grand jury decided not to indict Ferguson, Mo., police officer Darren Wilson in the shooting death of 18-year-old Mike Brown.

The GRAND JURY DECIDED NOT TO INDICT ME TOO! But that changed a week LATER! MAYBE, BUT NOT LIKELY N THIS CASE #PUTyourTrustNGODnotINtheWORLD! — Adrian Peterson (@AdrianPeterson) November 25, 2014

Peterson’s tweet is in reference to his indictment in September by a grand jury in Montgomery County, Texas, on a charge of injury to a child. He pleaded no contest Nov. 4 to a reduced charge of misdemeanor reckless assault, thus avoiding jail time.

Peterson is currently appealing his NFL suspension.

Peterson added more tweets before and after the comparison to his own case:

MY HEART GOES OUT TO MIKE BROWN’S FAMILY! MY THOUGHTS & PRAYERS ARE WITH YOU! #PRAYING4PEACE — Adrian Peterson (@AdrianPeterson) November 25, 2014

What a Sad sad world! #PRAYHARDPEOPLE#IAM pic.twitter.com/odC2DHY8h6 — Adrian Peterson (@AdrianPeterson) November 25, 2014

If you don’t like what I have to say, It’s simple just don’t follow me! Your opinion doesn’t apply to me, I know who I am! #You~without~sin — Adrian Peterson (@AdrianPeterson) November 25, 2014
