22-Year-Old Florida Pastor Is Fighting for His Life After Being Shot In “Out of Control” Road Rage Case

August 3, 2014 Christian

Steven Simon

A 22-year-old West Palm Beach pastor who began preaching as a child was fighting for his life Saturday after he was shot in the head in what authorities call “a case of road rage gone completely out of control.”

Steven Simon had just left a church service in Martin County on Thursday when he apparently angered another driver as both headed north on Interstate 95 near Stuart, according to Martin County Sheriff William Snyder.

A woman riding in Simon’s car told authorities the other driver first slowed his vehicle in front of them, then pulled alongside and opened fire.

“A routine traffic jam [that] escalates into road rage, and then into a tragic attempted murder, is sad,” Snyder said at a Friday press conference. “A completely innocent driver trying to get out of traffic and get home got into a road rage situation where one shot at least was fired. Possibly more.”

On Saturday morning, Simon’s wife Emma said her husband was “doing good.”

Sheriff’s department spokeswoman Christine Weiss said Simon remained “in a medically induced coma, in critical condition.”

Following the shooting, Simon was taken to Lawnwood Regional Medical Center in Fort Pierce, Weiss said.

The shooting happened before 10 p.m. after Simon and the other driver neared Mile Marker 96, just north of the Hobe Sound exit, deputies said.

After the shooting, Simon’s 2014 Honda Pilot veered off the highway and crashed through a metal barrier before coming to a stop, investigators said. The first deputy on the scene found Simon slumped in the driver’s seat.

“Officer help me. I’m going under, I need oxygen,” Simon said, according to the deputy first on the scene.
