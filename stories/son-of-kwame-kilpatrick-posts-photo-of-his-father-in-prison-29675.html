Son of Kwame Kilpatrick Posts Photo of his Father in Prison on his Birthday

Date not recorded in the original archive Black

Tweet from Kwame Kilpatrick’s son Jalil Kilpatrick on Twitter

A photo posted by Kwame Kilpatrick‘s son on Twitter has offered the first public glimpse of the former Detroit mayor since he was sentenced to 28 years in federal prison last October.

Kilpatrick, clad in a prison uniform, is smiling in the photo, with a graying goatee and appears slimmer since he was mayor.

Jalil Kilpatrick posted the photo on June 8 with the caption, “Happy BDAY DADDY SEE YOU SOON! #freekmk”

Kilpatrick,,44, is serving the lengthy sentence at a medium-security federal prison in El Reno, Okla., after being convicted on 24 criminal counts that included racketeering conspiracy, extortion, fraud and bribery charges.
