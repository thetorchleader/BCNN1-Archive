Putin Jabs at Hillary Clinton in Response to her Likening him to Hitler; Says it’s ‘Best Not to Argue with Women’

June 4, 2014 National

Russian President Vladimir Putin took several jabs at Hillary Clinton’s gender in an interview with a French television station recorded Tuesday. (GREENFIELD/SIPA/GREENFIELD/SIPA)

Russian President Vladimir Putin took jabs at Hillary Clinton’s gender in response to her own statements where she likened the man’s actions to that of Adolf Hitler.

“It’s better not to argue with women,” Putin said in an interview recorded Tuesday with a French television station and posted as a transcript on the Russian President’s website. “But Ms. Clinton has never been too graceful in her statements.”

In March, the former secretary of state said Putin is using the same type of statements as the World War II-era dictator.
