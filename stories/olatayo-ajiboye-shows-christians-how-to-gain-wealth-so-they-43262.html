Olatayo Ajiboye Shows Christians How to Gain Wealth So They Can Help Their Communities in New Book, 15 Years a Billionaire

November 3, 2014 Books

ATLANTA, GA, (October 27, 2014) – Christian entrepreneur, Olatayo Ajiboye, understands the importance of money in society. In the pages of 15 Years a Billionaire, he seeks to help other Christians understand money, how it can and should be used, and the difference having and using money in a wise way makes in the world. Ajiboye’s goal is to recruit billionaires to the body of Christ over the course of 15 years.
Readers will discover a mathematical formula that will assist in increasing financial capacity. The program is called the Double Growth Milestone and it incorporates the principle of compounding currency through saving, investing, and giving. By following the principles in this book, readers will be inspired to stay on an active path to creating long-lasting wealth, and ultimately to becoming a billionaire. In so doing, readers are advised to work toward eliminating poverty in every city of the world and training others to become billionaires who will in turn train others to do the same.

15 Years a Billionaire contains quotations, success stories, a global community tool, and a roadmap to becoming a billionaire Christian.

Olatayo E. Ajiboye is a Christian, and an inspired and passionate entrepreneur who believes that money is the answer to everything. He is determined to recruit billionaires to the body of Christ. He has an irrefutable passion for building a city without poverty through his weekly program titled “Billionaire Christian”, a program he dedicated to building courage and teaching biblical financial principles. He is the inventor of the Double Growth Milestone (DGM), and the founder of TalentCity.org, a platform he created to ignite a war against poverty in Christian society. He is also the founder of Talentcity International, Ltd. (www.talentcity.ng), and cofounder of Alliance Gygabyte Constructions and Properties Ltd. He serves as the director of operations for Alliance Constructions. Olatayo E. Ajiboye, his wife, Grace, and their daughter, Aura of God, live in Abuja.

To find out more about Olatayo Ajiboye, visit: www.15YearsaBillionaire.com.

To book Olatayo Ajiboye for speaking engagements and/or book signings, please email: engrolatayo@gmail.com.

15 Years a Billionaire is available on Amazon.com.
