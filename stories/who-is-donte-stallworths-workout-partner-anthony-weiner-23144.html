Who is Donte Stallworth’s Workout Partner? Anthony Weiner

May 21, 2014 Sports

Morning gym work and now @anthonyweiner is ready to try out for the NFL. (Twitter / @DonteStallworth)

Donte Stallworth may not be on an NFL roster now, but he often shares his daily workouts and attempts to keep in shape on social media. On Monday, he shared a photo with a new workout buddy: Disgraced former Congressman Anthony Weiner.

Weiner, who was last seen in the spotlight in a failed mayoral campaign in New York last year, resigned from Congress after a lewd Twitter scandal in 2011.
