Colorado Woman Who Cut Baby Out of Pregnant Mother Charged With Attempted Murder

March 28, 2015 What is this World Coming To?

The woman accused of luring a mother who was seven months pregnant to a basement and cutting the baby girl from her womb was charged Friday.
(Photo: KUSA)

The woman accused of luring a mother who was seven months pregnant to a basement and cutting the baby girl from her womb was charged Friday.

Dynel Lane, 34,​ was charged with first-degree attempted murder, unlawful termination of pregnancy in the first degree, first-degree assault and second-degree assault .

Prosecutors did not file murder charges against Lane. The coroner found no evidence that 25-year-old Michelle Wilkin’s baby showed signs of life outside the womb.

According to the police affidavit, Lane’s husband believed he heard the baby, Aurora, gasp for breath.

“I know it has been widely reported… that the witness observed Aurora taking a gasping breath,” District Attorney Stan Garnett said. “However, upon a thorough examination by the Longmont Police Department, the witness clarified that Aurora was still. Her mouth was open, but she was not breathing, which is consistent with the medical evidence from the autopsy.”
