Brandy and her Fiance Have Broken Off Their Marriage Engagement

April 16, 2014 Entertainment

Photo: Al Powers/Invision/AP

The boy isn’t hers anymore. Brandy and fiance Ryan Press have broken up and ended their engagement, a rep for the R&B singer confirms to Us Weekly. “They are no longer together. The engagement is off, but the friendship is still on,” the rep says in a statement.

PHOTOS: Biggest celebrity splits

Back in late December 2012, Us was first to break news that the Grammy winner, now 35, was engaged to the music executive. “They decided to take a step back and reevaluate the relationship,” the rep explains. “Brandy is 35 now — that’s what her whole #35 on Twitter is about, becoming her own woman.”
