The House of the Lord 40th Anniversary Celebration – “Remember, Refocus, Re-dream”

August 15, 2014 Christian

Bishop Joey & Pastor Cathy Johnson

2014 will be a year of continued celebration for Bishop F. Josephus Johnson II and The House of the Lord church family as we celebrate 40 years of ministry.

The House of the Lord was founded by a young Pastor Johnson on March 6, 1974 in the living room of his parents. Pastor Joey, as he was affectionately called, said yes to the call of God on his life and began his preaching and teaching ministry with just three members; his wife, Pastor Cathy Johnson, his mother, Minister Evelyn Johnson and a close friend of the family, Sister Willie Mae Sewell.

Between those three members in March 1974 forward to March of 2014, God has blessed The House of the Lord to see thousands of men, women, boys and girls either profess Jesus Christ as their Lord and Savior, rededicate their lives to Christ, or with Christian experience make The House of the Lord their church home. To this we say “To God Be the Glory, Great Things He Has Done”.

Bishop Johnson’s ministry journey has not only seen souls saved and lives changed, but God has blessed The House of the Lord to move to 4 different locations, before they would finally build and continue building at their present location, right in the heart of West Akron, Ohio. And at every move, while there were many naysayers, the miracle working power of God proved over and over again that with God, all things are possible. And so today, The House of the Lord is a church of many people of different colors, cultures and communities, worshipping together each week as one beautiful and real family.

While finding favor with God and with man, Pastor Joey has always extended genuine love and concern for other pastors and churches. Many of those pastors came together in March of 2004 as Bishop F. Josephus Johnson II was ordained the Presiding Bishop of the Beth-El Fellowship of Visionary Churches, becoming their apostolic covering. Again we say, “To God Be the Glory, Great Things He Has Done!”

During the month of September, we will honor Bishop Joey & Pastor Cathy on September 12th with a special Founder’s Day Service with Pastor John K. Jenkins, Sr., Senior Pastor of First Baptist Church of Glenarden in Upper Marlboro, MD bringing the Word and again on September 14th with morning Worship Services at 8am, 10:30am with Dr. Jerry Fryar, Senior Pastor, Gospel Lighthouse Church of Columbus, OH and our evening service with Dr. R.A. Vernon of The Word Church, Cleveland, OH at 6pm.

We’ll culminate our celebration on November 14th with a Service of Celebration featuring The House of the Lord Celebration Arts Ministry which will include choir, drama, dance etc.

Consider celebrating with us in 2014, as we continue giving God the glory for the work He has done, the work He is doing and the work He is yet to do.

September 14 – Founder’s Day Celebration, THOTL, Akron, OH

Friday & Saturday – October 17 & 18 – Beth-El Fellowship of Visionary Churches Leadership Conference and Convocation, THOTL, Akron, OH

Friday, November 14 – THOTL Celebration Arts Ministry Concert, Akron, OH

For more information visit our website at www.thotl.org.
