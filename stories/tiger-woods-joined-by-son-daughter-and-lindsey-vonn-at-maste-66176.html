Tiger Woods Joined by Son, Daughter, and Lindsey Vonn at Masters

April 8, 2015 Sports

Tiger Woods waits to put as girlfriend Lindsey Vonn, son Charlie and daughter Sam look on during the Par 3 Contest. Ezra Shaw, Getty Images

This might be the most fun Tiger Woods has had at the Masters.

With daughter Sam, son Charlie and girlfriend Lindsey Vonn tagging along, the four-time Masters champion played the Par 3 Contest for the first time since 2004 on Wednesday. They all appeared to be having a ball, with Woods and 7-year-old Sam playfully bumping one another as they made their way to the ninth green.

Charlie, 6, followed a few steps behind, proudly toting three of his dad’s irons that were almost as big as him.

Woods didn’t speak to reporters afterward, climbing into the front seat of a golf cart with Charlie on his lap and Sam and Vonn in the back seat. But in a statement distributed by his publicist, Woods said, “We had an absolutely great time. These were memories for a lifetime.”

The Par 3 is one of those traditions that makes the Masters so unique. Children, spouses and parents take over caddie duties, making for some adorable sights.

And it’s a light-hearted event because no one is trying to win — quite the opposite, in fact, because the Par 3 champion has never won the green jacket in the same year.

(Sorry, Kevin Streelman, the Par 3 champ at 5-under. Better luck, next year.)

Woods stopped playing the event before he had children. But when Sam and Charlie were watching the Par 3 last year, they asked if they could be a part of it.

“I said, `OK, we’ll see,'” Woods, who missed last year’s Masters after having surgery on a pinched nerve, told ESPN before his round. “It comes closer — and I hadn’t played in a while — and they started bringing it up.”
