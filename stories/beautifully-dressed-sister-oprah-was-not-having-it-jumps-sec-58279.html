Beautifully Dressed Sister Oprah Was Not Having It, Jumps Security Line at Academy Awards — and We Say Rightfully So

February 23, 2015 Entertainment

An elegant and floor-length dress didn’t stop Oprah Winfrey when she arrived at the Academy Awards on Sunday evening.

In fact, the 61-year-old media giant didn’t even slow down when faced with a security line.

The long-time television host and producer instead ducked under a velvet rope and made a run for it on the red carpet.

All seemed to be well with Oprah’s arrival until she got stopped by security with a few people ahead of her.

Oprah didn’t wait around to be searched or halted but instead lifted the black rope that had her stuck in the line.

Once on the other side, the one-time winner of the Jean Hersholt Humanitarian Award hurried away, almost tripping on her long skirt while a grinning security guard followed closely behind.
