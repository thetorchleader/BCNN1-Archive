Belfast, Northern Ireland Is Headed Towards Legalizing Abortion

October 12, 2012 Uncategorized

Mass murder could soon become a stark reality in Northern Ireland. That’s because Belfast is allowing the first private abortion clinic to open its doors there.

According to the Associated Press, the Marie Stopes clinic next week will begin offering non-surgical abortions to women whose pregnancies are less than nine weeks in gestation. Only women whose pregnancies doctors deem dangerous to the mother’s physical or mental health will be receive the miscarriage-causing medication. Of course, that’s an open invitation for unscrupulous doctors to give the nod to any and all abortions.
The abortion industry sees a huge opportunity in Belfast because it will draw women from across the nation. Statistics from the United Kingdom’s Department of Health reveal that at least 4,000 women from the Republic of Ireland traveled to England and Wales last year to have an abortion.
“This is an attempt by Marie Stopes to challenge the pro-life ethos and to try and circumvent the laws,” Niamh Ui Bhriain of the Dublin-based Life Institute, told Reuters. Marie Stopes clinic insists it will work within the law.
But it’s a slippery slope. Once you open the door for abortion, or any sin, you let give the enemy an invitation to wreak his havoc. Abortion is murder. If you murder a grown woman, you can spend life in prison. If you murder a pre-born child–and Marie Stopes brags on its website about preventing about 4.5 million “unintended pregnancies” in 2011–you collect a few hundred dollars.
