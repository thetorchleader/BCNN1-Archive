Donnie McClurkin Gets "Hooked" on Real Housewives of Atlanta, Calls NeNe Leakes "Beautiful"

December 27, 2012 Uncategorized

While the Christmas season has many Christian leaders professing their love for Jesus Christ and celebrating His birth, Pastor Donnie McClurkin was professing his love for Real Housewives of Atlanta reality TV star, NeNe Leakes on Twitter.
According to the recording artist and pastor of Perfecting Church, on Christmas Eve, he was alone at “home a little sick and recuperating,” so he got “hooked” on the drama-filled RHOA marathon.
“It was intriguing,” Pastor McClurkin said of the show. He also called Leakes, former stripper turned television star, “beautiful,” noting that he had met her once.
“Aww Thanks Donnie,” responded Leakes, re-tweeting McClurkin’s flattering words to her more than 900,000 followers.
The self-proclaimed “very rich” actress also stars in NBC’s new gay comedy “The New Normal,” with a basic premise that centers on a homosexual couple who hires a surrogate to have their baby.
