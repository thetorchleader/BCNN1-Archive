Love, Peace and Happiness Family Christian Fellowship Church Begins Annual Living Word Conference

May 31, 2015 Black

Love, Peace and Happiness Family Christian Fellowship Church kicks-off their Annual Living Word Conference 2015 on Monday, June 1, through Sunday, June 7, at their edifice at 4951 South Figueroa Street in Los Angeles.

According to Bishop Leon and Dr. Jacqueline Martin, conference hosts, “This conference will be like none other, as it will not be a traditional conference.  Rather, it will be an anointed and glorious time of fellowship as the spirit of God flows and rejuvenates the body of Christ.

“Bishop Richard Sanders of Mount Pilgrim Baptist Church in Compton will be the keynote speaker for Wednesday night.  Bishop Sanders has a powerful testimony that will stir your hearts about how God brought him back from death’s door.”

The conference theme is ‘Connecting To Kingdom Greatness Through the Power of the Holy Spirit.’  The Institute Hour takes place on Wednesday, June 3, through Friday, June 5, at 6 p.m., and evening worship begins at 7:15 p.m.

Other conference speakers are Dr. Marie Gardner from San Francisco, Overseer Curtis Perryman of Kingdom Life Faith Center in Los Angeles, Pastor Donald Martin of Fresh Anointing New Life Center in Claremont, and Bishop Alfred Smith, Sr., of New Hope Christian Church in Phoenix, AZ.
