Break Up Your Fallow Ground: It is Time to Seek the Lord (Part 2)

June 17, 2012 Uncategorized

Please turn in your Bibles to Hosea 10:9-13:

O Israel, thou hast sinned from the days of Gibeah: there they stood: the battle in Gibeah against the children of iniquity did not overtake them. It is in my desire that I should chastise them; and the people shall be gathered against them, when they shall bind themselves in their two furrows. And Ephraim is as an heifer that is taught, and loveth to tread out the corn; but I passed over upon her fair neck: I will make Ephraim to ride; Judah shall plow, and Jacob shall break his clods. Sow to yourselves in righteousness, reap in mercy; break up your fallow ground: for it is time to seek the Lord, till he come and rain righteousness upon you. Ye have plowed wickedness, ye have reaped iniquity; ye have eaten the fruit of lies: because thou didst trust in thy way, in the multitude of thy mighty men.

Sunday Evening Evangelistic Hour

PRAYER

Last week, we began this series of messages titled “Break Up Your Fallow Ground: It Is Time to Seek the Lord”. I truly believe that God is calling the church to repentance. He is calling us to repentance because he wants us to be effective and  powerful in our witness for him, and we cannot be that if we are living in sin, or if we are tolerating sin in our lives or in the lives of others.

We saw how the Prophet Hosea called out the children of Israel because of the sins that were being committed among them even though they were God’s people. We also saw how God called these people to repentance — to break up their fallow ground. We covered what it means to break up our fallow ground and the means by which we should go about doing this. Allow me to recap that briefly.

In our passage, Hosea 10:9-13, you might recall from last week that we read a message which God gave to the Prophet Hosea to give to the children of Israel. As you know, after the reign of King Solomon, Israel began to slide into sin, idolatry, and rebellion. Hosea tells the children of Israel: “thou hast sinned from the days of Gibeah.” Basically, he is saying, Israel your sin has been going on for a long time. The time Hosea refers to ‘in the days of Gibeah’ is recorded in Judges chapters 19 – 21. The story is of a Levite — a priestly man — who was confronted by a group of homosexual men in Gibeah who wished to have homosexual relations with him. Instead of this, the Levite gave the men his concubine and the farmer who the Levite was staying with, gave the men his daughter. And these men abused the women all night. When this wickedness was made known throughout Israel, the tribe of Benjamin went to war against the Israelites. This is the situation that Hosea is referring to when he says that Israel has been sinning since the days of Gibeah.

Matthew Henry’s Commentary tells us that Hosea urged the children of Israel to break up their fallow ground for six main reasons: (1) because it is high time to do so; (2) because if we repent, God will bestow upon us grace, forgive us and restore us to Himself; (3) if we live righteously, God’s mercy will be our reward; (4) our wickedness causes us to reap iniquity therefore, we should do what is necessary to let God save us; (5) sin will never produce good fruit in our lives; and (6) if we become comfortable in sin, we will find ourselves continuing to fall from God’s grace.

Unfortunately, in our churches, so many sermons and church services are wasted because the people of the church will not break up their fallow ground and deal with the sin in their lives. A preacher may preach long and hard, but it does no good if he is talking to hard-hearted people and if his heart is hard as well. Charles G. Finney said that these people, “are only half converted, and their religion is rather a change of opinion than a change of their hearts.” These people have a mechanical religion that does not reach deep into their hearts.

I have often preached revival meetings in churches where people had already made up their minds that they were not going to repent and change their behavior. One of the reasons why I refuse to take as many meetings as I have in earlier years is because I was going to Bible believing churches and the people had already made up their minds that they were not going to repent and change. They had already made up their minds that they were not going to pray as they should. They liked that girlfriend they had on the side. They liked that boyfriend they had on the side. They liked partying on Friday and Saturday night and falling off into the church on Sunday morning to see if they could keep the party going.

In fact, as an example of this, as the Lord allowed me to travel across the United States and around the world, I frequently preached a sermon on prayer titled “A Praying Time” or “Prayer: The Main Ingredient”. One of the most shocking things that I discovered was that not only were many in the pews not praying at all, but many in the pulpit were not praying as they should have been either. Preachers, in private conversation, would share with me, ‘Bro. Whyte, I hate to admit it, but I don’t pray as I should.’ One pastor who picked me up from an airport in a European country, where I was to preach a revival in his church, frankly told me, ‘I really have not been praying for this revival and I don’t even know if I want revival.’

That is the problem with the church. Our hearts are so cold, hard, and indifferent to the things of God, that our fallow ground must be broken up before the Lord can begin to work in our lives and in our church.

With that being said, today, we are going to pinpoint some of the sins in the church that we need to confess and repent of. This message will not be theoretical. This is an action message. If you are not willing to act upon what I am talking about, then you may not want to hear what I have to say. By the end of this message, I want you to examine your heart, confess all hidden sin, and have your fallow ground — your hard, unused, unproductive ground — broken up. It is time to break up our fallow ground because it is time to seek the Lord. You see, beloved, you don’t have any fruit in your life because you have fallow ground that you have to deal with.

So, let’s begin.

1. First of all, let’s look at the sin of ingratitude. Remember when Jesus Christ healed ten lepers and only one came back to thank Him. Luke 17:17-18 says that Jesus asked, “Were there not ten cleansed? but where are the nine? There are not found that returned to give glory to God, save this stranger.” It is sad to say but so many of us often receive blessings from God’s hands yet we fail to thank him. I often remind my children that I will not tolerate them being unthankful for all of the blessings of God.

God had to punish the children of Israel while they were in the wilderness because of their ingratitude. God had worked great miracles in bringing them out of slavery in Egypt, yet they constantly complained. When they complained about food, God gave them manna. Then they got sick of manna and grumbled because their was no meat. So, God gave them meat, and they got sick of that. They were ungrateful for God’s many blessings.

Numbers 11:1 says, “when the people complained, it displeased the Lord: and the Lord heard it; and his anger was kindled; and the fire of the Lord burnt among them, and consumed them that were in the uttermost parts of the camp.”

How many times in your life can you remember where God has blessed you and you have not expressed your gratitude to Him? Go before God and thank him for those blessings now. Unfortunately, I believe that we can find in our lives many things that we have failed to thank God for. Ask God to forgive you for being ungrateful and make it your purpose to thank God for every blessing that He has given you.

2. The second sin is a lack of love for God. Jesus Christ told the church at Ephesus in Revelation 2:4: “I have somewhat against thee, because thou hast left thy first love.” I wonder how many of us have let other things and other people get in the way of our relationship with God. Just think how you would feel if you discovered that your spouse or children loved someone or something else more than they loved you. Now, imagine how Jesus Christ feels after He suffered and gave his life to save us from Hell, and yet we withhold from Him our complete devotion and love and give it to something else or somebody else. In fact, some give it to the devil, the world and other enemies of God.
 
Sometimes, we let our careers get in between us and God. We love the job that God gave us more than we love God. We love the wife or husband God gave us more than we love God. We love the children, or the house, or the car that God gave us more than we love God. Jesus Christ said our love for everything else should seem like hate compared to our love for him. He said in Luke 14:26: “If any man come to me, and hate not his father, and mother
, and wife, and children, and brethren, and sisters, yea, and his own life also, he cannot be my disciple.” Our love for God should cause us to be willing to lay down our lives for Him just like He laid down His life for us.

If something stands between you and God, confess it as sin and get rid of it today. Do not let any idol, whether it is your family, friends, career, money, power, or hobby, stand between you and God. Return to your first love.

3. The third sin is a neglect of the Bible. The Bible says in Luke 4:4, “It is written, That man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word of God.” How many days have gone by when you didn’t make time to read the Word of God? Now, you may say, ‘Well, I meant to read it. I just didn’t have the time.’ My friend, you do not have time not to read the Bible. Unless you make the time to read the Bible, you will never have the time because the devil does not want you to read the wonderful love letter from God called the Bible.

There are many reasons why you ought to read the Bible. One of them is because you will get to know Jesus better through reading the Word of God. Jesus Christ said in John 5:39 says, “Search the scriptures; for in them ye think ye have eternal life: and they are they which testify of me.” If you want to deepen your relationship with God, you should start by reading His blessed book — the Word of God.

Another reason why you should be reading the Bible is because the Bible is a guidebook for life. The Bible has something to say on every major issue you will face. Psalm 119:105 says, “Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.”

Allow me to share with you these beautiful words written about the Bible by the folks at Gideons International. I first read these words when I was a young man. Back in those days, they gave you a little Bible when you went into the military. These words impressed me then and they still impress me now:

“The Bible contains the mind of God, the state of man, the way of salvation, the doom of sinners, and the happiness of believers. Its doctrines are holy, its precepts are binding, its histories are true, and its decisions are immutable. Read it to be wise, believe it to be safe, and practice it to be holy. It contains light to direct you, food to support you, and comfort to cheer you.

“It is the traveler’s map, the pilgrim’s staff, the pilot’s compass, the soldier’s sword, and the Christian’s charter. Here Paradise is restored, Heaven opened, and the gates of hell disclosed.

“Christ is the grand subject, our good the design, and the glory of God its end. It should fill the memory, rule the heart, and guide the feet. Read it slowly, frequently, and prayerfully. It is a mine of wealth, a paradise of glory, and a river of pleasure. It is given you in life, will be opened at the judgement, and be remembered forever. It involves the highest responsibility, will reward the greatest labor, and will condemn all who trifle with its sacred contents.”

Without God’s Word to guide us our lives will be spent at random and our Christianity will be a miserable failure. Knowing God’s Word will prevent us from being swayed by ‘every wind of doctrine’ that comes our way. If you don’t read the Word of God, you won’t give the Holy Spirit anything to work with inside of you.

No matter how long it has been since you opened the Bible on your own, confess your sin and begin setting aside time each day to read God’s Word.

4. The fourth sin is the sin of unbelief. Jesus Christ often rebuked his disciples for having “little faith” and not trusting God. Yes, not trusting God is a sin. Did you know that you are calling God a liar when you refuse to believe what He says? By not believing God, we deprive ourselves of the benefits of faith. Faith is what gives us the benefits of salvation. Faith also brings answers to prayer. Matthew 21:22 says, “whatsoever ye shall ask in prayer, believing, ye shall receive.” Faith is what makes our ministry effective. Jesus Christ told his disciples that if they had faith in God they would have the power to move mountains. Elsewhere, the Bible says that the “prayer of faith will heal the sick.” Without faith in God, nothing we do will be as effective as it should.

What are you doubting God about in your life? Are you doing things in the flesh without the faith that God can take your feeble work and do something great with it? My friend, if you are refusing to have faith in God for something in your life, confess it to Him as a sin, and cry out saying, “Lord, I believe, help thou mine unbelief.”

5. The fifth sin is the neglect of prayer. Jesus Christ said in Luke 18:1: “Men ought always to pray, and not to faint.” My friend, one of the greatest sins in our lives today is the sin of prayerlessnes — in our personal lives, in our families, in our churches, and for our communities. I do not think that the problem is that many of us do not know how to pray, but that for whatever reason, we simply do not pray. Not praying is a sin because it is disobedience to a specific command of God and it is contrary to common sense. Samuel understood this.  Samuel said in 1 Samuel 12:23-24, “Moreover as for me, God forbid that I should sin against the Lord in ceasing to pray for you: but I will teach you the good and the right way: Only fear the Lord, and serve him in truth with all your heart: for consider how great things he hath done for you.”

Without prayer in our lives, we will be unable to resist the devil and temptation. “The main reason why people do not want to pray is because they do not want to obey.” People don’t pray as they should simply because they do not want to let go of their sin. They know that if they are going to pray effectively they will have to let go of the sin in their lives. They know that if they pray, God will lead them away from temptation, sin and evil. And if you don’t want to be led out of temptation, evil and sin, then you will not want to pray.

When we are in a spirit of prayer, God will warn us of temptation, alert us to people who mean us no good, help us make the right decisions, and direct us throughout each day. Prayer is our lifeline to God. Part of putting on the whole armor of God is putting on the spirit of prayer. If you have been neglecting prayer, you are missing out on the protection, blessing, and guidance of the Holy Spirit.

Prayer is considered by many to be hard work. The devil only makes it seem hard because your flesh is against it and the devil does not want you to do it. If you have not been praying as you should, confess this sin, and begin to set aside specific times each day to pray and ask for God’s guidance in your life.

Well, we have covered some important ground today but unfortunately, we have many other sins to discuss. I want to encourage you to examine your heart and see if any of these sins are lurking there. If so, begin to break up your fallow ground by asking God to forgive you and cleanse you of your sins. It may be painful. It may be unpleasant, but this is an exercise that every follower of Christ should engage in. Your spiritual life will benefit tremendously because of it. If the Lord should tarry his coming and we live, I hope that you will be with us next week so you can continue on this journey with us of getting our hearts right with the Lord and serving Him.

__________________

Now, if you do not know Jesus Christ as your Saviour, allow me to show you how you can be saved from sin and the punishment of sin.

First, please understand that you are a sinner and that you have broken God’s laws. The Bible says in Romans 3:23: “For all have sinned and come short of the glory of God.” Please understand that because of our sins, we deserve punishment in hell. Romans 6:23 says “the wages of sin is death…” This is both physical death and spiritual death in hell.

But here is the good news. John 3:16 reads, “For God so loved the world, that He gave His only begotten Son, that whosoever bel
ieveth in Him should not perish, but have everlasting life.”

The phrase, “For God so loved the world” means that if you are in this world, God loves you. No matter what you have done or where you are in life.

The next phrase, “that He gave His only begotten Son”, refers to Jesus Christ. He is God’s son who suffered, bled, and died on the cross for your sins and for mine, and He was buried and rose again.

Our next phrase is “that whosoever believeth in Him”. The word “whosoever” means anybody at anytime. The phrase “believeth in Him” means to trust in Him, to depend upon Him, to rely on Him, or to have faith in Him for your salvation.

Our next phrase, “should not perish”, refers to eternal punishment in a place called hell.

And, lastly, the phrase “but have everlasting life” means to live eternally in Heaven with God. 

The Bible also says in Romans 10:9 and 13: “That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved…. For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.”

Dear friend, if you are willing to believe on the Lord Jesus Christ for salvation, please pray with me this simple prayer: Heavenly Father, I realize that I am a sinner and that I have done some bad things in my life. For Jesus Christ sake, please forgive me of my sins. I now believe with all of my heart that Jesus Christ died for me, was buried, and rose again. Lord Jesus, please come into my heart and save my soul and change my life today. Amen. 

If you prayed that prayer and meant it from your heart, congratulations on doing the most important thing in life and that is trusting Jesus Christ as your Lord and Saviour!

God bless you and have a great week.
