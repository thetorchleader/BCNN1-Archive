Transformation Christian Center in Lexington, North Carolina Finds a New Building and Sets Out to Transform Lives In the Community

June 8, 2013 Uncategorized

Transformation Christian Center has found a new home in Lexington by sharing space with another church, New Life Seventh-day Adventist at 922 Cotton Grove Road.


Pastor Curtis McLaughlin stands beside a sign for Transformation Christian Center on Cotton Grove Road.
While the partnership is not typical, the pastors of the two small churches said the arrangement makes sense and benefits both congregations. The approximately 30 members of New Life, led by Pastor Ronnie Williams, who also has a church in High Point, meet on Saturday instead of Sunday and on Wednesday night. The 18 members of Transformation, led by Senior Pastor Curtis McLaughlin, meet on Sunday and every other Thursday night.
Transformation had been meeting at the Quality Inn conference room since it started in Lexington in 2009 and had broken ground on a new building site at the corner of Holly Grove Road and Thomason Street. However, the untimely death of the nondenominational church’s founder, the late Bishop Otis Lockett Sr., in October 2012 has kept those plans from going forward at this time, McLaughlin said. Transformation is affiliated with the Memphis-based Church of God in Christ, the largest predominantly African-American Pentecostal church in the world, with about 6 million members.
McLaughlin said he met Williams and the two discovered sharing costs could benefit both congregations in the interim. Transformation began meeting at the Cotton Grove Road church building in May.
“It’s been a blessing, and I think it’s going to be a great opportunity,” McLaughlin said. “The one common denominator we have is the Lord Jesus Christ, and we both believe in the Bible.”
