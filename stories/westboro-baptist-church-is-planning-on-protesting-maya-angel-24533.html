Westboro Baptist Church Is Planning on Protesting Maya Angelou’s Funeral

May 30, 2014 Black

The Westboro Baptist Church is known for protesting soldier’s funeral’s across the United States. The group is now planning to protest renowned poet Maya Angelou’s funeral. The St. Louis native died Wednesday in her Winston-Salem home at age 86.

The church has asked their Twitter followers for information on Maya Angelou’s funeral. They also tweeted various hateful things about Angelou on Wednesday and Thursday.

Angelou’s funeral arrangements have not yet been announced.
