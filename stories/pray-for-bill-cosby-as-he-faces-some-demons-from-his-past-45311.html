Pray for Bill Cosby as he Faces Some Demons from his Past

November 15, 2014 Entertainment

Bill Cosby (Photo: Evan Vucci, AP)

At least two of Bill Cosby’s talk-show appearances have been canceled amid resurgent allegations he has sexually assaulted multiple women over the years.

On Friday, his publicist, David Brokaw, announced he would not appear on the Nov. 19 episode of The Late Show With David Letterman. Staff at The Queen Latifah Show say Cosby’s camp “postponed” his Oct. 30 visit.

On Saturday, during an interview with NPR’s Weekend Edition, interviewer Scott Simon asked Cosby about the “serious allegations raised” about him. The comedian, in response, shook his head to signify no. So Simon asked if he wanted to address the allegations. Cosby again shook his head, and said nothing.

The uproar against Cosby picked up steam this week when The Washington Post published an online column by one of his accusers, Barbara Bowman. “Cosby won my trust as a 17-year-old aspiring actress in 1985, brainwashed me into viewing him as a father figure, and then assaulted me multiple times,” she wrote.

Cosby didn’t help matters when he invited his Twitter followers to turn him into meme, inviting ridicule.

Those incidents followed a standup comedy performance by Hannibal Buress, in which he called Cosby a rapist.

Cosby, 77, has never been charged for any of these alleged incidents, though he did settle a civil suit with one accuser in 2006.

The comedian had originally scheduled public appearances to promote the opening of Conversations:African and African-American Artworks in Dialogue, featuring works he and wife Camille have on loan to the Smithsonian Museum of African Art.
