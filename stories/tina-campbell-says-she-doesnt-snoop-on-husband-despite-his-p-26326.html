Tina Campbell Says She Doesn’t Snoop on Husband Despite His Past Cheating

June 12, 2014 Entertainment

Tina Atkins-Campbell (Credit: GETTY)

If everyone were like Tina Atkins-Campbell, 40, of the award-winning duo Mary Mary, popular snooping apps like “Connect,” “Trick or Tracker,” and “Phone Tracker,” wouldn’t have a prayer of being downloaded.

Even though her husband Teddy Campbell has gone on record admitting he cheated multiple times with various women, the missus says she doesn’t snoop on him.

“I’m not that chick,” she told Sister 2 Sister Magazine in an exclusive interview. She and her sister Erica Atkins-Campbell are featured on the cover of the urban publication’s June/July issue.

“I think too much of myself to be a detective behind some man. And if I’m going to trust you and give you a chance, I’m going to give you a real chance and a real chance is not holding your hand and micromanaging,” she said.

When it comes to secretly prying on a spouse, a 2013 UK study by Mobile Phone Checker, a cell phone outlet company, revealed that men are more likely than women to look through their partner’s phone without their knowledge.
