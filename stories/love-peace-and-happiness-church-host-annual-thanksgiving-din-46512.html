Love, Peace and Happiness Church Host Annual Thanksgiving Dinner

November 23, 2014 Black

Bishop Leon Martin

Bishop Leon and Dr. Jacqueline Martin and the members of Love, Peace and Happiness Church (LPH) will minister to the community through their Annual Pre-Thanksgiving Dinner.

The free event takes place on Sunday, November 23, immediately following the 11:15 a.m. worship at the LPH Los Angeles location at 4951 S. Figueroa Street. During the service, Bishop Martin will offer prayer for those with special needs.

“We extend an invitation to all to come and enjoy this worship experience and old-fashioned Thanksgiving Dinner that is designed for the community, the homeless and hurting,” said Bishop Martin.

“Love, Peace and Happiness Church have presented this event for the past several years.  It is our endeavor to extend to the community an appreciation of thanks and express our love to the misfortunate during this economic crisis.”
