Bobbi Kristina Turns 22

March 4, 2015 National

It is Bobbi Kristina Brown’s 22nd birthday on March 4. The daughter of late singer Whitney Houston and Bobby Brown is currently fighting for her life at the Emory University Hospital in Atlanta since almost a month now. The reality star and singer celebrated her last birthday (in 2014) with her boyfriend Nick Gordon and the couple was very happy.

“Thank you all for my birthday wishes !! ‪@nickdgordon I love you more then you’ll ever know! Always nxt 2U:) ‪#B&C,” tweeted Bobbi on March 4, 2014. The singer also posted a picture of a Louis Vuitton Bag that was gifted to her by Nick on her birthday. Another picture showed Bobbi in a tiny black two-piece bikini by American lingerie brand Femme Couture. She credited Nick and the brand for her great birthday picture. Check it out here.

On March 6, Nick also tweeted a picture of Bobbi in a swimsuit where she looked very thin. “I gotta bad b-tch ‪@REALbkBrown,” Nick wrote along with picture of Bobbi. Bobbi was criticised by media for the same picture as she had lost a lot of weight.
