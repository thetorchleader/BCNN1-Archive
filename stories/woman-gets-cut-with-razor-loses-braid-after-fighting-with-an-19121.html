Woman Gets Cut With Razor, Loses Braid After Fighting With Another Woman Over Man at Oregon Church

April 22, 2014 What is this World Coming To?

KREM.com

A woman suffered a large cut to her arm and lost a braid during a fight over a man that followed an Easter church service, police said.

Officers were dispatched about 2:45 p.m. to Oliver Elementary School in Southeast Portland on a report of a fight between two women, said Sgt. Pete Simpson. The school is used by the Life Restoration Church for Sunday services.

Police found that Anjeanette Marie Brown, 32, had a large cut on her arm and was missing a woven braid.

Naomi Etta Perkins, 30, was arrested for third-degree assault.
