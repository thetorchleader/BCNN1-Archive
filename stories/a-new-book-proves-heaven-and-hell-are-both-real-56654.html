A New Book Proves Heaven And Hell Are Both Real

February 13, 2015 Books

In the later part of 2004, Olabisi Obideyi experienced an ‘open vision’ experience which helped her to understand without a doubt that Heaven and Hell are both real. Her life changing encounter of heaven and hell, which she describes in Hell On Earth are both daunting yet extremely convincing. With great clarity and detail, she recalls the emotional, physical, and mental torture she endured in her encounter of hell. She also describes her time in heaven, giving her the opportunity to thoroughly compare and contrast hell and heaven for the reader.

In Hell on Earth, Olabisi Obideyi takes readers along on her journey. Her story is challenging, and will be life-changing for any reader. While people have various beliefs about heaven and hell, Olabisi removes any confusion or misunderstanding and leads all people to avoid the tortures of hell by trusting in Jesus Christ for salvation.

Pastor Olabisi became the founder and Senior Pastor of Freedom House International Ministries following a Holy Spirit encounter she had back in the year 2004. Further to her initial deliverance experience in the early part of year 2004, Pastor Olabisi had another major ‘open vision’ encounter of heaven and hell in the latter part of the year 2004 which are now detailed in this book. The combination of her experiences have further strengthened her passion to spread the Good News of Christ’s deliverance powers across the globe using all available avenues including television, radio, Internet and also through her Holy Spirit inspired writings. Pastor Olabisi holds an LLB (hons) degree in law.

For more information about Olabisi Obideyi and her contact details visit: www.PastorOlabisi.com.

Hell on Earth is available on Amazon.com and Amazon UK.
