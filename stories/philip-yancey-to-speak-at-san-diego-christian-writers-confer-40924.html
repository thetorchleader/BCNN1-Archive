Philip Yancey to Speak at San Diego Christian Writers Conference This Week

October 23, 2014 Books

Philip Yancey

Critically acclaimed author Philip Yancey will speak at the San Diego Christian Writers Conference, October 24 & 25, in San Diego, which is being held at Maranatha Chapel.

According to a news release, Philip has sold fifteen million copies of his books worldwide, making him one of the best-selling evangelical Christian authors. Two of his books have won the ECPA’s Christian Book of the Year Award: The Jesus I Never Knew and What’s So Amazing About Grace?

The SDCWG Conference includes practical workshops, author consultations and a Friday night writer’s Q&A Roundtable with the faculty.

With the theme, Writing for Life, the annual conference is known for its inspiration, practical help, and motivation for both beginning and experienced writers!

Yancey will lead an impressive faculty that will include ACFW’s Agent of the Year Chip MacGregor, New York Times bestselling writer of Heaven is for Real, Lynn Vincent, and corporate educator Ellie Kay. Ellie received the Army’s highest civilian medal for improving the quality of life for soldiers and their families and is the author of 14 books.

Other notable faculty includes Barbara Nicolosi-Harrington, the Founder of Act One, a program to train and mentor Christians for careers as Hollywood writers and executives. Barbara has appeared as a cultural commentator on ABC, NBC, CBS, NPR, among others.

Another is Arlene Pellicane, the author of 31 Days to Becoming a Happy Wife, 31 Days to a Happy Husband and 31 Days to a Younger You. Arlene has provided expertise as a guest for the Today Show and Family Life Today.
