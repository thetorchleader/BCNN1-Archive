Donnie McClurkin Christmas Gospel Special on TBN

December 9, 2011 Uncategorized

Up and coming gospel music recording artist Janice Gaines will appear on a special Christmas Edition of TBN’s Praise the Lord, hosted by Pastor Donnie McClurkin, on December 13, 2011 at 10pm ET/7pm PT. Other guests slated to perform include Micah Stampley, Trent Cory, and Beckah Shae.
Gaines will perform original arrangements of “Little Drummer Boy” and a Christmas Worship Medley of “O Come, O Come Emmanuel” and Amy Grant’s “Breath of Heaven.” Produced by Dwan Hill for Crystal City, Inc., the two holiday songs will be available for download on iTunes on December 13, 2011.
Janice Gaines just wrapped her second year touring as a worship leader with the Women of Faith “Imagine” conference, leading as many as 12,000 women in worship each weekend in sold-out arenas across the country.
