Yolanda Adams and T. D. Jakes Inspire Teens to Dream Big at 2015 Disney Dreamers Academy

March 9, 2015 Christian

TD Jakes inspires teens and families at Disney Dreamers Academy 2015 (Credit: Instagram)

Yolanda Adams and Bishop T.D. Jakes inspired teens to dream big.

100 youth, chosen out of nearly 10,000 applicants, enjoyed an all-expense paid trip to Walt Disney World Resort for Steve Harvey and Essence Magazine’s 2015 Disney Dreamers Academy.

“If you can get your focus on what’s in front of you, instead of what’s behind you, you can do some amazing stuff,” said Jakes, 57.

Among the teens, called “Dreamers,” were aspiring actors, chefs, entrepreneurs, engineers, doctors and journalists, with a deep passion to make the world better.

“I am telling you right here and right now, it does not matter where you start. It matters where you finish. Use what’s working against you to work for you.”
