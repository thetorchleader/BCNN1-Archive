Rick Warren Shows Concern for Religious Freedom at Georgetown University Event

February 13, 2013 Uncategorized

California Pastor Rick Warren says religious freedom is under attack in America.
The Saddleback Church senior pastor called President Obama’s birth control mandate a direct attack on religious liberty, and he said the U.S. Supreme Court will strike it down.
Warren went on to say that if pastors are barred from praying at public functions because of their beliefs about homosexuality, then 99 percent of American pastors will be excluded.
Warren made his remarks Tuesday at a forum on religious liberty at Georgetown University in Washington, D.C.
