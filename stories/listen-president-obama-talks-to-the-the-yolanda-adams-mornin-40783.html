LISTEN: President Obama Talks to the ‘The Yolanda Adams Morning Show’ About the Importance of Voting in the Midterms

October 23, 2014 Black, Politics

President Barack Obama has accomplished a lot in six years, but he won’t be able to make more without support in the forthcoming midterm elections. He talks exclusively to to “The Yolanda Adams Morning Show” about the importance of heading to the polls this time around.

Listen to the audio player to hear the plans President Obama has that will help African-Americans, and what he needs the community to do to make it happen. Find out more about the voting process, laws, and how social media will play a role in this exclusive interview.
