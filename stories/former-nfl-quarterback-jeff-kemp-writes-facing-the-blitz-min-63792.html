Former NFL Quarterback Jeff Kemp Writes “Facing the Blitz” Ministry Book

March 26, 2015 Books

A former NFL quarterback-turned-family advocate explains in a new book that facing the difficulties of life is like a football team facing a blitz.

Jeff Kemp and his father Jack were the first set of father-son quarterbacks in the NFL, and now the son has written “Facing the Blitz.”

The book uses the metaphor of the blitz, the author explains, because during dangerous and painful moments, and despite difficult times, there is still the chance to turn them into something with a “bigger and better purpose.”

After his time in the NFL, Jeff is serving with the ministry FamilyLife as a marriage and relationship speaker.
