Will Jameis Winston Throw at the NFL Combine?

February 17, 2015 Sports

(Gary A. Vasquez-USA TODAY Sports)

Former Florida State quarterback Jameis Winston is mulling whether to participate in throwing drills Saturday in Indianapolis at the NFL scouting combine, according to his quarterback coach.

“It’s a game-time decision,” George Whitfield Jr. told USA TODAY Sports Monday morning. “He’s a very big believer in himself, so it’ll be interesting. He’s prepared to do it. Whether he goes in and does it will probably be determined a couple of days from now.

“At this time, he hasn’t made a decision.”

One thing is for sure: Winston will show up in shape, Whitfield said.

He laughed off the misconception created by a picture that appeared Saturday on Twitter showing a puffy-looking Winston when the 6-4, 230-pound quarterback first began workouts in San Diego with Whitfield five weeks ago.

“He’s lost about 12-15 pounds,” Whitfield said. “When you hear stuff about Jameis, the weight and he’s getting fat — What do people think that I’ve been golfing while he’s been playing Call of Duty?

Whitfield said the picture was taken on the second day of workouts. The picture shows a bungee cord wrapped around Winston’s waist, and pulled tightly to help keep Winston positioned properly during a drill.
