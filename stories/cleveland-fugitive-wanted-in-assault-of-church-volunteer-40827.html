Cleveland Fugitive Wanted in Assault of Church Volunteer

October 21, 2014 Black

Officials are searching for a suspect wanted on felonious assault charges.

Fifty-one-year-old Roderic Benson allegedly assaulted a volunteer at a local church in the West Side of Cleveland, according to the Northern Ohio Violent Fugitive Task Force.

Officials are offering a reward for information that would lead to his capture.

Officials describe Benson as a black male, about 5-foot-6 and 165 pounds.

Benson lived in the East Side of Cleveland but is now believed to homeless.
