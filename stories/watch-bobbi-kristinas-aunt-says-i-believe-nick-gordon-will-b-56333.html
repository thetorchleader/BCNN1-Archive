WATCH: Bobbi Kristina’s Aunt Says ‘I Believe Nick Gordon Will Be Charged’

February 11, 2015 Entertainment

© Provided by TheWrap

Bobbi Kristina Brown’s aunt believes two things about her nieces life: It will be saved, and her boyfriend Nick Gordon is responsible for almost ending it.

Leolah Brown told Fox 5 Atlanta that she thinks Gordon will “110 percent” be charged for a crime when the investigation into the mysterious circumstances surrounding Bobbi Kristina’s health crisis is completed.

“I’m not going to make a comment about that, but I believe Nick Gordon will be charged with this,” Bobby Brown’s sister said during a 16-minute interview. “I hope that he will be charged with this, as well. Soon. I really do.”

Gordon and a friend found Bobbi Kristina face down in a bathtub filled with water in her Atlanta-area home on Jan. 31 and rushed her to the hospital, where she has been on life support ever since. Authorities are investigating Gordon’s possible involvement, as well as injuries found around Bobbi Kristina’s face and mouth.
