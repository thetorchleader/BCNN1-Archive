Church of God In Christ International Mass Choir to Record CD Project In Honor of Bishop Charles Harrison Mason’s 150th Birthday

August 18, 2014 Entertainment

Church Of God In Christ International Mass Choir

MASS CHOIR REHEARSAL CALL FOR MASON LIVE RECORDING

The IMD will be celebrating the 150th birthday of the founder of the Church Of God In Christ, Bishop Charles Harrison Mason, by recording a CD project commemorating his life, leadership and legacy.

THE FIRST REHEARSAL CONVENES:
Monday, August 18th – 7:00 PM
Greater Community COGIC (East Memphis Location)
5151 Winchester Road
Memphis, TN 38118
(901) 542-5255

Please be aware that there is a registration fee a SPECIAL uniform for the three days. FOR MORE INFORMATION CALL 1-800-304-2411 or email the IMD Office of Public Relations at rbriggs@cogic.org.
