T.D. Jakes, Paul S. Morton, and Other Christian Leaders, Celebrities Mourn the Loss of Myles Munroe, Family, and Ministry Associates

November 10, 2014 Christian

Bishop T.D. Jakes

There are few biblical clinicians who’s view of ministry wasn’t enriched in some way by this tremendous bible scholar. His voice changed the way we viewed the Kingdom! Dr. and Mrs. Myles Munroe and associates, gone but not forgotten! #DrMylesMunroe

Bishop Paul S. Morton

Dr Myles Munroe touched so many lives including mine he will be greatly missed

Yolanda Adams

My heart is extremely heavy upon hearing of the passing of my Dear Friend Dr. #MylesMunroe and his Family and Staff. An Encourager and Inspirer to all He knew. May the LORD GRANT SOLACE AND COMFORT TO THOSE WHO MOURN WITH ME!! Love Yall Yolanda

Alfred Edmonds Jr.

Prayers & God’s matchless comfort & peace to the family and congregation of Dr. & Mrs. Myles Munroe. #RIP #TrustGod

Creflo Dollar

Taffi and I are saddened to hear about the passing of Dr. Myles Munroe and his wife, Ruth. We send our prayers and condolences to the Munroe family and all families affected in today’s tragic event.

Fred Hammond

Like the rest of the body of Christ I’m Beyond shocked and devastated at the news of Pastor and friend @mylesmunroe. Heartbroken!..

Mara Brock Akil

So sad… May their families be comforted at this time… RT @zellieimani: Update: Dr. Myles Munroe

Rickey Smiley

Prayers and condolences to the family and friends of Dr. Myles Munroe who tragically died in a plane crash today…

Ricky Dillard

Life is but a vapor. We celebrate the Creator for the life and legacy of Dr. Myles Munroe. “Tragedies are common place…”

Benjamin Crump

My condolences go out to the family of Pastor @mylesmunroe, his spouse and passengers who lost their lives in today’s tragic plane crash.
