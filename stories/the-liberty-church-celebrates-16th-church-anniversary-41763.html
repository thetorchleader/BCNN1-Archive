The Liberty Church Celebrates 16th Church Anniversary

October 26, 2014 Black

Pastor David Cross

The Liberty Church members continue their Sweet 16th Church Anniversary on Sunday, October 19, at 8:45 a.m. and 11 a.m., at 14725 South Gramercy Place in Gardena.

The theme is ‘Always Abounding’ based on 2 Corinthians 9:8 and Pastor Cross plans to deliver a message revealing the ‘Steps to Financial Overflow.’   Also, financial counselors will be available on Wednesday, October 22, at 7 p.m., to provide detailed information.

On Saturday, October 25, the celebration will culminate with the First Family Fun Basketball Competition Day from 10:30 a.m. until 4:30 p.m.  The contest will include the 3-point shoot-out, free throw, slam-dunk, 3-on-3 and arcade basketball for small children.  Contestants must have a sponsor(s) to participate. Awards will be presented to all winners.
