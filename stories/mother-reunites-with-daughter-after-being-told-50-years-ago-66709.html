Mother Reunites with Daughter After Being Told 50 Years Ago that She Was Dead

April 11, 2015 Black

The daughter she thought she lost in childbirth actually had been adopted by another family.

Hospital staff told Zella Jackson Price, 76, that her daughter died only hours after she was born 49 years ago. But for reasons that aren’t exactly clear, Price’s daughter, who was actually very much alive, was adopted by another family, Fox 12 reports.

Price was reunited with her daughter, Melanie Diane Gilmore, after Gilmore’s children did some digging. They were able to break the news to their mother and reunited Gilmore and Price via video chat.
