James Arthur McAbee, the ‘Pistol Packing Pastor’ in Texas, to Give Up Concealed Gun Licenses, Accepts Plea Deal on Marijuana Charge

August 21, 2014 Christian

A Beaumont preacher known as the “Pistol Packing Pastor” will have to surrender his concealed handgun licenses after taking a plea deal on a marijuana charge.

The Beaumont Enterprise reports James Arthur McAbee pleaded no contest Wednesday to one count of marijuana possession. He was sentenced to two years deferred probation and a $2,000 fine. He will also surrender his concealed handgun licenses from Texas, Arizona and Florida.

The state dismissed an unlawful carrying of a weapon charge as part of the plea agreement.
