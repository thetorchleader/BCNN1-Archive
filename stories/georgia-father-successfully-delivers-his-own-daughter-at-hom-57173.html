Georgia Father Successfully Delivers His Own Daughter at Home

February 17, 2015 Health

Eric and Taneca Matthews

For many couples, childbirth is one of their most memorable moments. For Eric and Taneca Matthews, the birth of their second child was made even more special.

Eric helped deliver his own daughter at home thanks to a quick thinking 911 operator.

It all happened so fast and in the blink of an eye, baby Peyton came into the world.

It all started last Tuesday when Eric got home from work unusually early.

Turns out, his wife Taneca had just gone into labor so he quickly called 911.

“I could feel her coming so I knew we weren’t going to make it anywhere,” said Mrs. Matthews. “So I told him to just go ahead and call.”

Eric says the operator was great and walked him through a lot of things.

“The operator said ‘I’m going to stay on the phone with you until the EMTs come’ and then I saw a forehead and I just dropped the phone,” said Eric.
