Find Out How to Forget the Past and Make 2012 Your Best Year in Book by Bestselling Author Daniel Whyte III

December 23, 2011 Books

National bestselling author, Daniel Whyte III, has released an updated version of his book titled “How to Forget the Troubles, Problems, and Failures of the Past and Make the New Year the Best Year of Your Life.”
Whyte writes in his introduction, “We are living in ‘times that try men’s souls.’ These are truly difficult days for many people around the world. This little book is written to encourage you to forget the troubles, problems, and even failures of the past and to truly have the best year of your life. If you do what I am encouraging you to do in this little book, by the grace of God and with His blessings on your life, you will have a great year. How do I know? Because in my own life, each of the last five years have consistently gotten better than the year before, and this year (2011) has been the best year of my life because I am following the simple principles contained in this book. So shake the previous year off of you, and get ready for the best year of your life!”
Whyte goes on to say, “I am thankful to God for giving us time-markers. I believe that in His grace and mercy, He has done so to give us feeble and frail human beings the joy and privilege of starting over. Like so many others, I get excited about a New Year.”
This short book will show you: 
    •      How to learn from your past troubles and problems and then forget them
    •      How to recover from your past failures and sins and then forget them
    •      How to get away from negative, toxic people
    •      How to forgive others and how to seek forgiveness
    •      How to set new goals for the future and accomplish them
    •      How to prioritize your life
    •      and How to make the New Year the best year of your life!
Laced with biblical principles, life-changing pointers, and powerful quotes on living life, loving God, and looking forward to a bright future, How to Forget the Troubles, Problems, and Failures of the Past and Make the New Year the Best Year of Your Life will bless readers’ hearts and help them make the New Year the absolutely best year of their life.
Many readers will also enjoy that the book now includes a Christian New Year’s song written by the author. The song is titled “Let All Past Troubles Be Forgotten” and is to be sung to the popular and familiar tune of “Let Old Acquaintance be Forgot” (Auld Lang Syne).
Daniel Whyte III has spoken in meetings across the United States and in twenty-three foreign countries. He is the author of seventeen books. He is also the president of Gospel Light Society, a worldwide evangelistic ministry that reaches thousands with the Gospel each week, as well as president of Torch Ministries International, a Christian literature ministry which publishes a monthly magazine called The Torch Leader. He holds a Bachelor’s Degree in Theology from Bethany Divinity College, a Bachelor’s degree in Religion from Texas Wesleyan University, and a Master’s degree in Religion from Liberty Baptist Theological Seminary. He has been married to the former Meriqua Althea Dixon, of Christiana, Jamaica for twenty-five years. God has blessed their union with seven children.
For more information about this dynamic book, visit: http://www.TheBestNewYearofYourLife.com and http://www.DanielWhyte3.com.
How to Forget the Troubles, Problems, and Failures of the Past and Make the New Year the Best Year of Your Life is available in e-book format on Amazon.com KINDLE for just $2.99, where readers can buy it and start reading it in 60 seconds or less. It can also be downloaded onto the Sony Reader, iPad, Blackberry, iPhone, and other e-book formats. The print edition is also available for $7.99.
