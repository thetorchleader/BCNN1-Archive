After Mother Is Discovered Dead in Fridge in Houston, Baby Is Found Safe in Mexico

December 13, 2014 Black

Eleven-month-old Achilles Lambert.
(Photo: Provided by Houston Police Department)

A baby reported missing after his mother’s body was discovered in a refrigerator at a Houston apartment was found safe in Mexico on Friday.

Patrick Lambert, father of 11-month-old Achilles Lambert, was taken into custody Friday afternoon by Mexican authorities with help from the FBI, according to Houston police. Both are being returned to the U.S.

Achilles was found in Querétaro, Mexico, about 10 hours south of the border.Investigators are still looking into how Patrick Lambert, 38, and Achilles ended up in Mexico.

The boy’s mother, 27-year-old Anastacia Oalikhena Lambert, was found dead inside a refrigerator in the family’s apartment in Houston on Monday. Police named Patrick Lambert as a person of interest in her murder.
