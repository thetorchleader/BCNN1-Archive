Black World War II Vets to Receive Congressional Medals

May 28, 2012 Uncategorized

The Montford Point Marines — African Americans who were subjected to segregated training and relegated to supporting roles in World War II — will be honored next month with a Congressional Gold Medal at ceremonies in Washington, D.C.
Even after President Franklin D. Roosevelt ordered the military in 1941 to accept African Americans, the Marine Corps balked at taking them into their ranks. Those joining the Corps were cast in the secondary role by the degrading belief they lacked the character and skills to fight for their country.
Last year, Congress voted for, and President Barack Obama approved, a Congressional Gold Medal for their service.
