New Orleans Father “Devastated” After Youngest Son is Second in the Family to be Gunned Down

September 5, 2014 National

Family and friends gather on Thursday, Sept. 4, for a vigil in memory of William Eleby, 25, who was gunned down the day before in the area of South Saratoga and Amelia streets. (Jonathan Bullington, NOLA.com | The Times-Picayune)

Just off the kitchen of his bustling home, Griffin Eleby sat by himself — head in hands — at the edge of the mattress in the back bedroom.

“My youngest son,” he repeated, mustering every ounce of available strength in his tired body to maintain composure. “I am just so devastated.”

Family and friends filled Eleby’s home Thursday evening (Sept. 4), one day after the 54-year-old’s youngest of four children was fatally shot down the street from the family’s home in the 3600 block of Danneel Street.

William Eleby, 25, was walking in the 2100 block of Foucher Street around 4 p.m. Wednesday when gunshots rang out, striking him multiple times. He managed to run to nearby Amelia Street before collapsing in the 2000 block.

He was pronounced dead at the scene.

William Eleby is the second member of the Eleby family taken by gun violence in the last four months. Griffin Eleby’s 14-year-old great nephew, Miquial Jackson, was shot and killed in May.
