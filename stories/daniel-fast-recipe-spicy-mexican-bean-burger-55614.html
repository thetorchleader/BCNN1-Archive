Daniel Fast Recipe: Spicy Mexican Bean Burger

February 7, 2015 God & Food

Spicy Mexican Black Bean Burgers (lowfatveganchef.com)

Minutes to Prepare: 10
Minutes to Cook: 15
Number of Servings: 8

Ingredients

1-16oz red kidney beans, drained & mashed
1 c. coarsley chopped onion
1 medium carrot, steamed & mashed
2 T. picante sauce or salsa(spicy or mild to taste)
1 c. crushed rice cakes (approx. 2 cakes)
1/2 c. whole wheat flour
1/2 t. salt (to taste)
1/2 t. pepper (to taste)
dash of chili powder

Directions

1. Mix all ingredients in a large mixing bowl. Add more flour to create a firmer mixture, or more salsa if mixture is too stiff.

2. Form mixture into balls & form into patties.

3. Bake 450 degrees, 10-15 minutes, until firm, brown, & done.
