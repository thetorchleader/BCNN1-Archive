WATCH: Portland Church Worships Outside After Church Burns Down

October 10, 2011 Uncategorized

Sunday morning a Portland congregation held their service within a few feet of where their church burned to the ground.

The Free Will Baptist Church went up in flames last Tuesday, but the congregation was determined to meet for their weekly worship anyway.
Members sang songs from underneath a tent on the property in Portland. They say it’s something they’ve done every Sunday since 1955, and even though this Sunday they have no walls to worship in, service should go on.
“To hear the congregation singing and knowing that we are having service; this is not stopping us; we are having service,” said member Shawn Gregory.
His father, Pastor Kenneth Gregory, kept Sunday’s sermon uplifting, “God shall rise this place up again out of the ashes. It will rise again. He is blessing me right now, looking at all these people, smiling faces. He is blessing me right now,” he said.
But it’s no doubt been a difficult week for Gregory and the entire congregation. On Tuesday an electrical problem sent Free Will up in flames.
The neighboring church daycare was evacuated, and all of the employees and children made it out uninjured. But, explaining this devastating loss to the youngest members of Free Will hasn’t been easy. “They keep looking at the window, and the church is broken, yeah the church is broken, but if God didn’t want us to have a new one, he wouldn’t have made it catch on fire,” said Jessi Kirisits.
“I said we are going to have a different kind of church. Kind of like a picnic church. We are going to have church outside and have one heck of a day,” said member Sarah Conway who also works with the children in the daycare center.
