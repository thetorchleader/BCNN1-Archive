President Obama Meets With Naghmeh Abedini, Says Freeing Pastor Saeed Is a ‘Top Priority’

January 22, 2015 Christian

Naghmeh Abedini

Before his speech at the Caven-Williams Sports Complex, President Obama met privately with Naghmeh Abedini, the wife of a Boise pastor now imprisoned in Iran.

Saeed Abedini has been in Iranian custody for nearly two-and-a-half years, and is two years into an eight-year sentence for what Iranian authorities call “undermining state security.”

His family and other supporters say he’s being punished for practicing his Christian faith, and many of them rallied at BSU before the president’s speech today.

Naghmeh Abedini has urged the president to listen to her plea for Saeed’s freedom, and today, she had the president’s ear.

“He did mention that he was getting updates and he was touch with John Kerry and this was a priority for him, he mentioned it was top priority something that was always brought up,” said Naghmeh Abedini.

Naghmeh says the president urged her to set up a meeting with the administration’s new religious freedom ambassador.
