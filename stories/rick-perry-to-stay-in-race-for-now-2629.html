Rick Perry to Stay In Race for Now

January 4, 2012 Uncategorized

After initially signaling that his campaign might be over, Rick Perry is suggesting today that he won’t abandon his presidential bid.
The Texas governor wrote on his Twitter account: “Here we come South Carolina!” He said it’s “the next leg of the marathon.”
Perry attached a photo of himself jogging near a lake.
After winning 10 percent of the vote yesterday in Iowa, Perry said he would return to Texas to see “whether there is a path forward” for his White House bid. He told supporters in Iowa last night that he appreciated their work, but that he needed to consider whether there was a viable strategy for him to restart his campaign in South Carolina.
