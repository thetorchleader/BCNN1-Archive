Maryland Man Charged with Attempted Murder After Intentionally Hitting Two Police Cruisers with Dump Truck

January 9, 2015 Black

Gene Thomas Brandon Jr. show in police video in custody
(Photo: Prince George’s County Police)

The driver of a dump truck that struck two Prince George’s County Police cruisers in Glenarden on Wednesday morning has been charged with attempted murder, say police officials.

Gene Thomas Brandon Jr. is accused of intentionally hitting the cruisers in the parking lot of the Woodmore Town Center shopping center at 11:35 a.m. The police cars were empty at the time.

Police released video of the incident on Thursday, the same day the announced the charges against Brandon.

In the video you see the truck approach, stop briefly, then pass the cruisers one way. The truck then comes back the other way, headed directly towards the cruisers. It hits and pushes the cruisers for some distance. To the right, you see what appears to be two people who were next to the cruisers run away.
