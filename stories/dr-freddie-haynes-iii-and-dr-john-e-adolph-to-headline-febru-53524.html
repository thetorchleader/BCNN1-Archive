Dr. Freddie Haynes III and Dr. John E. Adolph to Headline February Revival at Mount Moriah Baptist Church of Los Angeles

January 25, 2015 Black

Dr. Freddie Haynes III and Dr. John E. Adolph will headline the Annual February Revival hosted by Pastor Melvin V. Wade, Sr., and Mount Moriah Baptist Church of Los Angeles.

The services begin on Sunday, February 1, at 6:30 p.m. and continue through Wednesday, February 4 with worship each day at 12 p.m., and 7:30 p.m.  Dr. Adolph will preach during the day and Dr. Haynes will be the evening speaker.

“The historic Annual February Revival at Mount Moriah has been a source of spiritual restoration to countless numbers of individuals in the city of Los Angeles for nearly 50 years. We invite the community to share in all of the worship services,” said Pastor Wade.

The Rev. Dr. Freddie Haynes is the senior pastor of Friendship-West Baptist Church in Dallas, TX. Nationally known as a dynamic preacher and orator, Dr. Haynes is also a passionate social activist in the fight for economic justice and empowerment.
