Andrew Luck Improves Despite Loss to Pittsburgh Steelers

August 20, 2012 Uncategorized

Andrew Luck’s second go-round in this NFL preseason started much rockier than his first. There was no touchdown on the first play, for starters.
But his night ended just as impressively.
The Colts’ rookie quarterback bounced back from a pair of early interceptions and a 14-0 hole to lead his offense to 17 second-quarter points, giving Indianapolis a 17-14 lead before the top overall pick in April’s draft retired to the sideline for the evening.
Luck finished 15-for-24 for 175 yards and a rushing touchdown Sunday night in Pittsburgh. He is credited with two interceptions but the second came off a bobbled pass to T.Y. Hilton in which the rookie receiver lost control of the ball before it landed in the hands of the Steelers’ Cortez Allen.
“It was a rough start,” Luck told NBC’s Michelle Tafoya during the third quarter. “But I think we got better today, and that’s what it’s all about.”
The Steelers’ Daniel Hrapmann hit a pair of fourth-quarter field goals that proved the difference in a 26-24 Pittsburgh victory. The Colts will next travel to face the Redskins on Saturday in their third preseason game.
