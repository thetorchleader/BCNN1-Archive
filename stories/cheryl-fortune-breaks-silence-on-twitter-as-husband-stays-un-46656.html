Cheryl Fortune Breaks Silence on Twitter as Husband Stays Under the Radar

November 24, 2014 Entertainment

GETTY

Prayer. Carrie Underwood. Song lyrics.

This is the subject matter of three consecutive tweets sent from the official Twitter page of Cheryl Fortune, wife of legally embattled gospel artist James Fortune.

While Mrs. Fortune’s husband wisely stays under the radar amid allegations that he was involved in a domestic dispute leading to his Oct. 23 arrest, she is resurfacing.

News reports quote sources speculating that Mr. Fortune threw a bar stool at Mrs. Fortune and injured her, but she has not spoken publically at all about the case.

Instead, on Twitter, she continues sending brief messages.

On Nov. 21, she said, “Prayer changes things. If you are going to worry, why pray: if you are going to pray, Why worry. #Alwayspray”—a re-tweet from life coach Pamela McGee.

On Nov. 22, she wrote, “Carrie Underwood’s voice and music is EVERYTHING!!!!”
