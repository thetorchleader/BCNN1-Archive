Chicago Pastor Vows to Rebuild After Fire Tears Through Church

October 29, 2012 Uncategorized

Congregation invited to neighboring church for Sunday service
Two days after a fire tore through a venerable church in Chicago’s Englewood neighborhood, its pastor told members of his congregation Sunday that with God’s help they could rebuild.
“Though we had lost a great part of our church … God is still in control. … We can raise up even stronger,” the Rev. Ronald Richardson said.
On Friday morning, an extra-alarm blaze destroyed the rear of the Love, Faith and Praise Church of God in Christ, which was built in the 1860s. Nearly 200 firefighters responded, some of whom arrived as flames were shooting through the roof.
Nobody was injured, but officials initiated an investigation.
On Sunday, the church held a service across the street after the pastor of Antioch Missionary Baptist Church South allowed the congregation to use its building.
“I saw the look on (Pastor) Richardson’s face of ‘what am I going to do next?'” Antioch’s pastor, the Rev. Carsie Barnes III, said. “The Lord placed it in my heart to offer up our building.”
About 60 people from both congregations piled into the pews of Antioch on Sunday preparing to sing worship songs and listen to Richardson’s sermon. The energetic service was marked by gratitude and sadness as congregants got used to their new surroundings.
“I want you all to loosen up now. We’re across the street at our brother’s house,” Richardson told his congregation. “Some might say (it’s) hard to be at someone else’s pulpit … but it’s not. I don’t feel like I am away from home.”
