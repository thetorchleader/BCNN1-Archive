North Texas Hit by Tornadoes; Storms Leave Damage, Floods

April 27, 2015 National

Damage in Rio Vista after Sunday’s storms. (Photo: WFAA)

Residents across North Texas are cleaning up after storms rolled through Sunday, leaving behind damage and floods. A total of 19 tornadoes were reported, but not all of them have or will be confirmed.

The severe weather threat is lower Monday, but scattered to numerous storms are still expected.

Confirmed tornadoes were spotted in the following locations Sunday:

  • Erath-Comanche County line, 4:30 p.m.
  • 11 miles southeast of Stephenville, 8 p.m.
  • Glen Rose, 9:10 p.m.
  • Cleburne State Park, 9:40 p.m.
  • West of Rio Vista, roughly 10 p.m.
  • Grandview (time unknown)
