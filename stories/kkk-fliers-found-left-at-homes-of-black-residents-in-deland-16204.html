KKK Fliers Found Left at Homes of Black Residents in Deland, FL

April 1, 2014 Uncategorized

Several African-American residents in DeLand said they woke up Sunday to find racially insensitive fliers at their homes.

The fliers promote the Ku Klux Klan with the headline, Loyal White Knights and make hateful comments about African-Americans, blaming them for crime and violence.

Top video: Ariz. students riot after NCAA loss

Several residents said the fliers showed up in plastic baggies with candy in them. In one instance, a woman’s children, attracted by the colorful candy, picked a baggie up.

The same fliers have shown up in other Central Florida cities.
