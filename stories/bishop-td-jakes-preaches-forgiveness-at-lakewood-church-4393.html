Bishop T.D. Jakes Preaches Forgiveness at Lakewood Church

March 12, 2012 Uncategorized

A well-known pastor from Dallas is spreading the word of FORGIVENESS right here in Houston. 
Bishop T.D. Jakes addressed the congregation at Lakewood Church on Saturday morning, preaching about the power to forgive. It is a concept that many churchgoers agree can be difficult at times. 
Twin sisters Dee and Kiala Washington drove in from Missouri.
“There’s a lot of things I was holding on to and I was able to ask God to move into my life and it really spoke to me,” admitted Dee.
“When we heard that he was coming to teach this lesson about letting go, we knew that God was speaking to us because we definitely need help with, you know, letting a situation go,” said Kiala.
