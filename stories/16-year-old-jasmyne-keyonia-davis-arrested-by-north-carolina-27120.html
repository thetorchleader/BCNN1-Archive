16-Year-Old Jasmyne Keyonia Davis Arrested by North Carolina Police for Calling 911 Because Mother Took Her Cellphone

June 18, 2014 Black

Jasmyne Keyonia Davis
JOHNSON COUNTY SHERIFF’S OFFICE

Police responded to an emergency call only to learn that 16-year-old Jasmyne Keyonia Davis called the authorities because her mother grounded her and took her cellphone.  North Carolina police arrested a teen after she called 911 because she was upset that her mother grounded her and took away her cellphone.

According to ABC 11 News, police arrived at 16-year-old Jasmyne Keyonia Davis’ home a little after midnight Tuesday after the emergency operators received a call from a female who was reportedly screaming and crying.

Police soon discovered that Jasmyne was the caller and that she was upset because her mother took away her cell phone.

Neighbors told the new station that Jasmyne “had been going through typical teenage rough patches with her mom that escalated recently with several police visits to their home.”
