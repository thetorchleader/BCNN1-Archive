Church of God In Christ to Host Assemblies of God Leader, Dr. George O. Wood, During C. H. Mason Birthdate Commemoration

August 21, 2014 Christian

The Church of God in Christ will host Assemblies of God General Superintendent Dr. George O. Wood during its upcoming conference at its world headquarters in Memphis, TN.

Dr. George O. Wood, general superintendent of the Assemblies of God, Springfield, Missouri, will be the guest speaker at the Church of God in Christ (COGIC) C.H. Mason Heritage Symposium & Celebration on Monday, September 8, 2014, 7pm at Mason Temple. The Symposium & Celebration will commemorate the 150th birthdate of COGIC founder Bishop Charles Harrison Mason. The celebration will convene September 8-10, 2014.

Presiding Bishop Charles E. Blake, Sr. says, “The Church of God in Christ is honored and elated to have Dr. George Wood as the keynote speaker during the C.H. Mason Heritage Symposium & Celebration. The Church of God in Christ and the Assemblies of God have a long history together that dates back to the late 1800s. I am personally looking forward to this time of sharing and fellowship.”

A popular speaker and writer, Dr. Wood was elected chief executive officer of the Assemblies of God at the 52nd General Council in August 2007. As general superintendent of the U.S. Assemblies of God, part of the largest Pentecostal denomination in the world, he is a member of the denomination’s Executive Leadership Team and Executive Presbytery. Prior to his present position, Wood served the church as its general secretary for 14 years. He was assistant superintendent of the Southern California District from 1988-93.

Assemblies of God has over 12,700 congregations in the United States with over 3 million members and adherents. The U.S. Assemblies of God is part of a larger World Assemblies of God Fellowship, with a membership of over 66 million.
