Tina Knowles Lawson on Finding Love at 61

April 23, 2015 Black, Entertainment

Tina Knowles Lawson
PEOPLE/CLIFF WATTS

When you look at Beyoncé and Solange and wonder what they’ll be like in the years to come, you don’t have to look any further than their mother, Tina Knowles. Knowles is the epitome of beauty and grace at 61 years old, and her recent wedding to actor Richard Lawson proves that flawlessness is hereditary.

The happy couple exchanged wedding vows April 12 aboard a yacht in Newport Beach, Calif., surrounded by family and friends. To say that the wedding was immaculate would be an understatement. In an interview with People, Knowles Lawson discussed her wedding day and finding love again.

“To walk down the aisle and see all my friends and family and to see my handsome man standin’ there, and to know that at 61 you can still find love and have a magical day like that … it was really beautiful,” she said.

Knowles Lawson told the magazine that although she’s known her new husband for decades, it wasn’t until a year and a half ago that they reconnected, and within four months, she knew he was the one.

“There was a time when I doubted if I would find love again,” said Knowles Lawson, who divorced Beyoncé and Solange’s father in 2011. “But I was determined to be happy.”
