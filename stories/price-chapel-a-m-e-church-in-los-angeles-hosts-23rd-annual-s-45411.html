Price Chapel A.M.E. Church in Los Angeles Hosts 23rd Annual Singing Ministers Concert Today

November 16, 2014 Black

Rev. N.W. Martin, guest soloist

The musical talents of several local preachers take center stage at the 23rd Annual Singing Ministers Concert on Sunday, November 16, at 6 p.m., at Price Chapel A.M.E. Church, 4000 West Slauson Avenue in Los Angeles.

The Price Chapel A.M.E. Trustee Board will host the program featuring an ecumenical group of clergy singing hymns, gospel and contemporary selections. The legendary Pres Blackmon of KTYM radio station will serve as emcee.

Inviting the public to attend, Pastor Benjamin Hollins said, “We look forward to your presence.  Join us in making a joyful noise unto the Lord and support our 13 guest ministers in giving God glory, honor and praise!”
