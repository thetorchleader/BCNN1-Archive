Season 3 of ‘House of Cards’ Leaked Early, Then Quickly Yanked by Netflix

February 12, 2015 Entertainment

Surprise!

Remote-adjacent fans of House of Cards got to peek at Season 3 of the Netflix hit show on Wednesday, when the streaming service quietly made new episodes available.

For a few minutes, anyway. Those quick to hop on their Netflix accounts could start watching 10 new episodes, though the streaming service yanked them down within minutes.

Season 3 of the series was originally scheduled for release on Feb. 27.

The show’s official Twitter account made a joke of it: “This is Washington. There’s always a leak,” they tweeted.
