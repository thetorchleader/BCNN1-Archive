Local Black Pastors in Houston Honored For Their Service to the Lord and to the Community

May 19, 2013 Uncategorized

The Houston Forward Times, one of the city’s oldest African American weekly newspapers, held its second annual Visionary Pastor’s Awards ceremony at the George R. Brown Convention Center.


Honorees Pastors Byron Stevenson, Edwin Davis and Michael Pender (Grady Carter / For the Chronicle)
The event, which included a VIP reception and a gospel concert, raised nearly $100,000 for the Julius and Lenora Carter Scholarship and Youth Foundation. The late Carters founded the Forward Times 53 years ago.  Among the pastors honored were Edwin Davis, Samuel Compton, Reginald Devaughn,  Terrance Johnson, Gregg Patrick, Michael Pender and Byron Stevenson. 
“These pastors are doing great work in our community,” said Forward Times Publisher Karen Carter Richards.  “These pastors don’t have mega-churches, but they are helping the youth and the elderly with wonderful programs and we wanted to applaud them for that work.”
