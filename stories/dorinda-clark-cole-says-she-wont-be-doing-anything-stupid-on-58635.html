Dorinda Clark-Cole Says She Won’t Be Doing Anything Stupid on “Preachers of Detroit”

February 25, 2015 Entertainment

Dorinda Clark-Cole

Several Christian reality stars have proven to be an embarrassment to the church in a major way.

But GRAMMY® award-winning gospel artist and Oxygen’s Preachers of Detroit star, Dorinda Clark-Cole, said she won’t be engaging in any “stupid” behavior.

In a recent interview with the Yolanda Adams Morning Show, Clark-Cole said “On Preachers of Detroit, I know it’s a message that we are given. I told God, I said ‘Lord, you can trust me with this. I’m not gone do nothing stupid.’”

Some people have expressed skepticism according to her cousin and fellow recording artist, J. Moss, who recently told Sister 2 Sister Magazine, “She’s going to catch backlash from it. It’s already started, online chatter. You know, ‘Why did she do this?'”

He added, “If she wasn’t ready for this type of show, then she shouldn’t have signed up.”
