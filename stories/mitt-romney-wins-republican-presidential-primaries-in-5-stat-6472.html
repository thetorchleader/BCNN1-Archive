Mitt Romney Wins Republican Presidential Primaries In 5 States

June 6, 2012 Uncategorized

Mitt Romney has won the California Republican presidential primary, completing a sweep of contests in five states.
Romney also won presidential primaries Tuesday in New Jersey, South Dakota, New Mexico and Montana.
 
Results:
 California
     1,116 of 21,993 precincts – 5 percent
    
     x-Mitt Romney, 389,631 – 81 percent
     Ron Paul, 44,249 – 9 percent
     Rick Santorum, 23,217 – 5 percent
     Newt Gingrich, 17,959 – 4 percent
     Buddy Roemer, 3,438 – 1 percent
     Fred Karger, 8,257 – 0 percent
    
 Montana
     30 of 794 precincts – 4 percent
    
     x-Mitt Romney, 34,214 – 71 percent
     Ron Paul, 6,201 – 13 percent
     Rick Santorum, 4,100 – 9 percent
     Newt Gingrich, 1,890 – 4 percent
     No Preference, 1,745 – 4 percent
    
 New Jersey
     6,169 of 6,330 precincts – 97 percent
    
     x-Mitt Romney, 183,286 – 81 percent
     Ron Paul, 23,450 – 10 percent
     Rick Santorum, 11,777 – 5 percent
     Newt Gingrich, 7,051 – 3 percent
    
 New Mexico
     789 of 1,541 precincts – 51 percent
    
     x-Mitt Romney, 45,684 – 74 percent
     Rick Santorum, 6,118 – 10 percent
     Ron Paul, 5,956 – 10 percent
     Newt Gingrich, 3,615 – 6 percent
    
 South Dakota
     664 of 710 precincts – 94 percent
    
     x-Mitt Romney, 31,767 – 66 percent
     Ron Paul, 6,154 – 13 percent
     Rick Santorum, 5,554 – 12 percent
     Uncommitted, 8,668 – 6 percent
     Newt Gingrich, 1,948 – 4 percent
The victories add to a delegate total that already exceeds the number needed to win the nomination. Romney has 1,398 delegates. He needs 1,144 to win the Republican nomination at the party’s national convention in August.
Romney will take on Democrat Barack Obama in the fall.
A total of 264 delegates are at stake in Tuesday’s contests – including 169 in California – and Romney has a chance to win them all.
After Tuesday, only one Republican presidential primary remains, in Utah on June 26.

SOURCE: Associated Press
Stephen Ohlemacher
