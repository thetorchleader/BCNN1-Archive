LISTEN: Samuel Chand, Robert Fullerton, and Mark Kwame Amoateng on BC Book Review Show #268

May 4, 2015 Books

The authors that we are featuring today are: Samuel Chand, author of “Leadership Pain”; Robert Fullerton, author of “Business Success Based on Biblical Principles”; and Mark Kwame Amoateng, author of “How to Receive From God”.

The purpose of this show is to highlight wholesome Christian books, promote them to our audience, encourage you to buy these books for yourself and for others, and most importantly, to see people come to Christ and lives changed for the glory of God.

MUSICAL SELECTION: “Living for Jesus” by Rance Allen, “Do Not Pass Me By” by Deitrick Haddon, and “Nothing Without You” by Jason Nelson
