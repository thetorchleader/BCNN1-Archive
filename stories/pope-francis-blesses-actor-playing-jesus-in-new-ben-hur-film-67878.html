Pope Francis Blesses Actor Playing Jesus In New Ben-Hur Film

April 16, 2015 Entertainment

(PHOTO: INSTAGRAM/ NAZANIN BONIADI)
Rodrigo Santoro blessed by Pope Francis in Rome on April 15, 2015.

The Brazilian actor Rodrigo Santoro, who is cast as Jesus Christ in the forthcoming remake of biblical epic Ben-Hur, has been blessed by Pope Francis at the Vatican.

Timur Bekmambetov’s film is currently shooting in Rome. Boardwalk Empire’s Jack Huston takes the title role as Judah Ben-Hur, the fictional Jewish prince introduced in Lew Wallace’s popular 1880 novel Ben-Hur: A Tale of the Christ and immortalised on the big screen by Charlton Heston in William Wyler’s 1959 blockbuster.

Santoro and fellow cast member Nazanin Boniadi attended a weekly papal audience event during a break from production, where they met Pope Francis. The pair also took a picture in front of the Vatican.

Christ is featured briefly in the Wyler’s film, although the director chose not to show his face in deference to contemporary views on the depiction of Jesus. Bekmambetov’s take is expected to go further as it courts religious audiences in the US.
