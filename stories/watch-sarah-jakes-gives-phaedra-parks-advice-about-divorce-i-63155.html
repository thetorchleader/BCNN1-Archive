WATCH: Sarah Jakes Gives Phaedra Parks Advice About Divorce In “Real Housewives of Atlanta” Appearance

March 23, 2015 Entertainment

Photo Credit: BRAVO

Bishop T.D. Jakes’ youngest daughter Sarah Jakes-Roberts, 25, proves that your mess-ups can become the platform for your ministry.

On Sunday’s episode of Bravo’s The Real Housewives of Atlanta, the preacher’s kid shared experienced-based advice about divorce.

In a conversation with cast member Phaedra Parks, who is contemplating splitting up with her incarcerated husband Apollo Nida, Jakes-Roberts said, “I know it’s hard, especially when it plays out in front of everyone and you haven’t even had a chance to process it, so I completely understand.”

After divorcing her former NFL husband Robert Henson in 2012, the popular author and speaker married Los Angeles-based pastor, Touré Roberts, in a private ceremony in November 2014.

These days, the drama of her first union is behind her.

“Sarah handled every moment that she confronted in her life with such dignity,” said Parks during the show. “I think she’s the best person to talk to about my issues.”
