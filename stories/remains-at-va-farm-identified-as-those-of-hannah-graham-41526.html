Remains at Va. Farm Identified as Those of Hannah Graham

October 24, 2014 National

Hannah Graham
(Photo: Charlottesville Police Department)

Remains found in Albemarle County have been positively identified as University of Virginia student Hannah Graham, county police officials reported Friday.

The remains were discovered on Oct. 18 on an abandoned farm on Old Lynchburg Road, just ten miles from the downtown mall in Charlottesville where the 18-year-old sophomore from Alexandria, Va., was last seen. She disappeared the morning of Sept. 13.

“Our thoughts and prayers go out to the Graham family and Hannah’s friends during this incredibly difficult time,”Albemarle County police said in a statement Friday.

The county medical examiner’s office notified Graham’s parents, John and Sue Graham, soon after the identification was confirmed. The Grahams visited the field early Friday afternoon and released the following statement later:

“When we first met Chief (Timothy) Longo he promised to find our precious daughter, Hannah, and during five long weeks his resolve to fulfill that promise never wavered. When we started this journey together we all hoped for a happier ending. Sadly that was not to be, but due to the tenacity and determination of Chief Longo, Hannah is coming home to us and we will be eternally grateful to him for this.”
