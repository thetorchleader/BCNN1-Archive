The Digital Sermons of Tim Keller

July 9, 2012 Uncategorized

A two-decade span of digital archives from author and pastor Tim Keller’s sermons is being made available for the first time ever by Logos Bible Software.

Keller is pastor of Redeemer Presbyterian Church, known as “one of Manhattan’s most vital congregations.”
He said people have long been requesting that his sermons be made more accessible to the public.
“But Redeemer hasn’t been able to do that … it’s a very big project,” Keller said. “However, the people at Logos now have come and said they will make those manuscripts available.”
The Timothy Keller Sermon Archive is comprised of 1,233 sermons, ranging from 1989-2011.
Keller gained national attention for his New York Times bestseller The Reason for God.
