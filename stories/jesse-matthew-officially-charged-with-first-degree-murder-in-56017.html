Jesse Matthew Officially Charged with First Degree Murder in Death of UVA Student, Hannah Graham

February 10, 2015 Black

Jesse Matthew Jr. has been officially charged with first-degree murder in the 2013 death of University of Virginia student Hannah Graham, local media in Charlottesville, Va., report.

The Albemarle County Police Department would not confirm the charge Monday night and said that officials would take part in a press conference at 11 a.m. ET Tuesday to discuss the case.

Charlottesville CBS affiliate WTVR and NBC affiliate NBC29 cite unnamed sources in their reports that Matthews has been charged.

Graham, then 18, was in her second year a the Charlottesville school when she vanished last September. Her body was found on an abandoned property in Albemarle County, Va., on Oct. 18. She was last seen with Matthew leaving a bar on Sept. 13th.

Matthew, 32, was previously charged with abduction with intent to defile in the Graham case.
