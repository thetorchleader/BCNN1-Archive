Herman Cain to Appear on BET this Thursday

December 6, 2011 Uncategorized

Herman Cain has simply been the topic of every political discussion lately; whether or not he is a viable candidate will be left up to the people. At any rate, a first time look into the life of the entrepreneur will be offered up to those who wonder about the man’s ethnic identity in all aspects of the phrase.
On Thursday, Dec. 8 at 9pm E/P on BET, he will give attention to the black  audience on what is being called, “The Curious Case of Citizen Cain.”
The half-hour news documentary will beg the question of what does it mean to be a black conservative? It will also offer an explanation of what the Republican calls “scams perpetrated on blacks” by liberal black leaders and of course the sex scandals.
