Joseph’s Dream and Destiny

November 21, 2011 Uncategorized

by Joey Johnson 
January 9, 2012
Click here to read previous articles by Joey Johnson

All right, back to Joseph. About this time, Joseph had a dream. The
dream was about his destiny. It was in keeping with the destiny that
flowed through Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

The dream seemed to be
prophesying Joseph’s rise to a place of authority and prominence
in the world, a place from which his brothers would have to bow down to
him.

Joseph didn’t keep the dream to himself. He told his brothers.
Family mess! Although there is little negative information about Joseph
in the Bible, he did not escape the family mess of his family. The
impact of family mess on Joseph is illustrated right here. Joseph
seemed to flaunt the favoritism of his father in the faces of his
brothers.

He wore that coat of many colors with pride. He seemed oblivious to the
impact of his actions upon his brothers. Instead of holding it until
Friday night at the bar mitzvah, he came out with that leather coat
with all those colors. He sashayed in front of his brothers. If you
have siblings, you know what he probably said: “I’ve got
something you don’t have!”

He told them the dream without regard to its impact upon their
feelings. It does not seem that the dream was for them. It was for him.
The dream was God telling him, “I’m going to do something
for you.” But he had to go out and tell everybody else.

No, Joseph didn’t escape the family mess in his family. But by
the grace of God, he did rise above it!
