Take Your Honey to the Caribbean for Valentine’s – Here Are the Best Hotels

February 6, 2015 caribbean, God & Family

For a nightcap to remember, the house cocktail called the ‘Calabash Cove Bombshell’ is a tropical concoction of fruit juices, banana liqueur and vodka. (Photo: Mikael Lamber, Calabash Cove Resort)

The Caribbean’s recipe for a romantic rendezvous is tried, tested and true. It starts with the views from a seaside suite, improves with a table for two and reaches perfection via a butler who makes sure a couples-only holiday is a top-shelf tryst. Whether you’re rekindling an old flame, enjoying quality time together or celebrating Valentine’s Day, our list of hot hotels for couples raises the bar on sweetly sensual vacations.

Puerto Rico

A world away from the hustle and bustle of the San Juan strip, Copamarina on Puerto Rico’s south coast is the real deal for couples looking for a few days away to explore the road less traveled. Fronting the calm Caribbean Sea (San Juan is on the rockier Atlantic Ocean side), in the seaside town of Guanica, the laid-back resort is a two-hour drive from the airport. The trek is well worth the effort for the comfy rooms, hammocks made for two, ferry rides to nearby Gilligan’s Island, breakfast at the open air Las Palmas (ask Luis to whip up a fabulous omelette), and stellar service that gives couples plenty of privacy without hovering. The resort’s twenty acres also boast a spa, swimming pools and dreamy day beds on the beach.

St. Lucia

Swim-up suites with Jacuzzis and an infinity edge pool affectionately called Sweetwaters is just the beginning of couple-friendly fun at the Calabash Cove Resort. Tucked away between the majestic Piton Mountains and featuring uninterrupted stretches of white sand, the sea-view cottages (there are only four) are stand-outs for couples. Dining at Windsong is a delectable crowning jewel with its Asian-inspired menu (we recommend octopus tempura with chili seaweed) and hypnotizing views of sunsets over the Caribbean Sea. For a nightcap to remember, the house cocktail called the ‘Calabash Cove Bombshell’ is a tropical concoction of fruit juices, banana liqueur and vodka.

Anguilla

Ultimacy Villa on Anguilla’s northeast shore snuggles the villages of Shoal Bay East and Island Harbour, oozing grandeur around every turn. The luxury lodge was built for sweeties who like to cuddle, with plenty of room to unwind and lots of nooks and crannies for total seclusion. The butler, housekeepers and chef are pros, taking care of every possible detail. Should a massage be on the afternoon agenda, a session with spa therapists can be arranged.
