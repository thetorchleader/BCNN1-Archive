First African-American Commander of North Carolina Highway Patrol, Colonel Richard W. Holden Sr., Dies at 77

August 23, 2014 Black

Colonel Richard W. Holden Sr. (Photo Courtesy: State Highway Patrol)

A pioneering lawman – the first African-American commander of North Carolina’s Highway Patrol – is dead.


Retired Col. Richard Holden Sr. died on Thursday. He was 77 years old.

Holden was one of the first African-American state troopers, and rose through the ranks during his 35 year career with the Highway Patrol. He became commander of the Highway Patrol in 1999.

A public visitation will be held Tuesday from 12 p.m. to 8 p.m. at Lea Funeral Home in Raleigh.

Source: ABC 11
