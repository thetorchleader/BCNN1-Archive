Chicago Pastor Brings Gang Members to God from a Rooftop

January 13, 2012 Uncategorized

In the nearly two months since Corey Brooks, a little-known minister, began living, praying and preaching in a tent pitched on the roof of a vacant motel, politicians, hip-hop musicians and grieving mothers have traveled to the South Side for an audience with the “rooftop pastor.”
Now Mr. Brooks could emerge as “the new face in the leadership lineup,” said Robert T. Starks, a political science professor at Northeastern Illinois University.
“He’s on the verge if he can solidify his base, if he can keep the momentum going,” Mr. Starks said. “He’s attracting a lot of young people, which is very important.”
Mr. Starks said with all of Mr. Brooks’s newfound attention, political power brokers also have their eyes on the rooftop. “It will be interesting to see if the system is able to co-opt him as it has so many other black ministers in this town,” he said.
Mr. Brooks, who celebrated his 43rd birthday on the roof on Monday, said he had no interest in politics, at least not when it comes to elective office. “I’ll never run for office,” he said. “But I will always fight against the social ills of our society.”
Mr. Brooks — who continues to deliver his Sunday sermon to parishioners in his New Beginnings Church via video stream — is trying to raise $450,000 to buy the motel, raze it and eventually replace it with a community youth center directly across the street from his church in the 6600 block of South Martin Luther King Drive.
Since he first climbed atop the roof on Nov. 22, he said more than 2,000 people from as far away as South Africa and Sweden have contributed a total of $237,000.
“Pastor Corey Brooks has struck a nerve,” said Mark Allen, chairman of Black Wall Street Chicago, a black economic development advocacy group. “He has set off a little storm in terms of other young leaders saying what visibility, what kind of drastic, daring, bold act can I do to call attention to the issues confronting the African-American community?”
