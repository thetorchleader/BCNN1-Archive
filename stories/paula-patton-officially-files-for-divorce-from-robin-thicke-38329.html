Paula Patton Officially Files for Divorce From Robin Thicke

October 9, 2014 Entertainment

Robin Thicke and Paula Patton arriving on the red carpet for the 56th Grammy Awards at the Staples Center in Los Angeles Jan. 26, 2014.
ROBYN BECK/AFP/GETTY IMAGES

Paula Patton will soon be a single woman after officially filing for divorce from Robin Thicke. The couple, who have been together since high school and married for nine years, separated earlier this year after allegations of Thicke cheating.

Patton and Thicke released a joint statement on their separation saying, “We will always love each other and be best friends; however, we have mutually decided to separate at this time.”

According to TMZ, Patton has gotten the divorce ball rolling, even after Thicke released his failed Paula reconciliation album. Patton cited irreconcilable differences and is looking for joint legal and physical custody of their son, Julian.

Recently, Thicke blamed his personal problems on his use of drugs and alcohol. During a deposition in Los Angeles Federal Court regarding the lawsuit with the family of Marvin Gaye, Thicke admitted to taking vicodin and said that he had had a “drug and alcohol problem” for at least a year.
