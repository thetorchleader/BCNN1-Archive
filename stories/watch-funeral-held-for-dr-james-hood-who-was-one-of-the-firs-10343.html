WATCH: Funeral Held for Dr. James Hood Who Was One of the First Blacks to Integrate the University of Alabama

January 25, 2013 Uncategorized

One of the first African Americans to enroll at the University of Alabama was laid to rest Thursday afternoon.

Dr. James Hood’s funeral services were held at the First United Methodist Church in Gadsden.
Hood is notably known for enrolling at UA alongside Vivian Malone Jones in 1963, at the height of the Civil Rights Movement when the campus was all white.
Family, friends, and dignitaries from the City of Gadsden and the University of Alabama, were amongst the crowd of mourners, who bid their final goodbye to Hood.
