Glenn Beck Calls Tim Tebow ‘Too Controversial’ to Get Hired by NFL or ESPN

June 6, 2013 Uncategorized

Glenn Beck says Tim Tebow is too controversial to get hire by the NFL, or even ESPN.
If you tuned into Beck’s TV/radio show recently, you might have thought you were listening to a sports-talk station. After a lengthy segment about Redskins QB Robert Griffin III’s new $2.5 million home in Virginia, the conversation turned to Tebow.
“Politically, there’s different sorts of trouble now,” Beck said about sports. “You can be a good guy, look at Tebow, you can be a good guy. Clean life, but be on the wrong side politically, and you and you are doomed.”
Beck may know about Tebow, but apparently wasn’t quite up to date on how he performs on the field. He turned to his co-hosts and asked “Is he good?”
The concensus from the Beck bullpen is similar to what most have said — Tebow is a great football player, but maybe not a good quarterback.
Then the conversation took a strange twist.
“I love the fact that you can get a chance if you’ve murdered somebody, if you’ve murdered somebody …” Beck said.
