WATCH: NFL Hall of Famer Jim Kelly’s “Playbook For Dads”

September 9, 2012 Uncategorized

FIGURING IT OUT
Jim is an NFL Hall of Fame quarterback who led the Buffalo Bills to the playoffs eight times, including four Super Bowls.  Though the Bills never clinched the coveted Super Bowl trophy, Jim says that the losses were both a blessing and a curse. They were a blessing because he was living a great life as a professional football player but they were a curse because losing wasn’t fun.  Jim says that character is revealed most clearly in the losses of our lives.  Trials and challenges in life reveal and build our ability to persevere. 


In 1997, Jim and Jill were excited about the arrival of their son, Hunter. A few weeks after he was born, Hunter was diagnosed with Krabbe Leukodystrophy, a fatal genetic disease that affects the central nervous system. Doctors said he would live a short time. Though Hunter defied medical odds and lived long enough to see the Bills retire his dad’s number and witness Jim’s induction into the Hall of Fame, Hunter passed away when he was eight. “Hunter taught me what’s most important in life,” says Jim. “God used his life to reveal my weaknesses and His strength.”  Shortly after Hunter’s passing, Jim and Jill went through a difficult time in their marriage.  “It was the suffering in my life, losing Hunter and almost losing my marriage to Jill, that ultimately created in me deep personal change.” Jim committed his life to the Lord in 2007.  “Christianity wasn’t the magic bullet to a perfect life, but it is the source of my hope and identity,” says Jim.  He says it took him awhile to get a full grasp but says he continues to be a work in progress.   “As a young man, for me, it was losing four Super Bowls and coming back each year to try again….God would challenge us through Hunter’s life and death….He would challenge us through moments in our marriage when I didn’t think we would make it.  Sometimes I responded in the right ways, sometimes I didn’t.  But the more I reflect on it, the more I think losing those Super Bowls prepared me, uniquely for what God knew I would go through later.”
Jim and Jill founded Hunters Hope Foundation to raise awareness about Krabbe disease and other leukodystrophies.
