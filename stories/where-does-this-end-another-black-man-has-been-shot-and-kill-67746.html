Where Does This End? Another Black Man Has Been Shot and Killed by Police; Houston School Placed on Lockdown After Man Leads Police on High Speed Chase

April 16, 2015 Black

Frank “Trey” Shephard

Police officers fatally shot a man who led them on a chase Wednesday morning in northeast Harris County.

According to a Harris County Sheriff’s Office spokesman, the chase began about 10:40 a.m. in the Crosstimbers area of US-59. Houston Police tried to pull over the driver of a blue Chrysler 300 due to “suspicious activitiy,” including two unsafe lane changes. As they approached the car, the driver fled.

A chase ensued through northeast Houston along Beltway 8 before the driver exited at Wallisville Road and eventually ran through an intersection at Castlegory Road, striking two vehicles.

The driver got out of the car and the officers told him to raise his hands, officials said. He then appeared to reach back inside the vehicle, according to the Harris County Sheriff’s Office. That’s when it appears, deputies said, that two police officers opened fire on him, firing 10-12 rounds. The man died at the scene.

During the chase, the man called 911 dispatchers and told him that he had a child in his car and would harm the child if police pursued him. No child was present in the car, however. No weapon was found in the car.

“This was a very dangerous situation that the HPD had to deal with,” Dep. Thomas Gilliland said.

The man was identified as Frank “Trey” Shephard, a 41-year-old barber and father of three, his uncle said.

“I’m looking at it on TV saying ‘why would you run from the law?’ Not knowing it was my nephew,” said Chris Shephard. “My sister, his mom , called and told me that they shot and killed him.”

Shephard’s mother stood at the crime scene perimeter with her hands clasped over her mouth and her wide eyes full of tears, repeating “oh my God.”
