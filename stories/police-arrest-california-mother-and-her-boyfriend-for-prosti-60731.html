Police Arrest California Mother and her Boyfriend for Prostituting Woman’s Two Daughters

March 9, 2015 Black

Antwana Muhammad
RIVERSIDE COUNTY SHERIFF’S DEPARTMENT

A California mother and her boyfriend were arrested last week after they allegedly prostituted both of the woman’s daughters, one of whom is 14.

According to the Press Enterprise, Antwana Muhammad, 38, and Charles Anthony Smith, 32, were arrested after police begin investigating allegations that the couple was exploiting the woman’s 14-year-old daughter. Through the investigation police learned that the woman’s adult daughter was also being exploited.

The Press Enterprise notes that, the couple placed online ads advertising the teen and her sister for sex. Authorities told the newspaper that the mother also allegedly drove them to appointments and told them how to charge for each act. Authorities noted that Muhammad and Anthony kept all of the proceeds.
