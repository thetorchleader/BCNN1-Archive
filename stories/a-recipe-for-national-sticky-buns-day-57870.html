A Recipe for National Sticky Buns Day

February 21, 2015 God & Food

Celebrate National Sticky Buns Day with this recipe

It’s National Sticky Buns Day and who can resist a warm, gooey sticky bun? Here’s a recipe I’ve developed over the years by combing several recipes.

It’s a little time consuming, but so-o-o-o worth your time and efforts. What better way to spend an icy day than baking up this ooey, gooey goodness?

Masters Family Sticky Buns

The instant mashed potato flakes give this dough a tender texture. The recipe also is a little unusual, since it uses both yeast and baking powder.

Dough:

1 package dry yeast

1/2 cup warm water

2/3 cup sugar

1/3 cup shortening

1/4 cup instant mashed potato flakes

1 1/4 teaspoons salt

1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder

1 cup milk, scalded

1 egg

3 1/2-4 1/2 cups flour

Filling:

1 tablespoon cinnamon

3/4 cup light brown sugar

1/4 cup butter, melted

Glaze:

1/2 cup butter, softened

3 tablespoons honey

1/3 cup dark corn syrup

1 cup brown sugar

1/2 cup chopped pecans

Combine yeast and warm water in a small bowl; set aside.

In a large bowl, mix sugar, shortening, potato flakes, salt and scalded milk. Set aside to cool down to 110 F.

Add egg and yeast-water mixture and blend well. Combine 3 1/2 cups flour and baking powder and add to the wet mixture to form a dough. Knead until smooth, adding more flour as needed. Dough should be soft and moist, but not sticky and tacky. Place in a greased bowl, cover and let rise in a warm place for 1 to 1 1/2 hours.

While dough is rising, combine glaze ingredients in a bowl, except for the pecans, and beat with an electric mixer until fluffy. Divide topping mixture between two 9-inch baking pans or one 9-by-13-inch baking dish. Divide chopped pecans between the two pans.

When dough has risen, punch down and allow dough to rest 10 minutes.

While dough is resting, combine cinnamon and light brown sugar for the filling.

Once dough is rested, divide in half and roll each half into a 12-by-15-inch rectangle. Spread 1/8 cup of the melted butter over each rectangle. Sprinkle with cinnamon-brown sugar mixture. Starting at the long edge, roll dough toward you to create a jellyroll. Cut roll into 1/2-inch slices and place in prepared pans. Cover and let rise in a warm place until rolls have doubled in size.

Bake at 350 F. until browned.

Remove rolls from oven and allow to cool 2 minutes. Run a knife around the edge of the pan, cover with a serving plate and invert pan. Tap the bottom of the pan to release buns, then remove pan.

Allow to cool and serve or freeze.
