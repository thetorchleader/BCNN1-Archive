Video Reportedly Shows Solange Knowles Attacking Jay Z; Beyoncé Posts Prayer for Help With Relationships

May 12, 2014 Entertainment

Evan Agostini/Invision/AP; Larry Busacca/Getty Images

Whoa, here’s something we never expected to see. TMZ has video of what looks like Jay Z, Beyoncé and her sis, Solange Knowles, riding in an elevator at a Met Gala after-party last week at the Standard Hotel in NYC. In the video, which appears to be from a surveillance camera, Jay Z, Beyoncé and Solange step into the elevator before things turn scary, with Solange screaming at her bro-in-law and lunging at him in a violent attack.

Neither Jay or Beyoncé fight back, and luckily there is a bodyguard-type who does his best to restrain Solange, but she still manages to slip out of his grip several times.
