Greater Ebenezer Missionary Baptist Church in Los Angeles to Celebrate Convocation 2014 on April 9th

March 30, 2014 Uncategorized

Pastor Solomon Drake

Children and adults will enjoy Convocation 2014 sponsored by Greater Ebenezer Missionary Baptist Church, 5300 South Denker Avenue in Los Angeles.  The gathering will open on Wednesday, April 9, and continue through Friday, April 11, at 6:30 p.m.

The theme is ‘The Foundation of God Standeth Sure’ with a scripture reference of II Timothy 2:19.

“We invite the public to share these three nights of joyful celebration as we reflect and remember the Foundation.  There will be classes for all ages, from toddlers, children, youth, young adults to mature, seasoned men and women of the laity and ministers of the Gospel,” said Pastor Solomon L. Drake.

The agenda will include worship, prayer, songs and Bible Study. The instructors will be Pastor Drake, lay ministry workers and ministers from Greater Ebenezer. Also, clergy from throughout greater Los Angeles will preach and teach at the convocation.
