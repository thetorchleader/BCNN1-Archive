Mary Mary’s Tina Campbell Voted Favorite Pregnant Celebrity

May 25, 2012 Uncategorized

Tina Campbell of Mary Mary was voted a favorite pregnant celebrity! Tina won the poll that was running on Belly Itch. Through the poll, readers could vote for their favorite celebrity who is currently pregnant. 
Other celebrities who made it to the top favorites were Drew Barrymore, Reese Witherspoon, Kourtney Kardashian, Tori Spelling, Tamera Mowry- Housley, Sienna Miller, Sarah Michelle Gellar, and Lily Aldrige. 
The gospel artist Tina Campblell, also stars in Mary Mary’s reality TV show called “WE TV”. Currently, Tina and her husband Teddy are expecting their 5th child. Her due date is in August. 
Gospel duo Mary Mary, just released a new record called Go Get It in May 8th, 2012. The first single from the album is a title song “Go Get It” to which they also recorded a music video.
