Bishop Patrick L. Wooden Sr. Consecrated to Sacred Office of Auxiliary Bishop In COGIC

November 16, 2014 Christian

Dr. Patrick L. Wooden Sr.

Dr. Patrick L. Wooden, Sr. has been saved and serving the Lord for more than 30 years. He has been in the pastoral ministry for more than 25 years. He accepted Christ at the age of 16 and his life has been an example of what God can do when you give Him your whole heart at an early age. He attributes his success to God’s grace, mercy and unmerited favor. He resides in Raleigh, NC with his wife of thirty years, Pamela McNeil Wooden. They have two adult children, a son-in-law and two grandchildren.
