Good for Her — Whoopi Loses 30 Pounds

April 16, 2014 Entertainment

Comedian Whoopi Goldberg during an interview on April 3, 2014. (Lloyd Bishop/NBC/NBCU Photo Bank via Getty Images)

Whoopi Goldberg is looking better than ever — and her fans are definitely taking notice.

The acting icon and “The View” co-host took part in a Facebook chat with her fans Wednesday and when one asked how much weight she has lost recently, she replied, “30 pounds!”

Goldberg also spoke about adjusting to being a great-grandmother at just 58 when a fan asked what she liked more, being a mom or grandmother.

“I liked it all, but now I am trying to figure out how to deal with being a great-grandma. It’s kinda fun,” she added.
