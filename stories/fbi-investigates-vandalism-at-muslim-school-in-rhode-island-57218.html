FBI Investigates Vandalism at Muslim School in Rhode Island

February 17, 2015 National

A Muslim school in West Warwick, Rhode Island, was targeted by anti-Islamic graffiti over the weekend. The FBI has joined police in investigating the incident. (Islamic School of Rhode Island)

A Muslim school in Rhode Island was targeted Saturday by vandals with obscene graffiti and the FBI has now begun a civil rights investigation.

NBC News reports the Islamic School of Rhode Island provided pictures showing graffiti that says “Die Pig” and “F— Allah now this is a hate crime”.

The Council on American-Islamic Relations tells NBC News it considers the vandalism a hate crime and urged “the strongest possible charges [be] brought against the perpetrators once they are apprehended.”

The vandalism occurred a day after a vigil was held at the school for three Muslim students killed in North Carolina, according to the Providence Journal.

“I find it very upsetting,” Rabbi Sarah Mack of Temple Beth-El in Providence tells the Journal. “The Muslim community in Rhode Island is very open and peaceful.”

The Boston division of the FBI has joined West Warwick, R.I., police in investigating the incident, the Journal reports.

“The FBI takes these matters very seriously,” the agency said in a news release.

Hilmy Bakri, the president of the school’s Board of Trustees, released a statement on the school’s Facebook page in regard to the vandalism.
