President Obama, Mitt Romney Spending Millions on Dishonest Ads

August 10, 2012 Uncategorized

President Obama and GOP contender Mitt Romney are taking a break from the campaign trail Friday.
Still, a new round of campaign ads from both sides is keeping the fight for the White House heated.
Both candidates are spending millions of dollars on negative ads. One estimate suggests the total will top $1 billion by Election Day.
Now, both sides appear to be throwing “fact” out the window.
The Romney campaign is crying foul over the latest ad from a super PAC supporting Obama. The ad implies that Romney is responsible for the death of a woman whose husband worked for a company that was shut down by Bain Capital.
“When Mitt Romney and Bain closed the plant, I lost my healthcare,” the woman’s widower says in the ad. “And by then it was stage four. There was nothing they could do for her. And she passed away in 22 days. I do not think Mitt Romney realizes what he has done to anyone.”
In fact, the man’s wife died years after Romney left Bain Capital. The ad is by far the most controversial of the campaign.
It comes after the president criticized super PACs supporting Romney for spending millions on negative ads.
“Over the next three months, you will see more negative ads,” Obama told an audience at a campaign event in Colorado. “I mean, these super PACs, these guys are writing $10 million checks and giving them to Mr. Romney’s supporters.”
