North Carolina’s Calvary Church Remains Tight-Lipped on Pastor John Munro’s Leave

August 26, 2014 Christian

Dr. John Munro

Questions mount up about a controversy at one of Charlotte’s largest churches.

“He calmed the church,” said World Magazine Vice President, Warren Cole Smith.

The man who brought peace in the storm for Calvary Church is now in the middle of his own. Senior Pastor, Dr. John Munro, is on administrative leave, and based on a statement released to the congregation, we know there is a third-party investigation ongoing.

The senior pastor was recently cut from the radio program “Back to the Bible”. The organization’s CEO said they terminated the relationship with Munro due to inappropriate conduct and behavior detrimental to its mission.

An elder told the Calvary congregation of the investigation last Sunday, and Munro did not speak.

Smith, who has covered church controversy over the years, says the church is taking the right steps.

“The church is handling this in a very responsible way. A lot of churches try to hide or try to hide what’s going on at the church. They’ve been very public in terms of telling their membership. We don’t know exactly why John Munro has been placed on leave right now, but I would argue that at this point of the process that’s appropriate,” said Smith.

Smith also agrees with going outside for review of the allegations.

“Too often churches try to handle this internally. They don’t have the objectivity and they don’t have the skills to deeply investigate these types of situations,” said Smith.

The church released a statement Monday morning saying all communications involving the investigation would remain within the church family to preserve its integrity. Back to the Bible did not return our phone calls.
