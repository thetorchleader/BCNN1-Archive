Mississippi Parents Upset that Police Pointed a Gun at Their Special Needs Son

February 10, 2015 Black

Paul and Angela Thompson Roby claim that police pulled a gun on their 6-year-old son.
FOX 6 SCREENSHOT

A Jackson, Miss. Couple is furious after they claim police pointed a gun at their 6-year-old son who is special needs.

According to Fox 6, Paul and Angela Thompson Roby and their son were visiting with Angela’s mother when police arrived looking for 23-year-old Carneigio Gray, Angela’s brother for failure to appear in court on a paraphernalia charge from 2012.

“My son was telling the police officers to stop to not do that,” Angela Thompson Roby told the news station. “Please don’t hurt his uncle and that’s when the guns were drawn on him and my mother was telling them hey please don’t point your gun at my grandbaby. Please don’t do that.”

Ridgeland PD Lt. John Neal claims that Gray resisted arrest but did not comment on whether a gun was aimed at the boy.
