Man Who Works at Disney in Orlando Accused of Sexually Attacking Co-Worker

August 11, 2014 Black

Gideon Hunte, 49
(Photo: Orlando Sentinel)

A Walt Disney World Resort custodial worker was arrested on Friday on allegations of holding a co-worker against her will and sexually battering her in a closet at their place of work.

Gideon Paul Hunte, 49, was arrested on charges of kidnapping and sexual battery.

Orange County Sheriff’s Office officials said that the woman claimed that Hunte, whom she knew from seeing him at work, pinned her against a wall in a custodial closet on Thursday evening and forced her onto her knees while attacking her.

According to the Sheriff’s office, the victim, who was not identified, told deputies that she resisted throughout the duration of the attack, which lasted about five minutes.

She said she thinks that her attacker stopped the attack because she would not keep quiet.

The incident was reported by the victim to her supervisor, who contacted police.
