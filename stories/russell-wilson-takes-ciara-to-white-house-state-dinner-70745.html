Russell Wilson Takes Ciara to White House State Dinner

April 29, 2015 Sports

After all that commotion, we finally have an answer. 

When Russell Wilson arrived at the White House State Dinner on Tuesday, his grandmother was nowhere in sight. Instead, he was standing side-by-side with Grammy Award winner Ciara.

That’s right — he duped us all.

While The Washington Post initially reported that Wilson would be attending last Saturday’s White House Correspondents Dinner with Ciara, the Seahawks quarterback threw the world a curve ball when he posted a Facebook video of him dancing with his soon-to-be “date” — his grandmother.

Wilson probably should’ve used a fake of this magnitude back in February.
