“Growing Pains” Star and Father of Robin Thicke, Alan Thicke, to Get his Own Reality Show

March 12, 2014 Entertainment

TVGN has given Alan Thicke has own reality series.

The network ordered 14 episodes of “Unusually Thicke,” described as more “Modern Family” than “Keeping up with the Kardashians.” It will focus on Thicke’s relationship with his younger wife, Tanya and his sarcastic teenage son, Carter.

There will also be guest appearances from the “Growing Pains” star’s older son, musicianRobin Thicke, as well as his friends David Hasselhoff, Bob Saget, John Stamos, Magic Johnson, Wayne Gretzky, Bill Maher and Tom Green.
