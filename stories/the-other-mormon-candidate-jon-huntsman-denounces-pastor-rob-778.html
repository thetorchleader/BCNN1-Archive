The Other Mormon Candidate, Jon Huntsman, Denounces Pastor Robert Jeffress’ Comments About Mormonism and Calls him a ‘Moron’

October 11, 2011 Christian, National

GOP presidential hopeful Jon Huntsman denounced the Dallas pastor who called the Mormon church a “cult” and decried the flap over the religion as a “political sideshow” in the campaign.
Huntsman, who is Mormon, was asked by CNN about comments made by Robert Jeffress, a pastor at First Baptist Church in Dallas and a supporter of Texas Gov. Rick Perry, who is also competing for the GOP presidential nomination.
“The fact that … some moron can stand up and make a comment like that … it’s outrageous,” Huntsman said in an interview today with CNN’s Wolf Blitzer. “Second of all, the fact that we are spending so much time discussing it makes it even worse.”
After a speech on foreign policy, Huntsman told reporters in New Hampshire that the resulting flap is a “political sideshow” that detracts from a discussion on the economy and other key issues.
Friday, Jeffress called the Mormon church a “cult” and said, “Mormonism is not Christianity.” The comments came as Jeffress tried to explain the differences between Perry and Mitt Romney, who leads national polls on the race and who is a member of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.
