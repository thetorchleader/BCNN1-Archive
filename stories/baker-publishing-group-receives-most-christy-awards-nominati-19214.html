Baker Publishing Group Receives Most Christy Awards Nominations

April 23, 2014 Books

Baker Publishing Group books received 10 Christy Awards nominations, the most of any publisher. The 15th Annual Christy Awards, which honor excellence in Christian fiction, will be presented June 23 at the International Christian Retail Show in Atlanta.

Tyndale House Publishers books also fared well, receiving four nominations, while Thomas Nelson and Zondervan titles also scored a combined four nods for HarperCollins Christian Publishing.

The eight categories for the 2014 Christy Awards are Contemporary, Contemporary Romance/Suspense, Contemporary Series, First Novel, Historical, Historical Romance, Suspense and Visionary. Each category has three nominees, except in case of a tie.
