Parents of James Foley Say They Knew Where Their Son Was for 6 Months, But U. S. Government Did Nothing to Save Him

October 8, 2014 National

After speaking with President Barack Obama by phone on Aug. 20, 2014, John and Diane Foley talk to reporters outside their home in Rochester, N.H. (PHOTO CREDIT: Jim Cole, AP)

Monday on BBC News the parents of James Foley said they knew where their son and 17 other hostages were for six months and the U.S. government did nothing to save him.

Diane and John Foley told the heartbreaking story of wondering if anything was happening to save their son form the Islamic terrorists who eventually beheaded him.

The Foleys were respectful as the said they were hoping somebody competent was doing their job to save their son but now they have learned for six months while they were waiting and getting no updates from the Obama administration no attempts were made to save James and 17 other hostages.
