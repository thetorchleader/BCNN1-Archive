Author, Mentor, and Motivational Speaker, C. NaTasha Richburg, to Feature Books for Youth and Young Adults at 2014 Full Gospel Conference in Atlanta, GA on July 9-11

May 19, 2014 Books

Author, Mentor, and Motivational Speaker, C. NaTasha Richburg

ATLANTA, GA, (May 5, 2014) – C. Natasha Richburg and CNR Ministries will feature books for youth and young adults at the 2014 Full Gospel Conference in Atlanta, GA. Richburg’s books seek to assist youth and young adults in building their lives on the Word of God and making positive decisions for life change through faith-based, inspirational literature. The books written by C. NaTasha Richburg have definitive and open ended questions that allow for an intrapersonal understanding of learned principles. The openness of short stories blend with scriptural references to provide youth leaders an opportunity to flow as the spirit of God leads.

Richburg’s books include: The Day Night Light, A Hoodie Ain’t a Coat, Drama to Driven, LIFE…What’s Up With Yours? Who’s Renting Space in Your Head? The Truth About the Lie I Live, Every Story Has Two Sides, and Healthy Waters Don’t Cry Like This. All titles are perfect for youth Bible study, small group discussion, or even for youth to read and discuss on their own or with their friends.
Her latest 2014 book titled, The Day Night Light, includes the lyrics of CNR Ministries’ Jeremiah’s Kall, a group of gospel hip hop artists who seek to present audiences with high energy Spoken Word and Hip-Hop concerts without using profanity, derogatory lyrics and/or degrading images and opportunities to support community-based civil objectives that will leave audiences with lasting expressions of optimism. Click here to watch the Jeremiah’s Kall videos. Find out more about the artists here.
The 2014 Full Gospel Conference will be held in Atlanta, GA on July 9-11 at the Georgia World Congress Center. The conference is hosted by Bishop Paul S. Morton, and will include Bishop William Murphy, Jr., Bishop Joseph W. Walker, III, Bishop Tommie Triplett, and Dr. Jackie McCullough among others. The theme of the conference is “Celebrating Legacy, Pursing Destiny: INTEGRITY.” For more information about the conference, call 1-877-909-7915, email info@fullgospelconference.org, or visit www.fullgospelconference.org.
C. NaTasha Richburg has a B.S. in Business Administration from Frostburg State University, a MBA from Morgan State University, and a MIS from Johns Hopkins University. Mrs. Richburg is currently attending Morgan State University in pursuit of a Doctorate in Urban Leadership Education. With over 25 years of federal government service, Mrs. Richburg is also President and CEO of CNR Ministries, an author, mentor, and motivational speaker. Mrs. Richburg has also served as an adjunct instructor at the University of Maryland Baltimore County in the Information Systems department for over 15 years. Mrs. Richburg is married to her husband, Melvin, and has four children. Motivating others to achieve “excellence” is the force behind her passion that “[a] wise man is strong; yea a man of knowledge increaseth strength” (Proverbs 24:5).
For more information about C. NaTasha Richburg and Jeremiah’s Kall, visit: www.JeremiahsKall.com and www.theCNRministries.com.
To book C. NaTasha Richburg for speaking engagements and/or book signings, please email: cnrministries@verizon.net or 410-340-0165.
All books by C. Natasha Richburg are available on Amazon.com, Amazon Kindle, BarnesandNoble.com, BooksAMillion.com, and ChristianBook.com.
