2-Year-Old Girl Found Safe After Being Reported Missing from New Birth Missionary Baptist Church

October 17, 2012 Uncategorized

UPDATED: 1:27 p.m., Oct. 16: According to a media reports, the missing 2-year-old girl was found safe at Lithonia High School. C
——–
A two year-old girl was abducted this morning outside the Fitness Center at New Birth Missionary Baptist Church in Lithonia, DeKalb County police say.
According to preliminary reports to authorities, the incident happened around 9:30 a.m. when a caregiver left the child unattended in a car outside the church’s fitness center at 6400 Woodrow Rd. in Lithonia.
The child and the car were gone when the caregiver returned, police said in a press release.
