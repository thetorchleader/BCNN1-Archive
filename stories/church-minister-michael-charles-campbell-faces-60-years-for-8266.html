Church Minister Michael Charles Campbell Faces 60 Years for Raping 12-Year-Old Girl

September 21, 2012 Uncategorized

Florida minister Michael Charles Campbell (pictured) has been convicted of raping a girl repeatedly and now faces up to 60 years in prison for his offenses, according to the Orlando Sentinel.

Lying to her father that he was going to have her volunteer at a funeral home where he worked in December 2006, Campbell reportedly picked up the 12-year-old youth in a hearse, telling her he was actually going to help establish her modeling career with a “skit.” While driving, Campbell convinced the girl to disrobe, adding that he needed to “take a look at [her] first.”
Once Campbell drove to a hotel, according to the girl, he showed her how to put a condom on and then “kissed and fondled her” for several hours. He then gave her $44.
Soon after, the youth told the police.
But a year later, Campbell picked her up yet again for another “skit,” and even though the girl reportedly attempted to resist, with Campbell telling her that “[this] was how Brittany Spears and Beyonce got their start,” she was not successful.
