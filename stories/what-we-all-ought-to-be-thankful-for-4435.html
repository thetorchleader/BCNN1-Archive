What We All Ought to be Thankful For

November 22, 2011 Christian

Thanks is a word we teach our children at a very early age. We teach “giving thanks” to our children, but do we apply it to our lives? Here are some things we can be thankful for this year.

WE CAN BE THANKFUL FOR GOD’S GRACIOUSNESS
The Bible says in Ephesians 5:20, “Giving thanks always for all things unto God and the Father in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.”
We should thank the Lord all the time — for the good times as well as the bad. We can give thanks because we know that God can use whatever circumstances we are in for His glory and our benefit.
WE CAN BE THANKFUL FOR GOD’S GREATNESS
God is great because of what He can do. Jeremiah 32:17 says, “Ah Lord GOD! behold, thou hast made the heaven and the earth by thy great power and stretched out arm, and there is nothing too hard for thee.”  
The same God who created the heavens and the earth cares for you. He freed the Israelites from their bondage in Egypt and He can free you from your bondages. He walked on water and calmed the sea and He can calm the storms of your life. 
WE CAN BE THANKFUL FOR GOD’S GOODNESS
God is good because of His MERCY. The Bible says in Psalm 107:1, “O give thanks unto the LORD, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.”
God showed us His mercy when He sent His Son, Jesus Christ, to die on the cross for our sins. Romans 5:8 says, “But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.”
Because of our sins, we deserve death. Romans 6:23 states, “For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.”
Jesus Christ died on the cross to pay the penalty for our sins. He died in our place to bring us to God and He rose from the dead to give us the gift of new life. 
John 3:16 says, “For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.”
Pray and ask Jesus Christ to save you today, and you, too, will have something to thank God for. If you will accept Jesus Christ as your Lord and Saviour, please pray this prayer with all of your heart: Heavenly Father, I realize that I am a sinner and that I have done some bad things in my life. For Jesus Christ sake, please forgive me of my sins. I now believe with all of my heart that Jesus Christ died for me, was buried, and rose again. Lord Jesus, please come into my heart and save my soul and change my life today. Amen.  
This year, let’s remember to thank God. He deserves our thanks for who He is and because of what He has done for each and every one of us. A prayer of thanksgiving from each of us is a good beginning to giving thanks not just one day a year, but always and for everything. 
Remember, friend, Believe by faith. Share the faith. and Keep the faith!
