Brooklyn Church Leaders on Both Sides of the Soda Ban Debate Announce a Strategy to Combat Obesity and Heart Disease

April 9, 2013 Uncategorized

Today, church leaders from St. Philip’s Christian Church, Mt. Pisgah Baptist Church, COFAITH Church, Ephraim Judah International Ministries, and Mt Zion announced a Faith and Wellness initiative, a year-long program designed to promote healthy lifestyles among African-Americans living in the Bedford Stuyvesant, Brownsville and East New York sections of Brooklyn.

The initiative was devised last month after a New York Supreme Court Justice struck down Mayor Michael R. Bloomberg’s plan to prohibit the sale of large sugary drinks in New York City, a group of north Brooklyn church leaders on both sides of the debate developed a strategy to combat the high rates of obesity and heart disease and increase overall wellness in their communities.
The first in a series of events will begin on Saturday, April 13, 2013, noon-5:00 p.m. with Health Matters: Faith & Fitness Expo at the Brooklyn Stuy Dome, a multi-fitness complex adjacent to St. Philip’s Christian Church, 765 Lafayette Avenue, Brooklyn. The Expo will be free and feature activities for all ages, including health screenings, yoga, zumba, basketball, breathing workshops, spiritual aerobics and much more.
“Regardless of your position on the size of soft drinks, there is no denying that Brooklyn has high rates of obesity, diabetes, heart disease and strokes,” said Pastor NaRon Tillman, St. Philip’s Christian Church.
Pastor Tillman continues, “Religious activities from bake sales to the traditional Friday fish fry are deeply rooted in food, and most of these selections tend to be high in saturated fat. We believe that our churches along with churches around the country can play a role in encouraging good physical health as an important part of a person’s overall spiritual well-being.”
For more information or to register, log on to: http://www.spccbrooklyn.org or call 718.453.0691.
