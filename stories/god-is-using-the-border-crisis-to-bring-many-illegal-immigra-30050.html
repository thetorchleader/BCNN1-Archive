God Is Using the Border Crisis to Bring Many Illegal Immigrant Children to the Gospel

July 12, 2014 Christian

June 20, 2014 Immigrants who entered the U.S. illegally stand in line for tickets at the bus station after they were released from a U.S. Customs and Border Protection processing facility in McAllen, Texas. (AP)

God is using the border crisis with illegal immigrant children to bring many of them to the Gospel.

Many churches and ministries are helping the children, including holding voluntary worship services for them.

Associate Pastor Dan Trevino, Baptist Temple in San Antonio, Texas, says he’s been preaching to a thousand kids at a time. So far more than 2,000 have come to Christ.
