LISTEN: Al Sharpton Talks to Tom Joyner About Upcoming March on Washington

December 4, 2014 Black

Rev. Al Sharpton  talked to the Tom Joyner Morning Show about the lack of indictment in the case of Eric Garner in Staten Island and admitted he wasn’t surprised by the ruling.

“No I really wasn’t. Local state grand jury have to deal with local police everyday. That’s why you need federal prosecutors that have no relationship with local police. Next Saturday the 13th, we are going to march on Washington,” he says.
