Louisiana Gov. Bobby Jindal Prays for Spiritual Awakening to Take Place In America

January 7, 2015 National

Seventy-two of us met at the Louisiana Governor’s Mansion a couple of weeks ago to plan The Response-Louisiana: A Call to Prayer for a Nation in Crisis with Governor Bobby Jindal.

​Jan. 24, at the Pete Maravich Assembly Center, 10:00 AM-4:00 PM. KTBS reporter—Shreveport’s ABC affiliate—Rick Rowe (friend of Dr. Fred Lowery) showed up with a camera. This three-minute video came from that meeting—the video has been shown four times on ABC ​Greater ​Shreveport​ (Ark-La-Tex​​)​ in the last two weeks.

Unbelievable, or actually BELIEVABLE​, is all that I can say.
