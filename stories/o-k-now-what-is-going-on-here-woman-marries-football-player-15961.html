O.K. Now, What is Going On Here? Woman Marries Football Player, Abuser Ray Rice, After he Knocked her Out?

March 29, 2014 Sports

Ray Rice (Andrew Weber-USA TODAY Sports)

One day after he was indicted on charges for aggravated assault, Baltimore Ravens running back Ray Rice married the woman he is accused of striking, according to reports.

RB Ray Rice got married Friday night to Janay Palmer. Ceremony had been planned for couple of weeks. Got indicted Thursday, married Friday. — Adam Schefter (@AdamSchefter) March 29, 2014

Ravens Insider: Ray Rice marries Janay Palmer amid assault case bsun.md/1gCaoHE—
Baltimore Sun Sports (@BaltSunSports) March 29, 2014

According to the report, the ceremony had been planned for several weeks.

Rice was arrested Feb. 15 after an alleged altercation with his then-fiancée, Janay Palmer, in Atlantic City, N.J.

Rice’s attorney, Michael Diamondstein, told USA TODAY Sports earlier in the week that his client is innocent.
