‘Empire’ Star Taraji P. Henson to Host ‘SNL’ April 11th

March 26, 2015 Entertainment

Take these cookiesssss, Empire fans.

Taraji P. Henson, who plays the fur-loving matriarch on the hit Fox series, will host Saturday Night Live on April 11. Mumford & Sons will be the musical guest.

Her TV son Jussie Smollett posted his approval on Instagram: “You deserve all of this so much.”

We know that Lucious Lyon doesn’t have ALS. And we know his jailbird ex-wife Cookie is power-mad, to say the least. Which gives SNL writers plenty of raw material to work with for the show.
