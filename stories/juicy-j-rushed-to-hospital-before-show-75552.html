Juicy J Rushed to Hospital Before Show

May 24, 2015 Entertainment

Juicy J was rushed to the hospital before a show last night in San Francisco.

Juicy J was schedule to perform at The Fillmore in San Francisco last night but, before the show, the Memphis rapper began experiencing shortness of breath and was quickly rushed to the hospital.

He sent out an apology tweet to fans, asking them to pray for him and, today, we’ve gotten word from J that he’s doing okay. The doctor who saw Juice at San Francisco Memorial Hospital told the Taylor Gang co-boss he hasn’t been getting enough rest. When recovered, the Juiceman promises to finish “The Hustle Continues” tour and make up for his missed show in San Fran.

We wish Juicy J a speedy recovery.

I wanna apologize to my fans in San Fran I had to rush to the hospital I’m having shortness of breath I love you all pray for me — juicy j (@therealjuicyj) May 24, 2015
