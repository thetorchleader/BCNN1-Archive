Barnabas Fund Helps Christians Affected by Disastrous South-East Asian Floods

October 29, 2011 Uncategorized

As people across south-east Asia continue to suffer from the worst floods for 50 years, Barnabas Fund said it is sending urgently needed help to Christians in four countries.

Barnabas Fund works to support Christian communities around the world where they are facing poverty and persecution.
Barnabas Fund reported in a news release that hundreds of people, many of them children, have been killed in the floods, and millions have been seriously affected. Many have been forced to flee their homes, have seen their livelihoods swept away, or are living without sufficient water, food or sanitation.
Barnabas Fund said that in Thailand, one of the worst-hit countries, thousands of residents are rushing to leave the capital, Bangkok. Some northern parts of the city are already 90 percent submerged, and this weekend a huge run-off from the central plains is expected to inundate more districts.
Barnabas Fund said that about a third of all Thailand’s provinces are affected, and more than 360 people have died there alone. Cambodia, Burma (Myanmar), the Philippines, Laos and Vietnam have also been ravaged by the floods, triggered by unusually heavy seasonal rains.
Helping Christians in Need
Barnabas Fund has launched an urgent appeal to help Christians caught up in the disaster. Working through Christian partner organizations, the group said it will be providing a variety of assistance.
In Burma, Barnabas Fund said, the organization plans to feed 1,338 Christian families, mainly by providing rice and oil. In addition, the group will rebuild 112 houses.
