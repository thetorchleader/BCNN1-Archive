JetBlue Is Offering Free Flights for Police Officers Attending Funerals of Slain New York Police Officers

December 25, 2014 Black

Officers Rafael Ramos and Wenjian Liu
TWITTER

JetBlue airlines is extending its services to law-enforcement officials, offering free flights along its route network to officers wishing to attend the funerals of the two New York Police Department officers killed in Brooklyn, N.Y., the New York Post reports.

JetBlue has promised to fly up to two cops from each department to the city from anywhere along its network.

“We’re honored to do what we can to support the communities we serve, and our team has made flights available to law enforcement agencies … who wish to send representatives to New York to support their brethren,” JetBlue spokeswoman Sharon Jones told the Post.
