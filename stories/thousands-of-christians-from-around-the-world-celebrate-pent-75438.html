Thousands of Christians from Around the World Celebrate Pentecost 2015 With “Empowered 21” Movement In Israel

May 24, 2015 Christian

Empowered21 / Facebook

Thousands of Christians from all over the world came to Jerusalem as part of a movement called Empowered 21. They came to worship, pray, and celebrate Pentecost 2015.

Organizers of Empowered 21 believe the gathering represents the largest meeting of believers in Jesus to celebrate Pentecost since the first one nearly 2,000 years ago. They represented every continent.

“We are all from Burkina Faso, West Africa, and being here for this conference is very meaningful because we are in the Holy Land,” one participant told CBN News.

A woman from Hong Kong said she came because she wanted “to experience more of the Holy Spirit.”

“We came with Spirit Life Church from Arizona and the Rock from Anaheim and from Katy, Texas,” another attendee said.

“Empowered 21 is a relational network. This is a key word,” Empowered 21 President and Global Co-Chairman Billy Wilson told CBN News. “We are relating together in the Spirit-filled, Spirit-empowered movement around the world.”

“So this is the bringing together of Pentecostal and Charismatics across denominational lines to talk about unity, the power of the Holy Spirit, and how we can help new generations connect with God,” he said.

“The Pentecostal/Charismatic movement is growing dynamically,” Global Co-Chairman George Wood explained. “It’s the growing edge of Christianity. It’s been estimated by various scholars that the Spirit-empowered movement embraces between 600 to 700 million believers.”
