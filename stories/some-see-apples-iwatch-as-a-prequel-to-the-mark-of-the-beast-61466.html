Some See Apple’s iWatch as a Prequel to the ‘Mark of the Beast’

March 13, 2015 Second Coming Watch

The reality of mobile lifestyles have increased demands on mobile computers and all eyes are now on the upcoming release of the iWatch.

More than just a watch, the iWatch will have processing abilities like a computer and communication abilities like a smartphone.

It will also have Apple Pay capabilities, raising concerns with critics that society is getting too close for comfort to invasive technologies.

The Apple Pay systems uses fingerprint scanners to complete purchases, which some critics say represents the “mark of the beast” prophesied in the Bible.

People can already use smartphones for financial information and transactions. How likely is it we will soon be using implanted chips for the same purpose?

Privacy Expert and best-selling author of the children’s book, I Won’t Take the Mark, Katherine Albrecht answered this question and more.
