Michelle Williams Encourages Followers to Break Generational Curses

February 25, 2015 Entertainment

Destiny’s Child alum and current star of Oxygen’s ‘Fix My Choir!’ Michelle Williams (Oxygen/Facebook)

Destiny’s Child alumna Michelle Williams’ latest album is breaking ground and inspiring listeners to live bold and biblical lives.

Her fourth solo album, Journey to Freedom, addresses the star’s struggle fighting depression and how she sought the Lord.

“I’m still on the journey,” Williams tells the LA Times. “It’s a journey of leaving the past and moving forward and being free from anything that’s going to keep me from being my absolute best.”

Part of that journey is taking ownership of her life and breaking generational curses spoken over her.

“It can be tough knowing that YOU have to be the one to break the cycle. YOU can do it. Generational curses will be broken by YOU! Yep!” she tweeted yesterday.
