Harvey Weinstein Will Not be Charged with Groping Women

April 10, 2015 Entertainment

Hollywood producer Harvey Weinstein, will not be charged by the Manhattan District Attorney following the groping scandal with Italian model Ambra Battilana. (GARDINER ANDERSON / SPLASH NEWS)

Movie mogul Harvey Weinstein will not face charges in the alleged “casting couch” groping of a young Italian model in his Tribeca office, the Daily News learned Friday.

The Manhattan District Attorney passed on a prosecution of the Oscar-winning Weinstein in the bizarre March 27 encounter, said a source familiar with the prosecutor’s decision.

“This case was taken seriously from the outset, with a thorough investigation conducted by our Sex Crimes Unit,” said Joan Vallero, spokeswoman for the DA.

“After analyzing the available evidence, including multiple interviews with both parties, a criminal charge is not supported.”

The source said the DA felt the case involving model Ambra Battilana, 22, who met Weinstein the night before the Hollywood hotshot allegedly pawed her, could not be proven beyond a reasonable doubt.

The two were alone in the office during the meeting, creating a “he said/she said” scenario.
