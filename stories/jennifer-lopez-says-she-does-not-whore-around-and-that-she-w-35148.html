Jennifer Lopez says She Does Not ‘Whore Around’ and that she Would Get Married for a 4th Time

August 24, 2014 Entertainment

Jennifer Lopez told Chelsea Handler that she does not “whore around” and likes being in a relationship — find out what she thinks about getting married for a fourth time. Credit: Cindy Ord/Getty Images

No plans, no problems! Jennifer Lopez stopped by Chelsea Lately on Thursday, Aug. 21, where she chatted with the outgoing late-night host about her endlessly buzzed-about romantic life.

“I would get married again!” the thrice-married Lopez, 45, told Handler. “I like being in a relationship. I’m not one to like, whore around and stuff like that… that’s not my thing.”

Handler noted how the singer was last in a relationship with 27-year-old Casper Smart, saying: “You like younger guys.”

The now-single mother of two replied, “I dated one younger guy. My last boyfriend.” She added that among the many famous spouses and boyfriends she’s had in the past — largely with men who are older or in the same age group as her — Lopez first and foremost always looks for chemistry in a significant other.
