Wu Tang Clan Rapper Shyheim Pleads Guilty to Manslaughter Charge

July 18, 2014 Black

Rapper Shyheim, affiliated with the Wu-Tang Clan has pleaded guilty to a manslaughter charge stemming from a fatal car accident on New Year’s Day.

Shyheim Franklin, 35, surrendered to police in January in the days after the crash on Staten Island, which killed Felipe Avila Olea, 29. He was accused of driving into the victim’s car after ignoring a stop sign, and hitting a parked vehicle before fleeing the scene on foot.

Franklin has now pleaded guilty to second-degree manslaughter and a weapon possession charge relating to a gun which police officers found in his apartment last year.
