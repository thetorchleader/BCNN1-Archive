Toy-maker Hasbro Shares Soar After Company Beats Profit and Revenue Estimates

April 20, 2015 Business, zTransfer-UCN

Steven Leon demonstrates the Transformers: Robots in Disguise Super Bumblebee by Hasbro at the North American International Toy Fair on Feb. 14 in New York. (Mark Lennihan, AP)

Shares of toymaker Hasbro, sellers of brands such as Transformers, Nerf, Monopoly  and Play-Doh, soared 8% in pre-market trading after topping profit and revenue forecasts.

About two hours before the opening bell, Hasbro (HAS) shares were up $5.41, or 8.2%, to $71.30.

The company reported first-quarter earnings of 21 cents per share, topping forecasts of 8 cents. It also beat on the top line with revenues coming in at $713.5 million, above the estimate of $660 million. Hasbro said it had a negative currency impact from the strong dollar of $62.6 million.

“2015 is off to a good start with continued momentum in our business, led by growth in all of our Franchise Brands and the underlying strength in demand across international markets, including the emerging markets,” said Brian Goldner, Hasbro’s president and chief executive officer
