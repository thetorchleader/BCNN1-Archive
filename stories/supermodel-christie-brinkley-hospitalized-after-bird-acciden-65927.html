Supermodel Christie Brinkley Hospitalized After Bird Accident

April 7, 2015 Entertainment

Christie Brinkley shows off a nasty black eye she suffered at a resort…

Christie Brinkley is nursing a black eye and cut face after injuring herself while seemingly trying to save a bird during her Easter vacation in Turks and Caicos.

Over the weekend, the supermodel, 61, took a boat and then a plane to a hospital in Miami for treatment, reports New York’s Daily News.

On Monday, Brinkley posted a photo of herself in a hospital gown on Instagram, along with a good look at her swollen eye and a close-up of a gash under her brow.

“We interrupt this holiday to take a little unplanned excursion to Miami for cat scans and stitches following an accident trying to save a bird,” Brinkley wrote along with the shot.
