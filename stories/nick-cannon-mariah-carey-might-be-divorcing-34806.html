Nick Cannon, Mariah Carey Might Be Divorcing

August 21, 2014 Entertainment

Jim Spellman, WireImage

With rumors swirling that Mariah Carey’s marriage to Nick Cannon is ending, Cannon has decided to go ahead and put it out there:

“There is trouble in paradise…we have been living in separate houses for a few months,” Cannon told The Insider, denying infidelity to be the cause of the split.

Right now, Cannon says, he and Carey are focused on their 3-year-old twins.  “My main focus is my kids,” he says. The couple have been married since 2008.

Source: USA Today
