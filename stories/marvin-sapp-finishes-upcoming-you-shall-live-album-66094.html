Marvin Sapp Finishes Upcoming “You Shall Live” Album

April 8, 2015 Entertainment

Photo Credit: GETTY

GRAMMY® Award nominated recording artist Marvin Sapp has combined two of his favorite things: spending quality time with his daughters, Madisson and Mikaila, and working on music.

As the three enjoy their spring break getaway, Dad also completed his forthcoming album.

“Working while on vacation, with my daughters [sic] permission,” said Sapp, 48, on Facebook Monday, April 6.

He added, “Finale [sic] finished my CD today. Long process but well worth it.”

The Michigan-based Lighthouse Full Life Center Church pastor, who was recently elevated to the office of Bishop Designate, is slated to release You Shall Live on June 2, 2015.

His first official single “Yes You Can,” is now available.
