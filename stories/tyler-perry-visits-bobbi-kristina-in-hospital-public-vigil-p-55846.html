Tyler Perry Visits Bobbi Kristina in Hospital; Public Vigil Planned

February 8, 2015 Entertainment

Twitter | @cbs46
CBS46 on Twitter

Family members continue to surround Bobbi Kristina Brown, who remains at Emory University Hospital on life support.

On Sunday, Cissy Houston was spotted heading in, as was family friend Tyler Perry.

It appears that a public vigil has been planned for Monday in Riverdale, Ga., according to posts on Facebook and Twitter by her cousin Jerod Brown.
