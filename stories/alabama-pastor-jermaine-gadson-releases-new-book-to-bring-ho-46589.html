Alabama Pastor Jermaine Gadson Releases New Book to Bring Hope and Clarity Amid Ferguson Fear

November 24, 2014 Books

In order to bring hope and clarity in the midst of today’s social upheaval and unrest, Pastor Jermaine Gadson is pleased to announce the release of his new book, Tactics, Trends, & Traits of the Enemy: Equipping the Believer to Fight Back. Pastor Gadson explains how the Enemy uses strategies like fear and deception to steal, kill, and destroy a person’s life; especially in the case of Michael Brown.

The tragic death of Michael Brown, a young African American male who was shot and killed by a Ferguson police officer has sparked national attention, unmasking the racial disparity that has been woven deep within the Ferguson community. This case exposes the racial divides that remains in many cities across America today, fifty years after the Civil Rights Movement.

In Tactics, Trends, & Traits of the Enemy: Equipping the Believer to Fight Back, Pastor Gadson explicates how many of the dilemmas that terrorize our lives and communities are more spiritual than they are natural and can only be solved using spiritual methods. Social injustice, fear, and racial unrest all poses threats to the fabric of our society and can only be relinquished when we understand the true source behind these threats.

Tactics, Trends, & Traits of the Enemy: Equipping the Believer to Fight Back is a valuable resource to the citizens of Ferguson, as well as other communities across America that face similar struggles of fear, racial division, and social injustice. Citizens who live outside of these communities will be well-equipped with the necessary tools to proactively fight, ensuring that their communities do not succumb to similar spiritual attacks. Change must come to our cities in America, one that is marked by spiritual unity and social harmony. This book is a call to all American citizens to stand up to the unseen forces that attack our spiritual, social, and emotional well-being, with faith, steadfast hope, prayer, and God’s word.

Jermaine Gadson, the Senior Pastor of Faith Ministries, Inc., located in Birmingham, AL, earned a B.A. in Religion and a minor in Classics from Samford University, and a Master of Divinity from Samford’s Beeson Divinity School. He is currently pursuing a Doctor of Ministry degree. For more information, please visit: www.jermainegadson.com.
