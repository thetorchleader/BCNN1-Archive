National Baptist Convention President, Dr. Jerry Young, Issues Message of Thanks

October 23, 2014 Christian
Greetings in the Matchless & Magnificent Name of Jesus the Christ,

Allow me to express my profound gratitude and authentic appreciation to all of you for your  outstanding support evidenced through your consistent prayers, your charitable gifts and your conscientious volunteerism throughout the course of my campaign to become president of the National Baptist Convention, USA, Inc.

On September 4, 2014, I was tremendously humbled and honored to be elected president of our beloved Convention. Needless to say, it would not have happened without each of you. In fact, the words of Alex Haley convey the sentiment of my own heart when he says, “Anytime you see a turtle up on top of a fence post, you know he had some help.” I am indeed cognizant of the reality that without Christ and the various constituent entities of our Convention, my best efforts would have fallen short.  THANK YOU!!!!!

As your president, I commit to you that my earnest desire, as well as my determination, is to serve the collective constituents of our ENTIRE Convention. In fact, I look forward with great anticipation to your interactions, involvement, and insights as we commit ourselves to a servant leadership model, working together to accomplish what we believe God has ordained for us.

I am acutely aware that I have witnessed both the grace and the goodness of our Lord to have been entrusted with such a stupendous stewardship. Consequently, I humbly solicit your prayers and participation to the end that our partnership, plus the prevailing power of God, will impact our Convention in such a powerful way that we will witness a great transformation. This transformation will propel us towards developing a denominational thrust and witness in our country and ultimately, throughout the world.
