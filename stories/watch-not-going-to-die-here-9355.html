WATCH: "Not Going to Die Here"

November 16, 2012 Uncategorized

Nadine C. Keels
Paperback, 52 pp., $5.79
CreateSpace (April 10, 2012)
Admitting that you’re wrestling with depression or suicidal thoughts may not be the easiest thing to do as a Christian, but if you feel trapped in a sorrowful place, the way out has already been made. Not Going to Die Here, from The Song of Nadine, combines personal testimony, Scripture, and poetry to help you transition from sorrow into God’s incomprehensible peace and unspeakable joy. Wherever the hope of glory lives, hopelessness passes away.
