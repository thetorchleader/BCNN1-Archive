Black Pastors Urge Speedy End to Chicago Teacher’s Strike

September 11, 2012 Uncategorized

A group of African-American ministers is demanding that both sides settle the teachers strike right away.

Pictured: Members of the Chicago Teachers Union picket in front of Chicago Public Schools headquarters Monday.

Bishop Larry Trotter of the Sweet Holy Spirit Church says the strike means that instead of being in class, vulnerable young African American kids will be on the streets, at risk for violence:

“I want to say, by the power of those of us who are concerned about children and parents, we demanding y’all, to come on out and resolve this.”
Trotter appeared with about a dozen other black ministers from the South and West sides, many of whom are offering their churches as places where children can stay in the daytime while the strike continues.
Some of the ministers clearly blamed Mayor Rahm Emanuel, rather than the teacher’s union, for the city’s first teacher’s strike in 25 years.
