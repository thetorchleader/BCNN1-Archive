Indiana Serial Killing Suspect Darren Vann Upset by News Media

October 23, 2014 National

Darren Vann
(Photo: Uncredited, AP)

Serial killing suspect Darren Vann was held in contempt of court Wednesday after he refused to participate in a hearing, in part because he was upset by the news media’s presence at the proceeding.

Vann, who has been charged in the deaths of two women and has confessed to killing five others, refused to answer Magistrate Judge Kathleen Sullivan’s questions. She quickly ended the hearing and called on Vann to appear before her next week.

The court appearance by Vann came just hours before prosecutors formally leveled murder charges against him for the strangulation death of Anith Jones, 35, of Merrillville, Ind.

Jones was reported missing earlier this month, and her body was among the women Vann led investigators to last weekend. On Wednesday, he was making his initial court appearance on murder charges for 19-year-old Afrika Hardy.

Before the hearing in a courtroom in the Lake County Jail started, Vann was reluctant to leave a holding area and come into the courtroom, which was crowded with news media.

“Please tell your client this is an open courtroom and I have to let people in,” Sullivan told the client’s public defender.
