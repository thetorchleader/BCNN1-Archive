Geneva Thomas Arrested for Cracking Bottle Over Head of Co-Star

October 23, 2014 Entertainment

Geneva Thomas of Blood, Sweat and Heels
BRAVO TV

In today’s “When Keeping It Real Goes Wrong” news, a reality-TV star was arrested while shooting an upcoming episode.

According to TMZ, Geneva Thomas was arrested Tuesday after she reportedly cracked a bottle over another woman’s head. Law-enforcement officials told TMZ that the cast was on a boat in New York City shooting an episode of Blood, Sweat and Heels when Thomas got into an argument with Melyssa Ford.

Thomas was charged with felony assault, and Ford was taken to a hospital, where she received stitches.

Earlier this year, Thomas was also arrested on charges that she bailed on her taxi fare. One has to wonder if the recent mayhem will make it into an upcoming episode, or whether will Bravo keep it under wraps.
