Mother of Pastor John Hagee Goes Home to Be with the Lord at 98

May 5, 2012 Uncategorized

Vada Mildred Hagee, mother of John Hagee, has gone home to be with the Lord. She was 98.
Hagee was born in Alice, Texas on May 29, 1913 to Charles Albert and Martha Swick. She died on April 30, 2012.
Hagee lived most of her early life in Goose Creek, Texas. After graduating from high school, she earned her bachelor’s degree in Theology from Southern Bible College which later became Southwestern Assemblies of God University.
She married William Bythel Hagee in 1933. Hagee and Bythel had four sons, William Bythel Hagee Jr., John Charles Hagee, Jack Thomas Hagee and Michael David Hagee.
