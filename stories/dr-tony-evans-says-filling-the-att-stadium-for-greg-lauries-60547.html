Dr. Tony Evans Says Filling the AT&T Stadium for Greg Laurie’s Harvest America Event “Is a Goal Worth Shooting For”

March 8, 2015 Christian

Tony Evans

The evangelical event that drew an overflow crowd to American Airlines Center in October will return to Dallas-Fort Worth this fall with the hope of filling AT&T Stadium in Arlington, a venue so large it can handle a crowd of 100,000 or so.

Harvest America, led by Southern California pastor Greg Laurie, filled the AAC with 20,000 people in 2014, with an overflow crowd spilling onto AT&T Plaza outside.

“We thought it was a great success, and there was so much excitement that we decided to come back to Dallas-Fort Worth,” Laurie said this week. “So we were looking at some stadiums of in-between size, and we decided to go for something big and bold and just go for it.

“So this will be one night in a venue everyone knows, not just in D-FW but around the country,” he said. “It’s a state-of-the-art stadium with incredible screens, and we think it’s just a great place to set up this event for the country.”

While the event itself will be centered in Arlington, it will be broadcast simultaneously over multiple media platforms to every part of the U.S. and beyond, Laurie said.

Dr. Tony Evans, pastor of Oak Cliff Bible Fellowship in Dallas and a part of the local advisory team for Harvest America, said he expects churches from across the region to join together, and “that synergy of churches will drive the people” to the stadium for the free event.

“I think it is an ambitious goal, but I think it’s one worthy of the occasion,” Evans said. Filling AT&T “is a goal worth shooting for, and I think to a large extent is achievable.”
