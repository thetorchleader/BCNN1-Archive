New York Times Bestselling Author, Edward Klein, Says Even the President’s Closest Friends Believe Obama Is “In Over His Head”

May 24, 2012 Uncategorized

A New York Times bestselling author says political leaders, even the president’s closest friends, think Barack Obama is “in over his head.”
Edward Klein conducted nearly 200 interviews with Obama’s friends and dissatisfied colleagues for his new book, The Amateur: Barack Obama in the White House. He has learned that even some of Obama’s most fervent supporters are now convinced the president is dangerously in over his head.
