USA Today Wants to Know Why Did You Return to the Faith?

March 26, 2012 Uncategorized
Easter and Passover are coming up — the fundamental holy days of Christianity and Judaism. If you grew up going to church on Easter Sunday or getting together at the Seder table, left it all behind years ago, then returned to religion in the past year, we want to talk with you.
Did you flee the faith, reason your way out or just drift away? Why did you come back?
Did your spirit urge you to return to the rites, prayers and worship you once knew? Is this going to be your first Easter or Passover celebration in a long time?
Tell us in 200 words about your choice to return to religion.
Send it to cgrossman@usatoday.com with your e-mail and daytime phone number.
The best stories will be used in an upcoming article.
SOURCE: USA Today
