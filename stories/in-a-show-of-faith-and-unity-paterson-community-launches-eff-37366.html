In a Show of Faith and Unity, Paterson Community Launches Effort to Rebuild Its Church Destroyed by Fire

September 6, 2014 Christian

MICHAEL KARAS / STAFF PHOTOGRAPHER
Sandra Sanchez, of North Haledon, on the left in a white shirt, who donated $1000; Emile Taylor, of Paterson, left font; and Clarice Forbes, of Paterson, right, during a fundraiser worship service at Community Baptist Church for Central Baptist Church which last week was destroyed in a fire.

In the face of adversity, members of a Paterson church gathered Thursday night to raise funds to rebuild their church, which was destroyed by  a fire last week.

Rogeiris Balbi, a member of the church, Iglesia Bautista Central,  commonly known as Central Baptist, was among the performers at  the fund-raising event, which featured prayers, songs of worship and praise, and artistic performances, at the Community Baptist Church of Love on Broadway. It raised more than $2,000.

“God can restore what is broken and change it into something amazing,” said the 23-year-old, a graduate of Eastside High School.  “I think God has used this to prepare us,” said Balbi, adding that the church’s pastor, Jose Luis Castro “had been teaching us about faith. I think God is giving us the opportunity to rebuild the temple, so we can be united as the body of Christ.”

Often during the program, about 100 congregants and other participants referred to one particular Bible verse: “We have a building of God, a house not made with hands.”

Central Baptist has been temporarily holding services at nearby People’s Park Reformed Church. “Many pastors have offered the use of their sanctuary,” said Castro. “We are having a meeting of church members at the end of the week to discuss future plans and options for our church, including where to meet.”

Authorities have not yet disclosed the cause the fire, which broke out on the evening of Aug. 27  while congregants were engaged in Bible study.

“We don’t know how much it will take to rebuild the church,” which was insured, Castro said. “The community has been wonderful. I’m amazed at the support and help.”

Mayor Jose “Joey” Torres has offered to help expedite the municipal permits needed to rebuild the church.
