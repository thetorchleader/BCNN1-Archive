Amber Bullock Releases New Music “Lord, You’ve Been So Good”

May 28, 2012 Uncategorized

Amber Bullock became nationally known after winning season four of BET’s “Sunday Best,” and now she has another song for you to love called “Lord You’ve Been So Good.” 

This brand new single is produced by PJ Morton-the man responsible for “Over & Over” by Trin-i-tee 5:7, who is also the son of Bishop Paul S. Morton, the keyboardist of Pop group Maroon 5, and an extremely gifted solo artist.
