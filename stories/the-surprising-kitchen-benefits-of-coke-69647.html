The Surprising Kitchen Benefits of Coke

April 24, 2015 From Around the Block

© REX/Kieran McManus/BPI

Coke is more than something you can gulp down with a slice of pizza. Try experimenting with coke in your recipes or use it for removing stains. Click through this slideshow to learn more!

COPPER CLEANER
If your copper utensils have lost their sheen, soak them in Coca-Cola for an hour. The acid in the drink dissolves the stains from the metal.

BAKED HAM IN COLA
Take advantage of your slow cooker with this dish. For the recipe, click here.

COCA-COLA BARBECUE SAUCE
Mix cola and barbecue sauce in equal amounts. This adds to the taste of the original sauce. For the recipe, click here.

BARBECUE WITH COKE
Marinate meat in Coke for a couple of hours before putting it on the grill. Baste the meat with a fresh can of cola as it cooks.

COCA-COLA CAKE
Biting into a slice of cake can turn a good day into a great day. For the recipe, click here.

TASTIER DIPS
Try tweaking your favourite dip by adding few teaspoons of cola to give it some extra sweetness and zing.
