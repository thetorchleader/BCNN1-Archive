Kerry Washington Says It Was a Challenge to Film “Scandal” While Pregnant

August 10, 2014 Entertainment

Steve Granitz/WireImage

The last season of Scandal was full of many secrets, the biggest being Kerry Washington’s pregnancy.

Whether it was a long designer coat or a well-placed lamp, Shonda Rhimes’ team worked hard to conceal their lead actress’ pregnancy instead of writing it into her character’s storyline. The decision made it difficult for Washington to film the 18-episode season.

“All of last season was a challenge for me, on a few levels,” the 37-year-old told The Hollywood Reporter. “For one thing, I was going through this amazing physical transition, this physical journey of having a baby, that my character was not going through.”

The Emmy-nominated actress went on to explain that as an actress, she uses her body as an instrument, making a concealed pregancy that much more difficult to work through. “It was like seeing the keys on a piano jump around on a daily basis. It was tough for me to even maintain Olivia’s walk because of the changes,” she said.
