Nick Cannon Sues Wife, Mariah Carey

March 15, 2015 Entertainment

Nick Cannon is suing Mariah Carey for selling their home without his permission, according to TMZ.

I hope that happened after they split up.

The suit says Carey’s business manager, Michael Kane, engineered the sale of the $9 million Bel Air mansion the former couple once shared, and the Cannon didn’t find out until the sale was closed.

Cannon reportedly isn’t upset that the house was sold — just that no one kept him in the loop, TMZ reported. He’s also reportedly concerned about how the money will be divvied up. Cannon supposedly wants most of the $9 million until he gets a detailed accounting of how the dough is being split up.

Kane told TMZ the lawsuit is “frivolous,” and is “clearly a desperate act from a sadly desperate man.”
