Daniel Whyte III Publishes 500th Episode of The Prophet Daniel’s Report Broadcast/Podcast. We Give God All the Glory, Praise, and Honor and We Thank the Thousands of You Who have Listened!

November 6, 2014 Christian

On Wednesday, November 5, Daniel Whyte III released the 500th episode of The Prophet Daniel’s Report. This end-times/Bible prophecy broadcast has three aspects:

1. Signs that Point to the Return of Jesus Christ: An up-to-date report of news and world events through the lens of Scripture.

2. Prophecy Bootcamp which deals with the basics of prophecy, the Second Coming of Christ, and what will happen in the future according to the Bible. The aim is not to make predictions but to help you get prepared by understanding how things will unfold in the end times.

3. What You Need to do in Light of His Coming: Practical action points that you can take away from the show and apply to your everyday life.

You can listen to the 500th episode of The Prophet Daniel’s Report below.

Visit SecondComingHerald.com for more prophecy-related news and to listen to more episodes of The Prophet Daniel’s Report and the Second Coming Watch Update.

Daniel Whyte III has spoken in meetings across the United States and in over twenty-five foreign countries. He is the author of over forty books. He is also the president of Gospel Light Society International, a worldwide evangelistic ministry that reaches thousands with the Gospel each week, as well as president of Torch Ministries International, a Christian literature ministry which publishes a monthly magazine called The Torch Leader.

He is heard by thousands each week on his radio broadcasts/podcasts, The Prayer Motivator Devotional, The Prayer Motivator Minute, as well as Gospel Light Minute X, the Gospel Light Minute, the Sunday Evening Evangelistic Message, the Prophet Daniel’s Report, the Second Coming Watch Update, the Soul-Winning Motivator, the Reasons to Believe, the History of Christianity, Understanding God’s Great Salvation, The Cross in Culture, and the Brother’s Keeper.

He holds a Bachelor’s Degree in Theology from Bethany Divinity College, a Bachelor’s degree in Religion from Texas Wesleyan University, a Master’s degree in Religion, a Master of Divinity degree, and a Master of Theology degree from Liberty Baptist Theological Seminary. He has been married to the former Meriqua Althea Dixon, of Christiana, Jamaica for over twenty-six years. God has blessed their union with seven children.
