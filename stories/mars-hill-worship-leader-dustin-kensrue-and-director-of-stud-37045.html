Mars Hill Worship Leader, Dustin Kensrue, and Director of Student Ministry Resign; Leaders say They are Being “Defamed as ‘Immature'”

September 4, 2014 Christian

Dustin Kensrue

The worship director at troubled Mars Hill Church resigned on Monday, telling churchgoers he was taking leave out of love for them and out of his inability to keep dealing with an “unhealthy, fear-driven, self-protective leadership culture at MH.”

Dustin Kensrue posted a lengthy letter urging those who remain in the congregation not to be intimidated, saying:  “Refuse to be put in silos.  Talk with others about your concerns and do not let them label you as divisive.”

The director of student ministry at Mars Hill Bellevue also has resigned, stating a similar loss of faith with the church’s leadership.

“It has become clear to us over the last month, however, that we will no longer be able to continue forward in good conscience under the current structure and ministry culture,” wrote Adam Ramsey.

Kensrue was one of nine pastors who wrote last week to the church’s board of elders, saying the mega-church has brought problems down on itself by “continued lack of transparency” and “not seeking the truth.”  They recommended that controversial Senior Pastor Mark Driscoll remove himself from the pulpit and administration, and submit to a “restoration process.”

The letter was not well received by a newly reconstituted board, which issued its own we’re-taking-care-of-things missive.

With Kensrue’s resignation, four of the nine “have resigned or been relieved of their positions,” Warren Throckmorton reported Wednesday on the Patheos website.

“While the nine signers of the letter that was leaked last week have been met with gratefulness and an outpouring of prayer from the people of Mars Hill,” Kensrue wrote, “internally we have been dismissed and defamed as ‘immature’ among other epithets.

“We have been blamed for creating problems we did not create, but only exposed.”

Kensrue, in the band Thrice, was worship leader at Mars Hill Orange County in California.  He came north in 2012 to direct music for the church.

He said five pastors from Mars Hill Bellevue who signed the letter were informed that “we weren’t going to be fired at this point (I am assuming for PR reasons, but it was made clear where the door was).” Exiting out the door would be “just fine.”
