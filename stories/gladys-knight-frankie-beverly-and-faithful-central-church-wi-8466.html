Gladys Knight, Frankie Beverly and Faithful Central Church Will Do Concert Together

September 29, 2012 Uncategorized


Los Angeles based mega church, Faithful Central Bible Church, announced its partnership with Habitat for Humanity of Greater Los Angeles.

This October, the church is putting on a benefit concert that will include performances by Gladys Knight, and Maze, featuring Frankie Beverly, at the Hyatt Century Plaza Hotel.
The partnership was created to help build healthy homes, and healthy families, in impoverished communities.
But the work doesn’t stop there.
South Africa will be getting some love too.
