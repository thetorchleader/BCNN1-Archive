WATCH: Will Smith Plays a Conman In “Focus”

December 23, 2014 Entertainment

Being a conman probably isn’t as easy as Will Smith makes it look in the new trailer for Focus.

When he’s not busy being a professional thief, Smith’s Nicky Spurgeon also manages to find time to teach Margot Robbie’s Jess Barrett how to grift like a pro, and their professional relationship quickly comes to resemble an old married couple–even when their lives are in danger.

Focus releases in theaters on Feb. 27, 2015, but it won’t be the last team-up for Smith and Robbie. The two are also set to star together in Warner Bros.’ 2016 DC Comics film Suicide Squad.
