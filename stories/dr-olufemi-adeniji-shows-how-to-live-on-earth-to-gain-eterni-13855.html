Dr. Olufemi Adeniji Shows How to Live on Earth to Gain Eternity with God in Steps to Heaven

March 14, 2014 Books

If one would ask the average person whether they want to go to Heaven when they die, the majority of those who respond would say “yes”. Today, however, people both inside and outside of the church still falsely believe that good works, power, charity, and other external things can get them into Heaven. In Steps to Heaven, author Olufemi Adeniji explains actions believers can take in life to ensure an eternity with God in Heaven. He also emphasizes the inevitability of death and why no amount of wealth, power, privilege, or fame can stop the hands of time.

Adeniji answers the following question in this book: When your time comes, do you know what will become of your eternal soul? Adeniji openly discusses what happens to the soul and what God’s followers need to know to wrestle their souls from the grips of Satan in Hell and prepare them for eternity with God in Heaven.
In Steps to Heaven, Adeniji encourages readers to search for the truth about Heaven and Hell, to begin actively thinking about which fate their souls are destined for and make preparations to meet God and spend eternity with Him in Heaven. This book guides readers to the truth of Scripture and the reality of Heaven for those who believe.
Olufemi Adeniji is a retired and celebrated high school principal and educational administrator, who earned a Ph.D. from the University of North Texas. He has worked tirelessly in various positions of leadership and has vowed to use his writing to wrestle away souls from the hands of Satan in Hell and prepare them for eternity with God after their lives end on Earth.
