Pastor Melvin and First Lady Jacquie Wade of Mount Moriah Baptist Church in Los Angeles Celebrate 40 Years of Ministry

April 26, 2015 Black

Pastor Melvin and Lady Jacquie Wade

Mount Moriah Baptist Church of Los Angeles will conclude the 40th Year Appreciation and Recognition Celebration of Pastor Melvin and First Lady Jacquie Wade on Sunday, April 26, during the 9:15 a.m., and 4 p.m., worship services. Pastor Wade will also be celebrating his 50th year as a pastor. 

The theme is ‘Our Shepherd, 40/50 Years Staying True to His Calling”  (40 Years as our Pastor and 50 Years in Ministry) based on Jeremiah 20:9 and I Corinthians 1:23-24.  Horace Hall and Vicki Ward are the celebration chairs.

Under Dr. Wade’s leadership, Mount Moriah has engaged in local and national causes including the construction of the Mount Moriah Senior Villa, a forty-unit apartment building for seniors to live independently. The villa was built in honor of the late Dr. Earl A. and Mrs. Olga W. Pleasant.
