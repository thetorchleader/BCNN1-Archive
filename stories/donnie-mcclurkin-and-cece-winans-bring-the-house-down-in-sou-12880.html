Donnie McClurkin and Cece Winans Bring the House Down In South Africa

June 8, 2013 Uncategorized

Some people were born to entertain, to get everyone dancing and lifting their hands up towards the sky.
One of them is none other than US award winning music minister Donnie McClurkin who rocked Christians and music fanatics on Saturday at the Mavuso Trade and Exhibition Centre. The event, which was hosted by the Family Worship Centre International, attracted a vibey crowd of over 1 500 people who came for nothing but to worship. Worship they did as all the artists who performed made sure that they got their money’s worth.
The artist, who is also a pastor, had his local fans eating from the palm of his hands the moment it was announced that he, together with fellow US star Cece Winans were in the building.
Before that, the congregation had to endure a whole hour of waiting as the US stars’ crew worked on testing the sound. McClurkin got a little bit naughty as he peeped through the curtains of the upstairs dressing rooms, smiled and waved to his fans.
As expected, this invited loud screams from the ladies and whistles from the males which filled the whole hall. By the time he appeared from backstage with the microphone, the fans were already going crazy.
