Pebble Time to Start Shipping Out on May 27th to Backers

May 26, 2015 Technology

The first bundle of Pebble Time watches will start shipping out on May 27th to backers who were first in line to order the watch. Those who were first will get their tracking numbers in the middle of June, so not too much longer for those who are anxious to receive it. Unfortunately, I was not one of them to order the watch though I very much enjoyed the 1st generation Pebble watch.

Early backers were able to pay the price of $159 (regular price $199), while the second tier pays $179 and gets one of three colors (black, white, or red). Pebble Time Steel on the other hand won’t ship until July if you have ordered one of them. Nonetheless, there doesn’t seem to be any issues in the way from having the set of watches being shipped.

Pebble Time has some feature that most smartwatches have these days, but it’s still nice to see another smartwatch keeping up with the trend. Main features for the Pebble time are: color e-paper display, up to 7 day battery life (same as other Pebble watches and 10 days for Pebble Time Steel), newer interface, and a microphone for voice replies. For iOS users, voice replies are limited to Gmail notifications.
