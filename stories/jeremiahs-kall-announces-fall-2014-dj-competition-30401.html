Jeremiah’s Kall Announces Fall 2014 DJ Competition

July 15, 2014 Black

Jeremiah’s Kall, a Christian organization that promotes faith-based hip-hop music and hip-hop artists, announces its Fall 2014 DJ competition. This year’s search will take place in Maryland, Ohio, and Michigan.

For those interested in participating in the competition in one of the three locations, please submit the following by midnight on September 15, 2014 to jeremiahskall@gmail.com:
– Prepare a 3-5 minute MP3 DJ mix that includes at least two songs from at least one indie artist from Jeremiah’s Kall
– First name, Last name
– Stage name
– Mailing address
– Phone number
– Preferred competition location
To view artists signed by Jeremiah’s Kall, visit: www.jeremiahskall.com. Click here to listen to music by Jeremiah’s Kall artists or visit them on Reverbnation.
Jeremiah’s Kall hip-hop artists lyrically PROMOTE positive PROSE that POWERFULLY provides PROFOUND insight without PROFANITY.
