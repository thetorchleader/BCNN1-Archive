President Obama Readies for 9/11 Remembrance Week

September 7, 2014 National

President Obama
(Photo: Charles Dharapak, AP)

Memories of the 9/11 attack will shadow President Obama’s upcoming week.

In addition to honoring the anniversary of the 9/11 attacks on Thursday, Obama will lay out a strategy to counter the Islamic State jihadist group amid renewed concern about the possibility of new attacks on the United States.

On Tuesday, Obama meets with the bipartisan congressional leadership about his strategy regarding the Islamic State, which has taken large swaths of land in Iraq and Syria and is seeking to establish a caliphate that could launch attacks against U.S. interests.

The president is scheduled to address the American people about his strategy on Wednesday, the eve of the 13th anniversary of terrorist attacks on the World Trade Center and Pentagon.

Obama will recognize that anniversary Thursday by leading a moment of silence on the South Lawn at the White House.

The president and first lady Michelle Obama will then attend the annual September 11th Observance Ceremony at the Pentagon Memorial.

SOURCE: USA Today – David Jackson
