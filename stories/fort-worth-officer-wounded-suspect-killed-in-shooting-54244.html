Fort Worth Officer Wounded, Suspect Killed in Shooting

January 29, 2015 National

A Fort Worth police officer is injured and a suspect is dead after shots were fired Thursday evening.

The incident was reported in the 4800 block of Hildring Drive in southwest Fort Worth.

The officer was transported by ambulance to Harris Methodist Hospital for treatment of unknown injuries.

Sources tell NBC 5 the officer was shot in the stomach and is expected to survive.

Further details on what led to the incident have not yet been released.
