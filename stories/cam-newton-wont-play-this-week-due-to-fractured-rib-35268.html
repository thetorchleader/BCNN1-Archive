Cam Newton Won’t Play This Week Due to Fractured Rib

August 24, 2014 Sports

Carolina Panthers quarterback Cam Newton has a fractured rib and will not play in Thursday’s preseason finale at Pittsburgh, according to ESPN.com.

Newton suffered a hairline fracture to a rib during Friday night’s loss at New England, according to the website.

Panthers Coach Ron Rivera said he is optimistic that Newton will be able to play in the opener at Tampa Bay on Sept. 7.
