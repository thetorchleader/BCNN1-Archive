Mississippi Mother Pregnant With Triplets Evicted from Home

November 2, 2014 God & Family

Chevis Poole loading a UHaul truck (Source: WMC Action News 5)

A Horn Lake mother, with triplets on the way, handed over the keys to her house. Now, she and her family say they have no place to sleep.

Chevis Poole, 28, was evicted from her home after not paying her rent for the past few months. The young mother already has two small kids and says she could birth her triplets any day.

After WMC Action News 5’s Jerry Askin shared her story on Thursday, Poole says she’s gotten some support from the community, but it’s not enough to suffice. The desperate mother says she’s grateful for all of the calls, emails, and donations she’s received. A kind viewer even gave the family a U-Haul truck on Friday morning to help with the move.

Chevis Poole fell behind by nearly $4,800 in rent and eviction court fees. She says was put on bed rest and unable to continue working at a local bank. She had a job, was planning on having one baby, but wasn’t planning on triplets, especially without In Vitro Fertilization.
