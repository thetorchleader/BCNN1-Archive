An Early Picture of Jesus Has Reportedly Been Found In an Ancient Egyptian Tomb

May 1, 2014 Christian

The Egyptian government are planning to develop the site to protect this early Christian artwork (Picture: University of Barcelona)

We’ve seen images of Jesus on a pancake, pieces of toast and even bruises.

This time Christ appears to have been found underground in an ancient Egyptian city.

The mural was found in a room thought to be the tomb of a young writer and several priests, and is believed to date back to the 6th century AD.

University of Barcelona experts described the image to newspaper, La Vanguardia,as ‘a young man with curly hair, dressed in a short tunic and with his hand raised as if giving a blessing’.

Lead researcher Josep Padró said: ‘We could be dealing with a very early image of Jesus Christ.’
