Man Walks Into Lakewood Church Bookstore, Damages Items Before Being Arrested

August 30, 2014 Christian

click2houston.com

Investigators are releasing more details about the damage inside Lakewood Church following a disturbance that happened Thursday.

Police say 30-year-old Michael Fletcher walked into the bookstore of the church and damaged or destroyed four displays, one clock, 25 wind chimes, three books, four crosses and four plaques.

An off-duty deputy working security says he struggled to get Fletcher under control.

Officers had to cuff his hands and feet and then time him to a gurney to get him out of the church and into a patrol car.
