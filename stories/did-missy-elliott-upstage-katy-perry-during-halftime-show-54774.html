Did Missy Elliott Upstage Katy Perry during Halftime Show?

February 2, 2015 Black

Missy Elliott (center) and Katy Perry (left) perform during the Super Bowl halftime show at the University of Phoenix Stadium in Glendale, Ariz., Feb. 1, 2015.
CHRISTOPHER POLK/GETTY IMAGES

During Sunday’s Super Bowl halftime show, all eyes weren’t exactly on Katy Perry, especially when R&B superstar Missy Elliott joined the show.

It’s been over a decade since Elliott released new music, but that didn’t stop her from being the showstopper she is. During her three-minute set, she performed her popular hits “Get Ur Freak On,” “Work It” and “Lose Control.”

It’s safe to say that the Virginia native is back.
