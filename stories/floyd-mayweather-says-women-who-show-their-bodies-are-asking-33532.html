Floyd Mayweather Says Women Who Show Their Bodies Are ‘Asking to Be Disrespected’

August 12, 2014 Sports

Boxing champ says on his Instagram account that women should ‘dress how you want to be addressed’

Boxing champ Floyd Mayweather has upset some fans with a photo he posted to his Instagram account a few days ago:

“If a female shows half of her body, she’s asking to be disrespected,” says the quote, in part. Mayweather added a short caption to go with it, which says, “Dress how you want to be addressed.”

“Probably the dumbest thing you could have said,” one fan commented. “It don’t matter what a woman is wearing, she should never be disrespected,” said another.

Other fans did, however, agree, with one saying, “The truth hurts.”
