Michael Jackson’s Daughter Hospitalized After Suicide Attempt

June 6, 2013 Uncategorized

More details are emerging from Paris Jackson’s hospitalization, and what happened leading up to it, on Wednesday.
TMZ reports that the teen took “a large number of Motrin pills” and cut herself with a “meat cleaver.” The site says Michael Jackson’s daughter called a “suicide hotline” and left a “suicide note.”
People.com offers a slightly less dramatic picture of the scene, but confirms that Jackson, 15, “cut her wrists with a kitchen knife and took as many as 20 ibuprofen tablets.”
The Jackson family, in statement from lawyers, has asked for “privacy.” Katherine Jackson’s lawyer said Paris “is physically fine” now and is “getting appropriate medical attention.”
La Toya Jackson told ET that the family requests privacy while as they handle “these issues together.” Paris’ brother, Prince Michael, 16, has canceled an appearance on German television to be by Paris’ side, reports People.
Meanwhile, in addition to Beatles tune tweets and other messages shared on her Twitter feed, Paris also talked to fans this past week in a 20-minute video posted on YouTube on May 31. It shows Jackson in what she describes as her bedroom playfully demonstrating how she applies her makeup.
