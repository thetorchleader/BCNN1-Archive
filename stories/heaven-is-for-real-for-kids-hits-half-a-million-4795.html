‘Heaven Is for Real for Kids’ Hits Half a Million

March 22, 2012 Uncategorized

Since its release in November 2011 from Tommy Nelson, the children’s picture book Heaven Is for Real for Kids has sold over 500,000 copies worldwide. The book has been translated into 12 languages; in December 2011, an app based on the book was released.
SOURCE: Publishers Weekly
