Bethel AME Church in Marysville to Celebrate 160 Years of Service

May 23, 2014 African-American

Reverend Dr. Harold R. Mayberry preaching at the First African Methodist Church in Oakland, Calif., Sunday, January 15, 2012. Nearly two dozen prominent African-American pastors in the Bay Area are going to try to connect the Occupy movement to the community through a new effort called Occupy the Dream. Photo: Liz Hafalia, The Chronicle

Bethel African Methodist Episcopal Church in Marysville is celebrating its 160 years of service with a guest speaker Sunday at 115 Fifth St.

The Rev. Dr. Harold Mayberry, pastor of the First A.M.E. Church of Oakland will speak at the 4 p.m. service.

The anniversary celebration also includes a fellowship banquet at 6 p.m. today at the Peach Tree Golf and Country Club in Linda. Tickets are $35 per person. For reservations, call Brother Howard Wheatley at 742-2051.

Celebration festivities began Wednesday with an old-time religion, prayer and praise service.
