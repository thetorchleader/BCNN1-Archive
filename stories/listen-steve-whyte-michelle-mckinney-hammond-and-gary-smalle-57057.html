LISTEN: Steve Whyte, Michelle McKinney Hammond, and Gary Smalley and John Trent on BC Book Review Show #260

February 16, 2015 Books

The authors that we are featuring today are: Steve Whyte, author of “The Love Manual (Lovers Edition)”; Michelle McKinney Hammond, author of “Getting Smart About Life, Love, and Men”; and Gary Smalley and John Trent, authors of “Love Is a Decision”.

The purpose of this show is to highlight wholesome Christian books, promote them to our audience, encourage you to buy these books for yourself and for others, and most importantly, to see people come to Christ and lives changed for the glory of God.

MUSICAL SELECTION: “No Greater Love” by Smokie Norful, “Jesus Loves Me” by Chris Tomlin, and “Love and Happiness” by Al Green

Visit: BlackChristianBookReview.com
