Nexus 6 Release Launches Google Into ‘Phablet’ Market

October 16, 2014 Technology

Google’s Nexus 6 smartphone.
(Photo: Google/AP)

Count Google among the tech companies making the leap into the “phablet” market.

The company announced the Nexus 6 smartphone on Wednesday, which boasts a larger display measuring 5.96 inches, slightly bigger than Samsung’s Galaxy Note 4 and Apple’s iPhone 6 Plus.

Google boasts the Nexus 6 sports a massive battery, capable of 24 hours of use from one full charge. However, the company also claims users can get up to 6 hours of use after charging the device for 15 minutes. It will be available in 32 GB and 64 GB.

The smartphone will also feature Android 5.0 — code-named Lollipop — the latest version of their mobile operating system that will now work on other devices including smartwatches, TVs and cars.

Pre-orders for the Nexus 6 will start in late October.
