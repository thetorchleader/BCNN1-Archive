Philadelphia’s African American Museum Offers Free Admission on MLK Day

January 6, 2015 Black

The African American Museum in Philadelphia will offer free admission Martin Luther King’s Birthday, thanks to a $25,000 grant from Citizen’s Bank.

The bank’s donation is expected to allow more than 3,000 people to visit the museum at Seventh and Arch Streets free of charge on Jan. 19, the day the national holiday will be observed this year. Activities planned at the museum that day include readings, arts and crafts, historic reenactments, music, dance and cultural performances. Citizens Bank will also host a scavenger hunt to help children learn about the museum.

Winners of the museum’s 2015 “Sharing the Heritage” writing contest will receive awards and read to those in attendance. Mayor Nutter is expected to join museum administrators at the museum Wednesday to announce the grant.

Source: Philly.com | Julia Terruso
