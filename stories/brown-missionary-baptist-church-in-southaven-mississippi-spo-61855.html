Brown Missionary Baptist Church in Southaven, Mississippi Sponsors Free Preaching Conference, April 17-18

March 15, 2015 Black

Bartholomew Orr is the Senior Pastor of Brown Missionary Baptist Church.

Contending for the Faith Preaching Conference

Dates & Times
Friday and Saturday, April 17 & 18, 2015 (All day event on April 17th – One-half day on April 18th)

Event Description
An explosive conference to empower pastors, ministers, and Bible teachers along their path of delivering God’s Word. No matter your years in ministry, this 1 1/2 day FREE conference is for you. (Seating is limited).

To register, go to church website – www.brownbaptist.org, scroll down and click on Conference icon to register. For further information, please contact Minister Muriel Taylor, Conference Coordinator via email, muriel.taylor@brownbaptist.org.

Event Location
Brown Missionary Baptist Church (South Campus)
7200 Swinnea Road
Southaven, MS 38671

Event Contact Person
Minister Muriel Taylor

Email
muriel.taylor@brownbaptist.org

Phone
901-288-8011
