Reggie Jackson is Thankful to God That he’s Traded Away From Thunder

February 19, 2015 Sports

In the frenzy of NBA trade deadline day, Reggie Jackson went to the Detroit Pistons in a three-team trade that saw the Oklahoma City Thunder pick up Enes Kanter from the Utah Jazz.

The embattled sixth man was pretty mum throughout the season about wanting to leave, but the prevailing suspicion was that he desperately wanted to go somewhere where he could be the man and run his own team. When he found out he’d been dealt to Detroit, he didn’t hide his excitement.
