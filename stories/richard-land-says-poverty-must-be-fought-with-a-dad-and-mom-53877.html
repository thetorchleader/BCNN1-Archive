Richard Land Says Poverty Must Be Fought With a Dad and Mom

January 27, 2015 Christian

Richard Land

When dealing with the issue of poverty, a Christian leader says presidential candidates must first deal with what works and what does not.

One hundred Christian leaders are asking presidential candidates to post a three-minute video outlining their plans to reduce poverty and hunger.

Dr. Richard Land, president of Southern Evangelical Seminary, says posting videos is fine.

He is not a candidate for any political office but says if he posted a video, he would refer to “For Richer, For Poorer,” a study that shows approximately 37 percent of income inequality can be attributed to one thing: the breakup of the family:

“If you grow up in a home with an intact family, with a mom and a dad who are married to each other by the time you’re an adult, it means a $6,200-a-year difference in your annual income if you’re a male, $4,700 difference if you’re a female,” says Land, citing the study.

He continues: “If you grow up in an intact home and you marry a person who’s from an intact home, and you stay married to each other by the time you’re in your mid-30s, that means a $42,000 difference in family income.”
