Family of Bobbi Kristina Told to Prepare for the Worst

February 2, 2015 Black

Bobbi Kristina Brown and Nick Gordon arrive at Tri-Star Pictures’ Sparkle premiere at Grauman’s Chinese Theatre Aug. 16, 2012, in Hollywood, Calif.
FRAZER HARRISON/GETTY IMAGES

Updated Feb. 1, 7:12 p.m.: A source close to the family tells ET that her father, Bobby Brown; husband, Nick Gordon; and others have been told to prepare for the worst and that the longer Bobbi Kristina remains in the coma, the more likely it will be that she will not regain consciousness. In a statement today, Bobby Brown asked for privacy for the family.

Feb. 1, 7:49 a.m.: Bobbi Kristina Brown, who lies in a coma in a Georgia hospital in a medically induced coma after she was found unconscious in a bathtub, had recently tweeted about angst over the approach of the third anniversary of her mother’s tragic death. Her mother, Whitney Houston, was found dead on Feb. 11, 2012, in a bathtub at the Beverly Hilton. Bobbi Kristina tweeted both from her @Bkhg_fanpage, which she shares with husband Nick Gordon, and her personal account, @REALbkBrown.
