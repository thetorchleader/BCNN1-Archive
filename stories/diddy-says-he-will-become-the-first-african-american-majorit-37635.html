Diddy Says He Will Become the First African American Majority Owner of an NFL Team

September 8, 2014 Black

Diddy (Photo : Getty Images)

Diddy is raising the bar.

As if owning his on television network wasn’t boss enough, Diddy revealed his dream of owning an NFL team while talking with Bloomberg.

“I love sports, but I’m more of an owner type of guy,” he said. “It will happen. I will become the first African American majority owner, not having a small stake, but actually owning an NFL team. I think it’s time for that. A majority of the players that are in the NFL are African American but there are no African American owners. That’s one of my dreams.”

Diddy also spoke on Jay Z’s transition into sports management with Roc Nation Sports signing athletes like Robinson Cano and Kevin Durant but he isn’t too hot on taking that route.

“When I see my friend having a sports agency I support him,” the hip-hop mogul explained. “When I get the calls, ‘Oh, we need to do Bad Boy Sports.’ I’m like, ‘No, that’s not my lane. I stay in my lane. This is my lane I want to do.’ I’m just me. I do my own thing.”
