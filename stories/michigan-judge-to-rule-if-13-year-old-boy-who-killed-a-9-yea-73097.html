Michigan Judge to Rule If 13-Year-Old Boy Who Killed a 9-Year-Old Boy Will Stand Trial

May 12, 2015 Black

Is Jamarion Lawhorn a withdrawn, suicidal boy so traumatized by an abusive childhood that he shouldn’t be held criminally responsible for the stabbing death of a younger boy in Kentwood last August? 

Or is he simply a shy, stoic boy who grasps the severity of the crime and is fully capable of helping defend himself against a charge of open murder?

Those arguments played out in Kent County Circuit Court Monday during a competency hearing for the now-13-year-old boy charged in the Aug. 4 playground stabbing death of 9-year-old Connor Verkerke.

Both sides testified for about four hours today; a ruling is expected by next week on whether the case will proceed to trial.

In December, a state psychiatrist determined that Lawhorn understands the charges against him and can assist in his defense. That prompted defense attorney Charles Boekeloo to ask for a second, independent evaluation.

That evaluation came back in February, concluding Lawhorn is unable to assist in his own defense.
