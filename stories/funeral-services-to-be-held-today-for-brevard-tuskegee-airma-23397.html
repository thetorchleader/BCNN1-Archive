Funeral Services to be Held Today for Brevard Tuskegee Airman Hiram Mann

May 23, 2014 African-American

Titusville’s Hiram Mann was a pilot with the Tuskegee Airmen.
(Photo: Florida Today file)

Funeral services for Tuskegee Airman Hiram Mann are set for Friday.

Viewing is at 11 a.m. and the funeral service is at noon at St. James AME Church at 625 Dummitt Ave. in Titusville.

Mann, who was one of the few surviving original Tuskegee Airmen pilots, died Saturday. He would have turned 93 Friday.

Mann flew 48 combat missions as a pilot during World War II in Europe and went on to serve more than 21 years in the Air Force, retiring as a lieutenant colonel.

Source: R. Norman Moody, FLORIDA TODAY
