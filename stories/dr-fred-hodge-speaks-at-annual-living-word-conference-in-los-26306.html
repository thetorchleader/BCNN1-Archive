Dr. Fred Hodge Speaks at Annual Living Word Conference in Los Angeles

June 12, 2014 Black

Dr. Fred Hodge

Dr. Fred Hodge of Living Praise Christian Center in Chatsworth will preach at the Annual Living Word Conference sponsored by Bishop Leon and Dr. Jacqueline Martin and the Love Peace and Happiness Family Christian Fellowship Church (LPH).

The event opens on Monday, June 2, and continues through Sunday, June 8, at the LPH Los Angeles location at 4951 South Figueroa Street.

The theme is ‘Releasing the Power Within to Win.’ The Institute Hour starts on Wednesday, June 4, through Friday, June 6, at 6 p.m., and worship services begin at 7:15 p.m.

“We invite the community to join us for six great days of spiritual refreshing.  Come and be blessed by powerful teaching from dynamic men and women of God,” said Bishop Martin.

Other speakers are Bishop Richard Sanders of Mount Pilgrim Baptist Church in Compton, Dr. Marie Gardner of Hamilton Memorial Church in San Francisco, Pastor Donell Givens of Spirit of Truth Fellowship Church and Pastor Donald Martin of Fresh Anointing Christian Fellowship in Claremont.
