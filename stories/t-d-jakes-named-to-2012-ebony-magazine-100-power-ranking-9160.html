T. D. Jakes Named to 2012 Ebony Magazine 100 Power Ranking

November 10, 2012 Uncategorized

T.D. JAKES NAMED TO 2012 EBONY 100 POWER RANKING
Annual list cites contributions of most influential African Americans
T.D. Jakes, a global humanitarian and senior pastor of the 30,000 member Potter’s House of Dallas was named to the 2012 Annual Power Ranking by Ebony Magazine.  With a subscription base exceeding one million, Ebony is a publication that chronicles the African American lifestyle. 
For its annual “Power” issue, the editorial staff identified 100 primary “influencers and game changers who have made vital accomplishments during the past year.”  Jakes was listed among newsmakers from the worlds of business, politics, education, sports and entertainment, including Dr. Cornell West, LeBron James, Barack and Michelle Obama, Tyler Perry, Tom Joyner, Robin Roberts, Shonda Rhimes, Gabby Douglas, Toni Morrison, Magic Johnson, Dick Parsons, and John Lewis. 
 “The Ebony 100 ranking speaks volumes about the diversity of our accomplishments and the power of our collective influence across virtually every spectrum of society,” said T.D. Jakes. “To be named among such an esteemed array of luminaries is indeed an honor.”
The ranking was previewed at the flagship “EBONY Power 100 Gala Presented by Nationwide” held at the Lincoln Center in New York City on November 2nd to celebrate the honorees. The list appeared in the December 2012-January 2013 issue which hit newsstands on November 5th.
About T.D. Jakes
T. D. Jakes is a charismatic leader, visionary, provocative thinker, entrepreneur, and best-selling author who serves as the senior pastor of the 30,000-member church, The Potter’s House located in Dallas, Texas.  Named “America’s Best Preacher” by Time Magazine, Jakes brings a fresh, bold new perspective to real-world issues. Visit http://www.tdjakes.org.
About The Ebony Power 100
The EBONY Power 100 spotlights African Americans who personify “power” in all its manifestations–strength, focus, commitment and determination–those who innovate, elevate and shape the world in new and different ways. EBONY Power 100’s 21 categories include “The Anointed,” “The Environmentalists,” “The Powerbrokers,” “The Thought Leaders” and “The Young Gifted & Black.” The 2012 Power 100 list will appear in the Dec./Jan. issue of EBONY on newsstands the week of Nov. 5.
