Forbes’ List of ‘100 Most Powerful Women’ Includes Michelle Obama, Loretta Lynch, Oprah, Beyonce, and Others

May 27, 2015 Black

First lady Michelle Obama
WIN MCNAMEE/GETTY IMAGES

Michelle Obama, Oprah Winfrey and Beyoncé Knowles topped Forbes’ list of the world’s most powerful women of 2015. The women join other entertainers, policymakers, businesswomen and philanthropists who made the list based on their influence and impact.

Other black women joining Obama (10), Winfrey (12) and Knowles (21) include: CEO Ursula Burns (29), Attorney General Loretta Lynch (34), Ertharin Cousin of the United Nations (47), Nigerian Minister of Finance Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala (48), Wal-Mart and Sam’s Club CEO Rosalind Brewer (65), oil magnate Folorunsho Alakija (87), President-CEO of Robert Wood-Johnson Foundation Risa Lavizzo-Mourey (92) and Ellen Johnson-Sirleaf, president of Liberia (96)

The women who topped the list included German Chancellor Angela Merkel (1), U.S. presidential candidate Hillary Rodham Clinton (2) and philanthropist Melinda Gates (3).
