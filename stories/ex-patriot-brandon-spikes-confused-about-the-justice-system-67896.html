Ex-Patriot Brandon Spikes ‘Confused About the Justice System’ After Hernandez Verdict

April 16, 2015 Sports

BILL WIPPERT/AP
Linebacker Brandon Spikes plays for the Buffalo Bills last season.

Moments after Aaron Hernandez was convicted of first-degree murder and sentenced to life without parole, Brandon Spikes was bewildered.

The linebacker, who played with Hernandez at Florida and with the Patriots, seemed to disagree with the verdict.

“IM CONFUSED ABOUT THE JUSTICE SYSTEM THESE DAYS !!!” Spikes tweeted on Wednesday morning.

Many of the responses shared a general theme: “just don’t murder people,” someone replied.

Not amused, Spikes − one of a few NFL players to weigh in − once again tweeted about his friend.

“Let me get off this cus yall wanna joke and s–t smh #coldworld,” he said, later adding, “I’ll get back on tmrw when y’all done fake caring.”
