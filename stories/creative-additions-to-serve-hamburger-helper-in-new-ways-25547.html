Creative Additions to Serve Hamburger Helper In New Ways

June 6, 2014 God & Food

Add a few simple twists to create a new way to serve Hamburger Helper® for dinner!

Hamburger Helper® Double Cheeseburger Mac
Easy Additions
• 1 can (15 oz) Progresso® kidney beans, drained, rinsed
• 1/2 cup pickled jalapeno pepper
• 1/4 cup sweet pickle relish

Toppings
• 1 cup shredded Cheddar cheese (4 oz)
• 1/4 cup each panko bread crumbs and Parmesan cheese
• 1 cup diced tomato

Hamburger Helper® Beef Pasta
Easy Additions
• 1/2 cup sour cream
• 1 cup Green Giant® frozen cut green beans, thawed, drained
• 1 can (8 oz) sliced water chestnuts, drained

Toppings
• 1/2 cup sliced almonds
• 1/2 can (2.8-oz size) French-fried onions
• Chow mein noodles

Hamburger Helper® Stroganoff
Easy Additions
• 1 cup Green Giant® frozen sweet peas, thawed, drained
• 1 cup Green Giant® frozen chopped broccoli, thawed, drained
• 1 jar (2.5 oz) Green Giant® sliced mushrooms, drained

Toppings
• 1/4 cup shredded cheese (1 oz)
• 2 slices crisply cooked bacon, crumbled
• 1 green onion, sliced

Hamburger Helper® Lasagna
Easy Additions
• 1/2 cup small-curd cottage cheese
• 1 medium tomato, chopped (3/4 cup)
• 1 can (2 1/4 oz) sliced ripe olives, drained

Toppings
• 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese (4 oz)
• Coarsely crushed seasoned croutons
• Grated Parmesan cheese

Hamburger Helper® Cheeseburger Macaroni
Easy Additions
• 1 cup Green Giant® Niblets® frozen corn or 1 can (7 oz) Green Giant® Niblets® whole kernel corn, drained
• 1 medium tomato, chopped (3/4 cup)
• 1 jar (2.5 or 4.5 oz) Green Giant® sliced mushrooms, drained

Toppings
• 4 slices crisply cooked bacon, crumbled
• 2 green onions, sliced
• Coarsely crushed potato or tortilla chips

SOURCE: Betty Crocker
