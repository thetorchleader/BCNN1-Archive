Obama’s Education Secretary, Arne Duncan, Says He Supports Homosexual Marriage

May 7, 2012 Uncategorized

President Obama is getting renewed pressure to endorse gay marriage — courtesy of his own staff.
Education Secretary Arne Duncan said today on MSNBC’s Morning Joe that he favors gay marriage, separating himself from Obama, who supports only civil unions between same-sex couples.
When asked whether he thought same sex couples should be married, Duncan said simply: “Yes, I do.”
Duncan’s comment came a day after Vice President Biden said he is “absolutely comfortable” with the idea of gay marriage.
Gay rights groups have long called on Obama to formally advocate same-sex marriage, and trumpeted the comments of his aides.
Obama has long said his personal views on gay marriage are “evolving;” today, White House spokesman Jay Carney said, “I have no update on the president’s personal views.”
Carney also said that Obama’s record on gay rights issues is “unparalleled,” citing the end to the ban on gays in the military, and opposition to federal legislation defining marriage as between a man and a woman.
Biden’s statements on NBC’s Meet The Press triggered a flurry of statements from the White House saying the vice president was not trying to distance himself from Obama.
Joe Solmonese, president of the Human Rights Campaign, said Duncan’s comments are significant because the education secretary “communicates to all students that they deserve an equal shot at the American dream of love, family and equality.”
Solomense also pointed out that Obama’s housing and urban development secretary, Shaun Donovan, has also endorsed gay marriage.
“There’s no doubt in my mind that the president shares these values and that’s why it’s time for him to speak out in favor of marriage equality as well,” Solmonese said.
