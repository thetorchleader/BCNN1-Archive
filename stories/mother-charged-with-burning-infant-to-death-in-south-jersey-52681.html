Mother Charged with Burning Infant to Death in South Jersey

January 17, 2015 Black

A young South Jersey mother is in jail, charged with the burning death of her newborn baby Friday night.

Authorities say 22-year-old Hyphernkemberly Dorvilier of Pemberton, Burlington County doused her newborn baby with a flammable liquid then set the child on fire. She then sat crying in a car nearby, watching her baby burn in the street.

It happened around 11 p.m. along Simontown Road. Police arrived and quickly extinguished the flames.

The infant was airlifted to St. Christopher’s Hospital in Philadelphia and died two hours later.
