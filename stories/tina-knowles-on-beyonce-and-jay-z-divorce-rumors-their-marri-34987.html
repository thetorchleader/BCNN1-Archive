Tina Knowles on Beyoncé and Jay Z Divorce Rumors: Their Marriage Is ‘Perfect’

August 22, 2014 Entertainment

Tina Knowles, Solange Knowles and Beyonce Knowles attend recording artist Solange Knowles’ birthday party held at a private residence on June 23, 2008 in Los Angeles, California.
Alberto E. Rodriguez/Getty Images

Is Beyonce about to become a single lady?

Not according to her mother, Tina Knowles. Everyone knows the best way to get accurate information about someone’s marriage is to track down their mother on the street, which is exactly what the intrepid stalkers at TMZ did.

“Haters gonna be haters, and there’s nothing we can do about that,” said Tina Knowles, channeling the lyrics of Taylor Swift’s new jam. “Everything’s perfect.”

When the reporter asked her if it was hard on the family to read all the back-and-forth in the media about her daughter’s marriage, Knowles sensibly replied, “I just don’t even read the bull.”
