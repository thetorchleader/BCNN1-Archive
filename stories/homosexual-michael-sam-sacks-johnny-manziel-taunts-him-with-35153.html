Homosexual Michael Sam Sacks Johnny Manziel, Taunts him With his Signature ‘Money Sign’; Analysts say it is Still Likely he Will be Cut

August 24, 2014 Sports

Michael Sam (AP)

Michael Sam, meet Johnny Manziel.

Two of the most scrutinized rookies crossed paths in the fourth quarter of Saturday night’s St. Louis Rams at Cleveland Browns preseason game when Sam, the seventh-round defensive end, sacked the Browns quarterback.

Then, the former Missouri star, who is attempting to become the league’s first openly gay player, gave Manziel a dose of his own medicine, flashing Manziel’s signature “Show Me The Money’’ finger rub. Sam sacked Manziel again later in the game.
