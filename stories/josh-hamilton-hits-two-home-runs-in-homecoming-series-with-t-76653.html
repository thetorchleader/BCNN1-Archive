Josh Hamilton Hits Two Home Runs in Homecoming Series with the Texas Rangers

May 29, 2015 Sports

AP Photo/Brandon Wade

On Friday against the Boston Red Sox, Josh Hamilton blasted two home runs — in the same game.

They were the first home runs of the season for the 34-year-old outfielder.

Hamilton was playing just his fifth game back with the Texas Rangers after being traded by theLos Angeles Angels. (The former AL MVP previously spent five seasons with the Rangers.)
