LISTEN: Christian Citizens of America, Beware of Politicians in Sheep’s Clothing (The Cross in Culture #16 with Daniel Whyte III)

February 26, 2015 Christian

Daniel Whyte III

While most in the media were either criticizing or mocking Brian Williams and his lying about events that did not happen to him during his reporting for NBC Nightly News, David Axelrod, former adviser, friend, and campaign strategist for President Obama, revealed that the president told several lies of his own regarding his views on homosexual marriage.

Most of us are familiar with the storyline that from the 2008 campaign up until his announcement in 2012 that he fully supported same-sex marriage, President Obama had been “evolving” on the issue. However, in his new book, Believer, Axelrod says that that is not true. He states candidly that President Obama has always fully supported legalizing homosexual marriage. However, it was Axelrod who advised him to hold back on stating that view publicly, and one reason was because ‘opposition to homosexual marriage was particularly strong in the black church.’ Basically, President Obama lied about his true beliefs in order to get more Christian people to vote for him.

This means that the following statements from Obama — which many Christians clung to in order to justify voting for him — were false. In other words, millions were deceived.

In 2004, during his Illinois Senate race, Obama said: “What I believe, in my faith, is that a man and a woman when they get married are performing something before God, and it’s not simply the two persons who are meeting.”

During that same campaign, he told reporters, “The term ‘marriage’ itself has strong religious roots and a strong tradition that means something special to people in this country.”

During his 2008 campaign, at a civil forum moderated by Pastor Rick Warren, Obama said: “I believe that marriage is the union between a man and a woman. For me as a Christian, it is also a sacred union — God is in the mix.”

What is particularly disturbing about these statements is that each of them cite the Christian faith as the reason for Obama’s supposed lack of support for homosexual marriage. These statements were a calculated, deliberate move by candidate Obama to sway Christian voters, specifically black Christian voters, into his camp. Now, we all have come to grips with the fact that politicians often lie, twist truths, and talk out of both sides of their mouth. Unfortunately, it’s the nature of the Washington beast. But, I would have to say that this callous use of the sacred beliefs of Christianity and of millions of followers of Christ is on another level.

Writing in The Week, Matt K. Lewis stated, “Obama cited his belief in something sacred to buttress an argument he apparently didn’t actually believe. By making his faith an accessory to a lie, he subjected something sacred to something profane. For the faithful, this really is a damning revelation.”

Dear Christian citizen, Christian voter, I know that each of us would love to have the “perfect” candidate — one who honestly and fervently shares our views on matters that are important to us, including the issue of traditional marriage. However, although we must live in this world, and pray for and respect the governmental leaders that we are under, we do not have to be deceived by their lies and vote for liars. We must be prayerful and, by the grace of God, discern the right way to go and follow God’s leading no matter what the crowd is doing and no matter whether the candidate is black or white. We as Christians must always obey God rather than man. We cannot support evil. We must pray for wisdom and discernment regarding who to vote for or whether to vote at all. For example, after much prayer, this poor preacher did not vote for President Obama either time because God didn’t give me peace about it in my soul. We must not hitch our hopes to the stars of any politician. We must not put our faith and confidence in their words, but in God’s words; not in their plans, but in God’s plans. The devil is the father of lies and the master of deceit. He has and will continue to try to deceive the children of God and get them to participate in the works of darkness — even at the ballot box. While Christians should be involved in politics, we must not be taken in by any politician. It is our job to tell them what to do, not for them to tell us what to do.

In closing, let me leave you with this Scripture passage and quote.

Psalm 118:8 says, “It is better to trust in the LORD than to put confidence in man.”

Psalm 146:3 says, “Put not your trust in princes, nor in the son of man, in whom there is no help.”

President Abraham Lincoln said, “If you once forfeit the confidence of your fellow citizens, you can never regain their respect and esteem. It is true that you may fool all the people some of the time; you can even fool some of the people all of the time; but you can’t fool all of the people all the time.”

The Greek poet Homer said, “I detest that man, who hides one thing in the depths of his heart, and speaks forth another.“

———-

In light of what we have discussed today, I want to remind you that in our ever-changing world, there is one Person who never changes. That person is Jesus Christ. The Bible says that He is “the same yesterday, today, and forever.” He is not subject to the whims of society or popular opinion. He has an unchanging, eternal love for you. If you do not know Him as your Savior, I encourage you to get to know Him today. John 3:16 says, “For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.” Just believe in your heart that Jesus Christ died for your sins, was buried, and rose from the dead by the power of God for you so that you can live eternally with Him. Pray and ask Him to come into your heart today, and He will. Romans 10:13 says, “For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.”
