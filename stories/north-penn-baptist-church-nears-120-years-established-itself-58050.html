North Penn Baptist Church Nears 120 Years; Established Itself as the First Philadelphia Baptist House of Worship

February 22, 2015 Black

Devotional service is conducted by the deacon’s ministry.

North Penn Baptist Church distinguishes itself as the first Philadelphia Baptist house of worship that built its sanctuary from the ground up during the 19th century. Besides being just shy of 120-years-old North Penn boasts of more than 250 “participating members,” according to its pastor, the Rev. Warren H. Marshall. Most live in the surrounding North Philadelphia community or once lived in walking distance of the edifice located at 2419 -23 N. 27th St.

Deacon John Cheeseborough has been a member for eight years. He is one of the younger members who is very involved in the life of the church. He is vice chair of the deacon’s board, oversees the music ministry, and is active with the youth ministry. He said that being busy and involved draws him closer to God.

“This is more than a church and even more than family,” Cheeseborough said. “It’s like being part of a large [extended] family that has a warm and homey spirit. We just care about each other, and we have a lot of fun.

“Our pastor is real. He is open with us, will drop what he is doing if you need something, and is willing to clear his plate to serve. You couldn’t ask for more of a pastor. You know that you can reach him when you need him,” Cheeseborough said.

Ann Taylor joined the church in May 1988 the same month and year that Marshall arrived. She initially visited the church with her parents at the invitation of her aunt and cousins who were already members. Though she had not had a home church in many years, it was the church’s hospitality and the pastor’s commitment that enticed her to join.

“When I first came I was a little nervous, but I soon felt right at home,” Taylor said. “Soon my father joined, then my mother, and now my husband has joined. This is because my pastor is amazing. He’s there for everybody giving what you need. Because he is so compassionate the church has that same giving spirit.”
