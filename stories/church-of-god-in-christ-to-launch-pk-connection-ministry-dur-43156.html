Church of God In Christ to Launch PK Connection Ministry During 107th Holy Convocation

November 2, 2014 Christian

Join us for the first ever sessions designed specifically for those who were raised by ministry workers. Connect, Grow, Change & Heal!!

Bishop Charles E. Blake Sr. & the Church of God In Christ invite you to join the Bishop Michael Hill Sr. in the first ever sessions specifically designed to be a a support group as well as offer healing to those who were raised by ministry workers.

In these first of their kind open sessions you can connect, grow, change and heal. Remember, He’s your God too. Don’t miss this life changing, life enhancing experience. This coming November 5-6 & 8 be a part of the PK Connection
