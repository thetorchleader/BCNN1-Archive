First A.M.E. Zion Church Host 95th Session of the Southwest Rocky Mountain Annual Conference

May 31, 2015 Black

Pastor Kelcy Steele

Pastor Kelcy Steele (above) and the members of First A.M.E. Zion Church will host the 95th session of the Southwest Rocky Mountain Annual Conference.

Bishop W. Darin Moore, presiding prelate of the Western Episcopal Area and Mrs. Devieta Moore, missionary supervisor, will convene the meeting from Tuesday, May 26, at 7 p.m., through Sunday, May 31, at First A.M.E. Zion, 1449 W. Adams Blvd. in Los Angeles.  Business sessions begin each day at 10 a.m. and evening worship starts at 7 p.m.

For information, call (323) 734-5370.
