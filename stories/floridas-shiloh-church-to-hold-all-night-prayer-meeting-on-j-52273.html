Florida’s Shiloh Church to Hold All-Night Prayer Meeting on January 16

January 14, 2015 Christian

Join us as we gather for a night of prayer. Pastor Charles will teach in Orange Park at 10 PM and Downtown at 12 midnight this Friday evening (1/16) during All-Night Prayer. Each member is asked to attend for at least one hour.

SOURCE: Shiloh Church
