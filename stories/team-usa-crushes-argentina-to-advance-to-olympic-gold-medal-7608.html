Team USA Crushes Argentina to Advance to Olympic Gold Medal Match

August 10, 2012 Uncategorized

Kobe Bryant in the first quarter. LeBron James in the second quarter. Kevin Durant in the third quarter, Carmelo Anthony in the fourth quarter. Ultimately, just too much Team USA on Friday for four quarters.


The scoring load broke down rather cleanly. Bryant had 11 of his 13 points in the first quarter, James nine of his18 points in the second, Durant 12 of his 19 points in the third, and Anthony 12 of his 18 in the fourth as the U.S. rolled to a 109-83 victory against Argentina in the semifinals.
The Americans will meet Spain for the gold medal on Sunday. The Spaniards, featuring Bryant’s L.A. Laker teammate Pau Gasol, trailed by 11 at the half before turning the game around in the final two quarters and defeating Russia 67-59 in Friday’s first semifinal. It will be a rematch of the 2008 Beijing Olympics final, which the U.S. won. The Americans have won 16 consecutive Olympic games.
