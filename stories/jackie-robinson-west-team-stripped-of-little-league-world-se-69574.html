Jackie Robinson West, Team Stripped of Little League World Series U.S. Title, Leaves Little League for Babe Ruth Organization

April 24, 2015 Black, Sports

The Jackie Robinson West baseball team announced it will leave Little League and next season compete in the Babe Ruth League. (Jose M. Osorio, Chicago Tribune)

Citing a “level of disrespect” shown to Jackie Robinson West, the championship team that became a national sensation has cut ties with Little League.

The team was stripped of its Little League World Series U.S. title in February after team leaders were accused of breaking rules to allow players from outside district boundaries to compete on the JRW championship squad. The South Side youth team will play this season in the Cal Ripken Division of the Babe Ruth League, according to a team spokesman.

JRW still maintains its charter with Little League but chose to play within another organization, the spokesman said.

“It’s largely based on the disregard and disrespect shown by Little League that had a 43-year-old relationship with Jackie Robinson West,” spokesman Glenn Harston said.
