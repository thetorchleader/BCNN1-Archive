Convicted Murdered Who Escaped Chicago Jail Captured

April 6, 2015 Black

© Courtesy of the Kankakee County Sheriff’s Office/AP Photo This undated photo provided by the Kankakee County Sheriff’s Office shows Kamron T. Taylor.

A three-day manhunt for a convicted killer ended Friday night when he was arrested by Chicago police after being identified by a distinctive neck tattoo, authorities said.

Kamron T. Taylor, 23, of Pembroke Township in Kankakee County, was arrested on Chicago’s Far South Side on Friday night after police, responding to a suspicious person call, chased him down on foot. Taylor had been on the lam since early Wednesday morning, when he broke out of a Kankakee jail where he was waiting to be sentenced for murder.

Taylor’s brazen escape – in which he choked and beat a corrections officer, then walked out wearing the officer’s uniform – was assisted with information from his aunt, a former guard at the jail, authorities said Saturday.

Though handcuffed and wearing the black-and-white striped Department of Corrections jumpsuit, Taylor swaggered into court Saturday appearing confident, looking back at the gallery several times as prosecutors laid out the charges.

In addition to a slew of felony charges awaiting him in Kankakee County – including attempted murder and escape – he’s now also facing unlawful use of weapon charges for allegedly carrying a loaded handgun when arrested Friday night.

Judge James R. Brown ordered that Taylor be held without bail and set his next court date for Tuesday.

If not for a tattoo on his neck that says “Gertrude,” Taylor might not have been connected to his Kankakee crimes.

“We had no idea, initially, who he was,” Chicago police Officer Oberrian Montilla told WGN-TV.

Montilla and Officer Matthew Kozlovski were on patrol in the Calumet Heights neighborhood about 9 p.m. Friday when they responded to a call of a suspicious person near the 9200 block of South Stony Island Avenue, according to Chicago police. As they approached Taylor, he began to run, officers said.

“He ran, and we gave chase and placed him in custody,” Montilla said.
