Enroll Alabama Is Working to Get Alabamians In the Healthcare Program by March 31st

March 19, 2014 Uncategorized

Brenda Godhigh is resting a little easier Tuesday night knowing she’s covered if a health emergency strikes.

“This was just a God send,” exclaimed Godhigh.

The Birmingham retiree has been without health insurance since 2012. The company she worked for pulled the plug on coverage for retirees under 65 and she’s been worried everyday she’s lived without it.

“Some problem comes up; no one knows when they’re going to need health insurance,” added Godhigh.

She called a local organization for help on understanding the new healthcare law. On Saturday, a member of the group ‘Enroll Alabama’ walked her through the steps of signing up through the new online marketplace.

“I asked him if he could come out to my church after services, and someone else in my church might have needed it,” explained Godhigh. She and fellow church goers are not the only ones taking note of an upcoming deadline.

“Offices are overwhelmed with calls and people requesting appointments,” explained Sonja Smith with the Enroll Alabama office in Birmingham. She says they were prepared for a swarm of calls leading up to March 31.
