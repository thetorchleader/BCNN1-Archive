Taraji Goes Off on People Being Upset About One of the Boys on the Show Calling President Obama a ‘Sell Out’ – “Everybody Don’t Like Barack Obama”

February 24, 2015 Black

–“Empire” star Taraji P. Henson responds to backlash stemming from the hit show’s second episode where her on-screen son calls President Obama a “sellout” during a scene. (Uptown Magazine)”I’m just proud that people are talking. Everybody don’t like Barack Obama. People are out there saying that. That’s what art is supposed to do: expose this s—. You know, we’re dealing with subject matter that’s not really dealt with. Art is supposed to challenge, start a conversation, so let’s lift the carpet up and deal with this dirt. It’s not like we bashed Barack Obama and the show went off. No, Lucious Lyon (Terrence Howard) jumped in his [son’s] ass. That’s real life stuff. So I’m glad you’re mad. Do something about it. Go in the hood and talk to the kids so they don’t do dumb stuff like that.”

Source: Black Voices
