IntelliDyne LLC Promotes Shirl Jenkins to Chief Human Resources Officer

June 23, 2014 Black

Shirl Jenkins

FALLS CHURCH, Va., June 17, 2014 /PRNewswire-iReach/ — IntelliDyne, LLC, a leading Enterprise Infrastructure Management IT consulting firm, is pleased to announce that Ms. Shirl Jenkins, its Senior Vice President, has been promoted to the position Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO) and Executive Vice President. As Chief Human Resources Officer, Ms. Jenkins will be responsible for the strategic development and senior management of IntelliDyne’s growing human resources organization, leading recruitment and retention and expanding its corporate culture as the company ramps up for significant anticipated growth in public sector Healthcare IT markets. With over twenty five years in the Human Resources field, Ms. Jenkins will continue to bring expertise in diversity, reward and recognition, talent acquisition management, and organizational and professional development to help IntelliDyne grow and thrive.

Tony Crescenzo, IntelliDyne’s CEO, stated “Shirl Jenkins is a tenacious corporate culture builder. Under her leadership, she has successfully initiated, developed and launched a series of the most significant human capital programs in our corporate history, resulting in IntelliDyne’s current success. Shirl has helped enhance our employee engagement, achieve higher employee retention and enabled the company to recruit top caliber talent. We are honored to recognize Ms. Jenkins for her proactive leadership role and her pivotal contributions to IntelliDyne’s growth.”

Since joining IntelliDyne in 2009, Ms. Jenkins has taken a leadership role in developing IntelliDyne into a recognized employer of choice. As a result of the programs Ms. Jenkins has established, IntelliDyne has earned unprecedented industry recognition in recent years, including 2014 Helios Apollo HR Award for Innovative Employee Development & Engagement Practices, 2014 Fairfax Chamber of Commerce Outstanding Corporate Citizen Award Finalist, 2014 Alliance for Workplace Excellence (AWE), 2014 AWE Health & Wellness Award, 2014 American Heart Association Fit Friendly Company and 2014 Washington Post Best Places to Work finalist.

Before joining IntelliDyne, Ms. Jenkins worked with SRA International for 18 years serving her last seven years as the Vice President of Human Resources. During her tenure at SRA, Ms. Jenkins won multiple internal and external awards and was widely recognized for her outstanding leadership efforts, including her role in positioning SRA for recognition as one of Fortune® magazine’s list of the “100 Best Companies to Work for in America” for 8 consecutive years.

About IntelliDyne, LLC

IntelliDyne, LLC is a consulting firm that enables better business performance through innovative technology solutions. We manage public sector programs that deliver higher operational efficiency and measurable value to clients. We advise, develop, and execute effective solutions in Information Assurance/Cyber Security, Cloud Computing, Application Development and Mobile Computing, Analytics, Business Process Management, Data Center Consolidation, Enterprise Collaboration and Enterprise Infrastructure Management.
