Leaked Unretouched Photos of Beyoncé Cause Outrage

February 19, 2015 Entertainment

© The Beyonce World/.
Unretouched photos of Beyoncé surface!

One Beyoncé fan site is feeling the sting of her rabid fans, the “Beyhive,” after it posted 224 unretouched photos of the superstar, showing her (gasp!) flawed.

The Internet was buzzing after the images were leaked by The Beyoncé World, a website that calls itself “the biggest Beyoncé fansite in the world.”

The images that showed the Queen Bey with less-than-absolutely-perfect-skin came from commercial and ad shoots for L’Oréal’s 2013 Feria and Infallible campaigns.

The evidence of skin inferiority didn’t last long, though, as the site pulled the images within about an hour, citing hate mail from the Beyhive.

“Due to the disdain of the BeyHive, we have removed the photos,” the owners of the website wrote. “We don’t want to cause any drama, nor do we wish to start fan wars. Some of the things we have seen posted were just horrible, and we don’t want any parts of it. We were just posting the photos to share the fact that our queen is naturally beautiful, at the same time she is just a regular woman.”

“A regular woman?” Beyoncé? Expect more hate mail in 3, 2, 1 …
