Wife of Actor Cuba Gooding Jr. Files for Separation After 20 Years of Marriage

April 23, 2014 Entertainment

Cuba Gooding Jr. holds up his Oscar award beside his wife Sarah following the 29th Academy Awards in 1997. On Tuesday, after 20 years of marriage, his wife reportedly filed for separation. (AP)

The Hollywood actor’s wife and former high school sweetheart, Sara Kapfer, filed for legal separation in Los Angeles Tuesday citing irreconcilable differences, TMZ reported.

It’s reportedly splitsville for Cuba Gooding Jr. and his wife of 20 years.

The Hollywood actor’s wife and former high school sweetheart, Sara Kapfer, filed for legal separation in Los Angeles Tuesday citing irreconcilable differences, TMZ reported.

The couple shares three children after their marriage in 1994.

Sadly, just last year the “Jerry Maguire” star publicly gushed about his marriage with his wife to the ladies of “The View” who reacted amazed by his 20-year dedication

“Are you the only one who’s still with someone by this time?” guest host, Suze Orman, asked.

“I’m lucky,” he explained of his ability to fall in love with her upon every new phase of their lives.

“Oh I wanna kill her sometimes,” he joked, “and she puts up with me.”
