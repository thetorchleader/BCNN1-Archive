“God’s Not Dead” Follow-Up, “Do You Believe?”, to Release March 20

January 14, 2015 Entertainment

When God’s Not Dead hit box offices in 2014, it set a precedent for Christian entertainment, allowing Hollywood to see there was value in the Christian dollar.

God’s Not Dead grossed more than $60 million, became one of the most popular Christian films of the year and gained a sequel.

Now it’s follow-up, Do You Believe? has released its trailer and set a release date: March 20.

According to its imbd.com profile, Do You Believe? takes viewers on not just a movie journey, but a life-long one.

“When a local pastor is shaken to the core by the visible faith of an old street-corner preacher, he is reminded that true belief always requires action,” the site says of the movie. “His response ignites a faith-fueled journey that powerfully impacts everyone it touches in ways that only God could orchestrate.”
