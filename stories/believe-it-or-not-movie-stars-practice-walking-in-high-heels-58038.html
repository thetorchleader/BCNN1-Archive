Believe It or Not, Movie Stars Practice Walking in High Heels to Avoid Embarrassing Falls on Oscar Night. Wow!

February 22, 2015 Entertainment

Actress Jennifer Aniston

It takes the crafty Hollywood acting veteran to know that Oscars rehearsal is 20 percent line reading, 80 percent shoe-testing.

That’s why Jennifer Aniston came to the Dolby Theatre Saturday in casual flip-flops and a small white bag containing the four-inch heels she will wear on Oscar Sunday.

Aniston slipped in her formal heels and tapped her feet on the hard Oscar floor with full concentration.

The heels did not match her casual pants and top. But no matter. There will be no fall like Jennifer Garner at Oscars 2006.

Even Hollywood newcomer Margot Robbie brought her massive Oscar show heels along and wore them on the stage on Saturday. This comes after extensive heel practice on Leonardo DiCaprio’s head.

She conducted herself without falling at rehearsals, even giving a prom queen wave as she took to the podium. Robbie then adorably finished her practice and actually sat on the Oscar stage steps to change back into her ankle socks and street shoes. Mission accomplished.

Even Oprah had her heels ready to go during rehearsals on Saturday night. However, she called a shoe audible, changed plans and neglected to put them on before taking the stage. That’s a fashion risk.

The only person who truly bucked the trend was the ever-glam Nicole Kidman, who took off her heels to walk on the Oscar floor in her bare feet, hidden under her flowing trouser legs.

Only after she stepped off the stage did she strap the heels back on, while happily chatting about dining options in Nashville.

Australians.
