Tony Dungy says Heisman Winner Marcus Mariota is the ‘Next Aaron Rodgers’

December 16, 2014 Sports

© Kelly Kline/Getty Images
Marcus Mariota has earned some high praise from Tony Dungy.

Marcus Mariota was already believed to be a future first-round pick entering the football season, but he has improved his stock and is even looking like a future No. 1 overall pick. In fact, former Super Bowl-winning coach turned analyst Tony Dungy says he believes Mariota is not only a great No. 1 pick, but also the next Aaron Rodgers.

Dungy was a guest on “The Dan Patrick Show” Monday and was asked for his opinion on the Oregon quarterback.

“I promise you this: Marcus Mariota is a fantastic player,” Dungy said. “He’s going to be a great pro. He can throw from the pocket. I watched him in practice, I watched him in training camp for three years when my son was at Oregon.”

Dungy’s son, Eric, attended Oregon before leaving for South Florida. He was a year ahead of Mariota, meaning the older Dungy saw the Heisman Trophy winner many times. He’s seen enough of Mariota to compare him to Rodgers, who is a Super Bowl MVP and three-time Pro Bowler.

“Not only is he going to be a great quarterback, he’s going to be a great person — face of the franchise — for whoever takes him. I think this is Aaron Rodgers in the waiting.”

One concern Patrick expressed over Mariota is the quarterback’s shyness. Patrick wondered whether the quarterback would be able to handle the media demands of the NFL. Dungy said that depends on what team drafts the QB.
