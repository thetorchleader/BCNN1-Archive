Lois Evans Shares Photo of Three Generations of the Evans Family

March 30, 2015 Christian

Lois Evans, wife of Dallas pastor and bestselling author, Tony Evans, shared a photo of three generations of the Evans family on Sunday night. The entire family had gathered in Waco for The Gathering — a coming-together of area churches for a night of worship and building bridges with the community. Dr. Evans was the main speaker at the event and his son, Anthony Evans, helped lead the singing.

Each of Dr. & Mrs. Evans’ four children are involved in Christian ministry and it is truly a blessing to see how God has used this family for His glory.
