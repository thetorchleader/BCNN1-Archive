Beyonce Joins Marco Borges to Launch Vegan Meal-Delivery Service

February 3, 2015 Black, God & Food

Beyoncé
ELSA/GETTY IMAGES

Want to know what Beyoncé eats on a daily basis? Want to have it delivered to your doorstep? Well, thanks to a partnership with her trainer, you can now enjoy vegan meals delivered right to your door.

Beyoncé and Marco Borges created 22 Days Nutrition, the name coming from the belief that it takes 21 days to break a bad habit. The meals range from $9.76 to $16.50 each, and are 100 percent plant-based, non-genetically modified, gluten-free, soy-free, dairy-free and organic. The meals can be delivered on a weekly basis, and ordered on the company’s site.

Beyoncé and Jay Z both adopted a vegan diet for 22 days, and according to the press release, still make the effort to have a more plant-based diet.

“All you have to do is try. If I can do it, anyone can,” Beyoncé said in the press release.

Some of the dishes included in 22 Days Nutrition are sesame-cabbage lentil bowl, ratatouille pasta with pesto, curried fried rice with vegetables and an almond-blueberry breakfast loaf.
