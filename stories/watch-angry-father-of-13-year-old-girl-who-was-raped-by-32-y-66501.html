WATCH: Angry Father of 13-Year-Old Girl Who Was Raped by 32-Year-Old Neighbor Destroys Perpetrator’s Car With Hammer

April 10, 2015 National

The father of a 13-year-old rape victim couldn’t get his hands on her alleged attacker, but apparently found his car instead.

Video taken outside the Pittsburgh home of Dustin Moffat showed an enraged dad violently jumping on a parked SUV and smashing the windows with a hammer, WTAE reported.

Moffat, 32, was arrested Tuesday after police say he filmed himself sexually assaulting the child and then posted the video online.

The sick attack, reportedly posted on the online gaming and chat site MocoSpace.com, was allegedly just one of several incidents involving the girl since 2011, police said.

The child reportedly told police that Moffat waited at her school bus stop for when she got home before forcing her into his nearby home.

She claimed Moffat would choke her, spit on her, stalk her and threaten her if she tried to resist him.

WTAE reported that two of Moffat’s previous girlfriends filed orders for protection against him under similar claims, one as recent as three-weeks ago. Those included stalking, spitting, physical violence and threats.

He now faces 29 sex-related charges, those being rape, sexual abuse of children, sexual exploitation of children, corruption of minors, statutory sexual assault, sexual assault, indecent assault, obscene and other sexual materials and performances, simple assault, stalking, unlawful contact with minor, involuntary deviated sexual intercourse, and aggravated indecent assault.

“Investigators believe there may be other victims and urge them to contact the Sex Assault/Family Crisis Unit at this time by calling 412-323-7141,” police said in a release.
