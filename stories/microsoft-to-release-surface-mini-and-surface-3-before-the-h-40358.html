Microsoft to Release Surface Mini and Surface 3 Before the Holidays

October 18, 2014 Technology

There are many tablets coming in the market this year especially from the tech giants (Apple, Samsung, Google) and now it is Microsoft’s turn. As we all know once the gadget is disclosed by the company the rumors about their release date continue until it is actually released. So same is the case with the release date of Surface Mini. The latest rumor says the Surface mini will be released along with the surface 3 before holidays.

Like Surface Pro, Surface Mini will also be coming with the digital pen Surface Mini comes with 8 inch display and is said to be powered by a Qualcomm processor. It also comes with 1 GB of RAM, and it will run Window RT 8.1. Surface Mini will also get a USB support just like laptop which is a amazing feature. Other than this the tablet will also support micro SD card.

As very less information is disclosed yet about Surface Mini. But according to the rumors it will cost somewhere around $450.

Microsoft has been talking about Surface Pro 3 but it did not talk much about Surface mini. Microsoft proudly described Surface Pro as “the tablet that can replace your laptop”. It will run on windows 8. And it is considered as the thinnest tablet from Microsoft.
