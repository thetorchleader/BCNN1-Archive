Please Pray for and Give Toward the Recovery of the Wife of Pastor Andrew Hamilton of Thomasville, GA

February 23, 2015 Christian

Pastor Andrew & Bessie Hamilton

The wife of Pastor Andrew Hamilton of the Berean Baptist Church in Thomasville, GA, suffered a stroke last month. She is currently recovering in the hospital.

Pastor Hamilton is a faithful church planter and evangelist who has planted churches across the South for many years. He and his wife, Bessie, have been married for 24 years.

Please pray for Bessie Hamilton’s complete healing. You can help the Hamilton family by giving financially as the Lord leads you as well. You can give online at http://www.gofundme.com/ljsmoo.

You can e-mail pastor Hamilton at born2@rose.net.

–BCNN1
