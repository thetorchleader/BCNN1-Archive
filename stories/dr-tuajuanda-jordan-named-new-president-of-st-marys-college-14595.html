Dr. Tuajuanda Jordan Named New President of St. Mary’s College of Maryland

March 20, 2014 Education

This undated handout photo provided by St. Mary’s College shows Tuajuanda Jordan. St. Mary’s College of Maryland has named Jordan, a dean and scientist from Lewis and Clark College, as the school’s next president. The St. Mary’s College board announced Jordan was unanimously approved as the school’s president after a four-month search. She will take office July 1. Photo/Steve Hambuchen, St. Mary’s College (AP Photo)

St. Mary’s College of Maryland has named a dean and scientist from Lewis and Clark College as the school’s next president.

The St. Mary’s College board announced Wednesday that Tuajuanda (ta-wan-da) Jordan was unanimously approved as the school’s president after a four-month search. She will take office July 1.

Previous president Joseph Urgo asked the board of trustees not to renew his contract last year.

Jordan is dean of the College of Arts and Sciences and professor of chemistry at Lewis and Clark College in Portland, Ore. Previously, Jordan was director of the Howard Hughes Medical Institute’s Science Education Alliance in Chevy Chase, Md.

The 53-year-old Jordan is a Maryland native from Forestville in Prince George’s County Maryland. She received her B.S. in Chemistry from Fisk University and a Ph. D. in biochemistry from Purdue University.

St. Mary’s College was designated Maryland’s state honors college in 1992. It enrolls more than 1,800 students.

Source: The AP
