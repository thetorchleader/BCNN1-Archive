16-Year-Old Preacher, Kory Logan, Hopes to Set Example for Youth With ‘Straight Talk’

September 3, 2014 Christian

(PHOTO: SCREENGRAB / WLWT CHANNEL 5 CINCINNATI)

Kory Logan said he could have easily accepted his calling at a younger age but he wasn’t ready to talk in front of a crowd.

Logan is 16 years old and like any other kid his age he enjoys sports, music, movies and hanging out with his friends. But this Lakota West High School junior’s main focus is on a higher power. Logan is a minister.

“Some know that I’m a preacher there, some don’t. For the ones that do know, sometimes (it is) challenging (and sometimes it’s) not, but most days it’s pretty cool,” Logan said.

Logan said he realized three years ago it was time to continue a family tradition. He is the fourth generation in a family of ministers.

“It was something I just kept praying about. God kept speaking to me about it. I announced my calling to preach and ever since then it’s just been going up for me so it’s been a blessing,” Logan said.

Logan was the keynote speaker at Youth Day at Lincoln Heights Missionary Baptist Church.

He said he understands the pressure teenagers are under …. That’s why his mission is to make a difference for youth by setting an example with straight talk.

“All we can do for our generation is pray for them. Sooner or later some will turn around; some might not but hopefully they will and see they have a bright future ahead of them if they just keep their head on straight,” said Logan.
