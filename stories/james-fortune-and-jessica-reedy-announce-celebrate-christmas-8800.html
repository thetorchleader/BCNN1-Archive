James Fortune and Jessica Reedy Announce ‘Celebrate Christmas’ Tour

October 19, 2012 Uncategorized

James Fortune and Jessica Reedy have announced their ‘Celebrate Christmas’ tour starting November 26th.
The tour will begin in Alexandria, VA on November 26th at The Birchmere, and will continue to New York at BB Kings and in Baltimore at Soundstage on November 27th and 28th, respectively. More tour dates will be announced soon.
Fans can expect to hear some of their favorites from Fortune and Reedy’s current releases along with some of the holiday favorites. Fans will also have a chance to hear songs from Fortune and FIYA ‘s brand  new Christmas album, Grace Gift, released on October 16th.
