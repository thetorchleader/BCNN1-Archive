First Ladies Health Day at World Changers Ministries in Los Angeles Sets a New Record

April 26, 2015 Black

First Lady Sherita Middleton of World Changers Ministries in Los Angeles has her blood pressure screened by Walgreens pharmacist Caitlyn Nguyen during the Fourth Annual First Ladies Health Day of Los Angeles Sunday.

First Lady Sherita Middleton of World Changers Ministries in Los Angeles has her blood pressure screened by Walgreens pharmacist Caitlyn Nguyen during the Fourth Annual First Ladies Health Day of Los Angeles Sunday.  Medical experts set a new record of providing more than 15,500 free medical tests in 30 participating churches during the Walgreens-sponsored event.

The health day premieres in Orange County on Sunday, April 26 in four churches. The are Second Baptist Church, 4300 Westminster Avenue, Santa Ana; Christ Our Redeemer AME Church, 45 Tesla, Irvine; Friendship Baptist Church, 17145 Bastanchury Road, Yorba Linda; and Johnson Chapel AME Church, 1306 West 2nd Street, Santa Ana.  Congregants from Reach Community Church and Temple Builders Community Church, also located in Orange County, are asked to visit Christ Our Redeemer for their free health tests.
