How Tim Tebow Can Save the Jets

September 25, 2012 Uncategorized

New York Jets cornerback Darrelle Revis suffered an injury to his left knee on Sunday, and an MRI revealed he tore his anterior cruciate ligament. New York is in trouble.
But one solution has risen above the others on Twitter. You can find it with this search: “Tebow Cornerback.”
Yes, Tim Tebow will have to save the Jets. Here are five (totally reasonable and not silly at all) ways Tebow can fix things in New York:
1. Say hello to Tim Tebow, shutdown cornerback.
Tebow has already proved this week he can deflect a pass headed in his general direction. (VIDEO)
2. Jets *always* run fake punt with Tebow
New York ran Tim Tebow for a fake-punt first down on Sunday and it worked because he is Tim Tebow. Tim Tebow!
This strategy could pay off BIG with the Jets never again having to play defense. And New York won’t miss Revis much if it never has to play defense.
