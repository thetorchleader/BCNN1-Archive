Joe Posnanski’s “Paterno” Edges Out Dinesh D’Souza to Debut at Number One

August 30, 2012 Uncategorized

Joe Posnanski’s Paterno sold 11,439 copies in its first week, good enough to place it #1 on the adult nonfiction bestseller list, edging the #2 book, Obama’s America by Dinesh D’Souza, by just over 300 copies. On the overall adult bestseller list across all titles, Paterno landed at #15. The numbers are according to Nielsen BookScan.
Regionally, areas of Pennsylvania saw the highest sales, with five of the top six areas coming from the state. The top sales areas were: Philadelphia, Harrisburg-York-Lancaster-Lebanon, Johnstown-Altoona, New York, Wilkes-Barre-Scranton, and Pittsburgh.
