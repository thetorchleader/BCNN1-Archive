Mike Shanahan Most Interested in Coaching 49ers, Working with Colin Kaepernick

January 5, 2015 Sports

© Mike Stobe/Getty Images
Mike Shanahan is most interested in working with the 49ers and coaching quarterback Colin Kaepernick.

Former NFL head coach Mike Shanahan has multiple suitors around the league, but one report from Bleacher Report’s Jason Cole indicates that he’s most interested in working with Colin Kaepernick as the San Francisco 49ers head coach.

The NFL insider indicates that Shanahan is willing to listen to the Buffalo Bills pitch, but multiple sources surrounding the veteran head coach are saying that’s meant as leverage against the 49ers, who interviewed Shanahan on New Year’s Day.

Shanahan believes that San Francisco gives him the best chance of winning immediately with the talent already on its roster, including Kaepernick.

If this report is correct, it could signal that a marriage between Shanahan and the 49ers is inevitable. We reported on Saturday that 49ers CEO Jed York had Shanahan high on the team’s list of potential head coaches to replace Jim Harbaugh.
