Rosie O’Donnell’s Lesbian Divorce Turns Ugly

April 30, 2015 Entertainment

Rosie O’Donnell and Michelle Rounds in happier times – 2014.
(Photo: Noam Galai, Getty Images)

Rosie O’Donnell split with wife Michelle Rounds in February.

Now, their divorce is getting contentious. Rounds filed for sole custody of Dakota, 2, the baby girl they adopted together, in New York on Tuesday.

O’Donnell’s ex isn’t happy with how the girl is being raised while with the former View host. Rounds tells Entertainment Tonight that Dakota would live in a “healthier” and “way more protective” environment if she had sole custody.

Rounds says, “I just want my child to have the best possible environment to grow up in, a normal life. Right now she’s got 24/7 nannies over there at Rosie’s house. I don’t want my child to have a 24/7 nanny.”

Rounds, a former IT consultant, has said their different parenting styles was a problem in their relationship.

“I give her mild discipline instead of like — carte blanche, do whatever you want — which is more or less how Rosie likes to do things,” she said.

TMZ says Rounds is making even harsher claims about O’Donnell.

Rounds also says it’s not about money. “I will walk away from the pre-nup if Rosie will give me full custody.”

Rosie’s rep told ET: “This is an absurd and desperate attempt to use a child for her own gain.”
