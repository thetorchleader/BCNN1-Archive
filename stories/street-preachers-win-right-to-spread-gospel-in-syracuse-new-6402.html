Street Preachers Win Right to Spread Gospel in Syracuse, New York

May 30, 2012 Uncategorized

With a federal court’s intervention, street preachers have won the right to spread the gospel in Syracuse, New York.

Michael Marcavage of Repent America is one of the two evangelists who filed suit “because of [the city’s] continued interference with our evangelistic affair.” He says U.S. District Court Judge Lawrence E. Kahn issued an emergency injunction, saying the street preachers would likely prevail if the case were to go forward.
“In fact, the city barred us from even handing out gospel tracts without a permit and threatened to arrest us if we did not obtain one,” he details. That demand reportedly aligns with the city’s “sound reproduction” ordinance.
The legal challenge follows an incident that began April 9, when police threatened Mike Stockwell of Cross Country Evangelism for his amplified free speech near the Centro bus hub in downtown Syracuse. But Marcavage says the judge also believes that violated the preacher’s rights.
