‘Happy’ Songwriter, Pharrell Williams, said he Waited on his Wife to Break Up with Someone Else for Nearly 3 Years

May 25, 2014 Entertainment

Singer/songwriter/producer Pharrell Williams has been gracing television screens across the world performing his hit single titled “Happy.”

The 41-year-old recently sat down with Oprah Winfrey to discuss his life, his career, and his newfound family.

On the conversation about his wife, model Helen Lasichanh, the talented artist admits that he waited two-and-a-half years for her to end a relationship with someone else before they began their relationship.

“She didn’t answer half of my text messages [at first],” Williams told Oprah. When she asked him if Lasichanh’s refusal of entertaining him while he was pursuing her intrigued him, the singer replied, “Egotistically, no. I was like, ‘Oh yeah?’ because I had that kind of money and because I had that kind of reach, I thought I was entitled and I had learned that no, she has a boyfriend and she’s not interested.”
