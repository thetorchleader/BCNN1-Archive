Terrell Owens Is Facing Foreclosure on One of the Last Houses he Owns

May 20, 2012 Uncategorized

Another tale of woe for Terrell Owens. 

Just days after facing the ire of his baby mamas on “Dr. Phil,” the former NFL star is now in jeopardy of losing his Miami beach condo — one of the last homes he still owns after being forced to sell off several others.
According to TMZ, Owens allegedly owes over $1 million on the property, and now, the bank wants to foreclose.
Deutsche National Bank sued him in April, claiming he still owes $1.47 million on the property and hasn’t made a payment in 6 months.
