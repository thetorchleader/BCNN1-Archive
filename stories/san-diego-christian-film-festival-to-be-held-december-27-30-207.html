San Diego Christian Film Festival to Be Held December 27-30

September 23, 2011 Uncategorized

The San Diego Christian Film Festival has announced that La Jolla has become the secured venue for the 2011 event. The second annual film festival is set for Dec. 27-30.

“We have outgrown last year’s venue,” said Richard Bagdazian, CEO of Glass Sea Productions which produces the festival told the ASSIST News Service. “We are expecting this year’s event to be bigger and better than last year. With almost 200 films having been submitted to this year’s festival, there is sure to be something for everyone in this year’s film lineup.”

The festival which features the best in contemporary Christian film from both new and established producers will be held at the La Jolla Marriott, located at 4240 La Jolla Village Drive, San Diego, CA. 92037
