BlackChristianNews.com Calls On Herman Cain to Step Down Now: Here Are Three Reasons Why:

And in our opinion Gingrich and Romney Ought to Follow Close Behind
Herman Cain says his wife, Gloria, didn’t know about his friendship with Ginger White until Monday when the Atlanta businesswoman came forward alleging they had a long-running affair.
The GOP presidential candidate also acknowledged in an interview today with the Union Leader that abandoning his White House bid “is an option” that he is considering as he reassesses his campaign. A decision is expected next week, after he sits down face-to-face with his wife.
Cain expanded on his relationship with White in his long-awaited session with the New Hampshire newspaper, which holds sway with conservatives. The former Godfather’s Pizza CEO said he gave financial assistance to White without the knowledge of his wife, to whom he’s been married 43 years.
Gloria Cain “did not know that we were friends until (White) came out with this story,” Herman Cain said. He admitted he should have told her about the friendship sooner.
Cain has denied having a physical relationship with White, who first told her story Monday to WAGA-TV in Atlanta.
