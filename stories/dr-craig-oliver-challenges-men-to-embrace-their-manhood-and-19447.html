Dr. Craig Oliver Challenges Men to Embrace Their Manhood and Become Men of God at “Maximizing Men” Conference in Atlanta

April 24, 2014 Christian

The 2014 Maximizing Men Conference kicked off at The House of Hope (Greater Travelers Rest Baptist Church) in Atlanta tonight. 

Dr. Craig Oliver delivered a dynamic message on what it means to be a man, to embrace manhood, and to become a man of God. He minced no words in defining manliness according to the Bible and declaring that God’s Word does not change to fit society. Dr. Oliver said that a man was meant to be with a woman as defined in Scripture.

The Maximizing Men Conference runs through Saturday, April 26, 2014. You can watch the conference live at BCNN3.TV. Find out more about the conference and view the conference schedule at maximumexp.org.

The Maximizing Men Conference is designed to equip men with fundamental resources to help them live maximized lives of Christian manhood. The 21st Century man is often exposed to harsh criticism, societal contempt, and holistic condemnation. This conference is an invitation for men to become maximized in their finances, future, families, and in relationships with females.

Led by Dr. E. Dewey Smith, Jr. as the conference host, Maximizing Men aims to expose men to some of the most influential and authentic leaders in the country. Every man is welcome! Come to the place where “manly” struggles are understood and we offer an opportunity for “real” men to discuss “real” issues while developing “real” faith for the “real” world. Come get maximized!
