Pastor John P. Kee Mourns Loss of Singing Partner’s Mother

March 9, 2015 Entertainment

GETTY

Pastor John P. Kee is mourning with one of his longtime singers who lost her mother.

His heart is aching for Sheila Lakin, whose voice has been heard on many of Kee’s musical compositions, most notably, some of his earlier work, like “He Is Able,” “I’ll Be Your Everything,” “I Shall Do,” and “The Anointing.”

“This was kinda rough tonight!” said Pastor Kee online after learning of the death of Sheila’s mom, Pastor Julia Lakin, Sunday evening, March 8.

“Pray For Me…. My Mommy transitioned tonight and I really need prayer,” wrote a broken-up Sheila on Instagram. “Rest In Jesus arms Mommy!!!!!! I Love You !!!!”
