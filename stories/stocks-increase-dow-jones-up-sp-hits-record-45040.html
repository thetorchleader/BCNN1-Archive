Stocks Increase, Dow Jones Up, S&P Hits Record

November 13, 2014 Finances

Trader William McInerney works on the floor of the New York Stock Exchange.
(Photo: Richard Drew, AP)

Stocks rose at the open Thursday, sending major indexes back into record territory on corporate merger news and Walmart earnings.

The Dow Jones industrial average was up 0.3% and hit a new intraday record high of 17,676.33.

The Dow was led higher by Walmart (WMT) after the nation’s largest retailer reported third-quarter earnings that beat Wall Street expectations. Walmart shares jumped more than 3%.

The Standard & Poor’s 500 index gained 0.2% and hit an all-time high of 2044.34. The Nasdaq composite index gained 0.5%.
