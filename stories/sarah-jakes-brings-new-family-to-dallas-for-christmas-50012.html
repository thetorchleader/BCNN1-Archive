Sarah Jakes Brings New Family to Dallas for Christmas

December 25, 2014 Christian

Instagram

After their whirlwind romance led to a November wedding, happy newlyweds Sarah Jakes and Touré Roberts are spending time in Dallas this Christmas.

On Instagram, the new wife and youngest daughter of Bishop T.D. Jakes posted a fun snap of the whole crew—including her parents—grinning widely.

With a simple caption that said, “The Fam Bam,” a photo full of smiles and children (lots of those!) perfectly captured the joyful holiday spirit.

“Brought a king to Dallas,” the 25-year-old author and speaker captioned another photo sans the rest of the group.

As the missus prepares to spend her first Christmas as a preacher’s wife, she is sharing her newfound happiness with her thousands of online followers.
