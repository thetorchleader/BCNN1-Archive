Simple Ways to Make Your Desktop Delightful

April 27, 2015 From Around the Block

© Courtesy Mackenzie Horan

10 To-Dos For A Delightful Desktop

1. First things first: upgrade the essentials. Whether you work from home or you’re eyeing the corner office from your cubicle, upgrading your desk chair is an easy way to inject style into your work space. There are plenty of chic rolling options but I love my bow-bedecked Parsons chair from Society Social for a little feminine flair.

2. Consider adding a lamp. Just because you can’t change the fluorescent lighting on your floor doesn’t mean you can’t take matters into your own hands. A colorful desk lamp or even a traditional table lamp is a great way to add less harsh lighting and make a chic statement.

3. Create a place for everything and put everything in its place. Need to change into heels when you get to the office? Dedicate a bottom desk drawer to storing shoes or place a small shoe rack beneath your desk. Work in PR and like to keep your recent press hits on hand? Pick up a few magazine files and organize them by date. The more strategic you are about storage, the more likely you are to stick with a system that works.

4. Ditch the idea of a junk drawer. Stock up on drawer dividers from the Container Store to corral paper clips, scissors, and staples in an orderly fashion. I swear by acrylic storage options so I can see everything at a glance.

5. Style your supplies. For supplies you keep in plain sight, don’t just stick them in an old coffee mug next to your computer. I keep two dozen periwinkle Le Pens in a monogrammed pencil cup on my desk at all times. Using a signature color keeps me from hoarding unnecessary pens and pencils and having a cute pen holder leaves me no excuse not to put them away.
