Rev. Jesse L. Jackson to Be Keynote Speaker at NBC’s Congress of Christian Education

March 20, 2014 Christian

Rev. Jesse L. Jackson, Sr. is scheduled to be the Congress President’s Education Banquet Keynote Speaker at the 109th Annual Session to be held in Dallas, TX, June 16-20, 2014.

Rev. Jackson is the Founder and President of The Rainbow PUSH Coalition and long time civil rights activist.

Wednesday, June 18, 2014
6:00 P.M.
Sheraton Dallas Hotel
400 N. Olive Street
Lone Star Ballroom A2-3
Dallas, Texas 75201
