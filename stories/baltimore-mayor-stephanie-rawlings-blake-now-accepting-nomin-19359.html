Baltimore Mayor Stephanie Rawlings-Blake Now Accepting Nominations for “Baltimore’s Top Neighborhood Dads”

April 24, 2014 African-American

Baltimore Mayor Stephanie Rawlings-Blake

Mayor Stephanie Rawlings-Blake is accepting nominations for the 8th Annual “Baltimore’s Top Neighborhood Dads” Contest. Each year the award honors dedicated men who have given selflessly to keep Baltimore’s neighborhoods Better, Safer and Stronger.  Nominees must be submitted from recognized neighborhood associations.

Download and fill out the nomination form(PDF File)

Mail your nomination form to:

Baltimore’s Top Neighborhood Dads
Office of Mayor Stephanie Rawlings-Blake
Room 250, City Hall
100 North Holliday Street
Baltimore, Maryland 21202

Nomination forms MUST BE RECEIVED no later than FRIDAY, MAY 16, 2014. A selection committee will review all of the nominations and determine Baltimore’s Top Neighborhood Dads for 2014.
