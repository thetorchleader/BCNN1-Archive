Greater Mount Olive Baptist Church Announces Spring Revival Plans

May 17, 2015 Black

Rev. Clinton McFarland

Spiritual renewal and refreshing is promised at the Spring Revival sponsored by Greater Mount Olive Baptist Church, located at 659 West Colden Avenue in Los Angeles.  

The three-night worship begins Wednesday, May 27, through Friday, May 29, at 7:30 p.m.  The revivalist is the Rev. Clinton McFarland, pastor of Grace Baptist Church in Atlanta, GA.

According to Greater Mt. Olive Pastor C. Lamar Simmons, “This is one revival you don’t want to miss! Pastor McFarland is an exciting Gospel preacher, pastor, and vocalist who ministers to thousands across the country.  Also, he is  a member of the renowned quartet, ‘The Christianaires.’
