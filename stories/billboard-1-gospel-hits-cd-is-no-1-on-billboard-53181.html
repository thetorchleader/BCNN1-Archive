‘Billboard #1 Gospel Hits’ CD Is No. 1 on Billboard

January 22, 2015 Entertainment

GETTY

Billboard has topped itself this week.

Fans rushed to get Billboard #1 Gospel Hits CD, a compilation of exclusively chart-topping gospel songs from throughout the decade.

The release from eOne Music is a double-disc collection that features 20 tracks from gospel heavyweights like Kirk Franklin, Hezekiah Walker, Marvin Sapp, Tamela Mann, Israel & New Breed and more.

Phil Thornton, vice president and general manager of urban inspirational at eOne Music said, “At eOne Music, we are thrilled to team up with Billboard to release a dynamic collection from artists who have led the sounds of gospel in this decade thus far.”

He added, “Billboard continues to be one of music’s preeminent destinations for fans to turn to, as an industry leader who bridges the fan experience to today’s best hits and what’s next.”
