Police Are Looking for Kevin Gates After Shooting at Concert

January 28, 2015 Entertainment

Kevin Gates (Courtesy of the Artist)

Kentucky police are looking for Kevin Gates after a shooting reportedly occurred at his Lexington show on Sunday, Jan. 25.

Police were called to Gates’ show after shots were fired, according to TMZ. One concertgoer suffered a “non-life threatening wound” while others were hurt when the crowd ran towards an exit.

Oddly enough, Gates continued to perform despite the shooting. Police want to question Gates on whether he saw anything considering he was on stage at the time.

A representative for Gates did not respond to Billboard’s request to comment at time of press.

SOURCE: Erika Ramirez
Billboard
