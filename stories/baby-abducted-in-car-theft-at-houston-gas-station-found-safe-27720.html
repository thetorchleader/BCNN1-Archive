Baby Abducted in Car Theft at Houston Gas Station Found Safe

June 23, 2014 National

Law enforcement officers at the scene where Genesis Haley was found safe. The baby was in the car at a Texaco station at 1110 North Sam Houston Parkway West when the car was stolen Monday morning. (Photo by Cody Duty/Houston Chronicle)

Houston police said the baby has been found safe. More details will be reported when they become available.

Authorities are searching for a missing 8-month-old girl who was abducted Monday morning when a thief stole her mother’s car at a gasoline station in north Houston.

Genesis Haley was in the car at a Texaco station at 1110 North Sam Houston Parkway West when it was stolen, officials said. The green 4-door Saturn Ion was later found abandoned, but the baby was not inside and so far has not been found.

Genesis has sandy brown hair. She was last seen wearing a white T-shirt and white pants. She had four barrettes in her hair.

Anyone with information about the case is urged to contact the Houston Police Department at 713-308-3600.
