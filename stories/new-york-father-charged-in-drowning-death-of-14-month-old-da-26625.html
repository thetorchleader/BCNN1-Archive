New York Father Charged in Drowning Death of 14-Month-Old Daughter

June 15, 2014 Black

MICHAEL SCHWARTZ/FOR NEW YORK DAILY NEWS
Dan Castellano has been charged with manslaughter in the drowning death of his 14-month-old daughter.

Dan Castillano, 23, was charged in the death of his daughter after he left the water running in the bathtub. The girl’s 3-year-old brother was also in the tub but unharmed. Castillano was released without bail after being arraigned for manslaughter Saturday.

The father of a 14-month-old Bronx girl who died after he allegedly left her unattended in a running bathtub was released without bail after being arraigned for manslaughter Saturday.

Dan Castillano, 23, left little Leslie Castillano in a bathtub with her 3-year-old brother on May 7 while he washed dishes in their Selwyn Ave. apartment, prosecutors said.

The tub overflowed with the children inside and flooded the apartment below.

He only realized that his child was in danger when a downstairs neighbor complained that water was leaking into her apartment.

“It looked like a waterfall,” downstairs neighbor Daisy Moran told the Daily News at the time.

Moran’s son and the building super knocked on Castillano’s door, but he was unaware that anything was wrong — until he went into the bathroom.

“He said, ‘Let me go check,’ ” Moran recalled. “That’s when he screamed: ‘The baby! The baby!’
