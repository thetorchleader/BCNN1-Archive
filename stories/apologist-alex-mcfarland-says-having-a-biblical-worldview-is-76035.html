Apologist Alex McFarland Says Having a Biblical Worldview Is Key to Solving Racial Tensions

May 27, 2015 Christian

Alex McFarland

An expert on religion and culture says a recent poll on race relations proves America needs to return to the Bible-based values and morals on which it was based.

The recent CBS/New York Times poll shows that 61 percent of Americans believe racial harmony is worse than it was 20 years ago. The poll also marks the first time since 1997 that majorities of both white and black respondents believe race relations are poor.

Dr. Alex McFarland, co-host of American Family Radio’s “Exploring the Word,” attributes this racial disharmony to a couple of factors, but foremost to the breakdown of the family.

“[There’s the] young people, especially young males, who really don’t grow up under the love, stability, and accountability of a mother and father,” he notes. “There’s also the abandonment of a biblical worldview – a worldview that says people are made in the image of God and all people have worth and value and dignity because they’re made in God’s image.”
