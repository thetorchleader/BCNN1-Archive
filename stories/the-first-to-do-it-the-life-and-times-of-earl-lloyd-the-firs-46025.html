‘The First to Do It – The Life and Times of Earl Lloyd, the first African-American in the NBA’ Documentary Set For Spring Release

November 20, 2014 Black, Sports

Earl Lloyd

A documentary about Earl Lloyd, who became the first African-American to play in the NBA way back in 1950, is supposed to be released in the spring although producers are still raising funds for it through Fanbacked.

Entitled, “The First to Do It – The Life and Times of Earl Lloyd, the first African-American in the NBA”, the film chronicles what led to Lloyd’s debut for the now-defunct Washington Capitols on Halloween night in a stadium outside of Syracuse, N.Y., in front of a crowd of only 2,500.

Lloyd would eventually become the NBA’s first full-time head coach, the NBA’s first African-American to receive an equipment sponsorship and the first African-American to win a NBA title.
