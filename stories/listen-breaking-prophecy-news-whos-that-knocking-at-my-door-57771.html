LISTEN: Breaking Prophecy News; Who’s That Knocking at My Door? Part 1 (The Prophet Daniel’s Report #526 with Daniel Whyte III)

February 20, 2015 Christian, Second Coming Watch

Daniel Whyte III

PROPHECY HEADLINES: British defense secretary says Russia is just as much a threat to Europe as ISIS is | Death toll from swine flu in India rises to 191 in last 2 months | Ukraine calls for European peacekeepers to enforce ceasefire after rebels seize key town | China to build Turkey’s new missile defense system which won’t be integrated with NATO’s | Indian military building stealth frigates and nuclear submarines

The Bible says in Hebrews 9:28: “So Christ was once offered to bear the sins of many; and unto them that look for him shall he appear the second time without sin unto salvation.”

PROPHECY BOOTCAMP: Our topic for today is titled “Who’s That Knocking at My Door?” (Part 1) from Dr. Ed Hindson’s book “Revelation: Unlocking the Future”. // SARDIS: THE POWERLESS CHURCH (PART 1)

“Have you ever attended a dead church? There is nothing worse than dead orthodoxy, dead traditionalism, or dead liberalism. The service is dull, lifeless, empty, and irrelevant. Everyone is bored and nodding off to sleep. No wonder our Lord said, “Wake up!” Sardis was a church that was ready to die. The light of the gospel was about to be extinguished. The power of the Spirit was about to fade. What is worse is that few people seemed to care…”

OCCUPY TILL I COME: “Waiting on the Second Coming — The Fire Next Time (Part 3)” by Ray C. Stedman

MUSICAL SELECTION: “Soon and Very Soon” by Andrae Crouch
