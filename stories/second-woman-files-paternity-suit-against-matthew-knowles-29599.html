Second Woman Files Paternity Suit Against Matthew Knowles

Date not recorded in the original archive Entertainment

Could Beyonce and Solange have another sibling out there? A woman in Texas says yes. Buzzfeed.com reports that TaQuoya Branscomb, a lingerie model turned real estate agent has filed papers in Texas saying that Matthew Knowles fathered her daughter, born in 2010.

Knowles, 63, already has had a child out of wedlock with Alexsandra Wright.

Knowles acknowledged his son, Nixon, and was ordered to pay child support of $12,000 a month, but claimed that his income was severely reduced when he was let go as his daughter’s manager. Not only were the payments reduced from $12,000 to $2485 a month, a judge ruled this year that Knowles had overpaid $110,000.
