Jada Pinkett-Smith Doesn’t See Anything Wrong with Willow’s Photo on a Bed with a Shirtless Man and Thinks People Who Do Are ‘Covert Pedophiles’

May 8, 2014 Entertainment

Jada Pinkett-Smith and daughter, Willow, at the New York City premiere of After Earth on May 29, 2013.
JAMIE MCCARTHY/GETTY IMAGES FOR MERCEDES-BENZ

Jada Pinkett-Smith is weighing in on the controversy set off by a photo of her 13-year-old daughter, Willow, and her daughter’s 20-year-old friend, Moises Arias. The questionable photo was posted on Arias’ Instagram page, but was quickly deleted after it became the subject of negative comments. The photo showed Willow lying on a bed with a shirtless Arias.

Everyone had their fair share of comments Wednesday in regards to the photo. Many felt it was inappropriate for a 13-year-old to even be friends with a 20-year-old, let alone appear in what some saw as a suggestive photo. Others felt that the context in which the photo was taken was missing and pointed out that obviously someone else was in the room with the two.
