7 Shot, 6 Dead In Houston-Area Shooting; SWAT Standoff Underway With Suspect

Date not recorded in the original archive National

Seven people were shot, 6 fatally, in north Harris County on Wednesday, according to Harris County Pct. 4 deputies.

Seven people were shot, 6 fatally, in north Harris County on Wednesday, according to Harris County Pct. 4 deputies.

The incident happened at a home in the 700 block of Leaflet in the Enchanted Oaks subdivision.

Deputies responded to a shooting and found the victims. Life Flight was called to the scene.

According to a KHOU source, the victims were ages five to 40. Six victims died.

The shooter led police on a chase that ended just a few miles from the shooting incident in another neighborhood.

Watch KHOU 11 News at 10 p.m. for a live update.

SOURCE: KHOU
