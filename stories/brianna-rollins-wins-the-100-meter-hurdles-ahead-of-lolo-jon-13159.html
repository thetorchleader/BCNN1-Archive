Brianna Rollins Wins the 100-Meter Hurdles Ahead of Lolo Jones

June 23, 2013 Uncategorized

While a beaming Brianna Rollins basked in her accomplishment, Lolo Jones slipped out of Drake Stadium.
Rollins closed Saturday’s session of the U.S. Track & Field Championships with a victory in the women’s 100-meter hurdles, setting an American record of 12.26 seconds in the process.
“I was just so overwhelmed and so emotional,” Rollins said. “It’s just so amazing.”
For Jones, a Des Moines native, it was something entirely different.
She placed fifth in 12.55 seconds, then broke event protocol by failing to exit through a media mix zone.
When an official caught up with her, Jones declined to meet with reporters. Jones has been surrounded by controversy all week since doing a on-line video where she complained about the $741 check she received for competing with the USA bobsled team last season. She is trying to qualify for the 2014 Winter Olympics in Sochi, Russia..
She told USA TODAY Sports when she arrived in Des Moines that she tries “to make people laugh or inspire them.” Some bobsled teammates were not laughing, and criticized her. Others offered some support.
Jones finished fourth in the 100 hurdles in London and lost the gold medal when she hit the next-to-last hurdle in Beijing.
Rollins, meanwhile, was asked if she could chase down the world record of 12.21 — just .05 offer her Saturday pace.
“I don’t know, we’ll see,” Rollins said. “We’ll see what God has planned for me.”
Thus ended a sun-soaked and bittersweet afternoon attended by 8,638 spectators.
