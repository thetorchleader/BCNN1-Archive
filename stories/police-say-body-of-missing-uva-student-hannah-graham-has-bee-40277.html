Police say Body of Missing UVA Student, Hannah Graham, Has Been Found

October 18, 2014 National

This undated photo provided by the Charlottesville, Va. police of Hannah Graham.
(Photo: AP)

Police believe they have found the body of missing University of Virginia student Hannah Graham.

Charlottesville Police Chief Timothy J. Longo announced at a press conference Saturday that human remains were found in an abandoned property around noon on Saturday. The body has not been formerly identified as Graham.

“Countless hours, thousands of hours have been spent by law enforcement and volunteers in an effort to find Hannah,” said Longo. “We think they have proved their worth.”

The announcement was made by the joint task forces investigating the student’s disappearance which has made national headlines.
