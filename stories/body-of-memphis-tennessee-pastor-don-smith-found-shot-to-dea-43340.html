Body of Memphis, Tennessee, Pastor Don Smith, Found Shot to Death

November 3, 2014 Christian

(PHOTO: FACEBOOK/PROSPECTPARKBAPTISTCHURCH)
Pastor Don Smith of Prospect Park Baptist Church in Memphis, Tennessee.

The body that was found Saturday morning in the 3400 block of Lichterman Street has been identified as 57-year-old Don Smith.

The Shelby County Medical Examiner’s office said the Smith died from a gunshot wound.

Police say two men walking their dogs found the body near a pond Saturday morning.

Smith was a Pastor at Prospect Park Baptist Church in Whitehaven.

His family tells WREG, they have no idea who would want to do this to the man but say he was a good Pastor and husband.

At this time, no arrest has been made.

Investigators are asking anyone with information to call CrimeStoppers at 528-CASH.
