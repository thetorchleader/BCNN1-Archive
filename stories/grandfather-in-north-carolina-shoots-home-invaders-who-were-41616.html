Grandfather in North Carolina Shoots Home Invaders Who Were Trying to Rape His Teen Granddaughter

October 25, 2014 Black

Jamie Lee Faison
FACEBOOK

One of the suspects died after the 67-year-old grandfather fired multiple times. A North Carolina grandfather suffered gunshot wounds after firing back at three home invaders—and possibly killing one of them, News 2 reports. The suspects also reportedly tried to rape his teenage granddaughter.

According to reports by the Robeson County Sheriff’s Office, the incident began on Monday around 10 p.m. when someone knocked on the door of the Lumberton home of the 67-year-old man, his wife and 19-year-old granddaughter.

Two of the three suspects, who were reportedly wearing black clothes, ski masks and gloves, pushed into the home, demanding money. The grandfather and his wife were ordered at gunpoint to open a safe. Meanwhile, all three armed men allegedly tried to rape the granddaughter.

That was when the grandfather grabbed a gun, firing at the three suspects. The suspects shot back, hitting the grandfather multiple times. They then fled in the man’s gold Cadillac, News 2 notes.

McLeod Hospital in Dillon, N.C., contacted authorities when two men with gunshot wounds showed up.

The sheriff’s office later got a tip that led officers to the body of 20-year-old Jamie Lee Faison in the stolen Cadillac. Faison had died from gunshot wounds.
