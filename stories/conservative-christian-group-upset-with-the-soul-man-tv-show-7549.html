Conservative Christian Group Upset with “The Soul Man” TV Show

August 7, 2012 Uncategorized

Conservative Christian group, One Million Moms, is upset with Cedric the Entertainer’s new TV show “The Soul Man” because it is not “family friendly.”


The group asserts the show is a “mockery of Jesus Christ” and the christian faith, saying it does not represent how pastors really are.
One Million Moms also does not believe Niecey Nash represents a good christian woman, saying, her attire and persona is inappropriate and highly sexualized.
