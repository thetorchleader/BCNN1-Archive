New Therapy for Prostate Cancer and Surgery for Diabetes Show Promise

April 17, 2012 Uncategorized

Prostate cancer treatment: An ultrasound therapy that destroys cancer cells shows promise for treating prostate cancer without causing the incontinence and impotence common after surgery or radiation. But the experimental treatment, called high-intensity focused ultrasound (HIFU), was tried on just 41 men and has not been directly compared with other therapies or assessed for long-term effectiveness.

Surgery for diabetes: The latest study to look at weight loss surgery as a diabetes treatment found that people who got the surgery did much better than people who continued standard diabetes medications. Eighty percent of surgery patients and none of the patients on standard medications were diabetes-free after 18 months. But the study only included obese patients who had already failed to control their type 2 diabetes with medication, so continued failure was not surprising, some experts say.
