Kirk Franklin Signs His First Gospel Music Group

August 20, 2012 Uncategorized

Gospel artist extraordinaire Kirk Franklin has signed his first music group, The Walls Group, to his label Fo Yo Soul Entertainment.
Hailing from Houston, Texas, The Walls is made up of four siblings, 20-year-old Darrell, 16-year-old Rhea, 15-year-old Paco, and 14-year-old Ahjah.
Raised by musicians, The Walls have been working diligently to perfect their sound and recently collaborated with producer Levi “Too” King and released their self-titled album.
Later this month, The Walls Group will embark on a national tour, stopping in major cities including Lexington, Ky., Indianapolis, Ind., and Austin, Texas.
