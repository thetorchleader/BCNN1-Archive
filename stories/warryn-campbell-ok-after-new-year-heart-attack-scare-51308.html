Warryn Campbell OK After New Year Heart Attack Scare

January 6, 2015 Entertainment

INSTAGRAM

Multi Grammy® winning producer and pastor Warryn Campbell, 39, kicked off the first full week of the New Year in the hospital with chest pains.

Suspecting a heart attack, the MyBlock Records CEO is rejoicing after receiving a good doctor’s report.

It turned out not to be serious.

“I’m all good, going home, thank you for your prayers,” he said in an Instagram video.

“God is amazing. Thought I had a heart attack, but it was just gas. I need a just gas praise,” he said, panning the camera around the hospital room.

The wide shot captured a small group of loved ones, including his wife, dancing.

News of the pastor of The California Worship Center’s health scare was first announced on Instagram Sunday, January 4.
