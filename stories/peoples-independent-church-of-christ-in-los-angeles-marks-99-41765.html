People’s Independent Church of Christ in Los Angeles Marks 99 Years

October 26, 2014 Black

Bishop Craig A. Worsham

People’s Independent Church of Christ will mark its 99th Anniversary with three uplifting events at 5856 West Boulevard in Los Angeles.

The celebration starts on Sunday, October 19, at the 8 a.m. and 11 a.m. worship services, said Bishop Craig A. Worsham, senior pastor.

“Pastor Gregory K. McLeod of Brookins Community A.M.E. Church will bring a message at 11 a.m. that will definitely help in your walk with the Lord,” said Bishop Worsham.

Pastor McLeod is known as a dynamic preacher. Called to the ministry at a young age, he received his first pastoral appointment at age 19.  He subsequently served as pastor at five churches before being assigned to lead Brookins in 2012.

“Then, on Saturday, October 25, at 10:30 a.m., our Anniversary Prayer Breakfast takes place in Gregg’s Hall and this event is open to the public as well,” added Bishop Worsham.  The ticket donation is $10.
