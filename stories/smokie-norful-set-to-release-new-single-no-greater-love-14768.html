Smokie Norful Set to Release New Single, “No Greater Love”

March 21, 2014 Entertainment

The wait is almost over!

Gospel force Smokie Norful is gearing up for the release of his new single “No Greater Love.” And took to social networks to unveil the striking cover. The new single is lifted from Norful’s sixth album , in which is scheduled to be released this year courtesy of Motown Gospel.

“No Greater Love” hits digital music stores on 3/18.
