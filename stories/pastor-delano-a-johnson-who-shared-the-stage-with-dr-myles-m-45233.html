Pastor Delano A. Johnson, Who Shared the Stage with Dr. Myles Munroe When he Preached his Last Sermon and Met with him for 5 Hours the Day Before he Died, Posts Reflective Message to Facebook

November 14, 2014 Black, Christian

Pastor Delano A. Johnson

Per a notice from Pastor Arthur Mackey and the Mount Sinai Baptist Church Cathedral in Roosevelt, New York, Pastor Delano A. Johnson will be speaking at the 31st ministerial and 15th pastoral anniversary celebration on Sunday, November 16th.

Delano A. Johnson shared Delano Johnson‘s status.
Some words makes no sense because they are to be understood in the future. I posted this on the third. Not knowing this would be the last time I shared the stage with my mentor, pastor, father and friend. Read it and apply it to your life.

Embrace your Creativity!™
Delano A. Johnson

CREATIVE THOUGHT!

“Take a minute to enjoy the memories that help make you the person you are today. If people were involved, call them if you can and thank them for the part they played, be it good or bad. Then continue to create more awesome moments worth remembering!” –Delano A. Johnson

Embrace your Creativity!™
Delano A. Johnson

Awesome service, great time yesterday. Oh yall didn’t know I sing huh! WIth mentor Dr. Myles Munroe and the rest of the System 3. plus Richa! Oh the memories.

With Richa Sands and Delano A. Johnson.
