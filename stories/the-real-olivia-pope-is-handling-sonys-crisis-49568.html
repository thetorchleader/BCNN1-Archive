The Real Olivia Pope is Handling Sony’s Crisis

December 20, 2014 Entertainment

(Photo: Chris Pizzello, AP)

Judy Smith, the crisis expert who inspired Scandal‘s Olivia Pope, is guiding Sony through the aftermath of its hacking crisis, according to reports by The Hollywood Reporter and TMZ.

THR says it has confirmed the hire, though it does not say by whom.

Her firm, Smith & Company, has offices in both Washington and Los Angeles, and her government contacts have no doubt come in handy when addressing both the political and entertainment industry aspects of the Sony hack.
