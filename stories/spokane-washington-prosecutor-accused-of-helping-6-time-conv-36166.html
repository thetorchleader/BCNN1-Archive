Spokane, Washington, Prosecutor Accused of Helping 6-Time Convicted Felon, Sent Bikini Picture and Exchanged Over 1300 Text Messages

August 30, 2014 What is this World Coming To?

Authorities are looking into whether Spokane County Deputy Prosecutor Marriya Wright tried to help six-time convicted felon Matthew Baumrucker hide from police when he was wanted on drug charges. She is on paid administrative leave.

A Washington state criminal prosecutor is under investigation after allegedly helping a wanted criminal elude police in March.

Marriya Wright, 34, had a relationship of sorts with Matthew Baumrucker, 31, a six-time convicted felon with the word “criminal” tattooed across his head, according to authorities.

A search of Baumrucker’s phone revealed the pair exchanged nearly 1,300 text messages and that he had a picture of the Spokane County deputy prosecutor in a bikini, reported KXLY.

Police are looking into whether she could be charged with rendering criminal assistance.

Baumrucker was wanted on drug charges at the time and he was under investigation for a possible assault in March.

The wanted felon assured a witness that a prosecutor named Marriya would be able to get him off the hook if police ever caught him,  authorities said.
