North Carolina A&T State University Choir to Sing at Metropolitan AME Zion Church in Birmingham

April 10, 2014 Uncategorized

The North Carolina A&T State University Choir (Photo provided by Metropolitan AME Zion Church)

The North Carolina A&T State University Choir will sing in Birmingham on Friday, April 11, 7 p.m., at Metropolitan AME Zion Church, 1530 Fourth Ave. North.

The choir is directed by Travis W. Alexander, director of university choirs and piano Professor. The concert is free and open to the public.

“It is a great honor when a Historically Black College & University brings students to Birmingham to share their musical talents and learn about the city’s rich civil rights heritage” said the Rev. Joshua Johnson, pastor of Metropolitan AME Zion. “We look forward to an outstanding program for the entire community to enjoy.”

The University Choir is the official ensemble of the university and the major choral unit for the College of Arts and Sciences and Department of Visual and Performing Arts.
