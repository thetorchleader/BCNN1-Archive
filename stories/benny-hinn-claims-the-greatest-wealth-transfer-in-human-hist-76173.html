Benny Hinn Claims the “Greatest Wealth Transfer In Human History” Is Coming, Encourages People to Sow a Seed Now

May 27, 2015 Christian

Photo: Benny Hinn Ministries

Televangelist Benny Hinn posted this message on his website:

It is estimated by many leading financial analysts that during the next three decades more than $16 trillion will transfer from one generation to the next!

Think about that for a moment…

How much is $16 trillion?

It is enough to carpet the entire states of Delaware, Rhode Island, and Florida in dollar bills.

If you lined up the bills end-to-end, the $16 trillion would circle the earth over 23,000 times!

Or if you put a stack of $100 bills together, that $16 trillion would rise 10,720 miles into the sky.

Two Questions for You
Isn’t it amazing that today’s financial headlines are reflecting the pages of Scripture?

As I’ve been sharing with you in recent months, the Word of God clearly tells us that we are coming into a time when we will see a glorious transfer of wealth for the preaching of the Gospel and for the financing of the end-time harvest.

With the greatest wealth transfer in human history underway now, I have two questions for you:

#1 Can it happen for you?

#2 And if you answered yes, how do you claim and receive it?
