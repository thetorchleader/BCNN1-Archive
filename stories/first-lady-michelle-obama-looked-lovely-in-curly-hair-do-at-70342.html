First Lady Michelle Obama Looked Lovely in Curly Hair-do at WHCD

April 27, 2015 National

Michelle Obama looked ridiculously fabulous at the White House Correspondents’ Dinner on Saturday, having eschewed her signature sleek, shoulder-length hairstyle for a curly bob paired with dangling earrings and a glittery silver dress by Zac Posen.

Basically, Mobama looked like she was ready to party — appropriate for a lighthearted affair that brings out droves of celebrities in addition to members of the press, and for which President Obama even participated in a sketch with comedian Keegan-Michael Key. (It’s funny. You should watch it. Also, the pitch perfect speech “SNL” cast member Cecily Strong gave.) She even went with a stronger makeup look than she usually does, with heavier liner and lashes and a pretty raspberry lipstick.

Known as a champion of American designers, the FLOTUS has had too many sartorial hits to count at this point, but should anyone be working on a coffee table compendium for when her husband is out of office: Please make sure this look gets priority.

Source: Fashionista | Eliza Brooke
