Break Up Your Fallow Ground: It is Time to Seek the Lord (Part 12)

August 26, 2012 Uncategorized

Please turn in your Bibles to Hosea 10:9-13:

O Israel, thou hast sinned from the days of Gibeah: there they stood: the battle in Gibeah against the children of iniquity did not overtake them. It is in my desire that I should chastise them; and the people shall be gathered against them, when they shall bind themselves in their two furrows. And Ephraim is as an heifer that is taught, and loveth to tread out the corn; but I passed over upon her fair neck: I will make Ephraim to ride; Judah shall plow, and Jacob shall break his clods. Sow to yourselves in righteousness, reap in mercy; break up your fallow ground: for it is time to seek the Lord, till he come and rain righteousness upon you. Ye have plowed wickedness, ye have reaped iniquity; ye have eaten the fruit of lies: because thou didst trust in thy way, in the multitude of thy mighty men.

Sunday Evening Evangelistic
Messages
Currently, we are in a series titled “Break Up Your Fallow Ground: It Is Time to Seek the Lord” which is based on Hosea 10:9-13. In this passage, Hosea calls the rebellious children of Israel to turn away from their sins, repent, and get right with God. 
Last week, we saw that God was willing to cut off the children of Israel from his blessings, because they were no longer being useful and putting forth good fruit. 
We must not forget that Israel was a very prosperous nation. Under the reigns of King David and King Solomon Israel had become very wealthy. It was also a very powerful nation because the trade routes from the north, the south, and the east all passed through Israel, so Israel was able to levy taxes on any goods that were being transported through its territory. 
But Hosea 10 tells us that Israel’s sin was in proportion to her prosperity. The more wealthy and prosperous Israel became, the more she turned away from God. She began to trust in the safety of her wealth and riches instead of in God who blessed her. 
America faces a similar danger. Because of our wealth, power, and prosperity, we have begun to trust in ourselves, in our riches, and in our government, and we have begun to turn away from God. If we continue down this path, we will receive the same judgement that Israel received.
Now, in this series of messages, we are addressing specific sins that folks in the church need to confess and repent of, so that we can get our hearts right with God, and so that we can ask God to send revival in our personal lives, our families, our churches, our communities, and our country. So far we have addressed thirty-two sins in total, and today, we are going to continue this series by addressing three more sins from our list.
I. The first sin we will address today is the sin of homosexuality. The Bible says in Leviticus 20:13, “If a man also lie with mankind, as he lieth with a woman, both of them have committed an abomination: they shall surely be put to death; their blood shall be upon them.” Homosexuality “is the act of intercourse between people of the same sex.” 
Unfortunately, this sin has been creeping into the church as well. Statistics show that one out of every five church members has a family member or close friend who struggles with homosexuality. God’s Word strictly condemns homosexual activity in the Old and New Testament. There are some people out there who will tell you that the Bible is outdated when it comes to homosexuality. But if we accept God’s Word without question on other points, we should do the same here. 
Homosexuality is a rebellion against God’s original plan for the world. It is an attempt to overthrow the order that God has set up in the universe. Romans 1:21-22 says, “Because that, when they knew God, they glorified him not as God, neither were thankful; but became vain in their imaginations, and their foolish heart was darkened. Professing themselves to be wise, they became fools…” 
In this passage Paul talks about the descent of man into idolatry and sinful rebellion. Even though man “knew God”, they deliberately refused to worship Him and instead turned to idols and idolatry. Because they had rejected God, they also rejected the natural law which God put in every man’s heart, and they began to do things that violated God’s original plan. Notice verses 26-27: “…for even their women did change the natural use into that which is against nature: And likewise also the men, leaving the natural use of the woman, burned in their lust one toward another; men with men working that which is unseemly, and receiving in themselves that recompence of their error which was meet.” 
You see, open rebellion against God and pride are at the root of homosexuality. To get the homosexuality issue fixed, one must get his heart fixed. He must repent of his pride and rebellion against God and admit that he is wrong for going against the order that God has placed in nature, and then turn away from the sin of homosexuality. If you are practicing homosexuality, I invite you to do this right now. Confess and repent of this sin and ask God to cleanse you and give you a new heart, mind, and spirit.
II. The second sin we will address today is the sin of faithlessness. The Bible reads in Matthew 14:26-31: “when the disciples saw him walking on the sea, they were troubled, saying, It is a spirit; and they cried out for fear. But straightway Jesus spake unto them, saying, Be of good cheer; it is I; be not afraid. And Peter answered him and said, Lord, if it be thou, bid me come unto thee on the water. And he said, Come. And when Peter was come down out of the ship, he walked on the water, to go to Jesus. But when he saw the wind boisterous, he was afraid; and beginning to sink, he cried, saying, Lord, save me. And immediately Jesus stretched forth his hand, and caught him, and said unto him, O thou of little faith, wherefore didst thou doubt?”
Faithlessness is simply a lack of faith. It is a lack of trust in God and in God’s ability to provide for us in every way. The basis of faith is trust. We should have faith in God because we trust him and because He has proven Himself over and over again, not only in our lives, but in the Bible, and in the lives of other believers.  Oftentimes, we are just like Peter in this story. We lose faith when we take our eyes off of Jesus and begin looking at our circumstances. We will never be able to overcome our circumstances if we do not have faith in the One who has already overcome the world and everything in it. 
If you have not trusted God as you should in your life, ask God to forgive you of this sin. Commit yourself to trusting God in every situation and ask God to give you a spirit that trusts in Him.
III. The third sin we will address today is the sin of abortion. Abortion is the taking of the life of an innocent, unborn child. As with every issue that we have covered so far, the Bible has something to say about this too. God tells Jeremiah in Jeremiah 1:5, “Before I formed thee in the belly I knew thee; and before thou camest forth out of the womb I sanctified thee, and I ordained thee a prophet unto the nations.” Job asked in Job 31:15, “Did not he that made me in the womb make him? and did not one fashion us in the womb?” 
These verses clearly show us that God is the one who forms human life in the womb. God regards this life so much that he “sanctified” and “ordained” Jeremiah to be a prophet before he was even physically born. Life does not just begin when a child is born physically. God knew Jeremiah even before He put him in his mother’s womb. 
When the life of an unborn child is taken — and now, in some cases, they are even allowing the babies to be born and then murdering them — but when this is done, we have become enemies of God, standing in the way of His plans for a child that we had nothing to do with creating.
Unfortunately, according to the Alan Guttmacher Institute and Physicians for Reproductive Choice, thirteen percent of all abortions are performed on women who are self-described “born-again” or Evangelical Christian women.  If you are one of those women who have had an abortion, encouraged someone else to have an abortion, or if you are a man who has pressured your wife or girlfriend into having an abortion, then confess it as sin now, and allow God to heal and cleanse you.
I believe that abortion is one of the main reasons why God is judging this nation because we are destroying innocent, helpless lives.
– – – – – – –
In closing, I want to encoura
ge you to not let any sin that we have discussed today or in previous weeks remain unconfessed. Allow God’s Holy Spirit to reveal every part of your heart. And get rid of anything that is not in line with God’s Word. Begin to break up your fallow ground by asking God to forgive you and cleanse you of your sins. Then you can enjoy the sweet fellowship of a relationship with God that is unhindered by sin and wrongdoing.
If the Lord should tarry his coming and we live, I hope that you will be with us next week so we can continue this journey of getting our hearts right with the Lord and getting back to serving Him. Break up your fallow ground: it is time to seek the Lord.
– – – – – – –
Now, if you do not know Jesus Christ as your Saviour, allow me to show you how you can be saved from sin and the eternal punishment of sin.
All you have to do is believe “that Jesus Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; and that he was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the scriptures” and you will be saved. And, like millions of others, you can come to know the risen Lord Jesus Christ for yourself.
John 3:16 says, “For God so loved the world, that He gave His only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in Him should not perish, but have everlasting life.” 
The Bible states in the book of Romans 10:9, 13: “That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised Him from the dead, thou shalt be saved.”  “For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.” 
If you are willing to believe on the Lord Jesus Christ for salvation, please pray with me the following simple prayer: Holy Father God, I realize that I am a sinner and that I have done some bad things in my life. For Jesus Christ sake, please forgive me of my sins. I now believe with all of my heart that Jesus Christ died for me, was buried, and rose again. Lord Jesus, please come into my heart and save my soul and change my life today. Amen.
If you just trusted Jesus Christ as your Saviour, and you prayed that prayer and meant it from your heart, I declare to you that based upon the Word of God, you are now saved and you are on your way to Heaven. Welcome to the family of God! I want to congratulate you on doing the most important thing in life and that is receiving Jesus Christ as your Lord and Saviour. For more information to help you grow in your newfound faith in Christ, go to Gospel Light Society.com and read “What To Do After You Enter Through the Door”. 
God bless you and have a great week.
