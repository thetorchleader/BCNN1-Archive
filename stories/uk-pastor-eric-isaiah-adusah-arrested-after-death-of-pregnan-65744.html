UK Pastor Eric Isaiah Adusah Arrested After Death of Pregnant Wife

April 6, 2015 Christian

Photo Credit: UK DAILY MAIL

A Christian couple’s ministry trip turned deadly and authorities suspect that the husband—a pastor and self-proclaimed prophet—may be responsible for his wife’s death.

Eric Isaiah Adusah has been arrested on suspicion of killing his three-months pregnant spouse, Charmain Adusah, in a hotel room in Ghana.

The body of the 41-year-old was discovered face down in a bath tub wrapped in a towel, according to the Criminal Investigation Department at the police headquarters in Koforidua, reports the UK Daily Mail.

Mrs. Adusah is believed to have been deceased four days before being discovered.

The events leading up to the suspicious death seemed pretty ordinary. Mr. Adusah, 28, had ministered at a three-day conference. Following the event, the two checked into the Mac-Dic Royal Plaza Hotel in Koforidua for a five-day stay on March 16.

According to Mr. Adusah, his wife wanted to stay in Ghana longer, so he left her there. Before boarding a flight to London, he told the hotel staff Mrs. Adusah did not want to be disturbed.
