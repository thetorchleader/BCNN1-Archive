Kim and Kanye Make It to 1 Year Anniversary

May 26, 2015 Entertainment

Instagram

Well this was well worth the wait!

Sunday marked one year since Kim Kardashian and Kanye West said “I Do” in Florence, Italy where they became husband and wife.

And while Kim was quick to share plenty of heartfelt photos and messages on social media throughout the day, Kanye remained pretty quiet. But rest assured Kimye fans, Yeezus had something special to say Monday night.

“Kim, I’m so happy to be married to the girl of my dreams,” he wrote on Twitter. “I love you and Nori so much!!! I would find you in any lifetime.”

She would later reply, “Awwww baby I love you so much!!!!!! I would find you too! Anywhere!!!!!”

In true Kardashian fashion, there were plenty of other family members sending their best wishes to the happy couple over the Memorial Day weekend.

Khloé Kardashian gushed about how much the pair has evolved ever since exchanging vows. “I’ve never seen either one of you filled with such ease and peace as I have since when you became husband and wife!” she wrote on Instagram. “That’s when you know you have found your soul mate. When you are still in love in a chaotic world. ”
