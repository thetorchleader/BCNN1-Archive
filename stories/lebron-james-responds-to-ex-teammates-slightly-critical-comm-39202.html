LeBron James Responds to Ex-Teammates’ Slightly Critical Comments

October 12, 2014 Sports

Steve Mitchell/USA TODAY Sports

The Miami Heat and Cleveland Cavaliers are in Brazil for a preseason game Saturday night, which will be the first reunion for LeBron James and his ex-teammates. James led the Heat to four NBA Finals appearances and two titles, but according to a number of Heat starters, James wasn’t the easiest guy to play with. Chris Bosh warned Kevin Love that he’ll need to make a number of sacrifices to accommodate James, Mario Chalmers made similar comments about Kyrie Irving, and Dwyane Wade revealed that he didn’t have fun last season.

Chris Bosh:

“You still have to go through things, you still have to figure out things on your own. It’s extremely difficult and extremely frustrating. He’s going to have to deal with that.”

Dwyane Wade:

Via Sporting News:

“Sometimes you can put too much on yourself, all of us, and it becomes a black cloud around. Last year wasn’t fun. I mean, there was no stretch of it [that was] fun. That whole season, to me, it’s amazing we made it to the Finals. It’s just honest.”
