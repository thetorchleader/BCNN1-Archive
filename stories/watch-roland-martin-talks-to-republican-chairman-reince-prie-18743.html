WATCH: Roland Martin Talks to Republican Chairman Reince Priebus

April 19, 2014 African-American

Roland S. Martin

Reince Priebus, chairman of the Republican National Committee, stopped by “NewsOne Now” Thursday to go head to head with Roland Martin in the first hour. The two touched on a number of topics, including the GOP’s outreach black voters and what Priebus thinks about Republican-led voter ID efforts. You won’t want to miss this!
