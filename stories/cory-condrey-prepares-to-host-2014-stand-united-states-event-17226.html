Cory Condrey Prepares to Host 2014 STAND UNITED STATES Event at Lakewood Stadium In Atlanta, May 24th

April 9, 2014 Christian

Saturday, May 24 2014
4:00 PM — 7:00 PM

ABOUT
STAND UNITED STATES is a day of worship and Word evangelistic crusade with dynamic speakers! Guests include Cory Condrey, Joann Rosario Condrey, Pastor Paula White, Vicki Yohe, Wess Morgan, William McDowell and many others. FREE General Admission tickets do not guarantee entry to the event. Admission is first come-first served. Crusade schedule is 5/24/14 5pm-9pm. For complete crusade schedule log on to www.ceafamily.org.
