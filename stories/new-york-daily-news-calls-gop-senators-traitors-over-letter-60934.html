New York Daily News Calls GOP Senators ‘Traitors’ Over Letter They Sent to Iran Behind President Obama’s Back

March 10, 2015 National

GOP senators DGAF and The New York Daily News is not shy about calling them out on it.Tuesday’s scathing front page accuses Mitch McConnell (R-Ky.), Ted Cruz (R-Texas), Tom Cotton (R-Ark.), Rand Paul (R-Ky.) and their “traitor” GOP colleagues of trying to “sabotage” President Barack Obama’s nuclear talks with Iran. In a bizarre move on Monday, Cotton and 46 other Republican senators ignored protocol and sent an open letter to Iran’s leaders, warning that whatever agreement reached with Obama would not be bound by the constitution.
