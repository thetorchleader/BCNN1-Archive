Police Search for 3 Men Believed to Be Responsible for Stray Bullet Ripping through Apartment, Killing a 15-Month-Old New Jersey Girl

October 15, 2014 Black

Generic image
THINKSTOCK

Sania Cunningham was bouncing on a bed with both her parents nearby when a stray bullet ripped through the apartment, striking her in her torso.A 15-month-old girl was killed by a stray bullet while playing inside her New Jersey home on Saturday, a mere two days after moving in, CBS 2 reports.

According to the report, authorities are searching for three men who were last seen wearing black hoodies and firing guns multiple times in the Irvington neighborhood. One bullet hit a woman’s SUV, and two hit a window and an air conditioner unit, missing one resident’s sleeping mother by inches, CBS notes.

However, one bullet went through the walls of the second-floor apartment where Sania Cunningham was bouncing playfully on a bed.

“There were several shots that were fired on the block,” Anthony Ambrose, with the Essex County Prosecutor’s Office, told the news station. “There were several ballistic evidence that were recovered. One bullet did pierce the house that pierced the baby.”
