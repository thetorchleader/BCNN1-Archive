Redskins Quarterback Robert Griffin III Reveals New Personal Logo by Adidas

April 6, 2014 Sports

Redskins quarterback Robert Griffin III on Sunday released his new personal logo designed for him by Adidas.

The logo, which features the quarterback’s initials and the No. 3 in the shape of a block heart, will appear on athletic wear.

Griffin and a team of artists from Adidas collaborated on the design.

Griffin unveiled the logo on his new Instagram account and issued the message, “This wouldn’t be possible without the love and support of my family, friends and fans growing up. This is for us and represents all of the work the people around me put in to help me succeed in life. Thank you from the bottom of my heart.”

SOURCE: The Washington Post
