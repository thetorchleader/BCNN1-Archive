‘Spy gate’ – Patriots Coach Bill Belichick Caught Checking Out Chrissy Teigen at White House Correspondents’ Dinner

April 26, 2015 Sports

© Jonathan Ernst/Reuters Model Chrissy Teigen arrives for the annual White House Correspondents’ Association dinner in Washington April 25, 2015.

Bill Belichick has been busted for spying again, only this time he won’t be penalized by the NFL as a result.

Belichick attended the White House Correspondents’ Dinner on Saturday and spent a few moments on the red carpet. SI swimsuit model Chrissy Teigen understandably caught his eye, and a photographer happened to snap the magical moment to bust the peeping coach.

Teigen’s husband John Legend, who was not present for the event, saw the photograph, linked to it, and added a great zinger over Twitter:

“Spy gate https://t.co/msQHB4iKoE,” Legend wrote.

Catch a good glimpse, Belichick?
