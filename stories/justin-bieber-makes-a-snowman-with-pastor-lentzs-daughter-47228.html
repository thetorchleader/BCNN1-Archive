Justin Bieber Makes a Snowman with Pastor Lentz’s Daughter

November 28, 2014 Entertainment

Justin builds a snowman on Thanksgiving eve

Do you want to build a snowman? Justin Bieber did! And the Canadian star was getting into the swing of American Thanksgiving on Wednesday, Nov. 26 with his friend and pastor, Carl Lentz’s children.

The “Heartbreaker” singer shared a sweet photo via Twitter of him and one of Lentz’s daughters fooling around in the snow having created a snowman as big as the little girl, on the pastor’s snow-covered front lawn.

“Made a snowman with this little cutie,” the 20-year-old singer captioned the snap. “Make memories with the ones you love! #HappyThanksgiving.”

Bieber has been hanging out with the pastor (who preaches at Hillsong NYC) a lot this month. The pair met back in 2008 via another pastor, Seattle-based Judah Smith, and according to Lentz, have formed a close friendship.

“I have a special role in Justin’s life, spiritually,” Lentz told the New York Post earlier this month. “But I’m not a life-motivational guy. We’re talking about making sure his life remains a blessing, not a burden.”

“When he’s with me, he rolls with me,” Lentz added. “He’s like a member of our team. He fits in like a piece of furniture.”
