Lecrae to Join LL Cool J, DJ Z-Trip, and Run-DMC for “Christmas in Brooklyn” Performance

December 8, 2014 Entertainment

WWBLS 107.5 and Hot 97 are gearing up to host the biggest holiday party in New York City. On December 19, the two stations will celebrate the season by bringing some big names to Brooklyn’s Barclays Center. So far, the lineup includes LL Cool J featuring DJ Z-Trip, Run-DMC and Lecrae.

According to the press release, “Christmas in Brooklyn” is for everyone in the family. “We’re in full holiday spirit mode and look forward to providing our listeners with the chance to see their favorite artists, many of whose careers began on WBLS and continued to excel on Hot 97. Emmis NY is excited to celebrate the holidays with our fans in New York,” they stated. So whether you’re in the mood for hip-hop or R&B, these stations have you covered.
