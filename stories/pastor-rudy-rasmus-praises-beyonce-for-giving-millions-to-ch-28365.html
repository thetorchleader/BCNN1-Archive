Pastor Rudy Rasmus Praises Beyonce for Giving Millions to Church’s Ministry

Date not recorded in the original archive Christian

Pastor Rudy Rasmus

Pastor Rudy Rasmus of Houston megachurch St. Johns Downtown says he doesn’t read the tabloids, but based on personal knowledge, he has nothing but the highest praise for Beyoncé Knowles.

Rasmus, who officiated Knowles’ wedding to Jay-Z, says Knowles has donated millions to the church’s ministry to the homeless and impoverished, in which she’s been involved since childhood.

“She’s an incredible human being. Has an incredible heart and has been extremely helpful in our mission and our ministry here,” he told a Houston news outlet.

Knowles is no stranger to controversy among Christians, in the past finding herself in trouble for her unwholesome performances and Instagram taboos, although she seems to retain some of her church roots.

But none of this seems to matter to her pastor, who bases his opinion of her completely on love, “period.”

“Love never fails. A lot of things will fail but love will never disappoint you,” Rasmus noted, paraphrasing 1 Corinthians 13 in defense of unconditional love.
