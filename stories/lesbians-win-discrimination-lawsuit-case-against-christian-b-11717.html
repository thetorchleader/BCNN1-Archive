Lesbians Win Discrimination Lawsuit Case Against Christian B&B Owner in Hawaii

April 16, 2013 Uncategorized

A Hawaiian bed and breakfast lost a discrimination lawsuit filed by a Southern California lesbian couple. The verdict was announced by Lambda Legal Monday.

The couple sued Aloha Bed and Breakfast after the Christian owner, Phyllis Young, said she was uncomfortable with them staying in her home because of her religious views.
A judge ruled in favor of the couple and ordered the bed and breakfast to stop discriminating, citing they violated the state public accommodations law. The law is intended to prohibit establishments from discriminating on the basis of sexual orientation, race, color, ancestry, religion, disability and sex — including gender identity or expression.
Jim Hochberg, a Honolulu attorney representing the bed and breakfast owner, said the ruling fails to acknowledge the First Amendment rights of the owner.
