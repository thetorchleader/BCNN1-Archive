Google Investors Say to EU Over Antitrust Case: Bring It On!

April 16, 2015 Technology

PHOTO CREDIT: AP

Google (GOOGL) investors to the EU: Bring it on!

Shares of Google are up 48 cents to $540.13 Wednesday – showing that investors aren’t all that concerned the European Union is pressing an antitrust case against the online advertising giant.

Investors’ reaction is pretty brazen while even well-known tech commentators see increased regulation as just one of the things that might foretell the decline of Google’s influence and power. It’s also a curious reaction given there’s direct evidence regulatory force can cause problems for big firms. Microsoft’s troubles with regulators a decade ago resulted in awkward situations – including needing to add promotions to competitors products within it own and the inability to compete with younger rivals, like Google.

Perhaps investors are just reacting to the fact that Google has beaten just about any regulator that’s tried to take issue with its business practices. Federal Trade Commission staff members recommended a suit against the online ad company since Google’s behavior, “has resulted – and will result – in real harm to consumers and to innovation in the online search and advertising markets,” according to documents first released by the Wall Street Journal.

Even so, the FTC’s commissioners voted in early 2013 to end the 19-month probe. Shares of Google are up $2.23, or 0.4%, to $566.03.
