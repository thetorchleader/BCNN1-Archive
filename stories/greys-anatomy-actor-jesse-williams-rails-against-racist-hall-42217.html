‘Grey’s Anatomy’ Actor, Jesse Williams, Rails Against Racist Halloween Costumes Depicting Dead Blacks

October 28, 2014 Black

In addition to the candy and the scares, Halloween has also become a time to brace ourselves for a slew of vile, racist costumes, showing humanity at its very worst.

Grey’s Anatomy actor Jesse Williams has seen those tasteless costumes, and he’s had enough.

In an epic Twitter rant, the Temple University-educated star asked his 784,000 followers what’s so funny about dressing up like Trayvon Martin and making memes about dead black people.
