First Episode of Mary Mary’s Reality Show Airs Thursday, March 29

March 11, 2012 Uncategorized

The sisters who make up the group Mary Mary, are a dynamic duo, not only making music but also living lives like superstars.
The mothers, singers, wives, and community members are getting ready to travel across the country to promote their sixth studio album, “Something Big.”
But in the midst of it all, Erica and Tina Campbell have to balance things out and keep their families first.
The ladies are getting ready to premiere their first self-titled reality series on WEtv.
