Lawyer Says Bobbi Kristina Brown Was Never Married to Nick Gordon

February 3, 2015 Entertainment

Bobbi Kristina Brown, pictured with husband is fighting for her life after being found face-down in a bathtub on Saturday morning, her family has revealed in a statement.

Bobbi Kristina Brown, the daughter of Whitney Houston and R&B singer Bobby Brown who is currently fighting for her life, was never married, a lawyer for Brown has told members of the media.

“We are currently investigating the events that led to the hospitalization of Bobbi Kristina,” Christopher Brown of Brown & Rosen LLC, legal counsel for Bobby Brown told TMZ and Access Hollywood late Tuesday. “To correct earlier reports, Bobbi Kristina is not and has never been married to Nick Gordon.”

The pair’s marriage had raised eyebrows last year. Houston raised Gordon like a son, although she never officially adopted him. But in January of 2014, Brown and Gordon appeared to confirm their marriage to TMZ. “I would tear up, this is exactly what she wanted, us together,” said Brown, then 20.
