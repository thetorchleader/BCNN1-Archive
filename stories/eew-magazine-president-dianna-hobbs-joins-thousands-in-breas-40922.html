EEW Magazine President, Dianna Hobbs, Joins Thousands In Breast Cancer Walk

October 23, 2014 Health

[CREDIT: Facebook]

Every October, EEW Magazine President & CEO, Dianna Hobbs, does something special to bring awareness and raise funds to fight breast cancer.

When her mother Annie, a survivor, was diagnosed in 2011, just a couple years after Hobbs’ aunt succumbed to the disease, she vowed to use her influence to aid in the fight.

On Saturday, Oct. 18, the award-winning urban faith-based media specialist, her two sisters, one niece and thousands of other Upstate New York residents participated in the American Cancer Society’s “Making Strides Against Breast Cancer” fundraising walk.

“I thought of my mom Annie the whole way and my sister-in-law Patrice—both breast cancer survivors. I also thought of my Aunt Ree, who, after fighting for many years, succumbed,” Hobbs said in a personal message on her official Facebook page.

Hobbs’ mother, then 60 years old, discovered her lump at home. She was tested and diagnosed with an aggressive and difficult to treat form of breast cancer known as “Triple Negative,” which accounts for only 10-15% of all breast cancers and could be responsible for an estimated 10,000 deaths annually according to the Triple Negative Breast Cancer Foundation.

Hobbs sister-in-law is a three-time survivor.
