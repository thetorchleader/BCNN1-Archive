Chuck Swindoll’s New Book Aims to Help Others Communicate Well

March 10, 2012 Uncategorized

Well-known pastor, broadcaster, and author Chuck Swindoll has written a new book — Saying It Well: Touching Others with Your Words — which is designed to help people communicate better.

Swindoll has not always been the great communicator that he became. He
actually had a serious problem with getting out his words.

“I was a stutterer,” says Swindoll. “If you saw [the movie] ‘The King’s
Speech,’ you kind of saw my life pass in review. I wasn’t a king but I
knew something of the embarrassment of not being able to get my words
out.”
 
But thanks to a faithful and committed speech teacher,
Swindoll overcame the problem because he was able to gain confidence in
himself.
 
“I think until you go through that process, you don’t
say it very well no matter what it is — especially if it’s designed to
touch the lives of others and make a difference,” the author explains.
 
Swindoll says that leads to authenticity.
 
“Being
authentic means you’re not faking it. You’re not trying to come across
as someone you’re not,” he says. “You say it well. You are representing
what you are convinced is the truth and you made that decision based on
good research. You’ve done your homework and you have grown to the place
that you have confidence that what you are saying is worth hearing.”
 
According
to the author, the book is not designed just for a Christian audience,
and it is not just a list of tips and tricks for conquering the fear of
speaking — but a manual on how to succeed in front of an audience.

SOURCE: Charlie Butts, One News Now
