Pastor Michael Youssef Explains Why Muslims Are Not Christianity’s Enemy

May 31, 2015 Christian

Michael Youssef

Pastor Michael Youssef wants people to know more about Jesus, jihad and peace.

“Jesus, Jihad and Peace: What Bible Prophecy Says About World Events Today” is the new book from Youssef, the pastor of The Church of the Apostles.

He also oversees the “Leading the Way” ministry that airs each weeknight on American Family Radio.

The pastor and author recently told the “Today’s Issues” radio program that there is too much “junk” going around, especially in churches.

“There are so many churches now having the Koran next to the Bible and ‘Chrislam,’ and all that junk is going around,” he said. “It’s so devastating seeing how people are so fooled and so deceived.”

One of the chapters in Youssef’s book is called “Are Allah and Jehovah the same God?” Youssef says Christians are not worshipping the same God.
