Andrae Crouch: Tribute Concert on January 20th, “Celebration Service” on January 21st at West Angeles COGIC

January 21, 2015 Christian, Entertainment

Andrae Crouch’s family has released the following details for the funeral arrangements of the Gospel legend.

Tuesday, January 20, 2015
Viewing: 4pm – 6pm
Tribute Concert: 7pm – 9pm

Funeral Service
Wednesday, January 21, 2015
11am – 3pm (Pacific Time — 2pm EST, 1pm CST)

Services will be held at the
West Angeles Church of God in Christ
3045 Crenshaw Blvd.
Los Angeles, CA 90016

You can tune in to both the concert and the funeral at BCNN3.TV or GLBN.TV.

–BCNN1
