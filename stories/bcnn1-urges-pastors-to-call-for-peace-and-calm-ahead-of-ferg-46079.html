BCNN1 Urges Pastors to Call for Peace and Calm Ahead of Ferguson Ruling and President Obama’s Immigration Announcement as Some Groups Reportedly Want to See ‘Civil War’

November 20, 2014 Christian

Police in Ferguson

BCNN1 has been informed via two reliable sources that there are white groups as well as black groups that want to see a civil war started over the Ferguson grand jury decision and over President Obama’s executive order on immigration among other reasons. One source has suggested repeatedly that President Obama is allowing things to happen the way they are so that he can call for martial law.

Here is the legal definition of martial law according to Attorneys Gerald N. Hill and Kathleen T. Hill: Martial law is “a system of complete control by a country’s military over all activities, including civilian, in a theoretical or actual war zone, or during a period of emergency caused by a disaster such as an earthquake or flood, with the military commander having dictatorial powers. In the United States martial law must be ordered by the President as commander-in-chief and must be limited to the duration of the warfare or emergency.”

Of course, we have no way of verifying that these statements are true, but we are somewhat taken aback at the strange build-up to the Ferguson grand jury decision and even to the President’s announcement on immigration. If you are paying attention, you would agree that this is highly unusual.

Be that as it may, we are calling upon Christian leaders who have some influence — such as T.D. Jakes, Rick Warren, Tony Evans, Ronnie Floyd, Fred Luter, Charles Stanley, Dwight McKissic, James Meeks, John Hagee,  Jerry Young, and others — to specifically call for peace and calm across the nation by any means necessary. This would be greatly appreciated. If there was ever a time your country needs you, it needs you now.

–BCNN1
