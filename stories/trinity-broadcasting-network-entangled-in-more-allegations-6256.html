Trinity Broadcasting Network Entangled In More Allegations

May 25, 2012 Uncategorized

The founding family of the Trinity Broadcasting Network finds themselves entangled in more allegations by a relative who filed a lawsuit against the ministry.
According to allegations detailed in a 180-page declaration filed in court, the granddaughter of Paul and Jan Crouch said her relatives sought to “destroy her.” The Orange County Register reports that Brittany Koper said after she approached relatives about the fiscal waste and illegal practices at TBN, she was fired. Koper said she was accused of “biting the hand that feeds you” and for being a “naïve little girl.”
