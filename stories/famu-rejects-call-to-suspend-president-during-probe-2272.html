FAMU Rejects Call to Suspend President During Probe

December 19, 2011 Uncategorized

Florida A&M’s board of trustees today rejected the governor’s call to suspend university President James Ammons while the hazing death of a band member is being investigated.
“We will stand firm against outside interference, no matter how well intended,” Solomon Badger, the FAMU trustees chairman, said during a board meeting that was held by conference call, the Associated Press reports.
The decision comes three days after the state medical examiner ruled that 26-year-old Robert Champion’s death on Nov. 19 was a homicide. Officials say he was beaten so severely that he bled internally and went into shock. He died within an hour.
