Gospel Star, Fred Hammond, to Receive 2012 Outstanding Achievement in the Arts Award

May 7, 2012 Uncategorized

Gospel music star Fred Hammond will receive the 2012 Outstanding Achievement in the Arts Award, announced District 8 Deputy Mayor Pro Tem Tennell Atkins. The Grammy-winning Hammond, who lives in Cedar Hill, succeeds past award recipients such as actresses Regina Taylor and Irma P. Hall, and actor Eugene Lee.
The Outstanding Achievement in the Arts Award, presented by Wal Mart, honors excellence in artistic endeavors.
The McDonald’s Inspiration Celebration Gospel Tour, which stars Fred Hammond, takes place Saturday, June 16 at Friendship West Baptist Church, 2020 Wheatland Road in Dallas.
Free tickets to the event will be available at the press conference – 11:30 a.m. Wednesday May 9 at Dallas City Hall, Flag Room, 6EN.
