Evangelical Minister Tameka Bates Protects Mother and Child from Subway Assailant

July 26, 2014 Christian

Tameka Bates (SCREENSHOT: WTXF)

A Philadelphia woman is being hailed as a modern day Good Samaritan.

Tameka Bates was riding the subway when she noticed a man harassing a young woman and her infant on the train.

Bates said she and the young mom both got on the train at the same stop. She noticed the man following the woman as she sat down.

The man, whom the mother did not know, proceeded toward the woman, asking to see the baby. When the woman said no, he then tried to hit the baby.

Subway surveillance video shows Bates protecting the mother and child by standing between them and the assailant. She also escorted the young mother away from the man.

Bates who is a Christian and a minister at Resurrection Evangelistic Church, boldly used the name of Jesus as a weapon against the man.
