ESPN Personalities Caught Gossiping About Tim Tebow While On-Air

January 30, 2013 Uncategorized

Even when they don’t know they’re live on-air, ESPN folks will never stop talking about Tim Tebow.
Hannah Storm and Mark Schlereth were caught having a private discussion while ESPN cameras unknowingly cut to them during the network’s Super Bowl media day coverage. Rather than talking about something interesting or relevant, like where to go for dinner in New Orleans or whether Jim Harbaugh still talks to Screech, they were talking about the New York Jets backup quarterback. In January. While at the Super Bowl.
The worst part is that Schlereth seems to be enjoying the conversation. He’s whispering about Tebow like a sixth-grade girl telling secrets before homeroom.
