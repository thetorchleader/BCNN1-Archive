Moderators, Pastors and Ministers Conference of the National Baptist Convention Cancelled

February 14, 2015 Black

A Message from Dr. Matthew Canada, President of the Moderators Auxiliary:

Greetings and thanks to all who joined us for the Mid-Winter Board Meeting in Jackson, MS.

IMPORTANT – Please help spread the word and share within your state and districts. Due to a scheduling conflict with National Executive Meetings – The Moderators, Pastors and Ministers Conference previously scheduled for March 2-5, 2015 is CANCELLED!

We look forward to seeing you in Detroit for the National Baptist Congress of Christian Education 2015 Session.

Source: National Baptist Convention
