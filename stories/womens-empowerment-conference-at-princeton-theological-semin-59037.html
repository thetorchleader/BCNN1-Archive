Women’s Empowerment Conference at Princeton Theological Seminary Offers a Day of Health, Finances, and Emotional Wellness Guidance

February 27, 2015 Education

NEW YORK, NY, (February 27, 2015) – At The Well Conferences will present A WHOLE NEW U: Girls Empowerment Conference on Saturday, March 21, 2015. Held at Princeton Theological Seminary, the daylong event offers informational and interactive sessions that will offer A WHOLE NEW perspective on your finances, health, wellness, and relationships. A buffet lunch is included.

The event is hosted by At the Well Conferences, Inc. Founder and CEO, Jacqueline Glass. The conference features topics such as: 1) Financial Wellness 2) Healthy Gourmet Cooking Class, 3) Emotional Well-being, and 4) Men’s Panel. The featured presenters include three dynamic women who will share their wisdom for developing A WHOLE HEART, A WHOLE SPIRIT, and A WHOLE BODY.

DEBRENA GANDY will show women how to create A WHOLE HEART. Debrena, who is from Seattle, Washington, is the bestselling author of two books, “All The Joy You Can Stand” and “Sacred Pampering Principles.”  She is also an international keynote speaker, seminar leader, business consultant, trainer, and performance coach. Debrena regularly appears as a contributing writer in both Essence and Heart & Soul magazine.  She also has been a feature in Oprah Winfrey’s, O Magazine and has appeared in Ebony, Upscale and Black Enterprise magazine to name a few.

DR. GLENDA HODGES will speak on cultivating A WHOLE SPIRIT. Dr. Hodges is the CEO of Still I Rise, Inc.  Founded in 2006, this organization serves as a community-based empowerment entity for survivors of domestic violence.  Dr. Hodges is also the CEO of The Women’s Wellness Center, a subsidiary of Still I Rise, Inc.  She lectures extensively in domestic violence and serves as the Director for Domestic Violence Services in District 9, Prince George’s County, Maryland.  Dr. Hodges is an Ordained Itinerant Elder in the African Methodist Episcopal Church, Second Episcopal District, Washington Annual Conference. Additionally, she is the former Associate Hospital Director at Howard University Hospital, Washington, DC and former Assistant Professor, Department of Community and Family Medicine, Howard University College of Medicine.

KAREN DENNIS will show attendees how to develop A WHOLE BODY. Karen is a Certified Personal Trainer, Master Trainer, and owner of Karen Dennis Power Trainer Studio LLC, a privately owned fitness studio that offers members and clients a level of service that cannot be found in today’s large ordinary gyms. Karen’s Power Training focuses on being spiritually connected, emotionally aligned, mentally tough and physically strong.

A Whole New U is a program of At the Well Conferences, Inc., a 501 (c)(3) non-profit organization empowering women since 2009. For more information and to purchase a ticket, visit www.AtTheWellConferences.org.
