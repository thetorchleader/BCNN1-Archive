Cate Edwards, Daughter of John and Elizabeth, Weds

October 23, 2011 Uncategorized

The oldest daughter of John and Elizabeth Edwards married her longtime boyfriend at the University Methodist Church in Chapel Hill Saturday with her father at her side.

“It’s just great,” said family friend Glenn Bergenfield. “(It) couldn’t be a nicer, better day for the Edwards family.”
After the wedding, Cate Edwards and new husband Trevor Upham walked arm-in-arm out of the church, while John Edwards left through the back with a police escort.
Bergenfield said the former North Carolina Senator was proud and happy to walk his daughter down the aisle.
“John is strong. He’s got some hard times ahead, but today is a wonderful day and John feels delighted that Trevor is going to be Cate’s husband,” Bergenfield said.
