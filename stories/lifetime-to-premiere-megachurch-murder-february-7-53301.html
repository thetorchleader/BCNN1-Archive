Lifetime to Premiere “Megachurch Murder” February 7

January 23, 2015 Entertainment

Following the successful premiere of the Angela Bassett Directed Whitney biopic, Lifetime is hoping to score big ratings again with a movie about a church scandal.

Megachurch Murder, starring Tamala Jones and Malcolm-Jamal Warner hits the small screen Saturday, Feb. 7.

Inspired by Shakespeare’s “Hamlet,” the film, also starring Michael Beach, Shanica Knowles, Romeo Miller, Corbin Bleu and Dawnn Lewis, is set in a modern day black church.

In Megachurch Murder Hannah’s life spirals out of control after her father Hamilton Spears dies in a cycling accident. The glue holding things together is Hannah’s new beau, Oliver. Not long after the tragic death, evidence pointing to a church conspiracy and threats towards her father begin surfacing, leaving Hannah determined to prove that Hamilton’s death was not an accident at all, but murder.

As she inches more closely to the truth, she realizes her own mother, Martha and her boyfriend’s dad, Clay just may be involved in the death of her father.
