Deion Sanders' Daughter Is Playing Bikini Basketball

January 18, 2013 Uncategorized

There is a professional bikini basketball league.
Deion Sanders’ daughter is playing in it.
Deiondra Sanders recently shared a photo of herself getting ready for practice with the Atlanta Peaches (because, of course).
The Bikini Basketball Association kicks off its season this summer, according to the league’s Twitter account, and features eight teams, which we will list in full so everyone can enjoy these names:
  • Miami Spice
  • New York Knockouts
  • Orlando Lady Cats
  • L.A. Ice
  • Minnesota Mist
  • Atlanta Peaches
  • Chicago Crave
  • Illinois Heart
