WATCH: Lightning Strikes Church In Maine

May 29, 2015 Christian

Lightning strike
(Photo: Carl Bouley)

This incredible footage captured around 6:00 p.m. Wednesday night by 16-year-old Carl Bouley shows lightning striking St. Gerard Mt. Carmel Church located in Grand Isle, Maine.

Bouley first posted the video on his Facebook page Wednesday and it has since taken off.

According to the Grand Isle fire chief, Gary Campbell, the same church was hit by lightning in 2007 causing some fire damage. This time around some shingles were knocked off the church and there is some electrical damage to the wiring inside. A couple of homes near the church also received some electrical damage.
