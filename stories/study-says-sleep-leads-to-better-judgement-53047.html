Study Says Sleep Leads to Better Judgement

January 21, 2015 Health

A study monitoring sleep in teenagers has revealed that a lack of it can directly increase risky behavior in addition to health problems.

This study is significant in that it shows that sleep doesn’t just lead to better health, it leads to better judgement as well. According to News Ledge, researchers discovered this from an analysis of survey data taken from more than 6,500 adolescents across the United States. This survey occurred in three waves across the U.S. from 1994 to 2002. Sleep patterns and both drug and alcohol use were among the factors surveyed.
