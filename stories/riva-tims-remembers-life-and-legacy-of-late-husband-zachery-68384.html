Riva Tims Remembers Life and Legacy of Late Husband Zachery Tims Jr. on What Would Have Been His 46th Birthday

April 18, 2015 Christian

Every year on this day, April 18th, we celebrate the Life and Legacy of the late great Dr. Zachery Tims, Jr. (date of his birth). We honor his accomplishments and pioneering spirit. We will never forget the good and positive endeavors Dr. Zachery did in this community and abroad. We the family are 3 years into establishing the Dr. Zachery Tims, Jr Enrichment Center – a place where leaders that may be struggling with burn out, depression, addictions can come and be Healed and Delivered. Please keep us in your prayers. ‪#‎Neverforget‬ ‪#‎Legacy4children‬
