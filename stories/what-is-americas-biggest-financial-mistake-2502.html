What Is America’s Biggest Financial Mistake?

May 17, 2012 Uncategorized

It’s a question that has been around for years: Should the U.S. go back to the gold standard? One expert says the answer is yes, but doing so won’t be easy.
American Family Radio’s (AFR) financial expert, Dan Celia, says there is not enough gold in the world to back the U.S. dollar.
“We’ve been running the printing presses at a frantic pace for the last three years,” he notes. “The [Federal Reserve has] been buying up all of this debt, 61 percent of the debt. That means that we have been putting massive amounts of dollars into the system.”
According to Celia, under the gold standard — or any commodity-based system — a dollar cannot be put into circulation unless a dollar’s worth of that hard asset is there to back it up.
“If we took dollars out of circulation, that actually increases the value of a dollar,” Celia explains. “Well, if the value of the dollar is increased, that generally means the value of gold is going to decrease.”
