Rupert Murdoch Defends All-White Cast of “Exodus: Gods and Kings”

November 30, 2014 Entertainment

Rupert Murdoch has weighed in online regarding complaints about the racial makeup of the cast of the upcoming film, Exodus: Gods and Kings, drawing substantial opposition in the process.

In a series of tweets on Friday, the media mogul, whose 20th Century Fox will release the film Dec. 12, defended casting decisions made regarding the biblical drama, which is set in ancient Egypt. The film has drawn criticism for having white, non-African actors in lead roles.

Christian Bale, who was born in Wales, portrays Moses and Australian Joel Edgerton plays the Egyptian Pharaoh Ramses in the Ridley Scott-directed film. Other roles are played by John Turturro, Aaron Paul, Ben Mendelsohn, Sigourney Weaver and Ben Kingsley.

Murdoch issued a series of tweets, opening wi th: “Moses film attacked on Twitter for all white cast. Since when are Egyptians not white? All I know are.”
