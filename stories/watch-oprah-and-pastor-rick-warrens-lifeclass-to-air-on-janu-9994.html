January 12, 2013 Uncategorized

Sneak Peek: Oprah and Pastor Rick Warren on Winning the Hand You’re Dealt

Oprah and Pastor Rick Warren discuss how to live with and maximize the hand you’re dealt. Plus, Pastor Warren reveals the five “cards” that make up our identity: chemistry, connections, circumstances, consciousness and–the card that can change our entire hand–choices. Tune in Sunday, January 13, at 9/8c.
