WATCH: Tim Tebow ‘All I Do Is Win’ Videos

November 30, 2011 Uncategorized

Tim Tebow made headlines over the past month for his late game heroics but a new YouTube video shows the quarterback has some rap skills as well.

The video shows a manipulated image of Tebow lip-syncing DJ Khaled’s
“All I Do Is Win” alongside hip-hop stars T-Pain, Ludacris, Snoop Dogg
and Rick Ross.

The song fits Tebow perfectly as he has revived a dead Denver Broncos team after being appointed the starting quarterback.

Since
Tebow took over the starting job, the Broncos have won five of their
last six games and are now just a game behind the Oakland Raiders in the
race for the AFC West title. The team has already won more games than
last year, when they finished an execrable 4 to 12.

Tebow was
criticized for his passing ability but has eight touchdowns to just one
interception this season. The quarterback does sport a disappointing
45-pass completion percentage.

Since Tebow was thrust into the
spotlight, he has attributed much of his success to Jesus Christ. Some
people around the league are not too happy about Tebow’s outspokenness
on his faith.
