Spike Lee Visits Wesley Snipes in Jail

March 8, 2012 Uncategorized

Film director Spike Lee recently visited Wesley Snipes in prison and says via his Twitter page the actor is in “great shape.”

Lee tweeted, “Yesterday I went to McKean Federal Correctional Insititute in West Hell, Pennsylvania to spend two great hours with my man Wesley Snipes.”
He added, “Mr. Snipes is in great shape mentally, physically and spiritually. We laughed and joked for the whole two-hour visit. He said when he gets out, it’s on.”
