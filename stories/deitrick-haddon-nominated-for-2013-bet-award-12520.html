Deitrick Haddon Nominated for 2013 BET Award

May 19, 2013 Uncategorized

Tyscot Music & Entertainment celebrates Deitrick Haddon on his 2013 BET Award nomination. This year, Deitrick Haddon is nominated in the Best Gospel Artist category for his CD, Deitrick Haddon presents Voices of Unity; A Beautiful Soul (Music Inspired by the Motion Picture).
To view the 2013 BET Award Gospel Nomination visit: http://www.bet.com/shows/bet-awards/2013/nominees/best-gospel-artist.html
The A Beautiful Soul CD features the radio hit, “No Betta”, featuring Deitrick Haddon and Faith Evans and the additional tracks, “Marching Orders” featuring Kierra Sheard, “The More I Praise You”, featuring Canton Jones and “New Life” featuring Fonzworth Bentley.
The entire CD is phenomenal and continues to receive awesome reviews along with the motion picture film, A Beautiful Soul, which stars Deitrick Haddon, Harry Lennix, Vanessa Bell Calloway, Golden Brooks, Robert Ri’chard, Lesley Ann Brandt, and Barry Floyd.
