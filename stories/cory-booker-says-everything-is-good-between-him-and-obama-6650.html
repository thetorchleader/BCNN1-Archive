Cory Booker says Everything Is Good between him and Obama

June 19, 2012 Uncategorized

Cory Booker says all’s cool with President Obama.

The Newark mayor, who caused an uproar by saying the Obama campaign attacks on Mitt Romney and Bain Capital were “nauseating,” said he “wasn’t at my best” when he said those things on NBC’s Meet the Press last month.
The New Jersey Democrat told Jay Leno on The Tonight Show last night that he wasn’t defending the presumptive GOP presidential nominee when he had some praise for investments made by private-equity firms such as Bain.
Still, Booker had to walk back his remarks and do some fence-mending with Obama, his friend since 2005. He said Obama has been “incredibly gracious” and “incredibly kind” about the whole flap, which dominated a news cycle for a day or so.
“That’s the shame about the kind of politics we’re in,” Booker said. “People often miss the essence of a man. We have a president, despite whether you’re on the right or on the left, he is a good person. He was the first person to come out and say, ‘Hey, you’re still my friend.’ “
