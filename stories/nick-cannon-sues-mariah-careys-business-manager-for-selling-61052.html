Nick Cannon Sues Mariah Carey’s Business Manager for Selling Their $9 Million Bel-Air Home

March 10, 2015 Entertainment

Christopher Polk/Getty Images For Nickelodeon

Nick Cannon is suing mad at Mariah Carey’s business manager Michael Kane. Why? Cannon is claiming that Kane sold the couple’s home without his knowledge!

According to legal documents obtained by E! News, the America’s Got Talent host has filed a lawsuit against Kane over the sale of his and Carey’s $9 million Bel-Air home (the 6-bedroom, 9-bath property was originally listed for $12,995,000 million). Cannon claims he was not informed of the house’s sale or of the closing date until Feb. 24.

While the 34-year-old has “no objection” to selling the home, Cannon alleges his “initial efforts to obtain copies of the Purchase and Sale Agreement and other documents pertinent to the transaction were rebuffed by Kane.”

The docs also claim Cannon “has yet to see the estimated settlement agreement nor has he been provided with any information about required closing costs and loan payoffs, and other planned disbursements from escrow.”
