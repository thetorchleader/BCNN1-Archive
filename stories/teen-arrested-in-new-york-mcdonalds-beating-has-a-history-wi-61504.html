Teen Arrested in New York McDonald’s Beating Has a History with the Law

March 13, 2015 Black

Aniah Ferguson, 16, has been charged with assualt and robbery for her alleged role in attacking a 15-year-old girl inside a Brooklyn McDonald’s.
CBS NEW YORK SCREENSHOT

Police arrested a 16-year-old girl Thursday in connection to the savage beating of a teenage girl caught on tape inside a Brooklyn, NY McDonald’s.

According to the New York Daily News, Aniah Ferguson, who police note can been seen in the 3-minute attack video wearing a hoodie and then only a bra, has been charged with gang assault and robbery as Ferguson reportedly took the victim’s purse.

The Daily News notes that this is the teen’s sixth arrest as other incidents including reportedly stabbing her brother in the arm during an argument that turned violent and allegedly punching her grandmother in the face. Ferguson’s grandmother filed an order of protection against her after the attack.

Police are still looking for five other girls that they believe were involved in the melee and while the victim has not been cooperative with the police, according to the Daily News, she has found redemption in her new found infamy. The Daily News notes that the teen who can be seen being punched and kicked in the video as onlookers cheer and yell has taken to Facebook posting, “Everyone Like I’m Famous Now.”

She has also posted photos of her face without bruises or marks to question reports that say she received two black eyes. In the end she tells her followers that she is “gucc!”— street slang meaning she is well.
