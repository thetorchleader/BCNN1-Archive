Nobel Prize-winning Mathematician, John Nash, and Wife Killed in NJ Car Crash

May 24, 2015 National, zTransfer-UCN

John Nash, the Nobel Prize-winning mathematician whose life story inspired the movie “A Beautiful Mind,” and his wife, Alicia Nash, were killed Saturday in a car crash on the New Jersey Turnpike, The Huffington Post and NJ.com reported, citing New Jersey State Police officials. Nash was 86.

The Nashes were in a taxi traveling southbound in the left lane of the New Jersey Turnpike, State Police Sgt. Gregory Williams told NJ.com, when the driver lost control, crashing into the guard rail. The Nashes were ejected from the car, Williams said.

Nash, who won the Nobel Prize for Economics in 1994, was 86, while Alicia Nash was 82.
