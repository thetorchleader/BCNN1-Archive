Kierra Sheard Set to Launch Full-Figured Clothing Line In October

May 8, 2015 Entertainment

Award-winning gospel singer turned fashion designer, Kierra Sheard is gearing up for the official launch of her anticipated full-figured clothing line.

“Eleven 60,” inspired by her mother Karen Clark-Sheard’s birthday—November, 1960—is set to debut in October 2015.

Sheard, 27, said it is a “clothing line that compliments the thick woman” who doesn’t wish to wear the “skimpy clothes,” or “muumuus” retailers offer.

To ensure maximum exposure of her original designs, the entrepreneurial woman of faith has teamed with multiple bloggers for promotional purposes.

“I’m really excited and thankful to a few of the fashionable bloggers who have been [an] inspiration and [have been] supporting me in the launch,” wrote Sheard on Instagram.
