Jada Pinkett Smith says Divorce “Rumors Are Completely Untrue”

April 14, 2012 Uncategorized

In an interview with Gala magazine, Jada Pinkett Smith said she’s had enough of the rumors suggesting her 15-year marriage to Will Smith is in shambles.
“Every year, one celebrity couple is under the microscope. This year, unluckily, it’s us!” she tells the publication, months after that the couple — parents to Trey, 18, Jaden, 13, and Willow, 11 — squashed a tabloid report claiming they were headed for divorce.
Jada, 40, tells Gala that rumors of marital strife couldn’t be further from the truth. She also slammed a report that the couple was no longer living under the same roof.
“That’s ridiculous. These rumors are completely untrue,” she says. “Will and I know the truth. I travel a lot because of my work, just like Will does….But our lifestyle hasn’t changed. Before we had the kids, we were already far away from each other quite often because of separate projects.”
