What Is Next for the Crystal Cathedral?

February 20, 2012 Uncategorized

One Christian activist says Orange County’s Crystal Cathedral must find other means to appeal to their supporters now that the church’s iconic property in Southern California and lavish events are no longer sustainable.
Crystal Cathedral CEO and senior pastor Sheila Schuller Coleman has announced she is temporarily leaving the mega-church’s board of directors. Coleman, daughter of church founder Robert Schuller, said she is stepping away as the board’s non-voting interim chairman as the church transitions after filing for Chapter 11 bankruptcy, and after selling its iconic property. The Garden Grove mega-church recently sold its property to the Roman Catholic Diocese of Orange.
 
Douglas V. Gibbs of Political Pistachio tells OneNewsNow that he hopes the Crystal Cathedral will be able to maintain its congregation.
“Any system — be it a religious system or be it a product — if it doesn’t appeal to the people, it’s not going to survive. Simple as that,” he says.
