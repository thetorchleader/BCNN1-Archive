Equipment Used by California Church to Minister to Gangs Is Stolen by Thieves

May 17, 2013 Uncategorized

The resonant voice of a Riverside church that has urged youths to reject gang life is quieter, after burglars twice this week broke into Peacemakers Ministries and stole equipment used to present street outreach skits and speeches.
Pastor Ruben Guitron said the first burglary happened sometime between Sunday night, May 12, and Monday morning, and the second early Thursday, May 16. Amplifiers, microphones, drum sets, cymbal stands, computers and keyboards were stolen. The thefts have been reported to police.
Peacemakers provides ministry throughout Riverside, but its base is on the Eastside. Peacemakers and other churches have sponsored marches, concerts and other events aimed at reducing violence between blacks and Hispanics.
Some events feature skits that use the microphones and amplifiers to project the sound to the large audiences that gather in parking lots. One such skit, held in the wake of the February 2012 slaying of Poly High student Lareanz Simmons, focused on a mother fretting for her oldest son’s safety while he ran around town as gunfire rang out and sirens wailed.
Guitron said he is not mad at the thieves.
“I don’t know if the person realizes the effect they are having on us helping the community,” Guitron said while standing in front of a pile of extension cords the burglars left behind. “The second thing is, does this person need help? Is this a person we should be reaching out to?”
He said it crossed his mind that the burglaries “might be a way to counter our work in the community. We’re a small ministry, but a very powerful ministry.”
