Disrespect: Raiders LB Sio Moore Calls 49ers QB Colin Kaepernick a ‘Chump’

December 8, 2014 Sports

The San Francisco 49ers had a pretty poor showing and lost to the Oakland Raiders 24-13 on Sunday.

Colin Kaepernick did not play well. The Raiders defense managed to sack him five times and intercept him twice. They also weren’t nice about it. They didn’t let Kaepernick hear the end of it.

The game’s over, but Raiders linebacker Sio Moore isn’t letting up. Moore took to Instagram and posted this overly-filtered photo with the caption: “There’s a difference between boys and Men… Freakin chump.”
