WATCH: Israel Houghton and Yolanda Adams Perform “How Awesome Is Our God” on “The Real”

May 14, 2015 Entertainment

Photo Credit: The Real

The co-hosts of of hit talk show “The Real,” got their worship on with multi Grammy®, Stellar and Dove Award winning gospel recording artists Israel Houghton and Yolanda Adams.

The two rocked the stage with the soul-stirring single, “How Awesome Is Our God.”

“Now, I grew up in church and I don’t know if there’s anything better than some good old gospel singing said “The Real” co-host Adrienne Bailon in her introduction of the artists.

The performance, which aired Tuesday, May 12, received rave reviews from viewers.
