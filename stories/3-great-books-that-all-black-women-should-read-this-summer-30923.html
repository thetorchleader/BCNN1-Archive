3 Great Books that All Black Women Should Read This Summer

July 19, 2014 Lifestyle

What’s on your reading list this summer? I know that summer is a time for relaxation and it’s the time many of us use to catch up on reading the latest juicy novels by some of our favorite authors.The popular ones always seem to be Kimberla Lawson Roby, Carl Weber, Reshonda Tate-Billingsly, Daaimah S. Poole, and many other talented writers. Their vivid storylines, plots and colorful characters keep us entertained and on the edge of our seats with anticipation. But this summer, I have been on an empowerment kick and choosing material that not only feeds my appetite for great writing, but also feeds my soul.
