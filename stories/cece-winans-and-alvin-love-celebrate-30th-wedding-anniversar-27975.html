CeCe Winans and Alvin Love Celebrate 30th Wedding Anniversary

June 24, 2014 Entertainment

Award-winning gospel recording artist CeCe Winans doesn’t spend a whole lot of unnecessary time on social media, but on Monday, June 23, she had something special to share.

The 49-year-old wrote “Got something to tweet about!” That something was the celebration of her 30th wedding anniversary.

“I honor, love and appreciate him so much!” she fawned over her soul mate Alvin Love, ending with, “To God be the Glory.”

CeCe met Alvin in the 80s during a night of family bowling. Not too long after, when he traveled to North Carolina to see the Winans family minister on a Christian television program, the two took a liking to each other.
