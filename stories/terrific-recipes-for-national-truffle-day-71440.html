Terrific Recipes for National Truffle Day

May 2, 2015 God & Food

Orange Creamsicle Truffles (Cooking Classy)

If you’re looking for an excuse to indulge, seize the day. May 2nd is National Truffle Day!

1. Orange Creamsicle Truffles: White chocolate,heavy cream, orange extract and zest come together to make one dreamy take on the classic flavor combo. (recipe and photo: Cooking Classy)

2. Chocolate Hazelnut Truffles: These creamy bites are rich with nutella, bittersweet chocolate, and chopped roasted hazelnuts. Roll them in sprinkles to make ‘em fun! (recipe and photo: Bake at 350)

3. No-Bake Cake Batter Truffles: You’ll only need a microwave and a food processor to whip up these funfetti-inspired treats. (recipe and photo: The Novice Chef)

4. S’Mores Truffles: Here’s a delightful spin on s’mores and truffles as we know them. These sinful little sweets hide a mini marshmallow in the center and are dusted with crushed graham crackers. (recipe and photo: The Comfort of Cooking)

5. Chocolate Cherry Truffles: “Ridiculously chocolatey, creamy, tangy… and messy to make. As in your hands will be covered with a thick layer or chocolate. It’s okay to lick.” (recipe and photo: Dash)
