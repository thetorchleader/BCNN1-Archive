Police ID Likely Homicide Victim in Washington State Without Finding a Body

August 30, 2014 Black

The blood found splashed on a pile of charred carpets and wood near Auburn, Washington, have been connected to a homicide victim, Brandon Shane Zomalt, with a criminal history, a KING-TV report says.

The blood-stained chunk of carpet found aflame in Auburn, Washington, has been connected to a homicide victim, a report says.

The blood that belongs to Brandon Zomalt, 30, according to DNA examined by the Washington State Patrol Crime Lab, has sparked a bodiless homicide investigation, according to KCPQ-TV.

The only evidence suggesting Zomalt’s death was a large amount of his blood found Aug. 13 on charred piles of carpet, towels and planks of hardwood flood.

Even though Zomalt may have been killed more than two weeks ago, nobody has reported him missing, KOMO-TV reported.

“The body is somewhere,” Cmdr. Mike Hirman of Auburn Police told KOMO-TV. “The fruits of the crime, if you will, were dumped here in Auburn and so we need to find out who this person’s associates are.”

He had an extensive arrest history dating back a decade in Washington state. He was imprisoned for pleading guilty to felony harassment charges in 2008 and 2012, court records show.
