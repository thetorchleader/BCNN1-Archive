Muhammad Ali Has Been Hospitalized Again

January 16, 2015 Sports

Muhammad Ali in 2011
WIN MCNAMEE/GETTY IMAGES

Only weeks after being admitted to a hospital, boxing legend Muhammad Ali returned to the hospital Thursday night. Ali was hospitalized last month because of a severe urinary tract infection, and now he has been readmitted for follow-up care, according to the Associated Press.

Ali’s spokesman Bob Gunnell hopes that the former boxing champ will be able to return home by Saturday to celebrate his 73rd birthday with his family.
