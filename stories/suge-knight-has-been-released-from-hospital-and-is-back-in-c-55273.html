Suge Knight Has Been Released From Hospital and is Back In Custody

February 5, 2015 National

Marion “Suge” Knight is out of the hospital and in the Los Angeles County Sheriff’s Department’s custody, TheWrap learned Thursday.

The rap mogul and founder of Death Row Records was released from medical treatment around 9 p.m. Wednesday, a LASD spokeswoman told TheWrap.

“He’s in the custody of the sheriff’s department,” she said, but didn’t clarify as to where Knight, 49, was being held.

On Tuesday, Knight  plead not guilty to murder and attempted murder charges in court before being rushed to a hospital for an unspecified “medical emergency.”

According to TMZ, Knight was suffering from chest pains where doctors treated him for a blood clot. Knight received blood thinners for his condition that can be monitored in the jail infirmary.

Before his hospital stay, the Los Angeles County District Attorney’s office say Knight ran over two men Jan. 29 with his truck following an altercation, TheWrap previously reported.
