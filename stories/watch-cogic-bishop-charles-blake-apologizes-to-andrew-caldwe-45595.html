WATCH: COGIC Bishop Charles Blake Apologizes to Andrew Caldwell and Rebukes Prophet Earl Carter for Preaching Against Homosexuality and Sin In the Church – What a Shame!

November 18, 2014 Christian

Bishop Charles E. Blake (Credit: Church of God In Christ)

This is a shame particularly in light of the fact that many of the pastors already knew that Prophet Carter had preached like this before in COGIC churches and the congregation and the majority of the preachers present were apparently 100% behind the message.

Presiding Bishop Charles E. Blake, Sr. of the Church of God in Christ (COGIC) made the following statement regarding the preaching of Prophet Earl Carter against homosexuality and others sins at the recent COGIC 107th Convocation which caused a young man, Andrew Caldwell, to get delivered from the sin of homosexuality and the resulting testimony that went viral.

What a joyous and inspirational experience it was to attend the 107th Holy Convocation for the Church of God In Christ in St. Louis, Missouri. More than 15 powerful messages were proclaimed to the glory of our Lord. 559 individuals accepted Jesus Christ into their hearts as Lord and Saviour. 97 individuals recommitted formerly their lives to the Lord Jesus Christ. 224 believers were filled with the Holy Spirit. There were 227 testimonies of miraculous healing and deliverance.

In addition to the wonderful spiritual blessings that we received, this convocation also provided physical, economic, and material blessings. Hundreds of residents of the surrounding communities attended our health fair where they had physical examinations and received advice from medical personnel. And also a job fair where corporations and employers came and interviewed hundreds of people on that day. 5000 community residents attended our Christmas in November event receiving groceries, clothing, blankets, toys, haircuts, medical exams. And then our delegates went on school visits in St. Louis and also sponsored community clean-up drives in that community. Approximately, 40,000 attendees brought millions of dollars into the economic system of the city of St. Louis and provided economic benefits to that city by our coming, more than 40,000 of us.

Prior to the Convocation, denomination leaders and members went into Ferguson to support and to participate in the struggle for justice after the shooting of young teenager, Michael Brown. During the Convocation, a special Pastors Forum was conducted regarding the incident, letters were read that were sent by us, by me, to the governor of the area and to other political leaders.

On Saturday evening, November the 8th, one of our more than fifteen speakers used terms and spoke in a way that was offensive and inappropriate. Though he alone was responsible for the nature and tone of his remarks, it was on my watch that this took place. Even though I was not present at the service on that night, as presiding bishop of the Church of God in Christ, I apologize for what seemed to be a harsh, uncompassionate, disrespectful spirit on the part of that speaker.

I also apologize to Andrew Caldwell, a young seeker for the Lord who came forth and made statements on that night regarding his change of heart and his purpose in life. His testimony literally went viral and it has brought upon him criticism and sardonicism. We extend our love, our prayers, and our support to that young man as he seeks to know God and to serve God.

We believe that we should reflect the love and compassion of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ in all that we do. And we do not in any way compromise our biblical position against same sex unions or in favor of the biblical teaching on matters of sexual conduct. But at the same time, we respect, or we expect, that our clergy and laity will be civil and considerate as they speak to men and women regarding issues related to our Christian faith. We love all people, regardless of their faith or their moral standard. And when we fail to express ourselves with love and humility we contradict the witness that we would strive to have to the world.

The Church of God in Christ wholly condemns acts of violence against and the subjugation of any person to verbal or physical harassment on the basis of their sexual orientation or their sexual stance. Such actions violate entirely the Christian’s obligation to love our neighbor as we love ourselves.

Blessed are the merciful for they shall obtain mercy. God will judge us all. If you have a love for God and if you love God’s Word, you are welcome to West Angeles Church and you are welcome to the Church of God in Christ.

Ecclesiastes 12:13 says, “Let us hear the conclusion of the whole matter, fear God and keep His commandments, for this is the whole duty of man. God will bring every work into judgment including every secret thing whether good or evil.”

Let the church say “Amen.”
