Settlement Reached In One Lawsuit Against Trinity Broadcasting Network

June 6, 2012 Uncategorized
The world’s leading Christian broadcasting network is now being accused of seeking to remove employees because of their age.
A settlement was recently reached in the lawsuit involving Trinity Broadcasting Network (TBN) and a former employee who said he was fired because of his age. The Orange County Register reports that Horst Brandt, who oversaw TBN’s computer systems, accused the ministry of age discrimination, among other things.
The lawsuit claims TBN leaders like Paul and Jan Crouch called Brandt “an old dinosaur” and referred to the building where he worked as “the old folks’ home.” The lawsuit also accused TBN leaders of making comments about Brandt’s German descent and comparing his work in the department to that of Nazi Germany. Brittany Koper, the granddaughter of Paul and Jan Crouch, affirms that the ministry was always looking for ways to remove older employees. Koper headed the human resources department while at TBN.
