Dr. J.B. Hardwick, Pastor and Elder Statesman of the Los Angeles Faith Community, Celebrated 83rd Birthday

November 30, 2014 Black

Celebrants at Rev. Dr. J.B. Hardwick’s (seated) birthday luncheon included (l-r) Bishop Richard Sanders, Rev. Dr. Marvis Davis, Rev. Dr. E. Wayne Gaddis, Rev. Dr. Dudley D. Chapman, and Rev. Dr Clinton Benton. (photo by C.J. Fossett)

“I feel fabulous,” said Dr. J. Benjamin Hardwick with a smile as he greeted members and friends at his 83rd birthday luncheon.

Renowned as an elder statesman of the faith community, Dr. Hardwick gathered with scores of well-wishers on November 24 who came out to salute his life, legendary ministry and social activist career.

In addition to serving 59 years as founder and pastor of Praises of Zion Missionary Baptist Church, he has also been on the forefront addressing social-economic issues to improve the quality of life for South Los Angeles residents.  The Praises of Zion complex houses a number of outreach and educational programs for the community.

Dr. Hardwick is also the president of the Western Baptist State Convention of California (WBSC) and the Rev. Dr. Marvis Davis, who serves as WBSC vice-president noted, “I thank God for Dr. Hardwick and our relationship.  I joined Praises of Zion in 1982 and he’s my pastor, my president, and my partner. He’s a great humanitarian and I thank God for the longevity of his ministry and his life.”

Sharing similar comments, Bishop Richard Sanders said, “He’s my president, he’s my pastor, he’s my friend. There’s never been a time when I’ve needed him that he didn’t come through.
