Tim Tebow May Fill In at Running Back for New York Jets

October 19, 2012 Uncategorized

It was just last week that the Jets found their running game, the previously missing-in-action Shonn Greene taking control of the offense and powering the team to a 35-9 win over the Colts. It was a needed push, a needed win, a needed spark.
Now, though, the Jets are looking at a roster decimated at running back, facing the distinct possibility that both Joe McKnight and Bilal Powell will be out Sunday against the Patriots.
So whom might the Jets turn to? Tim Tebow, of course.
Asked Thursday if the quarterback could see time as a rusher at Gillette Stadium, Jets coach Rex Ryan said, “That’s a possibility.”
“The thing about Tim, with him being a football player like we’ve always talked about, by playing quarterback he knows all the positions,” Ryan said. “So, can you plug him at running back? Can you plug him at tight end? I think the answer is yes.”
Offensive coordinator Tony Sparano was far less willing to discuss the option, saying, “There’s a possibility of a lot of things. We’ll see.”
Both McKnight (high ankle sprain) and Powell (separated shoulder) did not practice again Thursday, though Ryan was unwilling to rule out either for the game. He said the Jets would judge their availability as the week went on. Reports suggest that Powell will not play. McNight was quoted in the New York Post as saying, “I’m playing.”
If neither McKnight nor Powell play, the only two running backs on the roster are Greene — who had a career-high 161 yards on 32 carries against the Colts — and Jonathan Grimes, a rookie out of William and Mary who was signed off the Texans’ practice squad Sept. 25 and has yet to touch the ball in an NFL game.
“We have guys that can go,” Ryan said. “Obviously Shonn really took the majority of all the snaps, even on third down. Grimes has been here now for four or five weeks. There’s other things we can do.”
That brings up Tebow.
“I think you start with his skill set, but I think his skill set would be more based on power,” Ryan said. “I think he’s not going to be a blazer compared to other running backs, but he has power.”
