Bishop Eddie and Vanessa Long Honored With 2015 Spirit of Excellence Award

February 22, 2015 Christian

New Birth Missionary Baptist Church / Facebook

“The Spirit of Excellence Ascends in Atlanta”

(ATLANTA, GA – February 21, 2015) All roads will lead to 2101 Northlake Parkway in Tucker, Georgia
as E3 Magazine presents its 4th Annual Spirit of Excellence Awards. This black tie celebration
recognizes admirable leaders who have established and nurtured excellence in 16 professional
genres. The categories awarded include faith, business, politics, entertainment, and humanitarianism,
among others. In addition, there will a male Spirit of Excellence awardee and a female Elite Lady
awardee.

This year’s honorees include 100 Black Men CEO John Grant; Urban League of Atlanta CEO Nancy
Flake Johnson; CEO of TWD, INC. Thomas Dortch; Former President Columbus Georgia NAACP Edward
DuBose; Internet Business Strategist Chris Curtis Crawford; DeKalb County Judge Tangela Barrie;
Founder of Life Center Ministries Dr. Mary Crum; Assistant Principal – Bibb County School District
Dr.Carmalita Jones Dillard; Grammy, Dove and Stellar Winning singer/songwriter Billy Dorsey;
Renowned Dj Spinderella; Founder of New Birth Missionary Baptist Church Bishop Eddie L. Long; One
Vision Productions CEO Forrest Tuff; Grammy nominated singer/songwriter Deborah Hightower; CEO
of iYAGO Entertainment Group Janks Morton; and Musical Group DyNasti.

The host of the evening will be Tyler Perry’s House of Payne and Love Thy Neighbor Star Palmer
Williams and Actor/Producer Carl Anthony Payne (AKA Cole from the TV Sitcom Martin). Musical
entertainment by American Idol Finalist Frenchie Davis. Dr. Ambassador Juanita Bynum will be
making a special appearance. “I am honored to award so many wonderful individuals, who are true
pillars within the communities. It is our privilege to give credit where credit is due.” states Amy
Holmes, President/CEO of E3 Magazine and visionary of the Spirit of Excellence Awards.

The Spirit of Excellence Awards traditionally held in Atlanta will be in other cities across the nation in
the future. We have been fortunate over the years to share our nominee’s stories of triumph,
courage and hope. The 2015 Gala promises to be yet another wonderful night of inspiration.” Holmes
adds.

SOURCE: E3 Marketing
