LISTEN: Lutishia Lovely, Sharon Norris Elliott, and Antonio Aguirre Vila-Coro, on BC Book Review Show #269

May 24, 2015 Books

The authors that we are featuring today are: Lutishia Lovely, author of “The Perfect Revenge”; Sharon Norris Elliott, author of “Boomerangs to Arrows”; and Antonio Aguirre Vila-Coro, author of “Approaching the Canticle: St. John of the Cross for the Marathon Runner”.

The purpose of this show is to highlight wholesome Christian books, promote them to our audience, encourage you to buy these books for yourself and for others, and most importantly, to see people come to Christ and lives changed for the glory of God.

MUSICAL SELECTION: “No Greater Love” by Fred Hammond, “G.O.D.” by Canton Jones, and “O That Men Would Worship” by Stephen Hurd
