Oklahoma Police Officer Daniel Holtzclaw Who Raped 8 Black Women Faces New Allegations After 3 More Women Come Forward

November 5, 2014 Black

Daniel Holtzclaw in 2009, when he was a player for the Detroit Lions
NFL PHOTOS/GETTY IMAGES

Three more women have come forward to make claims against Daniel Holtzclaw, the Oklahoma police officer charged with raping eight black women while on duty, according to News 9.

Holtzclaw was arrested and charged with 26 counts of rape, sexual battery, indecent exposure and oral sodomy after women came forward in August.

Now three more women have alleged that Holtzclaw threatened them with arrest if they did not comply with his demands.

One woman reported that he told her, “You are going to give me some sex … or your ass is going to jail.”

Another victim said that Holtzclaw told her, “This is what you are going to have to do. I don’t want to take you to jail.”
