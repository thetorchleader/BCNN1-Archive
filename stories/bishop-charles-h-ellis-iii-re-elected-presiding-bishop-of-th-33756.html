Bishop Charles H. Ellis III Re-elected Presiding Bishop of the Pentecostal Assemblies of the World

August 14, 2014 Christian

2014 PAW Convention / Facebook

Election results from the 2014 PAW Convention, regarding executive officers in the general election, are:

* Bishop Charles H. Ellis III – Presiding Bishop
* Bishop Richard Young – 1st Asst. Presiding Bishop
* Bishop Theodore Brooks – 2nd Asst. Presiding Bishop
* Suffragan Bishop Donald Golder – General Secretary
* Suffragan Bishop Maxie Dobson – General Treasurer
* Suffragan Bishop J. Gregory Rodges – Asst. General Secretary
* Suffragan Bishop Craig High – Asst. General Treasurer

SOURCE: Pentecostal Assemblies of the World, Inc.
