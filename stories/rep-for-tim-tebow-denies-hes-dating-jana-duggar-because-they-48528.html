Rep for Tim Tebow Denies he’s Dating Jana Duggar because They Have Never Even Met

December 6, 2014 Entertainment

Clearing the air: Tim Tebow’s representative released a statement to USWeekly that he and Jana Duggar have ‘never even met’

Tim Tebow’s representative has made it official.

The 27-year-old and 19 Kids And Counting star Jana Duggar are not an item.

In a statement to USWeekly, the rep for the former Denver Broncos quarterback said, ‘Tim and Jana Duggar have never even met.’

Dating rumors began last month when Tim’s mom Pam met Jana’s parents, Michelle and Jim Bob, at a speaking engagement.

Star reported that Jim has made it his mission to set his 24-year-old daughter up with the football player.

The magazine reported that Jim and Michelle are so intent on matchmaking their daughter with Tim that they have even had ‘secret meetings’ with Pam at their home in Arkansas.

A source told the magazine: ‘Jim Bob is no dummy. Tim would be a perfect addition to the family and a feather in his cap.

‘Can you imagine the press attention a Tim-Jana wedding would get?’

One thing Tim and Jana, the oldest Duggar daughter, certainly have in common is their Christian belief, which both of them talk about openly.
