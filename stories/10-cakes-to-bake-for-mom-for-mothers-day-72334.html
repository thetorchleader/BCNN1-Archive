10 Cakes to Bake for Mom for Mother’s Day

May 6, 2015 God & Food, Lifestyle

Let her eat cake.

DAFFODIL CAKE
Freshly squeezed orange juice and zest gives this cake an intense flavor. You’ll love the sweet-tart cream cheese glaze. Recipe: Daffodil Cake

STRAWBERRY CREAM CAKE
Frosted with vanilla whipped cream, this layer cake has a delicious filling of fresh berries and strawberry jam. Recipe: Strawberry Cream Cake

CHOCOLATE-ESPRESSO ROULADE
For a coffee-loving mom, look no further than this impressive-looking espresso rolled cake. Recipe: Chocolate-Espresso Roulade

RED VELVET CUPCAKES
This updated version of the classic cupcake gets topped with a Creole-style cream cheese, made tangy with lemon juice. Recipe: Red Velvet Cupcakes

FRESH LEMON-COCONUT CAKE
Transport to the tropics with this lemon-filled layer cake with fluffy coconut frosting. Recipe: Fresh Lemon-Coconut Cake

PEANUT BUTTER SWIRL CHEESECAKE
Take your favorite childhood spread to new heights with this chocolate cookie-crusted cheesecake. Recipe: Peanut Butter Swirl Cheesecake

CHOCOLATE CREAM CAKES
Each of these individual cocoa powder-sour cream cakes has a middle layer of creamy melted white chocolate. Recipe: Chocolate Cream Cakes

PEACH-AND-PLUM ALMOND CAKE
Stone fruits take centerstage in this simple almond cake with lemon zest. Recipe: Peach-and-Plum Almond Cake

LEMON-VANILLA POUND CAKE
If you love lemons you’ll love this poundcake, which gets its strong lemony flavor from extract, zest, and juice. Recipe: Lemon-Vanilla Pound Cake

STICKY TOFFEE PUDDING CAKE
Make this date cake for the toffee sauce, a mix of brown sugar, butter, and half-and-half that gets poured over a slice before serving. Recipe: Sticky Toffee Pudding Cake

SOURCE: Delish
