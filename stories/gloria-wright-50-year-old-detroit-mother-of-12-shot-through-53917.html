Gloria Wright, 50-Year-Old Detroit Mother of 12 Shot through the Door of her Home and Killed

January 27, 2015 Black

Detroit Police are investigating the fatal shooting of 50-year-old Gloria Wright.

Wright, a mother of 12, was shot and killed in her home on the 15000 block of Parkside on the city’s west side Monday night. She was the only one home at the time of the shooting.

“I just want them to find out who killed my mom,” said Robin Hornbuckle, one of Wright’s daughters.

Wright’s killer fired multiple shots through the front door of the house where she lived with her boyfriend and three of her adult children.

Some of Wright’s children suspect she may have known her killer and was answering the front door when she was fatally shot.
