South Korea’s Myungsueng Church Marks 35 Years of Early Prayer Services

March 12, 2015 Christian

If you whine about waking up early for church, a congregation in South Korea might make you rethink those complaints.

The Myungsueng Church in Seoul has five morning church services beginning at 4:50 a.m. This year the congregation marked 35 years of rising early to pray.

CBN News joined with our Korean partners, C Channel, for the celebration. One attendee told CBN News Producer Stephen Little he’s been coming to the service for four years to be “spiritually enriched.”
