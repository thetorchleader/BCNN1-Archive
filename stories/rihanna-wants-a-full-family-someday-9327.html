Rihanna Wants a 'Full Family' Someday

November 16, 2012 Uncategorized

In a recent interview, pop star Rihanna told Ellen DeGeneres that a lot of men are intimidated by her and that while she is currently single, she wants a “full family” someday.
By confirming her relationship status Rihanna may have finally quelled rumors that she’s rekindled her romance with ex-boyfriend Chris Brown.
During the interview, DeGeneres asked the singer if she would rather be in a relationship before having kids or if she was comfortable being a single parent.
“I would prefer to have a full family,” said Rihanna. “I’m pretty sure I could handle it if I had to on my own, but that’s not what I’m striving for.”
