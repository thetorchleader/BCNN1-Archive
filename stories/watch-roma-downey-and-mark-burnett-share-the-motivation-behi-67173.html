WATCH: Roma Downey and Mark Burnett Share the Motivation Behind “A.D. The Bible Continues” Series

April 13, 2015 Entertainment

SOME BACKGROUND ON MARK AND ROMA
Mark and Roma have been married since 2007 and collectively have many TV acclaims.

Mark was born and raised in England and served in the British Army in the Parachute Regiment. Roma was born and raised in Ireland. Their passion and desire is to create productions that will encourage, uplift, and awaken another generation to the Bible. Knowing that in today’s culture this is a controversial subject, they still believe, “you have to step out there,” and while they are not trying to be experts when it comes to the bible, there is a message to get across. According to Mark, they want their work to “speak to all generations, genders and faiths.”
