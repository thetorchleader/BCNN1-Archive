Memphis Wants COGIC to Return Convention Location to City

November 20, 2014 Black

 

Evangelist Deborah Golden, from Hampton, Va. rejoices in the isle at the beginning the Order of Divine Worship at the FedEx Forum during the 100th annual COGIC Holy Convocation Nov. 11, 2007. A capacity crowd of more than 17,500 saints filled the building for the official worship and celebration.

Memphis has bid for the return of the Church of God in Christ annual convention amid issues raised by the church about holding it in its current location in Missouri.
