Pastor of New Zealand’s Destiny Church Criticized for Calling Cash-Covered Stage ‘a Sweet Smelling Fragrance’

December 5, 2014 Christian

Thousands of dollars strewn across the stage at Destiny Church has drawn the ire of critics on social media.

Photos tweeted by Bishop Brian Tamaki show a floor littered with $50 and $100 bills accompanied with the verse, “A Sweet Smelling Fragrance that is Acceptable to God (Phil 4:15-19) My God shall Supply all your need…”

Another tweet read, “The main stage at Destiny Church… this morning… littered with 10,000s of $100 and $50 bills….Attitude”.

Tamaki has previously denied suggestions that parishioners were expected to give exorbitant amounts of money, which critics claimed he in turn spent on an opulent lifestyle.

Reports in 2012 claimed struggling families had to take out personal loans to pay for church projects worth millions, while Tamaki was said to earn six figures as a direct result of his work with the church.

Pictures posted by Tamaki on Twitter during the weekend were met with derision from people asking where all the cash would go.
