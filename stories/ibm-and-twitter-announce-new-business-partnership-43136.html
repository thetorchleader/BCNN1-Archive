IBM and Twitter Announce New Business Partnership

November 2, 2014 Technology

At an IBM customer event held on Wednesday, computer electronics giant IBM and popular microblogging service Twitter announced a landmark business partnership, which can potentially trigger a revolution in the data analysis arena.

Under the terms of the transformative new partnership worked out by IBM and Twitter, the collaboration will essentially result in the amalgamation of Twitter’s vast networks of consumer data with IBM’s cloud based analytic analysis. The amalgamation will essentially underscore a three-fold approach which will help unlock the full potential of both platforms.

According to the information shared at the vent, the IBM-Twitter partnership will chiefly involve refinement of cloud based data; predictive trend computing; as well as full integration into new, emergent IBM-based cloud services.
