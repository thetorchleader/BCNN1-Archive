Deitrick Haddon and Michelle Williams Scheduled to Star In “Fix My Choir” TV Show

April 9, 2014 Entertainment

Deitrick Haddon is known for his unique, engaging style of gospel music and now the popular singer is continuing his expansion into the world of television. ‘Fix My Choir’ has just received a greenlight and is scheduled to debut later this year. The show was announced by Oxygen today, April 8.

The uplifting music-driven ‘Fix My Choir’ will feature choirs that have been struggling musically. Each choir will get a chance to be mentored by the Grammy-nominated Deitrick Haddon and Grammy-Award winning recording artist Michelle Williams of “Destiny’s Child” fame. Each week, the two will surprise a community, school or gospel choir and help them find perfect harmony both within their music and among their members. The series will show Haddon and Williams diving deep into the members’ lives to tackle the choir’s core issues including leadership, inter-personal conflicts, artistic direction and presentation, doing whatever it takes to get these choirs back on track with perfect pitch and new-found confidence. Haddon serves as one of ‘Fix My Choir’s executive producers.
