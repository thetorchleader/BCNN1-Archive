Man Dies After Being Stabbed Multiple Times Outside North Texas Mall

November 6, 2014 National

A man who was stabbed outside of Ridgmar Mall Thursday night has died, MedStar and Fort Worth police confirmed.

He had multiple stab wounds.

The man, reported to be 30 years old, was stabbed in a parking lot near the Old Navy and Dillards stores at the west Fort Worth mall just before 9 p.m. Thursday. MedStar reported that CPR was being performed at 9:16 p.m.

The victim was transported to Harris Hospital downtown, according to MedStar’s Twitter account.

Police investigators taped off a large area extending from the one of the mall’s west entrances near Old Navy to the middle of a parking lot where a red Ford Mustang had flat tires.

Police said it would be Friday before they released any additional information. Homicide detectives were processing the scene late into the night.

The mall starts its holiday season with “Santa’s Arrival Party” on Friday afternoon.

SOURCE: WFAA Staff
