Dr. Bobby Jones and the Nashville Super Choir to Perform Gospel Music at New Orleans Jazz Festival

April 14, 2014 Entertainment

Dr. Bobby Jones

The New Orleans Jazz Festival continues to be one of the largest music festivals in America with some of the top musicians in the world headlining the many back to back concerts throughout the day.

However, not only will Jazz Music be showcased but genres of music ranging from Gospel and R & B.

Dr. Bobby Jones and the Nashville Super Choir (NSC) will present Gospel Music under the Gospel tent on Saturday, April 26 from 3:55 – 4:55 PM.

Dr. Jones and his diverse choir also spend a great deal of time in Italy performing for the Summer and Winter Jazz Festivals. They will bring this same musical flavor to the New Orleans Jazz Festival. Bobby Jones and NSC are currently #5 on the national Billboard chart and will feature songs from his new CD that features Faith Evans, Howard Hewett, Ann Nesby, Shawn McLamore, Tommye Young West, and Renee’ Spearman. Fans from all over the world are expected to attend the three day affair with Dr. Jones #1 on their list.
