WATCH: Jackie Hill-Perry Participates In Panel About Homosexuality and the Church With Josh Moody, Kevin DeYoung and Justin Taylor

April 17, 2015 Christian

Jackie Hill-Perry, a spoken-word/hip-hop artist hybrid signed to Humble Beast Recordings, recently participated in a panel discussion about homosexuality and the church.

Hill-Perry is a professing Christian and former lesbian who, in 2014, married fellow poet Preston Perry and gave birth to their first child. She also released her Humble Beast debut album The Art of Joy.

Pastors Josh Moody and Kevin DeYoung, as well as Crossway senior vice president Justin Taylor joined Hill-Perry on the panel.
