Tina Campbell’s “It’s Personal” Album Release Has Been Delayed, But Fans Remain Supportive

May 16, 2015 Entertainment

The official artwork for Tina Campbell’s delayed debut solo album IT’S PERSONAL (Credit: Gee Tree Creative)

Binding the devil, speaking words of encouragement and staying upbeat: these are the reactions of Tina Campbell fans to her album delay.

One full week after the originally scheduled release date of her debut project It’s Personal has passed, the “Destiny” singer is explaining the setback.

“My plan didn’t go as planned for a May 7th album release. I sure didn’t intend to disappoint,” wrote the 41-year-old on Instagram.

“I suppose I’m learning about all the unexpected twists and turns of being independent, all while everyone, including me, watches and waits.”

One social media follower told Campbell, “God is just ‘putting something extra on it’—we will wait.”

That wait, according to Campbell, has no set period. “I wish I had answers for exactly when my CD will be available, but the truth is, I’m waiting & expecting just like all the fans,” she said. “When you’ve done all you can, you just rest in God’s plan.”

Another fan told her, “Its [sic] ok Tina. When it comes out it will bless the nations.”
