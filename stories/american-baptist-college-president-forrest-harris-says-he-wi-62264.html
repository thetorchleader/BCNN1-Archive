American Baptist College President Forrest Harris Says He Will Not Resign Over Lecture Series Controversy

March 18, 2015 Christian

American Baptist College, Forrest Harris

The American Baptist College is under fire for inviting an openly gay bishop to speak at its national lecture series.

Since 1924, the Nashville college has been catering to African-American students looking for a Christian education. The college played a major role in training leaders in the civil rights movement.

The college’s recent decision to invite a bishop in a same-sex marriage to speak at the school has outraged several pastors across the country.

“These pastors are protesting because Bishop Flunder is an open lesbian, openly and legally married to another woman, and a proud of aggressive advocate of this lifestyle,” said Star Parker with the Center for Urban Renewal and Education.

Tuesday morning, the National Baptist Fellowship of Concerned Pastors held a press conference in Nashville calling this a case of spiritual warfare within the Baptist faith.

“Satan has strategically and successfully sought to secure sanctioning of same-sex marriage in every sector of society, including our spiritual houses of worship,” said Pastor Dwight McKissick with Cornerstone Church.

But Dr. Forrest Harris, president of American Baptist College, remains unfazed by his critics. He said the responsibility of higher education is to be inclusive to all people.

“It is to expose students to world views, diversity of thought, diversity of philosophy, to expose them to critical thinking,” Harris said.
