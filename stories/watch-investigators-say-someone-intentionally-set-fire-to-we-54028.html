WATCH: Investigators say Someone Intentionally Set Fire to West Virginia Church

January 28, 2015 Black

A congregation is picking up the pieces after investigators say someone intentionally set their church on fire.

It happened before 4 a.m. Monday at the First Deliverance Church in West Side — just outside St. Albans.

Later Monday, the fire marshal officially ruled it arson.

Investigators say it appears someone broke in through the back of the building and set the fire.

That part of the building was destroyed. However, the actual sanctuary sustained smoke and water damage, but the structure itself may be salvageable.

Founders and pastors of the church, Howard and Estella Nappier, say someone broke in several months ago and got away with thousands of dollars worth of equipment.

The difference this time is that nothing was stolen, but the damage was much worse.

“Inside it’s horrible. It’s like: imagine coming home to nothing,” Latasha Nappier, the Nappier’s granddaughter, said. “The ceiling is gone, the walls are gone, it’s down to its original brick.”

The Nappiers started the church about 15 years ago.

“It’s just like someone kicked it down and set it to fire,” Howard said.

Investigators found several signs of forced entry, especially in the back of the church where the fire started and did the most damage.
