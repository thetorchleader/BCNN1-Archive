Florida Teen, Jamal Rutledge, to Be Honored for Helping to Save Police Officer’s Life

January 14, 2015 Black

Jamal Rutledge (center) and three Fort Laurderdale Police officers are being commended for helping to save another officer’s life.
NBC MIAMI SCREENSHOT

Five months ago, Fort Lauderdale Police arrested Jamal Rutledge —a juvenile defendant — for reported criminal mischief and burglary. Police took him back to the precinct where officer Franklin Foulks began processing Rutledge’s arrest. Foulks suddenly collapsed and instead of Rutledge bolting to freedom he came to the officer’s aid and now, Rutledge and three officers will be honored for their quick thinking and fast action that helped save Foulks life.

According to NBC Miami, during the incident last September, Rutledge and officer Foulks were the only two people in the room when the officer collapsed. Rutledge immediately began kicking the security fence and yelling for the officers.

“Sergeant Todd Bunin heard Rutledge’s cries for help and upon seeing Foulks clenching his chest, notified police dispatch to send help. Two other officers, Robert Norvis and Raymond Ketchmark, overheard Bunin’s radio transmissions and immediately responded to assist.” The officers cut off Foulks shirt and “used a nearby nearby Automated External Defibrillator to stimulate Officer Foulks’ heart,” according to the news station.
