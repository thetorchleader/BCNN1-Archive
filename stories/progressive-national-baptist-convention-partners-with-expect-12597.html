Progressive National Baptist Convention Partners with Expectations Project to Address Educational Inequality

May 20, 2013 Uncategorized

On April 24 PNBC President Dr. Carroll A. Baltimore, Sr. signed an partnership agreement with the  Expectations Project to address educational inequality in our nation’s schools.  
Nicole Baker Fulgham The Expectations Project President said, “we are thrilled to have the support of the Progressive National Baptist Convention and we are thankful for Rev. Dr. Baltimore’s leadership and commitment to eliminating educational inequity in our nation’s public schools. 
Many people of faith are looking for practical ways to put their faith into action around issues of poverty and equity, working to provide all children with a high quality education is one of the most powerful ways we can do that.”
