April Event in Dearborn, Michigan to Examine the Preaching Ministry of C.L. Franklin

March 8, 2015 Black

Third New Hope Baptist Church Sponsors The Voice of a Prophet: Examining the Preaching Ministry of C.L. Franklin For A NEW Generation

Event Description
The premiere national conference examinining the preaching ministry of Rev. Clarence LaVaughn Franklin, featuring dynamic workshops on preaching and powerful worship! Guest presenters include Dr. Alvin C. Bernstine, Bishop John R. Bryant, Rev. Tellis Chapman, Dr. Deborah Martin, Rev. Breonus Mitchell, Rev. Donald Parson, Dr. Nick Salvatore, Dr. Gina Stewart, Dr. Frank A. Thomas, Dr. Jacqueline Thompson, and Dr. Jeff Todd Titon. Pastor Edward L. Branch, Conference Host. Registration deadline March 31, 2015. #NextLevelPreaching at http://www.voiceofaprophet.com

Event Location
The Henry – Autograph Collection
300 Town Center Drive
Dearborn, MI 48126

Event Contact Person
E.L. Branch Pastor, Third New Hope Baptist, and Conference Host
