Great Gifts for Mothers Under $20

May 4, 2015 From Around the Block

SHOW HER YOU CARE
There is no one in the world like your mother. She gave you life and for that you should be eternally grateful. It’s nice to show your mom how much you care all year long, but on Mother’s Day it’s especially important to show your love and appreciation. Here are some ideas for affordable gifts from the heart.

From a framed photo to breakfast in bed, send a sweet message on Mother’s Day.

GOURMET TEA ($13)
More than a coffee alternative, tea is a ritual, providing time to sit back, relax, and enjoy a cup of comfort. As the world’s most popular beverage other than water, there are countless teas on the market, with many gourmet options for less than $20. Award-winning coconut white tea from the Art of Tea is a tasty blend that comes in a nice tin for $13.

HAND-WRITTEN CARD (FREE-$3)
This is one of the least expensive and most expressive gifts you can give. Use this opportunity to tell your mom in writing why she’s so special to you. Share some memories of when she taught you important life lessons or was there for you in ways you’ll never forget.

FRAMED PHOTO ($5)
A simple photo frame costs just a few dollars at a thrift or discount store. Rifle through old family photos or use a current favorite and frame it for your mom. She’ll think of you every time she sees it.

SLIPPERS ($15)
A practical gift that shows your mom that you’re thinking about her comfort. With over 3,000 options on Amazon under $15, you should be able to find a pair of slippers that suits your mom’s style, be it floral, furry, or funky.
