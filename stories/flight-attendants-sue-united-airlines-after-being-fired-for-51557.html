Flight Attendants Sue United Airlines After Being Fired for Insubordination

January 8, 2015 Business

Grace Lam and twelve other flight attendants had been serving for United Airlines for seventeen years. On July 14, 2014 when they were preparing a United Airlines Flight 869 when they got to know that a graffiti was discovered on the fuselage. The said flight had to fly from San Francisco to Hong Kong.

“She showed us the picture of the graffiti together with two faces that was taken on her phone. That’s the first time I saw the graffiti,” Lam said.

The graffiti had two faces and words ‘BYE BYE’ that were written in oil on the tail section that remains around thirty feet off the ground.

“I was scared. I was frightened,” Lam said. “I have flown as a flight attendant for total of 24 years, and I have never seen anything like this.”

Lam, along with the other crew, saw the act as a serious threat in wake of the disappearance of Malaysia Airlines Flight 370. But as per a 26 page complaint, the United Airlines didn’t deplane the Flight 869 and that the ground crew inspected just the auxiliary power unit that was near the drawings. They declared it a joke but the flight attendants refused to fly without a security sweep, and the flight was canceled as there was no crew available to fly with it.

In October the past years all the flight attendants were terminated for insubordination.

David Marshall, the flight attendants’ attorney, said that he believed the act was a moral mandate. He added that if such kind of threat surfaces, an airline needs to deplane and get all the passengers aboard off the plane. The flight should only be allowed to take off after a complete security sweep but the authorities didn’t do that.

Lam said that she didn’t have regrets. If the same happened today, she said she would do the same thing.
