Three Alabama Preachers Headline California Spring Revival

June 12, 2014 Black

Pastor Cedric Alexander

Three preachers hailing from Alabama headline the Spring Revival hosted by the Steward Board of Price Chapel A.M.E. Church.

The worship services take place on Wednesday, June 4, through Friday, June 6, at 7 p.m., in the Price sanctuary located at 4000 West Slauson Avenue in Los Angeles.  The theme is ‘Hear Our Prayer; Forgive Our Sin; Heal Our Land’ based on 2 Chronicles 7:14.

“Now is the time to renew and rejuvenate your walk with Christ by attending our revival. Our dynamic preachers will impart Christ-centered sermons to help you overcome the challenges of life,” said Pastor Cedric V. Alexander.
