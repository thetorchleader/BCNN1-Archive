Tina and Teddy Campbell to Renew Their Wedding Vows

June 17, 2014 Entertainment

Tina and Teddy Campbell

Wedding bells are ringing once again for reconciled couple Tina and Teddy Campbell. The award-winning Mary Mary singer revealed the big news on Instagram.

In a heartfelt message to her late father, Eddie Atkins, Jr., who prayed for the husband and wife before passing, the 40-year-old beamed about her healed relationship.

“Daddy you left a voice message telling us to ‘Be loving, understanding and forgiving’ two months before our marriage was greatly challenged,” began Tina in the very personal message.

“Guess God told you before He told me and I’m sure you prayed for this outcome. It wasn’t easy but I did what you told me.”

Instead of immediately walking away, which Tina contemplated, she said “Ted and I prayed and pressed our way through and God gave us a miracle. Wish you were here to see us renew our vows.”

The couple’s highly publicized marital woes played out on Season 3 of WE tv’s hit reality show Mary Mary, that drew millions of viewers weekly. For a while, it appeared that Teddy’s multiple sexual affairs would irreparably damage the union.

But such was not the case.
