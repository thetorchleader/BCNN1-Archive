Mother of 8, Kate Gosselin, Is Dating Again

January 2, 2015 Entertainment

Kate Gosselin and Jeff Prescott
Photo: AP / LinkedIn

Kate Gosselin may have a new beau in her life.

The “Kate Plus 8” star, 39, is reportedly getting cozy with Dreamstime CEO and founder Jeff Prescott, according to E! News.

The 51-year-old businessman, who is also a divorced father of three, has been seeing the reality TV star since the fall, sources tell the site.

The upcoming “Celebrity Apprentice” contestant, who divorced ex-husband Jon Gosselin in 2009, told E! News in 2011 that she’s become “open-minded” when it comes to love.

“If there’s anyone out there who wants to deal with us, that would be good,” she said, referencing her crew of eight kids.

In 2013, she said that finding a new partner to help raise her kids wasn’t a focus. “If someone is willing to do that, that could be the right person, but it’s certainly not something I expect,” she said, hinting that her life is quite chaotic.

“It’s a shocking life,” she added. “You can talk, talk, talk and to walk in my door and actually try that on and wear it, gosh, that’s going to have to be a brave person. I don’t know. Maybe I don’t believe that person exists.”
