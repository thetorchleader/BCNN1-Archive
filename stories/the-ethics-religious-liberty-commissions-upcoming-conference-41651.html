The Ethics & Religious Liberty Commission’s Upcoming Conference on Defending Traditional Marriage Is Sold Out

October 25, 2014 Christian

The Ethics & Religious Liberty Commission of the Southern Baptist Convention organized “The Gospel, Homosexuality, and the Future of Marriage” conference, which starts Monday in Nashville.
(Photo: File / AP )

A sold-out national conference aimed at equipping Christians to strengthen and defend marriage between a man and woman, amid a changing cultural landscape, starts Monday in Nashville.

The Ethics & Religious Liberty Commission of the Southern Baptist Convention also will train pastors and church leaders how to minister to same-sex couples and those experiencing same-sex attraction.

Speakers also will address how to build healthy marriages, discuss sexual identity issues with children and how to build common ground with people who have different views.

“We do have to do a better job of articulating a biblical version of marriage and sexuality,” said Russell Moore, president of the commission.

Interest in this conference exceeded expectations. Organizers planned for 700, but more than 1,200 pastors, church leaders and others signed up.

Moore said that “every single church, every single family” needs to equip the next generation on how to think through these questions and articulate a Christian perspective with civility and kindness.

“Often these conversations degenerate into shouting matches that don’t respect the humanity of the individuals,” he said.

In a video message posted on the convention website, Moore told a story about the questions his son had after meeting a boy who said he had a boyfriend.

“My first reaction is to shelter my son. He’s too young to start thinking about these questions of sexuality,” he said.
