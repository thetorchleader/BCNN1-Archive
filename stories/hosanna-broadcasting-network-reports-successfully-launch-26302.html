Hosanna Broadcasting Network Reports Successfully Launch

June 12, 2014 Black

Apostle Frederick K.C. Price and Pastor Fred K. Price of Crenshaw Christian Center, along with other Christian leaders, political and community leaders attended the launch of Hosanna Broadcasting Network’s (HBN) national broadcast at the 7th Annual ‘Breakfast with the Clergy’ on April 26. 

Apostle Price was the first to turn the switch on to start the national broadcast process to 14 plus million homes on RRSat, the Glory Star bouquet and ROKU.

“From the inception of our media ministry, Apostle Price and the Crenshaw Christian Center family have been in total support of everything we have done,” said Ms. Tersit Asrat, HBN founder and president.

During ‘Breakfast with the Clergy,’ HBN presented the Spirit Award to Pastor Price and Pastor Danny Carroll of Water of Life Church in Fontana.
