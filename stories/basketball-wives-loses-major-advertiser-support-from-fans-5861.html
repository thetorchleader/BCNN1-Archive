“Basketball Wives” Loses Major Advertiser, Support from Fans

May 11, 2012 Uncategorized

Anti-fan protests against VH1’s “Basketball Wives” are finally making headway with their campaigns.


Summer’s Eve, a feminine products brand, will no longer advertise on the program, the protest campaigners proudly claim on Change.org. Before the week is out, a second sponsor could ditch the reality show as well.
But the refusal to sponsor also includes Evelyn Lozada’s “Ev & Ocho.”
