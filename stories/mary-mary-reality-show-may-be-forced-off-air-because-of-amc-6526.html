Mary Mary Reality Show May Be Forced Off Air Because of AMC, DISH Networks Dispute

June 10, 2012 Uncategorized

Mary Mary recently announced that WE tv renewed their self-titled reality show for a second season. But a nasty dispute between AMC Networks and DISH Network, its carrier, spells trouble for Mary Mary and other shows on the WE tv network like Braxton Family Values and Bridezillas.  DISH has announced that it will drop the channel at the end of June.
As of right now, when you log onto wetv.com, you’ll immediately see a popup message that says, “DISH is dropping WE tv. If you are a DISH customer, you won’t be able to watch our shows…” The site also asks its viewers to “Tell DISH to put WE tv back on the air” by providing the information to contact the DISH Network directly.
On Sunday night AMC aired a spot informing viewers that they would lose the channel at the end of the month–a move that upset the DISH network. Subsequently, DISH moved AMC, normally in the channel 130 position, to channel 9069.
In a statement, AMC Networks said, “It is unfortunate that, in retaliation for an unrelated lawsuit, DISH is punishing its customers by threatening to drop the AMC Networks, and with this sudden, dramatic change in channel position, making it extremely difficult for their customers to find and watch some of the most popular and acclaimed shows on television.”
