Alicia Keys Joins “The Voice” as an Advisor

September 4, 2014 Entertainment

Alicia Keys will lend her talent to the upcoming Fall season of NBC’s “The Voice.”

Announced as an advisor, Keys has signed on to join Pharrell Williams and will work side by side with him to mentor his team of hopeful contestants.

As previously reported, Keys is working on her sixth studio album and is expecting a second child with Swizz Beatz.

Little Big Town (Team Blake Shelton), Stevie Nicks (Team Adam Levine) and Gavin Rossdale (Team Gwen Stefani) will step in as advisors during the anticipated season as well.
