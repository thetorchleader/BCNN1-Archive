SAD: 5-Year-Old Boy Shot in Face on Detroit’s East Side

January 27, 2015 Black

Detroit Police say a 5-year-old boy was shot in the face on the city’s east side.

We’re told the boy was struck by a bullet that entered the window of a home on the 20200 block of Irvington near 8 Mile and I-75.

He is currently listed in critical condition.

Source: WXYZ
