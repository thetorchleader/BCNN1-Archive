Monica and Brandy Remember Their Last Moments with Whitney

February 15, 2012 Uncategorized


Monica and Brandy were scheduled to perform at the Clive Davis pre-Grammy party, which turned out to be a somber memorial for the late Whitney Houston.

The girls, however didn’t appear that night on stage and instead took the time to remember a mentor and idol.
Couple of days before the formerly rivaling singers were scheduled to go on stage, they had a moment or two with Houston as she watched the girls rehearse.
Camera’s caught a little of the action.
“Whitney was at our rehearsal. She’s so funny,” Monica confirmed via Twitter. “She told me U KILLED that run u stole from me at the end of ‘It All Belongs To Me.'” 
Monica also praised Whitney in an interview during rehearsals.
“It made it feel good when she was in the audience…just reminding us and helping us with sound and doing different things today,” Monica said. “Their expertise–hers or Mr. Davis’–it can’t be found anywhere else.”
