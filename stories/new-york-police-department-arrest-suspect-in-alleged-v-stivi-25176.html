New York Police Department Arrest Suspect In Alleged V. Stiviano Assault

June 3, 2014 Black

(Photo: Mark J. Terrill, AP)

The New York Police Department has taken into custody a suspect in the alleged assault of V. Stiviano, a person familiar with the investigation told USA TODAY Sports.

The person said they were not authorized to publicly speak on the matter because Stiviano has not identified the suspect and the investigation is ongoing.

Det. Martin Speechley, a spokesman for the NYPD, told USA TODAY Sports: “The latest we’ve got is that we’re talking to a person of interest.”

Stiviano, at the center of the Donald Sterling saga after recording a conversation that caught the Los Angeles Clippers owner making racist remarks, was in New York for a scheduled appearance with CNN’s Anderson Cooper when the alleged attack took place Sunday.

Stiviano reported she was attacked by two white men who punched her in the face and yelled racial epithets, according to Speechley. The attack reportedly took place at 7 p.m. outside Gansevoort Meatpacking NYC, a $500-a-night hotel.
