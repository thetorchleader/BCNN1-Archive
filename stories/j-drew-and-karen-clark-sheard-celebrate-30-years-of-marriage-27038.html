J. Drew and Karen Clark Sheard Celebrate 30 Years of Marriage; DeVon Franklin and Meagan Good Mark 2-Year Anniversary

June 17, 2014 God & Family

Monday, June 16th marked a very special day for two Christian couples, Bishop J. Drew and First Lady Karen Clark Sheard, as well as Minister DeVon Franklin and actress Meagan Good.

The Sheards happily celebrated 30 years of matrimony, while the newlywed Franklins marked their two-year anniversary. Both wives took time to gush about their men online.

In an Instagram post, the 32-year-old Think Like A Man Too star said “I feel like I’ve run out of words and ways to verbally express how madly, deeply in love with you I am.. and how full of joy.. blessed I am that Dad saw fit to bring us into each others lives -before we were even a light in our parents eyes.”

After expressing that she was “thankful, grateful and honored,” to be Franklin’s wife, she added, “Thank you for making my world brighter. I love you in this life and in the next.”

Not to be outdone, award-winning gospel singer and The Sheards reality star shared her undying love for her mate on Facebook, telling everyone that, after all these years, they are still honeymooning.

“Today I Celebrate 30 Wonderful years of Marriage to my honey Bishop J. Drew Sheard!! I am the most blessed and happiest woman alive.”

She continued, “I am so glad to be married to this Amazing man, the honey is still in the moon!!!!! y’all get it? honeymoon lol!! I Love You Honey Muah!!!!”
