FBI Joins Investigation of Black Man Found Hanging From a Tree in Mississippi

March 19, 2015 Black

Law enforcement believes that Otis Byrd is the man found hanging in Mississippi.

Authorities have not yet determined whether the death is a homicide or suicide

The Federal Bureau of Investigation is helping local authorities investigate the death of a black man found hanging from a tree in Mississippi.

Authorities discovered the body around 10a.m. Thursday morning, according to Claiborne County Sheriff Marvin Lucas. The sheriff’s team and state officials were conducting a ground search for a man reported missing when they discovered the body. “We found him in the woods hanging from a tree,” Lucas tells TIME. “Whether it was a suicide or a homicide is yet to be determined.”

Jason Pack, a Federal Bureau of Investigations agent, confirmed to TIME that local authorities had contacted the FBI and Mississippi Bureau of Investigations to assist with the ongoing investigation.

The Claiborne County Sheriff told TIME they had not been able to positively identify the body, though authorities say the body was found about a half a mile away from the missing man’s last known residence. The family last saw the missing man on March 2; a missing persons report had been filed on March 8.
