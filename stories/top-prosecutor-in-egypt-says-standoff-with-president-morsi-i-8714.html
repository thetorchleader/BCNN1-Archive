Top Prosecutor in Egypt says Standoff With President Morsi Is Over

October 13, 2012 Uncategorized

Egypt’s prosecutor general tells The Associated Press he has reached an “amicable” agreement with the country’s leader to retain his job, days after President Mohammed Morsi ordered him to step down.

Protesters throw stones after scuffles broke out between groups of protesters in Tahrir square when chants against the new Islamist president angered some in the crowd in Cairo, Egypt, Friday, Oct. 12, 2012. Thousands of supporters and opponents of Egypt’s new Islamist president clashed in Cairo’s Tahrir Square on Friday, hurling stones and concrete and swinging sticks at each other in the first such violence since Mohammed Morsi took office more than three months ago.(AP Photo/Khalil Hamra) 
Prosecutor General Abdel-Meguid Mahmoud spoke after a meeting with Morsi and his advisers to defuse a simmering crisis with the judiciary. Vice President Mahmoud Mekki told reporters Morsi agreed to keep Mahmoud in his post following a request from the Supreme Judicial Council.
Morsi had ordered Mahmoud’s dismissal in an apparent bid to appease public anger over the acquittals of former regime officials accused of orchestrating violence against protesters last year. But the law protects the prosecutor general from being fired by the president. Although the move has public support, many feared Morsi was infringing on the judiciary.
Source: The AP
