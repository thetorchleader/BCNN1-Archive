WATCH: Greater Travelers Rest Baptist Church Celebrates 138th Anniversary with “Before Christ, After Deliverance” Song and Dance

November 18, 2014 Christian

The Greater Travelers Rest Baptist Church (The House of Hope, Atlanta) celebrated its 138th anniversary with a B.C. (before Christ) song and A.D. (after Christ or after Deliverance) song for all 5 generations.

The church said: Today we celebrated 138 years as a church family. The three day long celebration was EPIC. Friday’s You are the Hope Volunteer Appreciation Awards Gala was simply AMAZING. Saturday, we did what we do best as a family: Serve the community through our Do Something Saturday initiatives. Finally on Sunday we had an incredible worship service, generational dance and history mural unveiling. WOOOOOOOW!!! That’s the way to celebrate a birthday.
