Truck Driver Collides With Two Waco, TX Churches In One Accident

August 11, 2014 Christian

Staff photo— Jerry Larson
Bishop Michael Outland looks over the damage to his Greater Christian Tabernacle Church on Sunday morning after a pickup truck damaged the exterior of another church located across the street at 15th street and Maple Saturday night. The truck eventually needed up in his church knocking pews over and taking out the men’s restroom.

Two Waco churches were closed Sunday after an alleged drunk driver collided with both buildings before being arrested Saturday night.

Waco police said Socorro Camacho Marquez, 62, was driving a 1995 Chevrolet Silverado pickup when he “used faulty evasive action” to avoid a car in front of him, and veered into the front wall of the Victory in Praise Worship Center, 1501 Maple St.

After that impact, the truck “continued into the building at 1516 Maple, where it came to a stop inside the building,” a police report stated. That building belongs to Greater Christian Tabernacle Church.

No one was injured in either impact.

The pickup hit an area where Victory in Praise Worship Center has its Sunday day care, officials said.

A report by Waco police Sgt. W. Patrick Swanton said officers dispatched to the scene of the crash at 8:24 p.m. Saturday determined that a 2006 black Chevrolet Impala was turning onto Maple from North 15th Street, followed too closely by the Silverado driven by Marquez.

The report did not indicate that the Impala was involved in any wreck.

Marquez was taken to Baylor Scott & White Hillcrest Medical Center and then to McLennan County Jail on a charge of driving while intoxicated.

Atmos Energy and Oncor Energy shut off gas and electric service to the buildings because of damage.

County jail officials said Marquez was released Sunday on a bond of $1,500.

Said Pastor Linda Thomas of Victory in Praise Worship Center, an affiliate of Christian Rescue Mission Church of Houston, “For however long it takes to get repairs made, we plan to increase our outreach work in the city. We already take food, meals and bottled water to apartment buildings and other places we want to reach, so we’ll take the opportunity to increase that work and reach more people.”

Her church and Greater Christian Tabernacle Church have no formal connection despite their proximity to each other, she said. Officials of Greater Christian Tabernacle could not be reached for comment late Sunday.
