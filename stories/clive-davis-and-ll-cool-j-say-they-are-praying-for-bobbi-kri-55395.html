Clive Davis and LL Cool J Say They Are Praying for Bobbi Kristina Brown

February 6, 2015 Entertainment

In this Aug. 16, 2012, file photo, Bobbi Kristina Brown attends the Los Angeles premiere of “Sparkle” at Grauman’s Chinese Theatre in Los Angeles. (PHOTO CREDIT: Jordan Strauss/Invision/AP)

American record producer Clive Davis and rapper LL Cool J recently weighed on Bobbi Kristina Brown’s hospitalization.

Davis and LL Cool J are both busy with the 2015 Grammys but they did not let an opportunity pass without sending their love, prayers and support to Whitney Houston’s daughter.

In a recent interview with Entertainment Tonight’s Nischelle Turner, Davis admitted that he is heartbroken that the entire Houston-Brown family is faced with such a huge ordeal.

Davis also revealed that he was previously in touch with Whitney’s sister-in-law, Pat, to offer their entire family support and later on asked everyone to grant the family’s request for privacy.

Back in 2012, Davis, who served as Houston’s mentor, was also preoccupied with the Grammys when he received news about the singer’s death, Billboard reported.

Just like her mother, Bobbi Kristina was found face-down and lifeless in a bathtub filled with water just a few days before her mother’s death anniversary, CBS Local noted.

Meanwhile, LL Cool J, who will be hosting this year’s awards show also weighed in on the incident and admitted that the timing is very unfortunate.
