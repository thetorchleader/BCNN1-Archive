Rapper Kendrick Lamar Engaged to Longtime Girlfriend, Whitney Alford

April 4, 2015 Entertainment

Kendrick Lamar and Whitney Alford
JASON MERRITT/GETTY

Wedding bells are in Kendrick Lamar’s future!

The Grammy-winning rapper confirmed Friday that he is engaged to his longtime girlfriend, Whitney Alford.

In an interview with The Breakfast Club on Power 105.1, Lamar, 27, responded to rumors that he had proposed to high school sweetheart Alford.

“Yeah, definitely,” the rapper said. “I’m loyal to the soul.”

For the most part, Lamar has managed to keep his personal life private, but in a 2014 New York Times Magazine profile, the musician opened up about Alford, saying, “She’s been here since Day 1.”

He echoed those sentiments in Friday’s interview, describing the importance of always having “real people” around you.

“Everybody that’s been around me has been around since Day 1 and I can’t change that. I don’t change for nobody,” Lamar said. “People that have been by your side – you’re supposed to honor that.”
