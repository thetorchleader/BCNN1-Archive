Columbus Short Violates Restraining Order

May 22, 2014 Entertainment

The estranged wife of Columbus Short claims he has violated his restraining order by contacting her repeatedly, according to TMZ. Tanee McCall-Short’s lawyer claimed in court Wednesday that the actor contacted her on six separate occasions.

McCall-Short’s lawyer could have asked to hold Columbus in contempt, but instead the attorney asked the judge to make the temporary order permanent.
