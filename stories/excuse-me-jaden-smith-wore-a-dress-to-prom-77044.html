Excuse Me? Jaden Smith Wore a Dress to Prom

May 31, 2015 Black, Entertainment

INSTAGRAM

Sixteen-year-old Jaden Smith is all about making bold fashion statements. So it should come as no surprise that he rocked a dress on Friday when he took actress Amandla Stenberg to prom, writes E Online.

Sternberg, 16, who played Rue in The Hunger Games, shared on her Instagram page on Friday a photo of herself decked in a shimmering bronze dress, standing next to Jaden, who was sporting a black blazer over what appears to be a long, black and cream color block over-the-knee shift dress, accompanied by black skinny pants, sneakers and a white and gold fingerless glove on one hand, the report says.

Since fashion appears to be his thing, it’s likely not the first or last time that Jaden will be spotted in public wearing a dress. While attending another prom recently, Jaden sported a white Batman costume.
