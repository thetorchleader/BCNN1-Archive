After Being Released from Prison, Bernard Kilpatrick Faces Huge Restitution Bill

February 21, 2015 Black

Now that Bernard Kilpatrick has paid his debt to society, he still has another debt to take care of: a $61,776.89 restitution tab.

That’s what the father of former Detroit Mayor Kwame Kilpatrick still owes the IRS in restitution for a tax evasion conviction stemming from the public corruption case that sent his son to prison for 28 years.

The elder Kilpatrick, who completed his 15-month prison sentence on Friday in Texas, has paid $535.35 in restitution payments so far and owes nearly $62,000 more.

But that’s chump change compared to what his son owes: $5.2 million.

According to the U.S. Attorneys office, Kwame Kilpatrick owes $4.36 million in restitution for his public corruption crimes. He has paid $2,810 since getting sentenced in 2013. His original restitution order was for $4.7 million.

Add to that, the ex-mayor still owes another $852,745 in restitution to the city of Detroit stemming from the text message scandal that triggered his eventual downfall. His last payment was made on Feb. 22, 2013.

Kilpatrick’s co-defendant and longtime contractor friend Bobby Ferguson, who got a 21-year prison sentence, also has a hefty restitution bill. His balance is $4.1 million, but he has made one payment: $25.
