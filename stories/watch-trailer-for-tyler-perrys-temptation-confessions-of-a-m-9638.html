WATCH: Trailer for Tyler Perry's 'Temptation: Confessions of a Marriage Counselor' Released

December 23, 2012 Uncategorized

The drama hits theater screens on March 29, 2013.
Jurnee Smollett-Bell may have the lead role in Tyler Perry’s upcoming title, Temptation: Confessions of a Marriage Counselor, but Kim Kardashian is getting plenty of attention for her role in the film. 
The trailer for the spring 2013 drama debuted on the web, and it covers terrain that seems to be familiar for a Perry film. Temptation, according to the film’s IMDB synopsis, revolves around a marriage counselor, Judith (Smolett-Bell), whose life “becomes complicated after she enters into a relationship with one of her clients.”
Kardashian plays an employee named Ava who works with Judith. As her character describes in the trailer, the client, Brice (Robbie Jones), is “the largest social media inventor since [Mark] Zuckerberg.” The trailer’s titles tease the movie with the line, “Have you ever had the feeling that something was missing?”
