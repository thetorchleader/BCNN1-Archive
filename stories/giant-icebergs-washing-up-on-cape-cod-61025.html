Giant Icebergs Washing Up On Cape Cod

March 10, 2015 National

Giant icebergs composed of sea ice and packed snow washed up onto a beach on Cape Cod over the weekend. (PHOTO CREDIT: Dapixara)

A brutal winter’s worth of snow and ice is washing up on the beaches of Cape Cod in the form of enormous, iceberg-like boulders.

The giant, human-sized boulders of ice ran aground in Wellfleet, Mass., over the weekend. WBZ meteorologist Eric Fisher says it might be a “once-in-a-generation” event. We imagine Boston is hoping the same.
