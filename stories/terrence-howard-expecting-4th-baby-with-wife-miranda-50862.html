Terrence Howard Expecting 4th Baby With Wife, Miranda

January 3, 2015 Entertainment

Talk about starting the new year off with a bang!

Just after the stroke of midnight, actor Terrence Howard took to the stage to rap at Orange County hotspot AnQi – but the big moment of the night was yet to come.

Following his performance Howard was joined on stage by AnQi owner Elizabeth An, who revealed to the crowd that the Empire star and his wife Miranda are expecting their first child together.

Miranda – who wed Howard in 2013 – “glowed with joy” as her husband rubbed her belly and the crowd cheered after the announcement, an onlooker tells PEOPLE.

Howard is already a father to three children – Aubrey, 21, Hunter, 19, and Heaven, 17 – from his first marriage as well as grandfather to Aubrey’s 3-year-old daughter Hazel.
