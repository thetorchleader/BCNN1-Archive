Franklin Graham Says ‘Activist Judges Are Overturning the Will of the People’ Regarding Homosexual Marriage

October 14, 2014 Christian

Franklin Graham

While the recent decision to allow same sex marriage here in North Carolina has been a relief to many, for some, it’s been a disappointment.

For conservative leaders around the state they say that disappointment is with judges not respecting the people’s original decision.

“It’s sad when a judge is able to overrule the will of the people,” Franklin Graham told NBC Charlotte on Monday. “This is a democracy, and the people spoke, and we’re seeing that activist judges across the country are overturning the will of the people. We saw that in California. We’re now seeing it here in North Carolina now. I don’t know what will take place.”

Graham, President of Samaritan’s Purse, was at Charlotte Douglas with the group preparing to send supplied s to refugees in Iraq.

He also expressed his disappointment with the way Ebola has been handled in the U.S.

“Ebola is the most dangerous virus in the world and I agree with the nurses in Texas, who say we have not received adequate training. The CDC, I think, has been late on getting out the protocols and the training that is necessary,” he said. “They have to have the training and treating Ebola is not like treating someone who has the flu. You contract Ebola and it’s going to kill you.”
