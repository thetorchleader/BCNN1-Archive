Darren Sharper Still In Los Angeles Jail with No Bail and No Trial Date Set

November 7, 2014 Sports

Former NFL All-Pro safety Darren Sharper has been in jail in Los Angeles since February. He is charged with two counts of rape related to him allegedly drugging women he had met while out at clubs and then raping them.
(Photo: Nick Ut, AP)

More than eight months after he was booked into jail here, Darren Sharper is still behind bars with no opportunity for bail and no trial date in sight.

It’s the worst time of his life after an All-Pro career in the NFL.

But it doesn’t have to be this way. The former NFL safety could take advantage of his right to a speedier process, forcing a judge or jury to determine his guilt or innocence more quickly.

Sharper has done the opposite, delaying his case every month since May. It’s been more than a year since one of his alleged date rapes in Los Angeles took place.

“The defendant is allowed to waive time until a judge decides it’s time to set a real date,” said Jane Robison, spokeswoman for the Los Angeles County District Attorney’s Office. “It’s the defendant’s right to waive as many times as his attorneys feel they need to prepare. Judges in Los Angeles are pretty lenient about it. It’s not unusual in Los Angeles for cases to go a year or more before the actual (preliminary) hearing.”
