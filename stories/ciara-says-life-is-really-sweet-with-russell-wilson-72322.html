Ciara Says ‘Life is Really Sweet’ with Russell Wilson

May 6, 2015 Entertainment

Photo by Olivier Douliery/Getty Images

Ciara is dating Russell Wilson, the Seattle Seahawks hunky quarterback.

She’s giggly and vague when his name comes up, but plays the romance just right in the press, when talking to us about her new album Jackie.

Why the crazy interest in her relationship?

“I can’t tell you. I can’t tell you what the world thinks,” she demurs with a smile.

The two went public at the White House Correspondents Dinner. They looked super-hot together.

Ciara keeps it cagey when asked about him. But she’s definitely very, very happy.

“Life is really sweet. I’m having some pretty amazing days, for sure,” says Ciara.
