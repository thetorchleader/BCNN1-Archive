Bobbi Kristina Not Taken Off Life Support; Bobby Brown Speaks Out on Daughter

February 5, 2015 Entertainment

Guest correspondent Steph Bauer speaks to Bobby Brown — Access Hollywood

Access Hollywood’s Stephanie Bauer speaks with Bobby Brown (Access Hollywood)

Contrary to reports, Bobbi Kristina Brown has not been taken off life support, Access Hollywood has learned.

While it has been widely reported that she is on a ventilator, and remains in a coma, there was never any official confirmation that Bobbi is — or has been — on life support since being hospitalized last week.

Meanwhile, another source told Access that reports that the Houston and Brown families are gathering to say goodbye to Bobbi is “a flat out lie.”

On Thursday, Access Hollywood’s Stephanie Bauer exclusively spoke to Bobby in Atlanta.

“How is your daughter doing?” Bauer asked the singer.

“She’s good,” he said of his 21-year-old daughter, who he had with the late Whitney Houston.
