Jay Leno Receives Mark Twain Prize for American Humor

October 20, 2014 Entertainment

Just days after announcing his return to TV, Jay Leno accepted the Mark Twain Prize for Humor at the Kennedy Center. Here, he arrives with his wife, Mavis. (Photo: Paul Morigi, WireImage)

Jay Leno was treated to a night of laughs in his honor on Sunday night at the Kennedy Center where he received the 17th Annual Kennedy Center Mark Twain Prize for American Humor.

A star-studded lineup of top entertainers, including his Tonight Show successor Jimmy Fallon, showed up to salute the comedian — and of course to poke jabs at his expense.

Jerry Seinfeld, Kevin Eubanks, Chelsea Handler, Seth Meyers, Garth Brooks and Wanda Sykes all participated in a roast and toast of the former Tonight Show host.

“It’s a perfect night and all of his friends are here,” said Eubanks on the red carpet before the show. The former Tonight Show bandleader and Leno’s longtime friend called the comedian “really cool and relaxed…that’s the Jay I know.”

“He’s an institution,” said Handler. “He’s been so good to me. He was integral in me having any career.”

Fallon remarked on what sets Leno apart: his “work ethic…it’s unbelievable.”

As for the guest of honor himself, he was “excited. So many awards shows are kind of phony because you campaign for them, you give a donation, wink, wink,” remarked Leno. “(But this one) you have no idea it’s coming, you get a phone call and it’s great.”

Fallon kicked off the evening’s festivities, calling Leno “a trail-blazing comedian” and “someone to look up to my entire career…he showed me that hard work does pay off.”
