Here We Go Again – St. Louis Police Officer Shoots and Kills Black Man, More Protests Ensue

October 9, 2014 Black

An off-duty St. Louis Metropolitan Police Department officer shot and killed a man Wednesday evening in the Shaw neighborhood, near the Missouri Botanical Garden southwest of downtown.

The officer involved was off duty, in uniform, working for a security agency on a neighborhood patrol, according to Lt. Col. Alfred Adkins, assistant chief of police.

At approximately 7:30 p.m. CT, the officer encountered three men who started running after the officer exited his vehicle. The officer pursued one man, lost him, and then found him again after he jumped out from behind some bushes.

The officer approached the man and a struggle ensued. During the struggle, police say, the 18-year-old man pulled a weapon on the officer and started firing.

The officer, fearing for his safety, returned fire, fatally wounding the man. A gun was recovered from the scene.

The officer, 32, and a member of the department for six years, was not injured.
