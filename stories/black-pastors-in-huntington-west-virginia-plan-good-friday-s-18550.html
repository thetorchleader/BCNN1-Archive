Black Pastors in Huntington, West Virginia Plan Good Friday Services

April 18, 2014 Uncategorized

Bishop & Sister Gary P. Brydie (wife, Alesia)

The Huntington Black Pastors’ annual Good Friday service will take place on Friday, April 18, at Calvary Missionary Baptist Church.

The service begins at 6 p.m., and Dr. Franklin Murphy will preside, according to a news release. Calvary Missionary Baptist is located at 915 8th Ave. in Huntington.

All are welcome to come and join in the celebration. Guest speaker for the service will be Bishop Gary Brydie, pastor of The Glorious Church of God in Christ.

Source: The Herald Dispatch
