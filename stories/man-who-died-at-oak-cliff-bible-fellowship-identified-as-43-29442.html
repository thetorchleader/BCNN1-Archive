Man Who Died at Oak Cliff Bible Fellowship Identified as 43-Year-Old Linton Wilgus

Date not recorded in the original archive Christian

Dallas police are investigating a homicide at Oak Cliff Bible Fellowship. (Christina Rosales/Staff writer)

Oak Cliff

 

Police have identified the man who died on the Oak Cliff Bible Fellowship campus on W. Camp Wisdom Road as 43-year-old Linton Wilgus, who was from the Tyler area.

A member of the church was sitting in his car just before the chapel opened at 6 a.m. for scheduled prayer services, when Wilgus pulled into the lot in a gray Ford Mustang and approached the church member’s car, Dallas police Lt. Barbara Hobbes said at a news conference Monday afternoon.

The church member noticed blood coming from Wilgus’ neck and took him inside the church so they could ask someone – another church member – to call 911, Hobbes said.

Wilgus ran “aggressively” toward the other member, but was subdued by the member he had talked to in the parking lot, Hobbes said.

By the time Dallas Fire-Rescue arrived at the scene, Wilgus had died. A knife was recovered from the scene, but police said earlier that Wilgus had not been injured on the church campus.

“There is no connection to the church and detectives do not believe parishioners have any reason for concern related to this incident,” Maj. Max Geron tweeted Monday afternoon.

Neither of the churchgoers knew Wilgus before the encounter, and Pastor Tony Evans said he was not a member of the church.

“After talking to the family, it’s my understanding that he frequently came to Dallas, although, why, I do not know,” Geron said in a phone interview.
