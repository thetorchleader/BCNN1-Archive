“Christian History” Magazine Features Life and Ministry of Billy Graham In Special Edition

November 20, 2014 Christian

The special edition, titled “Billy Graham – Apostle of changed lives and second chances,” examines many of Graham’s milestones, his family and impact on the culture. Articles and rare archive photos throughout the special issue mark a legacy of tent revivals, television and radio ministry, and evangelistic outreach on a global scale. Graham has also served as an advisor and confidant to a number of U.S. presidents.

Michael Austin, a Christian communicator and spokesperson for Christian History, contends Graham has communicated the gospel message more than anyone else in history. “No man has had more personal, one-on-one, impact for faith in Jesus Christ than Mr. Graham,” he says. “His legacy has been significant in changing the American society.”

Graham has also risen above ethical and moral failures that have burdened many evangelists. As Austin points out: “Billy Graham instituted a strict moral code in his organization that he and other leaders would never be in a room alone with another woman without the presence of other people.”
