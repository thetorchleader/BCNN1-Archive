Dr. Phil Stages Intervention For Bobbi Kristina’s Boyfriend, Nick Gordon

March 5, 2015 Entertainment

Peteski Productions/CBS Television Distribution

As Bobbi Kristina Brown remains in a coma, her boyfriend Nick Gordon also appears to be fighting for his life.

In an upcoming Dr. Phil exclusive — titled The Nick Gordon Intervention — the 25-year-old opens up about his physical and emotional health, which have steadily declined since Bobbi Kristina was found unresponsive in a bathtub last month.

“As we sit here right now, my son’s life hangs in the balance,” says Nick’s mother Michelle, who also appears in the special. “When Whitney was found, he administered CPR to her and called me as he was standing in front of her saying, ‘Mom, why couldn’t I do it? I couldn’t get air into her lungs.’”
