Fred Hammond to Debut “Cain & Able” Stage Play In Dallas, Texas, Next Month

January 9, 2015 Entertainment

Fred Hammond

Fred Hammond is officially debuting his new stage play, “Cain & Able” which will grace Dallas, Texas theaters next month.

With numerous Stellar, Dove, and Grammy Awards, Hammond, 54, has proven his worth in the gospel music industry. After 30 years, Hammond recently released his latest album, “I Will Trust” last November and is beginning the new year by releasing another type of production.

“Cain & Able” is a modern re-telling of the biblical story of brothers Cain and Abel found in the book of Genesis. Hammond’s play tells the story of a wealthy business developer who leaves his two sons a fortune.

When Cain gets jealous that his brother is left power of attorney over his family’s estate, he resorts to sinful behavior and stops at nothing to go after what he wants, according to backstage.com.

Hammond took to his Twitter and Facebook page to promote the upcoming show that will take place at the Bishop Arts Theater Feb. 5, 6, and 8.

“Family in the DFW area. I along with ‪@KingOfDramedy‬ will be previewing our play Cain & Able for some national promoters,” Hammond wrote on his social media pages. “Come hang with us.”
