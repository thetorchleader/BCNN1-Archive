iPhone 6 to Go on Sale in 36 More Countries by the End of October

October 13, 2014 Technology

If you’re still waiting for the iPhone 6 and iPhone 6 Plus to go on sale where you live, we have some potentially good news.

Apple on Monday announced that the new handsets will arrive in 36 more countries and territories across Europe, Asia, the Middle East, Latin America, and Africa by the end of October.

In addition to landing in China on Friday, the phones will also arrive in India and Monaco. They will then arrive in Israel on Oct. 23, and the Czech Republic, French West Indies, Greenland, Malta, Poland, Reunion Island, and South Africa on Oct. 24.

The rollout will continue in Bahrain and Kuwait on Oct. 30, then reach almost two dozen more countries on Oct. 31, including: Albania, Bosnia, Croatia, Estonia, Greece, Guam, Hungary, Iceland, Kosovo, Latvia, Lithuania, Macau, Macedonia, Mexico, Moldova, Montenegro, Serbia, South Korea, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Ukraine, and Thailand.
