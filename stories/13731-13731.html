Image Books Celebrating 60 Years of Publishing

March 13, 2014 Books

Image Books is celebrating 60 years of publishing works by leading Catholic clergy and laity.

Founded in 1954 to provide quality religious titles to readers at an affordable price, Image has a rich history of publishing current and classic Catholic-interest authors, including Pope Francis, Pope Benedict XVI, Pope John Paul II, Cardinal Timothy Dolan, Cardinal Donald Wuerl, Archbishop Charles Chaput, Father Robert Barron, Mother Angelica, Mother Teresa, John L. Allen Jr., Raymond Arroyo, Colleen Carroll Campbell, Bill Donohue, Scott Hahn, Ronald Rolheiser and Teresa Tomeo.

“We wouldn’t be celebrating our 60th anniversary without you, our readers,” said Katie Moore, publicist for Image. “We’re deeply grateful for their support over the years and to their dedication to Catholic studies and spiritual growth and to show our gratitude we’ve created a small video tribute to our authors and books.”

To view the video, visit imagecatholicbooks.com/video.
