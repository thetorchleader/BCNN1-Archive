Tom Brokaw says the Brian Williams Scandal is ‘Really Serious’

April 16, 2015 Media

© Alex Wong/Getty Images for Meet the Press Moderator Brian Williams watches a video which pays tribute to late moderator Tim Russert during a taping of “Meet the Press” at the NBC studios June 22, 2008 in Washington, DC.

Tom Brokaw is speaking out on the Brian Williams controversy that has beleaguered NBC News — as well as his own relationship with the news anchor.

During an event held Monday at the University of Chicago’s Institute of Politics, Brokaw discussed the controversy that led to Williams’ six-month suspension from the “NBC Nightly News.”

“There’s been way too much speculation about it,” Brokaw said. “I want to say two things. One is that Brian and I had a cordial relationship.

“You know, it was tricky because he succeeded me, and I had my own strong feelings about how things should be done, but I never interfered with it. I would make suggestions from time to time.”

Brokaw’s remarks come nearly a week after Vanity Fair published a feature about the behind-the-scenes strife that led to Williams’ suspension.

One source claimed that Williams’ habit of lying or exaggerating about his reporting exploits came from feeling insecure about following in Brokaw’s footsteps.

“I always felt (Williams) needed to jack up his stories because he was trying too hard to overcome his insecurities,” an unnamed executive said in the story.

“And he had to follow Tom, which brought its own set of insecurities. He likes to sort of tell these grandiose tales.”

Brokaw’s annoyance with Williams’ “grandiose tales” reportedly caused friction between the two newsmen, with a friend of Brokaw alleging that he didn’t push for Williams to be punished in February but “didn’t try to save him, either.”

According to the anchor’s friend, a “bitter” Williams reportedly declared after his suspension, “Chalk one up for Brokaw.”

As for how the network is dealing with the controversy, Brokaw admitted “this is a really, really serious case, obviously.”

“There’s a process underway,” he added, referring to the internal investigation being conducted into Williams’ stories.
