WATCH: Comedian Michael Jr. Brings Hope Through Laughter

August 5, 2012 Uncategorized

Everyone is raving about Comedian Kevin Hart, but there’s another comedian with the “heart” of God. He goes by the name of Michael Jr. He’s hilarious, serves Jesus, and is mission-focused–something EEW Magazine loves.
Hailed as one of today’s most gifted comedians who has been compared to comedic great, Bill Cosby, Michael Jr. has delivered laughs to audiences on The Tonight Show with Jay Leno, Jimmy Kimmel, BET, ABC, Comedy Central, and countless ministries throughout the nation.  
“Over the years I’ve looked for times and ways to bring laughter to shelters, prisons, halfway houses…places where hope is in short supply and laughter’s missing,” says Michael on his website. “This gave me a true glimpse of the real power of laughter.”
