Conservative Radio Host Accused of Sexually Assaulting 3-Year-Old Boy

October 18, 2014 National

Joshua Kimbrell (Source: Greenville Police Dept.)

A Greenville man and conservative radio host is behind bars, accused of sexually assaulting a 3-year-old boy, according to police.

Greenville police said Joshua Kimbrell, 29, was arrested on Tuesday and charged with first-degree criminal sexual conduct with a minor under 11 years old.

According to arrest warrants, Kimbrell was accused of playing a game with a victim, inappropriately touching and fondling him.

Kimbrell hosts Common Cents radio show weekdays on 92.9FM/660AM and is the Chairman of the Board for Palmetto Conservative Alliance Foundation.
