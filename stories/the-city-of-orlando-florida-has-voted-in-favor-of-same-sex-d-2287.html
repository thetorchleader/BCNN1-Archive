The City of Orlando, Florida, Has Voted in Favor of Same-Sex Domestic Partnerships

December 19, 2011 Uncategorized

Supporters applaud a unanimous vote of approval for a domestic partnership city ordinance. Dec. 12, 2011
A unanimous vote Monday gave final approval to a new city ordinance benefiting couples by giving them some of the same rights married couples have.
But, the ordinance brings up new and serious legal issues to unmarried couples, gay or straight. 
It’s seen as a victory for the gay community.
The new ordinance will give the same rights married couples have to unmarried couples within city limits. Among those are hospital visitations or making funeral arrangements. Those who register will also be entitled to making decisions for their partner whenever he or she is incapacitated.
“It’s a great first step on the road to equality,” said Jeffery Miller, who has been with his partner for 29 years.
But this first great step may be bringing new and serious challenges.
“But you still stand the chance of getting arrested,” said attorney David Fussell.
Some legal experts believe the ordinance contradicts a Florida statute that states that if a man and woman, not being married to each other, live together; they can face a second-degree misdemeanor charge.
