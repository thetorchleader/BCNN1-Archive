Ward A.M.E. Church Hosts Annual Lay Day Service

May 17, 2015 Black

Paula Williams Madison

Ward A.M.E. Church hosts their Annual Lay Day service on Sunday, May 17,  at 10 a.m., announced Pastor John E. Cager III.  

The featured speaker will be Los Angeles Police Commissioner Paula Williams Madison. Madison is chairman and CEO of Madison Media Management LLC, a Los Angeles-based media consultant company with global reach.

In 2011, Madison retired from NBCUniversal, where she was executive vice president of diversity as well as vice president of the General Electric Company (GE), then the parent company of NBCU. During her 22 years with NBCU, she held a number of leadership roles, including president and general manager of NBC4 Los Angeles, Los Angeles regional general manager for NBCU’s Telemundo TV stations and vice president and news director of NBC4 New York.
