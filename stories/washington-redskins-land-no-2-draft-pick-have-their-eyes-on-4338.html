Washington Redskins Land No. 2 Draft Pick; Have Their Eyes on RG3

March 10, 2012 Uncategorized

The Redskins and Rams have released a joint statement officially confirm the trade: “In order to execute each of our club’s plans for free agency and the upcoming draft, we have agreed to a trade between our two teams for the 2nd pick in the 2012 draft. We will submit this trade to the NFL for approval.”

The Washington Redskins apparently are determined to solve their long-time quarterback problem.
The team has agreed to send three first-round picks and a second-round pick to the St. Louis Rams in exchange for the No. 2 selection in next month’s draft, the Rams confirmed according to the St. Louis Post-Dispatch. The deal was first reported by FoxSports.com.
The move cannot be finalized until the start of the new league year, which begins next Tuesday at 4 p.m. ET.
The Indianapolis Colts are expected to take Andrew Luck with the first overall pick, setting up the Redskins to take Baylor quarterback Robert Griffin III.
The Rams, who are committed to quarterback Sam Bradford, the first pick in 2010, will get the Redskins’ No. 6 pick and second-round selection this year and two future first-round picks. St. Louis now has the sixth, 33rd and 39th picks in this year’s draft.
