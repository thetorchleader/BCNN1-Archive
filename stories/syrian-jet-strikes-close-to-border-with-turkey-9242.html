Syrian Jet Strikes Close to Border With Turkey

November 12, 2012 Uncategorized

A Syrian jet bombed the rebel-held town of Ras al-Ayn a few yards from the Turkish border on Monday, Syrian witnesses said.


Smoke rose from Ras al-Ayn after an airstrike by Syrian forces on Monday.
“The plane appeared in seconds, dropped a bomb and killed children, here is total chaos,” Nezir Alan, a doctor who started treating the injured immediately after the attack, said by telephone. Local officials, quoted by The Associated Press, said at least six people were killed but some witnesses said the toll was higher.
“There are at least fifteen dead, and those injured have to be transferred to a medical center and we do not have any here.”
Ambulances were rushed to Ceylanpinar, a Turkish border town near the site of the attack, Haber Turk, a private news television station, reported.
Another Syrian witness reached by telephone said that the dead had been dismembered by the attack and all rescuers could do was collect body parts.
Windows of shops and houses in Ceylanpinar were shattered and people on both sides of the border were seen running in panic, while military vehicles raced down streets as a huge cloud of smoke hung over the area, Haber Turk footage showed minutes after the explosion.
There were no immediate reports of any deaths or injuries on the Turkish side of the border.
Clashes in Ras al-Ayn have intensified recent days, prompting thousands of Syrians to seek refuge in Turkey.
