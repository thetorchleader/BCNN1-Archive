Costco Employee Stabs Co-Worker Ahead of Black Friday Store Opening in New York

November 28, 2014 What is this World Coming To?

Costco (Photo: Eileen Blass, USA TODAY)

One Costco employee reportedly stabbed another before the store opened for customers Black Friday morning in Nanuet, N.Y., according to police.

The victim was cut across the abdomen and face with a box cutter in the attack, which occurred about 6 a.m. in the bathroom of the store, Clarkstown Detective Lt. Glenn Dietrich reports to lohud.com.

The victim was taken to the hospital and is undergoing surgery.

Police have identified the suspect and are searching for him.

Investigators believe the two workers had an argument last week that led to Friday’s attack.

No customers were in the store when the stabbing occurred, police said.
