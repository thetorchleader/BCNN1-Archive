ICRS Honors “Champions of the Faith” with Awards Ceremony and Benefit Concert

July 14, 2012 Uncategorized

Former Orlando Magic executive Pat Williams will be among those honored as “Champions of the Faith” during the International Christian Retailers Show being held in Orlando.

Pictured: Lysa TerKeurst was named the Champions of the Faith author honoree
2012 Champions of Faith Awards and Benefit Concert will be held from 7-10 p.m., Saturday, July 14, at the Chapin Theater at the Orange County Convention Center, Orlando.
The Champions of the Faith Awards and Benefit Concert will be honoring businesses, organizations, entertainers, individuals and Christian retailers and vendors who are “Champions” of the Christian faith. Award recipients live their lives dedicated to the principles taught by Jesus Christ in work and also beyond their professions.
Those being recognized as Champions of Faith are:  Pat Williams, founder Orlando Magic (Sports); EMI Christian Music Group (Music); Campus Crusade (Charity); Lysa TerKeurst, Proverbs 31 Ministries (Author); Ed Kobel, president of the DeBartolo Group (Business); Thomas Nelson Inc. (Christian Product Supplier); and For All Bible Center (Christian Store Retailer).
The event is a fundraiser for the Premier Foundation, which supports missionary activities, The James Fund, World Vision, Compassion Intl., and many other non-profit ministries and charities. The organization has distributed more than $3 million in grants during the past 13 years.
The event is open to the public. Those registered for ICRS may purchase discounted tickets. Tickets may be purchased at http://www.itickets.com/events/283846.html or by calling (800) 965-9324.
For more details, visit www.thechampionsofthefaith.com.
SOURCE: The Orlando Sentinel
