Police in Tampa, Florida, Turn to Black Churches in Effort to Diversify Police Force

November 20, 2014 Black

Tampa police Chief Jane Castor spoke at a church on Sunday.

As the Bible-Based Fellowship Church held its services Sunday, with shouted proclamations and smashing drums, Tampa police Chief Jane Castor watched from the pews before taking her own turn at the pulpit.

She joked with the pastor about her more subdued Protestant church, then addressed the congregation with a serious message about diversity: Her department needs to look more like the community it serves.

Castor’s appearance was part of a new effort by the Tampa Police Department to recruit more black officers by visiting local churches. Police plan to attend services at a variety of churches next Sunday and Nov. 23.

“The Tampa Police Department exists to serve the community, and it’s very, very important to us that we mirror the community that we serve,” Castor said.

As she spoke, officers sat at a booth at the back of the church with brochures offering information on police dispatch and officer jobs, as well as organizations like adult citizens academy and the Tampa police explorer program.
