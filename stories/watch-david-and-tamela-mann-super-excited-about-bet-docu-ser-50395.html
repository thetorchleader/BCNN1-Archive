WATCH: David and Tamela Mann “Super Excited” About BET Docu Series, “It’s A Manns World”

December 29, 2014 Entertainment

Wednesdays in January just got more interesting.

After 26 years of marriage, Christian couple David and Tamela Mann are hitting the small screen—this time, as themselves.

You’ve met the Browns, known for playing father and daughter in Tyler Perry’s productions. Now, you get to meet the Manns, the real folks behind the internationally famous “Meet the Browns” play, television show and movie.

“Super excited!! Can’t wait for everyone to see It’s a Mann’s world!!” said David and Tamela in an online message to their fans.

Their BET docu series, “It’s A Manns World,” formerly dubbed “Meet the Manns,” kicks off Wednesdays in 2015, only on BET. It gives viewers a look beyond their on-screen personas, exclusive events and famous friends, to their real downhome lives.
