Evelyn Lozada Shares First Photos of Son, Carl Leo Crawford

June 7, 2014 Entertainment

Basketball Wives star Evelyn Lozada basks in the bliss of new mommyhood with son Leo, her first child with fiance (and LA Dodgers player) Carl Crawford.
PHOTO: PHOTO BY CHRISTOPHER AMERUOSO

Months after the birth of her baby boy, Carl Leo Crawford, Evelyn Lozada is sharing the first photos of her “best kept secret.”

Lozada welcomed her son with her future husband Carl Crawford on March 22. This is Evelyn’s second child, but the first for her and Carl as a couple. Lozada has a 20-year-old daughter from a previous relationship and we’re told she’s as excited to be a big sister as Evelyn is to be a mama.

The Basketball Wives star managed to keep her pregnancy on the DL until late in the game when she could no longer hide that huge baby bump!
