U.S. Homeland Security Contracter Attacked by Hackers

August 7, 2014 National

U.S. Department of Homeland Security

USIS, a major U.S. Homeland Security contractor that provides background checks for federal workers, has been a victim of cyber hackers on Wednesday.

The FBI has launched an investigation into the security breach that was apparently an “external cyberattack” done by hackers suspected to be working for a foreign government.

Department of Homeland Security spokesman Peter Boogaard said the agency has stopped sending employees’ data with USIS for the meantime until authorities are certain that additional data will not be put at risk. USIS conducts background security checks on DHS personnel.
