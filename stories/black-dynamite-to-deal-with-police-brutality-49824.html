‘Black Dynamite’ to Deal with Police Brutality

December 23, 2014 Black

Tyler, the Creator and Erykah Badu
FRAZER HARRISON/GETTY IMAGES; RICK KERN/GETTY IMAGES FOR SAMSUNG

You can’t turn the television on without hearing talk of police brutality. It seems as though the summer of 2014 will go down as one when the police went gung-ho and faced no repercussions. Michael Brown, John Crawford III, Tanesha Anderson and Eric Garner are just a few of the people this summer who met their fate at the hands of those sworn to protect and serve.

In January, the subject of police brutality will be broached a different way. Adult Swim’s Black Dynamite will air a special musical episode titled “The Wizard of Watts” on the subject of police brutality. With characters voiced by Erykah Badu and Tyler, the Creator, the episode is bound to be provocative.
