Stacey Dash Defends Bill Cosby

November 22, 2014 Black

Left: Stacey Dash and Bill Cosby (Getty Images)

Fox News contributor Stacey Dash took to Twitter to let everyone know that she was never assaulted by Bill Cosby.

The actress said she knew Cosby when she was a teen and that he was a “perfect gentleman” towards her.

While some users thanked her for her input on the matter, many users fired back, suggesting she was attempting to discredit the 15 women who have accused the 77-year-old comedian of rape.

Rape allegations against Cosby began making headlines after fellow comedian Hannibal Burress used it during a stand-up routine in October.
