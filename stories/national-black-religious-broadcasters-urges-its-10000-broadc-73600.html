National Black Religious Broadcasters Urges Its 10,000 Broadcasters to Speak Out Against AT&T/ DIRECT TV Merger

May 15, 2015 Christian

Rev. Dr. Sheldon Williams

The National Black Religious Broadcasters (NBRB), a nationwide coalition of over 10,000 black religious broadcasters representing an audience-share of some 25 million listeners, has publically urged its listener base to speak out against the AT&T/ Direct TV Merger.

NBRB points to antitrust guidelines and prior consumer fraud charges as the top reasons why the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) and the U.S Justice Department should reject this dangerous merger. NBRB strongly believes in the government’s role of protecting vulnerable consumers, particularly those minority populations who have fallen victim to both companies’ fraudulent practices in the past. The advancement of this merger will allow AT&T-Direct TV to do what they do best – steal and overcharge.

NBRB is calling on all Christians to oppose this merger.

​Rev. Sheldon Williams, NBRB President, reflected on the situation saying “As President of 10,000 African-American Religious Broadcasters, I am doing everything in my power to speak out against this merger. It is NBRB’s moral duty to do so. Allowing these two companies to move forward would spell tragedy for thousands of already economically devastated customers whose monthly bills would skyrocket as a result. NBRB opposes this merger in the strongest sense of the word, and we urge all religious groups to speak out with us.”

Beyond the dangers of inflated charges, NBRB is exceedingly distrustful of the companies in question due to their ongoing federal monitoring mandates. Rev. Williams went on to ask, “How can the government allow for this? This merger would have deep and significant implications for the African American community in the U.S.”

Allowing the AT&T/Direct TV merger would perpetuate the deceptive, fraudulent consumer practices already rampant in minority communities. NBRB urges the government to disallow this merger and do everything in its power to stop what could possibly be one of the biggest telecommunication consumer fraud machines in history.

About NBRB
The National Black Religious Broadcasters (NBRB) is a national coalition of over 10,000 black religious broadcasters throughout the country who utilize broadcast and cable television, the Internet and radio to host their ministries and spread the gospel according to our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ.

NBRB truly believes in the Great Commission, the ethics and teachings of Jesus and His commandment over our lives. This verse of scripture is the central cause of our missionary zeal to spread the Good News through broadcast and cable television, radio and Internet. We intend to save the world with this powerful gospel.
