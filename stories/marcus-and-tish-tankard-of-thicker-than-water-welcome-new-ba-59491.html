Marcus and Tish Tankard of “Thicker Than Water” Welcome New Baby

March 2, 2015 Entertainment

The couple share that they’re “blessed beyond measure” about the latest addition to the Tankard clan.

On this season of Thicker Than Water, fans have been introduced to two new members of the Tankard clan: Marcus and Tish, who have moved back into Ben and Jewel’s home in Murfreesboro, Tenn. Now, their circle is growing even more!

The couple are the proud parents of a newborn child, they tell the Daily Dish. Marcus and Tish welcomed their bundle of joy on Feb. 25 in Murfreesboro. The baby weighed in at a healthy 8 lbs., 3 oz.

So how are mom and dad feeling?

“Our hearts are so full,” Marcus and Tish say. “We never thought we could love someone as much as we love our little angel. Baby Tankard is truly heaven sent-absolutely beautiful and perfect in every way. Mommy and daddy are blessed beyond measure.”

Marcus and Tish are keeping one thing under wraps, however: the sex of their baby. The couple will be revealing their newborn’s gender on next week’s episode of Thicker Than Water—so tune in to find out!
