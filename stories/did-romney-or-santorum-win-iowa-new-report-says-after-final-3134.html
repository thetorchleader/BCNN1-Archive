Did Romney or Santorum Win Iowa? New Report says After Final Count, Santorum Won Iowa by 34 Votes; Romney Braces for Possible Reversal

January 19, 2012 Uncategorized

With Iowa Republicans scheduled to announce the official count from the caucuses later this morning, Mitt Romney appears to be bracing for the possible reversal of his eight-vote victory of Jan. 3.
“The results from Iowa caucus night revealed a virtual tie,” Romney said in a statement released this morning.
The former Massachusetts governor released the statement after the Des Moines Register reported that “there are too many holes” from certified Iowa totals to declare a winner, though Rick Santorum wound up with a 34-vote advantage after recounts.
Romney scored an eight-vote victory over Rick Santorum on caucus night, but counting mistakes have been revealed in recent weeks, and there is a possibility that Santorum actually received more votes.
The paper also reported:
Results from eight precincts are missing — any of which could hold an advantage for Mitt Romney — and will never be recovered and certified, Republican Party of Iowa officials told The Des Moines Register on Wednesday.
GOP officials discovered inaccuracies in 131 precincts, although not all the changes affected the two leaders. Changes in one precinct alone shifted the vote by 50 — a margin greater than the certified tally.
The certified numbers: 29,839 for Santorum and 29,805 for Romney. The turnout: 121,503.
In his statement this morning, Romney said: “I would like to thank the Iowa Republican Party for their careful attention to the caucus process, and we once again recognize Rick Santorum for his strong performance in the state.”
