CEO of Citigroup, Vikram Pandit, Plans to Raise Dividend

July 16, 2012 Uncategorized

Citigroup Inc. is aiming to hike its dividend for the first time since the 2008 financial crisis.


Vikram Pandit, chief executive officer of Citigroup Inc., center, rings the opening bell from the floor of the New York Stock Exchange in New York, U.S., on Monday, June 18, 2012. Citigroup Inc. is boosting revenue at its corporate and investment banking unit in Asia with rising fees from debt underwriting and cash management as initial public offerings shrink. Photographer: Jin Lee/Bloomberg *** Local Caption *** Vikram Pandit Photo: Jin Lee, Bloomberg / SF
Bank CEO Vikram Pandit said he expects to start discussing returning some cash to investors by the end of the year.
“I believe we will be in good shape and have the capital to be able to do that by the end of the year,” Pandit told the Sunday Telegraph in a rare interview. “That’s a decision that will have to be taken with our regulators and we will have those conversations at the end of the year.”
The bank did not ask the Federal Reserve for permission to raise the dividend last month, when it submitted its latest capital plan.
Citi currently pays a token amount of 1 cent per share every quarter. Pandit had previously promised shareholders a higher dividend earlier in the year.
The Fed in March said the New York-based bank did not have enough capital to raise its dividend and also be prepared to withstand another financial crisis. That was a blow to Pandit, whose 2011 compensation package of $15 million for last year and $10 million retention pay was rejected by shareholders in an advisory vote the following month.
Citigroup nearly collapsed during the financial crisis and was rescued by $45 billion in bailout money from the government in late 2008. In February 2009, Pandit said he would accept a salary of $1 until the bank was able to turn a profit.
The bank has reported profits for two consecutive years now. It is scheduled to post its second-quarter results before the market opens on Monday.
Source: The AP
