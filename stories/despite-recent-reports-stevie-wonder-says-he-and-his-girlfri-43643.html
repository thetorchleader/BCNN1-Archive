Despite Recent Reports, Stevie Wonder says he and his Girlfriend Are Not Expecting Triplets

November 5, 2014 Entertainment

Stevie Wonder performs at the Calling Festival at Clapham Common June 29, 2014, in London.
BEN A. PRUCHNIE/GETTY IMAGES

For the last couple of weeks, rumors have been circulating that legendary soul singer Stevie Wonder was expecting triplets with his girlfriend Tomeeka Robyn Bracy. Wonder, 64, and Bracy, 40, who have been together since his divorce in 2012, have a 1-year-old. But Wonder confirmed on Wednesday that they are not having triplets.

“The truth is, we’re going to have a wonderful daughter, being born in December. Her name is going to be Nia, which means purpose,” Wonderstated on a recent episode of The View.

He went on to say, “Rumors spread, and like I said in the song ‘Superstition’: When you believe in things you don’t understand, you suffer.”
