Eric Holder to Speak at Interfaith Rally and Prayer Service regarding Ferguson at Ebenezer Baptist Church in Atlanta

November 30, 2014 Black

V-103 and WAOK will join Pastor Raphael Warnock and Ebenezer Baptist Church at “The Community Speaks ATL” service/forum on Monday, December 1.  All young people in the Atlanta Metro area from high school and college and young adults are invited to participate.This event is happening to allow our young people to engage in true intergenerational conversation in response to the Michael Brown grand jury decision and to discuss the greater needs of our communities.

There will be an interfaith rally/service/forum at Ebenezer Baptist Church from 6-10 pm. V-103’s Greg Street will broadcast live. United States Attorney General Eric Holder will make an appearance and address the community. Young people are invited to gather, speak and be heard, and pray together.

This will be an open forum that will hopefully unite the community for the work of social justice ahead.
