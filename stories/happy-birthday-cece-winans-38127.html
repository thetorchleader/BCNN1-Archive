Happy Birthday, CeCe Winans!

October 8, 2014 Christian, Entertainment

Singer CeCe Winans is 50 today

Happy Birthday, CeCe Winans!

Today, October 8th, is CeCe Winans’ 50th birthday. For over 30 years, CeCe Winans has blessed the lives of countless people with her musical gifts and her heart for sharing the love and salvation of Jesus Christ with the world. Besides winning numerous awards, including ten Grammy Awards and seven Stellar Awards, she is the best selling female gospel artist of all time, with approximately 12 million records sold around the world.

We at BCNN1.com love CeCe and the entire Winans family and want to wish her a special happy birthday. We pray that many more millions of people will be blessed by her music and come to know Jesus Christ as their Lord and Saviour. In fact, that is CeCe Winans’ wish as well.
