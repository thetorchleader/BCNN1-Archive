More Churches Accepting Electronic Donations as Alternative to the Offering Plate

March 26, 2015 Christian

As more people use credit and debit cards, churches of all sizes are now considering accepting electronic donations in addition to the time-honored tradition of passing the plate during worship services.

A lot of churches are going high tech, explains Jim Lynch of church consulting firm TechSoup.

“In my experiences,” he says, “churches are more and more, and fairly rapidly, adopting three or four ways for people to donate to their church instead of just a single way with the donation plate.”

For example, some churches set up ATMs where parishioners can swipe their credit or debit card.

“And it’s very easy to do and it’s very inexpensive to do,” the consultant observes, “and it just gives people another way to donate to the church in a way that more and more people are doing.”
