Australian Woman Who Was Hanging Up Laundry Plunges 9 Feet Into Sinkhole

November 27, 2014 What is this World Coming To?

Photo: County Fire Authority

Woman was hanging out washing when she fell into three-metre deep hole in Springvale in south-east Melbourne.

A woman has fallen into a three-metre sinkhole in a Melbourne backyard.

The 45-year-old woman was hanging out washing in the Springvale backyard when she fell three metres into a grass sinkhole, a Country Fire Authority spokeswoman said.

Specialist equipment was used to pull the woman from the hole about 10 minutes later.

There was no damage to surrounding buildings, the spokeswoman said.

It is not yet known whether the woman was injured in the midday fall on Tuesday.

SOURCE: Australian Associated Press
