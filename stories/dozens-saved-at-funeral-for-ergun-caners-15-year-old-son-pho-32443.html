Dozens Saved at Funeral for Ergun Caner’s 15-Year-Old Son (Photos)

August 4, 2014 Christian

Brewton-Parker College president and Baptist minister Ergun Mehmet Caner (r) and his 15-year-old son Braxton (l) in happier times. Braxton committed suicide on Tuesday July, 29, 2014. (PHOTO: FACEBOOK/ERGUN CANER)

Funeral services were held today, August 2nd, for Braxton Caner, the 15-year-old son of Baptist professor and author, Dr. Ergun Caner. Tweets from the funeral attendees show that in spite of the pain and grief, the Gospel was presented and dozens of people accepted Jesus Christ as their Lord and Saviour.

 

RT @ScottColter: Dozens saved today @ Brax’s funeral. We wanted the Gospel given. Pic: His football team after. pic.twitter.com/bxtoeJA9u1 — Ergun Mehmet Caner (@erguncaner) August 3, 2014

 

RT @AsaNWalker: Drake Caner, 9-years-old, spoke @ his brother Braxton’s funeral. So strong. Not a dry eye here. He’s my hero today//Mine 2 — Ergun Mehmet Caner (@erguncaner) August 2, 2014

-BCNN1
