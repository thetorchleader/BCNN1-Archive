WATCH: Erica and Tina Campbell Share Why They Air Much of Their Dirty Laundry In Public

March 4, 2015 Entertainment

Credit: Arise 360 Entertainment

As GRAMMY® award-winning sister-duo Mary Mary prepares for the Season 4 premiere of their hit, self-titled WE tv reality show, they are explaining why they have chosen to take their private lives public.

Aside from the career benefits of increased exposure, Erica and Tina Campbell say there are deeper reasons.

“I only know how to be honest and I only know how to tell the truth and I don’t feel embarrassed by my life,” said 42-year-old Erica during the pair’s interview with Arise 360 Entertainment.

“Whenever I’m down, because of my faith, I don’t believe I’ll stay there forever. So if something bad happens, I don’t mind sharing it, because, even if it’s not worked out at that time, I believe that God will help me work it out.”

She also added, “I think it serves as encouragement for people to watch, especially working moms, or people of faith, or people who are kind of [like] I don’t know if I want to do this faith thing, or I don’t know if I want to do this forgiveness thing, or I don’t know if I want to step out on my own. Do I want to take that chance?”
