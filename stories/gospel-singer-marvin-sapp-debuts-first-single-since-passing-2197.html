Gospel Singer Marvin Sapp Debuts First Single Since Passing of His Wife

December 16, 2011 Uncategorized

Gospel singer Marvin Sapp releases his first single, an emotional ballad, since the passing of his wife in 2010, after a battle with cancer. 

Marvin Sapp performs “Never Would Have Made It” at the 2008 BET Awards in Los Angeles June 24, 2008. REUTERS/Mario Anzuoni (UNITED STATES)
Making its world premier on the “Steve Harvey Morning Show,” Pastor Sapp entitled his newest addition to his long list of inspiring gospel classics, “My Testimony.” 
“I wrote a song that, you know, really tells about what I went through and how, now that I look back over my life, over…the last 15 months, I can say, you know what, I made it through this, because I didn’t think I was going to.”
The song carries a solemn yet resounding tone as the 44-year old pastor and now single father of three, speaks of the difficulty and pain that he experienced loosing his wife and co-pastor of Lighthouse Full Life Center Church, yet not loosing his faith through the trial. 
“So glad I made it. I made it through. In spite of the storm and rain, heart-ache and pain, still alive declaring that I made it through. See I didn’t loose. I experienced loss at a major cost but I never lost faith in you,” Sapp says in the first verse of “My Testimony.”
