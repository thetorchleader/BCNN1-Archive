Fire Burns Through Bible Study Classrooms at Historical Houston Church

January 5, 2015 Christian

A 3-alarm fire burned through two floors of bible study classrooms at the St. John Baptist Church in Houston in the early morning of Jan. 4. (Photo: Metro)

The blaze burned through two floors of bible study classrooms

A 3-alarm fire burned through a historical Houston church built in 1899.

The Houston Fire Department (HFD) responded to a fire at St. John Baptist Church at 1:45 Sunday morning off the 2700 block of Dowling Street. The fire had burned through the two floors of bible study classrooms before reaching the roof.

“Our church is a strong church,” said Pastor Terrance Grant-Malone. “Every firefighter said that the building was fortified. That it was a strong. It was God ensuring that his people were taken care of.”

No one was inside the church at the time and no injuries were reported, according to HFD. HFD Arson is investigating the cause of the fire.

Dr. Martin Luther King Jr., Barbara Jordan, Dr. Miles Jones and other prominent figures have spoken at the church, according to the church’s website.
