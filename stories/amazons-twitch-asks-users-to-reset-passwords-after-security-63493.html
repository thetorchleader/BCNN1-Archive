Amazon’s Twitch Asks Users to Reset Passwords After Security Breach

March 24, 2015 Technology

The Twitch Interactive Inc. website is displayed on a computer in San Francisco, California, Aug. 25, 2014. (PHOTO CREDIT: David Paul Morris/Bloomberg/Getty Images)

Amazon’s billion dollar baby, gaming website Twitch, has suffered a security breach that will require all users to reset their passwords, the company said in a blog post.

Twitch did not reveal the extent of the security breach other than to say that “there may have been unauthorized access to some Twitch user account information.”

All passwords and stream keys will have to be reset and accounts linked to Twitter and YouTube have been disconnected, according to the post.

Individual users who were believed to have been impacted were warned that their email address, password, the last IP address they logged in from, and limited credit card information may have been exposed, according to an email obtained by VentureBeat.

Personal information including a user’s name, date of birth, phone number and address may have also been jeopardized, Twitch warned.
