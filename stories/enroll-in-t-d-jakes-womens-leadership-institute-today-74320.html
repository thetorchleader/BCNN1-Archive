Enroll in T. D. Jakes Women’s Leadership Institute Today

May 18, 2015 Christian
The deadline for the Women’s Leadership Institute is approaching. Classes begin on May 21st and registration closes May 31st.

The Women’s Leadership Institute

For more than 25 years, Bishop T. D. Jakes has uniquely provided coaching and teaching to improve the lives of women around the world. In this program, he culminates years of study to provide the insight women need to successfully lead at executive levels.
The Women’s Leadership Institute provides the tools women need to understand organizational culture; utilize social skills; mitigate obstacles to the “glass ceiling” while mastering the secrets of her executive presence.Believe in yourself and start with small steps, and you will find your path to success. Enroll today for the Women’s Leadership Institute

The Pastoral Leadership Institute

Pastors today need dynamic leadership training. The breadth of their role from ministry to administration requires a broad range of leadership behaviors and competencies to be effective. With more than 40 years’ experience growing a church ministry to more than 30,000 members worldwide, Bishop T.D. Jakes shares the keys to effectively developing as a pastoral leader. Don’t miss this amazing opportunity to register for the Pastoral Leadership Institute.
Source: T. D. Jakes School of Leadership
