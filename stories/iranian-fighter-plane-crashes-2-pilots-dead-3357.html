Iranian Fighter Plane Crashes, 2 Pilots Dead

January 26, 2012 Uncategorized

Iran’s semi-official Fars news agency says a
U.S.-made F-14 Iranian fighter jet has crashed in Bushehr province in
southern Iran.

Provincial governor Mohammad Hossein Jahanbakhsh says both the pilot and the co-pilot were killed in Thursday’s crash.

The
Fars report says the fighter plane crashed due to technical failure and
that authorities have recovered the wreckage outside Bushehr, a port
city with the same name as the province. Bushehr is known as the
location of Iran’s first nuclear power plant.

Iran
purchased many U.S.-made planes, including F-14s before the 1979
Islamic Revolution and during the rule of the late pro-Western Shah
Mohammad Reza Pahlavi.

SOURCE: The Associated Press
