Pastor David Cross of The Liberty Church Continues Easter Trilogy

April 5, 2015 Black

Pastor David Cross

Pastor David Cross of The Liberty Church will launch his Easter Trilogy 2015 series with a powerful message entitled, ‘How to Move from Crucifixion to Resurrection,’ revealing the steps that will help overcome any problem.

The sermon will be preached on Palm Sunday, March 29, at 8:30 a.m., and 11 a.m.. Also, the Community Ministry will distribute free food bags; the Children’s worship will include a petting zoo, hot lunch and a jumper; and free CDs will be given to all guests. The CDs are  ‘The 2 Worst Mistakes Women Make in a Relationship’ and ‘Blacks in the Bible, the Truth.’

On Easter Sunday, April 5, three special worship services will be held.  At 8:30 a.m., the Hour of Power features the TLC Praise Team and ‘The Real Easter Message’ by Pastor Cross.  The 10 a.m., and 12:15 p.m., services will include a two-part Easter message.

“Part 1 will be ‘Why the Enemy Doesn’t Want You To Know the Real Easter Message.’ When I learned this truth, it changed my life forever,” shared Pastor Cross.
