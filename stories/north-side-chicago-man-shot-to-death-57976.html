North Side Chicago Man Shot to Death

February 21, 2015 Black

Police near the scene where a man was shot in the Sheridan Park neighborhood Saturday evening. (Gregory Pratt, Chicago Tribune)

A man was fatally shot in the Sheridan Park neighborhood Saturday evening, police said.

The man, 27, was shot in the 4600 block of North Malden Avenue about 5:05 p.m., said Chicago police spokesman Thomas Sweeney.

The man suffered a gunshot wound to the back and was taken in critical condition to Advocate Illinois Masonic Medical Center for treatment, but he later died, Sweeney said.

The man was on the street when someone fired shots, hitting him, and then fled in a small silver vehicle, Sweeney said.

Police News Affairs had no further description of the attacker or the vehicle in which he escaped, and was releasing no further details.

Shay-Ann Heiser Singh, an attorney, lives blocks away from the crime scene and was knocking on doors for an aldermanic candidate just before the shooting. She said the “unpredictable sporadic violence terrifies” her.
