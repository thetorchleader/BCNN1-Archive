Milwaukee Dad Kills 5-Month-Old Daughter and 39-Year-Old Man, Stabs 2 Others

January 23, 2015 National

Police found the baby and a 39-year-old man dead inside the house, along with a wounded 19-year-old woman and 22-year-old man. The suspect was also inside suffering from likely self-inflicted stab wounds, police said. (PHOTO CREDIT: JRN.com)

A father stabbed to death his 5-month-old baby in her crib, then killed a 39-year-old man and wounded two people during a Friday morning rampage in Milwaukee, police said.

Among the stabbed were the infant’s 19-year-old mom and her 22-year-old boyfriend, who remain hospitalized, Police Chief Edward Flynn told reporters.

The deranged dad burst into the home in the 400 block of N. 39 St. near Clybourn about 8:30 a.m. Officers said they found the suspect cowering in a bathroom with superficial stab wounds.

The man wanted police to kill him, officers learned as they raced to the gory scene, Flynn said.
