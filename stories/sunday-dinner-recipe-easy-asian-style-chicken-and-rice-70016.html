Sunday Dinner Recipe: Easy Asian-Style Chicken and Rice

April 26, 2015 God & Food

http://www.campbellskitchen.com

The right combination of convenient ingredients like frozen vegetables, teriyaki seasoning mix and instant rice make this super-easy chicken stir-fry absolutely delicious.

What You’ll Need

1 tablespoon vegetable oil
1 1/4 pounds skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
1 can (10 1/2 ounces) Campbell’s® Condensed Golden Mushroom Soup
1 1/2 cups water
1 package (1.25 ounces) teriyaki seasoning mix
1 bag (16 ounces) frozen Asian stir fry vegetables
1 1/2 cups uncooked instant white rice

How to Make It

1 Heat the oil in a 10-inch skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook for 10 minutes or until well browned on both sides. Remove the chicken from the skillet.

2 Stir the soup, water, seasoning mix and vegetables in the skillet and heat to a boil. Stir in the rice. Return the chicken to the skillet. Reduce the heat to low. Cover and cook for 5 minutes or until the chicken is cooked through and the rice is tender.
