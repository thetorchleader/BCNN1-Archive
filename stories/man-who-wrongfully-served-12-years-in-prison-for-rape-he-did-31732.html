Man Who Wrongfully Served 12 Years In Prison for Rape He Didn’t Commit Is Finally Exonerated; Thanks God for Sustaining Him

July 28, 2014 Christian

A man convicted of rape will have his name cleared thanks to DNA testing by the Dallas County District Attorney’s Office.

Michael Phillips pleaded guilty in 1990 to raping a 16-year-old girl. He served 12 years in prison.

Phillips was not seeking an exoneration, but a review of an untested rape kit revealed the DNA from the crime scene matched another man who lived in the motel where the rape took place.

Phillips’ exoneration will be the first of its kind.

He said he had given up on trying to convince people of his innocence.

He accepted a plea deal because his defense attorney told him not to risk going to trial, fearing a jury would not side with a black man accused of raping a white girl who picked him out in a photo lineup.

He currently lives in a nursing home and uses a wheelchair to get around because of sickle cell anemia. But that didn’t stop him from finally getting his day in court on Friday.

“I never imagined I would live to see my name cleared. Six of my siblings died from the same disease, so I thank God for sustaining me in prison. I always told everyone I was innocent and now people will finally believe me,” he said.
