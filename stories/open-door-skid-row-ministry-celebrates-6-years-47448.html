Open Door Skid Row Ministry Celebrates 6 Years

November 30, 2014 Black

Multi-cultural worshippers gather at Open Door Ministry’s sidewalk church.

It’s celebration time for the Open Door Skid Row Ministry as members of the sidewalk church prepare for their 6th Anniversary Service on Sunday, November 30, at 3 p.m., at 506 South San Pedro Street in downtown Los Angeles.

“We invite the Christian community to come and celebrate with us.  Also, pastors, ministers, evangelists, choirs, singers, ushers, praise dancers, and rappers are welcome to share their God-given talent at this blessed anniversary celebration,” said Evangelist Geraldine Hughes, founder/CEO.

Since 2008, Open Door Skid Row Ministry has conducted sidewalk worship services every week comprised of prayer, praise and worship, sermon and concluding with a hot meal for all in attendance.  The ministry also distributes clothes, blankets, shoes, socks, sleeping bags and toiletries when available.

Explaining the reason for founding Open Door, Hughes said, “I believe that in order to save someone drowning, you have to go where they are, instead of waiting for them to come inside your church walls.

“Through this ministry, we have seen more than 3,800 souls saved and many have left the street and moved into housing, shelters, rehabs, getting jobs, and returning home.
