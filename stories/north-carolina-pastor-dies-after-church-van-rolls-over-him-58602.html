North Carolina Pastor Dies After Church Van Rolls Over Him

February 25, 2015 Christian

Hendersonville Police and fire crews were on scene Monday morning of what they call a freak accident.

Police say around 9:30 a.m. Henry Andrew Lineberger, 69, of Etowah was seen changing the brake fluid in a church van at New Beginnings World Outreach Center when tragedy struck.

City operations personnel were in the area cleaning up and found Lineberger unresponsive underneath that van in the middle of Summit Circle.

Police say the church van was in neutral when they got on scene and the van rolled because it was on an incline.

Police think Lineberger tried to stop the van and then it rolled over him.

“If you look at the ground, there’s four spaces where there’s some ice and there’s tire tread in the ice right there. That’s where it was parked, and it went straight down the hill into the roadway. And that’s where it ran over the gentleman,” Sgt. Brandon McGaha, Hendersonville Police Department said.
