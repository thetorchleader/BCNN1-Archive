Congregation from Henry Lyons’ Burned Down Tampa Church Holds Services Outside

February 8, 2015 Black

The congregation at New Salem Missionary Baptist Church in Hyde Park spent most of the day Saturday setting up tents so they could hold their Sunday services.

A Tampa church destroyed by a fire this week is working hard to create a place of worship for its Sunday services.

The congregation at New Salem Missionary Baptist Church in Hyde Park spent most of the day Saturday setting up tents.

Church members plan on attending service tomorrow right next to the shell of what used to be their place of worship.

Firefighters discovered flames shooting from the roof of the church, located at 405 N Oregon Ave, early Monday morning. The roof over the large red brick building collapsed, but no injuries were reported.

The church is home to more than 300 members.

“Our church is moving forward,” said member Raymond Melendez. “Regardless of what it is we’re strong Christians — we’re moving forward. We’re not going to let something like this stop us from moving forward.”

“God does things his way,” said Deacon John Billups. “And I know this is one of the ways. We were in the process of moving anyway and I think he’s pushing things along.”
