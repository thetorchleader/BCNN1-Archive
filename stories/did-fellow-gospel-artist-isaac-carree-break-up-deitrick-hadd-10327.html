Did Fellow Gospel Artist Isaac Carree Break Up Deitrick Haddon's Marriage?

January 23, 2013 Uncategorized

A public shaming is a powerful thing. Deitrick Haddon’s public Facebook post accusing Isaac Carree of an adulterous affair with ex-wife Damita Haddon, has drawn the accused out of his neutral corner–well, sort of.
In an exclusive statement to media personality Jawn Murray, which is posted on his AlwaysAList.com website, the “In the Middle” crooner addresses what he calls Haddon’s “mudslinging,” while neither confirming nor denying wrongdoing.
“It has been brought to my attention that Deitrick Haddon recently made some very hurtful statements about me. I, as well as my wife, are both shocked and confused by Deitrick’s actions,” Carree said.
“It saddens me that he would seek to engage in mudslinging with me, especially because he and I have spoken openly and honestly about this, even long before his allegations.”
Still, Carree is not comfortable disclosing the nature of those private discussions. “While I have no desire to drag these personal conversations into a public forum, he and I both know what the truth is. Still, I pray God’s best for him and his family,” said the artist who is presently promoting his latest single, “Clean This House”–a song that admits wrongdoing and asks for sanctification and cleansing from sin.
