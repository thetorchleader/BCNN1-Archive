Cousin Arrested and Charged with Second-Degree Manslaughter After 6-Year-Old Girl Is Found Dead due to Hypothermia Outside her Home

April 9, 2014 Uncategorized

BEMIDJI POLICE DEPARMENT
It remains unclear how long Mercedes Mayfield, 6, was outside before dying from hypothermia.

Mercedes Mayfield, 6, was found unconscious in an entrance to a Bemidji apartment building on Feb. 27. Her cousin, Rachel Downer, faces charges of second-degree manslaughter and felony neglect of a child.

The cousin of a six-year-old girl who froze to death outside her home in temperatures of -30 degrees has been arrested in connection with her death.

Tiny Mercedes Mayfield was discovered unconscious in the entrance to an apartment building the morning of February 27 in Bemidji, Minnesota.

Windchill drove the temperature down to -40 degrees.
