Dan Aykroyd to Make Donation to Family of Slain Philadelphia Police Officer

March 8, 2015 National

Dan Aykroyd has said he will donate to the family of a slain police officer in Philadelphia. (PHOTO CREDIT: Graeme Robertson)

The actor Dan Aykroyd said on Saturday he planned to make a donation to the family of a Philadelphia police officer who was killed in the line of duty this week.

KYW-TV reported that the actor made the announcement while visiting the Philadelphia Flower Show to promote his vodka company on Friday.

Officer Robert Wilson III was buying a video game for his son while on duty on Thursday, police said, when two brothers entered the store to rob it. Police said Wilson exchanged fire with the suspects and was shot multiple times in the body and head.

Police officials said the eight-year veteran saved lives by drawing fire away from other patrons in the store.

One of the two brothers was arraigned on murder charges on Saturday. Twenty-four-year-old Ramone Williams was arraigned early in the day. His brother, 29-year-old Carlton Hipps, remains hospitalised.

It was unclear if either suspect had an attorney who could comment on the charges.

SOURCE: The Associated Press
