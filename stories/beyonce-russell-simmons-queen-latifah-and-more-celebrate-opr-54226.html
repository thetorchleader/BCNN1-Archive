Beyonce, Russell Simmons, Queen Latifah and More Celebrate Oprah Winfrey’s 61st Birthday

January 29, 2015 Entertainment

Friends in high places: Beyoncé and Oprah Winfrey pictured together in 2013 in New York City

When it comes to celebrity friends, there is no question that Oprah Winfrey takes the cake.

The well-connected talk show legend was wished a happy 61st birthday on Thursday, by a slew of stars including Beyonce.

Beyonce posted a childhood picture of Oprah with a special happy birthday written across it.

Meanwhile Russell Simmons called Oprah a ‘true global titan’ in a birthday tweet to the star.

Precious actress Gabourey Sidibe added: ‘It’s Thursday AND its @Oprah ‘s birthday!!! Happy Birthday!! #OBT #OBackThursday.’

Ellen DeGeneres, Queen Latifah and Gloria Estefan also joined in with the celebratory messages on Twitter.
