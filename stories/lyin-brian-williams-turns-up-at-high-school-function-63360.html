Lyin’ Brian Williams Turns Up at High School Function

March 24, 2015 National

NBC anchor Brian Williams, center, at a March 21, 2015 fund-raiser for his high school with former classmates Cheryl Salayi Bucko and Karen Miller Timmo. (Photo: Courtesy of Joseph Buzzanco)

NBC Nightly News anchor Brian Williams made his first public appearance since being suspended from the network, donating $50,000 at a fundraiser to help his Catholic high school alma mater stay open.

Williams, who was suspended Feb. 10 when questions arose after he said enemy fire had hit his helicopter in a 2003 trip to Iraq and forced it down, came Saturday to the Save the Seraphs Gala at Shore Casino in Atlantic Highlands, N.J. Earlier this year, the Rev. Jeff Kegley announced that supporters must raise $1 million in two months to keep Mater Dei Prep School in Middletown, N.J., from closing in June.

“This is Mater Dei. It’s been a very, very important part of my life,” Williams told The (Red Bank, N.J.) Two River Times at the gala. “It’s a wonderful school, and it should be open for a long time, for everybody.”

Williams, a 1977 graduate, and his wife, Jane, attended the $125-a-ticket gala that raised $95,000 for The Seraph’s Fund. The fundraising campaign’s total now has reached $962,000.

“He was greeted with warm hugs,” gala chair Maria Buzzanco told The Two River Times. “Everyone was telling him how much they support him as well and thanked him for coming.”

The Seraph’s Fund achieved another key milestone last week, earning 501(c)3 status, which allows it to collect the binding pledges made in the past seven weeks.
