The Great Patriots are Concerned About Tim Tebow Magic

January 11, 2012 Uncategorized

The New England Patriots hope to end their recent history of slow starts in order to avoid the perils of Tebow Time in Saturday night’s divisional playoff game against the visiting Denver Broncos.
“We can’t fall behind early,” Pro Bowl nose tackle Vince Wilfork said. “It will be a long day if we do that.”
Top-seeded New England (13-3) was plagued by slow starts at the end of the regular season. It allowed Denver to score on its first three possessions and build a 16-7 lead before Broncos turnovers — two fumbles and a muffed punt — led to a 41-23 Patriots road victory Dec. 18.
The Patriots trailed the Miami Dolphins 17-0 at halftime Dec. 24 but rallied to win 27-24. They spotted the Buffalo Bills 21 points before storming back for a 49-21 win in the regular-season finale. Both came at home.
Coach Bill Belichick isn’t sure what else to do to keep the Patriots from living so dangerously.
“We talk about it every week. We always want to get off to a good start,” Belichick said. “We always want to play 60 minutes.”
