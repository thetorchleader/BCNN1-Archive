Adrian Peterson’s Charity Funds Allegedly Linked to Orgy

October 7, 2014 Sports

Adrian Peterson of the Minnesota Vikings looks on during a Dec. 15, 2013, game against the Philadelphia Eagles in Minneapolis.
HANNAH FOSLIEN/GETTY IMAGES

The Minnesota Vikings running back, currently facing child-abuse charges, is the subject of a report that claims his All Day Foundation charity funds were used to pay for a hotel room where the alleged romp took place.   Minnesota Vikings running back Adrian Peterson, currently facing child-abuse charges and banned from the team, is now the subject of a new set of allegations.

According to a Minnesota Star-Tribune report released Monday, in 2011 Peterson reportedly participated in an “orgy” at a hotel and Peterson’s All Day Foundation charity paid for the hotel room.

A 38-page report viewed by the Star-Tribune claims that Peterson, his brother, who was underage at the time of the reported incident, and a relative of Peterson, Chris Brown, allegedly rented the hotel room with four women.

The report claims that one of the woman got upset with Peterson after she saw him having sex with another woman. An hour-long argument ensued that included the woman telling Peterson that she was “emotionally attached” to the All-Pro running back. Peterson reportedly told the woman that he was engaged to a different woman and had a baby on the way.
