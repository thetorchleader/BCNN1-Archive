New Report says Walter Scott Was Behind on Child Support, and Had Been to Jail 3 Times Before for the Same Issue, but No Warrant Was Issued for his Arrest

April 10, 2015 Black

People participate in a rally to protest the death of Walter Scott, who was killed by police in a shooting, outside City Hall on April 8, 2015, in North Charleston, S.C. Video captured by a bystander showed now-fired Police Officer Michael Slager shooting Scott as he ran away. Slager has been charged with murder as a result of the incident.
RICHARD ELLIS/GETTY IMAGES

Walter Scott, the 50-year-old South Carolina man shot and killed by now-former North Charleston Police Office Michael Slager was indeed behind on his child support, but there was no actual warrant issued for his arrest, the Associated Press reports.

According to the report, counting court fees, Scott owed more than $7,800 (almost $7,500 in child support alone—though CNN reports that Scott owed more than $18,000 in back child support payments), with court documents showing that he last paid in the summer of 2012. AP discovered there was no bench warrant asking for his arrest.

Family members have said that Scott may have been attempting to run from Slager during Saturday’s traffic stop because he was afraid of going back to jail over child support. The father of four had been in jail three times before over the issue, the AP notes. In 2008, he was sentenced to six months in jail for falling behind by $6,800 after a traffic stop leading to charges on an open-container violation and driving while under suspension.
