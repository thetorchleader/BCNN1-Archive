Mary Mary’s Tina Campbell Completes Her First Sermon

January 21, 2015 Entertainment

Apostle Kim Daniels, Tina Campbell and Teddy Campbell pose for a snap following Tina’s first sermon (Credit: Teddy Campbell Instagram)

As Mary Mary’s Tina Campbell gears up to release a new book and debut solo album, she has added a new role to her resume: preacher.

In a January 18 Instagram post Teddy Campbell celebrated the completion of her first sermon.

In the caption of the photo of the happy couple posing with Apostle Kim Daniels, the doting hubby revealed the message title, “We Shall Overcome Today.”

The self-proclaimed “red-haired Mary” knows quite a lot about overcoming.

In 2013, Tina’s marriage was on the rocks after Teddy’s repeated infidelity came to light. But instead of giving up, the couple fasted and prayed, and invited fans from around the world to join them.
