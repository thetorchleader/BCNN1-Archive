Bestselling Author, Tia McCollors, Releases New and Exciting Novel, Friday Night Love

June 3, 2014 Books

Award-winning, bestselling author, Tia McCollors, releases her newest romance novel, Friday Night Love. It is the first book in her Days of Grace Series. 

Zenja Maxwell’s marriage is falling apart, thanks to a dalliance with infidelity on the part of her husband, Roman. She sets off on a cruise with her best friend, Caprice, prepared to give Roman a dose of his own medicine—on the very day he comes home, making promises to change. Zenja isn’t convinced. But Caprice comes up with a strategy to salvage the marriage—a seven–step plan developed by her ministry and called “Friday Night Love.”

Over the course of seven weeks, the plan outlines a series of assignments based on Ephesians 5:33, helping a wife to cultivate respect for her husband. Rekindling their relationship takes a lot of work, especially when the man with whom Zenja nearly cheated on Roman refuses to step out of the picture. Will their commitment to honor God with their marriage pay off in the end?

Tia McCollors is an award-winning, bestselling author, speaker, and writing coach. She graduated from the University of North Carolina (Chapel Hill) in journalism and mass communications aiming to make it to a spot as a TV network news anchor. But upon graduation her considerable writing and speaking talents lead her to the public relations industry where she established a successful career for a decade.She left the corporate arena to start a family and began focusing her creative energies on writing and speaking. Since then, both her family and career have blossomed. Tia and her husband have been blessed with three children and Tia has emerged as a popular, highly acclaimed author, releasing a number of books – both fiction and nonfiction—for a variety of publishers. She found success and acclaim with first novel, A Heart of Devotion (Moody) which was an Essence magazine best seller. She followed the popular debut with four more inspirational novels: Zora’s Cry, The Truth about Love, The Last Woman Standing, and Steppin’ Into the Good Life. In 2012, she released her first devotional book, If These Shoes Could Talk: Devotional Messages for a Woman’s Daily Walk.Tia’s latest book, Friday Night Love (May 2014) kicks off Days of Grace, her first series with Whitaker House.
