Gospel Singer J. Moss Says God Allowed Devastating House Fire to Increase Anointing, Experience

March 18, 2015 Entertainment

Credit: PAJAM

Last fall, GRAMMY ® award-winning gospel singer J. Moss lost everything in a devastating house fire, leaving him and his family with no place to go.

“Having to tell your wife, ‘our house is gone.’ Having to tell your children, ‘there’s nowhere to sleep tonight’ because of the fire, it’s devastating,” Moss said in a Christian Post Interview.

But now that the 43-year-old has a new home and recently released his fourth consecutive #1 album, Grown Folks Gospel, he is able to put the tragedy into proper perspective.

“During this project that was a huge, huge challenge for me, just trying to understand what God was trying to get me to see in taking all of our earthly things away that were in our home,” explained Moss.

According to the PAJAM artist, God prophetically told him the unfortunate events had a specific purpose: to increase his anointing and experience to make his ministry more relatable and potent.
