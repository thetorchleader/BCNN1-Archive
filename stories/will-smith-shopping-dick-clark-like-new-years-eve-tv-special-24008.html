Will Smith Shopping Dick Clark-Like New Year’s Eve TV Special

May 27, 2014 Entertainment

Move over, Ryan Seacrest — sources tell Page Six Will Smith and his company Overbrook Entertainment are developing a New Year’s Eve television special similar to Dick Clark Productions’ “New Year’s Rockin’ Eve with Ryan Seacrest.”

One insider said, “As of now, it’s being shopped to the networks. It would feature musical performances, possibly one by Will.”

Sources say Smith’s people want to film it in Miami and include his friend David Beckham: “They want to do something with Beckham and his focus on his Miami soccer team.”

It’s unclear whether Will’s wife Jada Pinkett Smith, son Jaden or daughter Willow could participate. Smith’s rep didn’t get back to us.

SOURCE: Page Six
