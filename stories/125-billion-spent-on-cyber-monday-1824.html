$1.25 Billion Spent on Cyber Monday

November 30, 2011 Uncategorized

Cyber Monday was the busiest online shopping
day ever with consumers spending an estimated $1.25 billion. That’s up
22 percent from the total spent last year on the Monday after
Thanksgiving.

Research group
comScore said Tuesday that it estimates 10 million people bought things
online Monday. The average shopper spent 9 percent more this year, or
roughly $125.

Cyber Monday sales topped $1 billion for the first time last year.

Source: The Associated Press
