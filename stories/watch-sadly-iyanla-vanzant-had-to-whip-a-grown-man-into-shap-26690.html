WATCH: Sadly, Iyanla Vanzant Had to Whip a Grown Man Into Shape to Get him to Become the Head of his Household and Gain Some Respect from his Wife and Children

June 15, 2014 God & Family

Life coach Iyanla Vanzant travels to New York, where a father’s marriage and his relationship with his children are in serious jeopardy.

Four years ago, husband and father Robert stopped drinking after years of doing so to excess. Unfortunately, his history of anger and lashing out at his family continues, causing more and more distance, resentfulness and hurt in his family.

Iyanla works with Robert and his wife, Donna, to remember the friendship they once shared. In an emotional moment, the family comes to grips with the damage caused by Robert and Donna’s inability to protect daughters Lauren and Frankie during the most vulnerable moments of their lives.
