Dolly Parton, Not Whitney Houston, Will Make a Great Fortune from the Famous Song “I Will Always Love You”

February 15, 2012 Uncategorized


Dolly Parton is set to earn millions from Whitney Houston’s cover of her song I Will Always Love You, it has been revealed.

The track, which Whitney covered for the soundtrack of The Bodyguard, was originally penned by Parton in 1973. 
Last month, Parton revealed to CNN that Houston’s cover had already raked in lots of cash for her. 
‘When Whitney did it, I got all the money for the publishing and for the writing, and I bought a lot of cheap wigs,’ she said. 
The track is expected to enter the charts this Sunday at number 10, according to figures from the Official Chart Company. 
Jennifer Hudson paid tribute to Houston at the Grammy Awards on Sunday by performing a cover of I Will Always Love You a capella. 
Houston is on course to have 27 singles in the Top 200 and three albums back in the chart this weekend.
