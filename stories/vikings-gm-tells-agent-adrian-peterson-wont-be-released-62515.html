Vikings GM Tells Agent Adrian Peterson Won’t Be Released

March 19, 2015 Sports

Adrian Peterson’s NFL  future remains in limbo. 

Minnesota Vikings general manager Rick Spielman reportedly told Peterson’s agent, Ben Dogra, the club wouldn’t release the superstar running back, according to USA TODAY’s Tom Pelissero.

Peterson wants out of Minnesota, but he’s still under contract with the Vikings through 2017.

If #Vikings were going to cut Peterson and get nothing, it would’ve happened in September. They want to keep him. AP still has concerns.

— Tom Pelissero (@TomPelissero) March 19, 2015

The 2012 NFL MVP was suspended indefinitely after being indicted on child abuse charges, missing every game except for the team’s season opener against the St. Louis Rams.
