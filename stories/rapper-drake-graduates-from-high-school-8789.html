Rapper Drake Graduates from High School

October 18, 2012 Uncategorized

Now Drake is 25 sittin’ on $25 mil and a diploma!

The rapper (real name Aubrey Graham) recently fulfilled all his necessary school work to officially become a high school graduate.
“97% on my final exam. 88% in the course. One of the greatest feelings in my entire life. As of tonight I have graduated high school,” Drizzy tweeted.
“Thank you to my teacher Kim Janzen for spending the last 5 months working tirelessly with me!! OVO SOUND,” he added.
