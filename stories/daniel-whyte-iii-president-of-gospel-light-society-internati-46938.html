Daniel Whyte III, President of Gospel Light Society International, Launches “With Talent On Loan from God…” Thanksgiving Evangelistic Outreach

November 26, 2014 Christian

Daniel Whyte III, founder and president of Gospel Light Society International has launched a worldwide evangelistic outreach campaign, “With Talent on Loan from God…—A Thanksgiving Celebration”. This Thanksgiving outreach aims to celebrate and show gratitude for the talents and gifts God has bestowed on His creation and inspires people to give their lives to Jesus Christ and begin living for His glory. The “With Talent on Loan from God…—A Thanksgiving Celebration”evangelistic outreach highlights the talents of 21 famous people from actors and actresses to athletes, singers, and businessmen. These famous people include: Matthew McConaughey, Russell Wilson, Kevin Durant, Peyton Manning, Kirsten Powers, Allyson Felix, Truett Cathy, Odell Beckham, Jr., Madison Bumgarner, Candace Cameron Bure, Denzel Washington, Sheila E., Bobby Bowden, Albert Pujols, Carrie Underwood, Tim Howard, Blair Underwood, Tony Dungy, Michelle Williams, Ben Carson, and Oprah Winfrey.

Whyte said, “On this Thanksgiving, I thank God for the talents and gifts that He has bestowed upon my family and me and that is no doubt the reason why I love to see people do what they are truly talented and gifted by God to do. It is a blessing to see singers, musicians, actors, actresses, athletes in all sports, writers, speakers, business people, doctors, cooks, auto mechanics, lawyers, and leaders of all types seemingly without effort and almost flawlessly doing what they were given by God the talents and the gifts to do.”

The “With Talent on Loan from God…” Thanksgiving evangelistic outreach is available via audio and video on over 700 sites around the world and in 100 languages. Click here to participate and share with family and friends.

Daniel Whyte III has spoken in meetings across the United States and in over twenty-five foreign countries. He is the author of over forty books. He is also the president of Gospel Light Society International, a worldwide evangelistic ministry that reaches thousands with the Gospel each week, as well as president of Torch Ministries International, a Christian literature ministry which publishes a monthly magazine called The Torch Leader.

He is heard by thousands each week on his radio broadcasts, The Prayer Motivator Devotional, The Prayer Motivator Minute, Gospel Light Minute X, the Gospel Light Minute, the Sunday Evening Evangelistic Message, the Prophet Daniel’s Report, the Second Coming Watch Update, the Soul-Winning Motivator, the History of Christianity, Reasons to Believe, the Covenant and the Cross, My Brother’s Keeper, and Understanding God’s Great Salvation.

He holds a Bachelor’s Degree in Theology from Bethany Divinity College, a Bachelor’s degree in Religion from Texas Wesleyan University, a Master’s degree in Religion, a Master of Divinity degree, and a Master of Theology from Liberty Baptist Theological Seminary. He has been married to the former Meriqua Althea Dixon, of Christiana, Jamaica for twenty-seven years. God has blessed their union with seven children.
