After Filing for Divorce, Pastor Mase Betha Reportedly Calls It Off

March 20, 2014 Uncategorized

Mase and Twyla Betha

Rapper Mase filed for divorce from his wife Twyla Betha in January, only to urge officials to scrap the request just weeks later, according to a report.

The former Bad Boy artist-turned-pastor, real name Mason Betha, cited irreconcilable differences when he filed for divorce in Georgia at the beginning of the year, alleging he had been separated from his wife of 12 years since August 2013.
