It Is Reported That Holly Petraeus Is Mad As Fire

November 12, 2012 Uncategorized

For Holly Petraeus, “furious would be an understatement” in describing her feelings after her husband of 38 years, Gen. David Petraeus, admitted to an extramarital affair and resigned from his post as director of the CIA, according to a report on Monday.
“Well, as you can imagine, she’s not exactly pleased right now,” retired U.S. Army Col. Steve Boylan, a former spokesman for Petraeus, told ABC News. “In a conversation with David Petraeus this weekend, he said that, ‘Furious would be an understatement.’ And I think anyone that’s been put in that situation would probably agree. He deeply hurt the family.”
