College Prep Summer Camp for Female Rising Seniors Opens Application Process

February 25, 2015 Education

Limited spots for two-week residential program to be held at prestigious Bryn Mawr College

PHILADELPHIA, PA, (February 24, 2015) – An exclusive residential summer camp program designed to prepare female high school seniors for college has opened its application process to receive students nationwide.

Now in its seventh year, the Residential College Prep Summer Program, which was created and is hosted by Teenshop, Inc®. — an award-winning, Philadelphia-based nonprofit for ‘girls with goals’® – is being held at Bryn Mawr College for two weeks from July 12th through the 25th.

The program combines elements of leadership, mentorship and scholarship to provide the participants with various exposure opportunities including methods for improving SAT scores, workshops on strengthening college application essays, money management skills, public speaking training, career-building strategies, STEM, etiquette for  life,  character-development, field  trip  to Washington, DC., visits  to other  area  colleges, health and fitness activities, CPR Certification, off-campus community service project, and much more.

“Because the challenges facing women in their career pursuits continue to grow, we thought it most appropriate to broaden the access to this program, which has only been available to Teenshop girls,” said Founder and CEO Elleanor Jean Hendley.  “Now, due to demand, girls nationwide will have exposure to some of the most accomplished female and male professionals in their respective fields in this intensive setting on the campus of Bryn Mawr.”

Teenshop, a weekend program founded in June, 1985, has five chapters located in California, New Jersey, and Pennsylvania, each one directed by a five-member volunteer team of  professional  women.  More than six thousand girls have been enrolled, and all graduates go to college.
