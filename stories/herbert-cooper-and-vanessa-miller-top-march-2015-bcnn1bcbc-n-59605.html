Herbert Cooper and Vanessa Miller Top March 2015 BCNN1/BCBC National Bestsellers List

March 3, 2015 Books

Bestselling authors, Herbert Cooper (But God: Changes Everything — Nonfiction) and Vanessa Miller (Heaven On Earth– Fiction) lead the bestsellers list of Black Christian authors on the March 2015 edition of the Black Christian News/Black Christian Book Company National Bestsellers List.

BCNN1.com has also released the March 2015 edition of the BCNN1/Amazon.com Kindle E-book Bestsellers List. This list ranks African-American Christian e-books based upon their sales rankings in the Amazon Kindle e-book store. Also, BCNN1 is releasing the March 2015 edition of the BCNN1/Barnes & Noble Nook E-book Bestsellers List. This list ranks African-American Christian e-books based upon their sales rankings in the B&N Nook e-book store.
