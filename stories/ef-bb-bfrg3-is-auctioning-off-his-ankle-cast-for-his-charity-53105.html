﻿RG3 Is Auctioning Off His Ankle Cast for His Charity

January 21, 2015 Sports

Thomas B. Shea/Getty Images

Robert Griffin III is looking to cash in on his misfortunes — for a good cause.

The Redskins’ quarterback is auctioning off an ankle cast signed by his teammates for his Family of 3 Foundation.

The cast, signed by DeSean Jackson and DeAngelo Hall among others, is available to bidders through Feb. 13. The current bid is currently $300 dollars.

Griffin had his teammates sign the cast after dislocating his ankle against Jacksonville on Sept. 14.

Perhaps something good will come out of the Redskins’ disastrous 2014 season.
