Unemployment Claims Rise Slightly Ahead of Monthly Jobs Report

October 7, 2011 National
The number of Americans filing first-time claims for jobless benefits rose a bit last week after dropping sharply the previous week.
Four hundred and one thousand people made initial unemployment claims, the Labor Department said. That was an increase of 6000 from the previous week’s figure.
The previous week’s number was revised up to 395,000 from 391,000. That means the drop from the week before that was a still substantial 31,000, but still lower than the 37,000 figure the government had announced earlier.
This week’s four-week moving average, which smooths out volatility in the data, was 414,000, a small decline from the previous week’s revised figure of 418,000.
The government’s monthly jobs report is due out Friday. Yesterday, a survey of private employers found that they had added a better-than-expected 91,000 jobs in September.
Source: The Lookout | Zachary Roth
