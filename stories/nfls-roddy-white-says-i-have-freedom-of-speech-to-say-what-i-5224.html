NFL’s Roddy White says I Have “Freedom of Speech” to Say What I Want About Homosexuals

April 14, 2012 Uncategorized

Atlanta Falcons wide receiver Roddy White is under fire in the lesbian, gay, bisexual and transgender (LGBT) community for a series of tweets deemed homophobic.
As OutSports first reported, when White was asked by a fan whether he would rather lead the league in receiving stats or win a Super Bowl, he responded: “Would u rather be gay or straight come on u know that answer.”
