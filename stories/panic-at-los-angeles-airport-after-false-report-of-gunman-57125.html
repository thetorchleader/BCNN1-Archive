Panic at Los Angeles Airport After False Report of Gunman

February 17, 2015 National

 

Someone wrongly announced that a gunman was on the loose at LAX on Monday, officials said. (George Rose, Getty)

About 20 passengers fled through emergency doors to the tarmac at a Los Angeles Airport terminal Monday after someone wrongly announced that an armed man was on the loose, authorities said.

The “misinformed announcement” apparently stemmed from a police pursuit of an unarmed driver, which ended outside Terminal 2, LAX Police Sgt. Belinda Joseph said.

Cops responded to an emergency call that a man might be trying to commit suicide. They found him, and he was taken to a hospital. But “someone said that there was a man with a gun, which was not true,” Joseph said.

Travelers who fled outside were “under observation the whole time” they were near planes, Joseph said.
