Atlanta Mayor Kasim Reed Announces that he Is Married and his First Child Was Just Born

June 9, 2014 Black

Sara-Elizabeth Langford and Atlanta Mayor Kasim Reed

Atlanta mayor Kasim Reed has two reasons to be happy.

According to the Daily Mail, the politician confirmed Thursday that he married his fiancé, former beauty queen Sara-Elizabeth Langford and are the proud parents of a baby girl.

The mayor’s office confirmed the birth of Maria Kristan Reed, who was born Thursday at an Atlanta hospital.

“Today, Sarah-Elizabeth Reed and I are blessed to announce the birth of our first child, Maria Kristan Reed,” Reed said in a statement to the Atlanta Journal-Constitution Thursday. ‘I am overwhelmed by the outpouring of love and support we have received from our family and friends. This is truly the happiest moment of my life. As we celebrate our daughter’s birth, we ask that you continue to respect the privacy of our growing family.”

News of Reed’s marriage officially closes the book on singlehood for a man who was considered to be one of Atlanta’s most eligible bachelors. In March, the popular politician announced his engagement to Langford via a statement.

“I could not feel more blessed that she agreed to marry me, and we look forward to building a family together in the greatest city in America,” Reed said. “I am honored to have our friends and family share this special moment with us and I ask that our privacy is respected as we celebrate this personal occasion.”
