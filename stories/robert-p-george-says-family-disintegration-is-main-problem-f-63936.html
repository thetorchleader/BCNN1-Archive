Princeton Prof. Robert P. George says Family Disintegration is Main Problem Facing America

March 27, 2015 National

Robert P. George speaking at ERLC Leadership Summit.

Speaking at the ERLC Leadership Summit, Robert P. George, the McCormick Professor of Jurisprudence at Princeton University, delivered an address on the state of the family in light of the overarching discussion about race. Citing issues from single-parent homes, absentee fathers, abortion, and black-on-black crime, he said, “The future of the family will determine the future of our country… The promise of America is unreachable without strong and stable families.”

He stated that the country will change when the marriage culture changes and said “human flourishing depends on marriage integration, yet what is promoted is marriage disintegration.”

During an earlier discussion in which panelists discussed the slogans, “Black lives matter” and “Hands up, don’t shoot”, he said, ‘If black lives matter in Ferguson — and they do — they also matter in the womb… Black lives matter in Chicago where black young men are killing black young men and sometimes innocent children are caught in the crossfire.’

–BCNN1
