Armina Mussa, Solange Knowles’ Business Partner, In Critical Condition After Stabbing

April 11, 2015 Entertainment

© The Associated Press Solange

Solange Knowles’ business and creative partner Armina Mussa, 24, is in critical condition after she was stabbed 10 times on Wednesday, Billboard has confirmed.

The police have arrested and booked a suspect, 26-year-old Arronesia Christophe, for second-degree murder after allegedly stabbing Mussa “outside a home in the 2200 block of N. Derbigny Street” in the New Orleans St. Roch area, according to Nola.com, which first reported the news. According to records filed at Orleans Parish Criminal District Court, Christophe was arrested at the location of the stabbing on Wednesday, around 7:03 p.m. by New Orleans police.

Courthouse sources told Nola.com that Solange attended Christophe’s first appearance at Orleans Parish Criminal Court on Thursday.
