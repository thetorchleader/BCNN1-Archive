Deitrick Haddon’s LXW Releases First, Full-Length Album

April 23, 2014 Entertainment

The return of Choir Movement touches down today with highly anticipated release of Deitrick Haddon’s LXW first, full-length album. This an amazing project has brought together 100 voices of multi-talented young people in the LA area. The critically praised debut album features a list of ‘Who’s Who’ in the current choir music sub-genre including Hezekiah Walker, Donald Lawrence, Ricky Dillard as well as a new generation of Gospel superstars (Jessica Reedy).

By creating music for youth choirs, LXW is “resurrecting the youth choir movement.” This project truly offers music that choirs across the country can sink their teeth into and actually sing. It also opens the opportunities for choirs to showcase the hard-to-duplicate vocal calisthenics that crowds for centuries have come to love. Sopranos, altos, tenors can shine separately and together on each track.

LXW wowed audiences just last month, as the opener of the “Celebration of Gospel” on BET. They were listed in the show’s highlights and talked about for days after the Celebration of Gospel aired. On Good Friday, audience’s souls were refueled with the national broadcast premiere of their full concert DVD on Oxygen. After viewing it, people were left asking why anyone else hadn’t thought of this sooner. Choirs around the world have been provided with new songs to sing during their church services, with this amazing project.
