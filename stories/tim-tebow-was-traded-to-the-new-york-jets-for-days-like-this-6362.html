Tim Tebow Was Traded to the New York Jets for Days Like This

May 29, 2012 Uncategorized
When Tim Tebow was traded to New York, we searched for reasons why. How would he fit into their scheme? What did it mean for Mark Sanchez’s future?
We really shouldn’t have thought about the football reasons at all. Tebow moves the needle. He makes money. This Jets regime is obsessed with raising its profile and owning the back page in New York. Mission Accomplished.
On the same day Tebow was shown off for his first OTA practice, the Giants — the defending Super Bowl champs — lost their top wide receiver Hakeem Nicks to a broken foot. Our friend Peter King of SI.com ran a great experiment to test how the five major New York City papers handled the stories.
