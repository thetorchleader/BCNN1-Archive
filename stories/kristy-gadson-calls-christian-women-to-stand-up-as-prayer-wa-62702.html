Kristy Gadson Calls Christian Women to Stand Up as Prayer Warriors for Their Families In “The Naked Prophetess”

March 20, 2015 Books

“Will you war with me?” Kristy Gadson prefaces her book, The Naked Prophetess, with this question. It is a question for Christian women to not only think about and consider, but one which should be answered. Using the book of the prophet Isaiah as a basis, Gadson calls women back to their places of authority in their families and in their positions at work, school, church and other arenas.

The Naked Prophetess is a prophetic call to women of faith to stand up and take their rightful place as prayer warriors and spiritual guardians of their families’ faith and future. In the spirit of the prophet Isaiah, whom God commanded to lay naked as a prophetic sign to God’s people, Prophetess Gadson exposes herself to the reader, by sharing how God has delivered her from the struggles of her past, as well as dreams, visions, and prophetic insights that God has shown her about the nation and its future. This book will inspire you, challenge you, and prepare you for the days ahead.

Women will be challenged to overcome discouragement, depression, debt, and doubt, among other things. Readers will be inspired to drop the weights that are holding them down and rise above negative situations to be all that God has called them to be. Gadson uses her own story and biblical examples to show forth the prophetic call upon Christian women and how they can use their calling to make a difference in the world.

Kristy Gadson is a wife, mother, evangelist, psalmist, and entrepreneur with a prophetic call to the nations. She has a B.S. in Accounting and a M.S. in Taxation. She currently serves alongside her husband, Jermaine, in ministry. Kristy and Jermaine are the parents of a daughter, Khloe. the Gadson’s reside in the greater metro area of Birmingham, AL.

For more information about Kristy Gadson, visit: www.FaithMinistriesInc.org.

To book Kristy Gadson for speaking engagements and/or book signings, please email: kristygadson@gmail.com.

The Naked Prophetess is available on Amazon.com and Amazon Kindle.
