Top NYC Mayoral Aide, Rachel Noerdlinger, Takes Leave of Absence to Spend Time with Family Following Son’s Arrest

November 17, 2014 National

Rachel Noerdlinger is chief of staff to Chirlane McCray. (Credit: Richard Drew/Associated Press)

Rachel Noerdlinger, the embattled chief of staff to Mayor Bill de Blasio’s wife, Chirlane McCray, announced on Monday morning that she was taking a leave of absence to spend more time with her family.

Ms. Noerdlinger, in a statement, said that following the arrest of her son on Friday on trespassing charges, she believes she needs to spend more time with her family at this time.

Her decision comes after a spate of news reports on her personal life became a distraction for the de Blasio administration.

Scrutiny of Ms. Noerdlinger’s personal life increased after reports that her boyfriend, Hassaun McFarlan, invoked her name in an attempt to get out of traffic violations.

Mr. McFarlan, who as a teenager was convicted of manslaughter and was arrested as recently as last fall, has been an ongoing source of problems for Ms. Noerdlinger.

In her statement, however, she said it was the public scrutiny of her son, Khari, that was behind her decision to take a leave of absence.

In October, The New York Post reported that Ms. Noerdlinger received special permission to live in New Jersey because her son had sustained devastating injuries in two car crashes. However, the paper reported that Khari had recovered enough to play linebacker full time for his high school football team.

Over the weekend, it was reported that Khari was arrested on Friday night for trespassing at a Washington Heights apartment building. He and three friends were drinking liquor in the lobby area, which is supposed to be used only by residents and guests, according to the reports.
