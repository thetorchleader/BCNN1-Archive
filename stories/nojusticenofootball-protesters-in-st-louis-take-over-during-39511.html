#NoJusticeNoFootball – Protesters in St. Louis Take Over during Game between Rams and 49ers

October 14, 2014 Black

Protesters’ banner during St. Louis Rams game Oct. 13, 2014
TWITTER SCREENSHOT

The revolution was televised during Monday’s game between the St. Louis Rams and San Francisco 49ers.

The revolution was televised during Monday Night Football. Hundreds of Ferguson, Mo., protesters who attended the St. Louis Rams game raised their voices and banners in protest of the August killing of Michael Brown by Ferguson Police Officer Darren Wilson.

Protesters in the stands could be heard chanting, “Hands up, don’t shoot,” and “Black lives matter,” from the stands. In addition to the protests, various videos were posted on social media that showed what was actually going on during the game.
