Deitrick Haddon, Lecrae, Michelle Williams Among Gospel Artists Nominated for BET Awards

May 19, 2015 Entertainment

On Monday, May 18, the list of BET Award nominations were announced.

While typical mainstream acts like Nicki Minaj, Chris Brown and Beyoncé are nominated, standouts in Gospel music are also being recognized.

This year, the “Best Gospel Artist” nominees include:

Deitrick Haddon

Erica Campbell

Fred Hammond

Lecrae

Mali Music

Michelle Williams

The BET Awards, which air on the BET network on June 28, celebrate the achievements of African Americans and other minorities in music and entertainment as well as sports.

SOURCE: EEW Magazine
