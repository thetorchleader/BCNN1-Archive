“Black Nativity” Is Out on Blu-ray and DVD

April 18, 2014 Entertainment

Blu-ray widescreen, DVD widescreen and UltraViolet digital download, 2013, PG for thematic material, language and a menacing situation

“BLACK NATIVITY” DELIVERS spot-on acting and powerhouse singing at every turn. The musical drama stars music favorites Jennifer Hudson, Tyrese Gibson and Mary J. Blige, and surprises with impeccable vocals from Angela Bassett and Forest Whitaker. The other surprise comes from Jacob Latimore, a 16-year-old newcomer, who steals the spotlight in the film.

Inspired by the Langston Hughes’ play, the holiday-themed film follows a troubled teen who is sent to live with his estranged grandparents in New York, while his mother struggles with financial problems in Baltimore. “This is a film that everybody in the family can go see together. What makes this movie powerful is you can relate and identify with somebody,” Gibson says.

At about two minutes each, the 10 special features include music and interviews from the cast and crew. “The Music Behind the Film: With Raphael Saadiq” highlights Saadiq’s role as music executive producer on the project. “Just thinking about what he does musically… gives me chills. He is the soul of what we do,” Blige says.

Saadiq is best known for his work on R&B and soul tracks, but jumped at this opportunity to tackle a largely gospel soundtrack. “I have a huge background in gospel. I started in church. Gospel was the root of everything for me. ‘Black Nativity’ gave me a lot of room to flex my gospel chops,” he says.

Saadiq’s talent put music novices Basset and Whitaker at ease. “He’s just so cool and relaxed and easy and makes you feel comfortable,” Bassett says.
