“Get Your Black Self Up and Change this City” — Jamal Bryant

April 28, 2015 Black

Baltimore sweeps up the ashes after a night of looting, arson, and violence

We know for a fact that the majority of black people in Baltimore (or in America for that matter) do not support or agree with the violence, looting, and rioting that has gone on in Baltimore over the past 72 hours. However, if our communities are going to change so that we do not have these kinds of flare-ups (or the threat of these kinds of flare-ups) every time something negative happens, it will have to be black people who do the changing. This changing must take place on several fronts:

  1. Black parents must get control of their children and keep them occupied with school and other constructive endeavors so that they do not even have the time or opportunity to be out creating havoc in the streets.
  1. Older black men must train young black men how to practice self-control and how to carry themselves around the police in a way that cuts down on the need for suspicion and lends itself to reacting in a negative way.
  1. Black churches should help get young people engaged in not just spiritual activities (such as attending church, singing in the choir, etc.), but practical life matters such as computer training, job training, college education, volunteer work, etc.

These are just some suggestions that will make things better for the black community and how it relates to the broader society on controversial issues of race going forward.

–BCNN1
