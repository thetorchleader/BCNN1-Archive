Cindy Trimm Launches Kingdom School of Ministry

February 27, 2015 Christian

Kingdom School of Ministry (KSM) provides spiritual equipping and enrichment to those seeking to pursue excellence in ministry leadership.

Kingdom School of Ministry is a comprehensive, Bible-based institute dedicated to preparing individuals to positively impact all strata of society utilizing the fundamental principles and mandates of the Kingdom of Heaven as delineated in the Bible.

The primary aim of KSM is to equip individuals within the context of personal purpose to maximize their potential and effectively impact present and future generations on both a local and global scale. By teaching spiritual truths from a biblical perspective, KSM seeks to prepare students to fulfill the Great Commission as outlined in Mark 16:15 and Matthew 28:19-20.

Currently, Kingdom School of Ministry offers the following courses:

  • Understanding the Kingdom
  • The Purpose of the Church
  • Order in the House
  • Prayer and Spiritual Warfare
  • Prophets and Prophecy
  • Praise and Worship
  • Kingdom Economics and Biblical Finance
  • Helps and Hospitality

If you are a seasoned minister, community leader, Christian professional, occasional volunteer, or just hungry for more of God, you will discover deeper meaning and higher purpose through these high impact course intensives.
