Manny Pacquiao to Have Surgery on Shoulder, Will Be Out for Nearly a Year

May 5, 2015 Sports

Manny Pacquiao (left) punches Floyd Mayweather against the ropes during their boxing bout at the MGM Grand Garden Arena. (Mark J. Rebilas, USA TODAY Sports)

Manny Pacquiao will undergo surgery this week to correct a torn rotator cuff in his right shoulder, the fighter’s advisor, Michael Koncz, told USA TODAY Sports on Monday.

ESPN, which was first to report the news, cited Los Angeles orthopedic surgeon Dr. Neal ElAttrache who said Pacquiao has a “significant tear” and will be out nine to 12 months.

Pacquiao, 36, suffered the injury during sparring for the Floyd Mayweather fight, which he lost via unanimous decision Saturday in Las Vegas. Koncz said the injury occurred around the first week of April.

“Manny should be commended for going through the fight because I certainly wouldn’t have done it if I was the boxer,” Koncz said during a phone call. “We’re not making excuses. It’s an injury that was exacerbated in the fight. And we’re not taking anything away from Floyd. Manny did his best on Saturday; Floyd was better at that moment and won the fight. He deserves the win.”

“The injury got worse, the MRI is the proof of that,” Koncz added. “The one taken today compared to the one that was taken in the first week of April, there’s a dramatic difference in the problem.”

Koncz said the surgery will be performed at an undisclosed location and that there will be a formal release of procedure and prognosis from the doctor following the operation.
