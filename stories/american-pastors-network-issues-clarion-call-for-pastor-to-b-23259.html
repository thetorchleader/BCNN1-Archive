American Pastors Network Issues Clarion Call for Pastors to Boldly Preach the Whole Counsel of God

May 22, 2014 Christian

Pastors are being challenged to preach against sin as part of the solution to turning the country around in terms of moral standards.

American Pastors Network is issuing a clarion call to that effect, encouraging pastors to boldly preach the whole counsel of God.

Sam Rohrer, who heads the organization, says pastors hold the key to “raising biblical truth in the public square,” and leading congregations to understand that scripture speaks to “all areas of life.”

Otherwise there is no instruction.

Some churches are reluctant to preach sin for fear of losing members of the congregation, but Rohrer contends that lacks the full counsel of God.
