Security Guard Shot Outside Houston Nightclub

March 1, 2015 Black

A security guard was shot outside of El Reventon nightclub early Saturday morning, according to Houston police.

Around 3:30 a.m. the security guard asked five people to leave the club after causing a disturbance. As they left, the security guard followed them out.

One man in the group went to his car and pulled out a gun. He started shooting at the security guard and the guard returned fire, allegedly hitting the suspect.

The group then fled the scene in the vehicle, according to police. The security guard was wearing a bullet proof vest at the time and was not seriously injured.

The suspect shot the guard in the chest and the vest stopped the bullet. The security guard only had a bruise from the impact and was not taken to the hospital. The club is located at 7398 Irvington at Yorkshire.

Source: KHOU
