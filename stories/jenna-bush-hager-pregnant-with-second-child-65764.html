Jenna Bush Hager Pregnant With Second Child

April 6, 2015 Entertainment

Jenna Bush Hager in October 2014.
(Photo: Nati Harnik, AP)

Jenna Bush Hager is pregnant with her second child.

The twin daughter of former President George W. Bush says she expects to give birth in August. She made the announcement Monday on NBC’s Today show, where she is a contributing correspondent.

Her 2-year-old daughter, Mila, is reportedly excited about the news. Hager told her Today show colleagues that Mila has been saying, “Baby sissy, baby sissy.”

Mila’s full name is Margaret Laura Hager, after her grandmothers, and is called Mila for short.

The Today crew presented Bush Hager with a tiny T-shirt that said, “Baby sister.”

Bush Hager, 33, is married to Henry Hager, son of a prominent Virginia Republican family.

When the two wed in 2008 in a private, family-only ceremony at the Bush ranch in Crawford, Texas, Jenna was the first presidential child in decades to marry for the first time during her father’s term.

Bush Hager was last at the White House in February when she joined first lady Michelle Obama to surprise tourists during the unveiling of the newly redesigned family dining room.

SOURCE: The Associated Press
