Parents Charged for Boy Being Found Dead In Septic Tank

April 3, 2015 What is this World Coming To?

The mother of 5-year-old Noah Thomas left her son to watch cartoons as she took a nap with her infant. When she awoke, 21/2 hours later, the boy was gone. His body would be found five days later in a Virginia septic tank. (screen shot/Fox 59)

The mother of 5-year-old Noah Thomas left her son to watch cartoons as she took a nap with her infant. When she awoke, 21/2 hours later, the boy was gone. His body would be found five days later in a Virginia septic tank.

Now, both parents are facing neglect charges in the case.

USA Today reports that Paul Thomas, 32, and Ashley White, 30, are expected to be arraigned today.

The pair were jailed and charged Thursday with two counts of abuse and neglect of children, the newspaper reported, citing the Pulaski County, Virginia, Sheriff’s Office.

Their son, Noah Thomas, was found dead March 26 in Dublin, Va.

The 6-month-old baby has been removed from the home.

The two counts against each parent reflect alleged neglect of both Noah and the infant.
