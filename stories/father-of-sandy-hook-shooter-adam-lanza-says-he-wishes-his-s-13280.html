Father of Sandy Hook Shooter, Adam Lanza, says he Wishes his Son Was Never Born

March 10, 2014 National

Peter Lanza insists that his son, who ultimately took his own life after his bloody rampage, would have killed him too had he gotten the opportunity. (DIANE HEBERT-FARRELL/GETTY IMAGES)

Peter Lanza talks to The New Yorker in an in-depth about his son, who killed 20 kids, 6 staff members at Sandy Hook Elementary School in Connecticut, along with his own mother, in Dec. 2012.

Adam Lanza’s dad says the boy would have killed him “in a heartbeat” and wishes his psycho mass murderer son had never been born.

In a shocking new interview, Peter Lanza dubbed his gun-crazy boy “evil” for killing his mom, 20 kids, six staff and then himself at Sandy Hook Elementary School, Connecticut, on Dec. 14, 2012.

“You can’t get any more evil,” he told The New Yorker.

“How much do I beat up on myself about the fact that he’s my son? A lot,” he added.

Lanza, a vice president for GE Energy Financial Services who divorced Adam’s mom Nancy in 2009, revealed he hadn’t seen his child for two years before the shocking shooting.

He also believes Adam would easily have killed him like he did his mother.

“I don’t question that for a minute. The reason he shot Nancy four times was one for each of us: one for Nancy; one for him; one for [his brother] Ryan; one for me,” he said.

Describing his youngest son as “socially awkward,” Lanza believed his child — diagnosed with Asperger’s — was in fact an undiagnosed schizophrenic.

“Asperger’s makes people unusual, but it doesn’t make people like this,” he told the magazine from his home in Fairfield County.
