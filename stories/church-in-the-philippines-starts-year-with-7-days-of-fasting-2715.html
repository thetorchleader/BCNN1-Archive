Church in the Philippines Starts Year With 7 Days of Fasting

January 7, 2012 Uncategorized

Victory Christian Fellowship (VCF),one of the country’s fastest growing Christian movements has started today (January 6, 2011) its 7-day prayer and fasting event to create spiritual breakthroughs in academic campuses and different communities in the Philippines.
The prayer fasting is being simultaneously held in different major provinces and cities in the Philippines.
Pastor Ado Bernardo, one of the key leaders of the movement said that this fasting is not just humbling ourselves or denying the desires of our flesh before God but this will help us grow and increase our faith in Christ.
In Zamboanga City, a young lady testified during last Wednesday’s prayer meeting how she was completely healed from cancer when she asked God for healing during in last year’s prayer and fasting. She said that she is now completely healed and is working for a popular local TV station.
Personal faith goals, family, school/career and ministry in the church are the four areas of prayer that each VCF member will focus on in the 7-day prayer and fasting occasion that starts at 6:00 every night.
