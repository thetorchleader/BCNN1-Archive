Superintendent Milton and First Lady Belinda Joyner Celebrate 20 Years of Ministry at Pacific Mount Olive COGIC

October 26, 2014 Black

Superintendent Milton and First Lady Belinda Joyner

The members of Pacific Mount Olive (PMO) Church of God in Christ will honor their pastor and wife, Superintendent Milton and First Lady Belinda Joyner, in an appreciation service on Sunday, October 19, at 3:30 p.m., in the sanctuary located at 1385 East 21st Street in Los Angeles.

This year marks Supt. Joyner’s 20th year in the ministry and his first year as the new pastor of the historic Pacific Mount Olive Church.

“The members are excited and greatly pleased with their godly leadership and Christ-like exemplary lives,” said Trustee Board Chair Anthony Spence.

“All are invited to this celebration service to God for this pastor and wife that are worthy of double honor.”
