Delicious Recipes for National Peanut Butter Fudge Day

November 20, 2014 God & Food

Peanut butter fudge. (photophnatic/Flickr)

National Peanut Butter Fudge Day is today, according to the food powers that be. Here are three recipes to help celebrate the day the right way.

Easiest Peanut Butter Fudge

In this simple recipe, the hardest part is waiting. Easiest Peanut Butter Fudge combines six ingredients you probably already have in your kitchen into a quick-to-prepare confection.

Prep time: 15 minutes
Cook time: 5 minutes
Total time: 1 hour 20 minutes

World’s Best Peanut Butter Fudge

Another easy-to-prepare dish, this recipe uses marshmallow creme to make the final product richer than run-of-the-mill peanut butter fudge.

Prep time: 5 minutes
Cook time: 20 minutes
Total time: 1 hour 25 minutes

Chocolate Peanut Butter Swirl Fudge

They say everything is better with chocolate, right? Use this recipe to sweeten your treat up with some chocolate swirls.

Prep time: 15 minutes
Total time: 3 hours 15 minutes
