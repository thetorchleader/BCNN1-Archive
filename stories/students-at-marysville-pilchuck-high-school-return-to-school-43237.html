Students at Marysville Pilchuck High School Return to School

November 3, 2014 National

Students and community members participate in a vigil for the students slain and injured in an Oct. 24, 2014, school shooting in Marysville, Wash.
(Photo: KING-TV, Seattle)

Teens return Monday to Marysville Pilchuck High School where three students were killed and two others were seriously injured more than a week ago before a student gunman killed himself.

Counselors at the 2,500-student school about 35 miles north of Seattle are preparing to help classmates grieve and move forward.

“The key is for the kids to know they’re safe there physically and emotionally safe there,” says Mary Schoenfeldt, the public education coordinator at the Everett Office of Emergency Management who has been counseling district leaders on their response in wake of the deadly shooting.

The school will reopen Monday, but it won’t be a normal day, she said.
