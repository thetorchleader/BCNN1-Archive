Park Hills Community Church Celebrates 28th Annual Women’s Day

May 17, 2015 Black

Pastor Alison Jones

Park Hills Community Church, Reformed Church in America, will celebrate their 28th Annual Women’s Day on Sunday, May 17, at 11 a. m., in their edifice at 5247 Overdale Drive in Los Angeles.  

The theme is ‘Women of God on the Move’ based on Proverbs 4:25-26.  The Rev. Lawrence K. Dove is the senior minister.

The Rev. Alison Jones is the guest preacher. She is the first lady of Los Angeles Community Church where she serves with her husband, the Rev. Richard A. Jones. Also, she leads the Wednesday Evening Bible Study.

A native of Pasadena, Pastor Jones is a fifth grade teacher at Price Elementary, a Christian school in Los Angeles.  She earned her Bachelor’s degree at North Carolina A and T State University and her Master of Divinity degree at Fuller Theological Seminary.
