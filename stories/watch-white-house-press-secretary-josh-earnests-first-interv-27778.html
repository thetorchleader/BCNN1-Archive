WATCH: White House Press Secretary Josh Earnest’s First Interview with POLITICO’s ‘Open Mike’

June 23, 2014 Media

Not surprisingly, POLITICO is the first outlet to do an interview with White House Press Secretary Josh Earnest in his first day in the role for its “Open Mike” series. A teaser for the conversation between Earnest and POLITICO’s Mike Allen is out and answers the question as to what Twitter handle Earnest plans to use and how the White House press office has responded to technology advancements.
