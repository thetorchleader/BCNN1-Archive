Can Grace Church of Dumfries, Virginia, Spark Spiritual Transformation In DC?

October 7, 2014 Christian

Grace Church of Dumfries, Virginia, is the 7th-fastest-growing congregation in the U.S., according to Outreach magazine. (Facebook)

Grace Church of Dumfries, Virginia, is the seventh-fastest-growing congregation in the United States, according to Outreach magazine’s annual list of the 100 fastest-growing churches in America.

The top 10 spot on Outreach’s list is praiseworthy recognition of the phenomenal growth under way at the community church in suburban Washington, D.C. Grace Church started out in a high school building in 1998 with 12 members, and as recently as 2007 had fewer than 35 people attending services.

Last year, 865 people joined Grace Church, a membership gain of 72 percent, Outreach magazine says in its annual list. The most astonishing fact about this growth is the number of truly changed lives that are reported.

More than 4,000 worshippers call Grace Church their spiritual home. They are drawn by the compassionate, direct and uncompromising leadership of its pastor, Bishop Derek Grier.

“The best advertisement is a changed life,” Grier tells Outreach magazine. “We’re not out to build a big church, but big people.”

“God is not going to ask, ‘How big was your church?'” Grier adds. “He’s going to ask me, ‘Did you teach what I wanted you to teach?'”
