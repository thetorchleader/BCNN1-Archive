Jerry Jones Is Not Allowed to Talk About Johnny Football Anymore

October 14, 2014 Sports

(USA TODAY Sports)

Going into the 2014 NFL Draft, Jerry Jones and Johnny Manziel seemed like a perfect match in many fans’ eyes. Jones, according to reports, was even physically restrained from drafting the former Texas A&M quarterback.

But now? Jones isn’t even mentioning his name.

After the Cowboys have enjoyed an impressive resurgance to start the season 5-1, Jones said he’s not even allowed to utter the name “Johnny Football” anymore.
