Chris Brown Should Probably Just Stick to Singing; says ‘Ebola Epidemic Is a Form of Population Control’

October 13, 2014 Entertainment

Chris Brown arrives on the red carpet at the MTV Video Music Awards Aug. 24, 2014, at the Forum in Inglewood, Calif.

Chris Brown should probably stick to singing instead of speaking about the current Ebola crisis. On Monday Brown posted a conspiracy theory-filled tweet that didn’t sit well with too many people.

And of course it was met with backlash.

Responses ranged from tweets calling Brown stupid and ignorant to even those who said maybe he should try some Ebola sometime. Talk about being mean!
