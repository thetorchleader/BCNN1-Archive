Models Kendall Jenner and Hailey Baldwin Attend Hillsong Church In NYC With Justin Bieber

November 11, 2014 Entertainment

Justin Bieber can’t get enough of church these days.

The 20-year-old singer has been spotted at a slew of churches and bible study sessions, since his latest breakup with Selena Gomez at the end of September. And last night was no different.

Justin attended a late Sunday (November 9) service at New York City’s Hillsong Church with longtime pals, Kendall Jenner, 19, and Hailey Baldwin, 17.

Eyewitnesses at the service took to Twitter to share their sightings of the famous trio.

Twitter user Hope Revelle wrote, “Super cool to be at Hillsong church for the first time tonight in NY and sitting in first row was Jesus, Justin B[i]eber & Kendall Jenner.”

While @Josh3Estrada7 posted, “Justin Bieber and Kendall Jenner at hillsong tonight,” alongside a picture of the singer and the two models.
