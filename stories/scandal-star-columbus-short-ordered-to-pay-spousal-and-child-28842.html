‘Scandal’ Star Columbus Short Ordered To Pay Spousal and Child Support To Estranged Wife

Date not recorded in the original archive Entertainment

Fired Scandal star Columbus Short has reportedly been ordered to pay $21,000-plus a month in spousal and child support to his estranged wife Tanee Short Credit: Jason LaVeris/FilmMagic

Things aren’t getting any easier for Columbus Short. The fired Scandal star has been ordered to pay spousal and child support to his estranged wife Tanee Short, TMZ reports. The couple share a 2-year-old daughter.

PHOTOS: Celebrity mugshots

According to the site, neither the 31-year-old actor nor his lawyer showed up for a court hearing on Thursday, June 26. Amid their absence, the judge reportedly ordered Short to pay $17,005 a month in spousal support and $4,542 in child support. In addition, he may have to pay $25,000 for Tanee’s legal bills.

PHOTOS: Biggest celebrity scandals and meltdowns

Short has had a couple of rough months. In March, he was ordered to stay away from his wife of nine years after being charged on Valentine’s Day with a misdemeanor spousal battery. On March 26, he was arrested on a felony battery charge after allegedly punching a man during a bar brawl on Feb. 15.
