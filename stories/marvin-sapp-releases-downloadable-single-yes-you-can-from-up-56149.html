Marvin Sapp Releases Downloadable Single “Yes You Can” From Upcoming Album “You Shall Live”

February 10, 2015 Entertainment

 

Award-winning gospel superstar, author, and pastor Marvin Sapp, releases his new single “Yes You Can,” available for download beginning today, Tuesday February 10, 2015.  The lead single is from Sapp’s forthcoming album YOU SHALL LIVE, on RCA Inspiration.

 

Featuring production from Aaron Lindsey, the powerful single has already inspired countless fans in eager anticipation of Sapp’s upcoming 10th solo album.  With an up-tempo beat and contemporary grooves, “Yes You Can” empowers listeners to find strength in God to triumph over every trial and test.  The stirring lyrics and Sapp’s voice provide encouraging affirmation to power listeners’ faith. “Yes You Can” marks another soon-to-be classic from the chart-topping Michigan native, following on the release of his last album, the #1 gospel hit I WIN.
