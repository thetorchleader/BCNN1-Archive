Don’t Spend More Than You Need to on that Christmas Tree

December 3, 2014 Finances

‘Tis the season for buying Christmas trees.
(Photo: valentinarr, Getty Images/iStockphoto)

With Christmas inching closer and closer, families are heading to stores, tree farms, and parking lots to pick the best Christmas trees to bring the holiday tradition into their homes.

The Christmas tree has become so popular that 8 in 10 Americans put one up in their homes, according to a 2013 survey by Pew Research Center. If you buy a real tree every year, money expert Matt Granite suggests buying your tree from a local seller to save the most money.

But if 2014 is the year you’ve decided to go artificial, you’re in good company. Today 80% of the trees in homes are artificial, according to a 2013 Nielsen survey.

So when is the best time to save big bucks on your new artificial tree? Granite says there is a two-week window where the prices and the inventory can’t be beat.
