Michael Sam Claims There Are Many Other Homosexual Players in the NFL – We Doubt It

March 27, 2015 Sports

Brandon Wade/AP

Michael Sam is still waiting for a third chance to play in the NFL after the Dallas Cowboys released him in October of last year. But while he waits, Sam is letting people know that even though he might be the only publicly out player competing for a spot, he isn’t the only gay man in the league.

via the Star-Telegram:

“I am not the only gay person in the NFL,” Sam said Thursday. “I’m just saying there is a lot of us. I respect the players that did reach out to me and had the courage to tell me that they were also gay, but they do not have the same courage as I do to come out before I even played a down in the NFL.”

Sam added that he had been contacted by “several different players” after he was drafted by the St. Louis Rams in the seventh-round of the 2014 draft, and becoming the first openly gay player drafted. While he respected them for coming out to him, Sam noted that they did not have thesame courage that he did to come out publicly.

Sam has been balancing his desire to return to the NFL with commitments to the ABC series Dancing With The Stars, and told the Star-Telegram the reason behind his decision to take part in the show is simple.
