Grady County Board of Commissioners Selects LaFaye Copeland as First African American Female Chairman

January 6, 2015 Black

LaFaye Copeland says she’s still at a loss for words.

“Sometimes I pinch myself and say am I in Grady County?” said Copeland.

Tuesday morning Copeland took on the role as chairwoman for the Grady County Board of Commissioners and made history. Not only is she the first African American woman to hold this position, but the first woman ever to do so.

“This is a historical day for me and Grady County because there never has been before in the history a woman chair,” said Copeland.

This isn’t the first time Copeland has made history. In 2012 she was elected as the first woman in Grady County to hold a position as Commissioner.

Copeland has only been on the board for 2-years; however her colleagues have taken notice of her strong work ethic. They say she’s more than ready for the position.
