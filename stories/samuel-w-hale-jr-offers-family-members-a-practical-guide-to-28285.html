Samuel W. Hale Jr. Offers Family Members a Practical Guide to Handling the Death of a Loved One in New Book

June 26, 2014 Books

Death is an inevitable part of life. At one point or another, whether suddenly or expectedly, death will come knocking for someone who we love. As much as it hurts and is painful to talk about, life for those left behind must go on, the funeral must go forward, the affairs of the deceased must be settled, and other family members must be helped to adjust to their new reality.

In the pages of When Your Loved One Dies, author and pastor, Samuel W. Hale Jr., offers a practical funeral preparation guide for families to go by which seeks to lessen the frightening and often frustrating discoveries and parts of the funeral and burial process. This funeral guide is written to help family members address the sobering, yet necessary, responsibilities of carrying out the many, and often unrealized tasks involved in funeralizing a loved one. Helpful insights, cautions, instructions, and Biblical resources are given to minimize the problems and to better enable the family to adjust to the varied experiences that preparing for a funeral can bring.

When Your Loved One Dies is not only a guide for family members, but for pastors and lay people as well. Those who have been ordained to perform funerals can read this book and be given the training necessary to address the many concerns that ministering to those who are grieving entails.

Samuel W. Hale Jr., is a graduate of the American Baptist College in Nashville, TN and the Southern Baptist Theological Seminary in Louisville, KY. He is also the pastor of the Zion Missionary Baptist Church of Springfield, Illinois. His ministerial experience includes pastoring, teaching, missions, religious journalism, and church ministry development. He also served as Director of the Contemporary Ministries Workshop of the National Baptist Congress of Christian Education for over twenty years. He has served in various mission and Christian education capacities during his more than five decades of Christian ministry.

He has authored the books: Confirmation for Ministry, The First Century Mission and The Twenty-First Century Church, and When Messias Comes.

Two of his main ministry objectives are reflected in Luke 24:45 – “Then opened he their understanding that they might understand the scriptures.” And in Matthew 28:20 – “…teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you…”
