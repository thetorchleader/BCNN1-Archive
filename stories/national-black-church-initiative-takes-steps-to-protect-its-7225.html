National Black Church Initiative Takes Steps to Protect Its President, Rev. Anthony Evans, After Shooting at Family Research Council

September 3, 2012 Uncategorized

National Black Church Initiative (NBCI), a coalition of African-American Churches, has put in place security measures to protect Reverend Anthony Evans, following the shooting incident at the Family Research Council, Washington DC, about three weeks ago.
