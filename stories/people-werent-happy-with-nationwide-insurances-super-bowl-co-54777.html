People Weren’t Happy with Nationwide Insurance’s Super Bowl Commercial and They Responded

February 2, 2015 Black

TWITTER

In the midst of the exciting Super Bowl between the New England Patriots and Seattle Seahawks, there was one commercial that stood out among them all. And it wasn’t because it was cute, funny or action packed.

Nationwide Insurance’s depressing Super Bowl ad proved that not all multi-million dollars ads are there to make you laugh. The ad, which featured a young boy talking about everything he won’t be able to do in life, but turns out to be dead because his parents didn’t have insurance, was met with tons of backlash and even memes online.
