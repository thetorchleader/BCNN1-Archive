First Hyperloop Transportation System to Start Construction in 2016 in California

February 27, 2015 Technology

The U.S is getting its first ever Hyperloop transportation system.

The Hyperloop track — which Hyperloop Transportation Technologies has announced it will start building in Quay Valley, Calif., in 2016 — will allow bus-sized capsules with 28 people aboard to travel through a tube with a low pressure environment at 200 mph.

“Our agreement with Quay Valley is a major milestone in the advancement of the Hyperloop project,” said Hyperloop Transportation Technologies CEO Dirk Ahlborn. “This installation will allow us to demonstrate all systems on a full scale.”

The Hyperloop, which will run alongside Interstate 5, is being assembled by a team of 200 professionals and engineers from NASA, Yahoo! and Airbus. A group of 25 students from University of California, Los Angeles, is also working on the transportation system and focusing on route planning, capsule design and cost analysis.

The UCLA students will also work on the Hyperloop test track that Elon Musk is already building in Texas to test versions of the tube, capsules and stations that will be used in California.
