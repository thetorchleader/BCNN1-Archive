WATCH: Sheryl Underwood Says Jesus Is Her Voice of Reason

January 27, 2015 Entertainment

Sheryl Underwood shared with CBS “The Talk” audience where she gets her daily guidance from (Credit: The Talk)

Who says you can’t name drop Jesus on network television?

CBS “The Talk” co-host Sheryl Underwood, 51, certainly did during a recent segment titled “Who Is Your Voice of Reason?”

“Jesus is my voice of reason,” said the outspoken comedian, after which, the audience applauded loudly.

The conversation was sparked by country superstar Blake Shelton telling the media his wife Miranda Lambert is his voice of reason, leading the talk show hosts to reveal who keeps them in check.

Aisha Tyler said her husband helps keep her on the right path. “I’m the gut person. He’s definitely the head person in our family, which is nice,” she shared.

British TV personality and music manager Sharon Osbourne said music executive Michele Anthony, her friend of more than 30 years, helps restrain her from making bad choices.
