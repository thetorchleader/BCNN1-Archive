Skateboarders Savagely Beat Up Teen Couple in California

March 30, 2015 Black

Brooklyn Smith (L) and Toyon Carlson
KTLA SCREENSHOT

A California teenage couple was savagely attacked late last week by a group of skateboarders who reportedly warned the teen girl not to come to that side of town after an alleged fight a month ago.

According to KTLA, Brooklyn Smith, 16, and her boyfriend Toyon Carlson were walking near the Venice Beach Skate Park Thursday night when a young man spotted them. A bystander recorded the incident and voices can be heard yelling, “F–k that b—h!” and “Go back home!”

“Before we could even say anything, they was like, ‘That’s her! That’s her!'” Smith told the TV station. “They just came out of nowhere on skateboards.”

A group of young men allegedly attacked both Smith and her boyfriend and at one point, one of the men appears to hit Smith in the back of the head with a skateboard and several attackers appear to kick the teen once she hits the ground.

On the video, an onlooker can be heard saying, “They just cracked her head open.”

Smith told the news station that before she began to black out she started screaming and believes that a woman, who covered the 16-year-old with her body, may have saved her life.

“If it wasn’t for her, Lord knows what they would be doing to me,” Smith told KTLA. “They were hurting her too … because she was screaming.”
