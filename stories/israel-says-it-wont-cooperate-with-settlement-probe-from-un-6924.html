Israel says It Won’t Cooperate with Settlement Probe from UN

July 7, 2012 Uncategorized

The UN’s top human rights body on Friday appointed three independent experts to conduct a fact-finding mission on how Israel’s West Bank settlements affect Palestinians.


Illustrative photo: A view of Efrat, a settlement near Jerusalem (photo credit: Nati Shohat/Flash90)
The president of the UN Human Rights Council, Uruguay Ambassador Laura Dupuy Lasserre, named three women to the panel: Christine Chanet of France, Unity Dow of Botswana and Asma Jahangir of Pakistan.
Dupuy Lasserre said their mission will be to look how the Israeli settlements impact “the civil, political, economic, social and cultural rights of the Palestinian people.” The panel is expected to begin work right away and report by next March.
The Geneva-based 47-nation council passed a resolution in March to establish such a probe.
