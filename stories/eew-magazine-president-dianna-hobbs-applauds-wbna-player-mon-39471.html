EEW Magazine President Dianna Hobbs Applauds WBNA Player Monica Wright for Staying True to Her Christian Convictions

October 14, 2014 Sports

Photo Credit: Urban Legacy Filmworks

Kudos to WBNA Minnesota Lynx player, Monica Ashante Wright, 26, for her choice not to compromise her faith convictions over a man.

That is what award-winning urban faith-based media specialist Dianna Hobbs is saying.

In a response to a Risen Magazine interview where Wright explained her choice to end her engagement to NBA All-star Kevin Durant, the EEW Magazine President shared her own thoughts about it on her daily radio show, Turnt Up with Sunny D.

“We read a lot about women compromising their own standards of self-respect in the name of landing and keeping men—especially rich and famous ones,” Hobbs told her audience. “But not nearly enough is said about the Monica’s of the world and they are out there to be sure.”

According to the author of the indie bestseller, The New A-list: Abstinence Makes the Heart Grow Fonder, plenty ladies see the value of obeying God in their relationships and life in general.

Added Hobbs, “I have spoken with many women like Monica who are committed to their values and I’m overjoyed that this positive story is being exposed. I pray that we will hear more testimonies like this beautiful, courageous young lady’s. They are so needed.”

In Wright’s interview, she told the faith-based publication, “Yeah, in my last relationship [with NBA All-Star Kevin Durant], I had to make a decision about compromising myself as a woman. I had to make a decision to leave a relationship, which a lot of people thought I was crazy to leave.”
