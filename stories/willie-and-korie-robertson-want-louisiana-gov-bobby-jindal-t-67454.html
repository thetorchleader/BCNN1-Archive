Willie and Korie Robertson Want Louisiana Gov. Bobby Jindal to Run for President

April 14, 2015 Politics

Louisiana Governor Bobby Jindal delivers remarks during the second day of the 40th annual Conservative Political Action Conference (CPAC) March 15, 2013 in National Harbor, Md. (Alex Wong—Getty Images)

They’re going local.

Duck Dynasty’s Willie and Korie Robertson are supporting Louisiana Gov. Bobby Jindal for president, the two revealed in a radio interview.

The Christian politician is renowned for calls to prayer for an American awakening.

Though Jindal hasn’t declared his bid, the Robertsons are ready.

“If he chooses to go on, I know the values he has … He’s got the values, he’s got the intelligence to do it. I’d love to see him run.”
