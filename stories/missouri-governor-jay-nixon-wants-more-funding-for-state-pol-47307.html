Missouri Governor Jay Nixon Wants More Funding for State Police and National Guard

November 29, 2014 National

National Guard troops stand in front of the Police Department in Ferguson (AP)

Missouri Gov. Jay Nixon called for a special state legislative session to address “critical funding” needs for the National Guard and state police amid violent protests in Ferguson this week.

In a late-night call with state legislative leaders, Nixon highlighted the session’s urgency to ensure Guard members are paid on December 15, his office said in a statement.

“Time is of the essence,” he said.

“It is vital that we act quickly so that we can fulfill our obligation to the men and women who are so bravely and capably serving their fellow citizens.”

His call comes as tensions returned to Ferguson streets late Friday night after a Thanksgiving lull.

Demonstrators have taken to the streets this week amid anger over a grand jury’s decision not to indict Ferguson Officer Darren Wilson in the August shooting death of Michael Brown, an unarmed teenager.

The grand jury’s decision Monday sparked protests in the St. Louis suburb and beyond.
