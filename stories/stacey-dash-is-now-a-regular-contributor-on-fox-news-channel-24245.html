Stacey Dash Is Now a Regular Contributor on Fox News Channel

May 28, 2014 Media

Stacey Dash’s willingness to speak out about her conservative views has paid off.

The actress has joined Fox News Channel as a regular contributor, the network announced.  A press release states the “Clueless” star will “offer cultural analysis and commentary” in various daytime and primetime programs.

“Stacey is an engaging conversationalist whose distinctive viewpoints among her Hollywood peers have spawned national debates – we’re pleased to have her join Fox News,” said Fox News’ executive vice president of programming, Bill Shine.
