Saks Fifth Avenue Employees Accused Of Stealing $400K In Merchandise

October 8, 2014 Black

Working at Saks Fifth Avenue can be a great job. You get to meet all kinds of people from all walks of life and let’s not forget that employee discount. But for four Saks employees and their outside accomplice, the New York Saks Fifth Avenue flagship store was instead a temptation they couldn’t ignore. Four sales associates conspired with outside help to rob the store of over $400,000 in merchandise, including handbags and shoes.

Tamara Williams, 36, Kriss Rockson, 45, Jason Chance, 25, Alaia Harrison, 20, and Michael Knight-Williams, 44, allegedly purchased approximately $400,000 in luxury goods from Saks’ Midtown flagship store.

The stolen goods included hundreds of handbags and shoes from designer brands including Chanel, Valentino, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, and more.
