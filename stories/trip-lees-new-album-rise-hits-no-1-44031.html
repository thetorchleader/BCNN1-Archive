Trip Lee’s New Album, Rise, Hits No. 1

November 7, 2014 Entertainment

Credit: GETTY

William Lee Barfield III, 26, best known as Christian rapper Trip Lee, has ascended to No. 1 on iTunes Hip Hop albums chart, as well as Billboard Gospel Albums chart.

Presently, he also sits at No. 2 on the rap albums chart, just under T.I.

It is undeniable: the men of Reach Records are having their best year ever.

For gospel fans, this new Holy Hip Hop invasion of their territory is taking some getting used to.

Right now, in the Top 10, there are two other rappers aside from Trip Lee (both from Reach Records) holding chart positions: Lecrae Moore and Andy Mineo.
