Milestones Agency of Metro Detroit and Neighborhood Legal Services Come Together for Black Male Leadership Conference

April 24, 2014 African-American

The Milestones Agency of Metro Detroit and Neighborhood Legal Services MI (NLSM) is hosting a free leadership conference for men, boys, mothers and fathers of sons, and anyone who has a vested interest in the development of our young men in the metro Detroit community.  This year’s theme is: “Black Males are an Asset.”

The conference will be held on April 19, 2014 from 9am – 3pm at the Student Center. It’s located at 5221 Gullen Mall on the campus of Wayne State University.

The conference will feature breakout sessions on financial literacy, the history of hip-hop, the effects of the media and entertainment industries on the mind of our youth, and combating the effects of fatherlessness.
