Former NBA Guard Javaris Crittenton Gets 23-Year Sentence After Pleading Guilty to Murder

April 29, 2015 Sports

Pool / Getty Images North America
Javaris Crittenton received 23-year sentence after agreeing to plea deal.

Former guard Javaris Crittenton pleaded guilty to the 2011 shooting death of a mother of four Wednesday.

Crittenton, who played with the Los Angeles Lakers, Memphis Grizzlies and Washington Wizards during his two-year career, accepted a 23-year jail sentence as part of the plea deal.

Crittenton was aiming for the man who allegedly robbed him days earlier in 2011 when he shot Julian Jones, according to prosecutors.

During a tearful apology to the family of Jones on Wednesday, Crittenton said he wished he could change places with the woman he killed and called it a horrible accident.
