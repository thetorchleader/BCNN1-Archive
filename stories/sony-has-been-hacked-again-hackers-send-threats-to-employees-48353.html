Sony Has Been Hacked Again; Hackers Send Threats to Employees’ Families

December 5, 2014 Technology

Sony staffers received a new email from hackers Friday, this time threatening their families, from the group who calls itself the Guardians of Peace or the “GOP.”

This, after Sony has spent all week shifting email to new servers, instructing staffers to change passwords, and turning back the clock to old-school time-punch cards in order to process employees’ payment. All of these efforts were made by Sony to thwart another attack by the GOP (easily confused with the political party). But an email that landed in many inboxes proved Sony’s scramble is so far insufficient. The email included a warning to all who did not assist the GOP that their families could land in harm’s way.
