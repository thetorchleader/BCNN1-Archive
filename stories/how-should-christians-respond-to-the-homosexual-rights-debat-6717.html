How Should Christians Respond to the Homosexual Rights Debate

June 23, 2012 Uncategorized
From gay rights in the military to ongoing battles over gay marriage, homosexual activism is constantly in the news.
Christian views on sexuality are often marginalized by major media outlets, and Christians are often portrayed as bigots because they don’t approve of homosexuality.
So what’s a believer to do or say in this day and age?
Dr. Alex McFarlan, author of 10 Answers for Skeptics, and the director of apologetics for North Greenville University, addressed that issue and more on the CBN News Channel’s Morning News, June 22. Click play for his comments.
