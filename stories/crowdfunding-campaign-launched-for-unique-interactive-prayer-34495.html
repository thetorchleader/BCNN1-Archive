Crowdfunding Campaign Launched for Unique Interactive “Prayer Motivator Devotional Bible”

August 19, 2014 Books

ATLANTA, GA, (August 19, 2014) – The Prayer Motivator Devotional Bible is an interactive prayer-focused edition of the Bible and is being revised and expanded. The new edition, which highlights over 400 verses and nearly 200 passages on prayer, will be published on November 28th.

In this interactive Bible, readers will be able to access over 500 episodes of the popular Prayer Motivator Devotional podcast, over 500 episodes of the Prayer Motivator Minute podcast, over 100 expositions on Bible passages regarding prayer, and over 100 expository audio messages on prayer by simply scanning a QR code with their smartphone or tablet.

The editor, national bestselling author Daniel Whyte III, says, “We hope this special prayer-focused edition of the Bible will inspire, encourage, and motivate readers to pray daily and throughout the day so that they can receive the benefits and blessings of a prayerful life. This simple volume is designed to motivate readers to pray, because through prayer, all sorts of wonderful things will begin to happen for you, your family, and whatever God has called you to do.”
The Prayer Motivator Devotional Bible includes the following features:
    * Over 400 Verses on Prayer
    * Nearly 200 Passages on Prayer
    * Hundreds of Motivational Quotes on Prayer
    * In-Depth Bible Study Commentary from Matthew Henry
    * Over 500 Podcasts from The Prayer Motivator Devotional
    * Over 500 Podcasts from The Prayer Motivator Minute
    * Over 100 expositions on Bible passages regarding prayer
    * Over 100 expository audio messages on prayer
    * Full Text of the Editor’s book, A Praying Time
    * 50 Princes and Princesses of Prayer
      And much more
The revised and expanded edition of the Bible will also be published on various digital platforms including as an iPhone/iPad app, as an Android app, and as an e-book.
To support the costly process of publishing The Prayer Motivator Devotional Bible and making it available in print, on multiple digital platforms, and to make it available free in both print and digital formats for people in countries around the world who cannot afford it, Torch Legacy Publications has just launched a GoFundMe campaign with a goal of $21,000. Supporters are encouraged to pledge any amount. View the campaign page at the following link: http://www.gofundme.com/prayermotivatorbible.
The publisher is offering several reward levels for those who contribute to the campaign. Supporters can receive one of the following e-books among other things as a “thank you” for their contribution.
  • A Praying Time
  • The Prayer Motivator (Volume 1)
  • The Prayer Motivator (Volume 2)
  • The Prayer Motivator (Volume 3)
  • When Man Began to Pray (Praying Through the Bible, Volume 1)
  • When God Has to Get Your Attention (Praying Through the Bible, Volume 2)
  • What If God’s People Stopped Playing and Started Praying? (Praying Through the Bible, Volume 3)
  • How Prayer Can Help You Have a Great Day Every Day (Praying Through the Bible, Volume 4)
Those interested in contributing can do so online at: http://www.gofundme.com/prayermotivatorbible.
The mission of Torch Legacy Publications is to turn many from darkness to light through presenting a clear, understandable presentation of the Gospel of the Lord Jesus Christ, and the propagation of clear, biblical principles for life change via preaching, teaching, and through multimedia. Find out more at www.torchlegacy.com.
