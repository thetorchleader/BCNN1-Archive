WATCH: Highland Park Presbyterian Church Which Broke Away from Presbyterian Church (USA) Chooses Rev. Bryan H. Dunagan as New Pastor

April 30, 2014 Christian

Rev. Dunagan is married to Alison Wheeler Dunagan, an Atlanta native, and they have 8-month-old twins, Ann and Wheeler.

The Pastor Nominating Committee (PNC) is thrilled to introduce our next Senior Pastor, the Rev. Bryan H. Dunagan, a native Texan who has been serving as the Teaching Pastor at Peachtree Presbyterian Church in Atlanta, Georgia. You, the congregation of Highland Park Presbyterian Church, elected members of the PNC in October 2012. With the input you provided for the Mission Study Report, the PNC has done considerable work on your behalf searching, praying and seeking God’s guidance at every turn. Here’s a first look at Bryan and his family as well as details about why the PNC enthusiastically and unanimously believes God has called Bryan to be the next Senior Pastor of Highland Park Presbyterian Church.
