17-Year-Old Shot by Police during Confrontation Charged with Attempted Murder

November 2, 2014 Black

© John J. Kim/Chicago Tribune/MCT A police officer guards the scene of a police-involved shooting at 51st and Wood Streets early Thursday, Oct. 30, 2014, in Chicago.

A 17-year-old boy was charged with attempted murder after police shot him Wednesday night during a shootout in the Back of the Yards neighborhood on the city’s South Side.

The teen, who was identified by his family as Tykwon Davis, was charged with two felony counts of first-degree attempted murder as a result of the incident, according to a news release from the Chicago Police Department’s Office of News Affairs.

About 11 p.m. Wednesday, police officers were conducting a traffic stop in the 5100 block of South Wood Street when they allegedly saw the 17-year-old exchange gunfire with another male from opposite sides of the street, according to the release.

When the officers approached the 17-year-old and announced who they were, the boy turned and fired the gun in their direction, the release said. The officers then fired back, striking the boy several times.

Police said they recovered a weapon on the scene, and the second offender is not in custody.

The 17-year-old was taken to Stroger Hospital in serious condition. No police officers were injured.
