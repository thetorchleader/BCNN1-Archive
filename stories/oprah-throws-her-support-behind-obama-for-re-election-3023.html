Oprah Throws her Support Behind Obama for Re-election

January 15, 2012 Uncategorized

President Obama can count on Oprah Winfrey’s support in 2012 — even if she won’t make a public spectacle of it.
Influential TV talk show host Oprah Winfrey says she is “100%” behind President Barack Obama as he campaigns for reelection this year.
The influential talk show host, who was a major presence on Obama’s campaign trail in 2008, is praising the President’s leadership and pledging to help him however she can, short of an express endorsement.
“I don’t need to endorse him because I am a 100% supporter of him and I’ve already endorsed him,” Winfrey told CNN on Friday. “If he or his office called me tomorrow and said that they needed me I would do whatever I thought would be of service.”
The non-endorsement endorsement follows a report last April that Winfrey would not publicly endorse Obama during his reelection campaign for fear of alienating viewers who might tune into her Oprah Winfrey Network — a suggestion she dismissed at the time.
Winfrey told CNN she stumped for Obama in 2008 to help educate Americans.
