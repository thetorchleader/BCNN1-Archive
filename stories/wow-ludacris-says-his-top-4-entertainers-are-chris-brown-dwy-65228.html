Wow! Ludacris Says His Top 4 Entertainers Are: Chris Brown, Dwyane Wade, Barack Obama, and Himself

April 3, 2015 Entertainment

Ludacris’ entertainment one-seeds (Original pictures USA TODAY Sports Images/Getty Images)

With eight albums and roles in box office blockbusters and Academy Award winners, Ludacris is a one-seed in the entertainment world. And the Atlanta-born rapper correctly chose three one-seeds to make the Final Four—Duke, Wisconsin, and Kentucky, even choosing the Wildcats to beat Notre Dame on the way.

His bracket’s looking pretty good, as is his weekend. Ludacris stars in Furious 7, which hits theaters today, and his newest album, “Ludaversal,” was released this week. He sat down to talk about both and offer his March Madness picks for our Celeb Pick ‘Em series.

If there were a March Madness for entertainers instead of basketball, who are the other three one-seeds (besides yourself)?

Chris Brown. Dwyane Wade. And Barack Obama.

If you were coaching a one-seed, what would your pregame speech be before playing a lesser team?

“Do you want me to lose my job?” That’s all I need to say, because if you care about me as a coach then you don’t want me to lose my job. And you’re going to go out there and win. Who needs to make some long speech? Just say that one simple thing and you’re good.

What advice would you give to young rappers and actors who are trying to make it?

Believe in yourself, and it can work if you work.
