WATCH: Serena Williams Injures Her Ankle, Comes Back to Win Match

January 15, 2013 Uncategorized

This looked bad.
Five games into her quest to win her fourth consecutive Grand Slam, Serena Williams lunged for a forehand, caught her right foot while sliding into a recovery and crumpled behind the baseline with her face buried in her hands. For a moment, it seemed that the blue hard court at Hisense Arena might do what no woman has in six months: get the best of the tennis superstar.
But following a seven-minute delay, a surgical cutting of her heavily-taped ankle and an equally-heavy re-tape, Serena returned to the court with barely a hobble. She had won four straight games before the injury, then won eight more after, defeating Edina Gallovits-Hall in a first-round match, 6-0, 6-0.
