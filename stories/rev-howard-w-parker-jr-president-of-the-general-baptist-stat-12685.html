Rev. Howard W. Parker Jr., President of the General Baptist State Convention of North Carolina, to Speak at Shaw University Alumni Association Scholarship Banquet on June 14

May 28, 2013 Uncategorized

The annual Shaw University Alumni Association scholarship banquet is June 14 in downtown Fayetteville.

The 6:30 p.m. banquet for the Fayetteville-Cumberland County chapter will be at the Metropolitan Room, 109 Green St.
Individual tickets are $30, and the cost to reserve an eight-person table is $250.
The scheduled speaker is the Rev. Howard W. Parker Jr., president of the General Baptist State Convention of North Carolina, which represents more than 500,000 Missionary Baptists in nearly 1,700 congregations across the state.
Parker is a native of Winston-Salem who majored in religion at Shaw University, the oldest historically black university in the South.
He is senior pastor at Sycamore Hill Missionary Baptist Church of Greenville, where he has served since 1981.
