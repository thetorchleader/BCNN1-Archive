14-Year-Old Girl Shot and Killed While Group of Young People Played with a Gun

January 27, 2015 Black

Detroit Police say a 14-year-old girl was shot and killed after a group of young people were playing with a gun on the city’s east side. A 13-year-old relative is believed to have pulled the trigger.

We’re told the incident happened in the 15000 block of Lappin, north of 7 Mile and west of Kelly Road, on Detroit’s east side.

Police say there was no adult supervision in the home at the time.

Source: WXYZ
