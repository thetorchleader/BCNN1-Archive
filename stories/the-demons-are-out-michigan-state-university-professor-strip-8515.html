The Demons Are Out! Michigan State University Professor Strips Naked In Front of Class

October 3, 2012 Uncategorized

Some of the craziest things happen during an otherwise normal day. We’re headed to work and we see a road rage incident.

Michigan State professor strips naked and starts walking the hallway when police catch up to him.
We’re at Walmart and a couple men run past you with a cart full of Tide and ram the doors to escape. Or, you’re in class at school and your teacher decides he’s going to strip naked.
Some students are telling that last story today after being horrified by their “eccentric” math professor’s erratic behavior during classes Monday.
