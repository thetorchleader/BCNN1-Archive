Kelly Rowland Gives Birth to a Baby Boy, Titan Jewell Witherspoon

November 5, 2014 Entertainment

Kelly Rowland showcases the new TW Steel Canteen Bracelet Watch at Feldmar Watch Company on September 4, 2014 in Los Angeles, California. (Photo by Jason LaVeris/FilmMagic)

It’s a boy!

Singer Kelly Rowland gave birth to a baby boy on Tuesday, reports People.

The 33-year-old singer and husband Tim Witherspoon named their new bundle of joy Titan Jewell Witherspoon.

The baby was born on 1:30 p.m. PT and weighed 7.5 lbs.

“We are thrilled to announce that today we are the proud parents of our first son,” said the former Destiny’s Child member in a statement. “We are blessed to report everyone is healthy and happy!”
