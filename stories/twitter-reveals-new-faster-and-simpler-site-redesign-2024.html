Twitter Reveals New ‘Faster and Simpler’ Site Redesign

December 9, 2011 Uncategorized

Social-networking hub Twitter will roll out a significant redesign of its Web and mobile platforms in coming weeks that promises to be “faster and simpler.”
They’ve launched a website and blog post to explain the future changes.
The layout is cleaner, featuring tabs across the top of the screen: Home, Connect and Discover. Home hosts the main feed of Tweets from other users; Connect is where they can directly contact others; and Discover is where they can find popular topics or hashtags and view the activity of followed users.
The redesign will roll out in the next few weeks, but mobile users can access the new look now by downloading and logging in to Twitter for either iPhone or Android.
