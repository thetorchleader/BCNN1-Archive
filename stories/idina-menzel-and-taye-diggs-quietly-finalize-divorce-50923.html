Idina Menzel and Taye Diggs Quietly Finalize Divorce

January 3, 2015 Entertainment

Idina Menzel and Taye Diggs

Idina Menzel and Taye Diggs quietly finalized their divorce on Dec. 3, according to TMZ.

Menzel, the powerhouse voice behind the hit Frozen song “Let It Go,” and Diggs, a fellow stage actor, had been married for 10 years.

The couple announced their split in December 2013 but divorce papers weren’t filed until November of this year, when Diggs filed the divorce petition in New York, TMZ reports.

Menzel and Diggs have a 5-year-old son named Walker Nathaniel Diggs.
