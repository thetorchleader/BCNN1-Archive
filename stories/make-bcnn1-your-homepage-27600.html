Make BCNN1 Your Homepage

June 22, 2014 Uncategorized

Below are the steps to set BCNN1 as your homepage for the most popular browsers.

Internet Explorer

  1. Click on the “Tools” menu or icon (cog) menu icon at the top of the screen
  2. Click on the “Internet options” item in the menu
  3. Click on the “General” tab in the top left corner of the Internet options window (if it’s not already selected)
  4. Enter “www.blackchristiannews.com” in the “Home page” text box
  5. Click the “OK” button at the bottom of the window

Google Chrome

  1. Click on the menu icon (three horizontal bars) menu icon in the top right of the screen
  2. Click “Settings”
  3. Select “Open a specific page or set of pages”
  4. Click “Set pages” in the “On startup” section
  5. Enter “www.blackchristiannews.com”

If you do not see the “On startup” section, click “Show advanced settings…” at the bottom of the page

Firefox

 

  1. Visit “www.blackchristiannews.com” using Firefox
  2. Click the grey globe icon globe icon to the left of the web address
  3. Drag it to the home icon, to the far right of the web address, and release
  4. Click the “Yes” button

Safari

  1. Click on the “Safari” menu at the top of the screen
  2. Click on the “Preferences…” item in the menu
  3. Click on the “General” tab in the top left corner of the Preferences window (if it’s not already selected)
  4. Select “Homepage” from the “New windows open with” drop-down
  5. Select “Homepage” from the “New tabs open with” drop-down
  6. Enter “www.blackchristiannews.com” in the “Homepage” text box
  7. Close the window
