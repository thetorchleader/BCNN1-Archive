Detroit Police Officer Joseph Weekley, Who Killed 7-Year-Old Girl in Raid, Will Only Face Misdemeanor Charge

October 8, 2014 Black

Aiyana Stanley-Jones
WXYZ-TV DETROIT SCREENSHOT

Michigan’s appeals court upheld the dismissal of Officer Joseph Weekley’s most serious charge in the killing of 7-year-old Aiyana Stanley-Jones during a 2010 raid that was filmed for TV. The most serious charge against Joseph Weekley, the elite Detroit cop who says he accidently shot 7-year-old Aiyana Stanley-Jones while conducting a raid for a murder suspect, has been dropped, the Associated Press reports.

On Monday, Michigan’s appeals court upheld the dismissal of the manslaughter charge.

Wayne County Judge Cynthia Gray Hathaway was the one who originally dismissed the involuntary manslaughter charge saying that there was not enough evidence to indicate that the cop had acted willfully.

Prosecutors had been hoping to have that ruling overturned but to no avail, AP notes.

Although Appeals Judge Michael Talbot agreed with prosecutors that Hathaway had made a mistake, he said that the ruling had to stand because once a defendant was acquitted, it could not be challenged. The appeals court doubled down on that decision on Monday, refusing the request for review.

The only charge that stands against Weekley currently is reckless discharge of a firearm, which is a misdemeanor.
