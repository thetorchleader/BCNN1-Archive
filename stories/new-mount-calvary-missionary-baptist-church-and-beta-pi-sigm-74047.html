New Mount Calvary Missionary Baptist Church and Beta Pi Sigma Sorority Inc. Hosted 3rd Annual Free Community Legal Fair in California

May 17, 2015 Black

Pastor Sonja R. Dawson

In an effort to provide essential resources and services to South Los Angeles residents, New Mount Calvary Missionary Baptist Church and Beta Pi Sigma Sorority Inc.,  will hold the 3rd Annual Free Community Legal Fair on Saturday, May 16, from 9:30 a.m. to 1 p.m. 

The event takes place on the New Mt. Calvary campus located at New Mt. Calvary Missionary Baptist Church is located at 402 East El Segundo Boulevard in Los Angeles.

“Truly, there is a concern for justice in our world today. More than ever, we are seeking to create opportunities for collaboration and cooperation between community, legal, civic and local officials to understand justice and achieve justice,” said the Rev. Dr. Sonja R. Dawson, pastor.

“Last year, we were proud to serve over 100 community members and are looking forward to serving more this year.”
