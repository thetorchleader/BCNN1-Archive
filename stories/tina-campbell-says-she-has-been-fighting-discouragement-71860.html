Tina Campbell Says She Has Been ‘Fighting Discouragement’

May 4, 2015 Entertainment

Photo Credit: GETTY

It’s hard out here without Mary Mary—at least for one-half of the Grammy® Award-winning duo, Tina Campbell.

The 41-year-old has taken to Instagram to thank her fans for their support of her recent solo endeavors and vent her struggles.

Tina explained in a lengthy post that completing and promoting her book I Need A Day To Pray and her debut solo album It’s Personal is something she has “never done before with limited experience, expertise, resources, & help to do it.”

A few weeks ago, the “Destiny” singer revealed that her “staff of me, my hubby, the Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost,” were doing all the required legwork.

Since 2013, the group’s hiatus—which both Tina and Erica Campbell insist is not a breakup—has ushered in new phases in each sister’s life.

Though Erica has thrived, already releasing two #1 albums, earning a GRAMMY® and six STELLAR® awards, Tina admits, “Setbacks have worked to distract and discourage me.”

Yet, Tina said she is determined to “move forward because God is with me and if God be for me, who can be against me-1 John 4:4.”
