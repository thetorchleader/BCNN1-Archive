National Black Church Initiative Calls On All Religious Leaders to Come Together as Mediators to Stop Violence In Israel

August 15, 2014 Christian

Leaders Must Convene in Jerusalem to Bring Peace

The National Black Church Initiative (NBCI), a faith-based coalition of 34,000 churches comprised of 15 denominations and 15.7 million African Americans, beckons leaders of all religious faiths to come together in a collaborative effort to help put an end to the violence in Israel and Palestine. The three great faiths must step up and do what God has commanded them to do through the power of their standings in this world. To do otherwise, is to render God helpless in the midst of this crisis. We condemn both Israel and Hamas for their evil acts against humanity.

Over 1800 Israelis and at least 9,500 Palestinians have been killed, and in the most recent wave of violence 80 percent of those killed have been civilians. This includes the deaths of 131 Israeli and 1,864 Palestinian children. 28,000 Palestinian homes have been destroyed leaving their inhabitants homeless. How long will the world sit back and allow Israel to use USA modern weaponry to kill and maimed the innocents.

President Obama, a Nobel Peace Prize winner, and the US Congress authorization of an additional 225 million dollars for the purpose of rearming Israel to kill the innocent are evil. This act alone renders the moral role of the US in this crisis meaningless. This is the height of — moral hypocrisy calling for peace and arming Israel to killing more innocents

Those who would defend Israel right to defend herself against Hamas rockets is short sighted given the continuous killing and the bombing of innocent targets – like the UN sanctuaries. After the UN repeatedly warned Israel of the where about of its facilities which houses women and children Israel has ignored those warnings and has continued to rain down bombs supplied by the US government killing scores of defenseless and innocent women and children. This is just pure evil. Alan M. Dershowitz, Harvard professor says Israel has a right to kill Hisses even if women and children get caught in the cross fire on CNN. This is the purest of evil.

Hamas should be equally condemned of hiring and firing their rockets close to civilian populations even though there is no definitive evidence supplied by Israel or the world of this act.

These numbers are startling, and religious leaders cannot allow them to continue to rise. The Black Church condemns the violence from both Hamas and Israel. It is imperative that we come together to promote peace and encourage all of the members of our communities to do the same.

Reverend Anthony Evans, NBCI President, is calling together international religious leaders to take the trip to Israel to do the necessary work. Our joint efforts will not only incite, but also facilitate an environment of peace amongst the followers of our respective faiths” Evans said. “When we work together for the greater good, we create an opportunity for peace talks to happen and cease-fires to take hold. The world needs leaders to step up and we are obligated to answer its call.”

Evans went on to condemn ongoing violence in the region, stating that “The Black Christian Church does not join the 95 percent of Israelis whose lips drip with interposition and nullification, as stated by the late Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. 95 percent of the people do not agree with anything but war. This in itself is evil. Those five percent who stand for peace are the children of God.”

NBCI hopes to use its unique international moral authority to call together the religious heads of all faiths to join Reverend Evans in his leadership efforts to put an end to senseless killing.

There must be an end to this perpetual violence and we as religious leaders have the power to make this happen. NBCI urges religious leaders to come together and make this happen. The time is now.

About NBCI
The National Black Church Initiative (NBCI) is a coalition of 34,000 African American and Latino churches working to eradicate racial disparities in healthcare, technology, education, housing, and the environment. NBCI’s mission is to provide critical wellness information to all of its members, congregants, churches and the public. The National Black Church Initiative’s methodology is utilizing faith and sound health science.

The National Black Church Initiative’s purpose is to partner with major organizations and officials whose main mission is to reduce racial disparities in the variety of areas cited above. NBCI offers faith-based, out-of-the-box and cutting edge solutions to stubborn economic and social issues. NBCI’s programs are governed by credible statistical analysis, science based strategies and techniques, and methods that work. Visit our website at www.naltblackchurch.com.
