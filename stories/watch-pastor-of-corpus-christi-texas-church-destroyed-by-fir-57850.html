WATCH: Pastor of Corpus Christi, Texas, Church Destroyed by Fire Vows to Rebuild

February 21, 2015 Black

An arson fire that happened overnight at a local church has church goers asking who would purposely set a church on fire? KRIS 6 News spoke with the pastor of that church today, who says, they’ll rebuild.

Pastor William Lanehart says, “It’s sad to say. You ask the question, ‘Why would someone want to try and burn a church down? You know, why?'”

Pastor Lanehart has been the head of the Apostolic Church of the Living God for almost twenty years. He says they’ve never had any problems with vandalism until recently.

“It is arson, the fire marshall determined it is arson. He even showed the spot right where they set the gasoline on the side of the building and lit the fire. It is arson.”

Pastor Lanehart opened the church with his retirement money back in 1997. He says, things haven’t always been easy, but seeing his congregation come together today is exactly why he chose to become a pastor and open his own church.

“When you’ve invested into it and done all this stuff, it’s good to see everybody come together in light of all the things we’ve gone through. You know how people are about churches and things and yet, we can still come together and be a church.”
