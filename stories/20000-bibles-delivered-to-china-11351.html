20,000 Bibles Delivered to China

March 12, 2013 Uncategorized

Some faithful believers have followed Christ for 50 years in China without access to God’s Word. In fact, about half of China’s 400 million believers don’t have a Bible, says Bibles for China.
That’s why they’re buying and distributing Bibles–complete Bibles printed legally in China–free of charge to rural Chinese believers. A distribution team returned last week from the Communist nation, where they gave out 20,000 copies of God’s Word in Mandarin.
“The team is tired physically but overjoyed and energized in our spirits,” says President Wendell Rovenstine in a recent Bibles for China e-mail update.
“We fully believe Isaiah 55:10-11, ‘As the rain and the snow come down from heaven, and do not return to it without watering the earth and making it bud and flourish, so that it yields seed for the sower and bread for the eater, so is My word that goes out from My mouth: It will not return to Me empty, but will accomplish what I desire and achieve the purpose for which I sent it.'”
Pray that the Bibles will help people draw closer to the Lord.
