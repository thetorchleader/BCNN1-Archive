Sheila Schuller Coleman Moves Ministry to Movie Theater, Holds First Service

March 19, 2012 Uncategorized

Just a short walk from where her father starting preaching more than 50 years ago, Sheila Schuller Coleman on Sunday told the 100 or so congregants in a rented movie theater that she is embracing the challenge of forming a new church.


Speaking in an AMC theater at the Outlets at Orange, the first temporary location for the new Hope Center of Christ, Schuller Coleman told the congregation “not to have harsh words for any other church.
“There are some people who would try to pit one church against another, but we are not going to do that,” she said. “Here at Hope Center, we cheer every other church.”
With popcorn buckets being passed to collect offerings, Schuller Coleman told the congregation that the $835 to rent the theater was paid by a donor. She said the newly formed church is still waiting for its tax ID number.
“You have a right to know that,” she said.
