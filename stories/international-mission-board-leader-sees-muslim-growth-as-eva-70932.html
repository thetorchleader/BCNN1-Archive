International Mission Board Leader Sees Muslim Growth as Evangelism Opportunity for Church

April 30, 2015 Christian

An evangelism specialist sees both challenges and opportunities ahead for Christians as the Muslim population continues to grow around the world.

According to a recent study from the Pew Research Center, Islam is rising at such a fast rate that by 2050 the number of Muslims in the world will nearly match the number of Christians.

John Brady, vice president of global engagement for the International Mission Board of the Southern Baptist Convention, says this is a challenge to believers because the biggest barrier to reaching Muslims is fear. But he says the church must remember that Christians have life’s true answer.

“Let them see that we’re people of prayer, that we’re people of the book,” says Brady, “and that we’re willing to do all the things that they sometimes think we don’t do. And we’re good moral people as well, because Christ living in us, and we’re not trying to hurt them but we’re trying to share with them what we know is truly life-giving.”
