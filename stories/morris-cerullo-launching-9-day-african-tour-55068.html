Morris Cerullo Launching 9-Day African Tour

February 4, 2015 Christian

Morris Cerullo

World-renowned evangelist Morris Cerullo, President of faith-based organization, Morris Cerullo World Evangelism, is embarking next month upon a courageous 9-day tour throughout the continent of Africa.

This 83-year-old Christian statesman and international evangelist has been traveling to the developing nations of the world for 68 years. He has dedicated his life to helping hurting people. This 9-day trip to Africa will include training meetings in the following cities: Lome, Togo; Cotonou, Benin; Lagos, Nigeria; Libreville, Gabon; Kinshasa, Congo; and Douala, Cameroon.

This tireless octogenarian will also consult with political leaders of these African nations to discuss economic aid, food and medicine for the beleaguered countries hit hardest by poverty, as well as political and economic corruption. Over the past six decades, Morris Cerullo has met with numerous heads of state who have requested mentorship, prayer and spiritual counsel. A number of these leaders claim their beginnings at Morris Cerullo’s training sessions, held at the former El Cortez Hotel in downtown San Diego. Morris Cerullo has continually consulted these pastoral and governmental leaders who now conduct humanitarian aid in their own countries through feeding, medical care and education programs for orphans, school children and the poor.
