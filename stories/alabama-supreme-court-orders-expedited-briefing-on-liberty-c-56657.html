Alabama Supreme Court Orders Expedited Briefing on Liberty Counsel Petition

February 13, 2015 National

Montgomery, AL – Late Friday afternoon, the Alabama Supreme Court ordered the probate judges who are issuing same sex marriage licenses to respond to Liberty Counsel’s mandamus petition.

“I am pleased that the Alabama Supreme Court has ordered an expedited briefing and is proceeding on a fast track,” said Mat Staver, Founder and Chairman of Liberty Counsel. “I suspect we will hear from the Alabama Supreme Court on our petition very soon,” said Staver.

Eighty-two percent of Alabama voters affirmed marriage as the union of one man and one woman. Last month, U.S. District Court Judge Callie Granade ruled against Alabama’s marriage laws. Her ruling does not automatically create a law that validates same-sex marriages, as is being alleged by the media and the ACLU. Judge Grande does not have authority to order all probate judges to issue marriage licenses to same-sex couples. The law in Alabama remains that marriage is the union of one man and one woman.

About Liberty Counsel
Liberty Counsel is an international nonprofit, litigation, education, and policy organization dedicated to advancing religious freedom, the sanctity of life, and the family since 1989, by providing pro bono assistance and representation on these and related topics.
