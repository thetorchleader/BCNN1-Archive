Love the Kentucky Derby? Here Are Some Drinks, Dishes, and Desserts

May 1, 2015 From Around the Block

© POPSUGAR Photography / Anna Monette Roberts

MINT JULEPS
In classic Derby style, serve guests a refreshing mint julep made with your Kentucky bourbon of choice.

May 1 kicks off the 141st Kentucky Derby! Watching the horse race is a great excuse to host a party and an even better excuse to enjoy some delicious Southern cuisine. Doesn’t drinking a mint julep and nibbling on pimento-cheese-topped biscuits sound like an ideal way to spend a Spring afternoon? If you’re ready to take a culinary trip (without the airline ticket), browse the best of our Southern recipes now.

CARRIAGE HOUSE BISCUITS
You don’t have to make a trip to The Carriage House in Natchez, MS, to enjoy these buttery short biscuits.

STUFFED MUSHROOMS
Combine sun-dried tomatoes, pine nuts, and herbs, and you’ve got one heck of a filling for stuffed mushrooms
