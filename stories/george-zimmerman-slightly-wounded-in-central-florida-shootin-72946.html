George Zimmerman Slightly Wounded In Central Florida Shooting

May 11, 2015 National

George Zimmerman, who won a controversial acquittal in the 2012 shooting death of Trayvon Martin, was involved in a shooting Monday in Central Florida, authorities said.

Lake Mary Police Department tweeted that “LMPD has responding to a shooting involving George Zimmerman. More information to follow.”

Lake Mary Police Chief Steve Bracknell said it appeared Zimmerman suffered a minor gunshot wound, WESH in Orlando reported. WESH said Zimmerman’s vehicle appeared to have a bullet hole in the passenger’s window.

“He walked normally into the ambulance, so he wasn’t being helped or nothing,” witness Ricardo Berrare told the station.
