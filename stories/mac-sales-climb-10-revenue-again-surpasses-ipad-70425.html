Mac Sales Climb 10%, Revenue Again Surpasses iPad

April 28, 2015 Technology, zTransfer-UCN

(Credit: Michael Homnick)

Apple on Monday said it sold 4.56 million Macs in the first three months of 2015, an increase of 10% over the same period last year.

Revenue for the Mac line was $5.6 billion, $187 million more than for the iPad, the second time the aged computer line has bested the sleeker tablet in the last three quarters.

Mac unit sales were under the average call of 4.7 million by more than three dozen analysts, according to a summary published by Forbes on Sunday, but higher than the estimate generated by research firm IDC last week. IDC had pegged global Mac sales at 4.45 million for the quarter, which would have been an 8% increase over 2014.

For the first quarter, IDC tagged the personal computer industry as down almost 7%, while rival Gartner had tapped the overall business at a 5% decline. Apple regularly outperforms the PC industry as a whole, even though its machines are considerably more expensive, and did so again in the first quarter.

The Mac ASP, or “average selling price,” in the quarter was $1,231, a drop of $27 from the quarter immediately prior and more than $100 less than the same quarter the year before.
