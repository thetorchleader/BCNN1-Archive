WATCH: Will Anyone Stop Iran from Destroying Israel?

May 31, 2012 Uncategorized

Former Israeli military intelligence chief Amos Yadlin says allowing Iran to build a nuclear weapon is more dangerous for Israel than a military strike on Iran’s nuclear facilities.

Speaking Tuesday night at a major conference, Yadlin Amos warned that Israel needs to have a strategic plan for an attack, as well as a plan for the day after.
His comments came after Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said nuclear talks with Iran are not slowing the Islamic regime’s progress toward enriching uranium.
The U.S. took part in Baghdad talks last week, and more negotiations are scheduled next month in Moscow.
The nuclear talks continue despite a vow by Iran’s military chief of staff that his country will pursue “the full annihilation of the zionist regime of Israel.”
