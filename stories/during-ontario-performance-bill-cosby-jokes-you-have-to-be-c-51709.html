During Ontario Performance, Bill Cosby Jokes ‘You Have to Be Careful Drinking Around Me’

January 9, 2015 Entertainment

In a move that could be construed as arrogance or simply genius comedic timing, during a comedy show, Bill Cosby briefly referenced the sexual abuse allegations against him.

“You have to be careful drinking around me,” he joked during an Ontario performance.

According to the Associated Press, Cosby made the remark at his second performance in a row after a string of cancellations that followed sexual assault allegations from more than 15 women. Some of the women accused Cosby of drugging them by slipping something in their drinks before he assaulted them

A woman who got up from one of the front rows and walked past the stage was asked by Cosby where she was going. When she answered that she was going to the lobby to grab a drink Cosby responded with the snide remark.

A few minutes later a heckler yelled at Cosby that he was a rapist.

When the crowd booed in response to the outburst, Cosby said, “No, no, stop,” while waving his hands.

The man was removed.
