Residents of New York Town Upset After Police Describe Shooting Suspect as ‘Dark Negro’

August 11, 2014 Black

Shooting suspect Shamir Allen’s description by the Lockport, N.Y., Police Department ‘is insulting (and) disrespecting,’ one critic says. (CNN)

An upstate New York police department has caught heat after it described a suspect in a string of shootings as a “dark Negro” in a line on his mugshot about his complexion.

Shamir Allen, 19, was stamped with the offensive, antiquated description by Lockport Police Department officials last week after being picked up by cops.

Black leaders from across the region have condemned the “disgusting” use of the phrase by the Lockport Police Department, calling the term “ignorant” and “insensitive.”

“That word was developed during slavery to subjugate, discriminate, to make people feel bad about themselves,” Erie County legislator Betty Jean Grant told WGRZ-TV.

“This right here is insulting, it is disrespecting, it is inaccurate and it’s a word that should have gone away at least since the 1960s,” she added.

Columnist and author Eva Doyle, who writes about African and African-American culture, said, “I think it’s a lack of education.”

Said Frank Mesiah, president of the NAACP Buffalo Branch: “Apparently, this shows there’s certain aspects of western New York where it appears racism is alive and well, especially within a police department.”
