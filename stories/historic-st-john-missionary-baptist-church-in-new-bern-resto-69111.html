Historic St. John Missionary Baptist Church in New Bern Restores Bell Tower

April 22, 2015 Black

After $100,000 and a year’s restoration, St. John Missionary Baptist Church on Walt Bellamy Drive will ring its bell at 4 p.m. Sunday in a rededication ceremony for the restored bell tower.
Chuck Beckley/Sun Journal

After about 50 years of silence, the bell at St. John Missionary Baptist Church in New Bern will ring out Sunday.

It has taken a year of work to restore the bell tower at the church on Walt Bellamy Drive to save it from years of deterioration and the threat of falling.

A re-dedication service with a ringing of the bell is set for 4 p.m. Sunday.

Pastor John Pierce said it was a blessing that the fundraising and work is finally done.

“It’s a great relief because it has been a long time coming,” Pierce said. “We are ecstatic that it is finished. It’s just wonderful. We were so blessed with community support to help us through it.”

About 10 local churches helped in the restoration effort, providing more than 10 percent of the funding for the bell tower, Pierce said.

The total project cost $100,000, nearly twice as much as first anticipated, and the church had to take out a $50,000 loan from South Bank to complete the work, he said.
