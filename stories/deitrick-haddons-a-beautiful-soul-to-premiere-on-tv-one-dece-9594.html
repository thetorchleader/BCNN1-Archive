Deitrick Haddon's "A Beautiful Soul" to Premiere on TV One December 22nd

December 21, 2012 Uncategorized

On Saturday, December 22nd, TV One will celebrate the holiday season with the world television premiere of the Tyscot Film A BEAUTIFUL SOUL featuring Deitrick Haddon at 8:00-10:00 PM/ET.
Loosely based off of the classic Christmas films, “It’s a Wonderful Life and “A Christmas Carol”, A Beautiful Soul puts a modernized twist on the timeless story of second chances.
Gospel artist Deitrick Haddon stars as Andre Stephens, an R&B superstar who has success, fame and fortune but has lost his way spiritually. After a traumatic event, Stephens is forced to examine his ways and make the ultimate decision that could change the course of his life forever.
The film also features Lesley-Ann Brandt from CSI: NY, Robert Ri’chard from The Vampire Diaries, Golden Brooks from Hart of Dixie, Vanessa Bell Calloway from Go On and cameos from John Salley and Bishop Noel Jones.
