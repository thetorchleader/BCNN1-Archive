Actress Vanessa Williams Hit with Demand for $370,000 in Unpaid Taxes

August 26, 2014 Entertainment

LUCY NICHOLSON/REUTERS
Vanessa Williams, seen arriving at Monday’s Primetime Emmy Awards in Los Angeles, was hit with a $370,000 tax bill from the IRS.

The Internal Revenue Service is demanding a whopping $369,249.89 from the 1984 Miss America.

Not even Miss America can dodge Uncle Sam.

The Internal Revenue Service demanded actress and former pageant queen Vanessa Williams pay up for nearly $370,000 in unpaid taxes, The Smoking Gun reports.

The IRS issued the lien earlier this month for the 1984 Miss America’s faulty 2011 tax return. The government organization is now demanding a whopping $369,249.89 from the actress.

Williams lives in Chappaqua, N.Y., but her IRS paperwork was filed under her accounting firm’s Manhattan address.

Williams was the first African-American woman to earn the Miss America Crown.
