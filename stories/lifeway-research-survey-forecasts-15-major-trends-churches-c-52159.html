LifeWay Research Survey Forecasts 15 Major Trends Churches Can Expect In 2015

January 13, 2015 Christian

Even though God’s Word never changes, His Church is always transforming. And according to new survey, churches are forecasted to undergo 15 major trends this year that are expected to evolve into permanent fixtures for congregations across the United States – ones that will likely change the way Americans do church.

Studying churches from coast to coast, LifeWay Research found 15 movements that will be noticed by America’s churchgoers throughout the year. LifeWay, a Christian organization designed to equip church leaders with insight and advice to help them effectively minister to their congregations and communities in culturally relevant ways, conducted the study to show Christians different “tipping points” that it predicts will substantially impact their fellowship, devotional times, and other facets of their spiritual lives.

Following are the 15 major trends predicted for America’s churches in 2015.

1. Smaller group gatherings ─ Intimidating mega-church amphitheaters often seen on television while televangelists speak to the masses on balconies and multi-tiered seating will likely give way to smaller sanctuaries and more Sunday services to make for a more intimate setting.

“The era of the large worship gathering is waning,” says Dr. Thom S. Rainer, who serves as the president and CEO of LifeWay Christian Resources of the Southern Baptist Convention. “Churches that are growing will likely do so through multiple services, multiple venues, and multiple sites. This trend will accelerate through the growing influence of Gen X and the Millennials.”

2. Increased difficulty in matching prospective pastors with churches with pastoral vacancies ─ Plugging in the right pastor with the right church will become an increasing struggle for many churches.

“This trend is growing and frustrating to both pastors and those in churches seeking pastors,” Rainer reports. “It is particularly frustrating for those churches that use the pastoral search committee model. I will not be surprised to see that model begin to change in 2015.”

3. The tipping point for small groups ─ Accountability and intimate “Acts chapter 2 fellowship” is on the horizon for more and more churches, as an increased number of Christians are seeing the value and spiritual rewards from bonding with a smaller number of fellow Christians – instead of just meeting in large impersonal sanctuaries.

“The evidence for the efficacy of small groups in the local church is too overwhelming to be ignored,” Rainer points out. “I see a new movement of ‘groups’ taking place that will be similar in growth as the Sunday school movement was in the late 19th Century through the first half of the 20th century.”

4. The beginnings of prayer movement in our churches ─ The unparalleled value of coming before God in prayer is also being realized in the group setting, where sermons, lectures, classes and potlucks are no longer the only gatherings more Christians are witnessing at their churches.

“I am seeing the growth of more and more organizations dedicated to prayer in the local church,” LifeWay’s president shares. “I am observing this passion become a greater emphasis with pastors, particularly Millennial pastors. This movement will accelerate in popular Christian culture with the release of the next movie by the Kendrick brothers in 2015.”

5. Continued increased in the number of multi-site churches ─ Instead of expanding church sanctuaries to look like high-capacity movie theaters of school-sized auditoriums, many churches are increasingly adding new campuses around town and in other cities to continue their increase in numbers while maintaining an intimate setting for their congregations.

“Two years ago, the multi-site movement in America reached a tipping point,” Rainer informed. “Their growth will continue unabated in 2015.”
