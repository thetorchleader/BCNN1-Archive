‘Being Mary Jane’ Set to Return in February 2015

November 4, 2014 Entertainment

Fans of BET’s scripted show Being Mary Jane will have to wait a little longer for their show to return. BET announced that Season 2 the popular show starring Gabrielle Union as Mary Jane Paul, won’t return to the airways until Tuesday, February 3rd.

The good news is that this season has been upped from 8 to 12 1 hour episodes with a season finale on April 21st.

Creator Mara Brock Akil and her husband director Salim Akil are back as the behind-the-scenes creative team. Earlier this year, BET announced that The Game, also created by Akil would be finishing up its run.

Via the press release, the second season of Being Mary Jane will find Mary Jane  once again juggling more than she can handle. On the home front, while both of her brothers seem to be getting their lives on the right track, her mother and niece are at war, causing everyone in the family to choose sides.
