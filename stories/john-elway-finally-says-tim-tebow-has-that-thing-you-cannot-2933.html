John Elway Finally Says Tim Tebow has that Thing You Cannot Teach

January 11, 2012 Uncategorized

John Elway has noticed an interesting trait regarding Tim Tebow.
“He is one of those guys who gets calmer in tougher situations,” the Denver Broncos’ executive vice president for football operations tells USA TODAY. “To me, that is a trait you cannot teach. He has that knack of staying calm in tough situations.”
While Elway, the Broncos’ two-time Super Bowl champion and Hall of Fame quarterback, doesn’t always like Tebow’s sometimes-flawed passing mechanics, he appreciates the young left-hander’s attitude after a defeat.
“He took the Kansas City loss very seriously,” Elway says of their 7-3 defeat in Week 17. “He was upset with the way he played. That just told me what kind of competitor he is. I think, sometimes, that doesn’t come across to the general public.”
