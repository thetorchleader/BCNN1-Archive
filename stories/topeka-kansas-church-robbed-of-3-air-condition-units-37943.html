Topeka, Kansas Church Robbed of 3 Air Condition Units

October 7, 2014 Black

Members of the Oakland United Methodist Church in Topeka spent much of Monday afternoon trying to understand why thieves robbed the church sometime Sunday night.

Church spokesman Leroy Cornell says three large air conditioning units were stolen sometime Sunday night or early Monday morning.

“It’s sad,” Cornell told Kansas First News “for anybody to get ripped off on these air conditioning units. You know, we just happen to be a number in somebody’s book.”

Cornell says despite the fact the three units, valued at about $20,000, were kept inside a padlocked, wire-enclosed space, the thieves didn’t seem to have any problems stealing the units.

Another church member says the loss was discovered as volunteers arrived at the church Monday afternoon to prepare for their “Harvest Mondays” food service to the needy. He says a police report was filed, and they were told the thieves probably stole the units for the copper they contained.

Church spokesmen say they hope to raise the money to get the units replaced by next spring.
