Richard Sherman and Girlfriend, Ashley Moss, Welcome Baby Boy

February 5, 2015 Sports

What’s better than winning a Super Bowl? Seattle cornerback Richard Sherman has a good answer for that question.

“My son sure does know how to make an entrance! 2/5/15… Is it a coincidence or is he just that clever?! Either way I’m ecstatic,” the Seahawks cornerback tweeted early Thursday.

Sherman and his girlfriend, Ashley Moss, weren’t expecting the baby until Feb. 12.

Sports Illustrated reporter Peter King said the baby’s name is Rayden in a congratulatory tweet Thursday morning. Hope coach Pete Carroll isn’t horribly disappointed.  Last week he said,“Can’t wait to see little Petey.”

On Facebook Thursday afternoon, Sherman added, “Haven’t felt anything better than this! Thank you God such a blessing! And thank you 12s for all the love and support.”

Sherman revealed that his girlfriend, a Mercer Island native who began dating Sherman after he became a Seahawk in 2011, was pregnant during the Super Bowl, leading to much discussion of whether he would face a conflict with playing in the game.

Sherman ended up facing no decision — Moss attended the Super Bowl — and said after the game he was surprised at the reaction.
