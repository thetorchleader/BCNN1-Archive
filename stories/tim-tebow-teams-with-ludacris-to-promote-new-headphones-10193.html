Tim Tebow Teams with Ludacris to Promote New Headphones

January 18, 2013 Uncategorized

It’s no secret that with the success of Dr. Dre’s Beats line, boutique headphones are seriously in style nowadays, and even Tim Tebow is getting in on the game.
On Thursday Tebow officially launched his signature series of headphones at the 2013 International CES conference in Las Vegas, according to Mashable. The headphones are a collaboration between Tebow and Soul Electronics, a company founded by rapper Ludacris.
Among the available items under Tebow’s line are the Combat+, which has noise-cancelling properties but is designed for athletes and heavy stress. However, another series of headphones might raise some eyebrows coming from Tebow, as the quarterback has named his headphones designed for airplane travel “Jet.”
