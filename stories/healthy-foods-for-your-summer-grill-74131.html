Healthy Foods for Your Summer Grill

May 17, 2015 From Around the Block

STEAK SANDWICH WITH GRILLED ONIONS
All modesty aside, this is the ultimate steak sandwich — marinated grilled flank steak mingles with hefty slices of grilled onion, ripe tomato, and peppery arugula leaves. Recipe: Steak Sandwich with Grilled Onions
Skip the creamy potato salads and fatty burgers. Instead, try some of these healthy grilling options.MOJITO-RUBBED CHICKEN WITH GRILLED PINEAPPLE
What’s better than sipping an ice-cold mojito at your barbecue? Eating a mojito-inspired dinner! Give your usual grilled chicken some major mojo with the essence of mint and lime. Garnish with sweet-as-summer grilled pineapple. Recipe: Mojito-Rubbed Chicken with Grilled Pineapple

SUMMER SQUASH AND CHICKEN
That charred taste of grilled chicken thighs adds depth of flavor. Take it one step further by marinating the tastiest and cheapest part of the bird in a zesty lemon mixture. With grilled squash, this dish is super-fast to prepare! Recipe: Summer Squash and Chicken

LEMON-MINT CHICKEN CUTLETS ON WATERCRESS
It doesn’t get much fresher — or faster to prepare — than this chicken-on-salad creation. Peppery watercress perfectly balances the perky, mint-infused dressing. Recipe: Lemon-Mint Chicken Cutlets on Watercress

CURRIED CHICKEN WITH MANGO AND CANTALOUPE SLAW
Cilantro, ginger, curry, and yogurt combine to create a great dressing and marinade for this healthy yet zesty grilled chicken salad. Recipe: Curried Chicken with Mango and Cantaloupe Slaw

CORN AND PEPPER JACK QUESADILLAS
Craving a cheesy quesadilla but concerned about the added fat? Not to worry. Our recipe includes low-fat Monterey Jack and reduced-fat tortillas — or go for whole grain and boost your fiber while enjoying this tasty app. Recipe: Corn and Pepper Jack Quesadillas

TURKEY CUTLETS WITH MELON SALSA
Prosciutto-wrapped turkey with a sweet and spicy salsa makes the perfect light, fresh summer dinner. Just be sure when purchasing your turkey cutlets to get a thicker cut than the thin scaloppines. Recipe: Turkey Cutlets with Melon Salsa
