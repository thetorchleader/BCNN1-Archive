New York Police Pull Gun On Young Black People to Stop Snowball Fight

February 1, 2015 Black

Police in New Rochelle, N.Y., draw guns on teens to stop a snowball fight.
YOUTUBE SCREENSHOT

A New York community is up in arms after video footage surfaced of a New Rochelle, N.Y., police officer holding several teens at gunpoint after responding to a 911 call about a disturbance that turned out to be a snowball fight, according to Raw Story, citing a report at Talk of the Sound.

In the video, the officer can be seen pointing his weapon at the kneeling teens earlier this week, telling them “Don’t f*cking move, guys,” as he approaches them while speaking into radio, the report notes.

A female narrator who filmed the incident explains what led up to the roundup: “They were having a snowball fight,” she says. “This group of guys was having a snowball fight and now a cop has a gun on them.”
