Desmond Tutu’s Daughter to Speak at Methodist Church In Nashville, Tennessee

September 8, 2014 Christian

(Photo: Larry McCormack / File / The Tennessean )

Nontombi Naomi Tutu, social activist and daughter of Nobel Peace Prize winner Archbishop Desmond Tutu, will speak at Gordon Memorial United Methodist Church at 8 and 11 a.m. Sept. 21 as the church celebrates its 138th year.

“Ms. Tutu’s work is inordinate, and her spirituality, leading all people to love and inclusion, is the heart of game-changing power,” said Senior Pastor Vance Ross in a news release.

The Rev. Candace M. Lewis, founder of New Life Community United Methodist Church, also will speak. Lewis is the first African-American female director of Path One, which seeks to plant new churches through the United Methodist organization.
