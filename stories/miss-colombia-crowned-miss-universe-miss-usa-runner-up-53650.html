Miss Colombia Crowned Miss Universe; Miss USA Runner-Up

January 26, 2015 Entertainment

Miss Colombia Paulina Vega is crowned Miss Universe 2014. (Photo: Timothy A. Clary, AFP/Getty Images)

She was this close to the crown!

Miss USA Nia Sanchez stood face-to-face with Miss Colombia Paulina Vega stood waiting for the big announcement at the end of Sunday’s Miss Universe competition in Miami.

“Take a deep breath,” said co-host Thomas Roberts, “This is a very special moment in your lives.”

And then he announced that the new Miss Universe is … “Colombia!”

Darn. That left Miss USA as the runner-up.
