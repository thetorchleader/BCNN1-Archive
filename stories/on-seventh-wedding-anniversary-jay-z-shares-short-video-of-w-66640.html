On Seventh Wedding Anniversary, Jay-Z Shares Short Video of Wedding to Beyoncé

April 10, 2015 Entertainment

Okay, so traditionally you’re supposed to celebrate your seventh wedding anniversary with a gift of wool or copper. Why? No idea.

But we’re glad Jay Z decided to celebrate with this amazing video of America’s version of a royal wedding instead!

Check out this never-before-seen footage from his and Beyoncé’s wedding on April 4, 2008!

Do they look crazy in love or what? Just look at how they gaze into each others eyes, before he slips a ring on her finger (with flawless blue nails), and they walk down the aisle as Mr. and Mrs. Shawn Carter.
