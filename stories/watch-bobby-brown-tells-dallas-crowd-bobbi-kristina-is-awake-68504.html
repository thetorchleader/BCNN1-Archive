WATCH: Bobby Brown Tells Dallas Crowd Bobbi Kristina ‘Is Awake’

April 19, 2015 Black, Entertainment

Bobby Brown and Bobbi Kristina Brown
SPLASH NEWS ONLINE; WENN

Could Bobby Brown have dropped a hint to concert-goers in Dallas that his daughter, Bobbi Kristina, is on the road to recovery?

On Saturday night (April 18) during a show at the Verizon Theatre, the singer told the audience “Bobbi is awake. She’s watching me.” Watch the video below via TMZ.

It’s unclear what Brown actually meant about the comment. Bobbi Kristina has been in a coma since being found unresponsive in a bathtub in her Georgia home in late January.

Billboard has reached out to Brown’s lawyer, Christopher Brown of Brown & Rosen LLC, for comment.

In early April, Brown made his first public appearance since the hospitalization of Bobbi Kristina, and was visibly emotional.
