Modern Ways to Display Your Digital Pictures

April 25, 2015 From Around the Block

Traditional frames can be so boring (not to mention cost way too much money). Revamp your home and finally display those digital pictures in a modern way the whole family will love.

UPCYCLE AN OLD WINDOW INTO A FRAME
Put different small pictures in each pane, or use one large print to add a textural element to the photo that’s totally on trend.

TURN A PICTURE INTO LARGE-SCALE ART
That cool vacation pic? Transform it into gallery-worthy art decor for your wall in just a few shorts steps. Get the tutorial here.

MAKE A CLOTHESLINE DISPLAY
Hang a handful of your favorite pictures and be able to easily switch them up every season. Get the tutorial here.

DISPLAY YOUR COLLECTION ON COASTERS
You shouldn’t have to limit your photo displays to the walls or mantle. These cuties are perfect for those Instagram snaps you love.

TRANSFER YOUR PHOTOS TO WOOD
When everyone is using canvas to blow up their photos, you’ll be ahead of the game with wood. Use a large piece of lumber to easily up your decor skills with this textured display.

CUT PICTURES BORDERS INTO SHAPES
Instead of picking out a traditional square frame, opt to cut out your own unique shape and add more personality to the room.

TURN A CURTAIN WIRE INTO A MINI DISPLAY
This setup requires no effort to execute and infuses a fun, playful vibe in the room. Get the tutorial here.

BUILD A MONOGRAM PHOTO CLIPBOARD
Gallery walls can sometimes come off as too perfect and flat. Add a little texture—and give the gallery an anchor point—with this monogrammed clipboard. You can mix and match with different colors, materials, and photo sizes underneath to personalize your display in a much more meaningful way. Get the tutorial here.
