16-Year-Old Who Drove from Ohio to Join Protests in Ferguson says Experience Was an Eye-Opener

August 26, 2014 Black

On Aug. 19, my parents and I drove to Ferguson, Missouri, to join the protests over the shooting of Michael Brown the previous week. Brown was only two years older than I am, and he got shot for no apparent reason. 

It made me look at how being black affects how we bring police into a situation. My family and I stood on the side of the road holding signs and chanting slogans such as “No justice no peace.”

I got interviewed by a St. Louis Public Broadcasting affiliate and then was on National Public Radio. I got to see African-American culture in the midst of a crisis, which was very interesting to me. The community was playing a role in the protest, by honking or holding signs.

Growing up in Athens, Ohio, I know that everybody knows me and doesn’t see me as a scary African-American teenager who might hurt them. It makes me very scared that when I leave Athens, I will not be judged by my personality but by my race.
