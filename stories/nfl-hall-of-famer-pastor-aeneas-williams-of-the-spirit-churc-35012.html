NFL Hall of Famer, Pastor Aeneas Williams of The Spirit Church Seeks to Make a Difference in Ferguson

August 23, 2014 Black

We know him as a pastor and now as a Hall of Famer. But what you may not have known is Aeneas Williams’ church, The Spirit Church, is located in Ferguson, Missouri.   Now he’s out in that community trying to make a difference following the unrest after the shooting death of Michael Brown.

One way Pastor Aeneas wants to change things is by helping to united the country.  He’s asking everyone to take to twitter using the hashtag #United with a picture of them holding up their index finger.

Pastor Aeneas challenged Mayor Slay, Rams coach Jeff Fisher, Missouri Highway Patrol Captain Ron Johnson and a host of others including President Barack Obama.

Send your tweet to @aeneas35 with the #unity
