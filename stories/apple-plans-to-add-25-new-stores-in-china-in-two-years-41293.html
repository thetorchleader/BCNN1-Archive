Apple Plans to Add 25 New Stores in China in Two Years

October 23, 2014 Technology

Apple Inc. plans to add 25 new stores in the Greater China region in the next two years, bringing the total number of stores from 15 to 40, chief executive officer Tim Cook today told Sina, a major Chinese news portal.

It is just a matter of time before China becomes Apple’s biggest source of revenue, according to the Chinese transcript of the interview. Sina didn’t publish Cook’s English remarks.

Sales of the Greater China region stood at $ 5.8 billion in the three months ended on Sept 27, accounting for 14% of Apple’s total revenue.

Cook’s China trip follows the Oct 17 debut of the iPhone 6 and 6 Plus in mainland China.
