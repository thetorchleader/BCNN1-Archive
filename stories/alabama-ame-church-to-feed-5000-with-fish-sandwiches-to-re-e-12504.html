Alabama AME Church to Feed 5,000 with Fish Sandwiches to Re-enact Biblical Scene

May 19, 2013 Uncategorized

The African Methodist Episcopal Church plans to feed a crowd of 5,000 with fish sandwiches on Saturday at the former Daniel Payne College campus on Daniel Payne Drive.
It’s called “Feed the 5,000,” a symbolic re-enactment of the biblical scene in which Jesus fed a crowd with five loaves and two fishes, which multiplied to feed a crowd of 5,000. (Matthew 14:13-21; Mark 6:30-44; Luke 9:10-17; John 6:1-15) 
“The whole idea is that in this changing world, God still speaks,” said AME Bishop James L. Davis. “This is our way of saying God’s word is still the same. He still does what he’s always done. He still will do what he’s always promised. We dramatically show that through the feeding of the 5,000.”
The event begins Saturday, May 18, at 9:30 a.m. and concludes at 3 p.m., with the Rev. Jamal Bryant, pastor of Empowerment Temple AME Church of Baltimore, preaching, music by 300-voice choir, along with rappers and dancers.
“”The word of God does not change,” Davis said. “God’s still loving and forgiving and merciful.”
The feeding will take place on the campus of the defunct Daniel Payne College, an AME school that closed down years ago. The last remaining building on the campus was damaged in the April 27, 2011 tornado. It was razed after that, Davis said.
