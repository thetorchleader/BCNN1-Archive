WATCH: H. B. Charles Jr. Interviews Florida Pastor Stephen Nelson Rummage

May 19, 2015 Christian

Pastor Stephen Nelson Rummage

Stephen Nelson Rummage is the Senior-Pastor of the Bell Shoals Baptist Church near Tampa, Florida, where he has served for six years. He is also featured speaker of the Moving Forward radio broadcast, the author of several books, and guest preacher for many conferences and conventions.

I highly recommend Stephen Rummage’s book, Planning Your Preaching. You should also read Engaging Exposition, which Rummage wrote with Daniel Aiken and Bill Curtis.

Pastor Rummage will be one of our guest speakers at the 2015 Cutting It Straight Expository Preaching Conference September 22-24, 2015, where he will lead workshops on planning your preaching and sermon delivery. You don’t want to miss it!

Enjoy this conversation with Stephen Rummage about his faith, ministry, and preaching!
