Tragic! The Tennessean Announces the “Abomination” Lectures Will Actually Be Held at National Baptist Convention’s Baptist World Center and at the American Baptist College

March 15, 2015 Christian

58th Annual Garnett-Nabrit Lecture Series
Presented by American Baptist College at National Baptist Convention, Baptist World Center
March 14 – March 18, 2015

American Baptist College will host the 58th Garnett-Nabrit Lecture Series where this year’s theme is “Ministry in Motion: Living Faith, Doing Justice.” Whether a first time attendee or an alumni who simply loves American Baptist College and its mission to produce effective leaders for Christian service in the world, you are in for an experience that will change the course of your life and ministry.

World-Renowned Lecturers:
Rev. Dr. Allan Boesak
Bishop Yvette Flunder
Rev. Dr. Delman Coates
Dr. Kenneth Robinson
Dr. John Adolph
Rev. Damien Durr
