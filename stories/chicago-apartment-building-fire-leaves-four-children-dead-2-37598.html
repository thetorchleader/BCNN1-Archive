Chicago Apartment Building Fire Leaves Four Children Dead, 2 Adults in Critical Condition

September 8, 2014 National

Firefighters at the scene of a blaze in an 18-unit building in the 11200 block of South Vernon. (Rosemary Sobol / Chicago Tribune)

Four children died and two adults are hospitalized in critical condition after a fire in a Far South Side apartment building Monday morning.

“Yes, it’s a tragedy,” said Assistant Deputy Fire Commissioner Michael Fox.

The blaze started at 3:25 a.m. in the 18-unit building in the 11200 block of South Vernon Avenue in the Roseland neighborhood, Chicago Police Department News Affairs Officer Ron Gaines said. Fire was blowing out of the second and third floors of the building when they got there, according to Fox.

Two people had jumped from the third floor to escape the fire and were taken to Advocate Christ Medical Center in Oak Lawn in critical condition, according to Fox.

The four dead children were also on the third floor of the building, officials said.

The cause of the fire is currently under investigation.

There is currently no reason to suspect foul play, according to another fire official on the scene.

Fifty people have been displaced, Gaines said.
