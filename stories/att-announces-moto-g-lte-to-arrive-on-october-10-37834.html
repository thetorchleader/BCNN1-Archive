AT&T Announces Moto G LTE to Arrive on October 10

October 6, 2014 Technology

AT&T, wasting no time shaking off the weekend, announced on Monday the upcoming arrival of the Moto G LTE. This isn’t the second-generation Moto G launched last month, mind you, it’s the LTE-ready model introduced this past June.

As Motorola’s low-cost entrant for smartphones, the handset runs Android 4.4 KitKat and features a 4.5-inch HD display. Powered by a 1.2GHz quad-core Qualcomm Snapdragon processor, the Moto G LTE houses a 5-megapixel rear camera, and 8GB of internal storage.
