Did Tim McGraw Slap a Female Fan at Atlanta Concert?

July 18, 2014 Entertainment

Tim McGraw (Credit: Sipa USA/REX)

Tim McGraw appeared to slap a woman around the face while performing in concert on Sunday.

In a video obtained by TMZ, the country crooner can be seen swatting a female fan away as she grabs at him while he sings on stage.

McGraw was performing in Lakewood, Atlanta at Aaron’s Amphitheatre when he went into the crowd while belting out Truck Yeah.

The clip shows a woman tugging at his jeans from below, and when she rips them, he appears to hit her across the face.

McGraw then looks to be giving the shocked fan a questioning arm gesture while continuing to sing.
MailOnline has contacted Mr McGraw’s representative for comment.

TMZ reports that the 47-year-old’s rep told them: ‘Tim was singing out in the audience and someone firmly grabbed onto his leg and wouldn’t let go as he was moving through the crowd.

‘He instinctively swatted to try to keep them from ripping his jeans (which they succeeded at doing!), and so he could get to more fans who could slap hands with him before the end of the show.’
