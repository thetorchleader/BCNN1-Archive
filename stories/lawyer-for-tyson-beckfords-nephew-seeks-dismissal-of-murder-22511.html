Lawyer for Tyson Beckford’s Nephew Seeks Dismissal of Murder Charges

May 16, 2014 Entertainment

Domonic Whilby the driver that killed MTA bus driver is arraigned at Manhattan Criminal Court on Thursday, February 13, 2014.

A lawyer for model/actor Tyson Beckford’s nephew is seeking the dismissal of murder charges against his client.

Dominic Whilby, 22, is accused of stealing a delivery van and attempting to drive himself home after a night out with Beckford at New York City’s Dream Downtown Hotel club in February. He then crashed into a transit bus, killing driver William Pena and injuring four others.

He was subsequently charged with manslaughter, two counts of grand larceny, three of criminal assault, criminal mischief and criminal trespass, and was later also hit with additional charges of felony second-degree murder.

Whilby has pleaded not guilty to all accusations and claims to have no memory of the incident. His lawyer, Harvey Slovis, is attempting to use the excuse to have the second-degree murder counts thrown out.
