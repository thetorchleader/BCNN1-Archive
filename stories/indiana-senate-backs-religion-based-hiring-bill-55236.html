Indiana Senate Backs Religion-Based Hiring Bill

February 5, 2015 National

The Indiana Senate is backing a bill that makes it legal for religious institutions that receive state and local contracts to hire based on religion.

Republican Sen. Travis Holdman said he sponsored the measure to restore the ability of religious institutions like Indiana Wesleyan University to receive state workforce training grants.

Last year, officials determined that the university’s religious lifestyle mandate violated state contracting requirements against employment discrimination.

Opponents of the bill say its language is too broad and could lead to lawsuits.

The measure now moves to the state House.
