Michigan Police Fatally Shoot Homeless Man 46 Times

October 29, 2014 Black

On Monday, the ACLU released the dashboard camera video Martin Hall’s shooting death. Police surrounded Hall, who was armed with a penknife and fired some 46 times.
ACLU

The ACLU released police dashboard camera video that shows 8 officers’ fatally shot Martin Hall, a 49-year-old homeless man who was armed with a penknife.  In 2012, 8 Saginaw policemen surrounded a 49-year-old mentally ill, homeless Michigan man, armed with only a penknife and shot him some 46 times and on Monday, the ACLU released the disturbing footage, the New York Daily News reports.

According to reports, police received a call from a shopper claiming that they had been in an argument with Martin Hall, 49. 8 officers responded along with a K-9 unit ad surrounded Hall. Officers reportedly shot 46 times and Hall collapsed and died on the pavement, the Daily News reports.

“He had no idea that those policemen would do that to him,” Martin Hall’s mother Jewel Hall, said in an interview included in the ACLU videotape, the Daily News reports.

“…He wasn’t a threat, I mean, he had a little pen knife.”
