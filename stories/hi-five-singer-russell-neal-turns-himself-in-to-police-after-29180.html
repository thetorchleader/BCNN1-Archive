Hi-Five Singer, Russell Neal, Turns himself in to Police After Murder of his Wife, Fitness Model Brasilia Martinez

Date not recorded in the original archive Entertainment

90s singer Russell Neal from Hi-Five is in police custody telling cops his wife was dead in their west Houston apartment. (RUSSNEAL28/VIA INSTAGRAM)

Earlier this week, Russell Neal walked into a Texas sheriff’s office at 6:10 p.m.to announce that his wife was dead and that he needed an attorney.

On Wednesday, Neal allegedly went to the Harris County station and told officers where to find his wife’s body in his west Houston apartment, notes the New York Daily News. The police knocked down the door and found Catherine Martinez’s body. She had been stabbed multiple times. She was a 24-year-old fitness model, as well as an aspiring boxer. She went by Brasilia Martinez.

The singer told police that he and his wife were having an argument before her death. He then refused to answer any more questions from the police.

The Hi-Five singer was taken into custody, and Martinez was pronounced dead at the scene. Neal has been charged with murder and is being held on a $100,000 bond.

An eyewitness source said Neal was calm as he was arrested.

“He wasn’t resisting arrest or fighting with them, or saying anything,” said Mary Alice Valencia, who works nearby the station, to KTRK. “When they brought him out in handcuffs, he was just as calm as he can be, and they just put him in a police car.”

Neal and Martinez’s neighbors were shocked to hear the news. One in particular said that Martinez seemed like a good mother.
