Oscars 2015: How to Watch the Academy Awards Live Online

February 22, 2015 Entertainment

Live coverage begins at 7 p.m. ET Sunday

For the second year in a row, ABC will stream the Academy Awards online through its Watch ABC app.

Customers with a participating pay TV provider can click here to access the broadcast of Sunday’s Oscar ceremony.

Livestreaming is currently available through Comcast, Cablevision, Cox Communications, Charter Communications, DISH, DirecTV, Midcontinent Communications, Verizon FiOS, Google Fiber and AT&T U-verse, among others.

Meanwhile, the Academy will offer behind-the-scenes video on its website for free (click here). The Oscars Backstage cameras will be placed strategically throughout the red carpet and backstage areas of the Dolby Theater to give viewers an inside look to Hollywood’s biggest night of the year.

ABC will also stream The Oscars Backstage footage on their Facebook page for free (click here).

For more information click here.

Live coverage of the 87 Academy Awards, hosted by Neil Patrick Harris, kick off Sunday night at 4 p.m. PT.

via Variety
