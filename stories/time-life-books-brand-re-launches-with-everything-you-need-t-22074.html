TIME-LIFE Books Brand Re-launches With “Everything You Need to Know About the Bible”

May 13, 2014 Books

TIME-LIFE Everything You Need To Know About the Bible

The iconic TIME-LIFE Books brand is being revived, with the company kicking off its re-launch with a broad new book about the Bible.

Everything You Need to Know About the Bible is being released Tuesday, May 13. The paperback is very visual, packed with classic art, charts, maps, and features like “Verses to Know.”

It focuses on the Bible’s essential history, characters, and messages while exploring how and why it was written.

For the first time ever, the brand will be sold in retail stores rather than direct mail.
