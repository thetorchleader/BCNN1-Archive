Hit-and-Run Victim Claims he Assaulted Suge Knight

March 17, 2015 Black

© Kevork Djansezian, Pool/AP Photo Marion “Suge” Knight, right, appears with his attorney Mathew Fletcher, left, in court for a hearing about evidence in his murder case on Monday, March 9, 2015, in Los Angeles, Calif.

The first man hit by Suge Knight’s truck in a deadly parking lot confrontation later boasted to police that he physically assaulted the rap mogul, a police report obtained by the Daily News shows. 

“I f—-d him up!” former gang member Cle (Bone) Sloan told an investigator with the Los Angeles County Sheriff’s Department, according to the report from a confidential source.

Sloan made the stunning admission as he lay in a hospital bed with several lacerations to his head, the report stated.

The second man hit by Knight’s truck, Terry Carter, died from his injuries.

Sloan allegedly told police that he had a verbal dispute with Knight at a prior location about three miles away and then made a beeline for the co-founder of Death Row records when he spotted him at Tam’s Burgers in Compton, Calif., a short time later on Jan. 29.

“Sloan said he contacted Knight and began to punch him through the driver side window,” the report stated.

“Sloan stated Knight tried to exit the red Ford but was stopped by his punches,” the report continued.

In his account to police, Sloan said he was walking away from Knight’s truck when he heard someone cry “Look Out!” He said he was attempting to turn around when he was immediately struck and run over by Knight’s Ford F-150 Raptor.
