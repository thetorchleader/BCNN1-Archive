Tim Tebow’s Arrival Won’t Change NY Jets Plans for NFL Draft

April 20, 2012 Uncategorized

NY Jets will draft best players, not ones to fit Tim Tebow’s style

There’s little doubt that the Jets will deploy Tim Tebow in myriad ways: quarterback, running back, fullback, H-back and special teams wild card.
However, the Tebow Effect will also spill into the draft next week.
Although GM Mike Tannenbaum admitted Tebow’s ability to play several roles is a plus, he maintained that the talented third-year player’s versatility would only be a “subtle tie-breaker” when deciding which positions to draft.
“Obviously, he has some versatility for us,” Tannenbaum said at a pre-draft press conference on Thursday. “He’s our backup quarterback. That’s how we’re listing him. That’s how we’re going to carry him on our roster… He has unique skill sets and athleticism, but in terms of how we’re looking at our strategy, he’s clearly a quarterback for us.”
The Jets apparently won’t target specific types of players — such as a blocking wide receiver — to help tailor an offense around Tebow’s skill sets. Contrary to what left guard Matt Slauson believes, the Jets won’t be running two separate offenses for Tebow and Mark Sanchez.
