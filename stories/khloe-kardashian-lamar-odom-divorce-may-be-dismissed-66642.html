Khloe Kardashian, Lamar Odom Divorce May be Dismissed

April 10, 2015 Entertainment

Khloe Kardashian (L) and Lamar Odom’s divorce may be dismissed. File photo by Jim Ruymen/UPI

Khloe Kardashian and Lamar Odom‘s divorce may be dismissed.

Sources told TMZ the 30-year-old reality star has failed to file for a default divorce judgment, and the case will be thrown out as early as this month.

Kardashian filed for divorce from Odom in December 2013, and the 35-year-old NBA free agent has yet to file a response or sign the papers. The reality star could finalize the divorce without Odom’s participation by filing the request for a default.

Kardashian and sister Kim Kardashian are currently visiting Armenia, and received a plot of land from the Vahanki residential community. The reality star shared the news on Instagram, but deleted the post after fans pointed out she was referred to as “Khloe Kardashian Odom” on the document.

Kardashian and Odom married after just one month of dating in September 2009, and separated due to the basketball player’s infidelity and rumored drug use. The reality star subsequently dated rapper French Montana, whom she has been involved with on and off since spring 2014.
