Pastor Michael Henderson Sr. Releases New Book Challenging Men to Live and Lead Biblically

October 20, 2014 Books

The battle for and against biblical manhood in society today is a massive battle that is being waged on many levels. While the devil seeks to destroy families and undermine biblical male leadership, God wants men to be equipped and challenged to be the loving, strong husbands, fathers, and leaders that they were created to be. In the pages of A Good Man Is Hard to Find: Adam, Where Are You? Pastor Michael Henderson seeks to offer such a challenge to men.

Using the story of Adam and Eve, along with personal experience, Pastor Michael Henderson seeks to redefine manhood according to biblical principles. Men who are struggling with what it means to be a man and who have never seen a good example of a man, will receive a biblical guide on how a good man should live and lead his life, his family, and his community.

A Good Man Is Hard to Find: Adam, Where Are You? is a clarion call and strong challenge to men to take up their rightful place in society. It is also a book that will help women better understand the men in their lives and assist them in accomplishing their goals.

Michael L. Henderson Sr. is a hands-on Pastor, trained Christian Counselor and dynamic Spiritual Mentor. He is the Founder and Senior Pastor of New Beginnings Church in Matthews, North Carolina. He personally coaches pastors and leaders across the country, and speaks at men’s conferences, marriage conferences, leadership conferences and revivals.

He is married to his ministry partner, Twanna Henderson. They have one son, Michael Jr.

For more information about Michael L. Henderson Sr., visit: www.MLHMinistries.com.

To book Michael L. Henderson Sr. for speaking engagements and/or book signings, please email: Bookings@MLHMinistries.com.

A Good Man Is Hard to Find: Adam, Where Are You? is now available on Amazon.com. It will soon be available wherever fine books are sold, including: Amazon Kindle and BarnesandNoble.com.
