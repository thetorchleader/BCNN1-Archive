Christians Push Back Against “Charlie Charlie” Social Media Trend With “Jesus Jesus Challenge”

May 30, 2015 Christian

The new social media trend called the “Charlie Charlie Challenge” is getting some push back from Christians across the country.

The challenge involves putting two pencils on a piece of paper in the shape of a cross and writing the words “yes” and “no” in the boxes outlined by the pencils, then summoning a demon named Charlie to move the pencils.

Joshua Feuerstein, an evangelist and social media personality, has created something to counter the challenge. It’s called the “Jesus Jesus Challenge.”

He then encourages people to share their faith using the hashtag #JesusJesus.
