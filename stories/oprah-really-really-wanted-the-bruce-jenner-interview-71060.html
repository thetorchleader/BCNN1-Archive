Oprah Really, Really Wanted the Bruce Jenner Interview

April 30, 2015 Entertainment

Oprah Winfrey (Jordan Strauss/Invision/AP)

Even with Oprah money and the ability to buy you, and you, and you all of her favorite things, Mama O doesn’t always get everything she wants.

In an interview with Women’s Wear Daily, the OWN Chairwoman says you kids and your Google have totally changed her approach to landing big interviews, like Bruce Jenner.

The problem is, and even now, because I started doing this with “Next Chapter,” there’s nobody — try to think of somebody … that you can’t go and Google and find out what has happened to them in the past week. … There are only a few. I wanted Bruce Jenner. Diane (Sawyer) got it.

So instead of being face-to-face with Jenner, chances are Oprah watched the two-hour 20/20special on the couch like all 17 million of us. And, like many celebs, the former talk show host took to Twitter to show her support.

.@DianeSawyer This interview is STUNNING! #BruceJennerABC

— Oprah Winfrey (@Oprah) April 25, 2015

“All of us deserve the right to be loved for who we are “. Bravo #BruceJenner

— Oprah Winfrey (@Oprah) April 25, 2015
