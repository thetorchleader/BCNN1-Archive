When Classmates Don’t Show Up to 10-Year-Old Minnesota Girl’s Birthday Party, Mother Invites 700 People through Facebook

April 21, 2015 God & Family

Jenny Moretter, of Shakopee, Minnesota, invited all of her 10-year-old daughter Mackenzie’s birthday party on Saturday, CBS Local reports. Unfortunately, none of her classmates showed up. You see, Mackenzie has Sotos Syndrome, a rare genetic disorder that delayed her brain development and makes it harder for her to make friends. When Jenny saw that her daughter was heartbroken, she took to Facebook and posted on a mother’s group, inviting total strangers to the celebration.

“I want so much for much for Mackenzie to belong and for her to have friends and people to accept her for who she is,” she wrote.

And it paid off because sometimes people are wonderful. Miraculously, more than 700 people joined the event and hundreds came to the party. Adorably, Mackenzie’s dad started to make two boxes of cupcakes when he realized he would need a whole lot more. Luckily, Mackenzie’s guests came bearing snacks, cake, and gifts for the little girl and made her day just about as special as a birthday can be. Happy Birthday, Mackenzie!
