WATCH: Kobe Bryant’s Wife is Not Only Beautiful, but She Can Shoot that Ball, Y’all

February 6, 2015 Sports

A video posted by Kobe Bryant (@kobebryant) on Feb 4, 2015 at 10:17pm PST

(Video and links contain NSFW language.)

Los Angeles Lakers guard Kobe Bryant is shut down for the season, but his competitive nature is still firing on all cylinders.

Bryant challenged his wife Vanessa to hit a backward shot over her head from the free-throw line, and she made it on just her fourth try.

According to his Facebook post, the shot happened at Staples Center at 4 a.m. While we’re not sure what they were doing there at that odd hour, Kobe has been known to play at all hours.
