Mike Huckabee Criticizes Obama Administration on New Radio Show

April 10, 2012 Uncategorized

Mike Huckabee kicked off his new radio show Monday promising “conservatism and common sense” as he launched into attacks on President Barack Obama right out of the box.
“Welcome to the community of conversation. You’ve just made a right turn, and you’ve arrived at the corner of conservatism and common sense,” the former Governor announced at the top of his show. “In this show we’re going to be confronting the issues and not the listeners.”
After the brief intro, Huckabee dove straight into the news, criticizing President Obama and Democrats for not taking responsibility for the GSA scandal, the “lousy job numbers” or high gas prices.
“Always the response is, ‘Well, it may be bad, but it could’ve been a lot worse,’ That’s been the explanation from this administration almost from day one,” he said. “Every time there’s bad news, there’s never an acknowledgment that we have to make it better, because we own this thing. It’s always, ‘Well, it’s bad. But, by golly, if it weren’t for us, it’d be worse.'”
“Here’s what I know,” Huckabee continued. “Gasoline is twice what it was since President Obama took office, and so many people have given up looking for work that it may just not matter.”
Huckabee returned from the first commercial break with a live interviews with Mitt Romney, a major get for the former Arkansas governor’s first program. Huckabee also interviewed presidential campaign consultants Dick Morris and Ed Rollins, and honored longtime 60 Minutes newsman Mike Wallace, who passed away over the weekend.
