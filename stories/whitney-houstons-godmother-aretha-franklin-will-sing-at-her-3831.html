Whitney Houston’s Godmother, Aretha Franklin, Will Sing at Her Funeral

February 15, 2012 Uncategorized
Whitney’s funeral will be held on Feb. 18 at New Hope Baptist Church and the private service won’t have a dry eye in the church after the R&B legend performs.


Whitney Houston’s mentor Clive Davis has personally asked the late singer’s godmother Aretha Franklin to sing at her funeral, according to Entertainment Tonight.
A source from Whitney’s camp told ET that when the 69-year-old singer was asked to sing she said, “How could I say no?”
SOURCE: Hollywood Life
