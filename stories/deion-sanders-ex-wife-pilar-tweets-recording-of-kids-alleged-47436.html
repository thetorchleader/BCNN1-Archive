Deion Sanders’ Ex-Wife, Pilar, Tweets Recording Of Kids Allegedly Saying That he ‘Kicked,’ ‘Choked’ Son

November 29, 2014 Sports

Is “Primetime” Deion Sanders the latest NFL athlete to be caught in a child abuse scandal? In an explosive new recording posted online by his ex, Pilar, Sanders’ young daughter is heard apparently telling her mother that the NFL Hall of Famer kicked and choked his son!

Deion, 47, won sole custody of their two sons — Shilo, 14, and Shedeur, 12 — and daughter Shelomi, 10, in a bitter child custody battle last year that continues to be waged in the media.

The Sanders children are featured in the show Deion’s Family Playbook on OWN TV, which focuses on parenting skills.

Pilar tweeted Wednesday a link to the recording she apparently made of two of her children talking about their father’s alleged physical abuse. “Justice shall take its course,” she wrote.

“Daddy kicked Shilo and choked him and kept on swinging him places and hitting him,” Shelomi is heard saying in a phone call to her mother.

“He did what?” Pilar Sanders is heard asking.

“Daddy choked Shilo and just kept on throwing him places, kicked him and he kept on hitting him,” she responded.
