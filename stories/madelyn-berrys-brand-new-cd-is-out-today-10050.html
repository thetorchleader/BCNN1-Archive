Madelyn Berry's Brand New CD Is Out Today

January 15, 2013 Uncategorized

Anyone who’s seen Madelyn Berry minister has experienced her dynamic and energetic praise.  Her infectious worship has made her one of the most talked about gospel artists in recent days.  Many fell in love with Madelyn Berry with the release of her riveting single, “You Deserve All Praise”. 

Now Madelyn has bottled her worship in a collection of 16 jaw-dropping songs called Release The Sound.  Released on January 15, 2013, the new project flaunts Madelyn’s contemporary Christian and Gospel influences and features the talents of fellow worship leaders Martha Munizzi, Darwin Hobbs, LaRune Howard and Trent Cory.
Fans of the singer will be able to hear all the songs during an interview on CJBRadio.com on January 15, 2013 at 4PM EST.  Berry will be sharing the inspiration for the songs during her interview with CJB’s Program Director, Kevin James.  On Sunday, January 20, 2013, Madelyn will be holding her National CD Release Celebration at Payne Chapel AME Church (212 Neill Ave., Nashville, TN 37206).  Her Album Release will take place during the Sunday Morning Service at 10AM CST.
Release The Sound has special meaning for Berry.  The song and the project were inspired by the teachings of her late pastor, Dr. Zachary Tims.  “Pastor Tims was preaching a series called, ‘It’s In Your Mouth'”, recounts Madelyn.  “He was talking about the importance of speaking the Word of God and the power that is in our words.  A lot of the songs, including the title tune, “Release The Sound”, were inspired by Dr. Tims”.  He is memorialized on the CD as he opens up the project with a stirring introduction.
