Mariah Carey Breaks Down on Stage, Cries, Loses Shoe

December 17, 2014 Entertainment

Credit: Dimitrios Kambouris/Getty Images

Merry MiMi meltdown! Mariah Carey got emotional during her first of six holiday concerts on Monday, Dec. 15, delivering a wild performance inside NYC’s Beacon Theatre.

The songstress took to the stage in a tight bejeweled red gown, where she sang her holiday hits including “All I Want for Christmas” and “Here Comes Santa Claus.” At one point, Carey unexpectedly lost a shoe but continued singing as she clutched her heel in one hand and a gold microphone in the other.

The most poignant moment from the Elusive Chanteuse’s show, entitled “All I Want for Christmas Is You: A Night of Joy and Festivity,” was when she sang her trademark 1993 ballad, “Hero.” Carey powerfully delivered the hit, until tears started streaming from her face. (It remains unclear what triggered the waterworks, but this Christmas will be Carey’s first as a single mom, after she and her husband Nick Cannon announced their split this past fall.)

She composed herself and then, her microphone pack fell off her glittery gown. “Really?” Carey said before the crowd. “I’m sorry — cut!” After an assistant ducked out to help her with the brief malfunction, Carey then picked up from where she left off.
