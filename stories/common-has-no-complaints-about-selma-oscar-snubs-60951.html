Common Has No Complaints About “Selma” Oscar Snubs

March 10, 2015 Entertainment

Common strikes a pose in New York City.
(Photo: Robert Deutsch, USA TODAY)

No, Ava DuVernay did not get a best director Oscar nomination for Selma.

Yes, David Oyelowo was ignored as best actor.

But Selma did get an Oscar win: best song for Glory, awarded to Common (who was also in the film) and John Legend. Yes, Common’s disappointed that his director and costars were overlooked, but he has no hard feelings.

“It was a voting process. Our whole movie was about voting. I didn’t have complaints about the process,” he says.

So what did he take away from the Academy Awards experience, which included a performance that brought down the house and made Chris Pine cry?

“The Oscars was the first time I felt, on a large scale, on a mass level, people were aware of me, who I am and what I stand for. I’ve reached certain levels of success,” he says. “I made a decision that I was going to deliver something in art that would be inspiring or be filled with something that was meaningful. It’s never been recognized on the level of the Oscars.”
