Artists Sense God Moving on Successful Winter Jam Concert Tour

March 11, 2014 Entertainment

Lecrae raps his blend of christian hip-hop during Winter Jam at the Bryce Jordan Center on Friday, January 17, 2014. (John Baranoski)

Snow wasn’t the only thing sweeping the United States this winter. The 2014 Winter Jam Spectacular started in January, and has traveled coast to coast with a total of 47 shows.

The Jam Tour is now the largest Christian music festival in the world. The first Winter Jam was launched by Newsong in 1995.

Worship is the heartbeat of Winter Jam rather than profit. Admission to the concert is only $10 and the show features 10 top Christian artists.

The 2014 concert includes diverse music, ranging from hip-hop to pop. Artists from LeCrae to Plumb to Colton Dixon fill the talent lineup.
