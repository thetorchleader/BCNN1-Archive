T.D. Jakes Speaks at 2014 IMPACT Conference in Ghana; President Mahama Praises Jakes’ Ministry

December 5, 2014 Eye on Africa

President John Dramani Mahama has lauded American motivational speaker and preacher, TD Jakes for his outstanding leadership qualities and support for the needy across the globe. 

The renowned international preacher is currently in Ghana for the 2014 edition of IMPACT, and will preach on Friday and on Sunday at the  Action Chapel International in Accra.

President Mahama noted that “Bishop Jakes is a Charismatic Leader, visionary, provocative thinker and entrepreneur who serves as the Senior Pastor of the Potter House, a global humanitarian organization …located in Dallas.”

He said T.D Jakes has, through his charitable works, extended  “a hand of help to the needy with the heart of compassion to the hurting and a message of inspiration to the disenfranchised.”

“Through the years, T.D Jakes’ ministry has taken the gospel of Jesus Christ around the world, helping victims of natural disasters and bringing the message of God’s love to hurting, wounded and troubled souls. May the voice, inspiration and transformative impact of Bishop T.D Jakes and the porter House forever continue,” the President added.

For his part, Bishop TD Jakes praised government for the numerous developmental prpjects it is undertaking.

“…When I came into Ghana, I was astounded at how much lifting is going on…how much empowerment, how much transformation is going on.”
