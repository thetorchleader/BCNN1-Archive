President Obama to Speak to Students at Lawson State Community College in Birmingham on Thursday

March 26, 2015 Education

Thursday will be an historic day for the city of Birmingham. President Barack Obama will be in town to deliver a speech to students at Lawson State Community College on Thursday afternoon.

The C-17 Transport plane arrived to deliver the specially equipped armored vehicles that will be part of the presidential motorcade.

Crews contracted by the city have been busily scraping out rough spots on Lakeshore Parkway to re-pave with a smooth coat of fresh asphalt.

Three Birmingham City Schools will dismiss students at 11 a.m. Thursday because of the visit.

Those schools are Oxmoor Valley Elementary, Valley Middle and Wenonah High School. Parents must pick up their children by 11:30 a.m., unless their child takes the bus home.

For Tanya Parham, the President’s visit is something she never expected to see in her lifetime. Parham has lived in the the area her entire life. She works at a barber shop across the street from Lawson State.

“Never, never in a million years, it’s awesome. I mean, words can’t even describe how you feel, it’s amazing,” She said.

FOX6 will be streaming the President’s visit live in the WBRC News app, on our website,www.myfoxal.com, and on the air.
