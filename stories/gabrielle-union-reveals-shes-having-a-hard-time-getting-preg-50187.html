Gabrielle Union Reveals She’s Having a Hard Time Getting Pregnant

December 26, 2014 Entertainment

Gabrielle Union and Dwyane Wade would like to have children together, but it’s not easy and in a new interview, Union holds nothing back about the subject.

Gabrielle Union and Dwyane Wade are happily enjoying the married life. After dating for several years, the couple married this summer in Miami in a super private ceremony filled with their celebrity friends. Now, the actress and NBA star have to deal with the old-age, cliché question about more children, as the two currently co-parent D. Wade’s three young sons Zaire, Zion and Xavier and settle into marriage. But, in a new interview with Uptown Magazine, Union admits that she is struggling to get pregnant.

“I think my brain and my heart are screaming baby fever. My ovaries, however, they’re coughing up dust,” the 42-year-old “Being Mary Jane” actress revealed. “Yes, it’s in my heart and in my mind, and we’ll see if my body catches up with that.”

Unlike many women who struggle with having a child, Union is not stressing herself out about any medical methods or adoption, right now. In her opinion, things will turn out best if she just allows what she wants to happen. Plus, raising her husbands’ handsome boys takes away a bit of the worry.

“I have really awesome step-kids, so I feel equally as fulfilled,” she added. “I’ve lasted 42 years without having a human being come from one of my orifices and I feel okay and fulfilled and happy. If it does happen, it’s a miracle-a medical miracle at least. I mean, we’d love to. We’ll see what my ovaries say. Cause right now they’re taking Geritol.”
