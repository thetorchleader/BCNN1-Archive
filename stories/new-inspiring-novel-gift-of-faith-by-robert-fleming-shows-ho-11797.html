New, Inspiring Novel, “Gift of Faith,” by Robert Fleming, Shows How a Pastor Overcomes Challenges to his Faith

April 16, 2013 Uncategorized

There are times in each of our lives that we could describe as heart-breaking, tragic, or painful. The death of a loved one, the loss of a job, the betrayal of a close friend — all of these things can cause one to falter in their faith. The rough times in our lives often give us good reason to stop, examine our situation, and ask ourselves whether we have enough faith to get through.

In Robert Fleming’s intriguing novel, Gift of Faith, Reverend Clint faces tragedy in his family. He soon finds himself questioning everything that he ever knew and believed about God, about life, and about himself as a man and as a pastor. After being placed in the midst of a group of church members dealing with grief, Reverend Clint hopes and prays he is able to offer some sense of renewal to the anguished souls. But he will soon find out whether he can handle all of his pain and theirs.

Robert Fleming is the author of nine books, editing another two anthologies, and also contributing several  short stories which have appeared in other collections. An award-winning journalist, he holds a degree in psychology and studied journalism at Columbia University’s School of Journalism. He has interviewed several personalities including Duke Ellington, Miles Davis, Patti LaBelle, Sarah Vaughan, Ray Charles and Bob Marley.
