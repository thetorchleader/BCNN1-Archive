Adrian Peterson to Face Jail After Marijuana Admission

October 14, 2014 Sports

Minnesota Vikings running back Adrian Peterson, who is under indictment on a felony child abuse charge in Texas, allegedly told a drug-test administrator that he had smoke marijuana in advance of a court-mandated drug test last week.
(Photo: Tom Gannam, AP)

Minnesota Vikings star running back Adrian Peterson could be facing a week in jail after allegedly admitting last week to smoking marijuana.

The judge in Peterson’s case, Kelly Case, told an audience last year that he gives a one-week jail sentence to those who test positive in their first court-administered drug exams. According to court records, Peterson admitted last week to smoking a “little weed” – a confession he made while submitting to a drug test in Montgomery County, Texas.

While Peterson allegedly told the person administering his drug test he had smoked “weed,” it is not publicly known whether Peterson tested positive for marijuana.

“On a first positive test, I give everybody one chance,” Case said in a video posted last year on YouTube. “I’ll stick them in jail for one week, and then I’ll pull them back out. I’ll reinstate their bond, and I’ll tell them on the record, `If you do this again and you don’t follow my rules, we’ll ask the (district attorney) to set a no-bond hearing, and we will hold you in jail.'”
