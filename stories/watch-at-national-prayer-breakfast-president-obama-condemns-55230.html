WATCH: At National Prayer Breakfast, President Obama Condemns Those Who ‘Highjack Religion for Murderous Ends’

February 5, 2015 National

The 2015 National Prayer Breakfast was held on Thursday, February 5. The keynote speaker was NASCAR legend Darrell Waltrip. Along with President Obama, other speakers included Andrew Young and Ebola-survivor and medical missionary Dr. Kent Brantley. Secretary of Housing Julian Castro read Scripture.

King Abdullah II of Jordan was to speak at the event, but returned to his country early due to the killing of a Jordanian pilot at the hands of ISIS.

In his speech, President Obama noted the release of Kenneth Bae from North Korea and his meeting with the wife of imprisoned Iranian pastor Saeed Abedini.

Pope Francis also sent a message to the National Prayer Breakfast, asking for prayer for himself and for persecuted Christians.

Southern Baptist Convention president, Ronnie Floyd, and his wife were in attendance, as was Filipino boxer Manny Pacquiao.

–BCNN1

__________

President Barack Obama is condemning those who seek to “highjack religion for their own murderous ends.”

Obama is speaking at the National Prayer Breakfast, an annual gathering of politicians, faith leaders and dignitaries in Washington. The president called on people of faith to push back on those who seek to distort religion.

Among those attending the breakfast is the Dalai Lama, the spiritual leader of Tibetan Buddhism. Obama says the Dalai Lama serves as a “powerful example of what it means to practice compassion.”

The president’s meetings with the Dalai Lama have consistently been criticized by China. Beijing sees the Dalai Lama as an anti-Chinese separatist because of his quest for greater Tibetan autonomy.
