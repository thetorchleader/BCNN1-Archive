Fred Luter Praises Racial Cooperation In Alabama

September 25, 2012 Uncategorized

The first black president of the Southern Baptist Convention has been impressed with racial unity in his travels through Alabama.


The Rev. Fred Luter, pastor of the Franklin Avenue Baptist Church in New Orleans, was elected in June as president of the 16-million-member Southern Baptist Convention, the nation’s largest Protestant denomination.
On Sunday night, he preached at Huffman Baptist Church for the Impact Birmingham evangelistic event organized by the Birmingham Baptist Association. Tonight he’s speaking at New Home Baptist Church in Pisgah at a similar service at 6 p.m. On Wednesday, he’ll be at Union University in Jackson, Tenn. 
At Huffman Baptist, Luter told a crowd of about 800 that President Obama called him after his election and said, “How does it feel to be the most popular president in the country?”
He’s also been in demand for interviews by national media, including Diane Sawyer of ABC News.
“I never did expect that,” Luter said in an interview today. “That’s been the biggest difference. Just the notoriety.”
