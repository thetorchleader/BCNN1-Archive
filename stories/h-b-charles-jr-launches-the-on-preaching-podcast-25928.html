H. B. Charles Jr. Launches “The On Preaching Podcast”

June 9, 2014 Christian

The On Preaching Podcast

This is the first episode of The On Preaching Podcast, the podcast dedicated to helping you preach faithfully, clearly, and better.

My goal is that this podcast will be a living room couch where we can sit together and talk about the important, nature, and practice of faithful, Christ-centered, biblical preaching.

In this inaugural episode, I attempt to answer three questions:

  1. Who is H.B. Charles Jr.? This podcast is not about me. It is about preaching. But as the host of this podcast, you should know a bit about me up front. After all, I hope we will spend a lot of time together in the weeks to come.
  2. Why a podcast? Why this podcast? I am doing this podcast for me. And I am doing it for you. I want to preach more faithfully, clearly, and better. And I hope to help you to do the same.
  3. What should you expect in the weeks to come? Here I talk briefly about the content and format of the podcast in the future. I have great expectations for this show. But please, be patient. This podcasting thing is new to me. But I trust I will get the hang of it as we go.
