Pastor E. Dewey Smith and Greater Travelers Rest Baptist Church Celebrate 138 Years

November 16, 2014 Christian

Today we celebrated 138 years as a church family. The three day long celebration was EPIC. Friday’s You are the Hope Volunteer Appreciation Awards Gala was simply AMAZING. Saturday, we did what we do best as a family: Serve the community through our Do Something Saturday initiatives. Finally on Sunday we had an incredible worship service, generational dance and history mural unveiling. WOOOOOOOW!!! That’s the way to celebrate a birthday.
