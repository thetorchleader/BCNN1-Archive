Are You BOLD?

October 29, 2011 Uncategorized

by Sandra Steen
October 24, 2011

Click here to read previous articles by Sandra Steen

“We were bold in our God.”   1Th 2:2 NKJV
These are challenging times and if you want to be led by God you must learn to be bold–not rude or insensitive, but BOLD, because God will sometimes direct you to do things others don’t agree with or understand. Any time you step outside the boundaries of what others think is acceptable, you risk rejection.  You can’t continually allow what others think to keep you from doing what you know God wants you to do. Confronting criticism becomes a little easier when you remember that, ultimately, “Each of us shall give account of himself to God” (Ro 14:12 NKJV).
After we have applied a careful balance of hearing God in all the ways in which he speaks; our next steps can appear bold to those who do not understand.  Hearing God “accurately” is tantamount to stepping out in boldness.  We must be reminded that God may speak through inner conviction, prophecy, the preached or taught word, two or three witnesses or we should strategically seek the safety of a multitude of sound counsel. Rick Godwin says “we avoid error when we allow one message to come through the various ways in which God speaks”.  At the end of all of our hearing…We must be doers!  (James 1:22)
It hurts to be criticized. But if we’re to succeed in life, we must have the attitude of Paul.  Ben Campbell Johnson paraphrases Paul’s words in First Corinthians 4:3-4: “I am not the least concerned with the fact that you are deciding what is right and what is wrong with me…Neither you nor anyone else can put me down unless I first put myself down (and I’m not doing that)…Though I don’t know of anything against me, my ignorance doesn’t mean that I am correct in my appraisal, because the final evaluation is in God’s hands.”
Secure people can handle being the only one doing something. They can also allow others the same choice; because they know we have been called to love one another, not analyze, judge and categorize one another! 
To avoid growing old and feeling like somewhere along the way we got lost and never succeeded at being what God called us to be, we must be BOLD and COURAGEOUS! Remember the Kingdom is calling for your originality!

“Originality implies being bold enough to go beyond accepted norms” (author unknown)
 
Much JOY
Sandra
