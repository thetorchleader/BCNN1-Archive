Evelyn Lozada Says ‘Thank You God for My Blessing’ After Giving Birth to Healthy Baby Boy

March 22, 2014 Entertainment

Evelyn Lozada (Imeh Akpanudosen/Getty Images)

Evelyn Lozada is celebrating the arrival of a healthy baby boy with her baseball star fiancé Carl Crawford.

The onetime Basketball Wives star revealed the birth as she tweeted: ‘I have EXCITING NEWS! Thank you God for my blessing!’

The as-yet-unnamed 8lbs boy is 21inches long and was born on Saturday morning at 3.55am MST.

A representative for 37-year-old Evelyn told OK magazine, for whom she writes a column: ‘New dad Carl is so excited and so is mommy.’

Evelyn is sure to be delighted by her son’s arrival, having previously boasted that she was ‘praying for a boy’.

The baby joins Evelyn’s 20-year-old daughter Shaniece from a previous relationship.
