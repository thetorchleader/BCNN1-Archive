Connecticut Pastor Sets Up Web-Based Hourly Prayer Sign-Up Program

Date not recorded in the original archive Christian

The Rev. Todd Foster

At each hour, someone is praying.

Choose an hour, check the box on the Web page, and say a prayer for peace, a prayer for church unity, and a prayer your own church has asked you to pray.

It’s a basic idea anyone can join in.

It was “just a thought that I had” to join in prayer “with like-minded churches in the area,” said Pastor Todd Foster of Church on the Rock on Hamilton Street, who came up with the web-based prayer sign-up. “We do so little together.”

His idea was simple: “to literally have prayer for 24 hours a day.”

The first week was a test, involving just Church on the Rock. Now it’s open to anyone. Just go to http://bit.ly/NewHavenPrays to sign up through Sunday and, for Monday through Aug. 31, go to http://bit.ly/NewHavenPrayerWatch.

Foster said he wants to keep the idea basic to involve as many churches as possible. Already, former New Haveners who have moved away to places such as Florida and Chicago have been praying. Several Greater New Haven churches are signing on, as well, including Elm City Vineyard, First Baptist Church in Milford, Bethel Assembly of God, Gateway Christian Fellowship and First Baptist Church in New Haven.

Foster would like each person to pray for three things:

• “The city of New Haven, its peace and harmony, especially over the historically violence-prone months of the summer.

• “The unity of God’s people in the city of New Haven and beyond.”

• A topic or issue requested by the individual’s parish or pastor.

But Foster said that however someone wants to pray is fine. “I didn’t want to be too prescriptive,” he said. “It was primarily done regarding the violence.”
