Richard Land Says North Carolina Is Crucial In Homosexual Marriage Debate

April 1, 2012 Uncategorized

A prominent Southern Baptist leader says North Carolina, the last state in the South without a constitutional amendment banning same-sex “marriage,” can play a key role in the national debate over the topic.
Speaking last week in Wake Forest, Dr. Richard Land — of the convention’s Ethics & Religious Liberty Commission — said U.S. Supreme Court justices are watching what the people of North Carolina say about the issue. A referendum vote is set for May 8.
 
Land told an audience at Southeastern Baptist Theological Seminary on Wednesday that he believes the Supreme Court would be hesitant to allow couples of the same gender to marry if other states join the 29 already with amendments defining marriage as between a man and a woman. Land said that could change if states like North Carolina reject such amendments.
SOURCE: Associated Press
