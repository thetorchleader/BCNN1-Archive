Mother of Gospel Singer Kurt Carr Has Passed Away

April 4, 2014 Entertainment

PositivelyGospel.com regretfully reports that Gospel giant Kurt Carr is mourning the loss of his mother, Mrs. Deloris Carr. Please join us in praying God’s comfort for Kurt Carr and his family at this time.

Funeral announcements are pending.
