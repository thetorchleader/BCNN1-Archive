Atlanta Commissioner Elaine Boyer Comes Clean says ‘I’ve Betrayed the People’ and ‘I Need to Resign Effective 5:00 Today’ (We Say, Elaine Boyer, We Love You for Being Honest)

August 25, 2014 National

DeKalb County District 1 Commissioner Elaine Boyer

Elaine Boyer, DeKalb County’s longest-serving commissioner, has announced her resignation from public office effective immediately this afternoon.

Boyer’s decision officially ends her 22-year run as an elected DeKalb official. Her announcement comes amid an ongoing federal investigation into several commissioners’ questionable use of taxpayer dollars.

Earlier this year, Boyer came under fire for allegedly using her county credit card to ring up thousands of dollars in personal expenses that included airplane tickets, car rentals, and a stay at a ski resort. She denied wrongdoing at the time and insisted she remained transparent with her spending.

Boyer spoke with WSB-TV about her decision to resign:

“It’s a very hard decision, and I’m heartbroken and saddened, but I need to resign,” said Boyer, the commission’s sole Republican and its longest-serving member.“I’ve betrayed the people and I’ve abused my position of power,” she continued, “and so I feel like I need to do this and publicly acknowledge that I’m ending my position today as of 5 o’clock.”

Boyer did not detail what she had done. But federal prosecutors in June had subpoenaed purchasing card records, including invoices and receipts, from all DeKalb County commissioners as well as nearly 300 county employees.
