LISTEN: U.S., Arabs carry out airstrikes around Kobani (Second Coming Watch Update #517 with Daniel Whyte III)

October 9, 2014 Second Coming Watch

Daniel Whyte III

Let’s take a quick look at today’s prophecy-related headlines which point towards the second coming of the Lord Jesus Christ and the end of the world as we know it.

First, today, under the Sign category of WARS & RUMORS OF WARS — According to The Los Angeles Times, U.S. and Arab allies again pounded Islamic State strongholds near Kobani, a Syrian border city on the brink of falling to the militants. Warplanes from the U.S. and United Arab Emirates carried out six strikes Tuesday and early Wednesday near the besieged town. The attacks were in addition to five strikes the day before. U.S. officials cautioned that airstrikes were of limited effectiveness in defending the town. Taking Kobani would mark a symbolic victory for the Islamic State which controls a vast swath of land across northern Syria and northern and western Iraq. Pro-Kurdish protests broke out throughout Turkey in response to the militants’ advance on Kobani this week. The events turned deadly on Wednesday as four people died in demonstrations, bringing to the total death toll to 19.

Second, today, under the Sign category of EARTHQUAKES IN DIVERS PLACES — According to Reuters, a magnitude 6.2 earthquake struck Mexico on Tuesday in the Gulf of California, 75 miles south-west of the city of El Dorado, but there were no immediate reports of damage. The quake was shallow at just 6.2 miles, and was felt inland in the capital of the western state of Sina-loa.

Third, today, under the Sign category of WARS & RUMORS OF WARS — According to The Associated Press, warships from North and South Korea exchanged warning shots on Tuesday after a North Korean ship briefly violated the disputed western sea boundary. The shots were fired into the sea and there have been no reports of injuries or damage to the ships of either side. The South Korean official said the North Korean ship was in South Korea-controlled waters for about 10 minutes before it retreated to its own waters.

Fourth, today, under the Sign category of THE TURMOIL IN ISRAEL & THE MIDDLE EAST — According to The Times of Israel, Police and masked rioters clashed on Jerusalem’s Temple Mount on Wednesday morning, as tensions ramped up ahead of the Jewish holiday of Sukkot. Rioters were chased by police into the al-Aqsa Mosque after fighting broke out following the opening of a gate for non-Muslim visitors. Protesters inside the mosque tried to spray flammable liquid out of windows toward officers. They also threw Molotov cocktails from within the mosque for the first time. Palestinians had reportedly prepared for the confrontation ahead of time, and had set up obstacles at the holy site to slow down police.

Fifth, today, under the Sign category of DISEASES & EPIDEMICS — According to CBS News, while national and international media is focused on the Ebola outbreak in West Africa, the CDC is reminding Americans that sexually transmitted diseases are an ongoing but hidden epidemic. In the United States, nearly 20 million cases of new STD infections are reported each year. Such infections can persist for a long time, and because some victims are not even aware they have a disease, they can easily spread it to others. About 50.5 million current infections are in men while 59.5 million are in women, for a total of 110 million Americans with STDs at any given time.
