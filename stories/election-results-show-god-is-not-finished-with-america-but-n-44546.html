Election Results Show God Is Not Finished With America, But Now We Must Prove That We Do Not Imagine Ourselves Finished With God

November 10, 2014 Christian

(Reuters)

Pundits have been analyzing the meaning of last week’s election results, with conclusions ranging from dissatisfaction with the current administration to voter suppression. But religion and culture expert Dr. Alex McFarland, says that at their core, the results show one thing.

“Last week, we saw a sweep of victories across the nation for candidates who value the sanctity of life, value the sanctity of marriage and value religious freedom,” McFarland says. “The implication is clear: God is not finished with America. In recent years, we’ve seen vitriolic attacks on the biblical foundations of our nation, but from her founding through recent years, America has been the nation that, while far from perfect, has stood for truth and helped lead the way in spreading the gospel of Jesus Christ by sending missionaries around the world.”

McFarland says as we see the American people electing leaders who value God’s law, we see that God’s purpose has not expired. While this is encouraging, he says, it cannot be an excuse for complacency among Christians.
