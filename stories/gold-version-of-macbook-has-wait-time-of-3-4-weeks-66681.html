Gold Version of MacBook Has Wait Time of 3-4 Weeks

April 11, 2015 Technology

Apple fans are apparently eager to switch up the classic silver MacBook for something new. The gold version of Cupertino’s super-slim new MacBook currently has a wait time of 3-4 weeks.

The new MacBook went on sale today in silver, space gray, and gold. But if you want the $1,299 or $1,599 version in gold, you’ll possibly be waiting up to a month to check out its new features. The space gray and silver versions, however, will ship in 1-3 days.

In PCMag’s review of the new MacBook, we gave it four out of five stars and found that “the 12-inch Apple MacBook is the thinnest, lightest Mac laptop yet, combining the power of OS X with extreme portability and more than 14 hours of battery life.”

It lost points, however, for only having one USB-C port and not coming with any adapters. The new keyboard and Force Touch trackpad, meanwhile, take some getting used to.
