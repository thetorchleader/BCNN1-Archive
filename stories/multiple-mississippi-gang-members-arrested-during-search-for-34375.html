Multiple Mississippi Gang Members Arrested during Search for Shooting Suspect

August 19, 2014 Black

Terry Womack
(Photo: Submitted)

Nine arrested on Fairgrounds Street, some with ties to Vice Lords, police say.

During the search for a Trenton shooting suspect last week, members of the Jackson Police Department and U.S. Marshals Service not only found the suspect in an apartment on North Fairgrounds Street, but also arrested eight other people, some with gang ties, police said Monday.

“This is a huge victory,” said Jackson Mayor Jerry Gist, who also had a message for gang members.

“Get out of the gang, or get out of town,” he said. “… If you stay in Jackson, continue to do what you’re doing to make this city unsafe for law-abiding citizens, then you’re going to jail, you’re going to prison, if you don’t die first.”

Lt. Phillip Kemper of the Jackson Police Department’s Gang Unit, said the case is ongoing, and therefore police cannot say which people arrested are documented gang members.

While at the apartment on North Fairgrounds Street on Friday, police saw drugs and guns in plain view.

After a search warrant was granted, police went back and searched the apartment and cars belonging to the people inside the apartment. They collected four illegal guns, marijuana, cocaine and hydrocodone.

The officers also found and collected drug paraphernalia and Vice Lords gang paraphernalia.

Kemper said bandannas, bylaw literature and clothing were found and labeled as gang paraphernalia.

All nine men — Jameon Tipler, Louis Dotson, Cedrick Woodruff, Terry Thompson, Terry Womack, Anthony Williams, Bobby Jones, Darel Darnell and Robert Cook — have been charged with simple possession of marijuana, possession of a schedule II controlled substance with intent to sell, possession of a schedule III substance with intent, possession of drug paraphernalia and possession of a firearm during the commission of a dangerous felony.

Tipler, Dotson and Woodruff also have been charged with being convicted felons in possession of firearms.
