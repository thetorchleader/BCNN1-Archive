Artists Prepare to Honor Kim Burrell at 2015 ESSENCE Fest In July

May 23, 2015 Entertainment

When gospel-jazz vocalist Kim Burrell burst onto the scene in 1995 with her debut solo album Try Me Again, the industry was never the same.

Now, two decades later, the 42-year-old respected vocalist is being honored for her musical genius at this year’s ESSENCE Fest.

We’re 40 days away from the festivities that begin on July 2-July 5.

Jessica Reedy, Jonathan McReynold’s and Jazmine Sullivan are leading the star-studded tribute to the three-time STELLAR award winner on July 5 in New Orleans.

At last year’s ESSENCE fest, Burrell was among the artists honoring gospel great Yolanda Adams.

Burrell was gracious and elegant on stage as she showered Adams with praise. “This woman stands tall and it’s not just because of her natural height,” said the Love & Liberty Fellowship Church pastor to the huge crowd.

“It is her spirit. It is her heart. It is her drive. It is her anointing,” Burrell continued, adding, “You have operated from your heart and your soul for years.”
