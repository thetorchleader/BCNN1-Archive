10-Year-Old Math Genius, Esther Okade, Is One of Britain’s Youngest University Students

February 24, 2015 Black

Esther Okade
TWITTER

On the one hand, Esther Okade is like any other 10-year-old, crazy about Disney’s Frozen and playing with her dolls.

On the other hand, the Telegraph reports, this Math genius is also studying to complete a math degree with Open University, despite her young age.

According to the news site, Esther is one of the UK’s youngest university students but she is already at the top of the class, three-weeks since her enrollment, even getting a perfect score on one recent test.

Apparently the math-whiz gene runs in Esther’s family, as her six-year-old brother, Isaiah is also showing his own genius, high school level math.

“Isaiah is following in his sister’s footsteps. He is doing calculus and advanced algebra,” Esther’s mother, Efe told the Telegraph. “Even when he was in my tummy Esther was already teaching him.”

Her mother said that enrolling a 10-year-old into university was “an interesting process.”
