Eboni Green to Talk About Using Social Media for Ministry Purposes on March 22 at Triumphant Baptist Church in Hyattsville, MD

March 20, 2014 Uncategorized

“The Great Commission has gone Digital” (Courtesy Photo)

For those who want to energize their ministry with 21st century technology, Eboni Green says “The Great Commission has gone Digital.”

She offers ways to multiply the message and extend outreach at a workshop, 10 a.m., March 22 at Triumphant Baptist Church, 6509 Riggs Road, Hyattsville, Md.

Green has lectured at Duke Divinity School and Howard and Southern New Hampshire universities. Her “passion is ministry and her gifting is media.” For more information visit digitalgreatcommission.eventbrite.com.
