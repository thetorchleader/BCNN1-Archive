Black and White Christians, this Election is Not a Black and White Thing; It is a Right and Wrong Thing

November 5, 2014 Christian

Word to the Wise: Black and White Christians, don’t allow the devil and others to push you to make this repudiation of President Obama a black and white thing, because it is not. It is a right and wrong thing!

By the way, it was not white people who put Republican Bruce Rauner in the governor’s mansion in President Obama’s home state. It was brave black people who do not want their beloved city of Chicago or the state of Illinois to become another Sodom and Gomorrah.

–BCNN1 Editors
