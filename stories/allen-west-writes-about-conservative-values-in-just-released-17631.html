Allen West Writes About Conservative Values In Just-Released Book, “Guardian of the Republic”

April 11, 2014 Books

Now more than ever – the American public needs a guardian of conservative values. Allen West can be that guardian. He personifies the American dream: rising from humble beginnings in Atlanta to serving as a great conservative leader in American politics. Throughout his life, Allen West has enjoyed a variety of opportunities to serve and lead. Today, he carries many titles ranging from Lt. Colonel, U.S. Representative, “Dad,” and Scourge of the Far Left.

In Guardian of the Republic: An American Ronin’s Journey to Faith, Family and Freedom, the best-selling author and leading political thinker offers a thoughtful voice in our noisy and fast-paced world. Speaking as a family man who has served our country in the military and again as an elected public servant – West has great insight to offer as a leader conservatives can trust.

West is committed to family, faith, tradition, service, honor, fiscal responsibility, courage and freedom.

You won’t want to miss this just-released book! Find out what this principled, informed conservative that respects our country’s history, philosophical roots, faith and freedom offers to get get America back on track!
