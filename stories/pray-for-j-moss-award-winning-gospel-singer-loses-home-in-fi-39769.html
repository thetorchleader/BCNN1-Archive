Pray for J Moss: Award-Winning Gospel Singer Loses Home In Fire

October 15, 2014 Entertainment

Grammy Award Winning producer and Stellar Award Winning artist J Moss was home alone on Tuesday evening when fire broke out. The fire quickly spread though out the house but Moss was able to escape. The cause of fire to be determined.

J Moss, best known for hit songs, “We Must Praise”, “Praise on the Inside”, “Psalm 150″ and collaborations with Kirk Franklin (“Could’ve Been Me”) and Byron Cage (“We Love You”) is requesting that everyone keep him, his wife and two sons in prayer.

PAJAM, encourages Moss’ supporters to send their love via twitter. You can tweet him @InsideJMoss.

We pray God’s hands comfort him during this time.
