In Celebration of Black History Month, Christian Book Company Sells Ebooks on Amazon Kindle for Only 99-Cents

February 10, 2012 Books
In celebration of black history month, Black Christian Book Company (www.BlackCBC.com), is presenting nearly 100 inspiring Christian books written by Christian authors that are on sale for 99 cents only on Amazon Kindle. This sale lasts for a limited time. Readers can download these books on their iPhone, Android, iPad, Windows Phone, Blackberry, or PC through Amazon’s Kindle reading apps. 
The books in this sale will be of benefit to people from all walks of life – children, young adults, women, men, pastors, teachers, parents, fiction lovers and anyone who just wants to read an inspiring book based upon the Word of God. Many of these books are directed towards young people to help them become great historymakers. Some of the bestselling books included in this sale are:
1. Dialogue with My Daughters by Jeffrey A. Johnson Sr.
2. Letters to Young Black Men by Daniel Whyte III
3. Healing Our Broken Village by Dr. Frederick D. Haynes III
4. Successfully Raising Young Black Men by Kevin D. Barnes Sr.
5. God Is Greater Than Family Mess by Joey Johnson
6. God’s Got My Back by Rudolph W. McKissick Jr.
7. Who’s Renting Space in Your Head? by C. NaTasha Richburg
8. Wisdom for Women of Worth and Worship by Natalie A. Francisco
9. Letters to Young Black Women by Daniel Whyte III
10. When Faith Fights by Shay Bills
The complete list of books and direct links to their Kindle pages are available at this link: http://www.blackchristianbookcompany.com/bcbc2/99-cent-sale. 
Each of these books are also available through Amazon Kindle’s Lending Library program. For Prime members of Amazon.com only, this program allows readers to choose from thousands of books to borrow for free as frequently as a book a month, with no due dates. Each Kindle page for the books in this sale includes information about the lending library. More information about the Kindle Lending Library program is available at this link: http://www.amazon.com/gp/feature.html/?docId=1000739811. 
Take advantage of this sale on great Christian books today and be encouraged and inspired to live your best life now. This sale will last for a limited time only.
