Rihanna’s ‘Oprah’s Next Chapter’ Interview Boosts Ratings for OWN

August 22, 2012 Uncategorized

Oprah Winfrey is tooting her OWN horn today, announcing that her “Oprah’s Next Chapter” episode featuring Rihanna topped all key African American demos in its time period on all of broadcast and ad-supported cable, ranked No. 2 in all of primetime overall among ad-supported cable, and was No. 1 in its time period in women 18-49 among all broadcast and ad-supported cable.
A press release from OWN also points out that the episode “posted triple digit growth across the key demos versus year ago numbers, and was the second highest rated telecast in OWN history.
