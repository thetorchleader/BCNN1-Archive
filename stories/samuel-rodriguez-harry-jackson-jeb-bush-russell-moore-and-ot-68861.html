Samuel Rodriguez, Harry Jackson, Jeb Bush, Russell Moore, and Others to Speak at National Hispanic Christian Leadership Conference Gathering on April 28-30

April 21, 2015 Christian

NHCLC/CONELA Annual Gathering to be held April 28-30 in Houston

WHAT:

The National Hispanic Christian Leadership Conference (NHCLC/CONELA) annual convention bringing together more than 1,000 Hispanic leaders to discuss issues of faith, education, immigration, racial reconciliation, religious liberties, Israel, and family and marriage.

WHO:

Convened by NHCLC/CONELA President Rev. Samuel Rodriguez and featuring guest speakers across politics, education, religion and business, including:

  • Jeb Bush, 43rd Governor of Florida;
  • Gov. Mike Huckabee, 44th Governor of Arkansas;
  • Luis Gutierrez, U.S. Representative of Illinois;
  • Reuven Azar, Deputy Head of Mission for The Embassy of Israel to the United States;
  • León Rodriguez, Director of U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services
  • Daniel B. Prieto, Director of Cybersecurity and Technology for the U.S. Department of Defense;
  • Dr. Russell Moore, President of the Southern Baptist Ethics & Religious Liberty Commission;
  • James Robison, Founder and President of Life Outreach International;
  • Rabbi Yechiel Z. Eckstein, Founder and President of the International Fellowship of Christians and Jews;
  • Galen Carey, National Association of Evangelical Vice President, Government Affairs;
  • Matthew Soerens, U.S. Church Training Specialist for World Relief;
  • Kelly M. Rosati, J.D., is the vice president of Community Outreach at Focus on the Family;
  • Ed Stetzer, Executive Director of LifeWay Research
  • John Mendez, Director of Faith Initiatives of The Libre Initiative;
  • Alton Garrison, Assistant General Superintendent of the Assemblies of God; and
  • Dr. Gus Reyes, Director of the BGCT Christian Life Commission.

NHCLC/CONELA is the world’s largest Hispanic Christian organization. It serves as a representative voice for the more than 100 million Hispanic Evangelicals assembled in over 40,000 U.S. churches and another 500,000 congregations spread throughout the Spanish-speaking diaspora. For additional information, visit http://www.nhclc.org.

WHEN:

April 28-30, 2015

A full schedule of events is available at: http://bit.ly/NHCLCschedule.

WHERE:

Crown Plaza Houston

8686 Kirby Drive

Houston, Texas

DETAILS:

The theme of the 2015 convention is, “From Survive to Thrive.” More than 1,000 global Christian leaders are expected to attend.

During the convention, NHCLC/CONELA will celebrate its merger, which became official last year, and reveal its new international name. It will also introduce new organizational leadership and launch an innovative Hispanic-focused television network in partnership with Trinity Broadcasting Network (TBN).

For more information visit: https://nhclc.org/2015-nhclc-board-convention.
