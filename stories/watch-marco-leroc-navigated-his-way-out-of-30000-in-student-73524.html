WATCH: Marco LeRoc Navigated His Way Out of $30,000 In Student Loan Debt; Now He’s Showing Others How to Do the Same

May 14, 2015 Finances

Marco LeRoc

Studies show more than 70 percent of all students graduating from four-year colleges had student loan debt in 2012.

That figure represents 1.3 million students graduating with debt, a number that’s up from 1.1 million in 2008 and 0.9 million in 2004.

Marco LeRoc is a former international student from the Togolese Republic (Togo) who found himself more than $30,000 in student loan debt after his freshman year at the University of Nebraska Omaha.

LeRoc managed to successfully navigate the rough the road out of college debt, and now he is also showing others how they can too with his new book Screw College Debt.

The author, entrepreneur and personal finance coach shared more about his journey with CBN News’ Efrem Graham. Click play to watch.
