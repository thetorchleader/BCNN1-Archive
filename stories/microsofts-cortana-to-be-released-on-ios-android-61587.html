Microsoft’s Cortana to be Released on iOS, Android

March 13, 2015 Technology

Cortana, Windows Phone personal digital assistant (PHOTO CREDIT: Sean Fujiwara)

It appears Microsoft personal assistant Cortana won’t be limited to just Windows devices.

According to Reuters, Microsoft plans on releasing a standalone app featuring Cortana available to download on Apple iOS and Google Android smartphones.

Cortana — named after the artificial intelligence featured in the video game series Halo — will also receive a big upgrade when Microsoft launches the PC operating system Windows 10, says the report.
