Donnie McClurkin Asks for Prayer as He Prepares to Minister In Paris Where Attack Was Planned on Churches

April 24, 2015 Christian

Donnie McClurkin (Credit: GETTY)

Gospel artist Donnie McClurkin posted on Facebook:

I’ve arrived in France…requesting your prayers as I minister! Share this and rally those who will pray! Just heard on BBC after taping my video that Global unrest is growing! Plans in place to shoot Christian church’s in Paris on Sunday morning (where i am preaching on Sunday) …I will preach, regardless…calling on those who will hold this up before God! Share this around the world!
