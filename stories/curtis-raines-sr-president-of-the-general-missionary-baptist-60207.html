Curtis Raines Sr., President of the General Missionary Baptist Convention of Georgia, Goes Home to Heaven

March 6, 2015 Black

The General Missionary Baptist Convention of Georgia sadly announces the passing of our President, Reverend Curtis Raines, Sr.  Reverend Raines passed February 21, 2015. We wish to express our condolences to his family and to The New Pilgrim and The Mount Zion Missionary Baptist Church families.
