L.A. Officials Want Eyewitness in Ezell Ford Police Shooting

November 23, 2014 Black

(L-R) Deputy Chief Earl Paysinger, Inspector General Alex A. Bustamante, L.A. City Councilman Curren Price, L.A. City Mayor Eric Garcetti, L.A. Police Chief Charlie Beck and L.A. County District Attorney Jackie Lacey. (photo by Charlene Muhammad)

Mayor Eric Garcetti, LAPD Chief Charlie Beck, and other city officials requested for eyewitnesses to come forward with information in the fatal police shooting of Ezell Ford during a Nov. 13 press conference at the LAPD Police Administration Building.

Flanked by Deputy Chief Earl Paysinger, L.A. City Councilman Curren Price, Los Angeles County District Attorney, Jackie Lacey, and Inspector General, Alexander A. Bustamante, Garcetti asked the public to come forward with any details they may have and informed that the LAPD would release Ford’s autopsy by the end of the year.

“We’re here today to ask for help from the public, and we’re here today united in the search for truth,” Garcetti told reporters.

“The community, the Ford family, our police officers and this city deserve nothing less, the truth and nothing but the truth,” Garcetti continued.

 In English and Spanish, the mayor reiterated the officials’ call for people to come forward to help the community, family, officers, and the department to get to the bottom of what happened when two officers allegedly shot Ford when he was subdued, on the ground.
