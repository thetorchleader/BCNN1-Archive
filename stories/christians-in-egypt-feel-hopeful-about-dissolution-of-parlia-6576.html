Christians in Egypt Feel ‘Hopeful’ About Dissolution of Parliament

June 15, 2012 Uncategorized

Compass Direct News (CDN) is reporting that Christians in Egypt welcomed today’s (Thursday, June 14, 2012) court decision to dissolve the Islamist-controlled parliament due to election irregularities.

CDN says that that decision “offered hope” for Christians, many of whom were convinced the country was about to be handed over to Islamists who would stifle religious freedom and persecute non-Muslims.
“The results of parliamentary elections shocked many Christians throughout Egypt, with parties affiliated with Islamist groups winning 71 percent of the seats in the People’s Assembly,” said the CDN story.
