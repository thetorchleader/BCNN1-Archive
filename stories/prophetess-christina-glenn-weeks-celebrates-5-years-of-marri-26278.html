Prophetess Christina Glenn Weeks Celebrates 5 Years of Marriage to Bishop Thomas Weeks III and Wishes Him a ‘Happy Birthday’

June 12, 2014 Christian

via Facebook:

Today, I honor my wonderful husband’s birthday. I celebrate his life and the love he has shown me, in these awesome 5 years of being together.
