Nancy Armour: Arrogance Catches Up With Tom Brady and the Patriots

May 13, 2015 Sports

Tom Brady

by Nancy Amour

For the smartest people in the league, Tom Brady and the New England Patriots sure are dumb.

Hammered by the NFL largely because of their own arrogance, Tom Brady and the New England Patriots just couldn’t leave well enough alone. For five days now, the quarterback’s agent and Patriots owner Robert Kraft have been whining and lobbing grenades at Ted Wells, and the NFL’s investigator finally fired back Tuesday – pointing out New England’s disingenuousness in the process.

“When I was appointed to be the independent investigator, no one at the Patriots or in Mr. Brady’s camp raised any issue with my independence or my integrity to judge the evidence impartially and fairly,” Wells said on a conference call. “In fact, Mr. Kraft, to my recollection, publicly said he welcomed my appointment.

“I think it’s wrong to criticize my independence just because you disagree with my findings.”

And what of Brady’s testimony, which his agent, Don Yee, claims Wells so nefariously omitted from his report? Wells said it was about as enlightening as one of the quarterback’s news conferences.

If he’s so convinced it exonerates Brady, Wells added, Yee ought to go ahead and put it out there himself.

“All of this discussion that people at the league office wanted to put some type of hit on the popular, iconic player in the league — the real face of the league — it just doesn’t make any sense,” Wells said. “It is really a ridiculous allegation.

“What drove the decision in this report was one thing: it was the evidence. And I could not ethically ignore the import and relevancy of those text messages and the other evidence.”

See, this is the problem when you’ve been the league masters at Xs and Os for nearly two decades. You develop a hubris that makes you think you can push the league around like you do the New York Jets, and that conceit winds up costing you more than the crime itself.
