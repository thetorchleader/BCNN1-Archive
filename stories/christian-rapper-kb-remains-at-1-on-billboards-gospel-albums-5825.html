Christian Rapper KB Remains at #1 On Billboard’s Gospel Albums Chart

August 5, 2012 Uncategorized

After making a #1 debut on Billboard’s Gospel Albums chart last week, Reach Records artist KB remains at the top with his new album, Weight & Glory.

HUUUUGE drop-off for Amber Bullock, from #3 to #21 this week. Not sure exactly why, but I’m hoping it’s just an administrative error of some sort. I’m expecting she’ll return next week…
Here’s the top 10 this week:
1 | KB – Weight & Glory
2 | Mary Mary – Go Get It
3 | Marvin Sapp – I Win
4 | William McDowell – Arise
5 | WOW Gospel 2012
6 | Charles Jenkins & Fellowship Chicago – The Best of Both Worlds
7 | JJ Hairston & Youthful Praise – After This
8 | James Fortune & FIYA – Identity
9 | Jessica Reedy – From The Heart
10 | Helen Baylor – Ultimate Collection
Next week, look for an impressive debut from J Moss, whose new album, V4…The Other Side, hit stores this week. Read Gospel Pundit’s review here.
