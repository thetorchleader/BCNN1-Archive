Eric Holder Criticizes President Obama’s Basketball Game

February 17, 2015 National

Attorney General Eric Holder speaks at the National Press Club. (Manuel Balce Ceneta, AP)

Living up to the trash talking of New York’s finest hoops stars, Eric Holder says President Obama is a pretty good basketball player … for a guy from Hawaii.

Zing.

Actually, the attorney general gave Obama — who is his boss, after all — his props when asked to compare the president’s basketball skills to his own during a National Press Club luncheon Tuesday.

“I’m from New York City, the home of basketball players like Nate “Tiny” Archibald, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Julius “The Doctor” Erving, Connie Hawkins, you know, Chris Mullin. The president is from Hawaii,” Holder said, drawing a few laughs.

Here’s the part where Holder — who learned to shoot and dunk in Queens — defers to the basketball player in chief:

Now, I’m just saying that his background, he’s a good ballplayer. He’s got a real good left hand. He has the ability to drive. He’s 10 years younger than I am. He’s in better shape, he’s still my boss.
