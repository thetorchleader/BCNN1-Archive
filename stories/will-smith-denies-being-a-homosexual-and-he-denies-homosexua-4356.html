Will Smith Denies Being a Homosexual and he Denies Homosexual Relationship with Fellow Actor

March 10, 2012 Uncategorized

Will Smith has slammed allegations that he is having a homosexual relationship with US actor Duane Martin.

The ‘Mission Impossible’ star, whose marriage to Jada Pinkett-Smith is said to be on the rocks denied the rumours that he is gay but a source told Star Magazine that their burgeoning relationship is causing problems at home.
A source revealed: “Will spends significantly more time with Duane than he does with Jada and the kids.”
The insider went on to claim that Jada Pinkett Smith is jealous of how close her husband is to the All Of Us actor: “[She] can’t stand Will’s relationship with Duane. “She feels like Will flaunts it in her face, taking him on exotic vacations while she’s sitting home alone.”
