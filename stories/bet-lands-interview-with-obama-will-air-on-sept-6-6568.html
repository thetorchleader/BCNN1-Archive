BET Lands Interview With Obama; Will Air on Sept. 6

August 23, 2012 Uncategorized
BET, the Black Entertainment Television network, will interview President Barack Obama today in Columbus, Ohio, POLITICO has learned.
The full interview will air on Sept. 6, and is his first with an African-American television outlet during the 2012 campaign season. The interview will air two months ahead of the presidential election and just days before the release of a highly anticipated book about Obama by Bob Woodward and a highly anticipated Vanity Fair article on Obama by Michael Lewis.
Many members of the African-American media have voiced frustration over their limited access to the nation’s first black president, including Roland Martin, who has addressed the issue on his TV One program “Washington Watch.”
“In his entire presidency, he’s had one interview with TV One, and that was more than 18 months ago,” Martin recently told POLITICO in response to a story about Obama’s soft-media strategy. “If you think he’s ignored mainstream media, how do you think those of us in the black press feel?”
Obama also gave an interview to Black Enterprise Magazine recently, published on Aug. 6.
UPDATE (11:16 p.m.): POLITICO has learned that Time magazine White House correspondent Michael Scherer has also landed an interview with President Obama today. Scherer’s interview will be conducted aboard Air Force One.
This post has been updated to clarify that the BET interview is President Obama’s first with an African-American *television* outlet in the 2012 campaign.
SOURCE: Dylan Byers
