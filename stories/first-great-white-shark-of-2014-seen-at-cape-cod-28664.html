First Great White Shark of 2014 Seen at Cape Cod

Date not recorded in the original archive Science

According to the Atlantic White Shark Conservancy, first great white shark has reached the Cape Cod. This year’s first great white shark was spotted on Saturday.

The year’s first great white shark has the length of 12 feet to 15 feet and is a female. The name given to the shark by the conservancy drawing winner is ‘Ping’.

It has been reported that the shark was first spotted by the plane and then three conservancy board members approached the great white in a boat. The area was one-quarter of a mile from Nauset Beach on the Orleans shoreline.

As compared with the last year, this year’s official shark sighting was two weeks earlier.

Ping is now the studied for the Massachusetts Shark Research Program white shark population study.

According to the recent report from National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, great white shark population is surging again.

SOURCE: Upstart Magazine
