Fake Eyelashes Pose Some Real Health Risks

March 11, 2015 Health

Fake eyelashes are nothing new. For years they’ve been used to give the appearance of longer, fuller lashes. But optometrists say they actually pose some very real health risks.

Experts say that the formaldehyde contained in some of the glue used on fake eyelashes can cause allergic reactions.

Some symptoms to be on the lookout for: swelling, redness, itching, or pain.

Pang said that any symptoms lasting for more than a day should prompt a trip to an eye care professional.
