Florida State Coach Jimbo Fisher Defends Jameis Winston in Autograph Inquiry

October 15, 2014 Sports

Florida State Seminoles head coach Jimbo Fisher talks with quarterback Jameis Winston (5) during the fourth quarter against the Wake Forest Demon Deacons at Doak Campbell Stadium.
(Photo: John David Mercer, USA TODAY Sports)

Florida State coach Jimbo Fisher found himself defending Jameis Winston again Tuesday night, this time in regard to an ESPN report that more than 900 signed Winston items were authenticated by the same company linked to Georgia running back Todd Gurley’s suspension last week.

“He’s never taken a dime for anything,” Fisher said. “He’s signed thousands of things. I mean, the guy sits for an hour and a half before a baseball game and signed and an hour and a half after a baseball game — I mean, there are thousands of things out there with his name on it.

“He is very accommodating to people.”

PREVIOUSLY: Winston investigated by Florida State for autographs

Unlike the Gurley incident, in which a dealer reportedly accused the star running back of taking money and had photo evidence of him signing, no one has come forward and alleged that Winston took any money.

The ESPN report centered on the fact that many of the Winston items were authenticated in sequential order by James Spence Authentication.
