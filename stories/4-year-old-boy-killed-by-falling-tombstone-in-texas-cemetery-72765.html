4-Year-Old Boy Killed by Falling Tombstone in Texas Cemetery

May 10, 2015 National

A 4-year-old West Texas boy died after he was struck in the head by a five-foot falling tombstone in a cemetery.

Levi Driver was playing while his family was nearby visiting a gravesite at the Ector County Cemetery when a nearly 100-year-old headstone toppled over Friday afternoon, Odessa Police spokesman Cpl. Steve LeSueur said.

The child was taken to Odessa Regional Medical Center where he was pronounced dead.

It was not immediately clear how the stone fell, although it’s in the oldest section of the cemetery.

According to KWES, the tombstone that hit the 4-year-old measures five feet tall.

Ector County’s Safety and Risk Management Coordinator Sam Brijalba told KWES ‘A lot of these tombstones date back to the 1800s, and I believe this one is 1919.’

Brijalba also spoke about uneven ground, telling the television station ‘That might be a factor, as moisture, because we’ve had some recent rains, but you know, I can’t say that the, you know, with any certainty that that was a contributing factor.’

Police said they’re continuing to investigate as an accident and no foul play is suspected.

Adriana Segovia’s father is buried at the same cemetery, and his grave is marked by a damaged headstone, KOSA reported.

Segovia told the television station she thinks headstones are being banged into by lawnmowers used on the grounds.

‘That was the first thing that came to my mind, it was probably loose and it fell on him,’ she told KOSA.

Brijalba told KOSA that ‘The tombstones themselves are actually the property of the family members. The family members are actually responsible for the headstones themselves.’
