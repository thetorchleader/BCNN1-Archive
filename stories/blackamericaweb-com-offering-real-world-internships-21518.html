BlackAmericaWeb.com Offering Real World Internships

May 8, 2014 You Can Work...

BlackAmericaWeb.com has an opportunity for a forward thinking, creative-minded person who has the ability to multitask and create timely, engaging content for the web and other digital platforms.

Candidate must be abreast on the latest in pop culture and new media technology.  We are looking for someone who can get their hands dirty in social media, web content and other new media platforms.

One of kind opportunity to gain real world experience while demonstrating talents and developing skills for leading site in African-American news & lifestyle,  complemented by a news generating broadcast network – we make news every day.

Responsibilities

  • Brainstorm, write, edit and produce content for BlackAmericaWeb.com and associated REACH Media sites
  • Assist with any social media needs
  • Possibly work with graphic and web projects in conjunction with Creative Services staff
  • Organize and maintain print and electronic files for the group
  • Help with day-to-day administrative tasks

Requirements

  • 1-3 years of experience creating editorial content
  • Proven ability to create compelling, shareable web content
  • Ability to multitask and adjust when new tasks are thrown at them
  • Works well with a team but also able to work independently

Eligibility:  A candidate must be a graduate student or an undergraduate student currently enrolled in a four-year accredited college or university or a Junior or Senior in High School.
