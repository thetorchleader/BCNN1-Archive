Nick Gordon Takes Legal Action In Attempt to Stop Bobbi Kristina’s Family from Banning his Visits

February 13, 2015 Black

© Jordan Strauss/Invision/AP File photo of Nick Gordon & Bobbi Kristina Brown in 2012.

The troubled lover of Whitney Houston’s comatose daughter has taken legal action in an attempt to stop her family from barring him from seeing her, a relative said Thursday.

Bobbi Kristina Brown’s boyfriend, Nick Gordon, 22, was banned from Emory University Hospital in Atlanta after he got into a heated argument with family members at her bedside, according to reports.

Some of Bobbi Kristina’s relatives, including her aunt Leolah Brown, have blamed Gordon for her grave condition, and want him to be prosecuted.

But now Gordon — who was raised as Whitney Houston’s unofficial adopted son — has gone to court to challenge the family, said Bobbi Kristina’s cousin.

“He’s taken legal action in that matter to get visits to see Krissi,” Jerod Brown said in an interview aired Thursday with WXIA-TV’s 11Alive in Atlanta.

Jerod Brown said he’d been acting as an intermediary for Gordon and Bobbi Kristina’s family, including her father, R&B singer Bobby Brown. He acknowledged the tension between the family and Gordon.

“I’m not in the business of pointing fingers,” he said. “You know, America is about guilty until proven innocent. Well right now, everybody’s innocent until proven guilty to me. The only thing I care about is the well-being of my cousin.”
