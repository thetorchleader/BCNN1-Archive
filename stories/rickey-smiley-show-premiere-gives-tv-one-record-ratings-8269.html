‘Rickey Smiley’ Show Premiere Gives TV One Record Ratings

September 21, 2012 Uncategorized

TV One has announced that its new original series “The Rickey Smiley Show” delivered the highest rated premiere in the history of the network.


(L-R) Jay Lewis, Roz Ryan, Gabriel Burgess, Rickey Smiley and Ajiona Alexus attend TV One & BobbCat Films premiere for latest sitcom “The Rickey Smiley Show” at The Buckhead Theater on September 17, 2012 in Atlanta
The comedy’s Tuesday, 9 p.m. debut drew 621,000 homes and earned 468,000 viewers among the coveted 25-54 crowd. The second original episode at 9:30 p.m. improved on the premiere, drawing 642,000 homes and 515,000 viewers in the 25-54 demo.
Over the four telecasts of the premieres and encores, “The Rickey Smiley Show” had a gross average audience of nearly 2.3 million viewers.
“We are encouraged by the viewer support of ‘The Rickey Smiley Show’ and consider it a strong endorsement that black sitcoms are reclaiming their place in the programming spotlight,” states Toni Judkins, TV One EVP Programming and Production.
