Dwayne ‘The Rock’ Johnson Pulls Out of Chris Christie Video “No Pain, No Gain”

July 17, 2014 Entertainment

Dwayne Johnson attends a promotional event cor “Hercules” on July 2 in London.
(Photo: Mike Marsland, WireImage)

Hours after New Jersey Gov. Chris Christie posted a Hollywood-style YouTube video that included The Rock, the video was pulled from the governor’s account.

And then it popped back up again, 3 seconds and one bulging actor shorter.

It isn’t clear exactly why the former professional wrestler, whose real name is Dwayne Johnson, was cut from the clip. But Christie’s Twitter feed had this Wednesday, along with a link to the new version of the trailer: “Unfortunately The Rock is (i)n high demand & won’t be able to appear in our Summer flick.”

Matt Katz, a reporter for WNYC-FM and -AM who covers Christie, tweeted Tuesday night that The Rock asked not to be included in Christie’s 82-second trailer. He is, after all, the star of the upcoming MGM Studios film Hercules, and the Christie video was the handiwork of the far more obscure “Statehouse Studios.”
