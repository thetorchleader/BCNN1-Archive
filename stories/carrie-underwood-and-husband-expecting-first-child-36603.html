Carrie Underwood and Husband Expecting First Child

September 1, 2014 Entertainment

D Dipasupil/FilmMagic

Carrie Underwood just shared some exciting news:

“In honor of “Labor” Day…Ace & Penny would like to make an announcement. Their parents couldn’t be happier!” she wrote on Twitter alongside a photo of herself with her two dogs, who are both wearing shirts saying they are going to be a big brother and big sister.

It’ll be the first child for the singer and her husband, hockey player Mike Fisher.
