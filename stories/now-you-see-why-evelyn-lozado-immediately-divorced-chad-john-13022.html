Now You See Why Evelyn Lozado Immediately Divorced Chad Johnson

June 18, 2013 Uncategorized

Women, If Someone Is Doing This Kind of Stuff to You, You Need to Run

Wow. Recently released photos of Evelyn Lozada’s forehead after she was head-butted by her then husband, Chad Johnson, show a horrific, nasty, bloody gash.
The photo above and others were taken by Davie, Florida police in the emergency room where Lozada was taken after Johnson’s attack on August 11, 2012. According to police, the forehead laceration measured 3 inches. (see more photos here.)
Johnson was arrested for domestic violence shortly after the incident which grew out of an over-heated argument between the then newlyweds. Lozada filed for divorce three days later.
Following the arrest, Johnson pled “no contest” to the domestic violence charge and was sentenced to 12 months probation and domestic violence classes.
