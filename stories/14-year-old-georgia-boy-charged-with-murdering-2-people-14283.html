14-Year-Old Georgia Boy Charged With Murdering 2 People Over Drug Deal

March 17, 2014 Uncategorized

Shooting victim Sterling Hargrave

Cobb County Police have charged a 14-year-old boy with murdering two people over the weekend.

The victims’ bodies were found Sunday at around 7:15 p.m. at Wild Horse Creek Park in Powder Springs.

Cobb County Police spokesman Mike Bowman said officers responded to the Macedonia Drive park after receiving a shots fired call. When they arrived, they found the bodies of 23-year-old Terrance Banks and 21-year-old Sterling Hargrave. Both men had apparently been shot to death.

The suspect was arrested and taken to the Marietta Regional Youth Detention Center on charges of felony murder and aggravated assault. His name has not been released because of his age.
