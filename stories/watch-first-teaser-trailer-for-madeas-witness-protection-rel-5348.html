WATCH: First Teaser Trailer for ‘Madea’s Witness Protection’ Released

April 19, 2012 Uncategorized

Tyler Perry’s latest Madea film will hit theaters this June, but he has released the first glimpse of the comedy via a brief teaser trailer. 

Madea’s Witness Protection is the first Perry film to feature a fairly large cast of white actors, including Eugene Levy, Tom Arnold and Denise Richards, but that doesn’t appear to have dissipated Madea’s madcap antics. Check out the clip below and tell us, will you be seeing this movie?
