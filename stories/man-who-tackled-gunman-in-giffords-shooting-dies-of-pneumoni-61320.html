Man Who Tackled Gunman in Giffords Shooting Dies of Pneumonia at 78

March 12, 2015 National

Bill Badger was praised for his ‘selfless, brave actions’ by former Rep. Gabby Giffords. (ROSS D. FRANKLIN/AP)

Bill Badger, hailed as a hero after he tackled Jared Loughner, the gunman who shot Rep. Gabrielle Giffords in a Tucson, Ariz., grocery store parking lot, died Wednesday of pneumonia. He was 78.

Badger, a retired Army colonel, was wounded before he helped tackle and hold down Loughner on Jan. 8, 2011.

Loughner opened fire during an event featuring Giffords, killing six people and injuring 13 others — including Giffords and Badger.

Giffords said Wednesday that she was saddened to learn of Badger’s passing. She was shot in the head and nearly killed — and still struggles to speak and walk.

“Bill was not only a great man and a proud Army veteran, he was a hero,” Giffords said in a statement. “I believe that Bill helped save lives that morning. And I will always be grateful to him for his selfless, brave actions.”
