Google Rules the Top 10 Most Used Apps List

August 23, 2014 Technology

Some popular apps can be a drain on your data plan.
(Photo: Thinkstock)

There are over 2 million apps available in the Apple and Google app stores, but 10 get used more often than any others.

A new study from ComScore ranks them, and points out that while the favorites are heavily used, most folks rarely bother to add new apps to their phones, even though they’re free.

We spend more time — 52% — with apps than we do with websites, according to the ComScore study, up from 40% in 2013.

The top:

1. If you find yourself constantly making new status updates and posting vacation photos for your friends, you’re not alone. Facebook is by far the most used app, used by 115 million folks monthly.

2. In second place, the Google owned YouTube app brings the latest music videos and viral talkers direct to your phone, with 83.4 million users.

3. Google, as a matter of fact, dominates the top 10 chart with 5 of the top 10. Beyond YouTube, in third place is the Google Play app — the vehicle for Android phone owners to get new apps, with 72 million users monthly. Android has nearly 85% market share of phones worldwide.

4. The Google search app — so prominent on Android phones, with 70 million users.

5. The personalized online radio service, Pandora, with 69 million listeners, making it easily the world’s largest radio station.
