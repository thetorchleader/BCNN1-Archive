U. S. Senate Finally Congratulates Rev. Fred Luter on His June Election as Southern Baptist Convention President

December 2, 2012 Uncategorized

It’s a little late, but the Senate finally got around to congratulating Rev. Fred Luter Jr. for his June election as president of the Southern Baptist Convention. Luter, pastor of the Franklin Avenue Baptist Church in New Orleans, was the first African-American selected for the leadership post.
The Senate resolution, offered by Sen. Mary Landrieu, D-La., passed Thursday. Landrieu introduced the resolution soon after Luter’s election.
But in these days of partisan gridlock, it takes time to advance even a non-controversial resolution.
When the resolution was finally brought up for consideration, it passed by voice vote.
