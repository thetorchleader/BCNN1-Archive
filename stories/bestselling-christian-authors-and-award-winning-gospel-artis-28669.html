Bestselling Christian Authors and Award-Winning Gospel Artists Come Together for The National Christian Writers Conference & Gospel Artists Expo in Washington, DC on August 1-2, 2014

Date not recorded in the original archive Books

ATLANTA, GA, (June 30, 2014) – The Crawford Irby International Foundation will host bestselling authors: Dr. Willie Jolley, Wendy Griffith, Eugene Williams, Jr., Jewel W. Williams, C. Natasha Richburg, Lane Ethridge, Kevin Wayne Johnson and others at the upcoming National Christian Writers Conference. The annual event will be held at Clarion Hotel in Oxen Hill, MD on August 1-2, 2014.

The two-day conference will present several bestselling Christian authors and award-winning Gospel artists who will offer solutions, strategies and knowledge on how to write and publish books or music in the Christian marketplace. As a not-for-profit event the conference is designed to promote literacy and expose writers, authors, music artists and public speakers to the publishing industry.
On Friday, August 1st, from 7:00 pm to 10:00 pm, attendees will hear from singer and songwriter, Stephen B. Steward; comedian Kasaun Wilson; inspirational hip-hop artist, Black Jewelz; sister singing group, HYM, among others. Dr. Anthony T. Mays, CEO of World Power Gospel Radio, will emcee the event.
On Saturday, August 2nd, bestselling authors: Dr. Willie Jolley, Wendy Griffith, Eugene Williams, Jr., Jewel W. Williams, C. Natasha Richburg, Lane Ethridge, Kevin Wayne Johnson and others will hold court and speak on topics such as writing, publishing, book marketing, and entrepreneurship. Panels and workshops will help current and up and coming writers expand their creativity, accomplish their goals, and launch out successfully into the Christian publishing industry.
The National Christian Writers Conference is held three times a year in San Diego, California, Orange County, California and in the Maryland/ Washington, DC area. The mission of NCWC is to help people publish stories, songs, and films that heal, inspire, and speak to the hearts of others living in communities around the world.
Registration is now open for the National Christian Writers’ Conference and Gospel Artists Expo. Attendees may register online at www.NCWCFR.com. Early bird registration is $100 for 2 days and $60 days for 1 day.
The Clarion Hotel National Harbor is located at 6400 Oxen Hill Road; Oxen Hill, MD 20745. For more information about the National Christian Writers Conference, please call 1-619-791-5810 or email ncwcsd@yahoo.com.
