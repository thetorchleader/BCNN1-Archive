Lancaster Theological Seminary in Lancaster, Pennsylvania Offering Theology Class Taught by Dr. Jeremiah Wright

May 22, 2014 African-American

May 19, 2014 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM 

Register now for a one-in-a-lifetime opportunity to take a Master Class from

REV. DR. JEREMIAH WRIGHT, Pastor Emeritus, Trinity United Church of Christ Chicago

A renowned man of faith who is praised as a
HOMILETIC GENIUS     THEOLOGICAL SCHOLAR     THE PASTORS’ PASTOR

The Master Class provides a survey and analysis of the many different theologies preached and practiced by the Black churches in the Diaspora from the days of the European enslavement of Africans through the 21st Century, comparing and contrasting the broad range of theologies found in Christian congregations and denominations in Africa with Black Christian churches in the Diaspora.

The course will take seriously the four Black Americas in the U.S. described by Eugene Robinson and the issues of class as explicated by Kerri Day, examining the reality that there is no monolithic Black church or Black theology. Two Continuing Education Units (2.0 CEUs) offered.
