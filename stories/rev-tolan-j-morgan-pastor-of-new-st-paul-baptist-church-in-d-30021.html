Rev. Tolan J. Morgan, Pastor of New St. Paul Baptist Church in Detroit, Urges Black Baptists in Texas to Recognize the Necessity of Sacrifice for the Gospel’s Sake

July 12, 2014 Black

Tolan Morgan from New St. Paul Missionary Baptist Church in Detroit, Mich., challenges the African-American Fellowship Conference participants to make sacrifices. (Photo / Texas Baptist Communications)

A Michigan pastor urged African-American Texas Baptists to recognize the necessity of sacrifice for the gospel’s sake.

Tolan J. Morgan, pastor of New St. Paul Baptist Church in Detroit, Mich., issued his call to sacrifice in his keynote sermon at the annual African-American Fellowship conference in Fort Worth.

“If you’re ready for God to do something new in your life, in your ministry, in your church, in your walk with God, something about you has to die,” Morgan said. “Something about you has to be sacrificed.”

The African-American Fellowship re-elected as officers President Oscar Epps, pastor of Community Missionary Baptist Church in DeSoto; Vice President Ponce Brown, pastor of Shiloh Baptist Church in El Paso; Secretary Gleen Samuels, pastor of New Millennium Baptist Church in Lubbock; and Treasurer Elmo Johnson, pastor of Rose of Sharon Baptist Church in Houston.

During his second term as president, Epps said, he envisions a year of growth for the fellowship.
