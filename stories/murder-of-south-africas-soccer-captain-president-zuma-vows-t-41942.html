Murder of South Africa’s Soccer Captain: President Zuma Vows to Bring Killers to Justice

October 27, 2014 Eye on Africa

Police, forensic investigators and media work outside the home of actress and singer Kelly Khumalo in Vosloorus, east of Johannesburg, Oct. 27, 2014.

South African President Jacob Zuma has called for police to “leave no stone unturned” in finding the killers of national soccer captain Senzo Meyiwa and bringing them to justice.

The 27-year-old Meyiwa was killed late Sunday during an attempted break-in at the home of his girlfriend, singer Kelly Khumalo.

Zuma made the statement Monday, saying “words cannot express the nation’s shock” at the loss.

South African police have offered a $23,000 reward for information that leads to the arrest of Meyiwa’s assailants.

Police say two armed men entered Khumalo’s home Sunday evening while seven people were gathered there.

They said Meyiwa was shot in the upper body while trying to protect his girlfriend and was dead on arrival at the hospital. The attackers fled following the shooting. No one else was hurt.

Meyiwa’s killing was the second death to hit the South African sports world in three days, after Olympic runner Mbulaeni Mulaudzi was killed in a car accident on Friday.
