Tom Joyner Interviews Carnival CEO Arnold Donald

August 29, 2014 Black

Carnival’s CEO Arnold Donald is partying with a purpose April 11th-19th on the 2015 Fantastic Voyage Cruise! 

“We are delighted that you took, so we have eight great lines, eight great cruise lines, and Tom and the Foundation chose the Carnival Line, which is our fun line.  And then they selected one of the best ships in the line, if not the best ship, the Carnival Breeze for the Fantastic Voyage,” Arnold says.

Click the link above to hear the entire interview or read it below!

TOM JOYNER:  This gentlemen sitting next to me right here on stage, his name is Arnold Donald.

ANTHONY BROWN: The man with two first names.

TOM JOYNER:  The man with two first names.  And this man sitting right next to me named Arnold Donald is the CEO of Carnival Cruise Lines.  A little known black history fact, huh?  Arnold, I am proud to say we have partnered with you for the Fantastic Voyage for another year.

ARNOLD DONALD:  Good morning, everybody.  I hope you’re having a great time here at the venue.  And Tom, we are delighted that you took, so we have eight great lines, eight great cruise lines, and Tom and the Foundation chose the Carnival Line, which is our fun line.  And then they selected one of the best ships in the line, if not the best ship, the Carnival Breeze for the Fantastic Voyage.

TOM JOYNER:  This is your newest ship.

ARNOLD DONALD:  It’s our newest ship.  It’s three football fields long.  It’s maybe 12, 13 stories high.  However, it’s booking up fast, so it’s not like the reunion, you can’t like drive in late.  When you come late it’s gone.  And the same thing with booking the cabins.
