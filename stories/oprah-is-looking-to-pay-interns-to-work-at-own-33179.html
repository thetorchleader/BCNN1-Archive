Oprah Is Looking to Pay Interns to Work at OWN

August 9, 2014 You Can Work...

Oprah Winfrey and her network OWN are looking for interns — undergraduates and graduate students.

The program will last for 10 weeks and not only will it be a college-learning experience, the internships are paid.

Typical internship requirements imply to this job. This includes completing at least the sophomore year in college and maintaining a 3.0 GPA.

The internships require the students to work at least 15 hours a week and requires to be taken for college credit as well.

The internship is a remarkable opportunity for students to garner hands-on experience while taking advantage of networking opportunities.

And students with a variety of interests and education backgrounds can apply: business, marketing, branding, legal, broadcast production, digital video and more.
