Joel Rosenberg Discusses How the Russia-Ukraine Crisis Lines Up With Biblical Prophecy

March 27, 2014 Christian

Author and Mideast expert Joel Rosenberg has written several best-selling books about Russia, Israel and the end times. (CBN)

Ukraine Defense Minister Ihor Tenyukh resigned Tuesday as thousands of Ukrainian soldiers withdrew from the Crimean Peninsula, leaving Russian forces in total control.

President Barack Obama downplayed Russian President Vladimir Putin’s power grab, saying Russia is merely a regional power and Putin is acting from weakness.

Nevertheless, Obama said Russia’s actions demand a response from international allied powers.

The U.S. Senate is set to approve sanctions against Russia and an aid package for Ukraine.

Meanwhile, the crisis in Ukraine and Russia’s aggressive actions may have greater implications than security in Europe.

Many analysts believe Putin has global ambitions that point to Russia’s role in biblical prophecy.
