Atheist Coalition Plans to Have Presence In Every State

July 17, 2012 Uncategorized
The Secular Coalition of America, an atheist group, is setting up lobbying groups in each state, and there will also be an office in Washington, DC.
 


OneNewsNow turned to Liberty Counsel founder Mat Staver, who notes that the Coalition plans to argue for the strict separation of church and state.
“Essentially we know what that means,” he remarks. “Anything that’s religious, particularly anything that’s Christian, needs to be censored, in their view. So I think that as believers we ought to take this very seriously, and we need to make sure that our voice is represented as well.”
