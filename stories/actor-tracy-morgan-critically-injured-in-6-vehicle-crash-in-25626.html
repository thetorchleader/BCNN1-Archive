Actor Tracy Morgan Critically Injured in 6-vehicle Crash in New Jersey; 1 Other Person Killed

June 7, 2014 Entertainment

Tracy Morgan (AP)

Actor Tracy Morgan was critically injured early Saturday after a crash involving six vehicles in New Jersey, police said.

At least one person was killed in the crash that happened on the New Jersey Turnpike. The cause of the crash remained under investigation.

Morgan is best known for his roles on Saturday Night Live and 30 Rock.

He performed in Indy at the Old National Centre in May 2014.
