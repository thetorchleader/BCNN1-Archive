Janet Jackson Makes Rare Public Appearance

April 30, 2015 Entertainment

(Tullio M. Puglia/Getty Images for Giorgio Armani)

Where has Janet Jackson been hiding?

The 48-year-old “What Have You Done for Me Lately?” singer made a rare public appearance Wednesday when she attended Giorgio Armani’s 40th anniversary dinner reception at Nobu in Milan, Italy. Jackson brought her husband, Qatar-based business magnate Wissam Al Mana, as her date for the starry soirée.

The six-time Grammy Award winner managed to stay under the radar, wearing a hooded blazer and black pants. Other famous attendees included Tom Cruise, Leonardo DiCaprio, Sophia Loren, Chris Pine and Tina Turner.

Jackson had not walked a red carpet since October 2014, for the Vogue Fashion Dubai Experience. Prior to that, her last public appearance was at amfAR’s 20th Annual Cinema Against AIDS event in May 2013.

The “Rhythm Nation” singer was last seen by paparazzi in February.
