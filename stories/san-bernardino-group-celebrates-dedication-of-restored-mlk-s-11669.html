San Bernardino Group Celebrates Dedication of Restored MLK Statue

April 13, 2013 Uncategorized

Robert Earl Luckett saw the 11-foot-tall Martin Luther King Jr. statue go up in 1981 in the Civic Center. And he watched it deteriorate with time.


Assemblywoman Cheryl Brown (D-San Bernardino), left, and school board member Margaret Hill, right, pose for a photo in front of the newly restored Martin Luther King statue in downtown San Bernardino April 12, 2013. (Gabriel Luis Acosta/Staff Photographer)
But thanks to a group of concerned residents, MLK has new life.
“It looks better than it ever did,” said Luckett, 58. “Those shoes really shine,” he said.
On Friday, the restored Civic Center statue was celebrated.
The event, Friday afternoon, recognized the community effort to restore the 1-ton bronze work. It also morphed into a surprise birthday event for Frances Grice, one of the early civil rights pioneers in San Bernardino. She turned 80 on Friday.
Grice was also one of the early organizers of the committee, which raised funds to pay for the renovation.
“There’s more coming,” said Luvina Beckley Knight, also on the executive committee with Grice.
