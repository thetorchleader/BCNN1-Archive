Road Rage Incident Leaves Houston Woman Who Was Driving to Work Shot in the Head

March 23, 2015 Black

Kay Hafford was shot in the head during a road rage incident in Houston March 20, 2015.
KHOU SCREENSHOT

Houston resident Kay Hafford, 28, was driving to work Friday when a driver cut her off. Hafford honked her horn, and that’s when the driver of a white SUV reportedly pulled up next to her and shot Hafford in the head and drove off.

According to ABC News, Hafford wasn’t aware that she had been shot when she pulled over to the side of the road to call police to report that the driver had been heckling her before he fired. It wasn’t until she was on the phone with the operator that she noticed she was bleeding.

Hafford was rushed to Memorial Hermann Texas Trauma Institute, where doctors removed bullet fragments from her brain, according to ABC News. Hafford was released from the hospital, and police continue to look for the shooter, described as a male driving a white Chevy Tahoe.
