Erica Campbell to Kick Off HELP 2.0 Tour This Spring

February 12, 2015 Entertainment

Erica Campbell, winner of Best Gospel Album for ‘Help,’ poses in the press room during The 57th Annual GRAMMY Awards at the STAPLES Center on February 8, 2015 in Los Angeles, California. (Credit: GETTY)

2015 is shaping up to be a great year for Erica Campbell who just nabbed the first Grammy® Award of her solo career for her debut album HELP.

After accepting the “Best Gospel Album” honor, the 42-year-old singer is preparing to kick off her tour this spring.

EEW has learned from her publicist, JoJo Pada of Ignition PR, that Campbell will officially hit the road for the HELP 2.0 tour which begins May 1st in Baltimore, MD.

No other cities have yet been announced.

“Thrilled! Overwhelmed! Honored! God is faithful and amazing,” said Campbell after her Grammy® win. “I’m just going to keep on singing like I’ve been singing.”
