Karen Clark Sheard Invites Women In Ministry to Her “Ladies 1st” International Summit

August 21, 2014 Christian

Karen Clark Sheard

It’s a girlfriend’s getaway designed to help women in ministry decompress, de-stress and delight in some much needed me-time. Who doesn’t love that?

Pastor’s wife and four-time Grammy® winner Karen Clark Sheard, 53, is hosting her “Ladies 1st” International Summit September 10th through 13th to give women respite from the daily grind.

“All of my leading ladies, I need a break, so I know you need a break,” said the first lady of Greater Emmanuel Institutional Church of God and Christ in Detroit, in a promotional video.

The world-renowned singer of the legendary Clark Sisters knows all about juggling family and ministry. Though she lives to pour into the lives of others, Clark-Sheard admits it can get exhausting.

“We love to serve. We love our church family,” and yet, she suggests that women “have a break together,” she said.

Who’s in?

According to the BET “The Sheards” reality star, this event is her way of showing appreciation and building up the women who sacrifice continually to get it all done.

Held at the Virginia Beach Convention Center, the summit will bring together pastors, co-pastors, bishops, and minister’s wife, widows and adjutants from all over the world and from every denomination.

For four days of sharing, healing and breakthrough, in a relaxing environment outside of the traditional church landscape, women will bask in the refreshing experience as one family.
