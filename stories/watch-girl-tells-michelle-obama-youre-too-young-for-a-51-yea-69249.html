WATCH: Girl Tells Michelle Obama ‘You’re Too Young for a 51 Year Old’, Receives Hug From First Lady

April 23, 2015 National

Anaya Brodie, left, hugs first lady Michelle Obama during the White Houseís annual “Take Our Daughters and Sons to Work Day” in the East Room on April 22, in Washington.
(Photo: Evan Vucci, AP)

Think it is safe to say that this compliment made Michelle Obama’s day.

Michelle Obama was fielding questions from children of Executive Office employees and local youth from the Boys and Girls Club and D.C. Child and Family Services.

A little girl asked the First Lady how old she was during the White House’s annual “Take Our Daughters and Sons to Work Day.”

When Obama replied, “I’m 51.”

Since the little girl’s first response was not exactly clear, Obama asked for her to be given a microphone.

Anaya Brodie then said, “You’re too young for a 51 year old.”

Naturally, Mrs. O asked Brodie to come on stage and give her a hug.
