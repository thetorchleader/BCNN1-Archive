Bishop James B. Walker to Preach Special Service at Grace Church in Connecticut

October 25, 2014 Black

Bishop James B. Walker

Pastor Thomas A. Mills, Jr. and congregation are pleased to announce that Bishop James B. Walker, presiding prelate of the Seventh Episcopal District of the Christian Methodist Episcopal Church, will preach Sunday, Oct. 26, at the 10 a.m. worship service. There will be great music and an inspiring message. The service will take place at the Trinity-on-Main in downtown New Britain.

The Seventh Episcopal District encompasses congregations in Connecticut, Massachusetts, New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Delaware, Maryland, Virginia, Washington, D.C. and North Carolina.

Bishop Walker, a native of Boley, Okla., was elected the 56th bishop of the C.M.E. Church at its 37th General Conference held in Mobile, Ala. He was assigned to the Seventh Episcopal District in July 2014 after serving as the presiding bishop of the Ninth Episcopal District for four years.

As former pastor of Phillips Metropolitan CME Church in Hartford for 27 years, he brings extensive pastoral experience.

Bishop Walker is a former president of the Greater Hartford Interdenominational Ministerial Alliance, as well as the former interim dean and adjunct faculty at Hartford Seminary. He is the founder and first executive director of the North Star Center for Human Development, Inc. Through his leadership, North Star was awarded a HUD grant to construct the Phillips Metropolitan Apartments, a 40 unit Senior Housing Community.
