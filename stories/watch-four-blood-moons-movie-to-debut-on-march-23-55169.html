WATCH: “Four Blood Moons” Movie to Debut on March 23

February 4, 2015 Christian

CURRENTLY IN PRODUCTION, AND PREPARING FOR A MID 2015 RELEASE!

In the movie “Four Blood Moons,” produced by Rick Eldridge and directed by Academy Award Winner, Kieth Merrill; these veteran film makers illustrate this fascinating phenomenon in a very compelling docu-drama.

Cinematic recreations of historical events from the United States, Israel and throughout the Middle East; along with expert testimonials from scientists, historians and religious scholars, are used to illustrate this story told in narrative format by a celebrity host.

————

In his riveting and highly acclaimed book, Pastor John Hagee explores the supernatural connection of certain celestial events to biblical prophecy—and to the future of God’s chosen people and to the nations of the world.

Just as in biblical times, perhaps God is controlling the sun, the moon, and the stars to send our generation a signal that something big is about to happen. The question is: Are we watching and listening for His message?

Find out more at http://fourbloodmoonsmovie.com/
