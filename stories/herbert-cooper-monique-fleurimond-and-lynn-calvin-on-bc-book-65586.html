Herbert Cooper, Monique Fleurimond, and Lynn Calvin on BC Book Review Show #264

April 5, 2015 Books

The authors that we are featuring today are: Herbert Cooper, author of “But God: Changes Everything”; Monique Fleurimond, author of “Lost in the Pews”; and Lynn Calvin, author of “I Have Two Daddies : An Earthly Daddy and A Heavenly Daddy”.

The purpose of this show is to highlight wholesome Christian books, promote them to our audience, encourage you to buy these books for yourself and for others, and most importantly, to see people come to Christ and lives changed for the glory of God.
