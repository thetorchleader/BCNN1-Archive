Tony Evans and Kim Brooks Top September 2014 BCNN1/BCBC National Bestsellers List

September 2, 2014 Books

ATLANTA, GA, (September 2, 2014) — Bestselling authors, Tony Evans (Raising Kingdom Kids — Nonfiction) and Kim Brooks (She That Findeth — Fiction) lead the bestsellers list of Black Christian authors on the September 2014 edition of the Black Christian News/Black Christian Book Company National Bestsellers List.

BCNN1.com has also released the September 2014 edition of the BCNN1/Amazon.com Kindle E-book Bestsellers List. This list ranks African-American Christian e-books based upon their sales rankings in the Amazon Kindle e-book store. Also, BCNN1 is releasing the September 2014 edition of the BCNN1/Barnes & Noble Nook E-book Bestsellers List. This list ranks African-American Christian e-books based upon their sales rankings in the B&N Nook e-book store.
