WATCH: Hip-Hop Artist Lord Jamar Says Ma$e Turned to Ministry to Avoid Problems In the Music Industry

August 28, 2014 Christian

Lord Jamar recently sat down with VladTV and shared his opinion on Mase returning to rap full time, saying that the Harlem rapper-turned-preacher-turned rapper has been teetering with that decision for years, and that more than likely he became a reverend as a way to avoid some street confrontations.

Jamar also spoke on Snoop smoking in the White House, calling it “gangsta” and saying that as a smoker, there’s something attractive about puffing where you shouldn’t, and he then tells the story of smoking weed during his own flights to Europe.
