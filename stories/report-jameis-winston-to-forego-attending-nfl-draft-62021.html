Report: Jameis Winston to Forego Attending NFL Draft

March 16, 2015 Sports

(Brian Spurlock-USA TODAY Sports)

The probable No. 1 pick in this year’s NFL draft might not be at the NFL draft.

As of right now, Jameis Winston doesn’t plan on making the trip to Chicago, according to Sports Illustrated’s Peter King. The Alabama native will be with his family and friends in the South.
