WATCH: “The Evidence is Overwhelming” that Noah’s Ark is Found

December 10, 2011 Uncategorized

It’s perhaps one of the most told stories in the Bible.

Cartoon sketches of Noah’s ark fill children’s books, and Hollywood even produced a modern-day adaptation in “Evan Almighty.”

Now, a group of scientists say they’ve found parts of the biblical ark.

Daniel McGivern and his team
claimed to have discovered two large sections of Noah’s ark resting
just below surface atop Mount Ararat in Turkey — where the Bible says
the ark came to rest.

“The mountain is treeless. The mountain is volcanic
with gases. There is no conceivable way that you could have an object
that big on a mountain,” McGivern said.

The team used military satellite imagery and ground
penetrating radar technology to locate the ruins. They believe the large
object is wooden.

“The evidence is overwhelming,” McGivern added. “This is the large piece from Noah’s ark.”

His evidence is based solely on imaging technology.
