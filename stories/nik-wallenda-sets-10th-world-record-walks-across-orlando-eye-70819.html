Nik Wallenda Sets 10th World Record, Walks Across Orlando Eye

April 29, 2015 National

© AP Photo/John Raoux, File In this April 27, 2015 file photo, Nik Wallenda answers questions at a news conference in front of the Orlando Eye in Orlando, Fla.

Famed tightrope walker Nik Wallenda set his 10th world record Wednesday after defying death and gravity to cross the 400-foot tall observation wheel in Florida without a balancing pole. 

The 36-year-old untethered Florida man battled slight gusts and slippery conditions for about five minutes to make history as the first to attempt and finish the dangerous walk along the Orlando Eye’s 6-inch wide beams as the attraction spun at 1 mph.

“This is definitely a dream of mine,” Wallenda said after shimmying across the narrow beams to complete the scary stunt, according to TODAY News.

“I’m living out what my family has done for over 200 years. To be able to fulfill these life-long dreams is an awesome feeling of accomplishment.”

Wallenda already has nine Guinness World Records in his pocket.

His feats including tiptoeing across Niagara Falls in 2012, negotiating a 2-inch thick cable over the Grand Canyon in 2013 and traipsing between two Chicago skyscrapers in November.
