Jay-Z Taking Break from Studio to Spend Time with Daughter

May 17, 2012 Uncategorized

New daddy, Jay-Z, confirmed he’s going on ‘maternity leave’ to spend some time with his baby Blue Ivy.
He’s taking a break from music for now to enjoy his life with his new family.
“I thought I would be more inspired to have all these new feelings to talk about, but I really just want to hang out with my daughter,” Jay-Z told Billboard Monday. “I want to enjoy this time for what it is.”
