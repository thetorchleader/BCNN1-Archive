Mother of Man Who Killed Two New York Police Officers Issues Apology

December 24, 2014 Black

Ismaaiyl Brinsley
TWITTER

Shakuwra Dabre issued a statement acknowledging her son’s troubles and offering condolences and apologies to the families of the two NYPD officers who were killed.

The mother of New York City cop killer Ismaaiyl Brinsley spoke out, apologizing for the havoc and devastation her son caused when he killed two police officers in cold blood before taking his own life, CNN reports.

“Ismaaiyl was a very troubled young man whose life was in turmoil and spiraling out of control,” Shakuwra Dabre told CNN affiliate WABC in a statement. “We were estranged for a few years, with occasional contact. I am deeply sorry for the loss of the two innocent men who were killed, and offer my sincerest condolences to their families.”

This is not the first time Dabre has voiced the horror she felt over what her son had done. She also offered condolences to the families of those slain in an interview with the New York Daily News, CNN notes.

“And please accept my deepest condolences, sincerely. As a mother now I hurt for the loss of my son. Just like they’re hurting for the loss of their sons,” she said.
