Mike Huckabee Wants More Christians to Get Involved In Civic Life

October 15, 2012 Uncategorized

Fox News Channel personality and former presidential candidate Mike Huckabee told members of a fledgling Christian organization in Cicero tonight that it is their responsibility to get more involved in civic life.
“Many (Christians) think they were recruited by the Secret Service instead of giving a bold declaration of the life of the Gospel,” he said.
Huckabee spoke at the inaugural banquet of the newly formed group Ten Good Men at the Conference Center at Drivers Village in Cicero. The non-profit organization was founded by David E. Long, president of Heritage Tire Sales in Syracuse.
Long has said the organization is dedicated to getting more Christians involved in civic affairs, and he hopes to create chapters in 300 cities nationwide.
About 130 people attended the banquet, including Rep. Ann Marie Buerkle, R-Onondaga Hill, who is the midst of a tight re-election campaign for Congress.
In his speech, Huckabee said Christians from all walks of life should use their gifts in a way that spreads and upholds Christian values. While some Christians believe they should isolate themselves from contemporary affairs, the opposite is true, he said.
“Don’t blame things that putrefy and get spoiled, because that’s what things do when they’re left alone and godless,” he said. “If things are dark, who’s going to turn the lights on? You are.”
That goes for politics as well as other endeavors, he said.
“If politics is dirty in this country, it’s dirty because not enough good, clean people get involved,” he said.
