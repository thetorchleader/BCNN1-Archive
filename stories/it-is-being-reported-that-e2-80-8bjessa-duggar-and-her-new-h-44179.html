It Is Being Reported that ​Jessa Duggar and her New Husband, Ben Seewald, Had Sex Inside a Room in the Church Before Their Wedding Reception; Woman Named Mary B says”her Daughters, the Bride’s Little Sister, and Other Girls Caught Jessa and Ben ‘Kissing’ and in the Midst of an ‘Act of Lust’ that Their Now Impure Eyes Cannot Unsee”

November 8, 2014 Entertainment

A Jessa Duggar rumor places her and new husband Ben Seewald having sex in a back room of the church before their wedding reception.

As you know, the family waits for their first kiss until their wedding day, according to The Spreadit. But the bride and groom and had other plans and decided to share the moment in privacy. The alleged rumor puts them in a back room of the church.

A woman named Mary B. wrote a comment on the blog “My Life As A Stay At Home Wife And Mother,” which started the Jessa Duggar rumor. She alleges that her daughters, along with bride’s little sister and several other girls, went looking for Jessa to walk with her to the wedding reception. When the girls opened the door where she and Ben had been “kissing,” they found the two in the midst of an “act of lust” that their now-impure eyes cannot unsee.

“I was very upset when I was told about the incident that was witnessed by the girls when the door to the room they were in was accidentally opened. I am not sure why they would not wait for the evening to pray and then consummate God’s marriage. The Lord has blessed them and brought them together. To hear so many people discussing what they inadvertently walked into was heartbreaking and troubling. Why did this happen? How could this beautiful, joyous day now be forever tainted and destroyed by rumors about what the girls may (or may not have) seen? And if this is true why would they commit such an act in the Lord’s home.”

We’ll never know for sure if the Jessa Duggar rumor involved lustful acts in that room at the church. Maybe we’ll have a better clue in the future. If her social media account is any indication, Duggar is enjoying the married life and being able to kiss whenever they want, according to The Inquisitr.

So far, Jessa and Ben have not made any comments about the rumor, with some fans even suggesting that the rumor has been fabricated.

There are some reports that suggest the newlyweds opted not to lock lips at the altar. Instead, the pastor informed the wedding guests about the couple’s decision to make their first kiss private. They also invited the bride’s parents, Jim Bob and Michelle Duggar, to show the wedding crowd a kiss.

While the Jessa Duggar rumor may or may not be true, she has sparked some controversy over a passionate kiss photo she shared on Instagram. Some fans are calling the image “awkward.” Duggar captioned the photo with, “It’s great living life with your best friend!”

Jessa Duggar was born from Michelle and Jim-Bob. She is also part of the family’s reality TV show called “19 Kids and Counting.” She married Ben Seewald on November 1, 2014.

While the Jessa Duggar rumor hasn’t been verified, she revealed that she wouldn’t kiss her boyfriend until their wedding day. They were only allowed to hug when saying hello or goodbye. They were not allowed to touch or feel.

Once married, however, Michelle Duggar says women should always submit to their husband sexually, explaining:

“Anyone can fix him lunch, but only one person can meet that physical need of love that he has, and you always need to be available when he calls.”

Fans of the hit TLC hit reality show “19 Kids & Counting” are familiar with the conservative Duggar family’s rules of courtship.

Those rules include only side hugs when courting, no hand holding until engagement, chaperoned dates and saving that first kiss until the couple is legally wed. It appears Jessa Duggar may have bent one of those rules as she reportedly gave Ben Seewald a full on frontal hug after he proposed following a romantic scavenger hunt in August.

If the Jessa Duggar rumor is true, it wouldn’t surprise most. Fans got a chance to see the proposal and all that led up to it in a special titled “19 Kids & Counting: Jessa’s Engagement” which recently aired. Their wedding will air on an upcoming episode.

The couple’s wedding was remarkable. Surrounded by their wedding party – including the maid of honor, Jessa’s sister Jinger, 20, and the best man, Ben’s best friend Dylan McMahan, 24 – the couple held hands and beamed as they became man and wife. Michelle Duggar says Ben is a kind person.

“Ben is a peacemaker and a gentle, kind man … I am grateful for the way the Lord brings two people together. We love Jessa so much and are so happy for her.”

Friends and family who attended the wedding say have dismissed the Jessa Duggar rumor. They were also in attendance when she was engaged. The small town of Bentonville was busy during the rehearsals.

Everyone says that husband Ben Seewald is a man of integrity who knows how to honor his wife and put her first. He is the oldest of seven children born to Michael and Guinn Seewald of Hot Springs, Arkansas. Friends say a committed relationship does a lot to mature a young man.

Jessa, the third daughter of Jim Bob and Michelle Duggar, began dating Seewald through a courting process featured on the TLC reality show, which portrays the family’s conservative religious values.

If the Jessa Duggar rumor actually happened, as its being portrayed, Jessa will be the only daughter to break those rules.
