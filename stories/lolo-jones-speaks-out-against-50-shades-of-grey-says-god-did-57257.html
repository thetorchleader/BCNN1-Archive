Lolo Jones Speaks Out Against ’50 Shades of Grey’, Says “God Didn’t Create Sex for That Purpose”

February 17, 2015 Entertainment

Fifty Shades of Grey may not have earned critical acclaim, but it pulled in huge numbers at the box office, taking in $81.7 million on opening weekend, and setting a record for the biggest Friday opening ($30 million) of any film to be released in February.

Seahawks quarterback Russell Wilson tweeted a short review of the movie last week and was widely criticized by fans for endorsing the movie given its steamy content. Olympian Lolo Jones, meanwhile, told her followers to “watch another movie.”
