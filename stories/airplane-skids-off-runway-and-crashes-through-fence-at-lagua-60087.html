Airplane Skids Off Runway and Crashes Through Fence at LaGuardia; Airport Shuts Down Until 7pm

March 5, 2015 National

Emergency responders are on the scene after a Delta plane skidded off the runway and crashed through a fence at LaGuardia Airport.

Delta Flight 1086 was coming in from Atlanta when it went off Runway 13 just after 11 a.m. Thursday, the FAA said.

Photos and video from the scene showed the aircraft sitting in the snow with its nose through a fence. Passengers could be seen exiting the plane off the wing and trudging through the snow.

Laguardia Airport: 131 passengers/crew on plane. 24 non-life-threatening injuries, 3 of whom transported at this timepic.twitter.com/UlvDtcuHUF — FDNY (@FDNY) March 5, 2015

My day so far. #crashland pic.twitter.com/dpNnGP5oQk — Steve (@steveblaze98) March 5, 2015

In an air traffic control recording archived on the website LiveATC.net, two people can be heard talking about the incident as it unfolded and a possible fuel leak.

Voice 1: “Tower you have an aircraft off 31 on the north vehicle service road, please advise, crash rescue, LaGuardia Airport is closed at this time.”

Voice 1: “Tower are you talking to the flight crew of MD-80?”

Voice 2: “I’m calling up 1086, but I’m getting no response.”

Voice 1: “OK sir, if he comes up he is leaking fuel on the left side of his aircraft heavily.”

Voice 2: “You said he’s leaking fuel?”

Voice 1: “Affirm, his wing is ruptured.”

CBS News’ Norah O’Donnell said she was on a different flight at the airport at the time of the incident.

“I was about to take off when the pilot came on the intercom and said, ‘Ladies and gentleman some terrible news, there has been an accident here at LaGuardia,’” O’Donnell told CBS2.

O’Donnell and fellow passengers sat on the runway for about 30 minutes before deplaning. She said from her vantage point, it looks like the plane jack-knifed.
