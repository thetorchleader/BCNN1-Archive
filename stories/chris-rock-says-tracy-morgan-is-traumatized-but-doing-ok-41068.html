Chris Rock Says Tracy Morgan Is ‘Traumatized’ But Doing ‘OK’

October 23, 2014 Entertainment

Fans have been wondering how Tracy Morgan has been doing ever since his accident in June. Co-star Chris Rock of their film ‘Top Five’ is giving some clues into the ’30 Rock’ star’s recovery.

Rock said on Wednesday to USA Today that Morgan is doing “ok.”

“I saw him not too long ago,” Rock said. “He doesn’t live too far from me. I went by his house and showed him a copy of the movie. He was very happy.”

Morgan plays a member of Rock’s crew in the film out in December. It also stars Rosario Dawson, Gabrielle Union, J.B. Smoove, Cedric the Entertainer and features cameos by Whoopi Goldberg, Adam Sandler, Jerry Seinfeld and more.

Top Five is about a comedian who wants a career change, but his reality TV star fiancée convinces him to put their wedding on television.

“He’s got physical therapists. There’s a lot of rehab going on. He’s traumatized – his friend died,” Rock continued about Morgan and the crash. “It’s Tracy – not the most stable guy in the world to begin with.”

Morgan’s limo was hit by a Walmart truck in June while coming back from a show. Morgan’s lawyer Benedict Morelli recently told Page Six that “the jury’s still out” on whether Morgan would perform again. Morgan filmed Top Five before his accident. “The doctors don’t know the answer. I don’t know the answer.”
