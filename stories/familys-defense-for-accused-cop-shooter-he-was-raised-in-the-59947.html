Family’s Defense for Accused Cop Shooter: ‘He Was Raised in the Church; His Parents Are Pastors’

March 4, 2015 Black

​

Jason Polanco is seen at his hearing in The Bronx Tuesday.
Photo: Robert Kalfus

The thug accused of shooting two Bronx cops after a bodega robbery was indicted on 33 raps including two counts of attempted murder of a police officer — while his family claimed he should be forgiven because he’s a good Christian who grew up in the church.

Jason Polanco, 24, ​pleaded not guilty to the charges in Bronx Supreme Court Tuesday. He ​could face life behind bars for the January shooting of the NYPD cops after he and a career criminal allegedly stuck up a Fordham bodega.

“Everyone makes mistakes. If God can forgive why can’t everyone else?” said Polanco’s cousin, Jennifer Zambrano, adding that both Polanco’s parents are pastors.

The shooter’s dad also brought up his son’s religion – which apparently didn’t stop the thug from blasting away at​ the two cops with a .44 Magnum​ ​– ​or posting online rants against cops.

​​“He’s not a bad person. He’s a Christian,” said Polanco’s dad, Johnny Polanco, who added that he thought the shooting was a mistake and his son wasn’t to blame.
