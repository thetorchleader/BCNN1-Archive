“Chicken Soup for the Soul” Writer Transforms Hardships Into Several Books

July 12, 2012 Uncategorized

The theme of today’s column is change. Let’s start with relieving neck and back pain.
Pleasanton author Annette Langer was recently selected by publishers of the “Chicken Soup for the Soul” series to contribute an article titled “A Long and Bumpy Road to Relief” about her successful experience overcoming serious neck pain.
She recounts the series of office visits, over-the-counter remedies, physical therapy, acupuncture and other attempts that failed to relieve her daily pain.
How did she finally succeed? Well, you’ll have to read the book, which is titled “Say Goodbye to Back Pain!” and features numerous firsthand accounts of people finding solutions to their pain. Edited by Dr. Julie Silver, of Harvard Medical School, the book’s contributors include men and women of all ages, as well as an 11-year-old girl and a veteran from Afghanistan.
Langer, who has lived in Pleasanton since 1998 and volunteers for the Pleasanton police department on patrol and in the investigations division, is a breast cancer and brain injury survivor. She has written several books, including “Healing through Humor: Change Your Focus, Change Your Life” and “A Funny Thing Happened on My Way to the World: Diary of a Fearless Travel Agent.”
Choosing to approach life with an optimistic attitude, Langer, through her writing, shares her personal stories to create inspiration and humor to bring about positive change in the lives of readers.
