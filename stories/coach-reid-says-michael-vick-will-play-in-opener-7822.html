Coach Reid Says Michael Vick Will Play In Opener

August 22, 2012 Uncategorized

Eagles coach Andy Reid says there’s no question Michael Vick will be ready to play the season opener despite injuring his ribs again, although he is unlikely to play in the remainder of the preseason.
“No. Really, in-season, you’d probably have him in there for the game. As I’m standing here today, unless I’m drastically changing something, I’m not going to play him (the rest of the preseason).”
The three-time Pro Bowl quarterback didn’t practice Wednesday after bruising his ribs and the soft tissue surrounding it during Philadelphia’s win at New England on Monday.
Reid didn’t officially rule Vick out for the Eagles’ next preseason game Friday at Cleveland but he made it clear that he was unlikely to play. Philadelphia plays the Browns again in Week 1 on Sept. 9.
Vick injured his ribs on his sixth play Monday night. He lasted just six plays against Pittsburgh in the Eagles’ first preseason game before being forced out by an injured left thumb.
Reid said Monday that he wasn’t concerned by the lack of reps Vick will see before the season opener.
“The thing you have to bank on is the experience,” Reid said. “That’s what you have to look at.”
