March 26, 2015 Entertainment

Source: Sony Music Head Shot / Sony Music

Pastor Marvin Sapp’s record-breaking solo career has been crafted from the gospel singer’s capacity to persevere through his personal hardships & later cast them into anthems that celebrate his testimony. The resilient “pastor who sings” continues to share his heart-warming brand of gospel music that mirrors his own troubled yet triumphant journey with “You Shall Live.”
