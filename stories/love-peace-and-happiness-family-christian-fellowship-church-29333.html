Love, Peace and Happiness Family Christian Fellowship Church to Open New Church in Compton, CA

Date not recorded in the original archive Black

Bishop Leon Martin

Love, Peace and Happiness Family Christian Fellowship Church will open its third edifice in the greater Los Angeles area.

According to Bishop Leon and Dr. Jacqueline Martin, spiritual leaders, the new church is located at 400 South Santa Fe Avenue in Compton. The first worship service is set for Sunday, July 6, at 3:30 p.m.

“You don’t want to miss this powerful opening impact service in the city of Compton.  I invite all of my Compton friends and radio partners to be part of the new beginning,” said Bishop Martin.

“All are welcome to come and share in this celebration of praise and worship unto Almighty God,” he added.

The Love, Peace and Happiness mass choir will sing and Bishop Martin will share his vision for the city of Compton.  Also, prayer will be offered to those with special needs attending the service.
