Matthew McConaughey on Marriage: ‘Let’s Go Make a Covenant, With You, Me, and God’

October 23, 2014 Entertainment

Camila Alves and actor Matthew McConaughey attend the Cinema Society with Bally & DeLeon hosted screening of LD Entertainment’s “Killer Joe” at the Tribeca Grand Hotel – Screening Room on July 23, 2012 in New York City.
Credit: Larry Busacca/Getty Images

Matthew McConaughey is one of the biggest movie stars in Hollywood today so it is surprising how candid the Oscar winner is in GQ’s November issue. The heartthrob talked about his marriage, his faith, and all those romantic comedies he did.

“I had to get to the point where I saw [getting married] as more than just the thing to do,” the Interstellar said. “I wanted to really want to. You know, I didn’t want it to be a destination; the fun is that we’re on the adventure together.”

Matthew has been married to model Camila Alves since 2012, but it wasn’t easy getting the True Detective star down the aisle.

“We talked about it spiritually. We did a lot of reading and talked to a lot of people that had been divorced, a lot of people that had been happily married. We talked to our pastor,” he said. “In the end, our understanding was, ‘Let’s go make a covenant, with you, me, and God.’ ”

“It took her going, ‘C’mon, Big Boy, Mr. Easygoing-We’ll- Get-to-It-When-We-Get-to-It. Either s**t or get off the pot,'” he teased.
