Dr. Tony Evans Says Fatal Cutting Did Not Happen on Oak Cliff Bible Fellowship Campus, ‘Non-Member’ Came Seeking Help

Date not recorded in the original archive Christian

Dallas police are investigating a homicide at Oak Cliff Bible Fellowship. (Christina Rosales/Staff writer)

Dallas police are trying to determine what led to the death of a man who sought medical attention on the Oak Cliff Bible Fellowship campus on W. Camp Wisdom Road early Monday morning.

Dr. Tony Evans, pastor of the 10,000-member church, says the man was a “non-member” who came to the worship center with a medical emergency early this morning. “Some of our members tried to help him,” said Evans, who arrived at the church around 8:30 Monday morning.

Senior Corporal DeMarquis Black, a Dallas police spokesman, says officers received an injured-person call at 5:50 this morning. Black said the man “was not injured at this location.” Black would not confirm this was a stabbing — or that it was a homicide. But, he added, homicide detectives are investigating.
