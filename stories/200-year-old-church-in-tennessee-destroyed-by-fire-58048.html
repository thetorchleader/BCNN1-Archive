200-Year-Old Church in Tennessee Destroyed by Fire

February 22, 2015 Black

The fire Saturday at Wilson County’s Cedar Grove Baptist Church, which is more than 200 years old. (Photo: Seth Rone)

A Wilson County church more than 200 years old was destroyed Saturday morning by a fire.

Crews were called to Cedar Grove Baptist Church on Cedar Grove Road around 8:45 a.m., when a member who lived nearby noticed fire on the side wall of the sanctuary, Wilson County Emergency Management Agency Director Joey Cooper said.

The white building was about 50 percent engulfed in flames when crews arrived and the roof collapsed after about 20 minutes.

No one was inside the church at the time of the fire and no firefighter injuries were reported.

Icy road conditions slightly delayed arrival of firefighters and ice on the ground around the church caused some firefighters to slip and fall.
