U.S. Stocks Bounce Back After 3 Day Lull

October 14, 2014 Finances

Traders on the floor of the New York Stock Exchange.
(Photo: Richard Drew, AP)

The stock market, after its worst three-day swoon since 2011, is bouncing back Tuesday.

As of 12:23 p.m. ET, the Nasdaq composite is up 1%. The S&P 500 is 0.8% higher, while the Dow Jones industrial average is up 0.5% — a gain of more than 80 points.

Giving the market a lift: Positive comments about the health of the U.S. economy from big bank CEOS at JPMorgan Chase and Wells Fargo.

Jamie Dimon, the CEO at JPMorgan, says despite weakening growth abroad, the U.S. economy is an “exception.” The domestic economy is showing signs of strengthening, he says.
