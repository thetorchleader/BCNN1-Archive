Pop Star Wife of Singapore Pastor Kong Hee Takes Stand In Embezzlement Case

July 15, 2014 Christian

Sun Ho (left), wife of Singapore’s City Harvest Church founding pastor Kong Hee (right), is set to take the stand in an embezzlement case involving the pastor and other church leaders. (Facebook)

Kong Hee’s pop-star wife is set to take the stand in an embezzlement case that has become a trial indeed for the founding pastor of Singapore’s City Harvest Church.

Sun Ho will offer her testimony in what Channel News Asia calls “one of the longest-running criminal trials” in the nation.

In May, a judge ruled that six current and former leaders—including the church’s founding pastor, Kong Hee—need to answer allegations that they misused millions in church funds to finance the career of Ho.

The other defendants are Pastor Tan Ye Peng, members Chew Eng Han and Lam Leng Hung, and accountants Serina Wee Gek Yin and Sharon Tan Shao Yuen. They all face 10 to 20 years if convicted.

Prosecutors in April said that church leaders conspired to misuse around 24 million Singapore dollars ($19.2 million) in donated money, which was to go to building-related expenses, to help fund Ho’s career. Additionally, four of the six—not including Kong—allegedly misused 26.6 million Singapore dollars ($21.3 million) to cover up the previous transactions.
