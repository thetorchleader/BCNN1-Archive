During Practice, Stephen Curry Hit 77 Three-Pointers In a Row

April 15, 2015 Sports

Ronald Martinez / Getty Images

Stephen Curry has a ridiculous shooting regimen that keeps his jumper automatic. According to ESPN, in practice on Tuesday he rotated around the perimeter taking 10 shots from each of several spots, totaling 100.

He made 94 out of 100. You might see that and think “test score,” not shooting practice.

OH YEAH. I almost forgot: he made 77 of those in a row. Seventy-seven. Straight.

Before any one of you truthers or killjoys says anything about “it was just practice” and “could he do that with a hand in his face,” I’d like to see you hit almost 80 NBA-range threes consecutively.
