New York Times Executive Editor, Dean Baquet, Promises to Rebuild After Downsizing

January 9, 2015 Media

Photo: AP

New York Times Executive Editor Dean Baquet pledged to rebuild the decimated media coverage at the Gray Lady in a wide-ranging memo meant to rally the troops.

The year-end downsizing saw the departure of nearly 110 newsroom staffers, including at least 21 involuntary departures.

The media desk was particularly hard hit, reduced to columnist David Carr and little more.

Ad columnist Stuart Elliott and TV reporter Bill Carter, who each had 20-plus years at the paper, opted for buyouts, along with Christine Haughney, who covered newspapers and magazines.

Among those hit with involuntary pink slips were Leslie Kaufman, a 16-year veteran who wrote about digital media, and Craig Hunter, a deputy media editor who had overseen Elliott’s ad column.
