Shonda Rhimes Wants a Cameo Appearance from Bill Clinton on ‘Scandal’

June 16, 2014 Black

If Shonda Rhimes could pick any real-life politician to make a cameo on “Scandal,” it would be Bill Clinton!

And that would be pretty iconic to have him on the show.

Remember “Scandal” is a show where former staffer Olivia Pope (Kerry Washington) is having an affair with the president

And Clinton lived out this storyline in real life.

So he already has much to relate to on the scandalous side of politics and having to “fix” it.

“That’s not really our thing, but I will say, ‘Bill Clinton, if you’re out there, we would love to have you… We would always love to have you,” Rhimes said.
