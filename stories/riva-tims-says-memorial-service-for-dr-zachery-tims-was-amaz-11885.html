Riva Tims Says Memorial Service for Dr. Zachery Tims Was “Amazing”

April 19, 2013 Uncategorized

Forgiveness comes full circle when you can bless those that hurt you. Pastor Riva Tims, ex-wife of the late Dr. Zachery Tims, has released the pain tied to her ex’s adulterous ways that ended their marriage.
On what would have been his 44th birthday, she held a memorial service she calls “amazing.”
“The Dr. Zachery Tims Jr. celebration of Life and Legacy was amazing. “Thanks to all the Pastors, friends and family for joining us,” wrote Tims in a Facebook message expressing her excitement about the April 18 turnout.
Despite Dr. Tims’ troubled life, many paid their respects to the leader who, in 2011, at 42, was found dead on the floor of a room at the W Hotel in Times Square.
During the birthday memorial service, the Tims children introduced the 5-year plan for The Dr. Zachery Tims, Jr. Enrichment Center–a rehab facility for leaders and people struggling with addiction, depression, emotional, and spiritual burnout.
“You are an amazing Woman, Pastor Riva!” wrote one of her Facebook supporters. “I often pray that I reach that point but I’m not there. You are amazing! May God continue to bless you and yours!”
