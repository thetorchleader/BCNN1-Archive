Thousands Expected to Turn Out for First UK National Day of Prayer and Worship

September 23, 2012 Uncategorized

A massive 70,000 people are set to attend event at Wembley Stadium


ON SEPTEMBER 29 over 70,000 people, from a wide range of churches across the denominational spectrum are expected to attend the first ever UK National Day of Prayer and Worship (NDOP) to be held at Wembley Stadium.
The NDOP is the brainchild of Pastor Jonathan Oloyede, pastor of City Chapel, in Whitechapel, east London. He has long held the desire to hold a major prayer gathering at Wembley, ever since he successfully organised a major prayer event at West Ham stadium in 2007.
A convert from Islam, Pastor Oloyede has had several visions of spiritual renewal and transformation, which included seeing Christians gathered together for prayer and worship at Wembley.
He has previously said that he hopes the NDOP plays a major role in transforming the UK through prayer, as well as, inspiring churches to settle their differences and proclaim the gospel.
The event is being supported by a range of black-led Pentecostal organisations, such as Kingsway International Christian Centre (KICC), the New Testament Church of God (NTCG), and the Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG).
