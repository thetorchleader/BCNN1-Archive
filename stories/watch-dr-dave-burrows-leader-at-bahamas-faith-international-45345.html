WATCH: Dr. Dave Burrows, Leader at Bahamas Faith International, Discusses What Lies Ahead for the Ministry; Celebration Services for Myles and Ruth Munroe to Be Held December 3, Funeral Service to Be Held December 4

November 15, 2014 Christian

The family and ministry of Dr. Myles Munroe is standing strong after a deadly plane crash claimed his life, his wife Ruth Munroe, and eight others, including an unborn baby.

At the conclusion of The Bahamas Faith Ministries International 2014 Global Leadership Forum his son Myles Munroe Jr. offered a statement on behalf of his family.

“I would like to make it known that while we are grieving our losses and are deeply saddened by this tragic event we have never been nor will we ever be or feel hopeless,” Munroe said.

Ministry leaders say Dr. Munroe’s vision and legacy will live on.

What lies ahead for the ministry? Dr. Dave Burrows, a leader at Bahamas Faith Ministries International and close friend of Dr. Myles Munroe, spoke to CBN’s Mark Martin about this and more. Click play to watch.
