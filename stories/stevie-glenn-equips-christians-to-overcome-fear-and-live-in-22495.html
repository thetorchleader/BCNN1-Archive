Stevie Glenn Equips Christians to Overcome Fear and Live in Faith in Book, Conquering Fear in a Dangerous World

May 15, 2014 Books

ATLANTA, GA, (May 5, 2014) – Tornadoes. Earthquakes. Floods. Storms. Fires. Disappearing planes. Sinking ships. Crashing buses. Colliding trains. Violence. Abuse. Death. War. Unrest. Injustice. With so many terrible things happening in the world today, many people, including Christians, find themselves living in a state of fear. Intimidation, depression, and defeat easily set in when things seem beyond one’s control or coming too fast for anyone to handle. However, instead of being under the circumstances, Stevie Gleen provides a way for Christians to get on top of the circumstances and live in the victory of Jesus Christ.In the pages of Conquering Fear in a Dangerous World, Glenn encourages Christians to believe that all things are possible through God, and reiterates the truth of Scripture in 1 John 4:4, “…greater is he that is in you, than he that is in the world.” Believers will be reminded that Christ has given them the power to overcome fear of man, circumstances, and conditions without reservation. Christians who are walking with God and choosing to believe the truth of God’s Word realize that such unfortunate events in the world are only temporary and that God is sovereign and omnipotent, allowing nothing to happen that He does not know about.

Stevie Glenn shares his deep knowledge of the Word and equips believers to live without fear in faith. The wisdom and knowledge contained in the pages of Conquering Fear in a Dangerous World will activate the joy, peace and unimaginable power God has given every believer to do the impossible.

Stevie Glenn is the founder and pastor of Infinite Visionary Training Center in Minneapolis, Minnesota. He has been divinely gifted to empower the people of God and to equip them with the divine knowledge and wisdom needed to walk in the supernatural power of God and establish the kingdom of God upon earth. Pastor Glenn encourages believers to believe in the impossible by bypassing the illusions of darkness that have hindered the peace, joy, abundance, and freedom in the body of Christ.
