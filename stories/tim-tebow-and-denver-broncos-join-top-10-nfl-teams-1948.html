December 6, 2011 Uncategorized

Readers can rank the teams 1-32 along with USA TODAY’s panel of NFL analysts. Click and drag the teams to where you think they should rate in the power rankings. Re-arrange as many of the teams as you like. Hit submit at the top of the page, and watch your rankings tabulate with all other users’ to produce “everybody’s rank” in the final column.
Here are the details on the layout: The first column represents the last week’s ranking of the USA TODAY panel (omitted this time since it’s the first rankings of the year) and the second column marks this week’s edition from our panel. The third column is rankings you’ve assigned, and the fourth column shows the cumulative rankings of everyone who has voted.
