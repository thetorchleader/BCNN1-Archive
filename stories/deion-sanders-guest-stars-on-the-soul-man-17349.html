Deion Sanders Guest Stars on “The Soul Man”

April 9, 2014 Entertainment

Deion Sanders

Well look who’s coming to “The Soul Man” for a guest appearance.

None other than NFL Pro Football Hall of Famer, Deion Sanders has recently been cast to guest star on an upcoming episode of the hit TV Land series.

“Neon Deion” will play himself in a 90s flashback scene as Lolli’s (Niecy Nash) rebound boyfriend who makes Boyce “The Voice” (Cedric “The Entertainer“) jealous. The episode is slated to air later this month.

Deion Sanders will join the list of guests stars that have appeared on previous episodes of “The Soul Man” like Martin Lawrence, Brandy, Yvette Nicole Brown, David Alan Grier and more.
