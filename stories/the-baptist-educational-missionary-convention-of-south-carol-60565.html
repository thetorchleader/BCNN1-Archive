The Baptist Educational & Missionary Convention of South Carolina One Day Session to Be Held on March 20th

March 8, 2015 Black

Dates & Times
Friday, March 20th, 2015
7:00 PM

Event Description
The Sandy River Baptist Association (Upper Division) will be hosting the ONE DAY SESSION for Region 5 of The Baptist Educational & Missionary Convention of South Carolina, under the leadership of Dr. John T. McCoy, Moderator of SRBA(UD), and Vice Pres. of Region 5 of The SC Baptist E&M Convention of SC.

Dr. Donald E. Greene, Vice Pres. at Large of The Baptist Educational & Missionary Convention of South Carolina, is our Keynote Speaker for the evening.

All Pastors of Region 5 of The Baptist E&M Convention of SC are asked to represent with $50; all Associate Ministers with $25, during this session. The SRBA Mass Choir, along with SRBA Combined Hymn Choir, are assigned to minister through song.

Help us get the word out by sharing this during your church’s announcements and on bulletin board, with your pastor’s approval. Pray with us for an anointed time in worship and in business!

Event Location
Flint Hill Baptist Church (Rev Alvis Yates, Host Pastor)
1214 Flint Hill St.
Rock Hill, SC 29730
