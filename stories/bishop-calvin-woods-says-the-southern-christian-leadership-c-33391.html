Bishop Calvin Woods Says the Southern Christian Leadership Conference ‘Is Still a Fruit Bearing Tree’

August 11, 2014 Christian

Southern Christian Leadership Conference President, Dr. Charles Steele

The 56th annual Southern Christian Leadership Conference kicked off today in Birmingham’s Kelly Ingram Park with a press conference where leaders stressed the importance of the SCLC’s mission and its international influence as well as Birmingham’s legacy.

“It’s one thing to achieve something, but in order to maintain it you’ve got to have hope just as strong as you had when you achieved it,” said Bishop Calvin Woods, president of Birmingham’s SCLC chapter. “A tree is known by the fruit it bears. The SCLC is still a fruit bearing tree, and we shall go into regions beyond to bring a great harvest.”

The annual convention returns to Birmingham this year in honor of the 50th anniversary of Civil Rights Act of 1964. The event will include panels, workshops and film screenings on the history of the civil rights movement as well as its future. SCLC President and CEO Charles Steele said the city’s legacy and influence made it the perfect location for this year’s conference, which includes guests from Germany, France, Russia and other countries.
