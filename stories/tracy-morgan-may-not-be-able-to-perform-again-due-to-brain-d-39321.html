Tracy Morgan May Not be Able to Perform Again Due to Brain Damage

October 13, 2014 Entertainment

The career of actor and comedian Tracy Morgan is in jeopardy after the accident he was involved in June which left one person dead and seven hospitalized. The comedian is performing a rigorous rehabilitation treatment, but has irreversible brain damage as a result of the crash in which his limousine was hit by a Walmart truck. When asked if the comedian will ever perform again, his lawyer, Benedict Morelli, told Page Six…“The jury’s still out. The doctors don’t know the answer. I don’t know the answer.”

As reported by TVNZ on Oct. 11, Tracy Morgan is slowly learning to walk again and the former ’30 Rock’ star is slowly recovering from brain injuries, a broken femur, broken nose and broken ribs. The actor was returning from a stand-up show on June 7 when a speeding Walmart truck slammed into his limousine. The accident caused the death of comedian James “Jimmy Mack” McNair, and left Morgan and two others in critical health conditions.

Retail-giant Walmart recently denied any responsibility in the accident. Morgan and his lawyer submitted a lawsuit in July against Walmart, which ensures that the truck driver was driving in “unacceptable” conditions. Morgan issued a statement last month saying he was not to blame for the accident as his federal lawsuit, filed in U.S. District Court in New Jersey, continues.

The lawsuit asserts that the driver had been awake for over 24 hours and fell asleep at the wheel, causing the accident that cost Morgan to lose several jobs, including a television series and a film he was working on. “But he’s doing everything possible to return,” said his lawyer. In a statement, Morgan described his daily struggle:”I want to thank my fans for sticking with me during this difficult time. I love you all. I’m fighting hard every day to get back.”
