Stocks Sluggish Ahead of Interest Rates Announcement

October 29, 2014 Finances

A trader at his post on the floor of the New York Stock Exchange.
(Photo: Richard Drew, AP)

Stocks are sluggish Wednesday ahead of the Fed’s 2 p.m. ET announcement on interest rates, with the Nasdaq getting dinged by Facebook.

As of 11:47 a.m. ET, the Dow Jones industrial average is up 0.1%, the S&P 500 is flat and the Nasdaq composite is 0.4% lower.

Quantitative easing, the policy experiment the Federal Reserve hatched during the financial crisis, is expected to be phased out by the Fed, which concludes a two-day meeting Wednesday.

The bond-buying program, dubbed QE by Wall Street, has been instrumental in helping the U.S. economy rebound from the Great Recession by keeping borrowing rates low for mortgages and other forms of credit.

Facebook is falling on its earnings news after the closing bell in the previous session.FB shares are down about 6%.
