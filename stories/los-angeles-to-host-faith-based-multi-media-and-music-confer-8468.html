Los Angeles to Host Faith Based Multi-Media and Music Conference

September 29, 2012 Uncategorized


The Gospel Announcers Guild will host “The Los Angeles Faith Based Multi-Media and Music Conference.”

This power packed conference will take place October 4th – 7th, 2012 at the newly renovated and luxurious Double Tree Hotel by Hilton in Culver City, CA., and is expected to be well attended. Tickets are on sale now.
The objective of the conference is to educate, motivate, and inspire each attendee seeking careers in the field of entertainment and the arts, by giving them an opportunity to meet, greet and network with execs, producers, directors, artists and other entertainment professionals. There will be panel discussions sharing a wealth of information on how to become a gospel artist, advance your career, become a music executive, actors/actress, investor, TV & film director, radio, voice over artist and much more. It’s the total faith based experience all under one roof!
“Our goal is to target the serious artist and others in the kingdom that is ready to move forward and walk in their calling/purpose. This conference will be very intense. If just one person is inspired and a soul comes to Christ, then our work will not be in vain”, says Guild President Dr. Bro. Steve Harris.
