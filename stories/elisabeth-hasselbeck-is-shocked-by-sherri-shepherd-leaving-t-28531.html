Elisabeth Hasselbeck is “Shocked” by Sherri Shepherd Leaving “The View”

Date not recorded in the original archive Media

Former View co-host Elisabeth Hasselbeck reacted to Sherri Shepherd’s exit during the June 27 episode of Fox & Friends (Credit: Heidi Gutman/ABC/Getty Images)

Former “The View” co-host Elisabeth Hasselbeck said she was shocked to hear of the departure of her former fellow panelist, Sherri Shepherd, from the ABC talk show.

“I was shocked, in some ways,” Elisabeth said on Fox News’ “Fox and Friends,” when her fellow hosts brought up the departures at her old haunt. (Jenny McCarthy Tweeted on Thursday night that she is leaving too as a result of Sherri’s exit.)

“I’m sure, even though you think that might happen someday, I’m sure it would come across to be a shock. I haven’t spoken to her in person yet. Set to happen though,” Elisabeth said of talking to her old co-star.
