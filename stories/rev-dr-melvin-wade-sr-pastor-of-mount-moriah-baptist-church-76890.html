Rev. Dr. Melvin Wade Sr., Pastor of Mount Moriah Baptist Church of Los Angeles, Celebrates 50 Years in Ministry and as a Pastor

May 31, 2015 Black

First Lady Jacqui Wade and Rev. Dr. Melvin V. Wade, Sr.

Bishop Kenneth C. Ulmer, pastor of Faithful Central Bible Church in Inglewood, preached a powerful sermon at the closing celebration honoring the Rev. Dr. Melvin Wade, Sr., pastor of Mount Moriah Baptist Church of Los Angeles. This year, Dr. Wade’s observed both his 50th year in the ministry and 50th year as a pastor. (photo by Tray Blackwell)

The First Family of Mount Moriah includes (l-r) Sydne Wade-Gardener, granddaughter; Rev.Melvin V. Wade, Jr., son; First Lady Jacquie G. Wade, Pastor Melvin V. Wade, Sr., and Susaun Wade, daughter. (photo by Tray Blackwell)

 

L.A. Councilman Curren Price said Dr. Wade had a long history of advocating for civil and social rights for the underserved. Dr. Wade’s outstanding efforts have been recognized by former President Bill Clinton, several L.A. mayors and many community and civic leaders. (photo by Alisa Aubert)
