Church of God In Christ NOT Affiliated With New Singles Dating Site

March 20, 2014 Christian

The Church of God in Christ does not own, operate nor is it affiliated with any kind of online dating site.

A website, without the church’s permission, has listed Church of God in Christ calendar events on its site. The denomination’s legal department is working to resolve the matter.

About the Church of God in Christ
The Church of God in Christ is one of the oldest Pentecostal denominations in the world and the 5th largest Protestant group in the United States with churches in 59 countries worldwide and a membership of nearly 6.5 million adherents.
