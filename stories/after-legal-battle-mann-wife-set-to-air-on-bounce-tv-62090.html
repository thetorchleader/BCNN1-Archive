After Legal Battle, “Mann & Wife” Set to Air on Bounce TV

March 17, 2015 Entertainment

Credit: Bounce TV

In November 2014, Bounce TV and Bobbcat Productions were embroiled in a legal battle over gospel’s most marketable couple, David and Tamela Mann.

Since that time, the two parties have apparently been able to resolve their differences and Bounce TV’s Mann & Wife premieres April 7.

In the Bounce TV original series, David and Tamela play newlyweds Daniel and Toni who have a blended family, each with two children from previous marriages.

Daniel, a police officer from Atlanta, and Toni, a school teacher from Dallas, along with their kids, all move into Daniel’s house in the Atlanta suburbs and have to adjust to their new lives together.

The controversy over the series began when, last February, Bobbcat Productions, headed up by Roger Bobb, pitched Bounce TV the series featuring the Manns. Then, eight days later announced its decision to go with BET for a reality show titled “Meet the Manns”—which later became “A Manns World.”
