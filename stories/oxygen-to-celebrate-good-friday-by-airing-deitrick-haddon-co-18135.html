Oxygen to Celebrate Good Friday by Airing Deitrick Haddon Concert Special

April 15, 2014 Entertainment

Credit: GETTY

Oxygen Media will celebrate Good Friday with a concert by one of its most popular reality stars.

The network has acquired “‘Preachers of L.A.’ Presents: Deitrick Haddon And LXW Live In Concert,” which will premiere for the first time on-air April 18 at 8:30 a.m. ET/PT. The concert special features the “Preachers of L.A.” star and his League of Xtraordinary Worshippers.

Following at 10 a.m. will be the launch of an all-day “Preachers of L.A.” season one marathon, where fans can relive all eight episodes of the freshman series featuring Minister Haddon along with Bishop Noel Jones, Bishop Clarence McClendon, Pastor Jay Haizlip, Pastor Wayne Chaney and Bishop Ron Gibson.
