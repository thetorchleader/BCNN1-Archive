Palestinian Home Sprayed with ‘Death to Arabs’

February 5, 2012 Uncategorized

Palestinians say extremist Jews have
scrawled their home in a West Bank village with offensive graffiti in
retaliation for an upcoming demolition of a wildcat Jewish outpost.

Resident Ayid Mazloum,
47, says the men sprayed his home with “Death to the Arabs” before
being chased from the village of El-Jinya.

Other
graffiti refers to an unauthorized settlement outpost that Israeli
forces demolished in January, and to another outpost slated for
demolition in March.

Mazloum says the vandals were discovered early Sunday morning and fled to the nearby Israeli settlement of Talmon.

Some extremist Jews have damaged Palestinian property in retaliation for Israeli government action against settlers.

An Israeli military spokeswoman said the army was investigating.

SOURCE: The Associated Press
