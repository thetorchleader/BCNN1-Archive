Facebook Teams Up with Access Hollywood to Debut ‘Mentions Box’ for Celebrities During Emmys

August 25, 2014 Technology

Seth Meyers is hosting the Emmy Awards. he says he’s tasked Amy Poehler and Tina Fey with helping write his Emmy jokes. (PHOTO CREDIT: Rodolfo Martinez, NBC)

Facebook will debut a “Mentions Box” for celebrities during the 66th Primetime Emmy Awards on Monday.

Facebook has teamed up with “Access Hollywood” to hand celebrities a large tablet computer to connect with their fans on the giant social network.

Mashable says it’s similar to a Q&A station that Instagram had at the Golden Globes in January.
