WATCH: Christie says Obama Is Not Leading

September 28, 2011 National

New Jersey Gov. Chris Christie indicated once again Tuesday night he will not be entering the 2012 presidential race — while calling for President Barack Obama to stop being a bystander in the Oval Office and criticizing Congress for being “at war with itself.”

While his speech at the Ronald Reagan Presidential Library in Simi Valley, Calif., was widely watched for any hints about his 2012 presidential ambitions, the governor said he would not be putting his hand up for the GOP nomination during a question-and-answer section at the conclusion of his remarks.
Faced with the inevitable question of whether he will run next year, Christie at first made a joke of the matter.
“I have to tell you the truth. You folks are an incredible disappointment of an audience. The fact that it took till the second question shows you people are off your game,” he said, to laughs.
“That is not American exceptionalism,” he added, referencing the key theme of his speech.
He then directed the audience to the Politico website, to watch a video that shows one minute and 53 seconds of his numerous 2012 refusals. “Those are the answers,” he told the crowd.
When asked again later by a woman — who made an impassioned plea for him to run “for my daughter and our grandchildren” — Christie got serious, saying simply he does not feel it inside.
“I hear exactly what you’re saying and I feel the passion with which you say it and it touches me,” he said.
“But by the same token, that heartfelt message you gave me is also not a reason for me to do it. That reason also has to reside within me.
