Dexter King Puts Malibu Home Up for Sale

February 16, 2015 Black

Dexter Scott King, the second son of Martin Luther King Jr., has listed a Malibu house for sale at $4.85 million. (Marc Angeles / Unlimited Style Real Estate Photography)

Dexter Scott King, the second son of civil rights leader the Rev. Martin Luther King Jr. and Coretta Scott King, has listed his dramatic contemporary house in Malibu for sale at $4.85 million.

The ocean-view house sits behind gates on more than an acre of lushly landscaped grounds. Garden paths traverse the property, which includes mature palm trees and more than a dozen fruit trees.

A cabana, koi pond, waterfall spa and a swimming pool complete the grounds.

The 6,823 square feet of turn-key living space has a home theater, a gym, an office, an elevator, four bedrooms and six bathrooms.

A catwalk hallway connects living space on the upper level. Balconies expand the views on two levels.
