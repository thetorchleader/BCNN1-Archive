Oldest Son of Pastor A. R. and Karen Bernard Dies from Asthma Attack; Funeral Services Announced

February 9, 2015 Christian

A. R. and Karen Bernard

Our prayers go out for the Bernard family as they have lost their son, Alfonso Bernard, Jr. to an Asthma Attack, February 4, 2015. Continue to lift up his wife and children: Janel Bernard, Alfonson Bernard, III, Justin Bernard, Christian Bernard, and Karcyn Bernard.

A. R. Bernard posted:

Rev. A.R. and Pastor Karen Bernard and the Bernard Family announce with great sadness the loss of their beloved son and brother.
Alphonso Bernard, Jr. on February 4, 2015
Services will be held at Christian Cultural Center, Thursday, February 12, 2015.

Viewing: 4:00 PM to 6:30 PM. Service: 7:30 PM

In lieu of flowers please give to: “Alfonso R. Bernard Jr. Children’s Trust”.

Your gift can be brought or sent to:
Christian Cultural Center
12020 Flatlands Avenue
Brooklyn, NY 11207
