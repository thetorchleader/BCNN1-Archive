“Celebration of Gospel” to Return to BET In 2015

January 30, 2015 Entertainment

Hallelujah! BET’s break out special program, “Celebration of Gospel” will return to the network in January of 2016. Performances for the “15th Annual Celebration of Gospel” will taped on December 5, 2015 and premiere in January 2016.

The network included the program in an announcement of its acclaimed mega-specials for 2015, which begins with the airing of the “BET Honors” during Black History Month. One of the largest platforms for gospel artists, “Celebration of Gospel” features spirited performances by some of today’s hottest and most talented artists from the worlds of gospel and R&B.

In 2014 following its highest rated showing in 14 years, reports alleging the show’s cancellation were the talk of the industry.

This writer directly verified via a BET Executive that the show was never “cancelled,” but it was not included among the list of special programs at an annual upfront event as details were still in the works.

The confirmation of the return of one of the network’s most watched programs will be a welcome relief for lovers of gospel music everywhere.
