Houston Texans Linebackers Coach Mike Vrabel Robbed of Three Super Bowl Rings

November 8, 2014 Sports

© Greg Bartram-USA TODAY Sports Sep 21, 2013; Columbus, OH, USA; Ohio State Buckeyes defensive line coach Mike Vrabel directs his defense against the Florida A&M Rattlers at Ohio Stadium. Ohio State won the game 76-0.

Houston Texans linebackers coach Mike Vrabel had his three Super Bowl rings stolen after his Texas home was broken into, he tweeted on Saturday.

Vrabel tweeted that the thieves smashed in the back door and took the memorabilia.

Vrabel, whose storied playing career with the New England Patriots spanned eight seasons, played in the NFL for 14 years.

After retiring from the Kansas City Chiefs, Vrabel was named an assistant coach at Ohio State. This past year, Vrabel accepted the job with the Texans shortly after Bill O’Brien was named the head coach.

The Texans are on a bye this week.
