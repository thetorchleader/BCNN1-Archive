Florida Man Loses His Life While Trying to Stop His Friend from Driving Drunk

August 21, 2014 National

Joseph Ianzano, 19, died after trying to stop a friend from driving drunk but paid the ultimate price. (PHOTO CREDIT: Facebook)

A Florida man lost his life while trying to save his friend.

Joseph Ianzano, 19, was trying to persuade a pal not to drive home while intoxicated Saturday night, but his vigilance resulted in his death.

The Coconut Creek resident grabbed on to the rear of the car as the alleged drunk driver drove away. The vehicle then struck a tree, killing Ianzano, who suffered severe head trauma.

The case is under investigation and no charges have been filed, Coconut Creek Sgt. Henry Cabrera told the Daily News.

About 200 people attended a vigil in his memory Monday. Participants said they were not surprised Ianzano had acted so selflessly.
