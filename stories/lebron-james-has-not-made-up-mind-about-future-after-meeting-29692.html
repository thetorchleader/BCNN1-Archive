LeBron James Has Not Made Up Mind About Future After Meeting With Pat Riley

Date not recorded in the original archive Sports

If Pat Riley has his way, he will share many more celebrations with LeBron James.
Jesse D. Garrabrant/NBAE/Getty Images

LeBron James’ highly anticipated meeting with the Miami Heat in Las Vegas ended Wednesday afternoon, but his uncertain future remains.

According to a person with knowledge of his situation, James has not made up his mind about whether he will re-sign with the Heat or head elsewhere. He is expected to meet with his family to discuss his decision, though there is no announcement expected on Wednesday night.

The person spoke to USA TODAY Sports on the condition of anonymity because of the private nature of the talks.

The Cleveland Cavaliers are considered the most dangerous threat to the Heat’s hope of bringing James back. The meeting, which lasted more than an hour, included James, agent Rich Paul, Heat President Pat Riley and Heat assistant general manager Andy Elisburg.

The Cavaliers’ three-team trade that was agreed upon Wednesday gives them the necessary salary cap space to sign James, but two people with knowledge of the situation said they were attempting to add yet another enticement to his recruitment by trading for Minnesota Timberwolves forward Kevin Love.
