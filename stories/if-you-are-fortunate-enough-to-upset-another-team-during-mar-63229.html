If You Are Fortunate Enough to Upset Another Team During March Madness, Should the First Thing You Do be Cursing Out the President of the United States?

March 23, 2015 Sports

Anthony “Cat” Barber was huge in NC State’s win over No. 1 Villanova. President Obama had picked Villanova in his March Madness bracket. So Cat had one question for his teammates after the game: “the f*** wrong with Barack Obama?”

(I also picked Villanova. The f*** wrong with me?)

He’s not the first to criticize the President after an upset win this tourney … the other guy got worried the Secret Service was after him.

Good team win tonight and Yall know I loveeee Obama HONEST!!

— CAT (@catbarber1994) March 22, 2015

Got nothing but respect for @barackobama. Love our president! Caught up in a moment, Sweet 16!!!

— CAT (@catbarber1994) March 22, 2015
