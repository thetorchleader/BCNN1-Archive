Teen Mom Star Maci Bookout, Son Survive Car Accident

January 19, 2015 Entertainment

Maci Bookout

Teen Mom star Maci Bookout and her 6-year-old son Bentley had a close call this weekend after their car rolled three times during an accident on the highway.

“Thanking God that Bentley and I were able to walk away from this nightmare with only a few bruises,” she posted on Instagram along with photos of her overturned car.

And one more reason the reality star, 23, is grateful to walk away unharmed? Her unborn baby she’s expecting with boyfriend Taylor McKinney.

Maci thanked her Jeep for saving her life as she and Bentley were both wearing seat-belts at the time.

“Thanking God that my unborn baby girl is perfectly healthy after this,” she wrote.
