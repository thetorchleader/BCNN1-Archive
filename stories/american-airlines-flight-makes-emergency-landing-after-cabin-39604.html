American Airlines Flight Makes Emergency Landing After Cabin Begins Cracking Mid-flight

October 14, 2014 National

PHOTO CREDIT: Alamy

An American Airlines flight was forced to make an emergency landing after passengers discovered cracks inside its cabin, the Associated Press reports.

The flight, was traveling from San Francisco to Dallas on Monday night, was carrying 184 passengers at the time of the incident, according to the report.

The plane returned to San Francisco and made an emergency landing there. No passengers were injured during the incident.

A spokesman for American told the AP that the decision to make the emergency landing was made by the flight’s pilot.

“The captain elected to return to San Francisco and landed the plane safely,” American Airlines spokesman Matt Miller said according to the report.
