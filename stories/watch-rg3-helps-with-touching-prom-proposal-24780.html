WATCH: RG3 Helps with Touching Prom Proposal

June 1, 2014 Sports

Robert Griffin III helps girl ask classmate with cerebral palsy to prom

Seventeen-year-old Juwaan Espinal, who suffers from cerebral palsy, received an invitation to the prom with some help from his favorite football player. Morgan Assel, a friend of Espinal’s, surprised Espinal with a giant banner at a lacrosse game. It read: “I know I’m not as cool as RGIII, but will you go to prom with me?”

Hiding behind the banner was Assel with RGIII himself.

Espinal said yes, and then posed for a selfie with RGIII.

“What a blessing this kid is to his community and he’s a ladies man!” Griffin wrote on Instagram.
