Tickets Now on Sale for the 13th Annual Super Bowl Gospel Celebration

December 7, 2011 Uncategorized

One of the most anticipated events during Super Bowl Weekend with NFL Legends and Grammy Award-Winning Artists

Tickets are now on sale for the Super Bowl Gospel Celebration coming to Indianapolis Super Bowl Weekend, Friday, February 3, 2011 at 7:30 p.m. at Clowes Memorial Hall of Butler University. Celebrating 13 years of inspiration, the NFL sanctioned event transcends the traditional Super Bowl Weekend activity, bringing together top NFL players and Grammy Award-winning artists together on one stage, for one night, on one accord.
Tickets are available at Ticketmaster locations in Indianapolis, www.Ticketmaster.com, Clowes Memorial Hall or visit www.superbowlgospel.com.  Prices are $99, $79, $59, and $39, additional service fees and taxes are not included in the listed prices.
The star-studded line-up for the 2012 Super Bowl Gospel Celebration will be announced next month.
Each year the event boasts marquee players including Donovan McNabb, Ray Lewis, Cris Carter, John Elway, Darrell Green, and Coaches Tony Dungy and Lovie Smith, and music icons including Patti LaBelle, Gladys Knight, Kirk Franklin, CeCe Winans, Yolanda Adams, Jaci Velasquez, Donnie McClurkin, Hezekiah Walker and many others.
The event also serves as a fundraiser with a portion of the proceeds benefiting national and local charities. To date, more than $400,000 and 5,000 tickets have been donated.
