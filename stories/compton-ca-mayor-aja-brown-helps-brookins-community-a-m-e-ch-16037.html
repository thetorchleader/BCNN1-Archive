Compton, CA Mayor Aja Brown Helps Brookins Community A.M.E. Church Celebrate Women’s History Month and Youth Sunday

March 30, 2014 Uncategorized

Compton Mayor Aja Brown (center with plaque) received a joyous response from youth and adults after speaking at the Women’s History Month/Youth Sunday celebration at Brookins Community A.M.E. Church on March 16.

Mayor Brown’s powerful message was entitled ‘Obedience is Better Than Sacrifice’ based on John 15.

Pastor Gregory McLeod, who presented the Mayor with Brookins Award of Excellence, is joined by First Lady Savalla McLeod, Youth Director Pamela Curtis, Program Chair Dolores Bolton, L.A. City Attorney Mike Feuer and members of the Young People’s Division. (photo by Haywood Galbreath)

Source: LA Sentinel
