LISTEN: Terror attack at synagogue in Jerusalem leaves 4 dead, 8 wounded (Second Coming Watch Update #534 with Daniel Whyte III)

November 18, 2014 Christian, Second Coming Watch

Daniel Whyte III

[ca_audio url_mp3=”https://www.buzzsprout.com/4994/222086-terror-attack-at-synagogue-in-jerusalem-leaves-4-dead-8-wounded-second-coming-watch-update-534.mp3&#8243; url_ogg=”” download=”true” align=”none”] (Download/Listen to MP3)

Let’s take a quick look at today’s prophecy-related headlines which point towards the second coming of the Lord Jesus Christ and the end of the world as we know it.

First, today, under the Sign category of THE TURMOIL IN ISRAEL & THE MIDDLE EAST — According to The Guardian, four Israelis were killed and eight more wounded in a frenzied assault by two Palestinian men on Jewish worshippers praying at a Jerusalem synagogue in the most lethal incident in the city in years. The two assailants who launched their attack with meat cleavers and a gun during early morning prayers were then killed by police officers in the ensuing gun battle at the scene of the attack. Three of the victims held dual US-Israeli citizenship, and one was a British-Israeli citizen

Second, today, under the Sign category of WARS & RUMORS OF WARS– According to the Daily Mail, the latest horrifying video of the mass murder of 16 Syrian soldiers and an American aid worker is very different from other Islamic State propaganda films. Posted on Sunday morning, it shows how the jihadists are seeking to heighten the disgust they provoke. Security and military analysts believe this new tactic is a plot to lure Western and other forces into a full-scale ground offensive which ISIS leaders believe they can win.

Third, today, under the Sign category of DISTRESS AMONG NATIONS — According to Agence France-Presse (AFP), the US, Australian and Japanese leaders on Sunday called for peaceful resolutions of maritime disputes, a day after Barack Obama warned of the dangers of outright conflict in Asia as China contests disputed territory. In a joint statement Obama, Tony Abbott and Shinzo Abe urged “freedom of navigation and over-flight, and the peaceful resolution of maritime disputes in accordance with international law.” The trio said they were committed to deepening their already strong security cooperation in the Asia-Pacific, which comes amid China’s increasingly assertive expansion in the region.

Fourth, today, under the Sign category of WARS & RUMORS OF WARS — According to Agence France-Presse (AFP), North Korea on Saturday warned of retaliatory strikes days after South Korea fired warning shots at a North Korean patrol near the border. South Korean troops fired warning shots as a Northern patrol approached the military demarcation line that designates the border, but there was no exchange of fire and the group eventually turned back. North Korea issued a statement that said “such reckless military provocations in the area may invite unforeseeable retaliation strikes.”

Fifth, today, under the Sign category of INCREASED PERSECUTION — According to Christian Today, as China becomes more politically conservative, some churches are facing greater pressure to fall in line with the Communist party.

Speaking anonymously, a spokesperson for Christian Solidarity Worldwide said that there has been a “knock on effect” for Christians following the introduction of a hard-line approach to various parts of civil society by the Chinese president. While it remains unclear as to who has commanded churches to have their crosses removed and demolished, many Christians trace it back to comments made by a provincial party secretary who said the crosses were too shiny and too visible on the skyline. It is not yet known what will happen in the future, but it seems likely that both registered and independent churches in China will come under increasing pressure.

The prophetic passage of Scripture we are looking at today is Genesis 17:20 which reads: “And as for Ishmael, I have heard thee: Behold, I have blessed him, and will make him fruitful, and will multiply him exceedingly; twelve princes shall he beget, and I will make him a great nation.”

Allow me to share with you some commentary on this passage from the Popular Bible Prophecy Commentary, edited by Tim LaHaye and Ed Hindson.

The second promise concerning Ishmael is found in Genesis 17:20. While Abraham receives divine instruction regarding circumcision, the sign of the covenant, along with an encouraging reminder of his wife’s prophesied pregnancy with Isaac, Abraham expresses concern that Ishmael not be divinely neglected. God responds that He has heard Abraham’s concern that “God hears” (Ishmael) not be overlooked. As Abraham’s son, Ishmael would also experience divine blessing and would become the “father of twelve princes,” who together would flourish into a “great nation” of innumerable descendants.

The third promise concerning Ishmael is found in Genesis 21:18. It is a reconfirmation to Hagar, discouraged after having been sent away, of God’s promise, previously made to Abraham, that Ishmael would flourish into a great nation. Indeed, Ishmael’s 12 sons are listed in 1 Chronicles 1:28-31.

The apostle Peter later uses Genesis’ account of Ishmael’s birth as typological of the Torah, in contrast to the typological use of Isaac, the son of promise, in reference to the believer’s freedom in the Messiah.

If the Lord tarries His coming and we live, we will continue looking at this prophecy in our next broadcast / podcast.

Our second coming quote for today is from Chrysostom. He said, “In the first advent God veiled his divinity to prove the faithful; in the second advent he will manifest his glory to reward their faith.”

John 3:16 says, “For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.” Just believe in your heart that Jesus Christ died for your sins, was buried, and rose from the dead by the power of God for you so that you can live eternally with Him. Pray and ask Him to come into your heart today, and He will. Romans 10:13 says, “For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.”

Keep looking up for your redemption draweth nigh. Let us join in the prayer of John the Revelator — “Even so, come, Lord Jesus.” God bless you.
