Financial Issues Force Upscale Harlem Barber Shop to Close

May 17, 2014 Finances

PHOTO/JEREMY DANIEL
Brenda Braxton, owner of BBRAXTON, inside of her upscale men’s barber shop and salon in Harlem. The grooming salon was closed on April 30, but she vows to bring it back.

Tony-nominated Broadway veteran Brenda Braxton, who has had roles in “Chicago,” “Smokey Joe’s Cafe” and “Dreamgirls,” closed her upscale men’s pampering palace on April 30.

Exit stage left.

Broadway veteran Brenda Braxton’s highfalutin Harlem men’s pampering palace has closed.

BBRAXTON, a barber shop that offered massages, manicures and cocktails along with a sharp haircut, shuttered on April 30 as it was unable to catch up on back rent and bills, owner Brenda Braxton told the Daily News Monday.

“I think I was a little bit before my time,” Braxton said. “Harlem didn’t show me the love that I thought I was going to get. People were not ready for this upscale barber shop.”

She declined to say how much she owed her landlord, Walter Edwards of Full Spectrum.

“I couldn’t catch up from previous years,” said the Tony-nominated actress, who has had roles in “Chicago,” “Smokey Joe’s Cafe” and “Dreamgirls.”
