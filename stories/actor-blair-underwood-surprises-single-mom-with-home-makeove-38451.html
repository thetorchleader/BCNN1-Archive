Actor Blair Underwood Surprises Single Mom With Home Makeover

October 9, 2014 Entertainment

Blair Underwood speaks during a screening of ‘The Trip To Bountiful’ at the Eisenhower Executive Office Building on February 24, 2014, in Washington, DC. (Photo by Kris Connor/Getty Images)

The Oprah Winfrey Network is presenting a new season of their Emmy-nominated DIY home makeover show, Home Made Simple, hosted by Soleil Moon Frye (former Punky Brewster child star).

This season, Home Made Simple boasts some amazing celebrity appearances, including actor Blair Underwood, singer Josh Groban, The Chew host Carla Hall, Shanice from OWN’s Flex and Shanice and renowned chef Ben Ford.

The show is a great example of celebrities working together with a common goal: to help transform the homes of deserving families and the lives of viewers. After the show, viewers can visit the show’s website, http://www.homemadesimpletv, to get step-by-step instructions on how to do the projects in their own homes.

In an upcoming episode, Underwood surprises and honors a hard-working single mom, who recently earned her Masters degree and inspired her daughter to attend college, with a new dual-purpose art room and guest room space to both fulfill her passion for painting and provide a welcoming bedroom for her daughter to visit.
