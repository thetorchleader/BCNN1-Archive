Second Baptist Church in Los Angeles Celebrates 130th Anniversary

May 17, 2015 Black

Pastor William S. Epps

Second Baptist Church (SBC) celebrates its 130th anniversary on Sunday, May 17, at 8 a.m. and 11:15 a.m., in its edifice at 2412 Griffith Avenue in Los Angeles. 

The Rev. Dr. William S. Epps, senior pastor, said that the guest preacher for both services will be Dr. Wallace Charles Smith, pastor of the historic Shiloh Baptist Church, one of the oldest African American churches in Washington, DC.

SBC has a long, storied history.  The church was the west coast home to Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr., where he gave one of his last sermons. Renowned leaders such as W.E.B. Dubois, Roy Wilkins, Paul Robeson, Adam Clayton Powell, Ralph Bunch, Johnnie Cochran Jr., Congresswoman Juanita Millender-McDonald, as well as many notable people have spoke at SBC.
