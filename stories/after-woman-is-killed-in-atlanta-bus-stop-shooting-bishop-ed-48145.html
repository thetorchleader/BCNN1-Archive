After Woman Is Killed In Atlanta Bus Stop Shooting, Bishop Eddie Long Leads Church to Pray for End to Community Violence

December 4, 2014 Christian

Bishop Eddie Long

According to the book of Genesis, it took the Lord six days to create everything under the heavens and on the Earth. On the seventh day, the book says, he rested.

It has been seven days since the fatal shooting of Marcaysia Dawkins, 19, and a local church congregation is not resting, because all is not good.

Bishop Eddie Long leads the New Birth Missionary Baptist Church in DeKalb County. He and his flock walked from their church to the scene of Dawkins murder, Sunday. There, they prayed for an end to violence in their community.

“It’s time to sit down, it’s time to pray together, come together, and stop the violence,” said Long.

Surrounded by supporters, Dawkins’ step-father Richard Gallon addressed the crowd and held nothing back.

“We tired of this [expletive omitted]. Lord forgive me. We tired of it,” said Gallon. “Just think about your mamma, your daughter, your sister, or anybody you ever known that walked to a bus stop. It could have been them.”

In some cases it has been; victims of a different crime at a different place and time. Shameika Brown was in attendance at the gathering. CBS46 talked to Brown nearly three weeks ago about being robbed at gunpoint as well, just outside her condominium.

“When he put me down execution style he took my wallet; my cellphone; my car keys at the time,” said Brown.
