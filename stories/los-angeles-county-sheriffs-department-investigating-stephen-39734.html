Los Angeles County Sheriff’s Department Investigating Stephen Collins on Molestation Charges

October 15, 2014 Entertainment

Actor Stephen Collins arrives at the Family Television Awards in Beverly Hills, Calif., on Wednesday, Nov. 29, 2006.
(Photo: Matt Sayles, AP)

A new investigation is being launched into the Stephen Collins molestation case, reports the Los Angeles Times and TMZ.

The Los Angeles County Sheriff’s Department is involved, meaning that three separate law enforcement units are now on the trail.

The Sheriff’s involvement has likely happened because some part of the original incident(s) fell within the department’s geographical jurisdiction.

Sheriff’s Capt. Shaun Mathers told the Times the department is working with detectives from New York, who have been looking into allegations Collins had sexual contact with at least two minors there.

The Daily News reported last week that NYPD’s Special Victims Unit was investigating allegations that Collins molested one girl in 1972 and exposed himself to two others, after Collins’ audio tape confession was made public by TMZ.
