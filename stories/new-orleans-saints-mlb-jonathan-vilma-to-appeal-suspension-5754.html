New Orleans Saints MLB Jonathan Vilma to Appeal Suspension

May 7, 2012 Uncategorized

Let the legal games begin.
Saints MLB Jonathan Vilma officially appealed his year-long suspension Monday, five days after NFL Commissioner Roger Goodell issued the penalty for Vilma’s involvement as a purported ringleader of New Orleans’ infamous bounty program.
“To be able to share, discuss and analyze the supposed evidence that has been gathered is a fundamental cornerstone of a fair and just process, and a vital prerequisite to uncovering the truth,” Vilma’s lawyer wrote in a letter to Goodell before providing a checklist of 17 items he demands the league provide in order to substantiate Vilma’s guilt and level of involvement.
“The failure of the NFL to conduct itself in a just manner has compromised the process and resulted in erroneous and damaging conclusions.”
