Mandisa and TobyMac to Perform Live on Good Morning America, Monday August 27th

August 26, 2012 Uncategorized

Two-time Grammy nominee  Mandisa is set to perform her hit song “Good Morning” featuring TobyMac LIVE on Good Morning America this coming  Monday, August 27th at 8:30 am, est.
Mandisa first hit the public seen as a contestant on American Idol. She is now well known for her Christian Contemporary songs “Stronger” and “Waiting for Tomorrow.” 
TobyMac, a Grammy award winning artist, is  well known for his songs “Made to Love” and his new single, “Me Without You” from his upcoming album, Eye On It, to be released this Tuesday, August 28th.
This is not the first time the two artists have collaborated. Mandisa was featured on TobyMac’s hit “Lose Your Soul”. The two have created another hit with Mandisa’s third single “Good Morning” from her latest album What If We Were Real. Both artists have expressed their excitement about their scheduled appearance on Good Morning America this coming Monday.
