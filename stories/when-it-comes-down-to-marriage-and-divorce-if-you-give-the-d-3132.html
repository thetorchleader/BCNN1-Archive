When It Comes Down to Marriage and Divorce, If You Give the Devil an Inch, He’ll Take a Mile – Look What Is Happening to Christian Couple, Deion and Pilar Sanders

January 19, 2012 Uncategorized

Deion and Pilar Sanders’ give and take was fodder for their reality show called “Deion and Pilar: Prime Time Love.” But after Deion’s divorce filing, Pilar filed a response this week that reads like script. 
In court documents, Pilar calls the Hall of Fame cornerback a narcissistic bully who cheated on her several times with several people.
She accuses him of unkind, uncaring, insensitive, cruel and unusual treatment, as well as physical, mental and emotional abuse of her and their three children.
But it doesn’t stop there. Pilar requests to punish Deion for immoral, corrupt, lewd, perverted, unnatural, sinful conduct that is “at the bottom of the depths of humanity.” And says that he needs to be punished “to protect every person on the planet.”
The allegations paint a completely different picture than the one portrayed for fans. And they put the couple’s $21 million ranch in Prosper as well as other assets in the crosshairs.
Pilar says the judge should throw out their prenuptial agreement and give her most of Deion’s money because she signed the deal under duress.
Even local celebrity divorce attorney Brad LaMorgese said he was taken aback by some of the tough language.
