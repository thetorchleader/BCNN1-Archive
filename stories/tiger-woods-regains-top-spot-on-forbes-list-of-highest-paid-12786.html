Tiger Woods Regains Top Spot on Forbes’ List of Highest-Paid Athletes

June 6, 2013 Uncategorized

According to Forbes, Tiger Woods once again reigns atop the list of the world’s highest-paid athletes. It’s a position he has occupied every year since 2001 except for last year, 2012, when Floyd Mayweather Jr. took the spot.
According to the article, “Woods pulled in $78.1 million over the last year from prize money, endorsements, appearance fees and golf course design work.”
Woods earned $10 million from appearance fees — most of which came overseas.
Not a bad chunk of change for a guy whom many thought would never recover from his scandalous ways just four years ago.
Woods’ $78.1 million topped Roger Federer ($71.5 million), Kobe Bryant ($61.9 million), and LeBron James ($59.8 million). Now that is an elite group of sportsmen if I’ve ever seen one.
The article is mostly about how Woods has bounced back since 2009, but I also found this tidbit from Forbes really intriguing:
“Revenues at Nike Golf rose 10% last year to $726 million after three straight years of declines.”
