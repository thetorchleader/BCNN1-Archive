WATCH: Dog Tries to Teach Baby How to Crawl

Date not recorded in the original archive Lifestyle

Courtesy: Valerie Stevens-Scott

In a heartwarming viral video, ‘Buddy’s Baby Crawling School’, Buddy, the dog, teaches Bear Bear how to crawl.


He is comfortably resting on a rug when he notices Bear Bear, the little baby girl, struggling to move forward. 

She desperately flaps her arms and flutter kicks her feet, like she’s trying her hand at on-spot swimming, but doesn’t move ahead. On seeing this, Buddy first contemplates pushing her from the back, but then decides to teach the little one the right technique to crawl.

He drags himself across the floor, showing Bear Bear how it is done, she looks at him in astonishment. Hopefully, she’ll learn soon.
