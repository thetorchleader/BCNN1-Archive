SAD: 17-Year-Old High School Graduate, Cheyanne Bond, Shot and Killed in New Jersey

Date not recorded in the original archive Education

Cheyanne Bond
YOUTUBE

Cheyanne Bond, 17, was killed over the weekend while out for a walk with her ex-boyfriend.

A Newark high school cheerleader was shot and killed two weeks after her graduation, and New Jersey police are searching for any witnesses, CBS New York reports.

“They told her to get on her knees and they shot her in the head execution style,” Michael Bond, the uncle of 17-year-old Cheyanne Bond, told the news station.

Cheyanne, who had just graduated from Malcolm X Shabazz High School in Newark, was walking with her ex-boyfriend Sunday night along the city’s West Side Park when both of them were shot, the news site notes. The ex-boyfriend survived.

“Just happened to be at the wrong place at the wrong time,” Michael Bond said.

“She just graduated; she was about to go to college,” a friend, Latayjsia Wilson, told CBS New York. “She was a real nice girl; that’s why I never thought it would be her.”
