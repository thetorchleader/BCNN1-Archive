3 Former Atlanta Educators Who Received the Stiffest Sentences in Test Cheating Scandal to Be Resentenced

April 22, 2015 Black, Education

Tamara Cotman, Michael Pitts, and Sharon Davis Williams photo

A judge plans to resentence three former Atlanta public school educators who received the stiffest sentences after they were convicted in a conspiracy to inflate student scores on standardized tests.

The case manager for Judge Jerry Baxter of Fulton County Superior Court said Tuesday that the educators, Tamara Cotman, Sharon Davis-Williams and Michael Pitts, will be resentenced April 30.

The three former district regional directors were the highest-ranking of the 11 ex-educators convicted of racketeering this month. Judge Baxter said he considered that, along with their refusal to accept deals offered by prosecutors, when he sentenced them last week.

Their sentences of seven years in prison followed by 13 years on probation are more than double what prosecutors recommended.

Source: The AP
