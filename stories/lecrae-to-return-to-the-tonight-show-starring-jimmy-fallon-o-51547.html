Lecrae to Return to ‘The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon’ on January 9

January 8, 2015 Entertainment

REACH RECORDS

Lecrae will be back on “The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon.”

After his seventh studio album Anomaly topped the Billboard 200, selling 88,000 records in its first week, “The Tonight Show” invited him to join its house band, The Roots, on Sept. 18. On Friday, Jan. 9, the Reach Records rapper will return “for a full performance,” as the ad that Reach art director Alex Medina shared on Wednesday reads.

DJ Promote, who DJ’d for Lecrae and Andy Mineo on The Anomaly Tour, will join Lecrae in his performance, according to DJ Promote. To watch Rapzilla’s mini documentary on DJ Promote, click here.
