Bill Cosby Accuser says She Was Drugged and Raped during ‘Cosby Show’ Role

May 1, 2015 Black, Entertainment

© The Associated Press Comedian Bill Cosby performs at the Buell Theater in Denver, Saturday, Jan. 17, 2015. Cosby, 77, is facing sexual assault accusations from at least 15 women, with some of the claims dating back decades.

Two more women plan to come forward with accusations that they were sexually assaulted by Bill Cosby — joining dozens of other alleged victims a day before the final show of the comedian’s U.S. standup tour. 

The women are scheduled to appear at a press conference in New York on Friday afternoon with lawyer Gloria Allred.

One, an actress who appeared in an episode during the last season of “The Cosby Show,” alleges she was drugged, raped and threatened by Cosby while he helped her prepare for the role.

More than 30 other women have made similar allegations against the comedian in recent months.

Cosby’s wife, Camille, and representatives have denied the allegations and questioned the credibility of some accusers.
