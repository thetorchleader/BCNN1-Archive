WATCH: ‘All Hell’ Breaks Loose In Season 4 of Mary Mary

February 17, 2015 Entertainment

Credit: WE tv

Believers in Jesus Christ are able to get through traumatic times drama-free, right? Wrong.

The official Season 4 trailer of WE tv’s Mary Mary shows what Erica Campbell calls “all hell” breaking loose.

Once Tina Campbell, 40, discovered her husband Teddy Campbell’s cheating ways, she momentarily lost it.

The fury she felt internally manifested externally with busted windshields and thoughts of murder, and suicide.

“Go crash the car, stab him, kill yourself,” were some of the thoughts Tina was having she said. “All this crazy stuff was coming to my head.”

Some of it, she acted on.

As tensions between the sisters mounted, Tina reunited with controversial manager Mitchell Solarek—shocking and upsetting Erica and her husband-manager Warryn Campbell.
