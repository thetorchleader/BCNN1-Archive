Luke Zamperini to Share Inside Faith Story Behind “UNBROKEN” Movie In March 19th Tele-Town Hall

March 18, 2015 Entertainment

Angelina Jolie with Louis Zamperini. Photo courtesy of Universal

Hosted by Faith Driven Consumer™ Founder Chris Stone, the Tele-Town Hall celebrates the upcoming Special Edition UNBROKEN DVD, which includes a 90-minute LEGACY OF FAITH bonus disc

Universal Pictures Home Entertainment is announcing a national Tele-Town Hall-to be held on Thursday, March 19th at 2pm ET/11am PT-featuring Louie Zamperini’s son, Luke Zamperini. Louie’s life and testimony inspired the best-selling book and Oscar-nominated film UNBROKEN, which comes to Blu-Ray and DVD March 24th with a special LEGACY OF FAITH bonus disc available on the DVD. Faith Driven Consumer Founder Chris Stone, a Certified Brand Strategist, will host the Tele-Town Hall. Widely recognized as the leading national expert on Faith Driven Consumers-a key audience behind the incredible box office success of UNBROKEN-Stone will facilitate questions from faith leaders across the country.

Details on the upcoming Tele-Town Hall:

WHAT: Tele-Town Hall on Special Edition UNBROKEN DVD with LEGACY OF FAITH Bonus Disc

WHEN: Thursday, March 19th @ 2pm ET / 11am PT

SPECIAL GUEST: Luke Zamperini, son of Louie Zamperini

HOW TO JOIN: Register by calling 800-248-3055 or by visiting http://www.unbrokenresources.org/luke-zamperini-tele-townhall/. On Thursday, call 888-410-3427 to join.

Universal recently released exclusive new footage from its upcoming Special Edition DVD, which includes the feature, film UNBROKEN and the LEGACY OF FAITH bonus disc which contains more than 90 minutes of extra features with an introduction by Greg Laurie. The bonus disc highlights interviews and documentary footage of the real-life Louie Zamperini, along with Billy Graham, Greg Laurie and many others. Highlights include: CBN’s “Coming Full Circle,” Greg Laurie’s Harvest Crusade Interview and the Billy Graham Evangelistic Association’s “Captured By Grace” documentary.

Adapted from author Laura Hillenbrand’s enormously popular book, UNBROKEN tells the inspiring true story about the resilient power of the human spirit. Olympian and war hero Zamperini survived on a raft for 47 days along with two other crewmen after a near-fatal plane crash in WWII-only to be caught by the Japanese navy and sent to a series of prisoner-of-war camps.

Faith Organizations Praise UNBROKEN:

Plugged In – “UNBROKEN is hard to watch but easy to praise. The horror of the inhumanity it depicts is wrenching. The triumph of one man’s spirit and heart is both astounding and deeply inspiring.”

Faith Driven Consumer – “UNBROKEN is an inspiring faith-friendly film that will solidly resonate with Faith Driven Consumer audiences. This movie positively presents core biblical concepts such as hard work, overcoming adversity, forgiveness, love, Christ-like self-sacrifice, and God’s sovereign hand in our lives.”

World Net Daily – “That such a film came out of Hollywood is exactly why I’m optimistic about what can happen when the light of God shines in the place where ‘light’ is followed by ‘camera’ and ‘action’!”

CBN – “UNBROKEN is…a real-life reminder of the healing power of forgiveness and faith. This film is fifty years in the making-and worth the wait!”

Movieguide – “UNBROKEN extols fighting for liberty amid injustice, refusing to compromise one’s values, perseverance, holding onto hope when there seems to be no hope, committing yourself to God, prayer, and forgiving those who persecute you because Christ first forgave you.”
