Kareem Abdul-Jabbar Hospitalized Again with Chest Pain

April 29, 2015 Sports

Kareem Abdul-Jabbar attends the Shooting Stars competition during the 2014 NBA All-Star weekend in February. (Ronald Martinez / Getty Images)

Kareem Abdul-Jabbar was hospitalized again Wednesday morning with chest pain two weeks after undergoing quadruple bypass surgery April 16.

The 68-year-old basketball Hall of Famer was taken to the hospital following a 911 call,according to TMZ Sports.

Only two hours before the 911 call, Adbul-Jabbar tweeted that he’s “doing fine & feeling better everyday.”

Six hours later, a tweet was sent from Abdul-Jabbar’s account to promote The Gift Back Project, but it’s not known if Abdulul-Jabbar himself sent the tweet. His condition has not yet been updated.
