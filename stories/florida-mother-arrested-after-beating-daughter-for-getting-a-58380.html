Florida Mother Arrested After Beating Daughter for Getting All F’s on Report Card

February 24, 2015 Black

Melany Joyce Alexander
WFTS TAMPA BAY SCREENSHOT

A Tampa Bay, Florida woman was looking for a way to turn her daughter’s grades around but authorities believe she took it to far when she allegedly beat her child with a leather belt and then crafted a t-shirt explaining her failing grades and threatening kids to leave her alone or she would “get another good woopin.”

Melany Joyce Alexander, 30, was placed under arrest Friday and charged with child abuse after staff at West Hernando Middle School called police when Alexander’s daughter arrived at school wearing a white t-shirt with the words, “I currently have all F’s in all of my classes. I am not aloud [sic] to have a boyfriend no time soon so back off before I another good woopin like I did last night,” according to WFTS Tampa Bay.

According to the affidavit viewed by the news station, Alexander reportedly hit her daughter with a ” leather belt with holes with metal divots, which left clear impressions in several places to include both arms, neck, chest, back and legs” which after having viewed the marks, authorities concluded were excessive.
