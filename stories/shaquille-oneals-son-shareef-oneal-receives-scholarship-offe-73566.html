Shaquille O’Neal’s Son, Shareef O’Neal, Receives Scholarship Offer From University of Southern California

May 14, 2015 Sports

Shareef O’Neal appears to be following in the giant footsteps of his father.

The world got a taste of just how good Shaquille O’Neal’s son is when Hoop Mixtape released a minute-long video earlier this week of him showcasing his skills. It turns out that the Shareef, who averaged 3.7 points per game as a freshman on Windward High School’s varsity squad in 2014-15, may attend the University of Southern California once his time comes.

I am blessed receive an offer from the University of Southern California #USC #blessed pic.twitter.com/XqthUGAD4r
— reef (@rfkta) May 14, 2015

Shaq spent three years at Louisiana State University, where he averaged a whopping 21.6 points, 13.5 rebounds and 4.6 blocks per contest. He parlayed that into the No. 1 pick in the 1992 NBA Draft, and we all know what happened after that.

While Shareef has a long way to go before he comes close to filling his father’s shoes, a scholarship offer from a Division 1 program is a step in the right direction.
