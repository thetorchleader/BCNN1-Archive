Roma Downey to Host and Mark Burnett to Produce New Miracles and Answered Prayers Series on TLC

October 23, 2014 Entertainment

TLC is teaming up with Mark Burnett and Roma Downey for a new six-part docu-series about miracles and answered prayers. Downey will host the specials while husband Burnett serves as executive producer.

“Mark and I feel passionate about providing projects that show people’s lives in positive and uplifting ways,” said Downey. “This is a wonderful opportunity to work with TLC and host a series that will inspire and encourage viewers to remember that even in the worst circumstances, prayer can lead to divine intervention.”

The untitled project is currently looking for individuals willing to share their stories at Lightworkers.com, which is the production company helmed by Downey and Burnett, and is controlled by MGM Holdings through United Artists Media Group.
