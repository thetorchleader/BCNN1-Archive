Some People Think that the Woman on Paula Deen’s Website Looks ‘Mammy-ish’

October 24, 2014 Black

THE LADY & SON

The longtime ‘friend’ and employee of the Deen family has been mistaken for a Mammy model. 

Paula Deen is back in the controversial spotlight and it’s because of an image featured on her website for Lady & Sons, a restaurant in Savannah, Ga. A Facebook group called For Black Women Only, found an image on the site that they deemed to be modeled off the image of a mammy.

“Paula Deen longs for the antebellum south when Blacks, especially black women were non-threatening servants.” The group also recommended that people should “kill the mammy within” and to stop associating with Deen.

According to Eater.com, the restaurant released a statement response to the claims that the photo is racist. The restaurant stated that the photo isn’t new, and has been on the site for five years. “The lady featured in the picture has worked at the restaurant for over 17 years and is like family to the Deens.”

If you recall, Deen said she had many black people who were like family to her during her deposition when she was sued for discrimination, but she still have no problem in calling them the ‘n’ word.
