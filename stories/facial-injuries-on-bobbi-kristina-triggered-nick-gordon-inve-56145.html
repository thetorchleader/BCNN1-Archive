Facial Injuries on Bobbi Kristina Triggered Nick Gordon Investigation

February 10, 2015 Entertainment

Bobbi Kristina Brown and Nick Gordon arrive at Tri-Star Pictures’ Sparkle premiere at Grauman’s Chinese Theatre Aug. 16, 2012, in Hollywood, Calif.
FRAZER HARRISON/GETTY IMAGES

An investigation into whether Bobbi Kristina Brown’s medical crisis was caused by foul play committed by Nick Gordon was triggered by facial injuries discovered by doctors, according to a new report.

As Gossip Cop reported, Brown has been in a medically-induced coma and breathing with the help of a ventilator since being found unconscious in her bathtub more than a week ago. Gordon and their friend Max Lomas are said to have performed CPR until paramedics arrived. Drugs were reportedly found at the couple’s Roswell, Georgia house, but it has not been said whether any illegal substances contributed to Brown’s condition.

Police told Gossip Cop there was an “open investigation” into what exactly happened, after which came word that Gordon was being targeted for possible foul play due to marks found on Brown’s body and an alleged history of domestic violence between them.

Now WSB-TV Action News in Atlanta cites sources as claiming those marks were found on Brown’s face, particularly around her mouth. The news comes at the same time well-wishers are gathering in Atlanta for a public vigil in support of Brown’s recovery. Gordon, who has not spoken publicly since the incident, has reportedly hired a lawyer.

SOURCE: Gossip Cop
