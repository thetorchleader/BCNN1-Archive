The Last Thing We Need is a ‘Basketball Wives’ Movie

May 3, 2012 Uncategorized

So, you can’t get enough of the weave-pulling, table-jumping, backstabbing antics of “Basketball Wives”? Never fear, the ever resourceful Shaunie O’Neal has teamed with another beautiful mogul, Tracey Edmonds, to bring the scream-fest to the big screen, reports Variety.

Fox Searchlight acquired the as yet untitled pitch from the “Soul Food” and “Jumping the Broom” producer which will be written by Elizabeth Warren for Edmonds’ Our Stories Films.
Based loosely on O’Neal’s life with NBA legend, Shaquille O’Neal, the film will revolve around a wife who moves to Miami with her newly signed NBA husband. There she will meet interesting friends who are also “about that life” and we will get to see how they deal with fame, jealousy and each other.
