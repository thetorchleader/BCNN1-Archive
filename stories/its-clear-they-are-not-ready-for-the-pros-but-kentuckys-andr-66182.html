It’s Clear They Are Not Ready for the Pros, but Kentucky’s Andrew and Aaron Harrison Reportedly Set to Declare for NBA Draft

April 9, 2015 Sports

After helping to lead the Kentucky Wildcats to back-to-back Final Four appearances, Andrew and Aaron Harrison have decided to declare for the 2015 NBA draft.  

Gary Parrish of CBS Sports had the report Wednesday:

No surprise. But a source told @CBSSports that Kentucky’s Harrison twins have decided to enter the NBA Draft. Story:http://t.co/85blTiueVD

— Gary Parrish (@GaryParrishCBS) April 8, 2015

 

Per Parrish’s report, Willie Cauley-Stein, Karl-Anthony Towns, Trey Lyles, Devin Booker and Dakari Johnson could be the next players to declare for the draft and are strongly considering doing just that, with Johnson the player most likely to return to Kentucky.

There’s not going to be any brainwashing, forcing, pushing either in or out,” Kentucky head coach John Calipari told reporters, via ESPN. “I want each kid to make a decision for themselves.”

It would appear the Harrisons have done just that.

The Harrison twins were a vital part of Kentucky’s last two runs through the NCAA tournament. Andrew averaged 9.3 points and 3.6 assists in 25.5 minutes per game in 2014-15, while Aaron put up 11.0 points and 2.6 rebounds in 25.8 minutes per contest.
