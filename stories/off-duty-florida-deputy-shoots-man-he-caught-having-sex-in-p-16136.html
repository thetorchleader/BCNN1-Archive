Off Duty Florida Deputy Shoots Man He Caught Having Sex In Pool

March 31, 2014 What is this World Coming To?

Officer-involved shooting in Boca Raton with off-duty officer (WPTV)

A man is in critical condition at Delray Medical Center after a deputy-involved shooting in suburban Boca Raton.

An off-duty deputy, who lives in Reflections of Boca Del Mar, entered the pool area of the complex and says he encountered a male and female engaged in sexual activity.

The off-duty deputy advised the couple that he is a deputy and asked the couple, who does not live in the area, to leave the premises.

They did so reluctantly, according to the Palm Beach County Sheriff’s Office.

A short time later the couple returned.

PBSO said the male suspect, later identified as Tony Ribeiro, 49, approached the off-duty deputy, who was sitting in a lounge chair, and hovered over him in a threatening manner.

An altercation ensued and Ribeiro raised a glass wine or champagne bottle and attempted to strike the off duty deputy, officials say.

The deputy fired his weapon, striking Ribeiro.
