Bishop Lyle and Pastor Deborah Dukes Are Trusting God to Give Their Church This Building In Two Years

March 10, 2014 Christian

Building Completion Stewardship Campaign

The Harvest Life Changers Church Building Completion Stewardship Campaign is a two-year program designed to fill the gap of critical funding needed for the 24-month construction period of our new worship center located on Jefferson Davis Highway in Woodbridge, Virginia (near the Northern Virginia Community College campus, off Interstate 95, Exit 156A).

This campaign represents, not only the promised occupancy of our new facility, but a vital step into a greater level of ministry to people and the testimony of God’s miracle for the Body of Christ.

What some thought was impossible is being made possible as we come together to build the Kingdom of God. Stewardship giving, which is outside of regular tithing and offerings, affords us the opportunity to worship through sacrificial giving while supporting the plan to bring the vision of our new worship center into reality.

The new state-of-the-art, 204,000 square foot building features a 4000-seat sanctuary, large parking lots, a chapel, a gymnasium, a food court, a children’s area, classrooms, offices and so much more. New ministry programs are planned to be released in the new building. Glory to God!

We believe that the magnitude of this vision stretches far beyond what our natural eyes can see. Our new edifice will house ministry programs that will transform the lives of individuals, families, marriages, children, and serve as headquarters for our various community and world-wide outreaches.

It is our prayer that you would support our Building Completion Stewardship initiative.
