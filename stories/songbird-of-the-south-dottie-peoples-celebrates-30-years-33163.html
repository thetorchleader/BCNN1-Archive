“Songbird of the South” Dottie Peoples Celebrates 30 Years

August 9, 2014 Entertainment

Dottie Peoples

Dottie Peoples, the Songbird of the South is celebrating 30 years as a recording artist. With recent appearances at the Gospel Music Workshop of America and concert dates from coast to coast, the Stellar award winner continues to bring joy to audiences with her music ministry. “I’m excited to share my new music with all of my fans,” said Peoples. “There is nothing more important to me than ministry and being able to see the power of music impact and inspire people. It is my prayer that this new song will encourage people.”

Peoples is excited about the release of Everything Must Change, the third single from her I Got This – LIVE recording. In addition to touring, Dottie delivers great gospel music via Dottie’s Praise House her syndicated radio show on Rejoice Musical Soulfood.
