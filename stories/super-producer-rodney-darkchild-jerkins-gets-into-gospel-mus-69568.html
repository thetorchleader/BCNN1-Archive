Super Producer, Rodney “Darkchild” Jerkins, Gets Into Gospel Music

April 24, 2015 Entertainment

Photo Credit: GETTY

GRAMMY® Award-winning Super Producer, Rodney “Darkchild” Jerkins, 37, is throwing his hat into the gospel ring.

The Christian music executive, known for his mainstream hits, has officially launched Lifestyle Music Group (LMG).

Jerkins, who says Lifestyle “is not a label,” explains that it is “a destination where a community of artists can exist and just be themselves, authentically expressing their God-given gifts without judgment.”

Currently, Lifestyle is home to Jerkins’ wife, Joy Enriquez and Tim Bowman Jr., whose new Jerkins-produced single, “I’m Good,” releases this spring.

Enriquez’s first single, “Hallelujah,” also produced by Jerkins, is slated for a Mother’s Day release.

Jerkins has made a name for himself working with big stars in secular music like Brandy, Michael Jackson, Justin Bieber, Britney Spears, and numerous others.
