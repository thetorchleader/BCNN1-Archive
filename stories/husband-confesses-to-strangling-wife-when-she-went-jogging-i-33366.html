Husband Confesses to Strangling Wife When she Went Jogging in Philadelphia Park

August 11, 2014 National

Christopher and Connie Murray (Facebook)

The husband of Constance “Connie” Murray confessed to strangling her at a Philadelphia park after failing a polygraph test, police say.

The body of the 46-year-old mother of two was found strangled at Pennypack Park the morning of Aug. 5 after disappearing the night before during a jog.

Christopher Murray, 48, has been charged with murder after he confronted his wife in a rage at the park about an “ongoing domestic discord between the two,” said Lt. Philip Riehl of Philadelphia Police to the Philadelphia Daily News.

Even though Murray told the Philadelphia newspaper his wife was a “beautiful woman, a loving, caring mother, a fabulous friend,” police considered Murray a suspect in his wife’s death very early on.

“There was a lot of good police work that went into this,” Riehl told the newspaper.

Murray reportedly left her home at 9 p.m., but never returned. Her husband and daughter filed a missing person report when they couldn’t find her after about three hours, the paper reported.

She was found at the edge of the park in the brush by a person walking their dog less than a mile from home.
