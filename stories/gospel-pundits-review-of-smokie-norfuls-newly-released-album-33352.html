Gospel Pundit’s Review Of Smokie Norful’s Newly Released Album, “Forever Yours”

August 11, 2014 Entertainment
Motown Gospel/Tre Myles Music
    

Since he burst into the gospel marketplace in the early 2000′s with his career-defining runaway hit, “I Need You Now,” Smokie Norful has been known for his passionate songwriting, precise vocals and boundless range. With the release of his new album, Forever Yours, Norful features many of these same hallmarks of his artistry.

Forever Yours is Norful’s first all-new studio album in 5 years, consisting of 10 tracks (the deluxe edition contains 4 bonus songs) and featuring production from heavy-hitters like Aaron Lindsey, Warryn Campbell and Elvis “Blac Elvis” Williams.

Fans can look forward to strong ballads, like the album’s lead single, “No Greater Love,” and “I Need A Word,” a piano-driven, passionate ballad that is incredibly reminiscent of Norful’s career-defining “I Need You Now.” Beyond the ballad, though, listeners will appreciate Norful’s treatment of upbeat and mid-tempo tracks like “He Loves Me,” laced with fun background vocals and a sing-song melody, and “I’ve Got What You Need.” With a familiar Motown vibe, the tune is something like Marvin Gaye or the Temptations might have recorded.
