Christian Sister Lolo Jones says ‘I Miss My Bobsled Derriere (Butt)’

July 20, 2014 Sports

A tale of two Lolos: as a track hurdler and a bobsledder at the Sochi Winter Games.

Lolo Jones mourns the end of her end. 

The summer and winter Olympian told ThePostGame.com that while she harbors absolutely no nostalgia for Sochi itself (“I’m honest”), the track star-turned-bobsledder does miss the bond with her fellow sledders in her adopted team sport. “Those girls were my everything out there,” she said.

Jones is also bummed that her hard-earned baby-got-back bobsled booty was the first to go as the 31-year-old works to get back into hurdling shape.
