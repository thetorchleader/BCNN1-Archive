Gunfire Breaks Out After Man Tries to Ram Gate at NSA Headquarters; 2 Injured — Developing…

March 30, 2015 National

Authorities say police were involved in a shooting after the driver of a vehicle tried to ram a gate at the National Security Agency in Anne Arundel County.

The incident happened at the NSA building off of Route 32 in Fort Meade around 9:30 a.m.

It is unclear if anyone was injured in the shooting.

This is a breaking news situation. Stay with FOX 5 for the latest.

______

Two people were being treated for injuries Monday at one of the gates at the huge military installation of Fort Meade in Anne Arundel County, according to the Associated Press.

Few details were immediately available about the incident, which the AP said occurred just after 10 a.m.

The AP said that Chad Jones, a spokesman at Fort Meade, said emergency responders were on the scene. Jones said he didn’t know how the injuries happened.

Anne Arundel County Fire officials confirmed that they had sent units to NSA’s facility but said they could not release any information because the incident is on the NSA property.

At 9:30 a.m., police at Fort Meade said, “we don’t have any information” at this point. They confirmed that it happened on the NSA side of Fort Meade.

The military installation of Fort Meade in Anne Arundel County has about 11,000 military personnel on site and another 29,000 civilian employees, according to its web site. The facility sits near the areas of Odenton and Laurel and is the third largest employer in Maryland. It houses the NSA and other federal agencies.
