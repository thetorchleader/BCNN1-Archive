Report: Broncos Would Trade Tebow if Manning Signs

March 12, 2012 Uncategorized

Denver may not be big enough for Peyton Manning and Tim Tebow.

The Broncos are among several teams in pursuit of Manning, four days into his free agency. But what would happen to Tebow, the incumbent QB in Denver, should Manning go there?

According to ProFootballTalk.com,
citing “a source with knowledge of the dynamics in Denver,” the Broncos
would likely trade Tebow instead of having Manning tutor the erratic
but winning left-hander.

The most obvious candidate would be the Jacksonville Jaguars, whose fans wanted the team to draft the University of Florida product in 2010. Of course, the Jags took Blaine Gabbert with the 10th overall pick last year. But what’s another quarterback controversy for Tebow?
