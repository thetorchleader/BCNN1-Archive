National Missionary Baptist Convention Re-Elects Dr. Nehemiah Davis as President

September 6, 2014 Christian

Pastor Nehemiah Davis (KHVN AM)

The National Missionary Baptist Convention of America has announced that Dr. Nehemiah Davis, pastor of the Mount Pisgah Missionary Baptist Church in Fort Worth, Texas, has been re-elected as the black Baptist convention’s president. The NMBCA’s 134th annual session wrapped up in Houston, TX, on Thursday, Sept. 4th.

The host pastor of the convention was Rev. Max Miller of the Mount Hebron Missionary Baptist Church. The convention was held at the Hyatt Regency Hotel in Houston.

On Feb. 25, 2014, Texas Rep. Marc Veasey honored Dr. Nehemiah Davis in the House of Representatives for “his exemplary life of service” and his 50 years as pastor of Mount Pisgah Missionary Baptist Church in Fort Worth.

Davis is also a member of the National Association of Christians in Social Work, the Fort Worth Chamber of Commerce, the Fort Worth Metropolitan Black Chamber, and the NAACP. He was a 2003 inductee to the Religious Hall of Fame in Dallas, Texas, and is one of the first recipients of the coveted Dr. W. N. Daniel Service Award.

Davis was elected to the presidency in September 2013. He succeeded the late Dr. C.C. Robertson, the pastor of the Bexar Street Baptist Church in Dallas, TX.

Find out more about the National Missionary Baptist Convention of America at http://www.nmbca.com/.

–BCNN1

Bio of Dr. Nehemah Davis

Dr. Davis is a native of Centerville, Texas. His scholastic achievements include a B.A. Degree from Mary Allen College in 1955, and B.D. (1966), M. Div. and M.R.E. degrees from southwetern Baptist Theological Seminary in 1968. He received his Doctor of Divnity degree from Guadalupe Baptist Theological Seminary in 1992. His other training includes Pastoral Hospital care of Sick from All Saints Hospital, Fort Worth, Texas. His graduate studies at North Texas University. He has received extensive training on administration and leadership skills.

Dr. Davis is a member of the National Association of Christians in Social work, the Fort Worth Chamber of Commerce, the Fort Worth Metropolitan Black Chamber, and the NAACP. Dr. Davis is listed in the Diretory of Consultants of the Health and Social Welfare Services in the Dallas and Fort Worth Metropolitan Areas (1968-1970).

Dr. Davis has served in denominational work for many years. A scholar and an educator, he works with the National Baptist congress as a Lecturer in Minister’s Department. He is past president of the Pastor’s Conference, National Baptist Convention of Armerica; and he is President of the Minister Conference of the National Baptist Missionary Baptist of America.

Dr. Davis presently serves as Chairman of S.A. Pleasant, Jr. Institute of the Missionary Baptist General Convention of Texas and Assistant Dean of the Congress. He is Dean of the Congress of the North Texas District Association: and a teacher in the Baptist Ministers’ Union of Fort Worth / Tarrant County and Vicinity. He is also a Post President of the Baptist Minister’s Union of Fort Worth/Tarrant County and Vicinity.

Dr. Davis is married to the former Dorothy Nell Cole. The couple has two daughters; Carol Michelle Davis-Jackson and Nina Caron Davis, one son-in-law; Andrew H. Jackson and two grandsons; Andwar Davis Jackson and Caleb Aaron Jackson.

Dr. Davis was a 2003 inductee to the Religious Hall of Fame in Dallas, Texas, and is one of the first recipients of the coveted Dr. W. N. Daniel Service Award. (http://mountpisgahmissionarybaptistchurch.net/)
