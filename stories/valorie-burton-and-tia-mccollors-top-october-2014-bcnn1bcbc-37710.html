Valorie Burton and Tia McCollors Top October 2014 BCNN1/BCBC National Bestsellers List

October 6, 2014 Books

Bestselling authors, Valorie Burton (Get Unstuck, Be Unstoppable — Nonfiction) and Tia McCollors (Sunday Morning Song — Fiction) lead the bestsellers list of Black Christian authors on the October 2014 edition of the Black Christian News/Black Christian Book Company National Bestsellers List.

BCNN1.com has also released the October 2014 edition of the BCNN1/Amazon.com Kindle E-book Bestsellers List. This list ranks African-American Christian e-books based upon their sales rankings in the Amazon Kindle e-book store. Also, BCNN1 is releasing the October 2014 edition of the BCNN1/Barnes & Noble Nook E-book Bestsellers List. This list ranks African-American Christian e-books based upon their sales rankings in the B&N Nook e-book store.
