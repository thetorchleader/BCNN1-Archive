Mo’Nique to Star in Faith-Based Film, ‘Blackbird’

April 26, 2015 Black

Mo’Nique and Julian Walker in scene from ‘Blackbird.’

It’s been over five years since Mo’Nique has appeared on the big screen and now, she has come roaring back in the Patrik – Ian Polk film, Blackbird.   

Blackbird tells the story of Randy Rousseau (played by newcomer Julian Walker), a 17-year old who loves the Lord, (he gives new meaning to the command to pray without ceasing), sings in the church choir and is a friend and caretaker for all, including his emotionally unstable mother, Claire (Mo’Nique).

Randy’s world is filled with turmoil as his father, Lance Rousseau (Isaiah Washington), has left the home, his younger sister has been kidnapped and he is struggling to come to terms with his sexuality as only a 17-year old could who just happens to live in southern Mississippi.

From the opening scene, Blackbird draws you in with its willingness to engage, and provoke the audience.  There are a myriad of themes running through this film that should and will ignite dialogue, mainly can you love a person of the same sex and the Lord at the same time without shame and fear of retribution from God and a community that you love?

The Larry Duplechan novel that the movie is based on was written over 30 years ago and, “The issues in the film are as prevalent today as they were then,” Duplechan said.  It’s a question that continues to haunt the church today and this film does not retreat from pushing the viewer to smash their rose colored glasses and look at faith and love through the lens of well, everyone, heterosexuals, lesbians, gays, bisexuals, transgender and queers.
