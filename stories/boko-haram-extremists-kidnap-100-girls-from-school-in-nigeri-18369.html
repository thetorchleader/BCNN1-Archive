Boko Haram Extremists Kidnap 100 Girls from School In Nigeria

April 17, 2014 Eye on Africa, Slider

Boko Haram

The Islamic extremist group Boko Haram is being blamed for kidnapping about 100 girls from a school in northeastern Nigeria Tuesday.

Just after midnight, gunmen killed a soldier and a police officer guarding a school in Chibok and abducted the teenage girls. According to an anonymous official, some of the girls escaped by jumping off the open truck as it was moving slowly along a road.

The kidnapping happened a day after suspected Boko Haram insurgents bombed a bus station, killing 75.
