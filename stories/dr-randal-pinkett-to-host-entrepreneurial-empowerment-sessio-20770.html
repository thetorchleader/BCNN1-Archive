Dr. Randal Pinkett to Host ‘Entrepreneurial Empowerment Session’ at Rutgers to Help Entrepreneurs

May 2, 2014 African-American

Dr. Randal Pinkett came to national attention as the first African American winner from the fourth season of Donald Trump’s “The Apprentice” on NBC.

Pinkett is an accomplished businessman aside from his work with the Trump organization. He co-founded BCT Partners LLC, a multi-million dollar management and consulting IT firm and he holds five degrees including a Master of Science in Electrical Engineering, a Master of Business Administration, and Ph.D. from Massachusetts Institute of Technology.

In addition to the most recent merger with Lynx Technology Partners, Dr. Pinkett and Lynx Technology will host an Entrepreneurial Empowerment Session on Saturday, May 3, 2014 from 9:00 am to 12 noon at Rutgers University, Busch Campus Center, 604 Bartholomew Road, Piscataway, New Jersey 08854.

As more and more faith-based entities recognize the need for economic empowerment, the Abundant Life Family Worship Church and the First Baptist Church of Lincoln Gardens gladly embraced the opportunity to sponsor the event for the community.
