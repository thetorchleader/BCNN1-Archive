4-Year-Old Brooklyn Boy Found Dead, Aunt Taken Into Custody

February 26, 2015 Black

Ethan Ali
FAMILY HANDOUT

Brooklyn paramedics walked into a Brownsville apartment Wednesday afternoon and found 4-year-old Ethan Ali dead, lying in the arms of his aunt, Kristen Dale. The boy’s body had belt marks and bruises on it. Police officers suspect that the young boy “was beaten to death with a strap,” the New York Daily News reports.

Medics were called to the apartment after Dale’s boyfriend walked in and found Dale and the lifeless boy. Dale was taken into custody for questioning. Ethan’s grandfather, Leroy Ali, said that he warned both Dale and her boyfriend, about beating young Ethan so severely.

“They did it all the time. They beat him and they beat him,” Mr. Ali told the Daily News. “He was always bruised. [Kristen] beat him with the belt. I told her to stop.”

Ethan’s father, Anthony Ali, and mother Natasha Ali, an at-home health aide who was at work at the time of the incident, came home Wednesday to find their apartment littered with law enforcement officials who had begun to investigate the death of their son. The mother was taken to the precinct and the father can be seen in a photo taken by the Daily News exiting the apartment with his hand covering his face in grief.

According to the Daily News, child services were never called to look into child endangerment issues relating to Ethan Ali and his family.
