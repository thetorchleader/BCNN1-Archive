Chris Wallace Pays Tribute to His Dad, Mike Wallace

April 15, 2012 Uncategorized

Chris Wallace paid tribute to his late father, Mike Wallace, on the air Sunday, calling the former “60 Minutes” correspondent his “best friend” and saying, “I can’t imagine life without him.”
Wallace kicked off “Fox News Sunday” by thanking everyone who sent his family notes on his father’s death. “Some of you had met my dad for a moment, some had watched him on television for years. But he meant something to you and your notes meant so much to me,” he said.
Wallace played a clip of his interview with his father from 2005 — a conversation that ended with Mike Wallace telling him, “I love you. And I’m proud of you.”
