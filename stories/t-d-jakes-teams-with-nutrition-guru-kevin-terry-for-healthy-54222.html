T. D. Jakes Teams With Nutrition Guru Kevin Terry for Healthy Super Bowl Snacks

January 29, 2015 Uncategorized

Credit: NoCheatsNeeded.com

Super Bowl Sunday is a big day for chowing down on pizza, wings, and fattening dips, but Bishop T.D. Jakes is encouraging you to snack healthier this year.

To introduce heart healthy treats, the 57-year-old Dallas mega pastor has teamed with rising nutrition and wellness guru Kevin Terry.

The two are co-promoting The 2015 Super Bowl Healthy Snack Guide which features recipes and six tips to help readers stay on track.

“A rising-star in the world of nutrition and wellness, Kevin Curry delivers savory meals along with powerful messages to help sharpen the mind and nourish the body,” says Jakes.

Recipes like Zucchini Basil Bites, Sweet Potato Chips, Cajun Chicken Meatballs and more, just might add some flair to your Super Bowl celebration.
