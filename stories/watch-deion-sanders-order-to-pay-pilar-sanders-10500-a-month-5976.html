WATCH: Deion Sanders Order to Pay Pilar Sanders $10,500 a Month In Child Support

May 16, 2012 Uncategorized

The Deion and Pilar Sanders divorce has become one of the most public celebrity divorces of 2012. But the couple has finally agreed to a support payment and visitation schedule for their children.
At a hearing in Collin County Tuesday afternoon, a judge ordered the NFL Hall of Famer to pay his estranged wife Pilar $10,500 a month in child support and for mortgage on a home she has in Celina, Texas.
Deion claims that Pilar took numerous items from the home while he was away working at the Super Bowl. The judge said Pilar must bring back six pods full of those items.
