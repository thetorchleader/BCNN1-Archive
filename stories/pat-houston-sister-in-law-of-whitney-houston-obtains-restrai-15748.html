Pat Houston, Sister-in-Law of Whitney Houston, Obtains Restraining Order Against Nick Gordon

March 27, 2014 Uncategorized

What a sad mess.

Whitney Houston’s sister-in-law Pat Houston just got a restraining order against against Nick Gordon, the husband of Whitney’s daughter Bobbi Kristina, over claims she feared for her safety, reports TMZ.

This all started when Gordon used his social media pages to post guns and making threats against Pat and her son, Gary Michael Houston.

On March 16, Gordon tweeted, in an apparent reference to “Nightmare on Elm Street” killer Freddy Krueger, “Pat b***h Gary M I’m Freddy to y’all n****az. F*** you suck my d***.”
