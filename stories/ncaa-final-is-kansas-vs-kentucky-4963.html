NCAA Final Is Kansas vs. Kentucky

April 1, 2012 Uncategorized

Pssst, Kentucky. There’s still another team to play. Inconvenient, but true.


They are not just the Kansas Jayhawks. Not for the next 48 hours, anyway. They are Kentucky’s Last Obstacle.
They are not just Thomas Robinson, All-American or Jeff Withey, shot-blocking, shot-altering, shot-bothering pest. Not for the next two days. They are the final big bodies in Anthony Davis’ way.
That’s the title they were meeting for Saturday night, Kansas and Ohio State, Robinson and Withey and the Buckeyes’ Jared Sullinger. The Wildcats had already moved on, beating their rivals in fluorescent orange, the Louisville Thrushes. Lots of buzz around that one. “This is my stage!”’ Anthony Davis had shouted at the end, as he threw the ball into the air at the end.
Nice of him, then, to let the Jayhawks and Buckeyes borrow it, since the NCAA insisted on a second game. Someone has to play Kentucky Monday night.
That’d be Kansas, rallying past Ohio State 64-62 in Saturday’s undercard, except it came last. After the fierce intensity of the first game, the Superdome was like a peaceful dining room with the lights turned down. At least early, as the Buckeyes went up 13 in the first half.
But then the Jayhawks rallied, and in the end came a riveting finish, with just enough Kansas free throws. An underdog was chosen for Monday night; a resourceful team that had to replace four starters this season.
“I don’t think anybody probably thought that we could get here,” coach Bill Self said afterward. And just when it seemed like a good idea to celebrate the feat down the street at the French Quarter, mixing in with the Kentucky fans, the coach quashed that.
“I told them Bourbon Street was in the locker room tonight,” Self said of his players. 
“That’s as close as they’re going to get to it.”
