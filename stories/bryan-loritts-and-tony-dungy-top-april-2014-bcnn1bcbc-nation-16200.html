Bryan Loritts and Tony Dungy Top April 2014 BCNN1/BCBC National Bestsellers List

April 1, 2014 Books

Bryan Loritts and Tony Dungy Top April 2014 BCNN1/BCBC National Bestsellers List

ATLANTA, GA, (April 1, 2014) – Bestselling authors, Bryan Loritts (Letters to a Birmingham Jail – Nonfiction) and Tony Dungy (You Can Do It! – Fiction) lead the bestsellers list of Black Christian authors on the April 2014 edition of the Black Christian News/Black Christian Book Company National Bestsellers List.

BCNN1.com has also released the April 2014 edition of the BCNN1/Amazon.com Kindle E-book Bestsellers List. This list ranks African-American Christian e-books based upon their sales rankings in the Amazon Kindle e-book store. Also, BCNN1 is releasing the April 2014 edition of the BCNN1/Barnes & Noble Nook E-book Bestsellers List. This list ranks African-American Christian e-books based upon their sales rankings in the B&N Nook e-book store.
