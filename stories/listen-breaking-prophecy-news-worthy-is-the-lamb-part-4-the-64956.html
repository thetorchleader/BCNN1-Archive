LISTEN: Breaking Prophecy News; Worthy is the Lamb! Part 4 (The Prophet Daniel’s Report #543)

April 2, 2015 Christian, Second Coming Watch

Daniel Whyte III

This broadcast is not about predictions, nor is it about setting dates as some foolishly have done, however it is all about preparation.

PROPHECY HEADLINES: U.S. releases military aid to Egypt, will send jets and missiles | Iraq declares victory over ISIS in Tikrit | Iran, Russia say framework agreement will be drafted on Wednesday | France aims for Israeli-Palestinian peace push in coming days | Canada to launch airstrikes in Syria within days

The Bible says in Revelation 17:14: “These shall make war with the Lamb, and the Lamb shall overcome them: for he is Lord of lords, and King of kings: and they that are with him are called, and chosen, and faithful.”

PROPHECY BOOTCAMP: Our topic for today is titled “Worthy is the Lamb!” (Part 4) from Dr. Ed Hindson’s book “Revelation: Unlocking the Future”.

The Lamb of God reaches forth His nail-scarred hand to receive the scroll from the Eternal Father. Who can deny such love? Such sacrifice? Certainly not the God of the universe! For He Himself is love. In that one incredible moment, the love of the Father meets the love of the Son. The scroll is passed and the triumph is secured. The Lamb of God now holds the title deed to the universe. Hallelujah! What a triumph!

Walter Scott writes, “Now the self-same Lamb bearing in His person the marks of His passion is here seen as the object of heaven’s worship. No voice is, nor can be silent when the slain Lamb appears.”
