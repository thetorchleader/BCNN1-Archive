San Diego Megachurch Pastor Miles McPherson to Release 'God in the Mirror'

February 25, 2013 Uncategorized

In a culture where image is everything, it’s tempting to define ourselves according to the cultural icons of our day, but Pastor Miles McPherson of the Rock Church, is going against the grain in his new book, “God in the Mirror: Discovering Who You Were Created To Be,” releasing from Baker Publishing Group March 1, 2013. In the book, McPherson encourages readers to think less of themselves and more about God’s plan for their life.
Engulfed by a popular culture saturated with images of idealized, air-brushed and unattainable physical beauty, men and women cannot escape feeling judged on the basis of their appearance and often times forged identity. In “God in the Mirror,” McPherson addresses the lies Satan uses to lead humans to sin; the obsession with image, the feeling of worthlessness and the pressure to follow the ways of society. McPherson explains to readers how they were created, why they were created, what their true purpose is, and how to remake themselves according to God’s original plan for their lives.
“Human beings are created in God’s image; if we really take that truth to heart and live our lives in light of it, everything changes,” said McPherson, who empowers readers with the knowledge of their capacity to reflect God’s heart to the world. “When we live more and more in Him, we begin to experience transformed relationships with each other and with God.”
