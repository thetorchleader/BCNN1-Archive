E. Dewey Smith Jr. to Lead “Maximizing Men” Conference With Pastor Bryan Carter and Others, April 24 – April 26 In Atlanta

April 9, 2014 Christian

Mission
The mission of M2 is to:

  • Equip men with practical principles for daily living
  • Empower men for maximized discipleship
  • Expose men to who they are in Christ.

We realize that building men builds a better future for everyone.

Vision
Our vision is to practically and powerfully equip all men to live maximized lives of Christian discipleship looking to Christ as our model of manhood.

About The Conference
Thursday, April 24 – Sunday, April 26 Atlanta GA

The Maximizing Men Conference, also known as M2 is designed to equip men with fundamental resources to help them live maximized lives of Christian manhood. The 21st Century man is often exposed to harsh criticism, societal contempt, and holistic condemnation. This conference is an invitation for men to become maximized in their finances, future, families, and in relationships with females.

Led by Dr. E. Dewey Smith, Jr. as the conference host and keynote speaker, we desire to expose men to some of the most influential and authentic leaders in the country. Every man is welcome! Come to the place where “manly” struggles are understood and we offer an opportunity for “real” men to discuss “real” issues while developing “real” faith for the “real” world. Come get maximized!
