“Duck Dynasty” Cast Makes Guinness Book of World Records for Duck Calls

May 22, 2014 Entertainment

The Robertson family is used to breaking records, but their latest one is going down in the books.

In Florida this week, the stars of “Duck Dynasty” led a crowd of 3,028 in making simultaneous duck calls — a feat recorded in the Guinness Book of World Records.

Record guidelines required the crowd to duck-call for one minute, but the quack-fest lasted for two.

“That was amazing!” Maj. Tom Louden of the local Salvation Army said. “I don’t know about you, but I can still hear ducks!”

The record was set Sunday at a fundraiser for the Salvation Army, named “The Call: Faith, Family and Facial Hair.”
