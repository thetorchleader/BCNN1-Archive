Dorinda Clark-Cole’s “Living It” Lands at No. 2 on Billboard’s Top Gospel Albums Chart

February 27, 2015 Entertainment

GRAMMY® award-winning recording artist, Dorinda Clark-Cole, 57, released her self-titled debut solo album more than a decade ago, back in 2002.

All these years later, she is still making a strong showing on the charts.

Her brand new album, Living It has landed the No. 2 spot on Billboard’s Top Gospel Albums chart, marking the highest chart position of her career.

WOW Gospel 2015 edged out the No. 1 spot, with Tasha Cobbs’ Grace EP presently sitting at No. 3.

In response to the new milestone, Clark-Cole shared on Facebook that she is “Sooooooooo excited” to have achieved “my highest chart position ever!”

Currently starring on Oxygen’s first season of Preachers of Detroit, the exposure Clark-Cole has gained from the high-profile show has no doubt boosted album sales.
