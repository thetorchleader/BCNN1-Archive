Katt Williams says Photograper was Filming Suge Knight’s Child

November 1, 2014 Entertainment

Katt Williams says he and Suge Knight were falsely arrested because they were not trying to steal a camera … they were trying to protect a 5-year-old child from an unethical photographer.
Katt talked to our photog hours after his release Wednesday in Malibu … he’s characteristically cryptic … but it seems he’s saying he and Suge saw the alleged victim shooting a 5-year-old who was alone in an alley and suggested the photog was doing something inappropriate.

According to Katt … the female photog said she would erase the video but then booked it, so they gave chase.  And, he says they never took the camera.
