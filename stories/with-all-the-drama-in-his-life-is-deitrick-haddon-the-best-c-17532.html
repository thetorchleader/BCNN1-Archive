With All the Drama In His Life, Is Deitrick Haddon the Best Choice to Fix Church Choirs?

April 11, 2014 Christian

Credit: GETTY

He is the new Iyanla Vanzant of Christendom. But instead of fixing lives, he is fixing choirs. Gospel recording artist Deitrick Haddon, along with sometimes R&B, sometimes gospel singer Michelle Williams, will be helping Christian leaders repair the dysfunction within their respective music ministry platforms.

But with all the drama and dysfunction in the Preachers of LA star’s own life, as well as his sidekick, is he or she really in any position to help others resolve their ministerial and personal issues?

As Matthew 7:4 asks, “Or how can you say to your brother, ‘Let me take the speck out of your eye,’ and behold, the log is in your own eye?”

In recent years, the once stable industry veteran has come unglued. Haddon’s 15-year marriage to singer Damita Haddon crumbled amid controversy and allegations.

Before his divorce was final, the former pastor moved on to a new woman (now wife), Domonique, and produced a baby from that adulterous affair.

Currently, Haddon is dodging questions surrounding inappropriate photos of his male genitalia allegedly circulating on the Internet, leaked by an ex-fling with whom he reportedly had an ongoing tryst while married to his first wife.

This is the man who will be fixing others’ broken music ministries?
