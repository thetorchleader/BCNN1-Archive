Egypt’s Muslim Brotherhood Nominates Presidential Candidate

April 1, 2012 Uncategorized

The Freedom and Justice Party, the political wing of Egypt’s Muslim Brotherhood, nominated a candidate for May’s presidential elections.
On Saturday, the FJP announced the nomination of Khairat al-Shater, 62, who just last week said he had no intention of running. The decision reverses the group’s previous announcement that it would not seek the nation’s highest office.
Al-Shater, who joined the Brotherhood in 1974, is considered one of the Brotherhoods’ top strategists.
During the Mubarak regime when the group was outlawed, he was arrested four times for Brotherhood-related offenses and served a total of seven years in prison.
