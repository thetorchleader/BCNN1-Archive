Christian Blogger Veronica Partridge Makes Decision Not to Wear ‘Lust-Inciting’ Yoga Pants and Leggings

January 22, 2015 Christian

Christian blogger Veronica Partridge’s convictions about wearing leggings have sparked a national conversation (Photo Credit: Facebook)

Millions of women wear leggings and yoga pants for comfort. One Christian blogger says, in her opinion, the tight pants are lust-inciting and inappropriate.

Veronica Partridge has sparked national conversation over her personal convictions.

“I will no longer wear thin, form-fitting yoga pants or leggings in public,” she wrote on her personal blog earlier this month.

“The only time I feel (for myself) it is acceptable to wear them, is if I am in the comfort of my own home or if I am wearing a shirt long enough to cover my rear end.”

The post titled, Why I Chose To No Longer Wear Leggings has gone viral, with over 30,000 shares on Facebook and has sparked thousands of comments online.

Partridge, a wife and mother, arrived at the decision after “having a conviction weighing heavy on my heart.”

This feeling further intensified, according to her, following a conversation with a group of men and women that agreed tight pants “creates a stronger attraction for a man to look at a woman’s body and may cause them to think lustful thoughts.”

When the devout Christian spoke to her husband Dale about what she was feeling, his answer solidified her position.
