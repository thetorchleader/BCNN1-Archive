WATCH: Is Porsha Williams’ Husband, Kordell Stewart, Right That She Allowed Fame to Pull Her Away from God and Change Her? Here Are 3 Videos Showing Her Preaching the Word and 3 Showing Her Committing a Crime Against One of Her RHOA Co-stars

May 4, 2014 Christian

Porsha Williams

Porsha Williams, last seen on “The View” explaining why she dragged her “Real Housewives of Atlanta” co-star Kenya Moore across the floor at the show’s reunion taping, has popped up today in YouTube videos preaching the Word at an Atlanta-area church.

Newly leaked videos of Williams on the speaker circuit show her at the altar of High Praise Christian Center Ministries in Lithonia, Georgia, in 2010, reports Radar Online.

Citing a “spiritual helmet” that can protect believers from drama, she says, “Just by being saved you can live in peace, because you ain’t worrying! All the problems that you be dealing with, all the lies that the devil’s telling you…[you can ignore] because [you] got the Word!”

RHOA Fight: Porsha Yanks Kenya Weave-First to the Floor

After weeks of buildup, he said/she said, a 911 call and a battery charge, the fight between Kenya Moore and Porsha Williams finally aired Sunday on Bravo’s “Real Housewives of Atlanta’ reunion show.

The two exchanged verbal barbs throughout the taping, but things started to go dirty south when former beauty queen Moore, 43, wielded a scepter in Porsha’s face.
