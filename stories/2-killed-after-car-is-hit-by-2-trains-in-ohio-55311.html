2 Killed After Car is Hit by 2 Trains in Ohio

February 5, 2015 National

Feb. 5, 2015: Location of a car vs. train accident in Eaton Township, near Grafton.
(Photo: WKYC)

Two people are dead after a car was hit by two trains in Lorain County.

The Ohio State Highway Patrol is on the scene of the accident at State Route 83 and Capel Road, near Grafton.

The car is a tan Chevrolet.

The patrol is not yet releasing the names of the victims.

In December, an empty tanker truck was struck by a train at the same crossing.

The accident comes just days after a Metro North commuter train hit a car in a crossing in Westchester County, N.Y., killing 6.

SOURCE: WKYC-TV, Cleveland
