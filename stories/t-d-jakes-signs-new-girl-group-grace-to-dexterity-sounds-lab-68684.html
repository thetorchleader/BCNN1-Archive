T. D. Jakes Signs New Girl Group “Grace” to Dexterity Sounds Label

April 20, 2015 Entertainment

The five ladies of Grace pose for a snap with Bishop T.D. Jakes and wife Serita Jakes (Credit: Instagram)

Five women have been selected to usher in a new musical chapter in Bishop T.D. Jakes’ ministry.

Psalmists Candy West, DeeDee, April Nevels, Ruby Cavazos and Shana Saint, each a Potter’s House of Dallas choir member, have collectively been signed by the mega ministry leader’s Dexterity Sounds label.

Named “Grace” from the Greek charis meaning “to rejoice,” the all-female quintet has released a new single, “This Time,” produced by Eric Dawkins.

One of the better known Grace members is April Nevel of the contemporary gospel group, The Nevels Sisters, who released her debut solo album Krazy Praise in 2010 on indie label, 7-Places.
