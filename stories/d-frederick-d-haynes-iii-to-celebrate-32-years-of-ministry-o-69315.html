Dr. Frederick D. Haynes III to Celebrate 32 Years of Ministry on April 26th

April 23, 2015 Christian

Join us Sunday, April 26th as we celebrate our Visionary Leader and Senior Pastor on 32 yrs of Ministry here at Friendship-West with special guest speaker Rev. Dr. Jeremiah A. Wright Jr. This promises to be a very special Sunday so please come show your love for Dr. Haynes on this special day!

Want to celebrate Pastor Haynes? Want to bless one who has blessed so many, so well, and so often? You can! Please feel free to participate in any of the ways listed below…

1. Upload on any social network your most creative video (32 seconds or less) in honor of Pastor Haynes’ 32nd anniversary using the hashtag ‪#‎FDH32‬

2. Post on any social network one way that you bless others as a result of Pastor Haynes’ ministry using the hashtag #FDH32

3. Take a second to click this link to give a financial gift via our website at http://www.friendshipwest.org

Thank you for joining us to celebrate Pastor Haynes!
