Cisco to Cut 6000 Jobs

August 15, 2014 Technology

A sign is posted in front of the Cisco Systems headquarters in San Jose, Calif. (PHOTO CREDIT: Justin Sullivan, Getty Images)

Cisco said it would cut up to 6,000 jobs as part of a major restructuring.

The company made the announcement after Cisco announced quarterly earnings and revenue that topped analyst estimates.

CEO John Chambers said Cisco must focus on areas of growth to become “the No. 1 IT player.”

Transforming Cisco “requires us making some very tough, tough decisions,” he said.

The job cuts would affect about 8% of the company’s work force. Cisco said it expected to record charges of up to $700 million.

The cuts come as Cisco continues to struggle in emerging markets and in its service provider video business.

A year ago the company said it would cut 4,000 jobs, or 5% of its work force.

Cisco posted fourth-quarter earnings of 55 cents a share on $12.36 billion in sales.

Analysts had expected the company to report earnings of 53 cents a share on $12.15 billion in revenue, up from 52 cents a share on sales of $12.42 billion a year ago.
