WATCH: Live Video Coverage of Ferguson Protests; Live #MichaelBrown Tweets

August 15, 2014 National

Residents of Ferguson, Missouri are currently staging a protest against the shooting death of 18-year-old Michael Brown, who was killed over the weekend by a police officer. He was unarmed at the time. Tensions in Ferguson reached a fever pitch last night with reporters arrested and police throwing tear gas, but Governor Jay Nixon announced today the State Highway Patrol will take over security in Ferguson, which may help to tamp things down.
