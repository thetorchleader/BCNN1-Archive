ESPN Monday Night Football Sideline Reporter Lisa Salters Joins List of Journalists Vowing Not to Call DC’s Football Team ‘Redskins’

September 3, 2014 Black

Lisa Salters

ESPN “Monday Night Football” sideline reporter Lisa Salters has reportedly joined a growing list of journalist who will not refer to Washington, D.C.’s team as “Redskins,’ according to Sports Illustrated’s Richard Deitsch. 

Those refusing to use the controversial nickname include Peter King of Sports Illustrated, CBS Sports’ Phil Simms, NBC’s Tony Dungy and the Washington Post editorial board.

Among those taking a neutral stance on the issue are ESPN reporters Josina Anderson and Adam Schefter.

The situation involving the “Redskins” name comes amid a recent decision by the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office, which described the name as being “disparaging to Native Americans.” As a result, six federal trademark registrations connected to the team were canceled.

Despite the increasing number of media reps not using the name, team owner Dan Snyder remained defiant regarding his take on the situation.
