Texas Pastor Shot and Killed by Son-In-Law In ‘Tragic Accident’

Date not recorded in the original archive Christian

Pastor John Warden, 59, (l) and his son-in-law Jared Priddy (r).
(PHOTO: FACEBOOK/MARISSA PRIDDY)

It was inside New Hope Baptist Church in Jarrell where Pastor John Warden spent his Sundays. Except this Sunday he never made it.

“At this point we haven’t been able to determine any criminal motive or intent and looks like a tragic accident,” said Cedar Park Police Chief Sean Mannix.

A tragic accident cops said was caused by his son-in-law — Jared Priddy. The duo went to the Cedar Park Center on Saturday to attend a gun show. They brought a semi-automatic handgun with them.

“What we do know is that the gun had been unloaded prior because it was brought into the gun show, inspected and tagged as unloaded,” added Mannix.

So when the men got back to their car, detectives said Priddy reloaded the gun — and it went off — striking Warden. At least one gun expert believes the shooting could have been prevented.
