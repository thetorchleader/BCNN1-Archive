Las Vegas Erotic Heritage Museum Offers Monica Lewinsky a Job and $250,000 for Infamous Blue Dress

May 28, 2014 National

© STR OLD/REUTERS/REUTERS
Monica Lewinsky wants to ‘burn the beret and bury the blue dress’ that made her famous but a Las Vegas museum is willing to pay handsomely for the apparel.

Monica Lewinsky may not want to bury her blue dress quite yet.

The Las Vegas Erotic Heritage Museum has offered her $250,000 for the stained piece of history — and has also thrown in a job offer to the unemployed ex-intern.

“I am prepared to offer you a job managing and curating the Erotic Heritage Museum, starting at once for $100,000.00 per year, with a 10-year contract including benefits and cost of living increases,” museum owner Dr. Harry V. Mohney wrote, according to the Las Vegas Review-Journal.

Mohney promised Lewinsky, 40, could use her “skills in communication and business to use in a positive way.”

Lewinsky has not responded.
