Bishop Joseph Walker, Pastor of Mount Zion Baptist Church in Nashville, Addresses Violence in Ferguson

August 21, 2014 Black

Bishop Joseph Walker III, senior pastor of Mt. Zion Baptist Church, has scheduled a town hall meeting tonight to encourage a dialogue between concerned Nashvillians and police here. Police Chief Steve Anderson, eight Metro commanders and local politicians are expected to attend.
(Photo: Dipti Vaidya / File / The Tennessean )

One of Nashville’s leading African-American pastors is joining with police tonight, Aug. 21, to address the violence that erupted in Ferguson, Mo., after the death of Michael Brown.

Brown, 18, was shot and killed by a white police officer earlier this month. His death triggered a wave of protests and unrest in the St. Louis suburb, with authorities sometimes using tear gas and rubber bullets against the crowds.

Bishop Joseph Walker, senior pastor of Mt. Zion Baptist Church, has scheduled a town hall meeting to encourage a dialogue between concerned Nashvillians and police here. Police Chief Steve Anderson, eight Metro commanders and local politicians are expected to attend.

The town hall will begin at 7:30 p.m. at Mt. Zion Baptist Church, 1112 Jefferson St.
