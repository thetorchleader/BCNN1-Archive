Tiki Barber Marries Former NBC Intern Traci Lynn Johnson Eight Days After Divorce from First Wife Is Finalized

July 20, 2012 Uncategorized

Retired Pro-Bowl  running back Tiki Barber is a married man – again. Barber wed former NBC intern Traci Lynn Johnson, tweeting several pictures from his Twitter account Thursday night.
“Many thanks to the NYC Clerk’s office, especially James Mitchell who married me to my love @TraciLJohnson!,” Barber tweeted. “Excited for the next chapters.”
Barber and Johnson celebrated their wedding at a French restaurant Le Cirque in New York City. Tiki’s brother Ronde, a cornerback  for the Tampa Bay Buccaneers, was also in attendance.
