EJ Johnson, Homosexual Son of Magic Johnson, Loses 100 Pounds

May 14, 2015 Entertainment

EJ Johnson, where did you go? 

The “Rich Kids of Beverly Hills” star has been slimming down since undergoing weight loss surgery last September, but we did not expect these results.

EJ looks fabulous.

EJ flaunted his new body in a new Instagram photo on May 13 and he’s literally half the man he used to be. Maybe less, even!

“It’s definitely somewhere far over 100,” he told Wendy Williams this week when asked how much weight he’d lost. “I mean like literally almost half my size.”

The son of NBA legend Magic Johnson also opened up about his decision to go under the knife to give his weight loss a jump start.
