Phillip K. Eure Appointed as the First New York Police Department Inspector General

March 28, 2014 Uncategorized

PHILIP EURE/VIA LINKEDIN
The city has appointed its first-ever inspector general for the NYPD — Philip Eure, head of D.C.’s Office of Police Complaints.

An official who oversees Washington D.C. police has been named inspector general of the New York Police Department.

Philip Eure (yur) was named Friday. He starts in May and will oversee staff of 50.

He’s been head of the District of Columbia’s Office of Police Complaints.

The New York City Council overrode former Mayor Michael Bloomberg’s veto to create the watchdog position. It also passed a law making it easier for people to sue over stop-and-frisk.

Besides responding to the outcry over stop-and-frisk, the new laws were propelled by concern about the NYPD’s extensive surveillance of Muslims, as disclosed in stories by The Associated Press.

Department of Investigation Commissioner Mark Peters had been given until April 1 to find an IG.

Source: The AP
