California Couple Drops Suit Over Citations for Bible Studies

November 22, 2011 Uncategorized

City has agreed to pay back the $300 in fines levied against Chuck and Stephanie Fromm, who hosted large weekly Bible studies without a permit. Changes to the municipal code are in the works.
A San Juan Capistrano couple has dropped their legal challenge of the $300 in fines they were charged for holding Bible studies in their home.
Stephanie Fromm said they dropped their appeal after the city offered to reimburse their money and re-examine the ordinance.
“We’re not looking to head up a battle,” she said Friday night. “We’re just so thankful they’re willing to work with us for the good of all the community.”
Chuck and Stephanie Fromm were hosting a weekly Bible study on Sundays that sometimes drew up to 50 people. After a neighbor complained about the gatherings, city officials determined the meetings required a conditional use permit.
The Fromms were cited twice after they refused to obtain the permit – $100 the first time, $200 the second time.
Following media reports of the case, the city was inundated with angry letters from around the nation.
City Attorney Omar Sandoval said the city never had an issue with Bible studies, but the Fromms’ gatherings grew so large that the city had to ask questions.
“It was always a question of whether they were a church or not, and they were not,” Sandoval said.
