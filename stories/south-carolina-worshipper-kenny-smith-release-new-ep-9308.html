South Carolina Worshipper Kenny Smith Release New EP

November 15, 2012 Uncategorized

Anointed and refreshingly gifted, Kenny Smith, a Columbia SC native has a heart for spreading the good news through music. This anointed vessel is determined to deliver the message of hope and faith. His love for God and passion for ministry has propelled him into the forefront of worship music today. 
A newcomer to the music industry, Smith has introduced a fresh sound to gospel music. His writing has produced songs like the sensational worship record “Prefect Place”. Most recently, his writing and vocal talents have been featured on the 2012 Stellar award winning album “WOW”(Teen Pure-N-Heart) with the hit song “He Did It For Me”.
