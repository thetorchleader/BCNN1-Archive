Liberty Church Presents ‘We Are One’

April 19, 2015 Black

Pastor David Cross

The Liberty Church will present ‘We Are One, A Choral Explosion of Praise’ on Saturday, April 25, at 5 p.m.

According to Pastor David Cross, “The worship will feature the anointed choirs of Greater New St. Matthews, Mount Pilgrim, and The Liberty Church with Psalmists Shannon Lorrick Timmons and Lady Rose Neale.” Also, Catherine Rachal and the Dr. H. P. Rachal Ecumenical Choir and Tony Jones and the L.A. Young Adult Choir will perform.

“Everyone, near and far, is invited to come and share in this extraordinary offering of praise from the instruments God has ordained for His glory and edification of His people,” added Pastor Cross.

Liberty is located at 14725 South Gramercy Place in Gardena.  For information, call the church office at (310) 715-8400.

Source: LA Sentinel
