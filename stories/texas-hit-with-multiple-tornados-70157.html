Texas Hit With Multiple Tornados

April 27, 2015 National

(Photo: WFAA)

The National Weather Service has issued a Tornado Watch for a wide section of North Texas effective until 5 a.m. Monday.

This watch does not include the DFW area.

A previous Tornado Watch included Tarrant and Dallas Counties, but that watch was effective only until 11 p.m.

Confirmed tornadoes have been spotted in the following locations:

  • Erath-Comanche County line, 4:30 p.m.
  • 11 miles southeast of Stephenville, 8 p.m.
  • Glen Rose, 9:10 p.m.
  • Cleburne State Park, 9:40 p.m.
  • West of Rio Vista, roughly 10 p.m.
  • Grandview

Several other possible tornadoes had been reported but not confirmed, including multiple in Erath County.

LINK: North Texas Weather alerts

1:28 a.m. – Ellis County Emergency Management spokesperson Stephanie Parker told News 8 that some trees were down and some street flooding had occurred, but no major damage had been reported. The next update was expected around 8:30 a.m.

1:20 a.m. – Rio Vista ISD has announced a delayed opening Monday. Classes start at 10 a.m.

12:55 a.m. – The National Weather Service expanded the Tornado Watch, effective until 5 a.m., to include Kaufman and Van Zandt Counties.

12:10 a.m. – Tornado Warning for Ellis county until 12:45 a.m. The National Weather Service said tornadoes and half-dollar size hail were possible in the severe thunderstorm moving through the area.

A full-fledged “bow echo” was over Ellis and Hill Counties. As it moves east, winds could be 80mph.

11:55 p.m. – Johnson County Emergency Management reports flooding and water rescues. Roofs reportedly missing in Johnson Co.

A trailer was flipped in severe weather near Rio Vista.

11:45 p.m. – Thunderstorms are expected to make their way into Dallas/Fort Worth. A Severe Thunderstorm Warning was issued for Tarrant, Ellis and Dallas Counties until 12:45 a.m.

11:30 p.m. – Tornado Warning for Ellis, Hill and Johnson Counties until 12:00 a.m.
