Kerry Washington Covers Allure

October 17, 2014 Entertainment

ALLURE

The Scandal star is flawless on the cover of the beauty magazine’s November issue. Kerry Washington is the epitome of effortless beauty on the cover of Allure magazine’s November issue. The 37-year-old star of  Scandal celebrated the cover by tweeting the beauty and fashion magazine:

Proving that less is more, the Bronx, N.Y., native not only has very simple makeup but also keeps it even more subtle, but still sexy, with a sweater and a hint of cleavage. It’s no wonder she made People’s “50 Most Beautiful List” in 2013.

Washington, who’s already having a spectacular fourth season on the series, recently divulged onThe Ellen Show  a few scandalous secrets. She stated that she hated kissing her co-stars and said that aspect of her job will always be awkward.
