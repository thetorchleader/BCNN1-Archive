Usher’s Ex-Wife says Homosexuals Should Wear Wristbands because It Is Not Fair to Women

December 7, 2011 Uncategorized

Usher’s ex-wife, Tameka Raymond, recently took to Twitter to express her feelings about gay men on the “down low,” and how they should be identified – for the benefit of straight women with lousy gaydar.
Last month, the 40-year-old mother and stylist filed a petition against ex-husband Usher for sole custody of their two sons and additional child support. But the singer isn’t the only target of her rage.
She also sent out a message to gay men who look straight, saying they should identify themselves with “bright wristbands” rather than Timberlands, caps or vests.
After receiving angry backlash, Raymond attempted to clear the smoke by making it clear, saying she was not talking about all gay men but was referring to those who participate in “trickery.” But she defended her decision to speak her mind.
Raymond isn’t the only celebrity to speak out about closeted gay men. Rapper Game also discussed his feelings about men on the “down low” who, he said, are responsible for spreading diseases.
