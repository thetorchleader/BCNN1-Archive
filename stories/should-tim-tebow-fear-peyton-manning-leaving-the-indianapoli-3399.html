Should Tim Tebow Fear Peyton Manning Leaving the Indianapolis Colts?

January 27, 2012 Uncategorized

A new coach and a new general manager spell a new era for any team. That became reality when the Indianapolis Colts overhauled their entire staff, leaving Peyton Manning in doubt for staying, too. Should those like Tim Tebow fear his moving elsewhere?
Denver Broncos fans would only dump Tebowmania for Hall of Famer
It seems nothing can halt the runaway train that is the Tebow bandwagon. Denver Broncos fans clamored for him to get the starting job early in the season and got rewarded with an unforgettable string of late-game heroics that resulted in the team’s first playoff berth since 2005. Tebow was a hero who got the most out of a team considered not that good, even winning a playoff game against the Pittsburgh Steelers. So why would Broncos fans welcome the 35-year-old Manning? Perhaps his four MVP trophies, Super Bowl ring, and lengthy playoff experience are good reasons. The Indianapolis Colts hold the top pick in the upcoming draft and expect to draft Stanford quarterback Andrew Luck. With a new head coach in Chuck Pagano, the outlook of the team will show an air of change, leaving Manning as perhaps the hottest commodity on the market. Tim Tebow might not like letting go of the starting job, but Denver would do their fans a disservice if they didn’t at least try to bring Manning in.
New York Jets rebel against Mark Sanchez and welcome Peyton rumors
Two AFC championship appearances clearly aren’t enough to win over a team. That combined with accusations of laziness and entitlement has brought the ire of some New York Jets players to the boiling point on Mark Sanchez. Several made their feelings known via Twitter, claiming Sanchez doesn’t work hard enough since he has no competition for the starting job. Unless he shows a willingness to go all in with the team, those same players believe the Jets should pursue Peyton Manning. It’s not a long stretch of the imagination. New York boasts a strong defense, good offensive line, and a talented ground of wide receivers including two Super Bowl experienced wide receivers. If anyone could help pry the AFC East away from the New England Patriots, Manning is the one. Whether Mark Sanchez realizes this is another story.
