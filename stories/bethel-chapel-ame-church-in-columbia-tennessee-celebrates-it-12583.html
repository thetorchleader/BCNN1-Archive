Bethel Chapel AME Church in Columbia, Tennessee Celebrates Its Annual Homecoming

May 20, 2013 Uncategorized

Bethel Chapel A.M.E. Church, located at 1125 South Glade Street, Columbia, will celebrate its annual homecoming Sunday.

“This is a time that we invite current and former members to worship together and thank God for his blessings down through the years,” said Rev. Trent Ogilvie, Bethel Chapel pastor.
Services will begin with morning worship at 10:30 a.m. with Dr. Kita Williams as the speaker.
Kita Williams is originally from Columbia and is a former member of Bethel Chapel. She is the daughter of Willie Morgan and LaLeta Morgan. Upon graduating from Columbia Central high school, she enrolled in Tennessee State University where she graduated magna cum laude with her Bachelor of Science in Biology and a minor in Chemistry. She graduated from Meharry Medical College and completed her residency in Neurology at the Medical College of Virginia which was recognized by the American Stroke Association as giving the highest level of stroke care. She is currently working as a neurologist in a multi-specialty practice at Baptist Hospital in Nashville. Her professional memberships include the American Academy of Neurology, American College of Physicians, Tennessee Medical Associations, American Medical Association, Phi Kappa Phi Honor Society, Beta Kappa Chi Honor Society and Golden Key Honor Society.
Dr. Williams has received awards from the American Academy of Neurology Sanofi-Aventis, West End United Methodist Church, Pfizer, Minority Medical Tennessee Independent Colleges and Universities Association, UPS, Meharry Founding Brothers, Tennessee Medical Association, Women’s Missionary Society and the National Women’s Business and Professional Club.
Her research background includes stem cell research at both the University of Louisville and University of Miami.
