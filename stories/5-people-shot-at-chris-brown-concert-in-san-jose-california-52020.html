5 People Shot at Chris Brown Concert in San Jose, California

January 12, 2015 Entertainment

Chris Brown performs onstage at Power 106 FM’s Cali Christmas 2014 at The Forum in Inglewood, Calif., on Dec. 5. He was at Fiesta nightclub in San Jose, Calif., for a Jan. 10 performance when gunshots rang out, injuring five.

Chris Brown’s concertgoers were “getting it in” Sunday.

The R&B bad boy was performing at a nightclub in San Jose, Calif., when gunshots rang out, wounding five people, TMZ reports.

Brown can be seen onstage in an Instagram video obtained by the gossip site at Fiesta nightclub performing his hit “Loyal” at approximately 1:20 a.m. with his entourage behind him.

The 25-year-old singer, who was there hosting a Capricorn Bash birthday celebration, became distracted by an altercation taking place in another part of the club, causing him to stop mid-performance.

“Oh, them n—as getting it in over there,” he said on the microphone, smiling.

Brown’s grin quickly disappeared when the sound of gunshots filled the hot spot, sending the crowd into a frenzy.

“Oh my god, oh my god, there’s a gun. Somebody’s shooting,” one attendee could be heard saying in the video.

Utter chaos broke out as clubgoers tried to exit as quickly as possible. The “Deuces” singer, who was not hurt, was ushered away by his security team as soon as the shots were heard.

“Somebody’s shooting guns!” the attendee continued yelling as more shots were heard.

Four people were rushed to the hospital and one victim showed up at the emergency room later, according to San Jose’s Mercury News. All the victims are expected to survive.

While several people were detained by police it remains unclear as to whether there were any arrests.
