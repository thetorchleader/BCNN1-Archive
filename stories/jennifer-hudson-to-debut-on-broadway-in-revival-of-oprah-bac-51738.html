Jennifer Hudson to Debut On Broadway in Revival of Oprah-Backed ‘Color Purple’

January 9, 2015 Entertainment

The rumors are true: Jennifer Hudson is headed for the stage.

It was announced Friday that J. Hud will make her Broadway debut this spring, in a revival of The Color Purple that premiered at London’s Menier Chocolate Factory. That company has earned acclaim for bare-bones productions of big-time American musicals — as has the production’s director, John Doyle, known for his minimalist stagings of Stephen Sondheim classics such as Company and Sweeney Todd.

But this new staging of Purple will hardly be small-scale in one sense: Oprah Winfrey, a producer of the original Broadway staging in 2005, is once again on board.

Hudson will play Shug Avery, another sexy entertainer — albeit one who has a dark past and has fallen on hard times. She finds an unlikely friend in Celie, the wife of her old lover and Purple’s heroine. The part of Celie earned a Tony Award for LaChanze, who introduced it in New York — and praise for one of Hudson’s fellow American Idol alumnae, Fantasia Barrino, who played her in 2007-8.
