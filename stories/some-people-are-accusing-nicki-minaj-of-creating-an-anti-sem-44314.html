Some People Are Accusing Nicki Minaj of Creating an Anti-Semitic Video

November 9, 2014 Entertainment

(YouTube)

We love you, Nicki, but what on earth were you thinking here?!

On Friday, the Anaconda rapper debuted a militaristic lyric video for new song Only, which features Chris Brown and her Young Money labelmates Drake and Lil Wayne. But as sites such as Buzzfeed and Billboard have pointed out, many fans have accused Nicki of using “Nazi imagery” in the visual, including a swastika-like recreation of the Young Money logo.
