Festival of Praise Tour Is Coming Soon to a City Near You

May 20, 2015 Entertainment

The Festival of Praise Tour is bigger and better than ever! The tour was an enormous success last year and this year, this National Tour is scheduled to run from September 30-December 6, 2015 as a 50+ city tour!

The fantastic tour features Multiple Grammy, Stellar, BET Award Winning Artists Fred Hammond, Donnie McClurkin, Kim Burrell plus Hezekiah Walker & Israel Houghton
(in Select Markets!)

This spectacular, creatively-themed presentation also includes an ultra- talented, all-star VIP supporting vocal chorus featuring Isaac Carree, Jessica Reedy & Zacardi Cortez! And as if that weren’t enough, we’re also presenting a new, state-of-the-art production element along with an incredible Chicago- based live band!
