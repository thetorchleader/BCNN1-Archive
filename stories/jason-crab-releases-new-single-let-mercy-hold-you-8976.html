Jason Crab Releases New Single “Let Mercy Hold You”

October 29, 2012 Uncategorized

Christian music beloved artist Jason Crabb releases new single “Let Mercy Hold You.” New Crabb’s single can be found on https://itunes.apple.com/us/album/let-mercy-hold-you-single/id566704526.
The newly released song is from the upcoming album which is scheduled to release in 2013.
Jason Crabb is teaming up with Sandi Patty for ‘A Christmas Celebration Tour.’ Crabb and Patty will depart on ‘A Christmas Celebration Tour’ in November.
