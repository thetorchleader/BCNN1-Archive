Is Coffee Your Religion?

September 1, 2014 Lifestyle

by Adam Butchart

Have you ever woken up and thought today is going to be a drug-free day, then 20 minutes down the line you find yourself sitting in front of the TV with a huge cup of coffee in your hand?

What is so delectable and compelling about this bitter black beverage?

The most appealing feature about coffee is the buzz and jolt it gives you. After every cup you find yourself just that extra bit more energetic than you were at 7am.

For most, coffee helps us function, and without it we’re left looking like the living dead; groaning and moaning through the day until we finally crawl back into bed.

Most regular coffee consumers are unaware of the addicting property their favourite all-day drink possesses. Quite simply, coffee is a drug, at least the main ingredient is, anyway. The small coffee bean we grind up on a regular basis contains the drug caffeine.

Caffeine in its scientific form is a central nervous system stimulant drug responsible for that sudden buzz we experience after drinking every cup, or two, or three.

I am a coffee addict. I’ve loved the drink for years and I shamefully know I’ll love it for many years to come. Only recently have I discovered just how difficult it is to give it up.
