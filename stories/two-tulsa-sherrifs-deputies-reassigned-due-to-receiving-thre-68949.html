Two Tulsa Sherrif’s Deputies Reassigned Due to Receiving Threats in Wake of Fatal Shooting

April 21, 2015 National

Sheriff Stanley Glanz speaks during a news conference about the reserve deputy Robert Bates’ shooting of Eric Harris April 20, 2015. MIKE SIMONS/Tulsa World

The Tulsa County Sheriff’s Office has reassigned two deputies involved in the undercover sting that led to a fatal shooting earlier this month, Sheriff Stanley Glanz said at a Monday morning press conference.

Meanwhile, attorneys who represent the family of the man killed in the sting held an afternoon press conference that focused on an alleged void in the qualifications held by the reserve deputy who fired the shot.

Monday morning’s press conference was the first where Glanz talked at length and took questions about the fatal shooting, which occurred April 2 when Reserve Deputy Robert Bates. 73, mistook his gun for a Taser during a sting operation, killing Eric Harris.

The news conference followed the release on Saturday of official records detailing Bates’ training and certification. Bates served as Glanz’s re-election campaign manager in 2012 and has donated cars and equipment to the Sheriff’s Office.

Bates’ training and relationship to Glanz and the Sheriff’s Office as a donor have been a focus of the fallout.

Deputies Joseph Byars and Michael Huckeby have come under scrutiny for their actions after the shooting, which occurred after an undercover gun buy.

In a video of the shooting, Byars can be heard saying, “F— your breath,” to Harris as he lay on the ground after being shot, and Huckeby is seen kneeling on Harris’ head.

Harris had just said something about not being able to get his breath.

Glanz said the two deputies were reassigned due to threats they and their families have received.

“The deputies have been reassigned for their safety,” Glanz said.

“There have been some threats against them and their families. They have a family and kids, and I’m very concerned about their safety.”

He added that the deputies have not been suspended and that an administrative review of their actions in the recorded incident won’t start for some time.
