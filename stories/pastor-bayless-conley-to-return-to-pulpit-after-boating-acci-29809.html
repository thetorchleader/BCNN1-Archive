Pastor Bayless Conley Returns to Pulpit After Boating Accident

July 16, 2014 Christian

Bayless “Pastor B” Conley (Facebook)

After surviving a boating accident that left him in critical condition in January, Pastor Bayless Conley is returning the pulpit this weekend.

Conley is pastor of Cottonwood Church in Los Alamitos, California, and a speaker known for applying the Bible to everyday life through his television program, Answers With Bayless Conley. The broadcast reaches into more than 200 nations in the world.

“Due to the severity of his injuries, some of his doctors felt that it would be about 18 months before he could resume his preaching duties,” the church said in a statement. “It has been approximately five and a half months since he was airlifted from Catalina, where the accident occurred, to the emergency room in Los Angeles.”
