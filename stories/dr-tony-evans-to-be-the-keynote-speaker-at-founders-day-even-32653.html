Dr. Tony Evans to be the Keynote Speaker at Founders Day Event at The Brook Hill School in Bullard, TX in October

August 6, 2014 Christian

The Brook Hill School, located at 1051 N. Houston St. in Bullard, recently announced Rev. Tony Evans as the keynote speaker of the school’s annual Founders Day celebration Monday, Oct. 13.

“We are very excited to have Tony Evans as our keynote speaker at this year’s Founders Day celebration,” said the Brook Hill administration, in a press release. “Evans is one of the country’s most respected leaders in evangelical circles. He is a pastor and best-selling author, as well as a frequent speaker at Bible conferences, crusades and seminars throughout the nation.”

Evans has been the senior pastor of Oak Cliff Bible Fellowship for over 35 years, starting the church with his wife, Lois, inside their home in 1976. The church began to grow, beginning with only 10 people in 1976 to now over 9,000 congregants with 100 plus ministries.

The first African-American to graduate with a doctoral degree from Dallas Theological Seminary, Evans served as an associate professor in the school’s Pastoral Ministries Department in the areas of evangelism, homiletics and black church studies. Evans is also a board member of the National Religious Broadcasters and previously served as an associate evangelist with the Grand Old Gospel Fellowship inPhiladelphia.

Evans also serves as president of The Urban Alternative, a national ministry that seeks to bring spiritual renewal to America through the church. His daily radio broadcast, The Alternative with Dr. Tony Evans, can be heard on more than 500 stations throughout the United States and in more than 40 countries.
