Joel Osteen to Appear on ‘The Dr. Oz Show’ to Discuss the Healing Power of Prayer Thursday Nov. 17

November 16, 2011 Uncategorized

Dr. Oz asks Pastor Joel Osteen about what prayer means to him, the connection between spiritual health and physical health and if prayer really has the power to heal.
This week Joel Osteen sits down with Dr. Mehmet Oz to discuss the power of prayer and healing on the Emmy® Award winning and nationally syndicated “The Dr. Oz Show.” The show will air Thursday, November 17 (check local listings).
In the show, Dr. Oz asks Pastor Joel Osteen about what prayer means to him, the connection between spiritual health and physical health and if prayer really has the power to heal. Dr. Oz also calls on Joel’s wife, Victoria, to weigh in on how to encourage those who are going through a difficult time to believe in themselves and focus on the positive. Two other guests, Ana (Tomball, TX) and Angel (Bourg, LA) join Dr. Oz and Joel Osteen to share their stories on how they were healed and how Pastor Osteen’s messages helped strengthen their faith daily.
