We’ve Made Up – Beyonce and Solange Post New Instagram Picture

May 20, 2014 Entertainment

Despite rumors of beef after the infamous elevator incident involving Jay Z and Solange Knowles, everything is apparently well with the entertainer and her sister Beyoncé.

A new picture posted on Instagram, titled “May 17th, 2014″ shows the two sisters smiling while posing together.

The latest pic immediately conflicts with talk of friction between the Knowles siblings. The chatter was further heighted when it appeared Solange had removed almost all photos of her big sister from her Instagram account.
