Family Advocate says Obama’s Endorsement of Homosexual Marriage Reduces his Chances of Re-election

May 12, 2012 Uncategorized

Traditional values groups are continuing to react to President Barack Obama’s public endorsement of “gay marriage.”

The president made his historic declaration during an interview with ABC News, portions of which were released Wednesday afternoon. Reaction from conservative religious and family-advocacy groups was swift and widespread.
 
Peter Sprigg with the Family Research Council says the president has “finally ended his hypocrisy” on the issue. In an interview with The Associated Press, Sprigg notes Obama has opposed every method of preserving the traditional definition of marriage. The FRC spokesman also comments on the fundamental problem he sees in how pro-homosexual groups approach the marriage debate.
 
“[Advocates of same-sex marriage] always make it personal,” says Sprigg. “It’s always about the individual stories and the personal stories. Now those may be very heart-wrenching, but we define marriage based on the generalization rather than on the exception. It is a social institution, not a government entitlement program designed to benefit particular individuals.” (See AP video report with Sprigg’s comments)
