The Great Herschel Walker’s Son is a Championship Cheerleader

March 4, 2015 Sports

Herschel Walker’s son is following in his father’s supremely athletic footsteps … only he’s not on the football field … he’s a state champion male cheerleader … and his dad couldn’t be prouder!

The former NFL star was out in D.C. where he was bragging about his son’s Texas cheerleading squad winning the NCA National Championship in Dallas last month.

“I never knew how athletic those people were,” Walker says … “Now, I’ve been spending a lot of time with it. And I’ve gotten involved with it.”

Walker is a full-on “Cheer Dad” — and spends a lot of time mentoring the cheerleaders.
