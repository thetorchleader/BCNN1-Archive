Tony Perkins and Louisiana Pastors Rally Behind Rick Santorum

March 19, 2012 Uncategorized

Republican presidential candidate and former U.S. Senator Rick Santorum (center) receives a blessing from Pastor Dennis E. Terry Sr. (left) after being interviewed by Family Research Council President Tony Perkins (right) at Greenwell Springs Baptist Church in Greenwell Springs, La., on Sunday. 

Perkins organized an event to support Santorum the weekend before Louisiana’s Saturday GOP primary. More than 1,400 people–including nearly 100 pastors–showed up to rally behind the former Pennsylvania senator.

Source: Charisma News
