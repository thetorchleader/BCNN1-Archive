Happy 53rd Birthday, President Obama!

August 3, 2014 Politics

Obama, who turns 53 on Monday, kicked off his birthday weekend Saturday on the green before heading to his retreat in the Maryland mountains.

President Obama will celebrate his birthday with a round of golf.

Obama, who turns 53 on Monday, kicked off his birthday weekend Saturday on the green before heading to his retreat in the Maryland mountains.

The Associated Press reported the commander-in-chief teed off against childhood friends Mike Ramos, Bobby Titcomb and Greg Orme for an early morning round at Andrews Air Force Base in Maryland.

The foursome, who go back to the President’s days growing up in Hawaii, were joined by eight other golfers.

Later in the day, Obama (inset) boarded Marine One for a short helicopter flight to Camp David, the AP reported.

The President plans to return to the White House Sunday afternoon.
