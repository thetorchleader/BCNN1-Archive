Southern Baptist Leader, Russell Moore, Congratulates “Fellow Mississippian Jerry Young” on Election as National Baptist Convention President

September 5, 2014 Christian

Dr. Jerry Young, pastor of New Hope Baptist Church in Jackson, MS, was elected as the next president of the National Baptist Convention, USA, Inc. Messages of congratulations poured in on Twitter after the announcement was made on Thursday night. Among them was one from Russell Moore, head of the Southern Baptist Convention’s Ethics & Religious Liberty Commission.

Congrats to my fellow Missisippian Jerry Young, new president of National Baptist Convention USA. http://t.co/0PNBrfaAnv (HT @AMBankstw)

— Russell Moore (@drmoore) September 5, 2014
