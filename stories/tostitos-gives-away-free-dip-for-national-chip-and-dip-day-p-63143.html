Tostitos Gives Away Free Dip for National Chip and Dip Day (Providing Your Name Is “Chip”)

March 23, 2015 God & Food

TOSTITOS® Guacamole Dip

Today is National Chip and Dip Day and Tostitos is celebrating by giving away free dip, sort of.

Tostitos, one of the marquee brands from PepsiCo’s Frito-Lay division, is giving away one free dip to anyone in America named Chip. If your name is Chip, all you have to do is visit www.EveryChipGetsADip.com and submit a picture proving your name is “Chip.” You’ll then receive a coupon for a free Tostitos dip!

“We all know that chips and dip go together perfectly, but we began to wonder if we were somehow neglecting some of the most important Chips out there,” said Jeff Klein, vice president of marketing, Frito-Lay North America. “So on a day called National Chip and Dip Day, we couldn’t help but wonder … isn’t it about time all Chips get their dip?”
