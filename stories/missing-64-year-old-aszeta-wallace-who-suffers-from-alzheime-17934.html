Missing 64-Year-Old Aszeta Wallace, Who Suffers from Alzheimer’s, Found at The Potter’s House Church in Dallas

April 14, 2014 Uncategorized

Aszeta Wallace (Dallas Police Department)

Update at 1:26 p.m. on April 13 by Sarah Mervosh:

Off-duty police officers found Wallace Sunday morning at The Potter’s House Church. She “did not know where she was or where she had been for the last couple of days,” police said.

Authorities took her to Methodist Hospital as a precaution.

Original post on April 12:

Dallas police are asking for the public’s help in locating a missing 64-year-old woman who was last seen Saturday morning in Red Bird.

According to police Aszeta Wallace was last seen around 9 a.m. after she left her home in the 7300 block of Brierfield Drive.

Police say Wallace suffers from Alzheimer’s. She is described as a black woman, who is about 5-foot-1 and weighs 135 pounds.
