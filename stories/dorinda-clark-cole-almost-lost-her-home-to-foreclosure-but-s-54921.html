Dorinda Clark-Cole Almost Lost Her Home to Foreclosure, But Says ‘God Came Through For Me’

February 3, 2015 Entertainment

Credit: OXYGEN

There are currently 12,423 properties in the foreclosure process in the Detroit area, home of three-time GRAMMY® and six-time STELLAR award winner, Dorinda Clark-Cole.

Recently, the 57-year-old nearly lost her home, but said, “God came through for me,” and through the process, took her faith to another level.

“I never knew what faith really was,” she said in a statement obtained by EEW Magazine. “I preach about faith, I tell people about it, but I never really knew what faith was until this journey.”

She continued, “For over 25 years, I’ve said ‘as you go through trials, each trial gets harder. You have to go through the test, to have a testimony; you have to go through the mess, to have a message.’ If I never knew this to be true for myself, I absolutely know it now.”
