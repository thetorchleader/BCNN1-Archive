British School Teacher Jailed for 6 Years for Supporting Islamic State

March 5, 2015 Uncategorized

Jamshed Javeed, whose family begged him not to travel to Syria to fight with Islamist militants, was sentenced to six years in prison by a London judge for committing terrorist offenses.

While Javeed was arrested before he could make the journey, the 30-year-old provided significant funds and bought equipment to help four other men, including his brother, travel to the war-torn country, Judge Michael Topolski, said at a sentencing hearing Thursday.

Javeed “had been sufficiently radicalized and committed to a violent ideology,” Topolski said. “You were willing to fight and die for” the Islamic State.

Javeed is the latest Muslim to be jailed after a sustained push by U.K. law enforcement to head off a potential domestic terrorist attack by fighters returning from Syria or Iraq. Police arrested record numbers on Syria-related charges in 2014 as westerners continue to try to enter the area of the country controlled by the Islamic State.

“Whether you believed you were fighting in a just cause or not is irrelevant,” Topolski said. “Because the law is clear, this was terrorism.” After his release from prison Javeed will be on extended bail for three years.

Javeed, who had been a chemistry in Manchester, England, said he wanted to fight against the Assad government in Syria and to provide humanitarian support.

“I always found Javeed to be one of the more thoughtful and least dogmatic of the prisoners I met,” Moazzam Begg, a former inmate in Guantanamo Bay who met Javeed while they were both in a U.K. prison, said in a letter handed into the court. “He’s not the sort of person to impose his views on others, or is he a defender of terrorism.”
