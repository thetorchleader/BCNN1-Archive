Nun Who Won Italy’s ‘The Voice’ Sings Madonna’s ‘Like a Virgin’ in Debut Single

October 23, 2014 Entertainment

Sister Cristina Scuccia is back and better than ever!

After winning the first season of The Voice Italy, the Catholic nun has released her first music video: a cover of Madonna‘s classic hit “Like a Virgin.”

And before you start scratching your hands about the song choice, she has a good explanation.

“Reading the text, without being influenced by previous interpretations, you discover that it is a song about the power of love to renew people [and] rescue them from their past,” she told the Italian newspaper Avvenire. “We have transformed this song from the pop-dance piece, which it was, into a romantic ballad.”
