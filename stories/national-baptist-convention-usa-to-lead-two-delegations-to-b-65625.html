National Baptist Convention USA to Lead Two Delegations to Baptist World Congress Meeting in Durban, South Africa

April 6, 2015 Black

Please join the National Baptist Convention, USA, Inc. on a journey of a lifetime to
the 21st Baptist World Congress, Durban, South Africa.

We are sponsoring two delegations:

Delegation 1:  July 20-28, 2015

  • Led by Dr. Calvin McKinney, General Secretary, and Dr. Clarence Moore, Southeast Region Vice President
  • Visiting Johannesburg, Durban and Soweto
  • Price:  $3399.00
  • Deadline for $500 deposit:  May 15, 2015
  • Price does not include $250 Conference Registration.  Click here to register for the Congress
  • Click here to download the flier for this trip

Delegation 2:  July 20-30, 2015

  • Led by Dr. William Thompson, International Relations Vice President
  • Visiting Johannesburg, Durban, Soweto and Blantyre
  • Price:  $4899.00
  • Deadline for $500 deposit:  May 15, 2015
  • Price does not include $250 Conference Registration  Click here to register for the Congress
  • Click here to download the flier for this trip

 

The Baptist World Alliance sponsors the annual Baptist World Congress meeting.  The NBC, USA, Inc. is a member of the BWA and supports its work.  The BWA Affirmations are as follows:

Mission

Networking the Baptist family to impact the world for Christ

Vision

The Baptist World Alliance is a global movement of Baptists sharing a common confession of faith in Jesus Christ bonded together by God’s love to support, encourage and strengthen one another while proclaiming and living the Gospel of Jesus Christ in the power of the Holy Spirit before a lost and hurting world.

 

Source: National Baptist Convention USA
