How to Handle Shut Doors

November 28, 2011 Uncategorized

by Corey L. Brown
November 28, 2011
Click here to read previous articles by Corey L. Brown

“When they came to the border of Mysia, they tried to enter
Bithynia, but the Spirit of Jesus would not allow them to” (Acts
16:7).

There comes a time when you have to change your focus. Often times we
have our eyes set on doing certain things, but for some reason God
doesn’t allow them to happen. Paul and his crew are spreading the
gospel to the Gentile community, and they have intentions of going to
Bithynia. They had a desire to enter this area, but the Spirit of Jesus
wouldn’t allow them to do so. As a result of being denied, they
went down to Troas where they changed their focus to the town of
Macedonia. They had plans of going to Bithynia, but Paul received a
vision that turned their attention to Macedonia.

When your plans don’t work out the way you desire sometimes you
have to change your focus. When things don’t go according to plan
sometimes you have to think about doing something different. When you
find difficulty in achieving what you’ve set out to accomplish
you have to envision going another route because God may have something
else in mind.

I believe in persistence, but some things aren’t meant to happen.
I believe in perseverance, but some things aren’t meant to take
place. You have to discern when it’s time to change your focus so
you won’t waste any valuable time.

If you’ve been working to achieve something and it’s yet to
happen, maybe God is trying to get you to concentrate on something
else. If you’ve been trying to accomplish something and it
hasn’t taken place, maybe God has blocked it in order to redirect
your attention. Every denial is not a disappointment. It only means
that God has something better in store for your life.

For further study, please read Acts 16:6-10.
