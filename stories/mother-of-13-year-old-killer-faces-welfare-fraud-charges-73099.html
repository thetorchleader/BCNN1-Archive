Mother of 13-Year-Old Killer Faces Welfare Fraud Charges

May 12, 2015 Black

Anita Lawhorn, the mother of Kent County’s youngest murder suspect, was booked into Kent County Jail today on felony welfare fraud charges for allegedly bilking the government out of more than $88,000. 

The mother of 12-year-old Jamarion Lawhorn is already facing trial on child abuse charges for beating her son a year before Jamarion allegedly killed 9-year-old Connor Verkerke at a Kentwood playground.

Lawhorn, 38, was supposed to appear in Kent County Circuit Court today on motions related to her upcoming abuse trial. Instead, she appeared in Grand Rapids District Court for arraignment on two counts of welfare fraud over $500, a felony punishable by four years in prison.

A probable cause hearing on the welfare fraud case is set for March 23 in Grand Rapids District Court. Coincidentally, it is the same day she was scheduled to appear for trial in Kent County Circuit Court on the child abuse case.
