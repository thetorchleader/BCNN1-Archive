Evangelist Stephen D. Pettit Sr. Named as New President of Bob Jones University

May 10, 2014 Christian

Steve Pettit (BJU.edu)

A longtime evangelist will take over the helm of Bob Jones University here, the first president not named Jones.

Stephen D. Pettit Sr., 58, of Pembine, Wis., replaces Stephen Jones, the founder’s great-grandson who will end his nine-year tenure Friday because of health problems. Jones announced his resignation in December, which in turn launched a months-long search for a new president to lead the school founded in 1927 by Bob Jones Sr.

The search committee started with 130 names before narrowing the list to a handful of candidates who were all “solid men evangelically” with “solid credentials,” said Larry Jackson, chairman of the university’s Board of Trustees.

“Our hearts kind of focused on one very quickly,” he said.

The board reached a decision late Saturday and by Thursday afternoon had elected Pettit as Bob Jones University’s fifth president. Hours later, he was introduced to the private university’s faculty and students for the first time to a standing ovation.

Before becoming president, Pettit had been a member of the university’s Board of Trustees.

Pettit, a graduate of The Citadel with a degree in business administration, spent the past 29 years as president of the Steve Pettit Evangelistic Team, traveling the world on more than 800 campaigns and crusades. He also is national director for Cross Impact Ministries, a Brookfield, Wis., group that helps local churches reach out to college students. Pettit grew up in Columbia, S.C., the oldest of four children of parents who met while in the Air Force.

He spoke of his role at the university during an unprecedented time in which he said change must be embraced with courage and humility.

He considers his main responsibility to ensure that the university fulfills its theological mission within the cultural and the academic soil of liberal arts higher education, he said. The evangelical Christian university has more than 3,000 students.
