Dr. William J. Shaw to Keynote National Baptist Convention’s Faculty Fellowship Banquet

April 22, 2014 Christian

Dr. William J. Shaw

The Faculty Fellowship Banquet will be held on Tuesday, June 17, 2014 at 5:30 pm at the Sheraton Dallas Hotel. The Keynote Speaker is none other than Dr. William J. Shaw, pastor of the White Rock Baptist Church, Philadelphia, PA and the immediate past president of the National Baptist Convention, USA, Inc. Known as “a preacher’s preacher,” Dr. Shaw’s presentation is sure to educate, provoke, encourage and challenge you to pursue excellence in your Christian development and journey on this side of heaven. Tickets are $50 and can be purchased in advance.

Dr. Ralph E. Johnson, Faculty President │ Dr. Cheryl Stuart, Vice President, Dallas, TX
