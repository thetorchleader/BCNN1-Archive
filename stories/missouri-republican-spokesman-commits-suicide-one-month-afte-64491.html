Missouri Republican Spokesman Commits Suicide One Month After Boss Killed Himself

March 30, 2015 National

Spence Jackson, media director for late Missouri Auditor Tom Schweich, was found dead of suicide Sunday. (PHOTO CREDIT: Fox2Now.com)

A top Missouri Republican spokesman killed himself over the weekend just four weeks after his boss, the frontrunner to nab the GOP nomination for governor, also committed suicide.

Spence Jackson served as media director for state auditor Tom Schweich, who shot himself Feb. 26 at his Clayton, Mo., home.

Jackson, 44, took a sick day Friday and was found dead Sunday evening of a self-inflicted gunshot wound at his Jefferson City apartment, police said.

Jackson had served with Schweich since May 2011, and had recently called for the ouster of Republican state Chairman John Hancock, whom Jackson accused of starting an anti-Semitic “whisper campaign” that Schweich was Jewish, according to the St. Louis Post-Dispatch.

Jackson was a veteran of state politics, serving as communications director for then-Secretary of State Matt Blunt in the early 2000s and at the Missouri Department of Economic Development.
