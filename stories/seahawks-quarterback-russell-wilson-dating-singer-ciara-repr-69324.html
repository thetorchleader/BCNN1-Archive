Seahawks Quarterback Russell Wilson Dating Singer, Ciara; Is Reportedly Taking her to the White House Correspondents Dinner

April 23, 2015 Sports

from @MLB
.@DangeRussWilson trying to be incognito at the @Rangers-@Mariners game.

Seahawks quarterback Russell Wilson has become a fixture at the annual White House Correspondents Dinner.

When Wilson arrives at the White House on Saturday, he will reportedly have the company of one of the most popular musicians in the world.

According to the Washington Post, Wilson will attend the event with Ciara (Full name: Ciara Princess Harris), who he has been romantically linked to since being spotted at a Mariners game together.

Rumors of the pair’s romance first bubbled to the surface last weekend when Wilson was spotted at a Saturday Seattle Mariners game trying to look inconspicuous in dark sunglasses (and what, no uniform?) sitting right next to a woman who conspicuously looked a lot like Ciara.

Neither party has confirmed or denied their coupledom, but thanks to AOL, it’s out there. Ciara is Russell Wilson’s “date” to the White House Correspondents Dinner and the streets will be watching. This is the same woman whose recent single “I Bet” is a Taylor Swiftian sendup of her ex-fiance, the rapper Future, with whom she has an 11-month-old son: “I bet you start loving me. As soon as I start loving someone else. Somebody better than you.”
