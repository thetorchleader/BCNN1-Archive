Dr. Frank E. Ray Sr. and New Salem Church Launch Mortgage Pay-off Program

October 23, 2014 Christian

Dr. Frank E. Ray, Sr.

Mortgage Pay-off Program
Please join the members of New Salem in the church mortgage pay-off program. You may pay $1, $2, or $3 a day for the next 2 years and all funds will go toward the mortgage. You don’t want to miss out on this opportunity for us to work together and eliminate this debt.
