Phylicia Rashad says ‘Forget These Women’; Thinks the Whole Thing Is Orchestrated

January 7, 2015 Black

NEW YORK, NY – NOVEMBER 05: Phylicia Rashad arrives at the ‘Theory Of Everything ‘ New York Screening at Lighthouse International Theater on November 5, 2014 in New York City.
PHOTO BY DAVE KOTINSKY/GETTY IMAGES

Many celebrities have come forward in support of Bill Cosby, including Whoopi Goldberg, Jill Scott and Keshia Knight Pulliam, after multiple  women claim that he drugged and sexually assaulted them. Cosby’s newest supporter is now giving her take on the situation, and thinks that it’s a well executed smear campaign.

Phylicia Rashad, who played Claire Huxtable on The Cosby Show, spoke to Showbizz 411 during a luncheon for the much applauded film Selma. Rashad, who has seemingly remained silent until the interview, is standing by her longtime colleague and friend.

“Forget these women,” she said. “What you’re seeing is the destruction of a legacy. And I think it’s orchestrated. I don’t know why or who’s doing it, but it’s the legacy. And it’s a legacy that is so important to the culture … Someone is determined to keep Bill Cosby off TV, and it’s worked. All his contracts have been canceled.”

Rashad, like many of Cosby’s celebrity and non-celebrity supporters, is attempting to discredit the women who have come forward, including former supermodels Beverly Johnson and Janice Dickinson. Rashad has also shown support for Cosby’s wife Camille, whom she referred to as a ‘tough woman’.
