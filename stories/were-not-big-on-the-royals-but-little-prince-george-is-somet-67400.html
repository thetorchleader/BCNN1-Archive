We’re Not Big on the Royals, but Little Prince George is Something Else

April 14, 2015 God & Family

In this photo provided by The Duke and Duchess of Cambridge and taken in late Nov. 2014, Britain’s Prince George poses for a photograph in a courtyard at Kensington Palace, London. (AP Photo/TRH The Duke and Duchess of Cambridge)

Prince George is proving to be one smart cookie!

While he may not even be two years old, Kate Middleton’s little one demonstrated his clever thinking skills during a recent mother-son lunch.

During a meeting with the owner of Naples Luxury Travel Advisors Claudia Gordon, the Duchess of Cambridge revealed Prince William was out of the country on business. What came next was pure brilliance from Prince George.

“[Kate] told me that his daddy, Prince William, was visiting China,” Gordon told Florida’s News-Press. “After hearing this, he went to the china cabinet, opened it and proclaimed ‘daddy is not there.’ She said they would work on his geography.”

Not your average child, right?!
