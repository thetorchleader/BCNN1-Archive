NY Jets Need to Give Tim Tebow More Chances to Spark Gang Green’s Offense – Ntarting Monday Night Against the Texans

October 8, 2012 Uncategorized

So far, the TebowCat offense has been a bust. In four games, Tebow has thrown one pass, completing it for nine yards. He has run nine times for 38 yards. Tebow gets in games for a play here or there, which destroys any chance Sanchez has to develop a rhythm and gives Tebow no chance to succeed.
The Jets enjoy creating drama with their player introductions for prime-time games, so you can only imagine what they might have in the works for Monday night.
Just a couple of years ago, they introduced the defensive starters before the season opener against the Ravens, and then saved the best player for last.
Darrelle Revis, who had just ended a contentious 36-day holdout, came running out through a cloud of smoke, and was greeted with a standing ovation as his teammates surrounded him at midfield. It was a nice reward for holding the Jets hostage all summer.
What can the Jets possibly do before the game against the undefeated Texans that can help fans take their minds off what looks like a season that is about to go into an irreversible tailspin?
How about they turn out the lights, blast some Springsteen through the stadium, then shine a spotlight on the field as Tim Tebow emerges through the tunnel riding on a white horse to save the season?
The Jets, who are 2-2 — but it feels like 0-4 — seriously need an energy boost after losing Revis and Santonio Holmes, their two best players, in back-to-back games the last two weeks. The offense has no weapons, the defense can’t stop the run or get off the field on third down and quarterback Mark Sanchez is hanging on for dear life to keep his starting job. If the Jets get blown out by the Texans, the pressure will intensify on Rex Ryan to start Tebow at home against Andrew Luck and the Colts on Sunday.
So far, the TebowCat offense has been a bust. In four games, Tebow has thrown one pass, completing it for nine yards. He has run nine times for 38 yards.
Ryan needs to take this one step at a time.
