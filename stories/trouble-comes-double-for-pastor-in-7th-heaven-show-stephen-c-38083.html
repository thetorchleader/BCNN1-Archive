Trouble Comes Double for Pastor In ‘7th Heaven’ Show, Stephen Collins

October 8, 2014 Entertainment

Stephen Collins (Photo: Matt Sayles, AP)

The fallout continues for Stephen Collins, whom we all knew as the solid, sweet minister dad on 7th Heaven.

But that, of course, was just a TV role.

Fans are seeing him in a new light after TMZ obtained a sad and shocking audio tape featuring Collins, 67, discussing three instances of child molestation.

It’s part of a nasty divorce Collins has been going through with actress Faye Grant. When he filed papers in 2012 after 27 years of marriage, she said she was “devastated.”

On the tape, Collins is heard talking to Grant and to a therapist about exposing himself to underage girls. One was 11 when he started, and Collins says it continued when she was “12 and 13.” That particular girl is thought to be a relative of his first wife, Marjorie Weinman.

“There was never any conversation. … There one moment of touching,” he says, “where her hand, I put her hand on my penis.”

TMZ says Collins never knew he was being taped. Grant did it to gather evidence for a possible felony, and molesting a child under the age of 14 qualifies.

But Grant, on Tuesday, reacted with surprise as well. “I woke up today to learn that an extremely private recording I handed over to the authorities in 2012 per their request in connection with a criminal investigation was recently disseminated to the press,” she told E! News, denying any involvement with the release of the tape.

The Associated Press reports that New York police have an open investigation into allegations that veteran TV actor Stephen Collins molested a teenage girl in the early 1970s. Police spokesman Stephen David says the complaint was filed in 2012 accusing Collins of forcing a then-14-year-old girl to commit a lewd act in 1972. David says the case remains open, but no charges have been filed.
