Tom Brady Writes Sweet Message to Retiring Model Wife, Gisele Bundchen

April 16, 2015 God & Family

© Jim Davis/The Boston Globe via Getty Images
Tom Brady paid a heartwarming tribute to wife Gisele Bundchen, who is retiring from modeling.

Tom Brady and Gisele Bundchen have a remarkable relationship, at least if social media is any sort of indication.

The power couple has had no hesitation when it comes to expressing their love, whether it’s on Instagram or Facebook, and that shows no signs of slowing down. With Gisele retiring from the modeling game, it was Brady’s turn to show his wife his admiration, respect and love for what she’s done.

Brady was in attendance Wednesday as Gisele walked the runway one final time. He thenposted a message to his Facebook page, congratulating her and declaring her the “GOAT” — the greatest of all time.

Brady wrote:

Congratulations Love of my Life. You inspire me every day to be a better person. I am so proud of you and everything you have accomplished on the runway. I have never met someone with more of a will to succeed and determination to overcome any obstacle in the way. You never cease to amaze me. Nobody loves life more than you and your beauty runs much deeper than what the eye can see. I can’t wait to see what’s next. I love you. ‪#‎GOAT‬ ‪#‎thebestisyettocome‬
