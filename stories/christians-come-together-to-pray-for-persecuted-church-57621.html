Christians Come Together to Pray for Persecuted Church

February 19, 2015 Christian

Christians worldwide are being persecuted. On March 14 Christian leaders and people of faith from around the country will come together in Orlando with the theme of Rebuilding the Wall.

Franklin Graham, Mike Huckabee, James Robison, Rick Santorum and many more will speak at

The Awakening 2015
March 14, 2015, 8:15 am – 5:15 pm
Faith Assembly – 9307 Curry Ford Rd. – Orlando, FL 32825

“Watching what is happening to Christians in the Middle East, it is only a matter of time before Christians start experiencing terror in America. To change America’s course, Christians across America are standing up to be counted!” said Mat Staver, Founder and Chairman of Liberty Counsel.

“The Awakening 2015 is not just another conservative rally. It is a day set aside when we will come together, hear from our spiritual and political leaders, and then pray for America and Christians worldwide!” Staver pointed out.

“A war is waging. We cannot stand idly by,” Staver concluded.

To find out more, go to TheAwakeningUSA.org or call 1-800-671-1776.

About Liberty Counsel
Liberty Counsel is an international nonprofit, litigation, education, and policy organization dedicated to advancing religious freedom, the sanctity of life, and the family since 1989, by providing pro bono assistance and representation on these and related topics.
