Union and Wade Want Top Secrecy at Their Wedding

August 29, 2014 Entertainment

The wedding of Gabrielle Union and Dwyane Wade is probably going to be a spectacular bash, but we’ll never know.

The high-profile couple wants to keep the details on the down-low. They are asking all guests to sign a confidentiality agreement and to leave their cellphones and cameras at home, according to BET.
