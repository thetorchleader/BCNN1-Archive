Police in Indiana Break Car Window, Use Taser on Black Man in Front of his Children

October 7, 2014 Black

Jamal Jones being arrested by Indiana officers after being tased and dragged out of the vehicle
YOUTUBE

Hammond, Indiana police have come under fire after one of their officers were caught on video smashing open the passenger window of the vehicle and tasing the man in the passenger seat, while his family watched, Fox32 reports.

According to the report, it was supposed to be a simple traffic stop for not wearing a seatbelt. Jamal Jones and his girlfriend Lisa Mahone, along with Mahone’s two children 14-year-old Joseph and 7-year-old Janiya, were driving from Hammond to a Chicago hospital where Mahone’s mother was reportedly dying.

“I said ‘oh my God, he’s pulling me over like I robbed a bank,'” Mahone said. “The whole situation was just crazy.”

Mahone, who was driving, gave the Hammond officers her license and insurance card, but then things took an ugly turn when they asked Jones for his ID. Mahone prompted her son to start recording from his cellphone in the backseat, as Mahone called 911, Fox32 reports.

“I gave him my license and insurance. I also let him know at the beginning to please hurry up because my mom is about to die,” Mahone reportedly told the emergency operator.

Jones, for his part, said that he did not have an ID on him as he had just gotten a ticket. He reportedly reached back to get the ticket from his bag, which was on the back seat, when the cops started to pull out their guns.

“I’m scared for my life,” Mahone said frantically to the operator. “He just pulled a gun on us and we don’t have a gun!”

“I don’t know you and I don’t know what you’re going to do,” an officer allegedly told Jones.

“That’s why I have my windows up. I’m not no harm to you right now. I got my kids in the car and you’re drawing your weapon,” Jones responded.
