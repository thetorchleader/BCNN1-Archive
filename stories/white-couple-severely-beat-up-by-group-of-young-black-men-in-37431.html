White Couple Severely Beat Up by Group of Young Black Men in Missouri

September 6, 2014 Black

Just when it appeared things might be calming down in the state of Missouri, a disturbing story comes to the fore about a group of young black men who viciously assaulted a white couple in Springfield. 

And the attack was caught on surveillance video.

The brutal attack on a woman and her boyfriend happened on Friday, August 22 in downtown Springfield, and the Springfield Police Department released the video as they seek leads to identify the perpetrators.

The couple, Meredith Cole and her boyfriend Alex Vessey, were both treated at a hospital for serious injuries after the ordeal.

According to KY3 News,  Cole said she was standing outside of the Outland Ballroom where Vassey was working as a DJ, when the group of men approached and began to sexually assault her.

She said she went inside the club to find Vassey and alert him to the assault.
