Bishop Donald Hilliard Jr. and Dr. Randal Pinkett Hold Dynamic Townhall on Fatherlessness in Black and Latino Communities

April 23, 2014 African-American, LCNN1

“Do You Know Your Daddy?” Townhall

The “Do You Know Your Daddy?” Townhall discussion at the Cathedral International Church in Perth Amboy, NJ, tackled important issues surrounding single-parent homes, including how fatherlessness impacts employment, education, social behavior, crime, and incarceration. 

The discussion was hosted by Dr. Randal Pinkett who is President and CEO of BCT Partners and an active member of First Baptist Church of Lincoln Gardens in Somerset, NJ.

Bishop Donald Hilliard, Jr., moderated the discussion and was joined by a distinguished panel including: Chris Broussard (ESPN sports analyst), Dr. Obery M. Hendricks (Professor of Biblical Interpretation at New York Seminary), Tyrone Muhammad (Director of the Cotton Funeral Home), Fredrica Bey (Executive Director of WISOMMM Holistic Preschool Center), and Kevin E. Taylor (Co-Pastor of Unity Fellowship Church NewArk).

Cathedral International live streamed the event and it was also broadcast on BCNN3.TV.
