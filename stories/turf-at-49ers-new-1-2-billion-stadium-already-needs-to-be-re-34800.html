Turf at 49ers New $1.2 Billion Stadium Already Needs to be Replaced

August 21, 2014 Sports

Levi’s Stadium #49ers

It’s been four days since the 49ers played their first game at the new Levi’s Stadium, and part of the field is already being ripped up and replaced.

The stadium cost $1.2 billion to build. But, you know, those first four days are the toughest on the turf.

From CSN Bay Area:

Coach Jim Harbaugh brought his team together less than an hour into practice Wednesday evening and abruptly led his team out of Levi’s Stadium due to poor field conditions.

Basic plays provided huge divots, and there were patches of sod that were still discolored from the 49ers’ game Sunday against the Denver Broncos.

General manager Trent Baalke, standing on the sideline, was clearly agitated by uneven turf, which has yet to take root. And when wide receiver Stevie Johnson fell hard after slipping on a simple out-pattern during a 7-on-7 drill, Harbaugh brought his team together near midfield to play it safe.

If the grass needs time to “take root,” why did they allow a preseason game to be played there?
