Ghanaian Archbishop Duncan Williams Blesses Paula White’s Third Marriage

April 28, 2015 Christian

Congratulations to my daughter, Paula White & son, Jonathan!
You will see the Glory of the Lord, the Excellency of our God…
http://ow.ly/MbgR4 #NeverStopBelieving #PapaLovesYou
(ARCHBISHOP NICHOLAS DUNCAN WILLIAMS/FACEBOOK)

Popular televangelist Paula White got married over the weekend to Jonathan Cain in a ceremony that also had Archbishop Duncan Williams, head of the Christian Action Faith International Church in attendance.

This will be Paula’s third marriage after her divorce from second husband, Randy White in 2007. She is said to have been in a series of storms that have disturbed her since she and Randy divorced and even before. But she came out much stronger than before.

Paula married her first husband, Dean Knight when she was just a teenager and they had their son, Bradley while she was still in her teens. But In 1984, she became drawn to the gospel and turned Christian and she divorced Dean, soon thereafter.

She however seems to be filled with joy with her new marriage. Her husband, Jonathan has been in the successful music groups Journey and Bad English.
