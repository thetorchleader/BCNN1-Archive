New York Cab Driver Ordered to Pay $15,000 After Telling Lesbian Couple to Stop Kissing

April 12, 2015 National

© Amanda Hall/Getty Images
New York Taxi cab.

A cabbie has been ordered to pay $15,000 for telling a lesbian couple to stop kissing in his taxi.

Administrative Law Judge John Spooner last month ruled cabbie Mohammed Dahbi violated a city human-rights law when he told the women he wouldn’t keep driving if they continued to smooch.

“Keep that in the bedroom or get out of the cab,” Dahbi said during the 2011 ride from Manhattan to Brooklyn, one of the women testified during a civil trial.

Christina Spitzer and Kassie Thornton said they exchanged just a quick “peck” on the lips, and were upset by what they perceived to be discrimination because of their sexual orientation.

Spooner ordered Dahbi to pay the couple $10,000 and a $5,000 fine to the city.
