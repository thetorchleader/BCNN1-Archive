Make that 100 problems.

Jay Z got a beatdown at the hands and feet of Beyoncé’s hot-headed little sister while the three rode in a Manhattan hotel elevator — and video of the attack is burning up the Internet.

A furious Solange Knowles, 27, went berserk on her brother-in-law last week, letting her temper and fists fly as they prepared to leave the ritzy Met Gala after-party at the Standard Hotel.

The embarrassing clash was filmed by the elevator’s surveillance camera, footage of which was published by TMZ on Monday.

Queen Bey’s steamed sibling, a singer herself, can be seen screaming, kicking and clawing at Shawn (Jay Z) Carter in the video, recorded on May 5.

Beyoncé looked on and appeared concerned but didn’t intervene, and a bodyguard quickly hauled the combative Solange away from the music mogul.

The video did not feature audio.
