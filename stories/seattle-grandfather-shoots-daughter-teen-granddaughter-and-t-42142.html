Seattle Grandfather Shoots Daughter, Teen Granddaughter, and Then Commits Suicide

October 28, 2014 National

Seattle Police say 3 are dead in South Seattle after a 60-year-old gunman shot his adult daughter, her teenager daughter, and himself.
(Photo: KING 5 TV, Seattle-Tacoma)

Seattle police say three people have died in South Seattle after a man in his 60’s appears to have shot his adult daughter and teenage granddaughter before turning the gun on himself.

Assistant Chief Nick Metz said dispatchers received a 911 call from a 10-year-old boy who reported shots fired inside a house.

Within three minutes, officers arrived at the residence and found an elderly man, an adult female and a teenage female dead at the scene.

Metz said it appeared the two women were shot by the elderly man, and that the man took his own life. Metz added that the elderly man was the adult woman’s father and the teenage girl’s grandfather.

Metz said the 10-year-old boy managed to escape and was unharmed. Extended family members were taking care of the boy.
