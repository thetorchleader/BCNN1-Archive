Israel Prime Minister Netanyahu says Prospect Poor for Peace With Palestine

January 31, 2012 Uncategorized

Israel’s Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu attends the weekly cabinet meeting in Jerusalem on Sunday. Peace prospects with the Palestinians are looking poor, Netanyahu said on Sunday after exploratory talks aimed at relaunching negotiations ended in deadlock.
