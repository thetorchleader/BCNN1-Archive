Tim Tebow’s Star Power Still Intact In Big Apple

September 29, 2012 Uncategorized

The 49ers sound prepared for what defensive coordinator Vic Fangio calls “The Tebow Package.”
Tim Tebow’s output in his first three games with the New York Jets, however, would be better described as: “The Tebow Envelope.”
Tebow hasn’t attempted a pass. He hasn’t run wild in his seven carries, including only one last Sunday — on a fake punt. Mark Sanchez’s only pass to Tebow comically bounced off his helmet.
Tebow’s impact on the New York tabloids obviously has been bigger than his 26 offensive snaps for the Jets. Their offense ranks 22nd in yards per game (330.3), one spot below the 49ers (335.3).
Put those franchises together Sunday and you’ve got 2-1 teams still awaiting boosts from their offseason’s marquee additions: Tebow in the Jets’ case, and wide receiver Randy Moss in the 49ers’.
“They want to get the ball to Santonio Holmes, first and foremost,” 49ers safety Donte Whitner said. “They want to move Tebow around and do things to distract you.”
In preparing for Tebow and the Jets’ wildcat offense, the 49ers at least found a way to kill time during this week’s layover in Boardman, Ohio, a stay that likely seemed longer than last season’s because this one followed a 24-13 loss at Minnesota on Sunday.
Tebow has never threatened Sanchez for the Jets’ starting quarterback job, and any other full-time role seems out of reach for one of the NFL’s most popular players. Then again, he might get more playing time against the 49ers, and coach Jim Harbaugh is among those “more than curious.”
“You have to be prepared for it,” Harbaugh said. “That’s our job.”
