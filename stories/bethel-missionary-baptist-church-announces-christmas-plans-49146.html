Bethel Missionary Baptist Church Announces Christmas Plans

December 14, 2014 Black

Pastor Reginald & Lady Juanita Pope

Bethel Missionary Baptist Church plans several holiday programs in observance of the Christmas season.  According to Pastor Reginald A. Pope, all events will be held in the Bethel edifice at 10905 South Compton Avenue in Los Angeles.

On Sunday, December 14, at 3 p.m., the Pastor’s Care Team will host Pastor John Johnson and the members of Progress Baptist Church. Minister and Mrs. McKinley Moore are the co-leaders.

The 3rd Annual Christmas Acapella Musical takes place on Sunday, December 21, at 7:30 a.m.  “The choirs of Bethel will be dressed in black and white and share songs such as ‘O Come Let Us Adore Him’ and ‘Go Tell It On The Mountain,’” said Pastor Pope, who will bring the message.
