Seahawk Percy Harvin Traded to Jets

October 19, 2014 Sports

Percy Harvin has now been traded twice in less than two years.
(Photo: Jake Roth, USA TODAY Sports)

It took Percy Harvin less time to wear out his welcome with his second NFL team than his first.

In a stunning move Friday, the Seattle Seahawks traded Harvin to the New York Jets for a conditional mid-round draft pick, a person with knowledge of the move told USA TODAY Sports.

The person spoke on condition of anonymity because neither team had announced the trade, which was first reported by FOX’s Jay Glazer.

It comes less than two years after the Seahawks gave up a package of picks, including a first-rounder, to the Minnesota Vikings for Harvin, the temperamental play-maker.

The Seahawks acknowledged the trade Saturday issuing a statement on the deal.

“Although this was an extremely difficult decision, we are constantly evaluating our team and believe at this time, that this is in our best interest to move the team forward. We thank Percy for his efforts that contributed to a Super Bowl XLVIII victory and wish him well.”

SOURCE: USA Today – Tom Pelissero
