Gas Prices Are Down for July 4th Travelers

July 3, 2012 Uncategorized

As millions take to their cars this week for Fourth of July getaways, they will be receiving a rare gift when they head to a service station: gas prices that are an average 22 cents cheaper a gallon across the country than this time last year.

Self-serve unleaded averages $3.356 a gallon in the government’s latest weekly survey. The findings released today show that gas prices continue their steady drop, down 8 cents a gallon in the last week alone.
The West Coast saw the biggest single-week drop, with prices falling nearly 11 cents gallon to $3.715 a gallon. The lower Atlantic region now has the cheapest gas of any of the regions tracked by the Energy Information Administration. Gas there now averages $3.173 a gallon.
The lower prices could hardly come at a better time as the nation begins the peak summer driving season, kicked off by a big midweek Fourth of July vacation period.
