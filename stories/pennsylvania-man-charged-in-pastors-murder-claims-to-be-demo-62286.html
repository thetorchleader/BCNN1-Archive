Pennsylvania Man Charged In Pastor’s Murder Claims to Be Demon Killer

March 18, 2015 Christian

Roland Zinneh claims he kills demons. (Facebook)

The man was seeking counseling for voices in his head when he reportedly asphyxiated the pastor trying to help him.

Roland Zinneh of Pennsylvania has been charged with first-degree murder in the death of pastor Connery Dagadu of Maryland.

Reports say Zinneh believed Dagadu was demon possessed, and when police tried to drag him from the scene, he yelled, “Demons! I kill demons!”

Zinneh asked Dagadu to help him pray over the “voices” in his own head. Patch reports that sometime during the prayer meeting, Zinneh was convinced it was Dagadu, instead, who had the demons, and began to attack him.

Dagadu operated World Impact Center from a rented basement. When the homeowner heard the commotion below her, she went to the scene and found Dagadu unresponsive.

When police arrived, Zinneh was pacing and shouting, and reportedly broke a window when they approached him.

Zinneh is now being held without bond.
