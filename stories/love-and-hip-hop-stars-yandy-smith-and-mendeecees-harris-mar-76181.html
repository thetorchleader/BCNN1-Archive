‘Love and Hip Hop’ Stars Yandy Smith and Mendeecees Harris Marry

May 27, 2015 Black, Entertainment

These two just put the love in Love and Hip Hop!

After documenting the highs and lows of their relationship for Vh1, reality stars Yandy Smith and Mendeecees Harris finally said “I Do” Monday night in a special televised ceremony.

In front of their co-stars and a few familiar faces including Kandi Burruss, Ray J, Renee Graziano and Karen Gravano, the twosome exchanged heartfelt vows on live TV.

“You’re the most beautiful woman I’ve ever met,” Mendeecees said at the altar. “I’m honored today to stand here and be your future husband.”

Yandy added, “I prayed many moons and sunsets that I’d be standing right here where I am…If ever the world should turn its back on you, I will be right here by your side with no judgment…I’m a better me because you love me.”

Hosted by Nina Parker, Big Tigger and Stevie J, the two-hour celebration also featured performances from K. Michelle and plenty of awwww-worthy moments including Mendeeces’ son serving as the ring bearer.

And did we mention the bride looked quite stunning in her “bling Princess” Pnina Tornai dress chosen by Vh1 viewers? Nice choice, America.
