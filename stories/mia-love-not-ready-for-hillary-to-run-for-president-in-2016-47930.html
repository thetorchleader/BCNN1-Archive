Mia Love Not Ready for Hillary to Run for President in 2016

December 3, 2014 Politics

A newly elected political trailblazer is definitely not ready for Hillary Rodham Clinton.

Mia Love, the first black Republican woman elected to Congress, had a succinct response for PEOPLE magazine when asked whether she wants to see the former secretary of State running for president in 2016.

“Nope,” Love responded.

Although Love is getting lots of attention for being a “first” in politics, the lawmaker-to-be played down the historic nature of her election in Utah’s 4th Congressional District. “It’s certainly not why I ran,” she said. “I wasn’t elected because I’m female or because I’m a certain color. We wanted to make sure we were focusing on issues.”
