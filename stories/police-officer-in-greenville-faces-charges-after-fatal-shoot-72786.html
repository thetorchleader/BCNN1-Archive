Police Officer in Greenville Faces Charges After Fatal Shooting of Husband

May 10, 2015 Black

A Greenville police officer is facing charges after an off-duty shooting that killed her husband, state officials said.

Sgt. Kyvona Moore was arrested Saturday and initially charged with aggravated assault, according to the Delta Democrat Times. Those charges could be upgraded, authorities said.

Moore is accused of shooting her husband, Reginald Ladell Moore, 44, at the couple’s home. Moore was off duty at the time of the shooting, authorities said.

Source: WAPT
