T.D. Jakes and Dennis Prager Join Pastor John Hagee for 3-Day “Feast of Tabernacles” Celebration

October 24, 2014 Christian

Feast of Tabernacles/Sukkot Celebration at Cornerstone Church

Cornerstone Church in San Antonio, TX, is holding a three-day Feast of Tabernacles/Sukkot Celebration on October 24-26, 2014. Pastor John Hagee will be joined by guest speakers: T.D. Jakes of the Potter’s House in Dallas, radio host Dennis Prager, the Israeli Ambassador to the United States Ron Dermer, and Pastor Matthew Hagee.

An official announcement by Cornerstone Church promises “a special time of celebration with fun, food, fellowship, [a] midway full of games, and free rides.”

Each evening, will feature a guest speaker: T.D. Jakes will speak on Friday at 7:30 pm CST and Matthew Hagee will speak on Saturday at 7:30 pm CST. The event will wrap up on Sunday with a special “Night to Honor Israel” starting at 6:30 pm CST. Dennis Prager, Ron Dermer, and John Hagee will speak.

Musical guests will include Canton Junction, Ricardo Sanchez, Aaron and Amanda Crabb, and The Hagees.

Admission to the event is completely free, and those who are unable to attend may watch online at GETV.org. The event will be held at: Cornerstone Church, 18755 Stone Oak Parkway, San Antonio, Texas.

The Feast of Tabernacles is the conclusion of the Jewish Fall holidays following Passover and Yom Kippur. It commemorates the wilderness journey of the Hebrews after they were delivered from slavery in Egypt and later became incorporated as a celebration of the harvest in Israel.

Pastor Hagee is well known as a staunch ally of Israel. He founded Christian United for Israel, the largest pro-Israel organization in the United States. The group has over 1 million members and conducts 40 pro-Israel events per month.

–BCNN1
