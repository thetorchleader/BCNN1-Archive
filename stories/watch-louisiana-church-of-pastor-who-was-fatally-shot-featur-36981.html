WATCH: Louisiana Church of Pastor Who Was Fatally Shot Featured In “Preachers of L. A.”

September 4, 2014 Christian

The Preachers Of L.A. cast has been alluding that this season will be the season that will bring you into the trenches. Tonight Bishop Noel Jones will visit a broken-hearted church.They must deal with the violent murder committed in their sanctuary.

A Louisiana pastor was fatally shot as he preached to a crowd of more than 60 during a revival service and a suspect has been arrested, law enforcement officials said Saturday.

The shooting occurred at about 8:20 p.m.on a Friday at Tabernacle of Praise Worship Center in Lake Charles, according to Calcasieu Parish Sheriff’s Office. Sixty-five people were inside at the time, including the victim’s wife. Pastor Ronald J. Harris Sr. was shot twice by the gunman. The man went into the church while Harris was preaching and “walked up to him and shot him at close range,” according to police records. The suspect shot him again when Harris fell to the floor.

The pastor was pronounced dead at the scene.
