Janay Rice Is Not At All Happy that an Insensitive Man Is Dressing Up for Halloween in a Ray Rice Domestic Violence Costume

October 25, 2014 Black, Sports

Costume portraying the Ray Rice domestic violence incident
REDDIT

Tasteless and tacky are the only words to describe this costume.Halloween is right around the corner, and many of us are already celebrating early. But a recent photo posted on Reddit caught the attention of plenty of folks. One person decided to celebrate Halloween dressed up as Ray Rice while dragging along a black blow-up doll that is supposed to be Janay Rice. In the world of tasteless costumes, this man’s costume probably tops the list.

The Rice family has been under scrutiny after Ray was caught on camera brutally abusing his wife, Janay, in an elevator at an Atlantic City, N.J., hotel. Although Ray Rice initially received a two-day suspension, he was eventually suspended indefinitely after the videotape surfaced in August.
