Dr. Dre Buys Tom Brady’s Mansion for $40 Million

June 14, 2014 Entertainment

Music producer Dr. Dre has bought the Los Angeles estate of New England Patriots quarterback Tom Brady and wife Gisele Bundchen for $40 million, according to TMZ.

The 18,298-square-foot mansion, located in the hills above the Brentwood neighborhood of Los Angeles, is situated on 3.6 acres of pristine canyon land, beyond the noise and traffic below.

Designed by well-renowned architect Richard Landry and his Landry Design Group, the estate includes seven fireplaces, five bedrooms, nine bathrooms, a sauna, a gym and a library.

On the property there are waterfalls, an infinity pool and ponds. The grounds are irrigated with a gray-water system.
