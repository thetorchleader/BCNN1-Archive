Greg Laurie Invites Churches Across the U.S. to Host ‘Harvest America’ Event

March 28, 2012 Uncategorized

“Harvest America is a stadium outreach event, featuring live music from well-known Christian artists and a message of hope…The event will be streamed live across the nation via a network of host sites, as a culmination of the Anaheim Harvest Crusade.”

In a unique opportunity offered for the first time by Greg Laurie’s Harvest Crusades, churches and local communities across the country can now participate in the last night of their annual evangelistic crusade at Angel Stadium of Anaheim via broadcast and live streaming technology.
Churches, organizations, small groups and individuals are invited to host the free Harvest America outreach event in their communities. To get involved, visit HarvestAmerica.com.
WHAT: 
Harvest America is a stadium outreach event, featuring live music from well-known Christian artists and a message of hope from Harvest Christian Fellowship Senior Pastor Greg Laurie. The event will be streamed live across the nation via a network of host sites, as a culmination of the Anaheim Harvest Crusade.
WHEN: 
Aug. 26, 2012, 7:00 p.m. ET
WHERE: 
Live at Angel Stadium of Anaheim and broadcast to hundreds of locations across the U.S.

Angel Stadium of Anaheim
2000 Gene Autry Way
Anaheim, CA 92806
WHO:
A ministry of Greg Laurie, Harvest Crusades holds annual stadium events designed to share the Gospel in an environment that is contemporary, entertaining and nonthreatening. Over the last 22 years, more than 4.4 million people have attended Harvest Crusades. This year, Harvest Crusades will be held in Anaheim on Aug. 25-26 and in Los Angeles on Sept. 8-9.
REGISTRATION INFO: 
HarvestAmerica.com
SOURCE: HarvestAmerica.com
