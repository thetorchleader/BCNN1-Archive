LISTEN: Julian McPhillips, the Attorney for Alabama Church Members Whose Pastor Admitted to a Host of Sins, Talks to Roland Martin

October 19, 2014 Black

10/17/14- Roland Martin talks to Julian McPhillips, the attorney who represented church members of the Shiloh Missionary Baptist Church. The church has been in the headlines after its pastor Juan McFarland revealed he was having sex with members of his congregation and that he also has AIDS.

On Thursday, October 16, the embattled church pastor was temporarily banned from the church with the help of Attorney Phillips.
