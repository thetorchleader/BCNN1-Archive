Christena Cleveland says ‘Ferguson is the New Montgomery’; ‘Dominant Christian Culture Won’t Proclaim That Black Lives Matter’

November 25, 2014 Christian

Christena Cleveland

Christena Cleveland, a social psychologist, professor at Bethel University, and author of Disunity in Christ, responds to the Ferguson grand jury decision in a series of thought-provoking tweets.

Pastors: If you're NOT preaching on the God-Who-Loves-Black-Men-&-Suffers-With-Them this wknd, I don't know why you're pastoring in America.
