Ex-Marine is Banned from Maryland High School After Complaining About Daughter’s Islam-Related Homework

October 31, 2014 National

A Marine dad said he was banned from his daughter’s Maryland high school after he called the school and asked that she be temporarily removed from a course studying the virtues of the Muslim faith.

Kevin Wood, a former corporal and eight-year veteran in the US Marine Corps, finds himself under a no-trespass order for asking that his daughter should be excused from Islam homework in her World History class.

The World History class of La Plata High School in Maryland is at the moment studying about Middle Eastern empires. The study informs how different religions contributed in shaping various empires throughout the history of the world.

According to Charles County Schools, the World History class was made an essential part of the county curriculum. In addition to politics, culture, geography and other things, the students are also taught about these histories.

Kevin Wood’s daughter worked on a homework assigned by her school’s World History class. Her history notes were on the Islamic religion. Wood was initially upset because he did not want his daughter to participate in instruction about Islam.

The former marine called school hoping to get his daughter excused. Wood spoke first to an administrative assistant and then to Vice Principal Shannon Morris.

The Wood family requested the school authorities that these lessons should be removed from the assignments instead something else should be assigned to their daughter’s homework. But the school didn’t allow it.

The high school ultimately issued a no-trespassing order on Kevin Wood in the hope of avoiding a confrontation on school grounds. The order prevents him from setting foot on his daughter’s public school campus.
