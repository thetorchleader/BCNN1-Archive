San Antonio, TX Pastors Come Together for Rally After 18-Year-Old Boy Is Murdered

September 7, 2014 Black

Quinton Jones, 18, shot and killed in August

Violence at a Northeast Side apartment complex is prompting pastors in the community to rally together in an effort to curb crime.

The effort comes after a murder at the Springhill Apartments last month.

Quinton Jones, 18, was shot and killed in front of his apartment.

Jones would have been a senior at Roosevelt High School.

Jones’ family members said a special event will be held in his honor Saturday and will serve as an opportunity to reach out to residents.
