90-Year-Old California Pastor Continues Preaching after Seven Decades in the Pulpit

October 30, 2011 Uncategorized

The Rev. Earl Chambers walked toward the church with his Bible in his hand and his wife, Shirley, by his side. With each step, other worshippers called out and approached them.
One woman gave Chambers a big hug. “I’ve heard you preach all my life,” said Mary Gaines, 53, of Sacramento. “You knew my parents long before I came along, didn’t you?”
The question brought a smile to Chambers’ face.
“Yes,” he said. “I guess I have.”
There’s a good chance that worshippers who have attended Church of Christ services in Northern California anytime during the last seven decades have heard Earl Chambers deliver a sermon.
Last month, he celebrated 70 years in the pulpit and is one of the longest-serving ministers in the area. His work has spanned from Yuba City to San Luis Obispo to Grass Valley. He also had a television ministry for more than three decades.
Now he is in Sacramento, serving as interim pastor for a small church on Florin Road. It would be hard to find anyone in the Church of Christ who has preached more.
It has been a big year for Earl and Shirley Chambers – each recently turned 90. They received hundreds of cards from well-wishers thanking them for the examples they set.
In their twilight years, the Chambers are showing churchgoers that ministry doesn’t have to end with age. Churches are going gray – and they know soon more seniors will fill their pews and lead them.
“You don’t stop just because you turn a certain age,” said Chambers. “I’m not saying it’s easy, but it has been worth it.”
His partner in ministry broke into a smile.
“It’s the life we chose a long time ago,” said Shirley Chambers.
