Johnny Manziel Fails to Make a Move In Browns’ QB Race

August 19, 2014 Sports

Johnny Manziel had an opportunity Monday to make a move in the Browns’ QB race. (Geoff Burke, USA TODAY Sports)

The Cleveland Browns starting quarterback job was Brian Hoyer’s to lose entering Monday night’s preseason game against the Washington Redskins, barring a dramatic meltdown.

But Hoyer, who stood atop the depth chart locked in a virtual playoff with rookie Johnny Manziel to prove he deserved to hold onto his job come Cleveland’s Sept. 7 opener, failed to do anything to strengthen his grasp.

Instead, he left the door open for the hotshot rookie by going 7-for-16 passing for 65 yards, the bulk of which came on a second-half touchdown drive against the Redskins’ backups.

“It couldn’t have been any worse,” Hoyer said of his play Monday night.

It was all there for Manziel to work his bright lights playmaking magic and steal the starting job.

Manziel finally got his shot with the first-team offense after cornerback Joe Haden returned an interception of Washington quarterback Robert Griffin III for 37 yards to the Redskins’ 46-yard line.

And then … pffft, the air seemed to go out of Johnny Football, who struggled badly in his big chance to grab control of the first-team offense.

“I’m hard on myself, and I think I need to get a lot better,” Manziel said.

Manziel completed just 4 of his 11 passes for 38 yards with a sack. He hardly looked like the long-sought answer the Browns have been searching for since their 1999 rebirth.

Instead of making coach Mike Pettine’s highly anticipated Tuesday decision to name his starter harder, Manziel might have made it easier. He gave Pettine little choice but to stick with Hoyer, who returned later in the second quarter.

“He made some plays and I think there’s some plays he wants to have back,” Pettine said of Manziel.
