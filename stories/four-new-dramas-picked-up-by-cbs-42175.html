Four New Dramas Picked Up by CBS

October 28, 2014 Media

CCH Pounder, left, and Scott Bakula star in CBS’ ‘NCIS: New Orleans.'(Photo: Skip Bolen, CBS)

All four new CBS dramas are going to enjoy this plot twist: They’re getting full-season pickups.

The network announced on Monday it was ordering full seasons of freshman dramas Madam Secretary, NCIS: New Orleans, Scorpion and Stalker. Broadcast networks typically order 13 episodes of a new series, then decide whether to order “the back nine,” which creates a full season of 22 episodes.

All four new dramas have performed solidly in the ratings during the first few weeks of the broadcast TV season.

New Orleans and Scorpion rank first and second in viewers, respectively, among new shows this season. Each has benefited from a strong lead-in: New Orleans (18.2 million viewers) follows top-rated NCIS, the show it was spun off from, and Scorpion(15.2 million), a drama about geniuses, has performed well in a time slot after TV’s most-watched comedy, The Big Bang Theory, which is also about geniuses.

Madam Secretary is averaging 13.5 million viewers, making it No. 4 among new series, and Stalker‘s average is 9.5 million.
