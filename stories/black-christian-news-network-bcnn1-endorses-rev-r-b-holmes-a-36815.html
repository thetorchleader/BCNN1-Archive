Black Christian News Network (BCNN1) Endorses Rev. R.B. Holmes and Dr. Clifford Jones for President of the National Baptist Convention USA

September 3, 2014 Christian

R.B. Holmes / Clifford Jones

National Baptist Brethren, You Have a Hard Choice, but the Future of the National Baptist Convention, the Black Church, the Black Community, and America is at Stake

Black Christian News Network (BCNN1) endorses Rev. R.B. Holmes and Dr. Clifford Jones for president of the National Baptist Convention USA. Either one of these men have the moral authority, integrity, wisdom, and foresight to radically turn the National Baptist Convention back to its original purpose of evangelism and missions.

–BCNN1
