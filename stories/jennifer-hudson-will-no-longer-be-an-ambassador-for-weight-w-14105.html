Jennifer Hudson Will No Longer Be an Ambassador for Weight Watchers

March 16, 2014 Health

The 32-year-old songstress has announced she will no longer be an ambassador for the company after a successful run.

Four years and 80 pounds later, Jennifer Hudson and Weight Watchers have amicably decided to part ways.

The 32-year-old singer, who successfully slimmed-down on their program, is moving on after being the company’s spokesperson since 2010.

“My experience with Weight Watchers has truly been life changing. I am grateful for what I have learned throughout the process and will always take it with me,” Hudson said in a statement.

“I have chosen to step away from my role as ambassador and I am excited for the year to come, with many new projects and lots of new music.”

Despite Hudson’s exit, Weight Watchers is not left without an inspiring starlet.
