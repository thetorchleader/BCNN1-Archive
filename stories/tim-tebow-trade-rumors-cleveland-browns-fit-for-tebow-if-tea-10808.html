Tim Tebow Trade Rumors: Cleveland Browns Fit For Tebow If Team Misses Signing Joe Flacco

February 13, 2013 Uncategorized

The Cleveland Browns are one of the teams heading into the 2013 offseason that could be in need of a quarterback and Tim Tebow could be a solid option for the franchise if they decide not to pursue Ravens Super Bowl winning quarterback Joe Flacco.
According to Peter King at SI.com, while it is unlikely, there is a chance the Ravens will find Joe Flacco’s free agent price too high and allow him to leave to another team. The Browns could be in a perfect situation to be that team, as they could take a player from a rival and they have nearly $50 million to work with in cap space.
Baltimore can designate Flacco a franchise player, meaning the browns could sign him, but would have to forfeit a draft pick. The team has a lot of room to work with, which is another reason why Tebow could be a good fit. Flacco would cost a high price, but Tebow would be relatively cheap and the team could use the money to build a better roster.
Cleveland is currently under a new regime after owner Jimmy Haslam purchased the franchise and head coach Pat Shurmur and executive Mike Holmgren are out, with Rob Chudzinski and Joe Banner serving as CEO. The last group picked current quarterback Brandon Weeden in the draft and Tebow could serve as a new chapter for the franchise.
The Jets have yet to find a trade partner for Tebow, with the Arizona Cardinals, Philadelphia Eagles and Jacksonville Jaguars all looking like possibilities at first, but that has cooled. Arizona’s new coach Bruce Arians is skeptical of Tebow, while the Jaguars flat out said they would not acquire him. The Eagles looked like a possibility with Chip Kelly as coach, but after Mike Vick resigned, that move is unlikely now.
