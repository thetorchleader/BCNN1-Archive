WATCH: President says, ‘We’ve Got a Former Presidential Candidate (Romney) on the Other Side Who Suddenly is Just Deeply Concerned About Poverty’

January 30, 2015 Politics

President Obama and Mitt Romney during the 2012 election. (Photo: David Goldman, AP)

President Obama didn’t mention 2012 election opponent Mitt Romney by name on Thursday, but made his target clear as he mocked Republican talk about income equality.

“We’ve got a former presidential candidate on the other side who suddenly is just deeply concerned about poverty,” Obama said told House Democrats during an issues conference in Philadelphia.

“That’s great!” Obama said as fellow Democrats chuckled. “Let’s go! Come on! Let’s do something about it!”

Then another needle: “I am glad that their rhetoric at least has shifted, but let’s now make sure that the policies match up with the rhetoric.”

For his part, Romney — who is mulling another presidential run in 2016 — says Democratic emphasis on government programs and regulations have failed the poor.

In a tweet following Obama’s jab, Romney wrote: “Mr. Obama, wonder why my concern about poverty? The record number of poor in your term, and your record of failure to remedy.”
