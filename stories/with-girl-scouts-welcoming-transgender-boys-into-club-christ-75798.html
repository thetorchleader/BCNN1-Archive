With Girl Scouts Welcoming Transgender Boys Into Club, Christian-Based “American Heritage Girls” Alternative Gains Momentum

May 26, 2015 Christian

American Heritage Girls is a faith-based scouting organization. (American Heritage Girls/Facebook)

When Patti Garibay gathered her fellow mothers around her kitchen table in 1995, determined to create a faith-based community for their daughters, she had no idea their group would multiply by 400.

Twenty years later, 40,000 American Heritage Girls are celebrating life and liberty, redefining scouting into faith and service. While that thought is nice, their impact is startling.

In the last year, alone, AHG gave 6,000 hours of community service. Garibay says most of their alumnae choose service-oriented occupations, going into the ministry or nonprofits. As for the churches that sponsor the troops, Garibay says they see their memberships increase, as well.

“When people see the red, white and blue uniforms, they know we have a bias for action in His name,” Garibay says. “American Heritage Girls is countering the culture; our girls do things upside down, do them the way Jesus would have done.”

The organization is built on biblical values and challenging the culture. Garibay and a group of mothers started the community when the Girl Scouts of America added an asterisk to their promise in 1993, allowing the pledgee to replace the word “God” with whatever she personally believed.

For AGH, it’s not about the “girl power” culture is promoting, but God’s power.

“Our philosophy is biblical and timeless,” Garibay says. “It’s important for girls to look for their identity in Christ (and) understand that through Him all things are possible.”

Through this, the girls are charged with leadership projects that enable them to stand politically and culturally for Christ.

The most sought-after badge is the Respect Life patch, which focuses not just on the unborn, but the elderly and differently abled, as well.

“(This patch represents) God has a plan for each of us,” Garibay says. “It’s beautiful to see girls serve at pregnancy care centers or nursing homes, just to be the hands and feet of Christ.”
