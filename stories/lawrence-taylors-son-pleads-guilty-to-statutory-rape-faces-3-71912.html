Lawrence Taylor’s Son Pleads Guilty to Statutory Rape, Faces 30 Years In Prison

May 5, 2015 Sports

Lawrence Taylor Jr., the son of legendary football player Lawrence Taylor, pleaded guilty to statutory rape, child molestation and sodomy charges and was sentenced to 30 years in prison, according to a report.

LT Jr. was arrested in July, 2013 for rape charges after a 13-year-old girl accused him of forcing himself onto her to perform oral sex on her. Another girl at the scene told police that a year and a half earlier, LT Jr. had sex with her five or six times.

Taylor Jr. was born in 1981, which means he was 30 or 31 when he had sex with a 12-year-old girl. That means he was 31 or 32 when he forced himself onto the 13-year-old in 2013.

LT Jr. was sentenced to 30 years in prison and will be eligible for release in 10.

Sadly, LT Jr. is in trouble for a sex crime like his father. In 2011, the Hall of Fame linebacker pleaded guilty to lesser charges after he had sex with an underage prostitute.
