WATCH: Local News Report on Pastor Bobby Davis’ Death After Confessing Adultery in Front of Church; Police Review Video of Confession Recorded by Church Member

March 16, 2014 Christian

Before you make a judgment about this preacher, he was probably one of the good preachers who made one mistake. May God have mercy on the preachers who have committed adultery and who are still committing adultery and have not confessed it.

*** Click here to read the earlier report on this story.

In Connecticut, Bishop Bobby Davis admitted to his church about an affair he had then drops dead of an apparent heart attack.

Davis, pastor and founder of the Miracle Faith World Outreach Church in Bridgeport, had a lot on his mind on Sunday and wanted to come clean about it before the church’s congregation.

…….

Davis was pronounced dead at Bridgeport Hospital. The cause of death is still under investigation, according to police sources who spoke to the newspaper.

Meanwhile, the Christian Post cited an unidentified church member who said it wasn’t Davis idea to come clean on his own. The member said Davis confessed to his wife earlier, and she insisted that he tell the congregation. The revelation freaked out Davis’ children, who “got physical,” according to the Christian news outlet.

Police have interviewed dozens of witnesses and are reportedly reviewing video from inside the church that was taken by a member.
