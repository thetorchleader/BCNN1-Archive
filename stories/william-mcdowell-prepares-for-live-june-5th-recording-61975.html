William McDowell Prepares For Live June 5th Recording

March 16, 2015 Entertainment

William McDowell

William McDowell has established himself as one of the nation’s prominent praise and worship leaders.

Favorites like “I Give Myself Away,” “I Won’t Go Back” and “Withholding Nothing” continue to dominate the Sunday morning worship at churches across the country. Withholding Nothing grabbed three Stellar Award nominations for Praise & Worship CD of the Year, Traditional CD of the Year and Traditional Male Vocalist of the Year.

The Lead Pastor of Orlando based Deeper Fellowship continues to set the standard with the release of a new single, Send the Rain while making preparations for a live recording on June 5th in Baton Rouge, Louisiana.
