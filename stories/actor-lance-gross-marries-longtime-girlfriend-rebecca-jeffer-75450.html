Actor Lance Gross Marries Longtime Girlfriend Rebecca Jefferson

May 24, 2015 Entertainment

Michael Kovac

Lance Gross is officially a proud husband!

E! News can exclusively confirm the Crisis star and fiancée Rebecca Jefferson said “I Do” and got married Saturday afternoon in a romantic ceremony near Los Angeles.

The ceremony, which began around 2 p.m. local time, featured touching vows written by the bride and groom. Hennessy also celebrated the special wedding by gifting guests including Terrence Jenkins, Kelly Rowland and husband Tim Weatherspoon with their XO collection.

“It was a perfect night for the two and they couldn’t ask for a better wedding,” a source revealed to E! News. “Everyone was dancing the night away.”

While the Hollywood couple tried to keep things quiet on social media, Gross teased fans about his weekend outfit choice when he opted to take the “classic route” with head-to-toe Tom Ford.

Sources first confirmed to E! News that the pair was indeed engaged and expecting their first child together back in July 2014.

“GOD has Gifted me. It’s like I get to unwrap you daily,” he shared on Instagram shortly after the news broke. “Your presence continues to bring me joy each time without exhaustion. I AM IN LOVE WITH YOU.”
