WATCH: First Baptist Church in Macon, GA to Celebrate 10th Anniversary

April 11, 2014 Uncategorized

Pastor James W. Goolsby, Jr.

To celebrate the 10th anniversary of Pastor James W. Goolsby, Jr. and Sister Gloria Goolsby, the First Baptist Church in Macon is hosting several community-oriented events this month and next month.  Yuwanda Duval, a 10th anniversary celebration member, shared with us some details.
To RSVP, call (478)-745-8368, E-Mail FBC@FirstBaptistMacon.org, or go online to http://www.firstbaptistmacon.org.  Here are the events and the dates (the First Baptist Church is on 595 New Street in Macon):

Saturday, April 12th
3:00 PM – Sunday’s Best Fashion Show at the FBC Fellowship Hall

Sunday, April 27th
11:00 AM – Worship Service with Pastor Lonnie Fletcher from Orange Grove Baptist Church in Culloden, Georgia

3:00 PM – Worship Service with Pastor John R. Lacy from The House of Change Ministries in Riverdale, Georgia

Wednesday, April 30th
6:00 PM – Movie Night in Fellowship Hall at the First Baptist Church (no charge)

Friday, May 2nd
6:30 PM – Date Night with Pastor and Mrs. Goolsby at O’Charley’s Restaurant in Macon (3740 Bloomfield Drive)

Sunday, May 4th
11:00 AM – Worship Service with Minister Jon Woodard, son of First Baptist Church

4:00 PM – Worship Service with Pastor D. Black from Beulah Missionary Baptist Church in Decatur, Georgia

Source: 41 NBC News
