Bethany Baptist Church of West Los Angeles to Celebrate 57th Anniversary

May 17, 2015 Black

Dr. Rocellia Johnson

As Pastor L.A. Kessee and the members of Bethany Baptist Church of West Los Angeles prepare to celebrate their 57th anniversary on Sunday, May 17, much praise will go to God for leading the Rev. Rocellia Johnson to organize this ministry. 

The Rev. Johnson, who served 51 years as Bethany’s senior pastor, retired in 2009 and now holds the title of pastor emeritus.

But, he continues to share his time and talents with the church along with devoting time to the educational institution that he founded, Bethany Christian Bible College (BCBC).

Based on his background, God prepared Pastor Johnson through spiritual guidance, experience and education to make a tangible difference in the lives of believers.

“When I migrated to Los Angeles in 1941, I joined the Metropolitan Baptist Church, under the leadership of Dr. George H. Washington, Jr., and I began my theological studies at the Los Angeles Bible Training School under his tutelage,” said Pastor Johnson.

He went on to earn a Master’s Degree in Theology at Fuller Theological Seminary, a second Master’s Degree at the University of LaVerne, and certification in family counseling at UCLA Extension.

In 1978, Pastor Johnson founded Bethany Christian Bible College and according to Pastor Kessee, “Dr. Johnson established the school in an effort to build a well-informed congregation.  He sincerely believed that God’s people, when given the Biblical facts, will normally do the right thing.”

Pastor Johnson explained, “I believe in the stronghold of the church. James 1:22 says, ‘But be ye doers of the Word, and not hearers only, deceiving your own selves.’
