Mount Olive AME Church Women’s Conference Begins May 17 in Slidell, LA

May 13, 2014 African-American

Hands raised in prayer. (Archive photo by Ted Jackson, NOLA.com | The Times-Picayune)

Mount Olive AME Church Women’s Conference will be held May 17 from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. at 2457 Second St., Slidell. A special Women’s Day program also will be held May 18, 3 p.m., featuring Elder Michelle Goodloe as the guest speaker. The theme is “Sisters with Anointed Gifts (SWAG)”.

Submit information for the north shore Community Connection to cwolfram@nola.com.

Source: NOLA
