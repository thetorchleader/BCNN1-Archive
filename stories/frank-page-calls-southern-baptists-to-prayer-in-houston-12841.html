Frank S .Page Calls Southern Baptists to Prayer In Houston

June 7, 2013 Uncategorized

I ask for your prayers as Southern Baptists converge from all over the United States and even from around the world to meet in annual session for our convention in Houston, Texas. Always a time of great fellowship, fun, spiritual inspiration, and hopefully some needed sharing of information, we gather to do what Baptists have done for many decades — transact the business of the convention.
For many years, the North American Mission Board has been the primary sponsor of an evangelistic event which occurs prior to the convention. Called Crossover, thousands of people over the years have come to Christ as evangelistic Southern Baptists have gone out into the cities of the convention prior to the meeting in a variety of ways, settings, and formats to share the Gospel.
For many years, I have been a part of this emphasis. Yes, it would have been a required part of my job during my short tenure as vice president of evangelization at the North American Mission Board. However, I participated in this event when I was pastor prior to that role and have continued to participate in the current role I have, though vocationally it is not connected.
