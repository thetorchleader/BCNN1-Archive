Barnes & Noble to Shave $50 Off Nook if Customers Purchase Digital Subscription

January 10, 2012 Uncategorized

Barnes & Noble said Monday it will offer
its e-reader devices at a discount if buyers purchase a digital
subscription to People magazine or The New York Times.

The bookseller will
shave $50 off the normally priced $249 Nook Tablet for buyers who also
purchase a one-year Nook subscription to People magazine.

In
addition, it will offer a free Nook Simple Touch or $100 off a $199
Nook Color e-book reader for people who also buy a one-year subscription
to The New York Times.

The People
subscription is $9.99 per month and The New York Times subscription is
$19.99 per month. The offers are good until March 9.

New
York-based Barnes & Noble Inc. has been considering its options for
the Nook business, including possibly spinning it off. It is facing
tough competition from Amazon.com, which offered its Kindle Fire for
$199 and its Kindle e-reader for $79 over the holidays. Barnes &
Noble sold its Nook Tablet for $249 and its black-and-white Simple Touch
e-reader for $99. Demand for the Simple Touch reader lagged
expectations during the holidays, Barnes & Noble has said.

Still,
combined sales of Nook products were brisk, up 70 percent compared with
a year ago during the nine-week period ended Dec. 31. Digital content
sales more than doubled. The company expects those sales to total $450
million in fiscal 2012.

Shares of Barnes & Noble rose 63 cents, or 5.6 percent, to $11.82 in afternoon trading.

SOURCE: The Associated Press
