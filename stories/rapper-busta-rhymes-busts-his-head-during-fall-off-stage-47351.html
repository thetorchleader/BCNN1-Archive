Rapper Busta Rhymes Busts his Head during Fall Off Stage

November 29, 2014 Entertainment

@HipHopEarly
.@BustaRhymes fell off stage and cut his head. He’s OK now.
Video: http://goo.gl/I5lHES

Oh no! Busta Rhymes suffered a head injury mid-performance after nosediving into a huge crowd while performing in New York City on Nov. 28. We’re glad he’s OK!
Rapper Busta Rhymes apparently got a little carried away while performing at Webster Hall in New York City on Nov. 28. The 42-year-old rapper slipped off a platform while leaning his way into the crowd, and ended up leaving the stage with a wound to his head. So scary!

The Grammy nominated rapper made a surprise appearance at O.T. Genasis’s concert, which of course got the crowd going nuts. As he made his way into the pit, he leaned in too far causing him to slip off the platform and into the crowd, while banging his head. He rushed off the stage to treat his bloody injury, but never returned, according to TMZ.
