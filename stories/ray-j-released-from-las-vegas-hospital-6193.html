Ray J Released from Las Vegas Hospital

May 23, 2012 Uncategorized

Ray J, according to an email update from his publicist, Courtney Barnes, has been released from the hospital in Las Vegas and is back home in Los Angeles.
The singer/actor had been hospitalized since Monday morning where he underwent an extensive battery of tests to make sure that he did not have a blood clot in his lungs which is  potentially a deadly condition that can occur following a long haul plane flight.
