Jets Heading Home After Summer of Tebowmania

August 17, 2012 Uncategorized
Tebowmania is heading home, along with those top-secret wildcat practices.
The New York Jets wrapped up training camp at SUNY Cortland on Thursday after three weeks filled with headlines and plenty of preparations for the season. The team plays its second preseason game on Saturday night – as the home team – against the Giants.
Starting quarterback Mark Sanchez says he feels pretty good about where Tony Sparano’s offense is at, and agrees with teammates that he expects to be one of the NFL’s top 10 players at his position this season.
Meanwhile, Tim Tebow is happy with how he’s progressing as the Jets’ do-it-all backup quarterback. Tebow says he has been able to easily adjust to his role, including the wildcat offense, which New York practiced again Thursday.
SOURCE: The Associated Press
