Will the Duggars and the Robertsons Collaborate?

April 15, 2015 Entertainment

Jana Duggar, Jinger Duggar, Sadie Robertson and Joy Anna Duggar spend some time together. (Sadie Robertson/Instagram)

Girls will be girls, which means some quality time for the female stars of two of faith’s finest TV families: The Duggars and the Robertsons.

Sadie Robertson debuted a picture on Instagram with Jana, Jinger and Joy Anna Duggar.

“(T)ruly the most beautiful girls inside & out. excited about our new friendship. thank you for being so sweet to welcome me into y’all’s beautiful home,” Robertson captioned the photo.

Some speculate the photo is evidence of a crossover between A&E’s Duck Dynasty and TLC’s 19 Kids and Counting.

Fans of both shows are sharing the love on the social media post, though.

The photo garnered 196,000 likes.
