Adjunct Professor at Liberty University and Regent University, Kim Wilson, says the Only Safe Sex Is No Sex; Spreads the Message of Abstinence to Fight HIV/AIDS in Minority Communities

May 6, 2014 African-American

Kim Wilson, Founder of God’s Active People/G.A.P Ministries

A Tuscaloosa native has started a global campaign promoting abstinence in the fight against AIDS and HIV.

Kim Wilson, who now lives in Georgia, says the Loretta Johnson Global Abstinence Project seeks to spread the message through churches, particularly in the black and Hispanic communities.

Safe-sex campaigns aren’t working, said Wilson, who is also the founder of God’s Active People ministries. If safe-sex campaigns were working, the number of black and Hispanic women contracting HIV/AIDS and the number of out-of-wedlock births wouldn’t be high and rising, she said.

The only guaranteed way to lower those numbers isn’t safe sex, she said: It’s no sex before marriage.

And the best way to promote abstinence is through churches, Wilson said.

“Roughly 90 percent of black Americans profess to be Christian,” she said. “Our ethnic group, as well as Hispanics, go to church more than any other groups. But yet we’re not getting across to our children that sex before marriage is a sin.

“Somehow, our children think pre-marital sex is OK. Research shows that black and Hispanics pray a lot, but they’re not implementing Christian principles in their lives when it comes to sex. That’s why I think it’s important to get this out to churches.”
