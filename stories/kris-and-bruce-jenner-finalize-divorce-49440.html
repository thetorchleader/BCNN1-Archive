Kris and Bruce Jenner Finalize Divorce

December 18, 2014 Entertainment

End of an era. Kris and Bruce Jenner announced they are ending their marriage after 22 years. Here they are in 2009 in Las Vegas. (Photo: Dan Steinberg, AP)

It’s over. Bruce and Kris Jenner’s divorce papers have been signed by a Los Angeles county judge.

E! News and TMZ report that although the split is legit, the two won’t be officially divorced until March 23, 2015, because of a six-month legal requirement.

So who gets what?

According to the documents, Kris will have to pay Bruce $2.5 million as an “equalization payment,” reports E!

Kris keeps the family home.

Kris will keep her 2012 Rolls Royce Ghost, her 2014 Bentley and her 2014 Land Rover.

Bruce will keep his 2011 Porsche 97 Coupe, his 2013 Cadillac Escalade and his 2007 Harley Davidson motorcycle.

They each get to keep their own airline miles.

And the two will divide various bank account and other assets.

Kylie Jenner is only 17, so both parents will share joint and legal physical custody of their youngest daughter, but neither will have to pay child support.

Kris filed for divorce in September citing irreconcilable differences; she listed their separation date as June 1, 2013.
