2,000 Earthquakes Recorded Beneath Iceland Volcano; European Aviation Put on Notice

August 19, 2014 Second Coming Watch

Iceland volcano

Iceland issued warnings yesterday to the European aviation industry of a potential volcanic eruption after a weekend of intense seismic activity beneath its largest system of volcanoes.

The country’s meteorological office issued an orange alert, its second highest warning, after it detected the movement of magma beneath the Bardarbunga volcano in the centre of the country.

More than 2,000 earthquakes have shaken the Bardarbunga volcano since Saturday, when threat warnings were raised to yellow alert. It is the most intense “earthquake swarm” to affect Bardarbunga since 1996.
