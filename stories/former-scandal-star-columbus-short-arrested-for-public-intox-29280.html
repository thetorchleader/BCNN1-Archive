Former “Scandal” Star Columbus Short Arrested for Public Intoxication

Date not recorded in the original archive Entertainment

Fired Scandal star Columbus Short has reportedly been ordered to pay $21,000-plus a month in spousal and child support to his estranged wife Tanee Short Credit: Jason LaVeris/FilmMagic

Columbus Short was arrested for public intoxication Friday in Dallas, Texas, police confirm to PEOPLE.

The former Scandal star was booked after allegedly fighting with security at a local bar called Katy Trail Ice House, according to an employee.

A worker says the bar employs off-duty police officers, and Short, 31, “got into an issue” with them at about 2 a.m. Saturday.

“He was uncooperative with police,” the employee says. “It was a minor issue. He was verbally assaulting the police.”

Short’s lawyer and manager did not immediately return requests for comment.

The incident is the actor’s latest brush with the law. Short, who will not appear in the hit ABC show’s fourth season, faces a spousal battery case stemming from his estranged wife’s claims that he threatened to kill her in April. He also pleaded not guilty to felony battery in May after allegedly punching a man in a restaurant.
