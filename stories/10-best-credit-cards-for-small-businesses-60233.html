10 Best Credit Cards for Small Businesses

March 6, 2015 You Can Work...

Of all the various types of business financing available, many small-business owners need credit cards to keep their businesses afloat. Most business financing is secured, meaning the loan is tied to an asset, but credit cards are unsecured, which can be an attractive benefit for many business owners. In fact, according to the 2012 Year-End Economic Report from the National Small Business Association, 31 percent of small businesses used credit cards that year to increase their cash flow.

Technically, any credit card can be used to access quick buying power, but a business credit card offers many unique benefits to small-business owners. Business credit cards help keep personal and business expenses separate and organized, often offer higher spending limits and incentives — like cash back or travel rewards — and can give a small business an additional infusion of cash.

If you are in the market for a credit card for your small business, here are our top 10 picks:

1. American Express Blue for Business Credit Card

The Blue for Business Credit Card from American Express offers members ways to earn up to two times the points on purchases. Cardholders earn one point for every dollar spent on eligible purchases and two points for each dollar spent on certain purchases made through the American Express travel website.

Cardholders can also earn 30 percent more points each year with the annual Relationship Bonus. There is a current 0% introductory APR on purchases for nine months, with a variable APR of 11.24%, 17.24% or 19.24% afterward, based on creditworthiness.

2. Capital One Spark Cash Select

The Spark Cash Select from Capital One offers small-business owners unlimited 1.5% cash back on every purchase, and new cardholders who spend $3,000 in the first three months receive a one-time additional $100 cash bonus.

There is no annual fee for this card, making its unlimited rewards, free employee cards and a current introductory rate of 0% APR until August of 2015 and variable APR of 12.9% to 20.9% afterward even better.

3. Capital One Spark Miles Select

This card is similar to the Spark Select card, but with additional travel rewards. Customers can earn an unlimited 1.5 miles per dollar spent on purchases, and new cardholders who spend $3,000 in the first three months receive $10,000 bonus miles, equal to $100 in travel.

There is a current 0% APR offer until August 2015 and a 12.9% to 20.9% variable APR afterward. Like the Spark Select, there is no annual fee, employee cards can be issued at no additional cost, and the rewards have no blackout dates, seat restrictions or minimum requirement to redeem.
