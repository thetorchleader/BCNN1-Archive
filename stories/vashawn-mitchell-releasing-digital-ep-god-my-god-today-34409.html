VaShawn Mitchell Releasing Digital EP “God My God” Today

August 19, 2014 Entertainment

Just months after wowing audiences in-person and online with the live recordings for his upcoming full album, Unstoppable, VaShawn Mitchell has announced that he’ll release a 4-song EP, titled God My God, on all digital platforms on August 19, 2014.

The EP will include Mitchell’s rising new radio single, “God My God,” along with “Amaze Me,” featuring Anthony Brown, “This Is To You” and “Your Name…The Moment.” Taste-makers have said that it will be the perfect preview of VaShawn’s powerful Unstoppable album, due in stores later this year.

Mark your calendars and make sure you grab God My God on the 19th of August! Order it right here, right now.
