“It’s A Mann’s World” Moved to Wednesdays on BET

February 10, 2015 Entertainment

Join David and Tamela Mann and the Mann family at a new time beginning Wednesday, February 11 as their popular show “IT’S A MANN’S WORLD” takes over the 9:30 p.m. CST/10:30 p.m. EST spot on BET. What will the Manns be up to next? Tune in and find out!

Known for their starring roles on stage and screen, Tamela and David Mann have become household names for their #1 comedy, “Meet the Browns.” Their new show, “IT’S A MANN’S WORLD” takes a peek behind their celebrity on-screen personas, exclusive events, famous friends and the at-home lives of David and Tamela Mann. Over the course of eight one-hour episodes, viewers get a taste of how David and Tamela balance being celebrities, entrepreneurs, and parents to four adult children – all while maintaining a successful 26-year marriage. Viewers may have met the Browns, but this show introduces them to the Mann’s world like you’ve never seen them before.

The series is executively produced by David and Tamela Mann in partnership with eOne along with Tara Long, Howie Miller, Kim McKoy, Roger Bobb, and Phil Thornton.
