Tufts University Kicks Out Student After She Was Raped

June 19, 2014 Education

Wagatwe Wanjuki
COURTESY OF WAGATWE WANJUKI/MOREA STEINHAUER

After Wagatwe Wanjuki was assaulted, she was asked to leave Tufts University.

Wagatwe Wanjuki was sexually assaulted by another student while attending Tufts University. But instead of punishing her attacker, the school asked her to leave.

Wanjuki, 27, is the woman behind the widely trending Twitter hashtag #SurvivorPrivilege.

According to the Huffington Post, Wanjuki took to Twitter shortly after the June 6 publication of George Will’s Washington Post editorial in which he decried the Obama administration’s focus on campus sexual assault and suggested that being a survivor of sexual assault has become “coveted status that confers privileges.”
