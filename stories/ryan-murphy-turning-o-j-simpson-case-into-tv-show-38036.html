Ryan Murphy Turning O.J. Simpson Case Into TV Show

October 7, 2014 Entertainment

O.J. Simpson in court in 2008 in Las Vegas.
(Photo: Jae C. Hong, AP)

Imagine this: The O.J. Simpson story in the hands of the man who brought us Glee, American Horror Story and Nip/Tuck.

You won’t have to imagine for much longer. It’s happening.

John Landgraf, CEO of FX, announced today that Ryan Murphy has come up with his next project, American Crime Story.

Like his American Horror Story, which kicks off a new and sure-to-be-creepy Freak Show season on Wednesday, this will be an anthology focused each season on a different true-crime story.

First up: 10 episodes of American Crime Story: The People v. O.J. Simpson, based on the book The Run of His Life: The People v. O.J. Simpson, by Jeffrey Toobin.

Murphy is set to direct.

The first two hours of the miniseries will be written by Scott Alexander and Larry Karaszewski (The People vs. Larry Flynt, Ed Wood, Man on the Moon).

Landgraf says in the announcement that the adaptation is “masterful,” and says “it will be a spectacular first entry in what is destined to become a series of great true crime-based miniseries.”

Murphy said he’s been “looking for the right property which could serve as an extension of the American Horror Story brand I love so much. The O.J. case was as tragic as it was fascinating – it seemed like everyone had a stake in the outcome. It was really the beginning of the modern tabloid age.”

Production begins early next year in Los Angeles.

SOURCE: USA Today – Ann Oldenburg
