WATCH: Georgia Pastor Says His YouTube Account Was Shut Down Over Sermon About ISIS

September 4, 2014 Christian

Pastor Daniel Ausbun

A Georgia pastor whose YouTube account was shut down has had it reinstated – with help from a Fox News reporter.

Pastor Daniel Ausbun of First Baptist Church in Moreland has posted sermons on YouTube for three years until an August 24 sermon on radical Islam and the ISIS terrorist group.

He tells OneNewsNow that he told the First Baptist congregation about the persecution of Christians in Iraq and Syria, and how ISIS was recruiting in the United States, relying on information from Fox News, CNN, and ABC News.

“It was almost like a news report. It was a very educational message,” he says of the sermon (see video below).

Two days later, Ausbun received notice from YouTube that his account had been terminated because the pastor broke the “terms of service.”
