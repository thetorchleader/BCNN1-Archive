Lolo Jones In Misunderstanding with Paralyzed Eric LeGrand

October 3, 2012 Uncategorized

Lolo Jones does not know who paralyzed former Rutgers football player Eric LeGrand is until she tweets he should be checked ‘for a concussion’ because he must have ‘been hit in the head.’ Suffice to say, she’s since learned.
Track star Lolo Jones claims she didn’t know who Eric LeGrand was. She does now.
LeGrand (@EricLeGrand52), the Rutgers football player paralyzed in an October 2010 game, sent a fun tweet to the Olympian Monday challenging her to a race.
Jones (@lolojones) responded, “Get Checked for a concussion. Clearly, u’ve been hit in the head… Cos u arnt beating a track athlete.”

LeGrand didn’t respond, but someone named @finae25 sent Jones a tweet saying, “u know he’s a quadrapalegic now?”
Eric LeGrand (l.) with NFL commissioner Roger Goodell.
Jones then went on defense, tweeting, “No, dnt know. sry dont know every football player. He challenged me 2 a race & I responded.” and then later “Great I’m gonna get murdered for that tweet. When I had no clue who that dude was. Just responding to any athlete who challenges me to a race.”
