WATCH: Christian Rapper Bizzle Calls Jay-Z an Illuminati Puppet

March 18, 2014 Entertainment

Christian rapper Bizzle speak out on why he thinks the Illuminati is real, explaining that he believes there are people behind the scenes “pulling strings.” When asked if he thinks it has anything to do with the devil, Bizzle says that the message that’s being sent out is the work of the devil, but he doesn’t think that it’s just straight devil worshiping.

He also opens up about Jay Z and his perceived connection with the Illuminati, claiming that the rapper isn’t at the table with the leaders, but is rather being used as a puppet for their work. Bizzle also believes that Kanye’s “Yeezus” album was a “mockery,” adding that because of his religious beliefs, he’s obviously going to have a problem with the album name.
