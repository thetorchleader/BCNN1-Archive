This broadcast is not about predictions, nor is it about setting dates as some foolishly have done, however it is all about preparation.

The Bible says in Colossians 3:4: “When Christ, who is our life, shall appear, then shall ye also appear with him in glory.”

PROPHECY BOOTCAMP: Our topic for today is titled “Worthy is the Lamb!” (Part 10) from Dr. Ed Hindson’s book “Revelation: Unlocking the Future”.

The worship of the Lamb is based upon His sevenfold worth to receive:

1. Power (“unlimited ability”)
2. Riches (“unconditional wealth”)
3. Wisdom (“mental excellence”)
4. Might (“superhuman power”)
5. Honor (“praise of character”)
6. Glory (“majestic radiance”)
7. Blessing (“thanksgiving”)

Each of these qualities is recognized in the Lamb Himself. They are also blessings that He bestows. But their emphasis here is on the qualities He possesses. They begin with a focus on His power and might and move to the more usual sequence of doxologies: honor; glory, and blessing. These rapid-fire expressions of praise and worship leap from the lips of the angelic host with the spontaneous recognition of who He is and what He has done. These thrilling crescendos lead to the grand finale of praise.
