Jameis Winston Files Counterclaim Against Accuser of Sex Assault

May 9, 2015 Sports

Tampa Bay Buccaneers quarterback Jameis Winston filed a counterclaim Friday against the woman who accused him of sexual battery, assault, false imprisonment and inflicting emotional distress after a 2012 incident. 

Winston captured the Heisman Trophy and national championship in 2013, posting a 27-1 career record at Florida State. The former collegiate star was selected first overall by the Buccaneers in the 2015 NFL Draft.

In his counterclaim, Winston alleges that his accuser’s accusations are “false, defamatory … and have maliciously and impermissibly interfered with Mr. Winston’s business and personal relationships.” He is counter-suing for damages in excess of $75,000.

Winston’s accuser filed her lawsuit against him on April 16 and is a former student at Florida State.
