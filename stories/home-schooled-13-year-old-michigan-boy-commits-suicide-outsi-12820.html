Home-Schooled 13-Year-Old Michigan Boy Commits Suicide Outside of Former Public School

June 6, 2013 Uncategorized

A teenager from Dowagiac, Michigan, took his life by a fatal gunshot in a wooded area adjacent to Dowagiac Middle School on 03 June 2013.

The boy has been identified as Darren James Price, 13, according to Tom Atkinson, the Dowagiac Public Safety Director.
Earlier in the day, Price was spotted by a school employee on the school grounds with a handgun. The employee, a custodian, said he saw the boy around 6:40 am, and notified the Principal after an unsuccessful attempt to make contact with him and ran into the woods.
Police were called shortly afterward and formed a perimeter around the area. It wasn’t long after that a single gunshot was heard.
Police found Price in the woods with a self-inflicted gunshot wound. He was transported to Borgess-Lee Memorial Hospital in Dowagiac, but died from his wounds.
Atkinson stated that there is no evidence Price was acting with another party or parties, but the investigation is ongoing. There was no immediate evidence available as to motive, and Atkinson stated,
“We may never know why this happened.”
There is currently no correlation to suicide rates and home-schooled children, and in spite of reports to the contrary, a higher percentage of home-schoolers enter and complete college than brick-schooled children.
