More Than 100 Texas Churches Seek to Fill McLane Stadium for Palm Sunday “Gathering”; Dr. Tony Evans to Be Main Speaker, Israel Houghton to Lead Worship

March 9, 2015 Christian

Pastors across Waco hope to show the world that churches can unite under one banner this Easter season by filling McLane Stadium in the first countywide church service held in Waco.

The Gathering will be at 5 p.m., Palm Sunday, March 29, at the 45,000-seat stadium in Waco with more than 100 churches from across Central Texas participating in the free two-hour worship service. Gates will open at 3 p.m.

The Rev. Ramiro Peña of Christ the King Baptist Church said the church receives a lot of criticism for its many divisions among denomination and racial lines.

Peña, who leads The Gathering’s organizational team, said they chose “unity” as the theme from John 17:20-21 where Jesus prays over the disciples and speaks about how unity among Christians convinces nonbelievers that Christ is real.

“Our effort is to send a clear message that we’re endeavoring to unite across racial and denominational lines. Especially when there seems to be so much racial division in America today. We want to say the Church of Waco is united,” he said.

Peña said he began planning the service after Drayton McLane Jr., the businessman and benefactor after whom the stadium is named, approached him at the beginning of football season about hosting an Easter service similar to the one held at Minute Maid Park in Houston by Joel Osteen and Lakewood Church.

Peña called local pastors, and the Waco service grew to include pastors from as far as Dallas and Austin who said their congregations plan to attend.

“I think if you have something you ought to use it to its fullest,” McLane told the Tribune-Herald. “I thought it would be a great Christian experience if we could have 30,000 to 40,000 people in Texas (worship at the stadium together). I like big ideas.”

The Rev. Tony Evans of Oak Cliff Bible Fellowship will be the main speaker, and Grammy Award-winning Christian artist Israel Houghton & New Breed will provide worship.
