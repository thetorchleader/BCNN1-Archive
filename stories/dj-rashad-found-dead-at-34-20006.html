DJ Rashad Found Dead at 34

April 28, 2014 Entertainment

DJ Rashad/promotional photo / Via Facebook: 152577002604

Chicago house music producer and footwork icon Rashad Harden was found dead Saturday afternoon on Chicago’s South Side. Rashad, 34, was found unresponsive in an apartment at 1:30 p.m., and was pronounced dead shortly after, the Chicago Police Department told BuzzFeed. Drugs and drug paraphernalia were found near his body, and police suspect a drug overdose. There were no visible signs of injury, and an autopsy is scheduled for Sunday.
