Lady Gaga, R. Kelly to Perform Following Obama’s Speech at Selma

March 7, 2015 Entertainment

On Saturday, President Obama will give a speech to commemorate the anniversary of the Selma-to-Montgomery march (which is marked each year by the Annual Bridge Crossing Jubilee).

If the president of the United States isn’t enough of an A-lister for you, the event has just added more names: Lady Gaga and R. Kelly. The two will reportedly perform after Obama’s speech, but we’re guessing they won’t reprise their uber-sexual duet “Do What U Want” this weekend.

T.I., Young Breezy, We are Toonz, Bigg Robb, the Impressions and Toia Jones from The Voice will also perform. Others, including Bono, Ruben Studdard, former president George W. Bush and members of the cast of Selma, will also be in attendance.

SOURCE: Billboard
