LIVE COVERAGE: At Least 20 Students Injured in Stabbing Spree at High School in Pennsylvania; Suspect in Custody

April 9, 2014 National

Franklin Regional High School in Murrysville, Pa., where stabbings have been reported, April 9, 2014. (WTAE)

A student suspect was in custody after at least 20 students were stabbed Wednesday morning at western Pennsylvania’s Franklin Regional Senior High School, authorities said.

WTAE-TV in Pittsburgh reported at least four medical helicopters transported victims, ranging in age from 14 to 17, from the school to hospitals. Dan Stevens, a spokesman for Westmoreland County emergency management, said none of the injuries appeared to be life threatening.

Information on what led to the stabbings and the conditions of the injured were not immediately released. Stevens said detectives are interviewing the suspect, a student at the school, as to a motive in the stabbings.

A student at the school told WTAE that students heard alarms for a fire drill, then screams from the stabbings.

The school is in Murrysville, about 15 miles east of downtown Pittsburgh. The school was releasing students to their parents after the incident, Stevens said.

WTAE says the incident happened about 5:20 a.m. MDT in the science wing of the school.
