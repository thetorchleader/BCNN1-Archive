Tragic – Oregon Family Killed in Car Crash While On Their Way to Church Retreat, Little Boy Left Orphaned

October 21, 2014 Christian

Three members of the Fefelov family from Tigard, Ore., were killed on Saturday when their vehicle collided with an oncoming SUV. Pictured, from left: Mila, 7 months, Tiffany, 27, Fred, 34, and Ewan, 5. Ewan Fefelov was not involved in the accident.

Tiffany and Fred Fefelov were killed, along with their 7-month-old daughter, in a head-on crash in rural Oregon Saturday. Their son, 6, was left orphaned as his family died on the way to a church getaway.

An Oregon couple headed to a church retreat died in a head-on crash Saturday when their SUV veered into oncoming traffic along a rain-slick road.

That crash also killed their 7-month-old daughter, orphaned a 6-year-old boy and shook an entire religious community.

State police said Tiffany Fefelov, 27, lost control of the 2010 Dodge Caliber on U.S. 20 in Santian Pass, Ore. Tiffany, husband Fred Fefelov, 34, and 7-month-old Mila Fefelov, died after colliding with a Chevy Suburban hauling a 32-foot trailer, The Oregonian reported.

“The family is beyond devastated and at a loss for words to express their extreme sadness of the loss of such a beautiful family,” Stephanie James wrote on GoFundMe, where relatives are seeking to fundraise burial expenses.
