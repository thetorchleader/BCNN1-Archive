All About Learning Press, Inc. Sponsors Titus 2:1 Conference

May 3, 2014 Bring the Children Home

“We don’t just sell products; our goal is to help parents teach reading and spelling.”

Publisher of All About Reading and All About Spelling sponsors upcoming blogging conference for homeschooling parents.

Eagle River, Wisconsin (PRWEB) May 01, 2014

All About Learning Press, Inc. (AALP), publisher of the award-winning All About Reading and All About Spelling programs, is pleased to be a sponsor of the upcoming Titus 2:1 Conference, a faith-based conference for homeschooling parents who blog.

Linda Difino and Kendra Ahlborn will represent All About Learning Press, Inc. at the conference and will offer information to bloggers who are interested in potentially joining the AALP affiliate program. With one of the strongest affiliate programs in the industry, AALP offers a 15 percent commission, a lifetime cookie, banners for website or blog use, partnership and support, and how-to videos and articles that illustrate ways to benefit from the affiliate program.

In 2013, AALP’s top 20 affiliates earned between $600 and $15,525 while helping other educators. “As a company, we don’t just sell products; our goal is to help parents teach reading and spelling,” explains author and curriculum developer Marie Rippel. “We are looking for affiliates who know and love our products, and who want to share what they know with other parents.”

The All About Reading and All About Spelling programs are based on the Orton-Gillingham instructional approach. These comprehensive programs use multisensory lessons to teach students the way they learn naturally—through sight, sound, and touch. With a mastery-based and sequential approach, All About Reading and All About Spelling are suitable for all ages and learning styles, and the programs are backed by lifetime support and a 100 percent money-back guarantee.

The Titus 2:1 conference will be held at the Hilton Indian Lakes Resort in Bloomingdale, Illinois on May 2-4, 2014. Keynote speakers include Heidi St. John, Marianne Sunderland, and Ashley Wiggers. For more information, visithttp://www.2to1conference.com.
