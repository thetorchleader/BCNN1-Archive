Craig Gross Launches Video Series for Christian Couples to Have Better Sex

January 17, 2015 Christian

Craig Gross

Dissatisfaction with their sex life is the number one reason married couples seek counselling. And less than 40 per cent of married couples say they are happy with their sex lives.

Yet, in the Church sex is often treated as taboo. This means that many struggling Christian couples feel unable to share their bedroom woes.

‘Porn Pastor’ Craig Gross, who founded XXXchurch.com, a resource for people fighting porn addiction, saw marriages around him falling apart due to issues with sex. He wanted to do something about it, so he partnered with Dave Willis of strongermarriages.org to create Best Sex Life Now – a new video series that the pair hope will help Christian couples improve their sex lives and, in turn, their marriages.

“Sex is an important part of marriage, and couples who have healthy sex lives generally have healthy marriages,” says Gross. “The Church often glosses over the serious issues many couples have in the bedroom.

“There is so much opportunity to explore God’s intention for married couples to have great intimacy. We don’t claim to have all things figured out but if we can begin to get couples to have a conversation and talk openly about sex with each other, I believe we will see stronger, happier marriages.”
