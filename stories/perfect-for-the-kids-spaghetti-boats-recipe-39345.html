Perfect for the Kids: Spaghetti Boats Recipe

October 13, 2014 God & Food

consumerqueen.com

Make spaghetti with a twist!

Ingredients

1 French Loaf
1 Jar of Ragu Spaghetti Sauce
1lb of hamburger meat
1 pkg of spaghetti noodles
2 cups of mozerella cheese
4 tbs of butter (depending on how much you want)
4 tsp of garlic powder
½ pkg of McCormick Spaghetti Seasoning
Parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. Boil spaghetti noodles till done (I add a little olive oil to my water to prevent sticking)
  2. Fry hamburger meat with spaghetti seasoning.
  3. Add hamburger meat and spaghetti sauce and noodles to a pan and mix well.
  4. Preheat oven to 425
  5. Slice french bread in half then in 4th’s.
  6. Add butter and garlic powder and heat in the oven on foil for about 5 minutes or until golden brown.
  7. Top with spaghetti mixture making sure you don’t overflow (you will have leftovers)
  8. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese on top.
  9. Bake for 5 mins or until cheese has melted.
  10. I add Parmesean cheese to top of mine while hot.
  11. You could also buy the pre-made garlic bread as well which works just as good. Do you have younger kids try using english muffins or bagels!
