“Million Man Prayer” Initiative to Celebrate Fathers on Father’s Day and Stand In Prayer for the City of Chicago

June 13, 2014 Christian

Corey Barksdale

As a Gospel artist, singing and songwriting flow freely from Corey Barksdale, but his heart is also strongly attached to empowering others within the community. His commitment to the community is linked directly with his new song “Persona”, a theme song of identity, inspiring others to become nothing less than who God created them to be.

Recently during the month of May, Corey was invited to join the 100 Positive Black Men program in Chicago, IL, sponsored by Chicago Public School, Daniel S. Wentworth Elementary.

While connecting with youth and other community leaders during the 100 Positive Black Men program, Corey was afforded the opportunity to also join men from the Chicago area on Sunday, June 15th for the “Million Man Prayer” initiative outdoor event.

As father’s and men are celebrated this week across the country, the “Million Man Prayer” initiative will celebrate fathers on Father’s Day and stand in prayer for the City of Chicago, the country and our nation to Stop the Violence and to Bring our daughters back.
