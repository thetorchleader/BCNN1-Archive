Denise Hunter Calls Allegations Against Her and Her Husband, Rev. John Hunter, a "Witch Hunt"

January 11, 2013 Uncategorized

For the first time, Denise Hunter publicly denies allegations against her husband, Rev. John Hunter, who was recently fired from First AME Church.
Los Angeles’ most prominent black church, First AME Church is suing its former minister and his wife, along with seven trustees and employees of the church’s non-profit arm, accusing them of financial mismanagement.
For the first time on Friday, Denise Hunter is set to publicly deny the allegations against her and her husband, Rev. John Hunter.
Hunter was forced out after serving eight years with the congregation amid questions over his use of church credit cards and a settlement in a sexual harassment lawsuit.
Denise Hunter called the allegations a “witch hunt.”
“We believe the lawsuit is totally frivolous,” Hunter, president of F.A.M.E. Corporations, tells NBC4. “It’s all part of an orchestration of damaging the reputation of John and Denise Hunter. It’s unfortunate.
“But, I think for the people who have known me and my husband, the work we do in the community will speak for itself,” she said.
The lawsuit also seeks to remove Denise Hunter as head of F.A.M.E., the church’s non-profit corporation which provides communiy and business development, housing, transportation and health services to low- and moderate-income people in South Los Angeles.
