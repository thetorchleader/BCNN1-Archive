Bethel Chapel AME Church in Columbia, TN to Celebrate Homecoming Sunday

May 23, 2014 African-American

G. Richard Davis

Reverend Trent Ogilvie and members of Bethel Chapel A.M.E. Church will celebrate homecoming Sunday. The Rev. Pattie Davis-Hardimon will be the 10:30 a.m. guest preacher.

Rev. Hardimon was raised in Clarksville and is a member of St. Peter A.M.E. Church. Having relocated to her native Nashville, she accepted the call to preach the gospel in Greater Bethel in the fall 1981 and received her license to preach in February 1982. She was ordained a deacon in 1984 and an itinerant elder in 1986 under Bishop C. E. Thomas.

Prior to her retirement from the pastoral ministry in October 2012, she pastored several charges of the South Nashville District in the Tennessee Conference of the 13th Episcopal District of the A.M.E. church. Currently Rev. Hardimon is an associate at Greater Bethel A.M.E. in Nashville. She also is an advocate for Tennessee Suicide Prevention Network and serves on the committee of the Suicide Prevention of the African Faith-Based Community which meets once a month.

She received her bachelor of arts degree from American Baptist College in Biblical Studies, concentration in Psychological counseling. Her concentration is in prison ministry. She has served as a volunteer with the Metropolitan Davidson County Sheriff’s Department holding Bible study on Sunday morning for incarcerated women.

She is the mother of two children, Candida L. Davis and George Hardimon III. She has a daughter-in-law JoAnn and two grandchildren Ashley Sawyer and George Hardimon IV.

A fellowship dinner will be served following the morning service with the celebration continuing at 2 p.m. with guest preacher Pastor G. Richard Davis and members of Fountain Creek Baptist Church & Ministries in Culleoka.

He is also the publisher of Fountain Creek Chronicle which is a quarterly newsletter that seeks to educate and inform the community about various resources and services in the community. He is the president of Back to Bethel Empowerment Center whose mission is to impart a message of empowerment in the lives of individuals and families within the community. He is committed to informing people of the power and choices they posses to affect change in their lives. He is a community oriented person who continues to involve himself in projects and services within the community.
