Six African-American Leaders Honored by McDonald’s of Central Indiana

February 11, 2015 Black

Tony Mason, Urban League President and CEO

The McDonald’s® Black History Makers of Today and Tomorrow® program is a celebration of six influential Indiana leaders who demonstrate extensive community involvement and outstanding professional and academic achievement.

The 2015 Black History Makers of Today and Tomorrow are:

  • Rick Hite, IMPD Chief of Police
  • Tony Mason, Urban League President and CEO
  • Yolanda McCoy, McDonald’s General Manager
  • Vanessa Moore, McDonald’s owner/operator
  • Anthony Murdock, RMHC African-American Future Achiever
  • Pamela Poore, McDonald’s owner/operator

McDonald’s Black History Makers of Today are established individuals who are excelling in their respective professions, as well as positively impacting their communities.
