Congressman Says Chaplains are Being Penalized for Not Supporting Policy Allowing Homosexuals to Serve Openly

April 21, 2012 Uncategorized

A Kansas congressman says chaplains and other military personnel are being penalized for failing to support the new policy allowing homosexuals to serve openly.
U.S. Rep. Tim Huelskamp says his Military Religious Freedom Protection Act has 45 cosponsors.
 
It would exempt chaplains from functions that violate their religious principles, prohibit punishment of troops for expressing their religious or moral beliefs about homosexuality, and bar the use of military facilities for same-sex “weddings” or civil unions.
 
Huelskamp told the House Armed Services Committee that he’s been told of one chaplain who was threatened with early retirement for forwarding an email reflecting on the military’s former “don’t ask, don’t tell” policy.
 
The Kansas Republican noted that using federal facilities for same-sex “marriages” would violate the federal Defense of Marriage Act.
