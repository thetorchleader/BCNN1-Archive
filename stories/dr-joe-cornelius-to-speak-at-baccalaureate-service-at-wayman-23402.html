Dr. Joe Cornelius to Speak at Baccalaureate Service at Wayman Chapel AME Church in Columbia, TN on Sunday

May 23, 2014 African-American

Dr. Joe Cornelius

Dr. Joe Cornelius will be the speaker for the baccalaureate service to be held at 2:30 p.m. Sunday at Wayman Chapel AME Church.

Cornelius has been an educator in Maury County for more than years where he has taught students from pre-K to higher education. He is an adjunct professor with the University of Phoenix and Core Coach with Tennessee State Department of Education.

He received his Bachelor of Science degree in biology, his Master’s in Education and his Doctorate in Education Administration from Tennessee State University. He is also a nationally known public speaker and has been published in several educational journals.

Source: Columbia Daily Herald
