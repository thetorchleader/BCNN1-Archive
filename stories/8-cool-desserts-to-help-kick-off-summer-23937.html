8 Cool Desserts to Help Kick Off Summer

May 26, 2014 God & Food

Chocolate-Dipped Oreo American Flag (The Pink Flour)

For many Americans, Memorial Day is not only a day to remember the men and women who died while serving in the United States Armed Forces but the unofficial kick-off to summer. Why not combine the two by serving festive, patriotic desserts at your Memorial weekend cook-out? I’ve rounded up the best food blogs have to offer. Simply click on the recipe title to be taken to the full recipe.

1. Double Berry + Coconut Pops (from An Edible Mosaic) are not only super-cute, they are packed with fruit and are a snap to make. Not to mention cooling on a sweltering May day.

2. Chocolate-Dipped Oreo American Flag (from The Pink Flour) is sure to delight adults and children alike. The idea is simple—Oreo cookies dipped in chocolate melts—but the result is striking.

3. Red, White, and Blue Berry Trifle (from Recipe Snobs) makes me happy both that blueberries and strawberries are in season around Memorial Day and that they’re colored red and blue. Here they are sandwiched between ladyfingers and a creamy filling to create an eye-catching trifle.

4. Red, White and Blue Stained Glass Jell-O (from Brown-Eyed Baker) is a patriotic twist on the old classic stained-glass Jell-O. It combines red and blue Jell-O with opaque gelatin made by mixing sweetened condensed milk with unflavored gelatin cut into festive shapes; this is one treat you don’t have to worry about melting.

5. Celebration Pretzel Sticks (from Lady Behind the Curtain) are a salty-sweet treat that’s a breeze to make. Simply dip old-fashioned pretzel sticks in colored white chocolate and dredge in festive sprinkles.

6. Fruit Pops (from Baker’s Royale) Who doesn’t love food on a stick? This is made by cutting apples and water into star shapes, garnishing with blueberries and skewering the whole thing on a straw.

7. Red, White and Blue Fruit Pizza (from Our Best Bites) is called pizza but it is actually more of a tart. The crust is a almond-flavored cookie, spread with cream cheese “sauce” and topped with fruit.

8. Fireworks Cake (from Bird on a Cake) features a red and blue marbleized cake topped with vanilla icing with a handpainted firework burst on top. She suggests using a cake mix but a homemade cake would work just as well.

SOURCE: Parade
Rachel Rappaport is a native Baltimorean, recipe developer and cookbook author. She has been sharing her original recipes on her blog, Coconut & Lime, since 2004.
