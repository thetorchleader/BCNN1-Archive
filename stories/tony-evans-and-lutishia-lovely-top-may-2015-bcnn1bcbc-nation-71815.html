Tony Evans and Lutishia Lovely Top May 2015 BCNN1/BCBC National Bestsellers List

May 4, 2015 Books

ATLANTA, GA, (May 4, 2015) — Bestselling authors, Tony Evans (30 Days to Victory Through Forgiveness — Nonfiction) and Lutishia Lovely (The Perfect Revenge (The Shady Sisters Trilogy) — Fiction) lead the bestsellers list of Black Christian authors on the May 2015 edition of the Black Christian News/Black Christian Book Company National Bestsellers List.

BCNN1.com has also released the May 2015 edition of the BCNN1/Amazon.com Kindle E-book Bestsellers List. This list ranks African-American Christian e-books based upon their sales rankings in the Amazon Kindle e-book store. Also, BCNN1 is releasing the May 2015 edition of the BCNN1/Barnes & Noble Nook E-book Bestsellers List. This list ranks African-American Christian e-books based upon their sales rankings in the B&N Nook e-book store.
