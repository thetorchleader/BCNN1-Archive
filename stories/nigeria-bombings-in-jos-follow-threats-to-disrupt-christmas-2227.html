Nigeria: Bombings in Jos Follow Threats to Disrupt Christmas Celebrations

December 17, 2011 Uncategorized

The Plateau State capital of Jos was targeted by a series of bombings last weekend, in which one man died and 11 people were injured, including one woman.

Sources have informed Christian Solidarity Worldwide (CSW) that the bombings follow threats to disrupt Christmas celebrations in the area that have been circulating for about two weeks. 
According to a news release from CSW, last Saturday night three bombs exploded at television viewing centers on the Bauchi Ring Road, claiming the life of one man and injuring 11 people, as crowds were watching a football match between Real Madrid and Barcelona. A fourth device failed to explode and was later defused by the bomb squad. So far no group has claimed responsibility. 
Elsewhere, CSW said, a woman was killed and two others wounded in Kaduna State when gunmen attacked Kukum Dutse village in Kagoro, Kaura Local Government Area (LGA) during the early hours of Dec. 11. The two surviving victims suffered gunshot wounds, and were transferred to Bingham University Teaching Hospital in Jos. 
Commenting on the weekend’s events, the Chief Executive Officer of CSW Nigeria, Rev. Yunusa Nmadu said in the news release, “I believe that northern elders could contribute more towards ending these frequent attacks on innocent citizens.
