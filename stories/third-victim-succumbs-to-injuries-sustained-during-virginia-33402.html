Third Victim Succumbs to Injuries Sustained During Virginia Tornado

August 11, 2014 National

A tractor-trailer truck lies in the median of U.S. 13 on July 24, 2014, in Cheriton, Va., as a fire engine responds to a nearby campground after a tornado touched down in the area. (Photo: Jay Diem, The (Salisbury, Md.) Daily Times)

The son of the New Jersey couple who were killed during a tornado in Virginia last month died from his injuries Saturday.

Lheandrew Balatbat, 13, was critically injured in the July 24 tornado at Cherrystone Campground on the Eastern Shore that killed his parents, Lord Balatbat and Lolibeth Ortega.

Lheandrew died at Children’s Hospital of the King’s Daughters in Norfolk, Va., according to a woman who established a fund to benefit he and his siblings.

Pam Barefoot said she received word Saturday the teen had died.

He had been in the hospital in a coma in critical condition since the day the tornado hit Cherrystone Campground.

According to nj.com, Lheandrew died after doctors declared him brain dead, said the boy’s aunt Yolanda Villanueva.

A fundraising site established by a co-worker of Lord Balatbat to benefit the couple’s children also announced the boy’s death late Saturday, stating, “It is with heavy heart that we inform you (of) the passing of Lheandrew Lloyd Balatbat, son of (the) late Lord and Lolibeth Balatbat. At a loss for words…Please keep the family in your prayers. RIP little man…”

Lheandrew Balatbat was severely injured and his parents, both 38, were killed when a tree fell on the tent in which they had taken shelter during the sudden severe storm that struck lower Northampton County, Va., the morning of July 24.

Two other children, girls ages 12 and 7, were treated at a hospital and released.
