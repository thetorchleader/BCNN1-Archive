Beyonce And Jay-Z Donate Blue Ivy’s Baby Gifts To Charity

January 13, 2012 Uncategorized

Beyonce and Jay-Z’s new baby girl has already broken a record after becoming the youngest act ever to be credited on a Billboard Hot 100 hit after her cry was featured on the record by her proud dad and it seems that at less than a week old little Blue Ivy Carter could now be credited with being the most generous celeb baby.
With millionaire parents like Beyonce and Jay-Z, Blue Ivy is not going to be left wanting for much so her superstar parents are doing a good deed by donating all their baby presents to charity on her behalf.
Speaking to the Daily Star, a source said: “They’ve been sent literally hundreds of gifts in the past few days. Oprah gave a huge trunk of children’s books and others like P Diddy, Kanye West, Rihanna and Mariah Carey have all been sending luxurious baby treats.”
Oprah, P Diddy, Mariah? That is one lucky baby!
The insider continued: “Jay-Z and Beyoncé have decided to donate a massive selection of the presents to local charities for young mothers.
“They’ve already got the most precious gift – their new baby daughter – so they want to help out other parents who haven’t been as fortunate.”
That’s kind! There must be some pretty special gifts coming in from their celeb pals.
However, there are some presents that the couple won’t be giving away and it looks like Blue is going to have expensive taste when she grows up!
