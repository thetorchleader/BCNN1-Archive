Martha Williamson Addresses the Miracle of Christmas With “Signed, Sealed, Delivered for Christmas” on the Hallmark Channel

November 18, 2014 Entertainment

Martha Williamson

ENTERTAINMENT EXECUTIVE
Martha began her career in comedy with the producing teams for Carol Burnett and Joan Rivers. Later she went on to write and produce situation comedies such as “The Facts of Life,” “Family Man” and “Jack’s Place.”

One of her most notable successes was her role as executive producer for the CBS series, Touched By An Angel (TBAA) from 1994 to 2003. During the nine-year run, the inspirational family drama garnered a weekly audience of 25 million viewers and a billion dollar franchise. Martha successfully guided the series into previously unchartered territory with her unique brand of storytelling that produced more than strong ratings: it changed lives.

She also sealed her place in history as the first woman to solely executive-produce two one-hour dramas simultaneously. “Promised Land” aired for three years on CBS. Her work has since been inducted into the Television and Radio Hall of Fame and honored with numerous awards, including the Edward R. Murrow Responsibility in Television Award, the Templeton Prize Ephiphany Award and nine Emmy nominations. She has earned the reputation as the “go-to” executive for inspirational family entertainment.
