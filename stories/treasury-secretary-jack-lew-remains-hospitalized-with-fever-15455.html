Treasury Secretary Jack Lew Remains Hospitalized with Fever Following Prostate Procedure

March 26, 2014 National

Jack Lew (Photo: SAUL LOEB, AFP/Getty Images)

Treasury Secretary Jack Lew remained in the hospital overnight because of a low fever following a prostate procedure, the department said Wednesday morning.

“After his successful outpatient procedure (Tuesday), Secretary Lew’s doctor recommended he stay overnight for observation due to a low grade fever,” said Treasury spokesperson Natalie Wyeth Earnest.

Lew, 58, is expected to resume his normal work schedule next week.

The former chief of staff to President Obama, Lew became Treasury Secretary last year.

SOURCE: David Jackson
USA TODAY
