WATCH: Rapper Jeezy Discusses Being Sued by Bishop T. D. Jakes for “Holy Ghost” Sample

November 4, 2014 Christian

Jeezy sat down with VladTV correspondent Jenny Boom Boom and briefly discussed being sued by Bishop T.D. Jakes over a snippet of one of his sermons being used on the remix to Jeezy’s “Holy Ghost” featuring Kendrick Lamar.

The “Seen It All” rapper says he understands why Jakes would feel some kind of way about being associated with him, but that there’s a different way he could’ve handled it. For example, Barack Obama has shouted Jeezy out in the past, but for political reasons, couldn’t be photographed with the rapper. Still, Jeezy appreciated the love shown.

When it comes to the song, Jeezy says it was an engineering mistake, as all parties involved agreed to remove the sample, but somehow it remained on.
