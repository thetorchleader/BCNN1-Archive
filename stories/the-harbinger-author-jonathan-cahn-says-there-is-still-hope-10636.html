'The Harbinger' Author Jonathan Cahn Says There Is Still Hope for America

February 5, 2013 Uncategorized

LIGHT TO THE NATIONS
Jonathan says when God sends a warning to a nation – a harbinger, He does it out of mercy, to call the nation to return to Him.  Because of His special call on America as a nation, when we depart from His righteous ways we see serious consequences in the land. 
The massive scale of the 9/11 attacks on the World Trade Center made for startling images and a new, sober reality for America.  But through this devastation Rabbi Cahn was given an understanding of what this meant to America through Isaiah 9:10, and the uncanny parallel this attack has to events in 8th century Israel.    God sent warnings to ancient Israel.  The attacks of 9/11 were a warning, and like ancient Israel, if the nation ignores God’s warning there will be dire consequences, Jonathan says.  In his book, The Harbinger, a prophetic mystery and message told in the form of a novel, Jonathan tells of the supernatural connection between ancient Israel and America.  An unnamed Prophet speaks. However, the events are real, filled with insights the Lord revealed to Jonathan in the years after 9/11.  There are nine harbingers of what happens after the first attack if the nation refuses to return to God.  The judgments are progressive, with periods of normalcy in-between, but continue if there is no national repentance.  The economic implosion and other national ills spring from this event.  Israel and America are the only two nations that God sovereignly planted as a light to the nations.  “Those who laid America’s foundations saw it as a new Israel, an Israel of the New World.  And as with ancient Israel, they saw it as a covenant with God,” the Prophet says.  To whom much is given, much is required. 
His new book, The Harbinger Companion, shares more insight into how God is reaching the nation.   In one state – Oklahoma – every member of the legislature, including the governor, received a copy of the book.  Many are concerned for the continued direction of the nation. Congressional leaders are reading it and taking it to heart. That is a good thing, for it means there is a possibility of a response, of heeding the warning so we won’t be judged.  There is hope that America will return to the Lord in humility, prayer, intercession and repentance.  The hope is revival.  How much is needed to turn the course of a nation?  In the case of Sodom, it was only 10 people.  We don’t know the answer, we must turn and seek the Lord.
