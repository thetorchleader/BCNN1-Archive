WATCH: President Obama Shocks 3 Moms by Giving Them a Personal Call on Mother’s Day

May 11, 2015 National

On Mother’s Day, most moms expect cards, flowers, chocolate and lots of hugs—but not a call from the leader of the free world.

President Barack Obama shocked three mothers on their special day with a personal phone call and it was all captured on video.

“Hi. This is Barack Obama. Your President,” he said calmly, eliciting stunned and excited responses from a trio of women who had previously written letters to the commander-in-chief.

The ladies had been reportedly told to expect a call from a White House official, but had no idea the president would actually be the one making the phone calls.

“No way!” exclaimed Patricia Church, a 50-year-old single mother of four.

“Way,” Obama chuckled, seeming to enjoy the reaction.
