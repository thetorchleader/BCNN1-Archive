WATCH: Bestselling Author, Dr. Tony Evans, Releases New Book, “America: Turning a Nation to God,” on January 1, 2015

December 31, 2014 Books, Christian

Dallas megachurch pastor and national Christian leader, Dr. Tony Evans is releasing a new book, America: Turning a Nation to God, on January 1, 2015. In this timely message, Dr. Evans presents the crisis we face and urges Christians to turn their hearts back to God in order to see revival in America.

In this illuminating work by Dr. Tony Evans, he examines the root of our nation’s social, economic and political unrest. Through insightful truths and illustrations, Dr. Evans not only brings to light the core cause of our country’s current instabilities but presents a plan for reclaiming freedom, morality and strength. We hold within the collective body of Christ not only the power but also the capacity to put our country back on the path of ascendancy. This straight-forward teaching, when embraced by believers in our nation, will usher in the greatest revival in American history.

Here is what others are saying about America: Turning a Nation to God:

“Tony Evans has assessed our condition as a nation, accurately diagnosed it, and is prescribing for us a biblical approach to returning to God. This is the need of the hour. Let’s get this book into the hands of as many people as possible and simultaneously call out to God together for the next great move of God in our nation.”

–Dr. Ronnie Floyd, president of the Southern Baptist Convention,
senior pastor of Cross Church,
and author of FORWARD: 7 Distinguishing Marks For Future Leaders

“Tony correctly understands that America’s greatest need is spiritual, not political or cultural. He urges us to take God at His Word and seek His face in an age when the church is surrendering the ground given to her by God. Read this book and let us believe God for the revival we need.”

-Dr. Erwin W. Lutzer,
senior pastor, The Moody Church

“These timely and poignant words from Evans will echo in the halls of church history. America: Turning a Nation to God shatters the idols that have hijacked our worship, even as God shakes the church to its foundations of prayer and fasting. May God awaken our hearts and revive our land.”

–Wilfredo De Jesús,
pastor, New Life Covenant Church, Chicago

Dr. Tony Evans

Tony Evans is a beloved pastor and national leader. He is the founder and president of The Urban Alternative, a national ministry dedicated to restoring hope in personal lives, families, churches and communities. Dr. Evans also serves as senior pastor of Oak Cliff Bible Fellowship in Dallas. He is a bestselling author and his radio program, The Alternative with Dr. Tony Evans, is heard on nearly 1,000 stations around the globe every day. Dr. Tony Evans is married to Lois and together they are the proud parents of four children, all of whom are kingdom men and kingdom women: Chrystal Hurst, Priscilla Shirer, Anthony, and Jonathan.

For more information about Dr. Tony Evans, visit www.TonyEvans.org.

To book Dr. Tony Evans for speaking engagements and/or book signings, please contact Heather Jamison at 800-800-3222 x232 or Heather@TonyEvans.org.

America: Turning a Nation to God is available at TonyEvans.org and Amazon.com.
