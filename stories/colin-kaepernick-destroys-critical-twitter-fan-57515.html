Colin Kaepernick Destroys Critical Twitter Fan

February 19, 2015 Sports

USA Today Sports Images

Colin Kaepernick is hard at work in the off-season to return his San Francisco 49ers to the playoffs next season. He decided on Wednesday to show all of his Twitter followers that his recovery days are not recovery days at all.

Because, you know, there are no off days.

Naturally, because the Internet is the Internet, a critical fan crawled out of the woodwork to give Kaepernick some expert #analysis.

Kaepernick wasn’t having any of it.
