WATCH: Gunman Robs Man Inside DC Church

September 6, 2012 Uncategorized


A gunman robbed a man at a church in northwest D.C. over the weekend.

Before 12:30 p.m. Saturday a gunman entered St. Stephen and the Incarnation Episcopal Church in the 1500 block of Newton Street, ran up the steps with gun in hand, demanded money from the 27-year-old victim and ordered the victim to the ground, police said.
The gunman went through the victim’s pockets, taking personal property including cash, a wallet and an iPhone, police said.
The gunman also pointed the handgun at a woman who entered the room, police said.
