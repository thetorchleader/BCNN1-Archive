WATCH: May We Introduce You to the Baddest Woman in Hollywood – Dr. Naima Imani Lett

June 15, 2014 Christian, Entertainment

Rev. Naima Lett, D.MIN, ABD

All you Christian moviemakers, Steve Harvey, Tyler Perry, T.D. Jakes, and Oprah Winfrey, you need to cast this young lady in a lead role in your next movie. She’s got the talent, the brains, the looks, and the joy.

Rev. Naima Lett, D.MIN ABD is bridging the gap between Faith and Hollywood as author of the upcoming book and series Confessions of a Hollywood Christian. She’s an award-winning actor, producer and co-pastor of Hope in the Hills, a new church plant in Beverly Hills.
Naima’s candid and humorous storytelling and love of God’s Word have made her an in-demand speaker and performer. Her weekly Hollywood Christian blog reaches globally, and she has toured her one-woman plays about women of the Bible internationally.
Naima received her BFA in Acting from Howard University, the first Masters in Media & Communications from Dallas Seminary, and she is currently the first female Doctor of Ministry candidate in BIOLA/Talbot’s Preaching program.
Naima has been married to her best friend Kevin for over a decade, and they are grateful to lead young artists and professionals in their Hollywood community.
