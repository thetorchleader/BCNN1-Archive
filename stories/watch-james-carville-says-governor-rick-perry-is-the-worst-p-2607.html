WATCH: James Carville says Governor Rick Perry Is the ‘Worst Presidential Candidate in American History’

January 4, 2012 Uncategorized

“Rick Perry is the worst presidential campaign, candidate in American history,” Democratic strategist James Carville said during CNN’s coverage of the Iowa Republican caucus. “Who is worse? He was on fire. He was on fire for a while. There’s nobody that had worse debates than he did.”

“There was nobody that raised more money to less effect,” he added. “Not Giuliani, not Fred Thompson, not John Connally, not anybody — given the expectations of where he started… I’m sorry, he was the worst ever.” (via Jeff Poor/Daily Caller)

Source: Real Clear Politics
