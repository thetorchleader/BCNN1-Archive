11 People Injured in Collapse of Buildings in Harlem

March 12, 2014 National

Firefighters battle a blaze at the site of a possible explosion and building collapse in the East Harlem neighborhood of New York, March 12, 2014.
(Photo: WABC video still via AP)

As many as 11 people have been injured in the collapse of two buildings in Harlem Wednesday after an explosion, according to media reports.

CNN reports that Con Edison, the New York utilities company, had been called to the site on 116th Street in East Harlem to check a possible gas leak just before the explosion.

One person with “serious trauma” was being treated at Harlem Hospital, and the hospital was expecting more injured people to arrive, said Lamarr Nelson, a hospital spokesman.

CNN reports as many as 11 people suffered minor injuries. Four people have reportedly been hospitalized.
