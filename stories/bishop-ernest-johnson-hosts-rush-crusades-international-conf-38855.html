Bishop Ernest Johnson Hosts Rush Crusades International Conference with Special Guest Pastor Wayne Chaney

October 12, 2014 Black

Pastor Wayne Chaney

Bishop Ernest Johnson will host Rush Crusades International Conference on Friday, October 10, at the Los Angeles Convention Center, 1201 South Figueroa Street in downtown Los Angeles.

The miracle and healing worship experience starts at 7 p.m., and features the Rev. Wayne Chaney, pastor of Antioch Church of Long Beach and a star on the TV reality show, ‘Preachers of LA.’

“All ministries in the greater Los Angeles are urged to join us in this effort to revive the practice of outreach beyond the walls of the church to win souls for Christ,” said Bishop Johnson.

“Our theme is ‘Jesus Is Knocking At The Door – Let Him Back In’ and our message aims to encourage churches to let Jesus back in as the head of His Church.”

According to Bishop Johnson, Rush Crusades is nearing its 20th anniversary. Founded by the Bishop in 1995, the ministry has presented conferences around the world and established more than 50 churches in the United States.
