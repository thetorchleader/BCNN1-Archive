Group 1 Crew Releases Two Christmas Songs for the Coming Holiday Season

November 6, 2012 Uncategorized

Group 1 Crew offers two Christmas songs to their fans to enjoy during this coming Christmas season.
Group 1 Crew Christmas EP features duo’s version of traditional song “O Holy Night,” and “No Christmas (Without You).” Group 1 Crew Christmas EP is available on http://smarturl.it/g1cchristmas.
“O Holy Night” song is also part of A Very Special Christmas project benefiting the Special Olympics.
