Protester Removed from Supreme Court After Outburst During Same-Sex Marriage Hearing

April 28, 2015 National

A man was removed from the United States Supreme Court by security after he loudly interrupted arguments being heard by the Justices in Obergefell v. Hodges, a case about the legality of gay marriage.

The details of what the man shouted are not known at this time, according to public information officer Kathy Arberg. However, according to Reuters, the man said, “If you support gay marriage, you will burn in hell.”

Ryan J. Reilly of the Huffington Post captured video of security moving in to remove the man.
