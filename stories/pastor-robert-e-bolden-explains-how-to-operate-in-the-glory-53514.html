Pastor Robert E. Bolden Explains How to Operate in the Glory of God in New Book

January 25, 2015 Black

Operating in the glory of God is a gift that all believers can experience and Pastor Robert E. Bolden explains how to do it in his new book, ‘Glory.’  Using the scriptures, the slim paperback reveals how to recognize and access the glory of God in your life.

“Many people have no idea what the glory is because they have never experienced it, either personally or through a church ministry.  This book will show you from the Word of God how the glory can and will show up in the midst of God’s people,” said Bolden.

“The glory of God is His fullness offered and placed at our disposal. Habakkuk 2:14 tells us that we are living in the glory of God and the knowledge of the glory of God will be seen on us like the waters that cover the sea.

“Regardless of whatever is going on in your life, you are at the perfect place where God wants you to be to receive and operate in His glory.  God wants us to realize, even stretch our faith all the way out to the point where God is saying He doesn’t care if your situation is dead and stinking.  He can make it come alive,” he declared.
