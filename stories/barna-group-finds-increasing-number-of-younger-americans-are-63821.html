Barna Group Finds Increasing Number of Younger Americans Are Becoming Atheists, Agnostics

March 26, 2015 National

New research reveals that atheists and agnostics are on the rise and they are younger today than they were 20 years ago.

Providing a glimpse into their new project Churchless, Barna Group found that 34 percent of American skeptics (atheists and agnostics) are under 30 years old, compared to 18 percent in 1993.

“It is increasingly common among Millennials to dismiss religion, God, churches, authority and tradition,” said David Kinnaman, president of Barna Group. “For years, some observers have claimed colleges and universities are a breeding ground for anti-God sentiment. The data does lend support to the notion that college campuses are comfortable places for young people to abandon God and assume control of their own lives.”

In a special CNN report on atheism Tuesday, David Silverman, president of American Atheists argued that young people are giving up on God because they are reading the Internet and doing their research on religion.

“Millennials are the future of atheism,” Silverman told CNN.

“More and more Americans are realizing that they do not have the responsibility to pretend to believe the lies that their parents believe,” the outspoken atheist commented.
