Bethel Missionary Baptist Church in Los Angeles to Celebrate 67 Years of Christian and Community Service

March 16, 2014 Uncategorized

Pastor Reginald A. Pope

Bethel Missionary Baptist Church will celebrate 67 years of Christian and community service on Sunday, March 23, at 3 p.m., in the edifice located at 10905 South Compton Avenue in Los Angeles.

The theme is ‘Principled and Polite for a Profound Purpose’ based on Ephesians 4:15.

“We welcome the entire community to celebrate with us as we rededicate ourselves to the mandate of the Master,” said Pastor Reginald A. Pope.

“Our guests will be Dr. J. Roy Morrison and the members of Jerusalem Missionary Baptist Church.”
