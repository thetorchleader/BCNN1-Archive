Lecrae’s “Anomaly” Wins Billboard Music Award for Top Christian Album

May 18, 2015 Entertainment

Recording Artist Lecrae attends the Soul Train Awards 2013 at the Orleans Arena on November 8, 2013 in Las Vegas, Nevada.
Jason Kempin/Getty Images North America

Last night Christian rap sensation Lecrae was nominated for two Billboard Music Awards, and took home the award for Top Christian Album for Anomaly.

Lecrae’s Anomaly has gained the emcee mainstream success as he has made appearances all over network TV, including ‘The Tonight Show with Jimmy Fallon’. Anomaly has also seen him make strides in the hip-hop community as his name has been thrown around among some of the most relevant rappers in music right now.

Lecrae faced competiton from fellow artists, Casting Crowns (Thrive), MercyMe (Welcome to the New), NEEDTOBREATHE (Rivers in the Wasteland), and Chris Tomlin (Love Ran Red).

Lecrae was also nominated for Top Christian Artist along with Casting Crowns, MercyMe, Newsboys, and the winners, Hillsong United.

The Reach Records rapper recently revealed he will be releasing a book, “Unashamed.”
