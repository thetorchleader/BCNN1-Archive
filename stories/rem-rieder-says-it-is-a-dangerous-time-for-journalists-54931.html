Rem Rieder says, It Is a Dangerous Time for Journalists

February 3, 2015 Media

The world has become a very dangerous place to be a journalist. We are reminded of that fact all too frequently.

Three Al Jazeera journalists have spent 400 days in jail in Egypt. The good news is that one of them, Peter Greste, was freed on Sunday. But his two colleagues remain in custody.

Washington Post correspondent Jason Rezaian has been imprisoned in Iran for more than six months. And the latest news on that front is not good. On Sunday, Rezaian’s family confirmed that a hard-line judge with Iran’s Revolutionary Court, one with a reputation for being tough on journalists and activists, had been assigned to Rezaian’s case.

A video released over the weekend appeared to show that the Islamic State had beheaded Japanese journalist Kenji Goto. That’s the same group of homicidal zealots who last year executed American journalists James Foley and Steven Sotloff.

And it’s less than a month since the massacre at the Paris offices of satirical weekly Charlie Hebdo left 12 people dead, eight of them journalists. The attack represented a savage assault on freedom of expression.
