‘New Day’ Host Kate Bolduan Pregnant with First Child

April 14, 2014 Media

NEW YORK, NY – JUNE 13: Kate Bolduan, co-host of CNN’s new morning show ‘New Day,’ poses for a portrait on its set inside the Time Warner Center in Manhattan, NY, on June 13, 2013. (Photo by Yana Paskova/For The Washington Post via Getty Images)Negative Number: 229427 | The Washington Post via Getty Images

This just in: Kate Bolduan is going to be a mom!

The CNN “New Day” co-host is expecting a baby in October, People reported Friday. The baby will be the first child for Bolduan and her husband Michael Gershenson.

“Words cannot describe how excited we are about starting our family,” she told People.

Bolduan said that the news comes after an already action-packed year, following the debut of “New Day” in June 2013 with co-host Chris Cuomo and news anchor Michaela Pereira.
