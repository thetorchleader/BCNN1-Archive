Stevie Wonder and More to Honor Andraè Crouch at Movieguide Faith and Family Awards Gala

February 21, 2015 Entertainment

Stevie Wonder and R&B artist Ledisi give tribute to gospel and contemporary Christian music trailblazer and legend Andrae Crouch

Family friendly entertainment will take center stage at the 23rd Annual Movieguide Faith and Family Awards Gala this Saturday, Feb. 21.

The pre-taped event honors the best family friendly movies and television programs. This year a special tribute featuring Stevie Wonder will honor the late gospel legend, Andraè Crouch.

“I knew Andraè back in the 70s,” Baehr told CBN News. “We were friends for a long time. Its a big loss.”

Baehr told CBN News how his organization has helped change media content through its annual report to the entertainment industry.
