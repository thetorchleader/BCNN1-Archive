Several Religious Rights Groups Say the Obama Administration's Birth Control Opt-Out Is Not Enough

February 1, 2013 Uncategorized
Within minutes of the Obama administration announcing an opt-out for religious nonprofits that object to the HHS mandate, several religious rights groups began to blast it.
Most said the proposal was neither a fix nor did it really allow opting out.
“We are extremely disappointed with today’s announcement,” said Kyle Duncan at the Becket Fund for Religious Liberty, one of the main public-interest law firms fighting the mandate. “HHS waited nearly a year and then gave us a proposed rule that still burdens religious liberty.” Complete statement.
“The government has no business putting religious freedom on the negotiating table, or picking and choosing who is allowed to exercise faith,” said Matt Bowman of Alliance Defending Freedom, a legal ministry also involved in numerous legal battles against the mandate. “The administration must immediately abandon the idea that it has the power to withhold or dispense our fundamental freedoms to whomever it chooses. The mandate is losing in court. The only acceptable solution is for the administration to obey the Constitution and its legal duty to protect religious freedom.” Complete statement.
“This narrow policy revision would only protect a small segment of organizations purchasing health care and would still leave scores of groups and individuals at risk in having their religious freedoms and beliefs trampled and crushed,” Rev. Patrick Mahoney, with the Christian Defense Coalition, said. “It continues to show that this administration is clueless when it comes to understanding the First Amendment with respect to religious freedom for all Americans and ensuring that everyone is protected from having their core beliefs and conscience trampled by the government.” Complete statement.
