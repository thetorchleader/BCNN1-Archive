Cindy Trimm to Hold 2015 Women In Ministry Network BootCamp In June

May 12, 2015 Christian

WIMN (Women In Ministry Network) hosted by Dr. Dorie McKnight
Friday, June 12, 2015 at 7:00 PM – Saturday, June 13, 2015 at 10:00 PM (CDT)

This Bootcamp will provide 2 intense days of prayer, praise, worship and teaching on the strategies of how to fight whatever battle you are or will be in until you win! Friday its about what to do in the trenches. Saturday sessions Boots on the Ground, Assignments!! Saturday there will be a luncheon with Prayer General Dr. Cindy Trimm who will give us tools from her book PUSH – Pray Until Success Happens.

The schedule is tight and the line-up will be powerful….Now OPEN TO BOTH MEN & WOMEN
