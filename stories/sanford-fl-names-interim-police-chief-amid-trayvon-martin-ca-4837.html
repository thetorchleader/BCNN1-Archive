Sanford, Fl. Names Interim Police Chief Amid Trayvon Martin Case

March 26, 2012 Uncategorized

Sanford City Manager Norton Bonaparte announced his choice of who will
serve as the city’s interim police chief amid the Trayvon Martin
investigation.

Pictured: Capt. Darren Scott, who will serve as the city’s acting police chief, addresses the media after the announcement Monday.

Capt. Darren Scott will take that position. He spoke briefly to reporters, but would not comment directly about the case.Last
week, Sanford Police Chief Bill Lee announced he would step aside
during the investigation into the unarmed 17-year-old’s shooting death.

A
volunteer neighborhood watchman, George Zimmerman, claimed he shot the
boy in self-defense inside a gated community in Sanford.Lee said
that he will only be stepping down temporarily in hopes to restore calm
within the community.

He said information about the case would not be
released, but on Monday, a newspaper article reported on explosive
claims made by unnamed authorities about Zimmerman’s account of the
shooting.Scott would not comment on the report.

SOURCE: WESH Orlando
