Abundant Life Ministries Holding Class on Forgiveness With Iyanla Vanzant, Vicki Yohe, and Tonex

November 7, 2014 Christian

The Word of God commands the church to encourage and to build up God’s people (Hebrews 10:24, Ephesians 4:11-16). As a part of our vision to encourage and build up God’s people, Abundant Life Ministries has developed Abundant Life Classes to go beyond the basics and holistically minister to the life challenges that hinder God’s people from living life abundantly (John 10:10).

Each month, Abundant Life Ministries will transform a regularly scheduled church service into a powerful, life changing Life Class. Renowned speakers, specialists, subject matter experts and dynamic panel guests from diverse backgrounds, will inform, inspire and challenge you in a completely transparent environment.

Through compassionate and holistic “Real Talk about Real Life,” Abundant Life Classes equip and empower God’s people to apply the wisdom of God to power for everyday living.

Next Scheduled Abundant Life Class:

  • Date: Sunday, December 14, 2014
  • Topic: Forgiveness — Letting Go To Live
  • Guest Speaker: Iyanla Vanzant
  • Registration: Seating will be limited, registration is required. Registration begins on November 6, 2014. To register for the event, Click Here or go to our “Events” page.
