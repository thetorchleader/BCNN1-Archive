Wife of Man Who Took Video of Eric Garner Is Arrested

August 7, 2014 Black

NYPD officers in 2013
SPENCER PLATT/GETTY IMAGES

On Sunday Ramsey Orta was arrested on criminal gun-possession charges. His wife, Chrissie Ortiz, was arrested Tuesday, and the family maintains that they are being singled out by police.

In what is surely just a coincidence, Chrissie Ortiz, the wife of Ramsey Orta, who recorded the video of Eric Garner’s death, has been arrested. Ortiz was arrested for assault after a dispute with another woman on Tuesday evening. She was later released with a desk appearance ticket to appear at an arraignment, reports The Gothamist.

Her husband was arrested on criminal gun-possession charges this past Saturday. Orta told the Staten Island Advance that he’s “100 percent sure” the police set him up.
