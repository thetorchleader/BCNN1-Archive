Sad: 3-Year-Old Detroit Girl Killed After Family Dispute on Facebook Spills Offline

October 19, 2014 Black

Three-year-old Amiracle Williams of Detroit was caught in a deadly shooting that began with a on Facebook.
WXYZ DETROIT SCREENSHOT

As a Detroit family grieves the loss of a three-year-old girl, police worked to sort out details of the senseless shooting that took her life last week, the Huffington Post reports.

The child, Amiracle Williams, was killed Thursday after a dispute on Facebook spilled offline, leading to a confrontation in front of her family’s Detroit home, police say. The child’s mother, 40, father, 47, and sister, 17, were injured in the incident. And one of the suspects, a 22-year-old black male, was also shot and injured, according to the report.

The shooting occurred after several young women had a physical fight and Amiracle’s father. He spotted the altercation that involved his daughters and felt they “may have been in some sort of danger and responded by shooting one person at the scene,” Detroit Police Chief James Craig said Friday at a police academy graduation, where graduates and the audience held a moment of silence for the child, the Post reports.

After the initial shooting, Craig said a second individual took out a machine gun and began to fire repeatedly, striking Amiracle and her three family members, the report says.

Late Friday, police had two suspects in custody, including the alleged second shooter, a 23-year-old man, and the alleged driver of a getaway vehicle who had been shot in the incident. The Post says the alleged shooter had an outstanding warrant for his arrest for a felony charge in Virginia. At least one of the men has been involved in local gangs, police said. A TEC-9 machine gun was recovered as part of the police investigation, which continues.

“[The shooting was] a decision made by a coward, once again, another coward that goes out and engages in senseless violence,” Craig said. “We will continue our focus on eradicating violence, and certainly dismantling these street gangs here in Detroit.
