Church Tech Startup Receives Funding Manna: $1 Million from Investor

March 4, 2015 Technology

Kansas City-based Aware3 LLC is singing a hymn of joy after receiving $1 million from a regional investor.

The church tech startup secured the round from Lincoln-based Nelnet Inc., which also will offer resources in the company’s plans to scale.

“We’re really excited,” Aware3 CEO Tony Caudill said. “We’re going to be able to leverage a lot of the things that they (NelNet) know today to help us scale from a sales side.”

A little over a year-old, Aware3 creates mobile applications for churches to engage their congregations. The apps — used by churches around the nation — provide churches with tools to deliver calendar events, live stream video, review scripture and receive gifts. The apps are branded according to the individual church and are available for Android and iOS devices.

A lifelong Kansas City native, Caudill said that the funding will boost marketing and advertising and allow for building additional features into the platform. Now with three employees, Aware3 plans to add up to six staff members ­— in sales and IT — in the next year.

“For us, it’s all about scaling,” he said. “We’ve been able to bootstrap this thing and be cash flow positive but we really want to take it to the next level so the funding will help us do that.”

Caudill is an alumnus of entrepreneur group Pipeline Inc., and graduated the program in 2014.

SOURCE: Kansas City Business Journal – Bobby Burch
