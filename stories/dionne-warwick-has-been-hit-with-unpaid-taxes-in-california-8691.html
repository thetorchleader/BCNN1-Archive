Dionne Warwick has Been Hit with Unpaid Taxes in California

October 12, 2012 Uncategorized

California’s Franchise Tax Board has put singer Dionne Warwick on blast, placing her on the latest list of the top 500 people and businesses owing the most in unpaid taxes.
Warwick, who now lives in New Jersey, owes California $2.6 million, according to the tax board’s list, released Thursday. She’s been delinquent since 2003, but her publicist, Kevin Sasaki, says Warwick has worked out a repayment plan with the state and is paying down her debt.
Not too long ago, Warwick was accused of owing the IRS $2.2 million in backed taxes but the department quickly recanted their claim  citing a clerical error.
Hopefully, this is another mistake.
