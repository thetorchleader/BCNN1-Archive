Christian Author Os Guinness Says Churches In U. S. Are Weak Because They’re Worldly

October 16, 2014 Christian

COURTESY OF MUDPREACHER

U.S. churches are weak because they’re worldly, according to Christian author Os Guinness.

Speaking at the Family Research Council in Washington, Guinness added that churches have lost influence in America because Christians are fearful and compromised.

He said this is a time when the world needs “a positive and persuasive alternative vision of human flourishing.”

Guinness said he thinks too many American Christians lack the “courage to be different and to clearly make a stand” in a culture that’s increasingly disdainful and even hostile to Christianity.
