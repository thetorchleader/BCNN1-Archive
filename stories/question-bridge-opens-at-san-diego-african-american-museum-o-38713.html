‘Question Bridge’ Opens at San Diego African-American Museum of Fine Art at the M.L. King Center at Bayview Baptist Church

October 11, 2014 Black

In “Question Bridge,” black men took the opportunity to ask hard questions, and give tough answers. It’s now at San Diego’s African American Museum of Fine Art.

Larry Audry has a question: “How do we deal with internalizing white values without having full access to its benefits?”

Tony Snow has another: “Does the government really want to stop crime?”

And from Edgar Yarbarough: “When will we as black men get real with each other?”

There are hundreds more questions from hundreds of African-American men, and answers as well, in “Question Bridge: Black Males,” a revealing, provocative video installation that opens Oct. 11 at the San Diego African American Museum of Fine Art at the M.L. King Center at Bayview Baptist Church.

“It’s really hard for me to think that anybody who walks into ‘Question Bridge’ with one idea about who black men are, whatever their assumptions are about the way black men think and feel, could leave feeling the same way,” said Chris Johnson, the Oakland-based artist who co-created the work.

The essence of Johnson’s project is rooted in a 1996 installation he did for the Museum of Photographic Arts in Balboa Park. In that initial version of “Question Bridge,” Johnson divided the African-American community into two segments — those who had stayed in the neighborhood, even after the enactment of civil rights legislation opened up other options, and those who left for whiter, more middle-class areas.

An African-American who had grown up in Bedford-Stuyvesant, in Brooklyn, but left for the Bay Area, Johnson had firsthand knowledge of the tension between the two groups, but he knew that if they were placed in the same room, they were unlikely to ask each other the hard questions. So he separated them and videotaped an individual asking a question; and then played that question to someone else and videotaped his or her answer. And then he put it together.
