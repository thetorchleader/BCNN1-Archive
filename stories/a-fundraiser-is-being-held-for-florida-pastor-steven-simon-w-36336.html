A Fundraiser Is Being Held for Florida Pastor Steven Simon Who Was Shot in the Head during Road Rage Incident

August 31, 2014 Black

Pastor Steven Smith

He’s walking and he’s talking, but Pastor Steven Simon still has some recovering to do after being shot in the head on I-95 at the beginning of August.

Saturday, hundreds of people helped raise money to see that he does make a full recovery.

The Martin County Sheriff’s Office says Simon was shot while inside his car as the result of someone else’s road rage.

Friends of his organized a fundraiser at the St. John Haitian United Methodist Church in Boynton Beach Saturday night.

Organizers expected a crowd of about 500 people, with a goal of raising $5,000 to give Simon for his medical bills and any future treatments.

Various community pastors were asked to help put on a church service for Simon. Guests are asked to give an offering in the middle of the service as a donation.

Friends of Simon say he is glad to be recovering and to be home with his wife and two children.

They’re thankful he’s able to be at the fundraiser with them.

“It scares you. It brings you back to reality of what kind of world we live in,” said Simon’s friend, Gary Hawkins.
