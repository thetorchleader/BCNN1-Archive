Chicago Mayor Rahm Emanuel to Award Championship Rings to Disqualified Jackie Robinson West Little League Team

February 14, 2015 Black

President Barack Obama and first lady Michelle Obama pose with the Jackie Robinson West All Stars Little League Baseball team in the Oval Office of the White House in Washington, D.C., on Nov. 6, 2014.
YURI GRIPAS/AFP/GETTY IMAGES

First, they were stripped of their title after team officials violated a rule that does not allow the roster to be comprised of players who do not live within certain geographical-boundaries.

Then on Friday it was reported that Chicago Mayor Rahm Emanuel still plans to honor the team for its historic win. He plans to present each member of the team with a championship ring next month, reports the Chicago Sun-Times.

The move comes after the mayor lost an appeal to the president of the Little League International to reverse his decision to strip the kids of the championship title, the newspaper notes. Emanuel plans to present the rings to the team at a March City Council hearing.

“Mayor Emanuel believes it is unfair for the organization to have punished the children who did nothing wrong,” said a mayoral source told the Sun-Times.

“These young men demonstrated tremendous character both on and off the field, and Chicago will honor them as the champions they are,” Emanuel said, according to the Sun-Times. “​The memories they created will last a lifetime, and so will the championship rings they have earned.”
