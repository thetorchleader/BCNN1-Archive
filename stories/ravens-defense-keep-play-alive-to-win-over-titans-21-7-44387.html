Ravens Defense Keep Play Alive to Win Over Titans, 21-7

November 9, 2014 Sports

© AP Photo/Gail Burton
Baltimore Ravens running back Justin Forsett (29) celebrates his touchdown as teammate wide receiver Kamar Aiken (11) looks on and Tennessee Titans cornerback Blidi Wreh-Wilson (25) walks away during the first half of an NFL football game against the Tennessee Titans in Baltimore, Sunday, Nov. 9, 2014.

Justin Forsett ran for 112 yards and two touchdowns to back a strong performance by the Baltimore defense, and the Ravens beat the Tennessee Titans 21-7 Sunday to end a two-game losing streak.

The Ravens (6-4) bottled up rookie quarterback Zach Mettenberger, who went 16 for 27 for 179 yards and an interception in his first road start in the NFL. He was sacked five times and rarely got the chance to comfortably plant both feet in the pocket.

Tennessee didn’t get its initial first down of the second half until 4:37 was left in the game. Soon after that, newcomer Danny Gorrer intercepted a pass to clinch the victory.

The Titans (2-7) have lost seven of eight. It was 7-all at halftime, but Tennessee had three three-and-outs in the third quarter while the Ravens took control.

Source: The AP
