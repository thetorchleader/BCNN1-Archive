Tina Campbell Admits Destroying Her Husband’s Car With a Hammer – Not a Gun

April 1, 2015 Entertainment

Photo Credit: Oxygen

Tina Campbell admits that, in a fit of rage, she did bust her husband’s car windshield, but denies using a gun to do it.

During a Tuesday, March 31 appearance on The Real, the WE tv reality star, 40, cleared up a few misconceptions and circulating rumors.

Co-host Tamar Braxton asked her, “It look like you done shot up the man car. Did you do that?”

“I did not shoot up [the man’s car]!” Tina emphatically stated, though she did lash out over her husband Teddy Campbell’s numerous affairs.

In fact, in a recent episode, Tina said voices were telling her “Go crash the car, stab him, kill yourself,” adding, “All this crazy stuff was coming to my head.”

Some of the “crazy stuff,” she did, which has been captured on camera for the fourth and current season of the show. Despite being irate and out of control, however, Tina said she stopped short of firing actual shots at Teddy’s well-loved vintage convertible.
