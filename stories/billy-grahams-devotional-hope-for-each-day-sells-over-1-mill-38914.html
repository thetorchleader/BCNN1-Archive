Billy Graham’s Devotional “Hope for Each Day” Sells Over 1 Million Copies

October 12, 2014 Books

Evangelist Billy Graham’s devotional Hope for Each Day recently sold more than 1 million copies.

“Billy Graham’s message of hope is perfect for these chaotic and troubled times,” said Laura Minchew, senior vice president and publisher of HarperCollins Christian Publishing Gift Books, Tommy Nelson Children’s Books and New Media. “The devotional helps readers keep their focus on Jesus, the only One who brings peace. The book continues to build in sales because the message is so critical today.”
