Arrest Warrant Is Out for Grammy Award-Winning Singer Anita Baker

March 22, 2014 Entertainment

Anita Baker

The 56-year-old singer was reportedly issued a bench warrant Wednesday after failing to show up at court in her legal battle with a painting and decorating company.

Grammy award-winning singer Anita Baker has a warrant out for her arrest.

The 56-year-old star reportedly failed to show up in court regarding a $15,000 dispute with a painting and decorating company for work done to her Grosse Pointe, Mich., home in 2011.

The “Sweet Love” songstress took to Twitter to address her shock over the legal situation.

“WHATTT??!!… sittin’ in the kitchen watching this on TV. BIG time crazy,” she wrote.

According to TMZ, court records show that the bench warrant was issued Wednesday.
