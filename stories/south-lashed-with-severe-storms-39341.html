South Lashed With Severe Storms

October 13, 2014 Uncategorized

Eastern portions of Arkansas, along with parts of Missouri, Tennessee, Mississippi and Louisiana, are at the greatest risk for severe weather on Monday (in red). Areas in yellow are at lesser risk, while non-severe thunderstorms are possible in the green area.
(Photo: Storm Prediction Center)

Severe storms ripped through the South overnight, with more bad weather continuing on Monday. At least one person was killed in in Arkansas and homes and property were damaged in Oklahoma, Texas, Missouri and Louisiana, according to the Weather Channel.

As of mid-afternoon Monday, a tornado watch – meaning conditions are favorable for tornadoes to develop – is in place for much of Arkansas and parts of Louisiana, Tennessee and Mississippi.

A tornado was reported Monday afternoon in Bawcomville, La., near Monroe. At least 38,000 people were without power in northeastern Louisiana.

Earlier, the Little River County, Ark., coroner confirmed that a man in his 30s was killed when the storms came through the county around 5:30 a.m. He died in his home on the outskirts of Ashdown.

The county sheriff said debris fell on the home where the victim died. Also, one of his deputies was injured while trying to get inside the home to retrieve the victim by an exposed electrical wire. Three other people were also injured.

One home lost part of its roof in Elmer, Okla.. Wind gusts of 75 to 80 mph were reported in southwestern parts of that state. A weak tornado was also confirmed in Chesapeake, Mo.

In Texas, thousands of homes and businesses lost electricity as thunderstorms soaked northern parts of the state. Most of the outages were concentrated in the Dallas-Fort Worth area.
