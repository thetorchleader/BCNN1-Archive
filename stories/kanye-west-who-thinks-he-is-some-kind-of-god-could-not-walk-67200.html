Kanye West, Who Thinks he Is Some Kind of god, Could Not Walk on Water

April 13, 2015 Entertainment

Kanye West may think he’s a god, but he can’t walk on water just yet.

During his visit to Armenia with the Kardashian klan, Yeezy hosted an impromptu free concert in Swan Lake in Yerevan on Sunday night. After running through a short greatest-hits set, Kanye started performing Good Life and decided to take a quick swim. People posted video of the stunt on Twitter.

Fans followed Kanye into the water for a soggy mosh pit – and so did the police, who cut the power to the show and escorted him from the lake.
