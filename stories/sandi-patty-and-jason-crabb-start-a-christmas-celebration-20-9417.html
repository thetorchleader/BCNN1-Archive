Sandi Patty and Jason Crabb Start 'A Christmas Celebration 2012 Tour'

November 27, 2012 Uncategorized

‘A Christmas Celebration 2012 Tour’ with Sandi Patty and Jason Crabb already started. The Christmas tour featuring two multiple Dove award and Grammy award winners began last Friday, November 23, 2012, in Hagerstown, MD at the Maryland Theatre.
Sandi Patty, “Listening to @jasoncrabbmusic sound check…..Mercy the boy can just flat out SANG!”
Jason Crabb reflects on the opening night, “Wow! What a night!! I had the best time tonight with Sandi Patty on the opening night of our tour, “A Christmas Celebration!” I was a fan before but I’m even more of a fan now!’ WOW!!!”
