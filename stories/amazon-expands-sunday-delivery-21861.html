Amazon Expands Sunday Delivery

May 12, 2014 Books

Amazon has opened up Sunday delivery to customers in another 15 cities in addition to the Los Angeles and New York metro areas, where the service was launched in November 2013.

Customers in the following locations will now be able to receive deliveries on Sunday: Austin, Tex.; Cincinnati, Ohio; College Station, Tex.; Columbus, Ohio; Dallas, Tex.; Houston, Tex.; Indianapolis, Ind.; Lexington, Ky; Louisville, Ky.; New Orleans, La.; Oklahoma City, Okla.; Philadelphia, Pa.; San Antonio, Texas; Shreveport, La.; Waco, Texas.

SOURCE: Publishers Weekly
