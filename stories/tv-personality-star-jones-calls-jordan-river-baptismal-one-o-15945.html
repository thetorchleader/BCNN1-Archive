TV Personality, Star Jones, Calls Jordan River Baptismal “One of the Best Days of My Life”

March 29, 2014 Entertainment

Star Jones (Credit: Getty)

In John 3:5, Jesus said, “Unless one is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God.” Attorney, TV personality, author and heart health advocate Star Jones, 52, is now calling the fulfillment of Christ’s command “one of the best days of my life.”

On Friday, March 28, the devout Christian, adorned in a flowing white gown and head wrap, was baptized in the Jordan River—where Jesus was baptized by John the Baptist.

As a part of her trip to Israel with AIPAC, America’s pro-Israel lobby dedicated to strengthening, protecting and promoting the U.S.-Israel relationship, Jones reaffirmed her faith in the deeply meaningful ceremony.

“Then Jesus came from Galilee to the Jordan to be baptized by John,” she shared on Instagram, quoting the passage of Scripture found in Matthew 3:13, tying her act of faith to its biblical origins. “What a blessing!” she added.
