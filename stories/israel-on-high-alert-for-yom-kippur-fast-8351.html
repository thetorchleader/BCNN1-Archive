Israel on High Alert for Yom Kippur Fast

September 25, 2012 Uncategorized

The Israeli security establishment as well as emergency services are preparing for the Yom Kippur fast day.


Cyclists riding on Tel Aviv’s Ayalon freeway. Photo by Daniel Bar-On
The IDF and Israel Police are on high alert, as police beefed up patrols in cities, and around synagogues, and officers were also stationed at the entrances to towns.
A comprehensive closure of West Bank border crossings went into effect on Monday night, and will continue until midnight on Wednesday.  The IDF Spokesperson unit stated that crossings will be allowed for cases of extreme medical or humanitarian emergency, and subject to the approval of the Civil administration.
Magen David Adom will reinforce its stations throughout the country with extra personnel on Yom Kippur, in order to provide rapid medical care,  should the need arise, for bicycle-riders, who take to the empty streets in droves, as well as individuals fasting.
