Family, Friends Remember Queens Rapper, Lionel Pickens, at Greater Allen AME Cathedral of New York

May 26, 2015 Black

Family and friends will gather Tuesday morning to say goodbye to a Queens rapper murdered in his car.

Lionel Pickens, 31, was shot and killed May 17 in a drive-by shooting in Queens.

Another man was also shot and survived. No arrests have been made. So far, police have no motive.

Pickens’ wake will be held at 11 a.m. at the Greater Allen AME Cathedral of New York in Jamaica. His funeral will follow at noon.

Pickens was married to Janelli Pickens. The two grew up together in Far Rockaway and had two daughters, ages 14 and 11, and a 4-year-old son.

Pickens was a member of fellow rapper French Montana’s “Coke Boys.” Pickens had just performed at a Brooklyn nightclub Saturday night and was going to a bar with a 27-year-old man but got there after it was closed. He was driving his friend to a nearby subway station in Queens when the deadly encounter took place on Queens Boulevard.

At 4:04 a.m., police said another car drove up, and a gunman opened fire, striking Pickens at least eight times.

“As soon as I heard gunshots, I ducked,” said eyewitness Raymond Rivera. Rivera was up the block where Pickens, though shot, was able to drive his Porsche. Pickens then crashed into another parked car.

“When he fell on the ground, he just laid there, he was motionless – he didn’t move, he didn’t scream,” Rivera added.

The other man in the car was struck in the back and suffered a punctured lung.

Source: ABC 7
