CNN Should Fire Homosexual Don Lemon for Saying This Word Twice during Live Oscar Coverage

February 23, 2015 Media

Don Lemon is in the headlines again, this time for saying “dick” on live television while gushing over Andy Samberg and Justin Timberlake’s popular “Saturday Night Live” digital short “Dick in a Box.”

In a red carpet interview ahead of the Academy Awards, the CNN anchor asked Andy Samberg, “How do you come up with stuff like ‘Dick in a Box’?”t enough, a few minutes later he described his interview to co-host Michaela Pereira, saying “I love all those sketches they do, Michaela, on ‘SNL.’ Those are the guys responsible for ‘Dick in a Box’…”

“Oh, you said it,” said a bemused Pereira, who rolled her eyes and added “my Mom’s watching, Don Lemon.”
