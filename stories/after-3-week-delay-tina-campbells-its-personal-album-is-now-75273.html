After 3 Week Delay, Tina Campbell’s “It’s Personal” Album Is Now Available

May 23, 2015 Entertainment

The official artwork for Tina Campbell’s delayed debut solo album IT’S PERSONAL (Credit: Gee Tree Creative)

After missing its original May 7 release date, Tina Campbell’s debut solo album, It’s Personal, is now available on iTunes.

While the reason for the three-week delay is unclear, the wait has officially come to end.

“Go tell a friend,” Tina said in a hashtag following the announcement of the album’s availability on Twitter, hoping to garner support.

While having an album pushed back is not ideal for any artist, as it can hurt the sales of a project, occasionally, the setback heightens eagerness, thus bolstering purchase numbers.

Fans and supporters of the singer are no doubt hoping for the latter.
