Titanic USA: America is the Titanic and the Titanic is Going Down

April 15, 2012 Uncategorized

I Thessalonians 5:3 says: “For when they shall say, Peace and safety; then sudden destruction cometh upon them, as travail upon a woman with child; and they shall not escape.”

PART ONE

PART TWO

PART THREE

The title of my message for today is “Titanic USA: America is the Titanic and the Titanic is Going Down”. 
100 years ago, on April 14, 1912, a great tragedy occurred in the dark and murky waters of the northern Atlantic Ocean. A great ship — the Titanic — which everyone believed to be “unsinkable” struck an iceberg and sank beneath the ocean waters on her maiden voyage. The ship was hailed as a “moving city”. It was the largest moving man-made object ever crafted. Titanic set sail from England to New York City with much fanfare and well-wishes. But it never reached its destination. 
The Titanic was warned of icebergs at least five times within the twelve hours before it met with tragedy. After icebergs were reported up ahead, the captains were so convinced of the ship’s invincibility that they had the ship continue full speed ahead. They were so confident that the ship would not sink that they only had 16 lifeboats on board when a ship of that size carrying that many passengers was supposed to carry 48. The men who designed and commissioned the Titanic were so confident in their abilities that they even had the passengers convinced that the Titanic would not sink. While the ship was going down, people came out on deck and had snowball fights, some people put on lifejackets and danced, still others who heard the news simply rolled over and went back to sleep. In this tragedy, 1,514 people drowned, suffocated or froze to death in the icy waters. Only 710 people were saved. All the ingenuity, all the technological prowess, and all the safeguards (or lack thereof) could not keep the Titanic from sinking. 
Men will say, Peace and safety; then sudden destruction cometh upon them…and they shall not escape.
I am here to tell you today, my friends, that there is another Titanic. And you and I are on it. That Titanic is the United States of America. Yes, I believe that America is the Titanic and the Titanic is going down. Allow me to share with you why this is so.
I. TITANIC USA IS SINKING BECAUSE AMERICA HAS LOST ITS WAY SPIRITUALLY
Let me bring to your remembrance the reason why America was founded. Men and women from Europe who wished to worship God freely were the first permanent settlers in America. These people came because they wanted to worship God freely and obey the Bible as they believed they should. These first settlers did not agree on every jot an tittle of the faith — you had Puritans and Baptists, Catholics and Quakers, Mennonites and Anglicans. But all of these people were deeply religious and were determined to raise their children to be Godly men and women. America’s founding fathers were also deeply religious people. All of them believed in God, and many of them professed to be believers in Jesus Christ. America was founded on the solid foundation of Judeo-Christian principles .
However, today, we are getting away from that.  As the Titanic was going down that fateful night 100 years ago, the band played a song titled “Nearer My God to Thee.” Today, America is far away from God. Many hearts have been hardened towards the things of God.
In today’s America:
  • We have preachers more interested in making a profit than praying for souls
  • We have people who claim to be Christians yet say they don’t believe the Bible is the word of God anymore
  • We have many Christians and preachers who believe that gain is godliness. These people believe if you’re not driving a Bentley, living in a house on porkchop hill and have a lot of money, you are not prospering. 1 Timothy 6:5-10 reads, “…supposing that gain is godliness: from such withdraw thyself. But godliness with contentment is great gain. For we brought nothing into this world, and it is certain we can carry nothing out. And having food and raiment let us be therewith content. But they that will be rich fall into temptation and a snare, and into many foolish and hurtful lusts, which drown men in destruction and perdition. For the love of money is the root of all evil: which while some coveted after, they have erred from the faith, and pierced themselves through with many sorrows.”
  • We have men who are supposed to be the spiritual heads and leaders of their families, churches and communities, in many cases, are AWOL everywhere and cannot be found, or they are weak and effeminate and have women leading them.
  • There was a time when the preacher and the church were respected. Now the church is becoming the butt of jokes for people who do it and for leaders who let it happen.
  • There was a time when people knew there was a difference between Christianity and false religions such as Islam and Mormonism
  • On the matter of race, we still find ourselves to be just as segregated as when MLK  Jr., said 11:00 on Sunday mornings is the most segregated hour of the week. And the church. instead of leading. is more often reacting to what the world is doing and reacting separately and against one another instead of together.
  • There was a time where if a leader in the church fell into sin such as adultery, he would have to sit down for a while and maybe get counseling before he could get in the pulpit again. but nowadays, preachers can commit gross sin on Saturday night and be in the pulpit on Sunday morning. Benny Hinn and Paula White can be caught in Rome serving the pope and get caught coming out of a hotel room holding hands, and get right in the pulpit and try to tell somebody something.
  • We have preachers who don’t preach the full counsel of God because they fear the people will leave them and they will take their money with them.
  • We have black and white preachers more concerned about their relationship with the president than with the prince of peace; who instead of telling the president the truth would rather curry his favor.
  • We have churches that are more concerned with filling pews rather than filling mansions in heaven
  • We have people who are more eager to watch or attend a football game than attend church on Sunday morning.
  • We have many Americans today who have completely turned their backs on God and the church. Many of these people are humanists and secularists. Statistics say that atheism is on the rise — in fact a bunch of atheists got together in Washington D.C. last month to hold a rally. There used to be a time in our country when atheism was so looked down upon that those who claimed to not believe in God were too afraid to crawl out of their holes. Nowadays, atheism is seen as “progressive” and modern.
It is reported that when a missionary and his wife arrived at the dock to board the Titanic, the wife looked up at the ship and said, ‘So, this is the unsinkable ship?’ One worker who was standing nearby boasted, ‘Even God Himself cannot sink this ship.’
In America today, we have gone so far spiritually that we are often at the point of defiance. We reject the clear teaching of God’s word, raise our fists in God’s face and say “I will do it my way.” And I believe one of the greatest sins we commit is not only being ungrateful but taking God for granted. Our pride and arrogance is part of the reason why Titanic USA is going down. The Bible says in Proverbs 1:24-27: “Because I have called, and ye refused; I have stretched out my hand, and no man regarded; But ye have set at nought all my counsel, and would none of my reproof: I also will laugh at your calamity; I will mock when your fear cometh; When your fear cometh as desolation, and your destruction cometh as a whirlwind; when distress and anguish cometh upon you.”
II. TITANIC USA IS SINKING BECAUSE AMERICA HAS LOST ITS WAY MORALLY
Not only is Titanic USA sinking because America has lost its way spirituall
y, it is sinking because America has lost its way morally. If some of the first Christian settlers of our country came back and visited us today, they would be appalled at what they would see: 
In today’s America: 
  • We have families that are breaking up left and right.
  • We have people who try on marriage like they try on clothes.
  • We have a society where sexual immorality is not only winked at, but in many instances it is encouraged.
  • We have a society where homosexuality is running rampant and put on public display.
  • We have a society where abortion — the taking of an innocent life — is on demand
 
But that’s not all. Those first settlers of our country would come back to find that: 
 
  • We live in a nation drowning in its own debt.
  • We have citizens who are afraid to say what they believe because they fear intimidation.
  • We have schools where students are not allowed to pray.
  • We even have activists who want to remove a harmless nativity scene from government property.
If the first Christian settlers of our country came back today, they would find a nation that has a vastly different moral compass or perhaps no moral compass at all.
Nearly everything that this country was founded on has been turned on its head. On a visit to the United States, the president of Iran stood in one of our universities and mocked us for tolerating homosexuality. He said, “In Iran we don’t have homosexuals like in your country.” America — once the most powerful nation on earth — is losing respect and influence around the world. I believe it is losing respect simply because we have turned our back on the righteous foundations of our country.  The Bible says in Psalm 11:3, “If the foundations be destroyed, what can the righteous do?” America is the Titanic and the Titanic is going down.
The first empire-builders of Babel were defeated because they decided to rebel against God and gain power their way. The kingdoms of Israel and Judah were plundered and carried away into captivity because they refused to worship the one true God and began worshipping idols. The Babylonian Empire fell because its kings were proud and lifted up their hearts against God. Greece was conquered because its people failed to live in unity and peace with each other. Alexander the Great’s empire was destroyed because of its corrupt and conniving leaders. Rome fell because of its debauchery and immorality which was even practiced by the emperors themselves.
I am sad to say that America is sinking because of these very same reasons.
III. TITANIC USA IS SINKING BECAUSE AMERICA’S LEADERSHIP HAS LOST ITS WAY
Now, we have dealt with the two main problems in America — and that is our spiritual problem and our moral problem. But a third problem in America, and one that I must deal with briefly, is the problem with our civil leadership. The reason why we have to deal with this is because the Bible says in Romans chapter 13 that our governmental leaders are also “ministers of God.” Verses 3 and 4 of this passage say: “For rulers are not a terror to good works, but to the evil…For he is the minister of God to thee for good. But if thou do that which is evil, be afraid; for he beareth not the sword in vain: for he is the minister of God, a revenger to execute wrath upon him that doeth evil.”
But unfortunately, our governmental leaders have lost their way as well. We don’t have anymore noble statesmen in America — be they Christian or non-Christian — who truly have the best interests of our country at heart. We don’t have anymore Patrick Henry’s who will say, “Give me liberty, or give me death!” We don’t have anymore Nathan Hale’s who will say, “My only regret is that I have only one life to give for my country.” We don’t have any more George Washington’s or George Washington Carver’s. We don’t have anymore Benjamin Banneker’s or Benjamin Franklin’s who requested that the Continental Congress begin each session in prayer to God. We don’t have anymore Frederick Douglass’ or Daniel Webster’s who will say we can only have true liberty if we sustain virtue. 
Instead, today, we have politicians who are more concerned about being re-elected than actually serving the people who elect them in the first place. In fact, just a couple weeks ago, our president was caught on tape telling the Russian president that once he got re-elected he would have more flexibility to deal with a certain issue. Today, we have politicians and leaders who set a bad example for the people who they are supposed to be leading. It seems as though our leaders need leadership themselves. Jesus Christ said that “if the blind lead the blind, both shall fall into the ditch.” Now the Republicans who are supposed to be made up of many conservative Christians are selecting a Mormon for the presidency who believes that Jesus Christ is going to reign from Missouri one day. He is certainly no better than the president we have and is probably worse. Talk about choosing between two evils, we certainly have it here in America today. And I believe that this bad choice of leadership for the president of the United States is a judgment by God against this country; we are getting exactly what we deserve.
The main reason why the Titanic went down was because of its leadership. The Captain of the Titanic, E.J. Smith, was a decorated and successful captain. However, as he was at the helm of the Titanic, he made a serious mistake — he gave the command for the ship to go full steam ahead in the face of danger. He was over-confident in his own abilities and in the ship’s abilities. And this was a fatal mistake.
In America, we need leaders who will take heed to warnings and reverse course when they can clearly see that they are going down the wrong path. I believe a lot of the problems in our nation will be solved if our leaders get the government’s house in order.
IV. WHAT SHOULD WE DO WHILE TITANIC USA IS GOING DOWN?
Now, you may be wondering, ‘Well, if America is sinking what am I supposed to do? What is the church supposed to do?”  Let me give you three things, and then we’ll close.
1. First of all, pastors and politicians need to stop lying to people and telling them that everything is alright and that everything is going to get better when, in fact, it is not. Preachers need to stop preaching the prosperity gospel when more and more Americans are falling into poverty. Politicians need to stop making promises that they cannot keep just to get elected and re-elected. I believe that our leaders will have much better relationships with the public if they simply tell the truth, be realistic, and work together to make things happen. The Bible warns us in I Thessalonians 5 about people who declare “Peace and safety; then sudden destruction cometh upon them, as travail upon a woman with child; and they shall not escape.” 
2. Secondly, we need to take heed to the warning signs that God is giving us. I believe that the earthquakes that we have gotten over the past few weeks is God trying to get our attention. Even scientists call this activity unusual. We are seeing outbreaks of tornadoes like never before. Some weather agencies are even saying that Tornado Alley has gotten wider. These unusual storms are causing loss of life and the loss of millions of dollars in buildings and other property damage. I believe these are warnings from God. 
On that fateful night, a young man who was on look out duty on the Titanic turned to one of his superiors and said, “I say old man we are surrounded by ice.” The man r
eplied, “Shut up, shut up! We’re busy.” So many of us are like this. We are so preoccupied with doing this or that, running here or there that we refuse to stop and take a look at the warning signs. The Titanic received five warnings about ice over the course of 12 hours. But they didn’t take heed. They were confident that their plans would work out. Let us not make the dangerous mistake of ignoring the warning signs. 
3. Thirdly, the church as a whole needs to rise up and do something. That Sunday morning, before the Titanic went down, Captain E.J. Smith held church services on the ship. Normally, this was to be followed by a lifeboat drill for passengers and crew, but on this day, there was no drill. After all, the ship was unsinkable. 
Many churches in America today are just like this. We have church service after church service and we go to conference after conference, yet nothing gets done. We don’t pray like we should. We don’t evangelize, We don’t disciple people. The church must return to being an active body of believers determined to carryout God’s plan. Every church is a body with hands and feet. We must begin to live out our faith by acting on it. 
In closing, I ran across a story about an unsung hero of the Titanic disaster. He was a preacher by the name of John Harper. He was the newly called pastor of the Moody Church who was on his way to Chicago. After the collision, he got his 6-year-old daughter into a lifeboat, but apparently made no attempt to save himself. Instead, he ran throughout the ship yelling, “Women, children and unsaved into the lifeboats!” Survivors report that he began witnessing to anyone who would listen, and even continued to preach after he had jumped into the water, clinging to a piece of wreckage. Harper’s final moments were recounted by a survivor four years after the disaster. “I was drifting alone on a spar that night, when the tide brought Mr. Harper, also on a piece of wreck, near me. He said, ‘Man, are you saved?’ ‘No,’ I said, ‘I am not.’ He replied, ‘Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ and thou shalt be saved.’ The waves bore him away, but strange to say, brought him back a little later, and he said, ‘Are you saved now?’ ‘No,’ I said, ‘I cannot honestly say that I am.’ He said again, ‘Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ and thou shalt be saved,’ and shortly after, he went down; and there, alone in the night, and with two miles of water under me, I believed.”
That story is a perfect example of what the church should be doing as the Titanic is going down. We should be busy telling souls about Jesus Christ and snatching people from the icy waters of sin , death, and hell. This is the only hope for America. Everything else that we have tried has failed. While the Titanic is going down, the church of Jesus Christ should be busy telling people how to be saved.
Now you might be listening today, and you might be one of those who are on the Titanic without Jesus Christ. You might be trusting in the security of your own good works. Or you might be nearly drowning in the seas of a sinful life and you face an eternity without God on the other side of death.
There is only one way to prepare for your eternal spiritual existence after this life — and that is by giving your life to Jesus Christ before you die. The fact of the matter is that anybody who dies without Jesus Christ will suffer an eternity separated from God in a place called hell. If you want to be delivered from your sin, from death, and from the eternal punishment of sin, allow me to share with you very quickly how you can accept the free gift of salvation that Jesus Christ died and rose from the dead to give to you.
John 3:16 reads, “For God so loved the world, that He gave His only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in Him should not perish, but have everlasting life.”  
“For God so loved the world” means that if you are in this world, God loves you. No matter what you have done. 
“that He gave His only begotten Son.” His name is Jesus Christ. He suffered, bled, and died on the cross for your sins and for mine, and He was buried and rose again. 
“that whosoever believeth in Him”, the word “whosoever” means anybody at anytime. “believeth in Him” means to trust in Him, to depend upon Him, to rely on Him, or to have faith in Him for your salvation. 
“should not perish” where? in hell. 
“but have everlasting life” in Heaven with God.  
The Bible also says in Romans 10:9 and 13: “That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved…. For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.” 
Dear friend, if you are willing to believe on the Lord Jesus Christ for salvation, please pray with me this simple prayer: Heavenly Father, I realize that I am a sinner and that I have done some bad things in my life. For Jesus Christ sake, please forgive me of my sins. I now believe with all of my heart that Jesus Christ died for me, was buried, and rose again. Lord Jesus, please come into my heart and save my soul and change my life today. Amen.
