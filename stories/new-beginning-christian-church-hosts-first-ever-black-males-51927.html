New Beginning Christian Church Hosts First-Ever Black Males Matter Awards & Showcase

January 11, 2015 Black

A ceremony on Saturday afternoon honored the contributions of black men along the coast.

The first-ever Black Males Matter Awards & Showcase celebrated 17 men. The event was held at New Beginning Christian Church and organized by Joy and Henry Gregory, a couple from Leland.

The two conceived the idea amidst nationwide protests in recent months over the lives of black men who have died in confrontations with police.

“To me as a black male, this event is our protest. We are making a statement,” said Henry Gregory.

“We see so many negative images, but this is an opportunity to see those positive images and to say to the brethren, ‘Thank you for being who you are,’” he said.

Those honored work and serve in diverse capacities, from ministry to public office. Stephen Bradley pastors Ekklesia Church in Wilmington and was among the award winners.

“Many times what you’re doing, you don’t really feel that you’re really being that effective, so it’s nice every now and then to get an ‘atta boy’ from someone,” he said.
