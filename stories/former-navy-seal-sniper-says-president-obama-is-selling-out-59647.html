Former Navy SEAL Sniper Says President Obama Is ‘Selling Out’ US National Security

March 3, 2015 National

A former Navy SEAL sniper is the latest U.S. veteran to speak out against Obama administration policies he says are putting America and its armed forces at great risk.

Scott Taylor’s new book is called, Trust Betrayed: Barack Obama, Hillary Clinton, and the Selling Out of America’s National Security.

Taylor says the administration’s national security plans and foreign policy are based on “wishful thinking” and that they are putting U.S. troops in danger.

Reporter/Anchor Mark Martin spoke with Taylor about the administration’s policies and actions regarding sensitive information leaks, ISIS, and the refusal to use the term “Islamic extremism.”

“The fact that we don’t take strong positions and we can’t even say things like Islamic terrorism in public, those things are harming our foreign policy,” Taylor told CBN News.
