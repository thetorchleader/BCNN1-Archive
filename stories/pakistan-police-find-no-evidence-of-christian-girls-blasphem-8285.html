Pakistan Police Find No Evidence of Christian Girl’s Blasphemy

September 23, 2012 Uncategorized

A Pakistani official has said that there is no evidence that would try a young Christian girl on charges of blasphemy. The official added that there is indication that the cleric of a nearby mosque may have put pages of a Quran into a shopping bag the girl was carrying.


A Pakistani police official says no incriminating evidence has been found that a young Christian girl desecrated Islam’s holy book.
But Munir Jaffery said his final report filed with an Islamabad court Saturday showed there is indication that the cleric of a nearby mosque tampered with evidence by putting pages of a Quran into a shopping bag the girl had been carrying.
The case gained huge attention because of the girl’s age and questions about her mental capacity.
SOURCE: The Associated Press
