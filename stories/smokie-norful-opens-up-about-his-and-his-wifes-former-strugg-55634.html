Smokie Norful Opens Up About His and His Wife’s Former Struggle to Have Children

February 7, 2015 Entertainment

Smokie Norful and wife Carla Norful, once infertile, are now the proud parents of three: Ashley (Not pictured), Tre and Ashton (Credit: Getty Images)

CDC statistics show one out of every five couples in America suffers from infertility problems and more than seven million women and their partners in the US are infertile.

Chicago-based GRAMMY® award-winning recording artist and pastor, Smokie Norful, 41, can relate.

During a Thursday, Feb. 5 appearance on TBN’s “Praise the Lord,” the “I Need You Now” singer opened up about his and his wife Carla’s former struggle to conceive.

“My wife and I, we have an awesome testimony of having to go through a lot of medical trials, a lot of medical challenges even, to try to conceive before we were ever able to have our first son,” he said.

Now the proud father of three—Ashley, Tré and Ashton—Norful reflects on what ultimately turned things around for his family.
