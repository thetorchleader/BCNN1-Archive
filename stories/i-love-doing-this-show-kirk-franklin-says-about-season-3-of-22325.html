“I Love Doing This Show!”, Kirk Franklin Says About Season 3 of “The American Bible Challenge”

May 15, 2014 Entertainment

The Game Show Network has a great mix and a monster hit with the game show The American Bible Challenge. It is the station’s highest rated original series of all time. The American Bible Challenge returns with hosts by Jeff Foxworthy and Kirk Franklin.

Kirk took a few minutes out to talk about the new show. Here is what he had to tell us.

E8: What did you think of your debut on the show last season?

Kirk Franklin: It was cool. I learned I didn’t know as much as I thought I did. (laughs) So, I learned so much.

E8: What’s different this season?

Kirk Franklin: There’s more music and more engagement.

E8: What did you most enjoy?

Kirk Franklin: I just love people I had such a great time with the people on the show.

E8: What was your favorite moment?

Kirk Franklin: There were these religious sisters on and they had swagger! It was great I really enjoyed them so much. We had fun.

E8: What is your favorite scripture?

Kirk Franklin: Romans 8: 28 -And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose.
