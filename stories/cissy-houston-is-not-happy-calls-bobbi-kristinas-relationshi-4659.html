Cissy Houston is Not Happy; Calls Bobbi Kristina’s Relationship with her “Brother” ‘Incestuous’

March 17, 2012 Uncategorized

Bobbi Kristina
may be flirting with disaster now that her grandmother is vocally
calling the 19-year-old’s relationship with her “adopted brother” Nick
Gordon “incestuous.”

Cissy Houston, Whitney Houston‘s elderly mother, previously voiced her disapproval of the two’s relationship, but she is now taking it one step further.

A friend of Cissy tells TMZ that the matriarch said “what they’re doing
is incestuous,” in reference to recent video of Bobbi Kristina and Gordon, 22, embracing and kissing in public.

Whitney never formally adopted Gordon, but reportedly took him in when
he was just 12 years old after his mother was unable to care for him.

He and Bobbi Kristina are not blood-related.

The pair do, however, refer to one another as “brother” and “sister,”
and Gordon currently resides at the Houston family home in Atlanta.

After rumors that the two are dating surfaced earlier this month, Gordon took to Twitter to slam haters.

“Yea we got a little closer and what!” he wrote.
