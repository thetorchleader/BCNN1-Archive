It Is Being Reported that Beyonce Is Planning to Divorce Jay Z After ‘On the Run’ Tour

August 16, 2014 Entertainment

The new Us Weekly has Beyonce on the cover under the headline “Enough! …Beyonce Plans Divorce.”

The tabloid magazine quotes “insiders” who say the 32-year-old singer has a plan of action in place for if/when she decides to call it quits with Jay Z. One source says, “she is done” after their remaining On The Run shows in Paris, France.

A source previously told Us Weekly that the spouses “stayed separately” while in New York and L.A. for their On The Run tour. And another insider said Beyonce went without her husband to look for apartments in Manhattan, including a $21.5 million penthouse in Chelsea.
