Queen Latifah Hosts the 2014 Hollywood Film Awards Show

November 16, 2014 Entertainment

Queen Latifah

The multi-talented award-winning actress, musician and talk show host Queen Latifah, will host the inaugural broadcast of the Hollywood Film Awards, live from the Hollywood Palladium Friday, November 14, 2014 from 8:00-10:00 pm. on the CBS Television Network. “I’m honored to be a part of such a legendary award show,” stated Latifah. “The Hollywood Film Awards have been a staple in the awards season for years, and I’m excited to be the first host to bring them to television!”

Source: LA Sentinel
