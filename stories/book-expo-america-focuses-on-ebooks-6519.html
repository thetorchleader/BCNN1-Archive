Book Expo America Focuses on eBooks

June 10, 2012 Uncategorized

People’s interest in e-books is mounting over time and the number of users is increasing. eBooks are portable and cheaper than paperbacks. People can read them via various tech-gadgets such as desktop PCs, smart phones, tablet PCs, and e-book devices. They are also available anytime and anywhere nationwide and worldwide transcending time and space.
On June 5 to 7, 2012, it was Book Expo America (BEA) Week in New York City, which means book industry people and fans were flocking to the Javits Center to meet, greet, sign things, have things signed, and accrue as many galleys as possible.
What caught visitors’ eye at this year’s book fair were the companies that are concentrating on digital distribution.
Apple’s iBookstore reportedly hosts 650,000 premium digital titles from more than 1,000 publishers, such as Urim Books, Random House, HarperCollins, BBC Audiobooks America, and Bloomsbury.
The Apple’s iBookstore participated in BookExpo America (BEA) 2012. Representatives from the iBookstore provided one-on-one conversations on June 5 and June 6.
Representatives of Urim Books attended BEA to research about the exciting new trends in the book industry and find opportunities to promote Urim Books’ e-works.
As of June 2012, Urim Books is distributing Dr. JaeRock Lee’s books in Portuguese, Spanish, German, English, Italian, Russian, Japanese, Korean beginning with Heaven, Hell, and The Measure of Faith in iBooks store for iPhone and iPad users.
Amazon Kindle Direct, Lulu and Xlibris were touting the successes of their self-published authors.
