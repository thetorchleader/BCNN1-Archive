Andrew Luck Leads Colts to 38-3 Victory Over Rams In First Preseason Game

August 13, 2012 Uncategorized

Earlier this week, Andrew Luck said he wasn’t sure when the Indianapolis Colts were playing their first preseason game. In other words, the rookie quarterback had his head so deep in the playbook that he wasn’t sure when he would be making his NFL debut.
Hey, it’s not like the game sneaked up on the league’s top overall draft pick last spring. It’s been on the schedule for several months.
But if you think Luck wasn’t ready, think again: On his first pass attempt, Luck tossed a short pass to running back Donald Brown, who took it 63 yards – untouched – for a touchdown to give the Colts a 7-0 lead over the St. Louis Rams.
Longtime Colts fans had seen something similar before. In 1998, Peyton Manning’s first NFL pass was an 8-yard route to Marvin Harrison, who sprinted for a 48-yard score.
“I think (quarterbacks coach) Clyde (Christensen) told me that about a week ago,” Luck said, according to The Associated Press. “I guess it’s coincidence. Funny, huh?”
It was just a five-yard toss, but who are we to downplay it?
Heck, Colts owner Jim Irsay, who apparently is rich enough to buy exclamation points by the bushel, couldn’t control himself: “Historic beginning!!!!!!!!!!!!” he tweeted. “The legend has begun!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!” (It’s okay, for the rest of the year, Luck is probably going to be a question mark.)
