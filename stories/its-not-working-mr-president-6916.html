It’s Not Working, Mr. President

July 7, 2012 Uncategorized

Obama’s excuses are getting old


The Obama recovery is sputtering like a limp balloon, generating a mere 80,000 jobs in the month of June and leaving the unemployment rate stagnant at 8.2%
At this sluggish job-creation rate — which didn’t even keep up with population growth — the country can expect a return to full employment by approximately the 12th of never.
President Obama and the rest of Washington ought to be treating this economic stall as the crisis it is — especially for 12.7 million jobless Americans, whose ranks swelled by 29,000 since May.
Yet the President stuck to his out-of-touch, glass-half-full message — that any growth, no matter how feeble, is “a step in the right direction.”
As if barely treading water were a step in any direction at all.
Obama also engaged in another round of fingerpointing — at the Bush administration, at Congress — that puts not a single paycheck in any worker’s pocket.
It’s true that Obama inherited the worst recession in a generation, and that Congress has stymied the President’s proposals without offering viable alternatives.
But it’s Obama’s job, as occupant of the most powerful office in the nation, to lead us out of the doldrums. And his excuses for not doing so are getting old.
