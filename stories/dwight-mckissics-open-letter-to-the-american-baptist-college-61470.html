Dwight McKissic’s Open Letter to the American Baptist College Board of Trustees – Who Left The Gate Open?

March 13, 2015 Christian

William Dwight McKissic, Sr.

AN OPEN LETTER TO THE ABC BOARD OF TRUSTEES
“Who Left The Gate Open?”
THE AMERICAN BAPTIST COLLEGE BOARD OF TRUSTEES
By William Dwight Mckissic, Sr.

How is it possible that a lesbian bishop, legally married to a female, be invited to preach and lead the Tuesday night worship service, at the prestigious 58th Annual Garnett-Nabrit Lecture Series, on the campus of American Baptist College, Nashville, TN? Who opened the gate and let Bishop Yvette Flunder, for two consecutive years, come and preach/lecture to impressionable students—while she is arguably the face of the—“I’m Black, I’m Gay, I’m a Christian and I’m Proud”—rapidly growing movement? This is a critical question that National Baptists must answer, address and redress: Who Left the Gate Open? How did we get here?

If National Baptists don’t close this gate, now—and I mean “soon and very soon”—we will see more and more episodes of same-sex married partners leading worship and in any and all capacities in Baptist churches. Does Bishop Flunder’s appearance at ABC signify the future for our churches, or a momentary lapse in parameters?

As I contemplate this question of responsibility and accountability as it relates to Bishop Flunder’s appearance at ABC, it reminds me of a story told by the late, great National Baptist preacher, Mack King Carter of Ft. Lauderdale, FL:

On the floor of an Annual Session of the NBC in the late ‘90’s, against the backdrop of a highly publicized money scandal the NBC was in the midst of, Pastor Carter went to the microphone and told a story similar to this: The owner of a country meat house, discovered early one morning that a fox had come in overnight and ravaged his meat. He’d also noticed on his way to the meat house that the worker assigned to keep the gate to the meat house locked at night, had neglected his duty the previous night. As the meat owner assessed the damage, he thought to himself, I don’t know who I’m angriest with: The fox that took the spoils, or the worker that left the gate open.

As many of us contemplate the question—“How did we get here?”—I’m like the country meat house owner; I’m unsure who deserves the most anger or responsibility: The fox that ravaged the meat or the worker that left the gate open?

Bishop Yvette Flunder didn’t invite herself to ABC. As it relates to responsibility and accountability for her appearance, she is most innocent. There is no basis to make her out to be a villain in this real live case scenario, or “reality show.” Who, then, are the responsible parties that must be held responsible and accountable for Bishop Flunder’s appearance? Who left the gate open?

Ultimately, the American Baptist College Trustee Board are the individuals responsible for Bishop Yvette Flunder—a lesbian bishop, married to Shirley Miller—preaching and leading worship at the Garnett-Nabrit Lecture Series. The ABC Board of Trustees has remained silent during this whole discussion. Not only are they silent, but to the public at large they are invisible. But, back to the question, who left the gate open?
