Upcoming “Destined” Movie Looking for African-American Boys and Girls

October 19, 2014 Black

African-American boys and girls are currently being sought to play the younger versions of some of Hollywood’s new talent in a feature film called “Destined.”

The movie is expected to shoot in the metro Detroit area beginning in early November.

Two of metro Detroit’s African-American filmmakers, Qasim Basir and Sultan Sharrief, are searching for African-American boys and girls who are 12 to 14 years old who would like to be in the film, which are said to be paid speaking roles.

Parents should send a recent snapshot of their child along with his or her age to detroitcastingcompany@gmail.com with the subject heading “TOMMY” for boys and “MAYA” for girls.

The deadline for submissions is Oct. 20.

SOURCE: Click on Detroit
Troy A. Blevins
