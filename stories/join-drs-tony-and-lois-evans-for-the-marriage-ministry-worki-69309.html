Join Drs. Tony and Lois Evans for the “Marriage & Ministry: Working Together Effectively” Webcast Today

April 23, 2015 Christian

Marriage & Ministry: Working Together Effectively
Pastors’ Wives Ministry- an affiliate of The Urban Alternative with Dr. Tony Evans
Thursday, April 23, 2015 from 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM (CDT)

Drs. Tony and Lois Evans will be joined with friends in ministry Larry & Devi Titus, and Vince & Rhoda Gonzales, to give insight on how to successfully navigate working in ministry as a married couple.

This is a free webcast for pastors and pastors’ wives, to encourage and equip them to fully embrace the different strengths and gifts each mate brings to the ministry they’ve been called to, working together effectively for maximum kingdom impact.
