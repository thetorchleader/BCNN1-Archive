Conservative Radio Host Michael Medved Battling Stage Three Throat Cancer

February 3, 2015 Media

Michael Medved

Popular conservative radio host Michael Medved has stage three cancer.

Medved made the announcement Friday during his radio show and on his website.

He said he’ll be taking a break from the broadcast while he undergoes treatment.

“I always knew that it would take something serious to keep me away from the microphone I cherish for an extended period of time,” he wrote. “As it turns out, I am facing something serious. It’s called cancer.”

Medved was diagnosed in the middle of December and began treatment immediately. He says his prognosis of full recovery is good.
