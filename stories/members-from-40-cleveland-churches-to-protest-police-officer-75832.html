Members from 40 Cleveland Churches to Protest Police Officer’s Acquittal

May 26, 2015 Black

Congregation members of Elizabeth Baptist Church a day after the not-guilty verdict was read in the case of Officer Michael Brelo, in Cleveland, May 24, …

Members of about 40 Cleveland-area churches planned to protest the acquittal of a white patrolman charged in the deaths of two unarmed black motorists.

Parishioners from the churches making up the Greater Cleveland Congregations planned to march through downtown Cleveland late Tuesday morning. The Northeast Ohio Media Group reports (http://bit.ly/1LCXZ3r ) the Rev. Jawanza Colvin said the rally would be peaceful and police had been contacted in advance.

A judge on Saturday found Patrolman Michael Brelo (BREE’-loh) not guilty of voluntary manslaughter in the November 2012 deaths of two unarmed motorists in a 137-shot barrage of police gunfire after a high-speed chase.

The Rev. Jesse Jackson told the Associated Press Monday that the verdict in Brelo’s case was an “unjust conclusion,” but urged that protests must remain nonviolent and disciplined.

Source: The AP
