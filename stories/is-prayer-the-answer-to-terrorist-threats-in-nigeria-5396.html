Is Prayer the Answer to Terrorist Threats In Nigeria?

April 21, 2012 Uncategorized

Threats against Christians in Nigeria are serious and represent more than persecution alone.
A terrorist group is claiming responsibility for deadly attacks against Christians, police and military in the Northern part of Nigeria. Since the government seems unable to effectively intervene and protect Christians, Rae Burnett of Christian Aid Mission believes the only effective approach is international prayer.
 
“That’s what we’re praying for and hoping for is that the Lord will bring his people together and protect them from this terrible onslaught of not only just Islam, but terrorism in the name of Islam,” says Burnett.
 
The terrorist group, Boko Haram, established a deadline, which has passed for Christians in the northern part of Nigeria, to either convert to Islam or leave. A relative few did leave.
