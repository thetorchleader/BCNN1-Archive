Price Chapel A.M.E. Church in Los Angeles to Begin Vacation Bible School on August 4th

July 27, 2014 Black

Rev. Don C. Wallace

Vacation Bible School (VBS) opens on Monday, August 4, at 6:45 p.m., at Price Chapel A.M.E. Church, 4000 West Slauson Avenue in Los Angeles.

Classes for children and adults will focus on the theme, ‘2014 Cultivation,’ with a scriptural reference of Matthew 13:1-23.  The lessons will share strategies to remove distractions that prevent believers from enjoying an abundant life in the Lord, explained Pastor Cedric V. Alexander.

“We welcome people throughout the community to bring their entire family to this week-long program,” he said.

“You will definitely grow in your walk with Christ through the series of exciting studies developed by our superintendent, the Rev. Don C. Wallace.
