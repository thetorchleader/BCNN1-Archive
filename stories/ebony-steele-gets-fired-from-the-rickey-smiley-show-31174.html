Ebony Steele Gets Fired from The Rickey Smiley Show

July 22, 2014 Media

Here’s some bad news! Ebony Steele has been fired from “The Rickey Smiley Morning Show.”

The radio personality graced the morning show’s fans with her raspy and distinctive voice for seven years, but that voice is no more.

Steele is being replaced by model Claudia Jordan, reports The Atlanta Journal-Constitution.

Speculation grew as Steele had been absent from the morning show as well as “Dish Nation.”

There is no known reason why Steele was fired from the morning show as of now.

Steele has yet to provide public explanation of her firing, but she reportedly feels no ill will to her former employers.
