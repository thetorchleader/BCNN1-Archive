4 People Hospitalized After Shooting at Houston Caribbean Festival

Date not recorded in the original archive caribbean, National

Four people are in the hospital after a shooting at the Houston Caribbean Festival. The shooter is still on the run.

Police say the shooting happened in an outdoor amphitheater on Lew Briggs Road.

Police believe all the shooting victims were male.

One witness, Deion Woodson, said, “It’s usually a friendly Caribbean fest, everybody have fun, but this time one person started fighting and that’s basically what happened and started shooting.”

Officials say two women were also injured after they were trampled by crowds leaving the scene. There were 5,000 people at the festival.

Investigators say 30 off-duty police officers were working this event, but none of them were inside the building at the time of the shooting.
