LISTEN: Volcano threatens homes in Hawaii; NATO approves new defense force (Second Coming Watch Update #503 with Daniel Whyte III)

September 6, 2014 Second Coming Watch

Daniel Whyte III

[ca_audio url_mp3=”https://www.buzzsprout.com/4994/202104-volcano-threatens-homes-in-hawaii-nato-approves-new-defense-force-second-coming-watch-update-503.mp3&#8243; url_ogg=”” download=”true” align=”none”] (Download/Listen to MP3)

Let’s take a quick look at today’s prophecy-related headlines which point towards the second coming of the Lord Jesus Christ and the end of the world as we know it.

First, today, under the Sign category of INCREASED NATURAL DISASTERS — According to Agence France-Presse (AFP), Hawaii’s Big Island has declared a state of emergency as lava from an active volcano threatens nearby homes, which could be evacuated within days. The Hawaii County mayor signed a proclamation after the lava flow from the volcano advanced to within a mile of a residential area. Hawaii Island, or the Big Island, is the largest of the eight main islands which make up the Pacific U.S. state — an archipelago that includes hundreds of smaller volcanic islands.

Second, today, under the Sign category of DISTRESS AMONG NATIONS — According to Reuters, NATO approved wide-ranging plans on Friday to boost its defences in eastern Europe, aiming to reassure allies nervous about Russia’s intervention in Ukraine that the U.S.-led alliance will shield them from any attack. The plan, adopted at a summit in Wales, includes creating a “spearhead” rapid reaction force and pre-positioning supplies and equipment in east European countries so they can be reinforced within days in a crisis. The 28-nation alliance also agreed for the first time to add cyber defence to its core mission, meaning that a major cyber attack on a member state could trigger a military response.

Third, today, under the Sign category of WARS & RUMORS OF WARS — According to The Los Angeles Times, Ukraine’s president announced a cease-fire on Friday in the 5-month-old conflict against pro-Russian separatists, halting gunfire and artillery exchanges in his nation’s embattled east amid deep skepticism that the truce will hold. Government forces and allied militiamen deployed around a strategic southeastern port abided by the cease-fire. But the fighters made it clear to reporters that they disagreed with the decision to hold fire on a day when they had succeeded in pushing back encroaching Russian troops and armor. At the conclusion of a NATO summit in Wales, President Obama said Western leaders were hopeful that the cease-fire would lead to peace but added that “based on past experience [leaders were] also skeptical that the separatists will follow through and the Russians will stop violating Ukraine’s sovereignty.”

Fourth, today, under the Sign category of INCREASED PERSECUTION — According to Religion Today, the building that is home to a 500-member Sudanese congregation has been seized by government security agents. Sudan’s National Intelligence and Security Services padlocked and shuttered the Sudan Pentecostal Church building in Khartoum on August 24th. A source who requested anonymity said parishioners fear the government might sell the church building which houses the Khartoum Christian Center which has been in that location for 20 years.

Fifth, today, under the Sign category of SIGNS IN THE HEAVENS — According to CBS News, NASA says a newly discovered asteroid will pass “very close” to Earth on Sunday. The space rock is estimated to be 60 feet long and it’s expected to fly over New Zealand about 2:18 p.m. eastern time. It is not expected to hit Earth or any of the thousands of satellites orbiting the planet. But it will give astronomers and scientists a chance to study it.

The Bible says in Luke 21:25: “And there shall be signs in the sun, and in the moon, and in the stars; and upon the earth distress of nations, with perplexity; the sea and the waves roaring.”

Our second coming quote for today is from Joe Stowell. He said, “The return of Christ represents not only the ultimate sense of accountability but the ultimate sense of hope as well.”
