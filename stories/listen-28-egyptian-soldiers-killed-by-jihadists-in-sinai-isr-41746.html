LISTEN: 28 Egyptian soldiers killed by jihadists in Sinai; Israeli minister: Map of Middle East being redrawn (Second Coming Watch Update #524 with Daniel Whyte III)

October 26, 2014 Second Coming Watch

Daniel Whyte III

Let’s take a quick look at today’s prophecy-related headlines which point towards the second coming of the Lord Jesus Christ and the end of the world as we know it.

First, today, under the Sign category of THE TURMOIL IN ISRAEL & MIDDLE EAST — According to Reuters, the Egyptian president convened an emergency meeting of his senior defense advisers on Friday just hours after a deadly terrorist attack claimed the lives of at least 28 soldiers in the Sinai Peninsula. At least 28 security personnel were killed by a car bomb that targeted two armored vehicles stopped at a checkpoint. At least 20 soldiers were also injured. Security forces in Egypt are facing a jihadist insurgency that has killed hundreds of soldiers and policemen since the army toppled President Mohamed Morsi last year.

Second, today, under the Sign category of THE TURMOIL IN ISRAEL & THE MIDDLE EAST — According to NPR, Israel’s Defense Minister Moshe Ya’alon says that a future map of the Middle East will look very different than the one that exists today. He said that the borders of many Arab states were drawn up by Westerners a century ago and wars in recent years show that a number of them are doomed to break apart. He said nations such as Libya, Syria, and Iraq are “artificial nation-states” and that the world is seeing the “collapse of this Western idea.”

Third, today, under the Sign category of DISEASES & EPIDEMICS — According to The Associated Press, Mali reported its first case of Ebola, marking a major setback for West African efforts to contain the deadly virus that now has affected six countries in the region and left nearly 5,000 dead. The patient was a 2-year-old girl who had come from neighboring Guinea, where the Ebola epidemic began last December. Also, in New York City an American doctor who worked with Ebola patients in Guinea tested positive for the disease after being placed in isolation at a hospital.

Fourth, today, under the Sign category of THE TURMOIL IN ISRAEL & THE MIDDLE EAST — According to The Jerusalem Post, a London-based newspaper is reported that the Palestinian Authority has agreed to delay by two months its plan to seek a Security Council resolution calling for an Israeli withdrawal to the pre-1967 lines within three years. The paper quoted Palestinian sources in Ramallah as saying that the PA leadership will give US Secretary of State John Kerry a two-month window to present his own plan for peace. The sources said that the PA leadership was nevertheless determined to proceed with the Security Council bid.

Fifth, today, under the Sign category of DISTRESS AMONG NATIONS — According to The Washington Times, Russian military provocations have increased so much over the seven months since Moscow annexed Crimea from Ukraine that Washington and its allies are scrambling defense assets on a nearly daily basis in response to air, sea and land incursions by Russian forces. Not only is Moscow continuing to foment unrest in Eastern Ukraine, U.S. officials and regional security experts say Russian fighter jets are testing U.S. reaction times over Alaska and Japan’s ability to scramble planes over its northern islands — all while haunting Sweden’s navy and antagonizing Estonia’s tiny national security force.

The prophetic passage of Scripture we are looking at today is Genesis 7:4 which reads: “For yet seven days, and I will cause it to rain upon the earth forty days and forty nights; and every living substance that I have made will I destroy from off the face of the earth.”

Allow me to share with you some commentary on this passage from the Popular Bible Prophecy Commentary, edited by Tim LaHaye and Ed Hindson.

The Genesis narrative records that eventually humanity had become so morally self-corrupted that the Lord determines to destroy His creation with a flood and start anew. However, Noah, a “righteous” and “blameless” individual who, like Enoch before him, experiences an intimate relationship with God, has “found favor” with the Lord. The Lord graciously determines to preserve a faithful remnant of humanity and to establish a covenant with Noah. He instructs Noah to build an ark as a vessel of preservation for Noah and his family, along with representative pairs of every animal. One week prior to the flood, God specifies to Noah that it will continue to rain for “forty days and forty nights,” again reiterating His plan to destroy all life outside the safety of the ark. The narrative records the fulfillment of this prophecy in Genesis 7:10-23.

If the Lord tarries His coming and we live, we will continue looking at this prophecy in our next broadcast / podcast.

Our second coming quote for today is from Vance Havner. He said, “When I studied arithmetic, I remembered that the answers were in the back of the book. No matter how I floundered among my problems, the correct solution was on the last page. I have failed often in working out life’s problems, and I dwell in the midst of a people who are hopelessly trying to untangle the riddle of this present age. But I am cheered by one unfailing uncertainty — there is a Book that solves the enigma, and the answer is in the back of the Book: ‘Behold I come quickly.’ ‘Even so, come, Lord Jesus.’”

If you are not ready for the return of Jesus Christ, may I encourage you to get ready today by receiving Him as your Savior. John 3:16 says, “For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.” Just believe in your heart that Jesus Christ died for your sins, was buried, and rose from the dead by the power of God for you so that you can live eternally with Him. Pray and ask Him to come into your heart today, and He will. Romans 10:13 says, “For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.”

Keep looking up for your redemption draweth nigh. Let us join in the prayer of John the Revelator — “Even so, come, Lord Jesus.” God bless you.
