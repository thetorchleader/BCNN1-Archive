Atheist Protests ‘Year of the Bible’ Billboard

March 10, 2012 Uncategorized

A billboard in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania, that protests legislation designating 2012 as the “Year of the Bible” in Pennsylvania is being criticized by a legislator who calls it racist.

State Rep. Thaddeus Kirkland objected to the image of a shackled black slave above a Bible verse that says, “Slaves, obey your masters.” Kirkland, who supported the House resolution, says the billboard takes the Bible out of context and depicts racism and hatred.
The billboard a few blocks from the state Capitol is co-sponsored by a group called American Atheists.
The organization’s Pennsylvania director says it wants lawmakers to repeal the resolution celebrating a book he calls “barbaric.”
Source: The AP
