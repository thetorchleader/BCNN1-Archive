Donnie McClurkin to Join New Christ Memorial Church In Celebrating Birthday of Andrae and Sandra Crouch

June 26, 2014 Entertainment

On Sunday, June 29th Pastor Donnie McClurkin will join the New Christ Memorial Church family celebrating the birthday of Gospel Music Legend Pastor Andrae Crouch. Crouch shares a July 1st birthday with his twin sister, Pastor Sandra Crouch. The multiple Grammy award winner has endured several medical challenges over the past couple of months and GOSPELflava joins others across the country praying for his healing and restoration.
