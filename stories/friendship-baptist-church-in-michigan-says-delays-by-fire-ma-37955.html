Friendship Baptist Church in Michigan says Delays by Fire Marshal Is Hindering Ministry

October 7, 2014 Black

Members of Friendship Baptist Church are giving the fire marshal one more day after he didn’t show up to inspect their church Monday morning.

Last week’s court order says that no one is allowed to interfere with or try to prevent the inspection. Trustee board members said that they spoke with the fire marshal on Friday and he confirmed the 9 a.m. appointment, but never showed up. Members say this continued delay is taking a toll on their church ministry.

“We can’t fulfill our duties in the way that we would have,” said Donald Suttles the Trustee Board Chair. “We’re just disheartened, you know because – and you know it just gets us because – why?”
