Church Potluck Leaves One Dead, 23 Sick

April 25, 2015 Christian

Heath officials are attempting to trace the suspect food from a church potluck that may have caused a possible botulism outbreak that left one person dead and 23 other churchgoers sick, according to a report by Fox News.

Health workers are speaking with those who attended the Sunday potluck at Cross Pointe Free Will Baptist Church in Lancaster, Ohio to determine what might have caused the deadly outbreak, officials stated at a news conference April 22nd.

Fairfield Medical Center announced Tuesday that one woman had passed away from the suspected illness. Health officials are gathering samples from the church’s trash bin for clues. They have also been inspecting church members’ homes to see if homemade canned items that were used in the potluck may be to blame, according to Dr. Mark Aebi, with Fairfield County Health.

Officials have narrowed down their search to several ingredients that seem suspicious, Aebi told the press conference.

A botulism antitoxin reached Ohio at midnight Tuesday from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. It was delivered to several hospitals treating the 23 patients with suspected cases of botulism, Dr. Andrew Murry reported.
