The Allstate Tom Joyner Family Reunion Set to Celebrate Family Labor Day Weekend

July 12, 2014 Black

ORLANDO, Fla., July 7, 2014 /PRNewswire/ — Tom Joyner, host of the nationally syndicated Tom Joyner Morning Show, along with Allstate Insurance Company, is celebrating family fun with the 2014 Allstate Tom Joyner Family Reunion®. Labor Day weekend, August 28th – September 1st,  families from all over the country will join Joyner and his celebrity friends for a weekend filled with stellar concerts, tickets to a Walt Disney World Park, interaction with celebrities and leaders, enlightening seminars, and endless activities for people of all ages inside the free expo that is open to the public.

The event will be held at the Gaylord Palms Resort & Convention Center in Orlando, Florida, and will feature concerts from The Jacksons and Kenny ‘Babyface’ Edmonds, a Gospel Explosion including Yolanda Adams and James Fortune, and appearances by reality stars Kandi Burruss and Porsha Williams.

“The Allstate Tom Joyner Family Reunion creates an atmosphere of one true family that celebrates people and what we love,” says Joyner, whose radio show airs in markets all over the country with an audience of more than eight million listeners.
