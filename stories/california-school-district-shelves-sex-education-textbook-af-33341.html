California School District Shelves Sex Education Textbook After Parents said it was Inappropriate for High Schoolers

August 11, 2014 Education, National

The cover of the textbook “Your Health Today” published by McGraw Hill. The book is causing controversy among parents in the Fremont Unified School District, where it is slated to be taught to their ninth-grade students. The parents say the book’s diagrams and explicit descriptions are inappropriate for high school students. (Photo: Your Health Today)

Fremont Unified School District officials appear to be reversing course on a textbook that parents say contains inappropriate references to masturbation, sex toys and bondage.

In a statement released Friday, Superintendent Jim Morris recommended that the Board of Education shelve the implementation of the book, “Your Health Today,” until parents’ concerns could be further examined.

More than 600 people signed a petition calling for the district to rescind its decision to use the 392-page book in ninth-grade health classes. The disgruntled parents included Asfia Ahmed, who thought the material was geared toward older students.

“I feel that it’s not age appropriate for these kids,” she said. “I have read the book from first page to last, and most pages talk about college kids. It doesn’t relate to these kids at all.”

The district said it had been in contact with the book’s publisher, McGraw Hill, and was exploring the possibility of modifying the text to both meet students’ needs and address parents’ concerns.
