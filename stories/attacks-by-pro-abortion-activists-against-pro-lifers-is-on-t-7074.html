Attacks by Pro-Abortion Activists Against Pro-Lifers Is On the Rise

July 14, 2012 Uncategorized
Two pro-life veterans have been injured in recent attacks by pro-abortion activists.
Everett Stadig, a 69-year-old man, was shoved to the ground by an abortion supporter while gathering signatures in Denver to try to get a personhood amendment on the ballot in Colorado. He was hospitalized with a broken hip.
 
In a separate incident, the home of pro-life leaders Keith and Jennifer Mason was vandalized and a huge stone tossed through their glass front door. Also, a Florida pro-lifer, Steve Kinn — a disabled veteran — was knocked to the ground and hospitalized.
Operation Rescue president Troy Newman is very familiar with violence against pro-lifers over the years.
