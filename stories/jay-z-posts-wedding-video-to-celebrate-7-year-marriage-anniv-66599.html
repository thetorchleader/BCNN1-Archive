Jay Z Posts Wedding Video to Celebrate 7-Year Marriage Anniversary

April 10, 2015 Entertainment

jay-z-beyonce

Jay Z is celebrating his wedding anniversary to Beyoncé with a gift for their fans.

The “99 Problems” rapper shared a brief clip of his and his superstar wife’s nuptials Tuesday on Instagram to mark their seven-year wedding anniversary, which the couple celebrated April 4.

Beyoncé and Jay Z famously kept their nuptials under wraps when they tied the knot in 2008, in a small, private ceremony held at their New York City apartment.

So it was a treat for fans to see the clip in which they exchange vows, with Jay Z wearing a tuxedo and Beyoncé dressed in a traditional white gown and veil.

After Jay Z slips a diamond wedding ring on his bride’s finger, the clip concluded with the triumphant couple walking down the aisle as husband and wife.
