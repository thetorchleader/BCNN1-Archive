President Obama Calls Boston Marathon Winner Meb Keflezighi

April 22, 2014 National

Meb Keflezighi after winning the Boston Marathon. (Photo: Elise Amendola, AP)

Boston Marathon men’s winner Meb Keflezighi says he’s received a lot of kudos since Monday’s race, but one stands out.

“I have received many congratulatory calls, but I just received THE call from President @barackobama,” tweeted Keflezighi.

Keflezighi is the first American to win the men’s Boston Marathon since 1983.

USA TODAY
