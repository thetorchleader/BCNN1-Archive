FOX to Feature Samaritan’s Purse Program Called “Operation Heal Our Patriots” as Labor Day Special – Tonight at 7p ET

September 1, 2014 Entertainment

Four couples featured in Operation Heal Our Patriots in photos released in July, 2014. (PHOTO: HTTP://WWW.SAMARITANSPURSE.ORG)

This Labor Day, Greta Van Susteren is honoring America’s veterans in the special, “The Next Battle: When Our Heroes Come Home,” airing Monday at 7p ET on Fox News Channel.

This documentary-style program is unlike anything you’ve seen on Fox News Channel. Shot with a hand-held camera by Fox News correspondent Griff Jenkins, Greta talks to veterans and their spouses who have traveled to the Samaritan’s Lodge Alaska for Operation Heal Our Patriots.

Thanks to Reverend Franklin Graham and Samaritan’s Purse, vets and their loved ones take part in marriage counseling, and spiritual and physical activities in the Alaskan wilderness.

“This is a program Griff and I put together ourselves because we have such a deep commitment to our vets and their spouses and all their sacrifices,” Greta says in the video above.

Check out behind-the-scenes photos below and see how drone technology played a role in producing this program.

Then, tune in Monday at 7p ET to Fox News Channel to watch “The Next Battle: When Our Heroes Come Home.”
