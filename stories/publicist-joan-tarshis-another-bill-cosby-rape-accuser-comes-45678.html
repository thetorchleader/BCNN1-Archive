Publicist Joan Tarshis, Another Bill Cosby Rape Accuser, Comes Forward

November 18, 2014 Black

An April 23, 2014 photo of Bill Cosby.
(Photo: Brad Barket, Brad Barket/Invision/AP)

The day after Bill Cosby refused to answer questions on NPR about multiple sexual assault allegations that have recently become public again, another woman has come forward with an incident from her past involving the actor.

Publicist Joan Tarshis wrote an essay for Hollywood Elsewhere on Sunday, recounting two times that Cosby raped her in 1969 when she was 19 years old.

Tarshis, who was trying to build a writing career in the business, writes that she had flown to Los Angeles from New York. She was staying with two friends who took her to have lunch with Cosby at cottage at Universal Studios, where he was shooting The Bill Cosby Show. He “never drank alcohol,” she writes, but he “always topped my Bloody Marys with beer, which he called a ‘redeye.'”

She writes that Cosby seemed to like her and so he kept inviting her back. “I was naturally flattered.”
