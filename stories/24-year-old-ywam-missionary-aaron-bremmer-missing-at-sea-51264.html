24-Year-Old YWAM Missionary Aaron Bremmer Missing at Sea

January 6, 2015 Christian

This boat operated by YWAM Ships capsized off the coast of Hawaii. (Courtesy/YWAM Ships)

Aaron Bremmer, 24, is lost at sea. The Youth With a Mission (YWAM) missionary was on a boat with five other sailors that capsized off Hawaii’s Big Island.

The Honolulu Star-Advertiser reports that the group was planning to sail to Pacific Islands to host medical and dental clinics, Bibles and water purification devices as part of their gospel-spreading mission.

“He was an amazing young man,” said his aunt Chris Mason told the Star-Advertiser. He had so much energy. He couldn’t be contained.”

Mason referred to Bremmer as a “guardian angel” who ministered to her through life’s trials over the past year.

Bremmer was on a 75-foot sailboat off the Kona Coast on Friday because dangerous weather threatened the vessel, according to the Houston Chronicle, and large waves flipped the boat two or three times a few yards offshore on Saturday.
