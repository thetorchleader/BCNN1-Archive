David Brody Reports that Tim and Beverly LaHaye Will Endorse Newt Gingrich

January 13, 2012 Uncategorized

The Brody File has learned that Newt Gingrich will receive an endorsement from Evangelical figures Tim and Beverly LaHaye. The announcement and more details are coming shortly.
Tim LaHaye is the author of the famous Christian “Left Behind” book series and Beverly LaHaye is the original founder of Concerned Women For America.
The LaHaye’s approached the Gingrich campaign about endorsing the presidential candidate.
