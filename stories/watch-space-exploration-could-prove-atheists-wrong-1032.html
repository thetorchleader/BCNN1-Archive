WATCH: Space Exploration Could Prove Atheists Wrong

October 23, 2011 Uncategorized

Now that NASA has ended its space shuttle program, many wonder what’s next for space exploration.

Dr. Hugh Ross, founder and president of Reasons To Believe, says returning to the moon offers the most intriguing potential for scientific discovery.
Click play to watch part two of Ross’ interview with CBN News Reporter Lee Webb. 

In part one, he discusses the link between the Bible and some of today’s scientific questions. 

In part two, Ross talks about the future of space exploration and also why Christians shouldn’t be offended by or disinterested in science.
