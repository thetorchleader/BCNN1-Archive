Holly Robinson Peete and Rodney Peete Getting Family Docuseries on OWN

May 8, 2015 Entertainment

Courtesy of OWN: Oprah Winfrey Network

The couple will star in the network’s newest addition to its Saturday night lineup.

OWN is getting into business with the Peete family.

The network announced the docuseries For Peete’s Sake, which follows the lives of 21 Jump Street star Holly Robinson Peete and former NFL quarterback and co-host of Fox Sports’ The Best Damn Sports Show Period Rodney Peete.

The show will look at how the husband-and-wife duo balance their very public careers with their four kids and wild-and-crazy mom, Dolores. It will join the network’s Saturday night lineup alongside original series Welcome to Sweetie Pie’s, Raising Whitley and 2 Fat 2 Fly.

“As longtime members of The Oprah Show family, we’ve watched Holly and Rodney navigate successful careers in show business, a devoted marriage and a growing, love-filled family,” said OWN president Sheri Salata. “This new series gives an entertaining and honest look at a modern family that just happens to live in the celebrity spotlight.”

Robinson Peete added: “Rodney and I couldn’t be more thrilled to call OWN our new TV home. We are excited to share our family’s chaotic bliss and zany journeys with OWN’s loyal and authentic audience.”
