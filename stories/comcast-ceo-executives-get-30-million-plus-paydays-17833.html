Comcast CEO, Executives Get $30 Million Plus Paydays

April 13, 2014 Media

Comcast CEO Brian Roberts
(Photo: Carol Klino)

There’s nothing fuzzy in the compensation packages of Comcast CEO Brian Roberts and other key cable company executives.

Roberts – leading Comcast’s proposed $45 billion buyout of cable rival Time Warner Cable – received compensation valued at $26.3 million in 2013, up 5% from 2012. That doesn’t include a $5 million gain in the value of his pension and deferred pay, Comcast says in its annual proxy.

Roberts gained another $34.6 million acquiring previously awarded restricted shares and exercising stock options, Comcast says.

Stephen Burke, head of Comcast’s NBC Universal TV and entertainment unit, fared even better, gaining $48.9 million from previously awarded stock and exercising stock options. That’s on top of compensation valued at $26.9 million and a $4.1 million gain in the value of his pension and deferred pay. Comcast said part of Burke’s compensation is tied to a new employement agreement signed last August.

Roberts, 54, has been CEO since November 2002. Burke, 55, joined Comcast in 1998 and became chief executive of NBC Universal in 2011.

Comcast’s total shareholder return was 41% last year.

Source: USA Today | Gary Strauss
