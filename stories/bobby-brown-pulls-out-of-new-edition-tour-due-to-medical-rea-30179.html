Bobby Brown Pulls Out of New Edition Tour Due to Medical Reasons

July 13, 2014 Entertainment

Bobby Brown has pulled out of his New Edition tour.

The former husband of Whitney Houston has decided to quit after being unable to keep up with his bandmates due to medical reasons, TMZ reports.

‘I apologize to the fans of New Edition,’ Bobby said in a statement. ‘I really wanted to be with my brothers, but presently I am unable to give the fans what they typically expect from New Edition.’

TMZ claims that a representative for the 45-year-old has revealed Bobby recently underwent ‘an intrusive medical procedure’.

The singer was apparently too exhausted to perform on stage as a result after being cared for by a doctor.

MailOnline has reached out to a representative for Mr. Brown for comment.

New Edition are currently touring America, and it seems the show must go on without Bobby.

The band are scheduled to perform in Thackerville, Oklahoma on Friday night.

They will then continue to travel across the country over the next three weeks, with their final stop being a Los Angeles concert on August 3.
