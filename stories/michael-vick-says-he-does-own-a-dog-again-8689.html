Michael Vick says he Does Own a Dog Again

October 12, 2012 Uncategorized
A day after declining to say whether he owned a dog, Michael Vick released a statement Thursday confirming that his family had obtained one.
“I understand the strong emotions by some people about our family’s decision to care for a pet,” the Eagles quarterback said in a statement released by his publicist. “As a father, it is important to make sure my children develop a healthy relationship with animals.
“I want to ensure that my children establish a loving bond and treat all of God’s creatures with kindness and respect. Our pet is well cared for and loved as a member of our family. This is an opportunity to break the cycle. To that end, I will continue to honor my commitment to animal welfare and be an instrument of positive change.”
Last week, Vick tweeted a photograph of himself with his daughter seated at a table, presumably at home. To their right was an open box of Milk-Bone dog biscuits. The tweet later was deleted and replaced by a similar photo without the Milk-Bone box.
