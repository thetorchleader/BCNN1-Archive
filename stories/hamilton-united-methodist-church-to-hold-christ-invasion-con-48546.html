Hamilton United Methodist Church to Hold Christ Invasion Contemporary Worship Experience

December 7, 2014 Black

Jasmine Obregon

Pastor Gary Bernard Williams invites the community, especially youth and young adults, to the Christ Invasion Contemporary Worship experience on Sunday, December 7, at 5 p.m., at Hamilton United Methodist Church, 6440 South Figueroa Street in Los Angeles.

The guest speaker will be Jasmine Obregon, a 16-year-old member of Hamilton. Her message, entitled ‘Where Is Your Light,’ speaks to the season of Advent. Music will be provided by Laythan Amour, his band, and soloist, Rush.  Following the service, refreshments will be served at the Coffee Café.

Obregon, who recently accepted her call to the ministry, attends Morningside High School in Inglewood.  She enjoys singing as well as participating in youth retreats and the United Methodist Annual Conference.

Christ Invasion is a youth and young adult collaboration of Faith and Hamilton United Methodist Churches, both under the spiritual leadership of Pastor Williams.
