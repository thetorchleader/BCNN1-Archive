People Mag Names Sandra Bullock “World’s Most Beautiful Woman”

April 23, 2015 Entertainment

© People magazine

It’s about time!

People named Sandra Bullock the “World’s Most Beautiful Woman” for 2015, and no one was more surprised than the actress herself. “No, really. I just said, ‘That’s ridiculous,'” she says. “I’ve told no one.” The news was announced on NBC’s Today.

What does beauty mean to the 50-year-old mom of 5-year-old son Louis Bullock? “Real beauty is quiet. Especially in this town, it’s just so hard not to say, ‘Oh, I need to look like that. No, be a good person, be a good mom, do a good job with the lunch, let someone cut in front of you who looks like they’re in a bigger hurry,” Bullock says. “The people I find most beautiful are the ones who aren’t trying.”

The magazine’s May 4, 2015 issue highlights 192 other beauties, including Laverne Cox, Jenna Dewan-Tatum, Ariana Grande, Gigi Hadid, Taraji P. Henson, Vanessa Hudgens, Padma Lakshmi, Shay Mitchell and Meghan Trainor, among others.

Previous cover girls include Michelle Pfeiffer (1990, 1999), Jodie Foster (1992), Cindy Crawford (1993), Meg Ryan (1994), Courteney Cox (1995), Julia Roberts (1991, 2000, 2005, 2010), Catherine Zeta-Jones (2001), Nicole Kidman (2002), Halle Berry (2003), Jennifer Aniston (2004), Angelina Jolie (2006), Drew Barrymore (2007), Kate Hudson (2008), Christina Applegate (2009), Jennifer Lopez (2011), Beyoncé (2012), Gwyneth Paltrow (2013) and Lupita Nyong’o (2014).

(E! and NBC are both members of the NBCUniversal family.)
