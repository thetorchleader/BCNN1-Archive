Patriots Coach Bill Belichick Says He Had ‘No Knowledge’ of Deflated Balls Until Monday

January 22, 2015 Sports

New England Patriots coach Bill Belichick makes a statement regarding deflated footballs in the AFC Championship game during a press conference.
(Photo: Stew Milne, USA TODAY Sports)

New England Patriots coach Bill Belichick said Thursday he had no knowledge of any intentional ball deflation by his team in the AFC Championship Game. He left it open for the NFL investigation to determine how that might have happened.

“We play with what’s out there and that’s the only way that I have ever thought about that,” Belichick said in his first expansive comments on the controversy since Sunday night’s 45-7 victory over the Indianapolis Colts.

ESPN has reported that 11 of 12 balls used by the Patriots were inflated below league-required limits.

“I have no explanation for that,” Belichick said.

He said that would be up to determination by a league inquiry.

“The NFL is investigating this situation,” Belichick said.

“We have cooperated fully and quickly and completely with every request that they have made and intend to be cooperative in any way that we can. I have no explanation for what happened and that’s what their looking into. … That’s something us should talk to them about.

Belichick said the controversy caught him by surprise.

“When I came in Monday morning I was shocked to learn of the news report about the footballs. I had no knowledge whatsoever of this situation until Monday morning,” Belichick said.

“I’ve learned a lot more about this process in the last three days than I knew or had talked about in the last 40 years coaching in the this league. I had no knowledge of the various steps involved in the game balls and the process that happened between when they were prepared and went to the officials (for a pregame check) and went to the game.”

Might three-time Super Bowl winning quarterback Tom Brady have played a role in the pregame preparation of the balls?

“We all know that quarterbacks, kickers, specialists have certain preferences on footballs,” Belichick said.

“They know a lot more about it than I do. They’re a lot more sensitive about it than I am. And I hear them comment on it from time to time. But I can tell you and they will tell you that there’s never sympathy whatever from me on that subject, zero.

“Tom’s personal preferences on his footballs are something that he can talk about in much better detail and (provide more) information than I can possibly provide.”
