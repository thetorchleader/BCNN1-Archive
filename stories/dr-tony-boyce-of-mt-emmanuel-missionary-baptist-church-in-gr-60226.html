Dr. Tony Boyce of Mt. Emmanuel Missionary Baptist Church in Greenville, SC to Celebrate 6th Pastoral Anniversary

March 6, 2015 Black

Dates & Times
Sunday, March 8, 2015 at 10:30 a.m. and 4:00 p.m.

Event Description
Everyone is encouraged to attend the 6th Pastoral Anniversary Celebration for Dr. Tony & Mrs. Schevelle Boyce on Sunday, March 8, 2015 at 10:30 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. The guest preacher for the 10:30 a.m. service is Pastor Lacey Eichelberger of the Inner Court Tabernacle in Greenville, SC. The guest preacher for the 4:00 p.m. service is Pastor Lenny Gaines of the Powerhouse Church in Pendleton, SC. For more information, please contact the church office at 864-232-5152.

Event Location
Mt. Emmanuel Missionary Baptist Church
316 Spartanburg Street
Greenville, SC 29607

Event Contact Person
Tony Boyce
