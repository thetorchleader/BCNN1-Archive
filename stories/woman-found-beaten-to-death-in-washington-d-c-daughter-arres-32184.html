Woman Found Beaten to Death in Washington D.C.; Daughter Arrested, Charged with Murder

August 1, 2014 National

Police tape is seen at the apartment complex where the victim lived.

A 54-year-old mother was found fatally beaten Thursday morning in Northwest Washington’s Columbia Heights neighborhood, D.C. police said, and authorities later arrested and charged her daughter with second-degree murder.

Police said they found two victims about 5:20 a.m. in the 1300 block of Columbia Road NW, about one block from the Columbia Heights Metro station.

Tajuana Lynn Hooks was pronounced dead at the scene. Her 30-year-old daughter, Keiva Hooks, was treated at an area hospital for injuries that were not life-threatening, a law enforcement official said.

Officials later charged Keiva Hooks, of Northwest Washington, with second-degree murder while armed. A law enforcement official said investigators believe that Keiva Hooks was under the influence of drugs when she allegedly attacked her mother, and that she injured herself after the fact.

SOURCE: Peter Hermann and Clarence Williams 
The Washington Post
