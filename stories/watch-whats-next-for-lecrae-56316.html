WATCH: What’s Next for Lecrae?

February 11, 2015 Entertainment

(PHOTO: REUTERS/MIKE BLAKE)
Lecrae poses with his award for best contemporary Christian music performance/song for “Messengers” backstage at the 57th annual Grammy Awards in Los Angeles, California, February 8, 2015.

LeCrae took home this year’s Grammy for Best Contemporary Christian Song/Performance for the track “Messengers” on his Anomaly album.

The win follows his Best Gospel Album for Gravity in 2013. LeCrae spoke with CBN News before he began his Anomaly tour.

He shared what he will be working on next and what music inspires him these days.
