USA Today to Quit Publishing USA WEEKEND

December 6, 2014 Media

USA WEEKEND, a weekly newspaper insert published by Gannett, will cease publication at the end of the year.
(Photo: USA WEEKEND)

USA WEEKEND, a weekly newspaper insert published by USA TODAY, will cease publication after its Dec. 28 issue, an acknowledgment of shrinking demand for print advertising and a move by the newspaper to eliminate duplicative products.

The publication, which runs a mix of features, style stories, celebrity interviews and recipes, is distributed to about 600 newspapers nationwide on Sunday, including 81 owned by USA TODAY’s parent, Gannett. With about 18 million in circulation, it’s the second largest Sunday insert after Parade.

Most of its content is produced by freelancers, but the shutdown eliminated about 30 jobs. USA WEEKEND editor Jim Lenahan will remain as an editor at USA TODAY.

USA TODAY’s weekend Life product, a Sunday insert section of entertainment and lifestyle news that is distributed to some local Gannett papers, will be offered to USA Weekend affiliates, said USA TODAY Publisher Larry Kramer.
