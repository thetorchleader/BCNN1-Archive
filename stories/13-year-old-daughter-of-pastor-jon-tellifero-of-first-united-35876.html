13-Year-Old Daughter of Pastor Jon Tellifero of First United Methodist Church in West Monroe, LA, Dies in Swimming Accident

August 28, 2014 Christian

Sarah Grace Tellifero

The 13-year-old daughter of First United Methodist Church West Monroe’s pastor was killed Sunday while on a church youth group trip to Lake Bruin in Tensas Parish.

Tensas Sheriff Rickey Jones said Sarah Grace Tellifero was swimming with a group of teens when she and another girl were apparently shocked while attempting to get out of the lake.

Jones, who described the accident as “tragic”, said it appears the girls were using a ladder on a pontoon boat to climb out of the water.

Initial reports indicate that a malfunction in the lift mechanism on the pontoon boat resulted in an electric charge to the boat’s ladder.

The accident reportedly occurred shortly after 2 p.m.

West Monroe resident Stanley Dupuy said the outpouring of support from the community immediately following the accident has been overwhelming.

“Ministers from all denominations were coming here to the church to pray for the students,” he said. “That’s what makes this community special.”

Tellifero was airlifted to Madison Parish Hospital. The second victim, identified late Sunday evening as Christina Elizarov, was flown to Rapides General Hospital whereshe reportedly was in the intensive care unit.

“Sarah was wonderful,” West Monroe High School student Gracie Putnam said at the church late Sunday afternoon. “I’ve never met a happier person than Sarah.” Tellifero was home-schooled.

News of the tragedy spread quickly through social media.
