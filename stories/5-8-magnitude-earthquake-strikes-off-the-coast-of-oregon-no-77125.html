5.8 Magnitude Earthquake Strikes Off the Coast of Oregon, No Damages or Deaths Reported

June 1, 2015 National

An earthquake struck off the coast of the U.S. state of Oregon on Monday, the U.S. Geological Survey reported.

It said the magnitude 5.8 quake occurred at sea 450 km (280 miles) west of Yachats at a depth of 10 km.

Source: Reuters

(Reporting by Jeremy Gaunt; Editing by Janet Lawrence)
