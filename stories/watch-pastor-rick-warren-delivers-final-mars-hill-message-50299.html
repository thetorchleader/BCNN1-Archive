WATCH: Pastor Rick Warren Delivers Final Mars Hill Message

December 28, 2014 Christian

Rick Warren (Patick Ecclesine for Parade)

Sunday, December 28th, Pastor Rick Warren preached our final Sunday as a Mars Hill Church body.

As we reflect on over eighteen years of ministry we are thankful that many of our churches will continue as new independent, autonomous churches. As we reflect on over eighteen years of ministry, and ultimately close the doors on Mars Hill Church, we are thankful that many of our churches will continue as new independent, autonomous churches.

Our hope is that this timely message will be an encouragement for the people that have called Mars Hill Church their home, as we reflect on the great impact our church has had on the kingdom of God, as well as an encouragement for the new churches emerging out of Mars Hill, and the potential they have to continue bearing much fruit.

SOURCE: Mars Hill Church
