Tavis Smiley and Cornel West Get Their Show Back on the Radio in Chicago

November 6, 2012 Uncategorized

Tavis Smiley and Cornel West can’t be held down very long, it seems.

Last week Tavis was in the news for blasting their former outlet, Public Radio station WEEZ, for dropping them because of bad ratings.
The hosts of the “Smiley & West” radio show are gaining coverage on the air in Chicago with two new affiliate stations.
On Sundays at 3pm Central, fans can tune in to WCPT – Chicago’s Progressive Talk, and on Saturdays at 11am Central on WVON 1690AM – The Talk of Chicago beginning Nov. 10.
“We are encouraged to see more stations add The Tavis Smiley Show and Smiley & West to their airwaves, making it available to more listeners nationwide.
