First Trailer Released for Joyful Noise With Parton & Latifah

September 27, 2011 Uncategorized

Queen Latifah turns into a choir director in the upcoming film, Joyful Noise, and we have your first look here!

In the movie, directed by Todd Graff, Vi Rose Hill (Latifah) and G.G. Sparrow (Dolly Parton) are at odds thanks to Hill’s promotion that Sparrow assumed was hers. Despite their feud, the two women must put their differences aside to save their singing group in the form of a national choir competition.
Watch the video below to check out the trailer! Joyful Noise serenades its way into theaters January 13, 2012.
