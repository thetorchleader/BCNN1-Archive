GM CEO Mary Barra Gives Away 5 Cars to Celebrate Company’s Milestone of Making 500 Million Vehicles

May 10, 2015 Business, zTransfer-UCN

Purple Heart recipient Trent Brining received an all-new 2016 Chevrolet Malibu from GM CEO Mary Barra and Alan Batey, president of GM North America. (Photo: GM)

General Motors has handed the keys to new vehicles to five customers around the world to recognize them as the automaker marks a milestone, having made 500 million vehicles over its history.

Each of the five patrons distinguished themselves in a particular way.

In the U.S., CEO Mary Barra gave a new Chevrolet Malibu to Trent Brining, who won a Purple Heart when he served in the Army. The presentation was made at the Kansas City, Kans., plant where the Malibu is made.

The others receiving a free car came from abroad:

• A farmer in Thailand who had been saving to buy his first vehicle for his family was given a new Chevrolet Colorado.

• A woman whose Opel Astra car was declared a total loss after a collision was given a Zafira Tourer. Both models are sold outside the U.S. under those names.

• A businessman from China known with a peach-and-purple-painted Chevrolet Cruze inspired his customization business will get a new Chevy Trax.

• Two Brazilians who drove their Chevrolet truck through 16 countries to get to Alaska will be given a new Chevy Cruze.

SOURCE: Chris Woodyard
