North Dakota: Driver and 17-Year-Old Student Killed When Train Crashes Into School Bus; 11 Injured

January 6, 2015 National

A school bus sits at the scene of a collision with a BNSF train just south of County Road 4 east of Larimore, N.D. on Monday, January 5, 2015. Grand Forks Herald photo by Sarah Volpenhein.

A 17-year-old student and a school bus driver died, and 11 others were injured after a school bus and a train collided Monday near Larimore, N.D., law enforcement said.

The collision happened around 3:40 p.m. local time about half a mile east of the town, the North Dakota Highway Patrol said.

The 62-year-old driver allegedly failed to stop on 36th Street Northeast where it intersects with the railroad, NDHP spokesman Lt. Troy Hischer said. There are a stop sign and railroad signs at the intersection, but no crossing arms.

The bus was struck on its right front side by a westbound BNSF Railway train.

Between 11 and 12 other people were injured, though their ages weren’t reported. The school bus serviced students in grades Kindergarten to 12th.

The two people who were killed were ejected from the school bus.
