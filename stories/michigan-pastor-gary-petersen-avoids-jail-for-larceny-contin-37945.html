Michigan Pastor Gary Petersen Avoids Jail for Larceny, Continues Preaching at New Church

October 7, 2014 Christian

Pastor Gary Peterson
Courtesy Photo

The pastor accused of using his church credit card at Victoria’s Secret and Red Lobster will not go to jail, but will have to pay back $4,000 to his former congregation — presumably from money he earns at his new church a few miles away. 

Pastor Gary Petersen was in Ottawa County Circuit Court on Monday, Oct. 6, where he was sentenced after pleading no contest to a reduced charge of attempted larceny.

The 62-year-old former pastor of Hudsonville’s Wellspring Church used the church-issued credit card he was given in 2006 to make purchases at retail stores, restaurants, pay for a cruise to Rome and a Colorado vacation, according to church officials who testified in a hearing in May.

Related: Former church pastor accused of using congregation funds for Rome cruise, Colorado vacation

Prosecutors allege Petersen opened the American Express card in 2006 and initially was providing monthly paper statements to the church bookkeeper until the account switched to electronic billing.

“Gary Petersen was using this card as his own personal ATM,” Ottawa County Assistant Prosecutor Sarah Matwiejczyk said in May.
