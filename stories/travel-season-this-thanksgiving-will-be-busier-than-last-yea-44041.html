Travel Season This Thanksgiving Will Be Busier Than Last Year

November 7, 2014 Lifestyle

IMAGE: FLICKR, ANDREW W. SIEBER

Flying this Thanksgiving? You won’t be alone.

Airports are expected to be even more crowded this year than last, and airlines are anticipating that they will carry about 24.6 million passengers between Nov. 21 and Dec. 2, according to trade group Airlines for America.

SEE ALSO: Know Before You Go: All the Airline Fees by Carrier

The only potential good news is that last year, Airlines for America — which represents the biggest carriers in the U.S. — forecasted a higher number of people traveling than actually did (it blamed the outcome on poor weather). What’s more, Thanksgiving travel has still not completely rebounded from pre-recession numbers before 2008.

Unsurprisingly, the worst travel days are Wednesday, Nov. 26, Sunday, Nov. 30, and Monday, Dec. 1. Meanwhile, the least busy travel days — again, unsurprisingly — are expected to be Thanksgiving and Black Friday.

The five busiest airports in the U.S. are Atlanta, Los Angeles, Chicago O’Hare, Dallas-Fort Worth, and New York’s John F. Kennedy.
