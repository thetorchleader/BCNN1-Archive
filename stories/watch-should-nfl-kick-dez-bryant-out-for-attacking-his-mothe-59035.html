WATCH: Should NFL Kick Dez Bryant Out for Attacking his Mother and for Other Violent Outbursts?

February 27, 2015 Sports

Back in 2012, Dez Bryant was arrested on a misdemeanor family violence charge after he allegedly assaulted his mother. The charge was later dropped, but not before Bryant’s mother explained to police in detail the way she claims her son attacked her.

On Tuesday, TMZ obtained an interview video from when Angela Bryant spoke to police following the 2012 incident. She claimed Dez got into a heated argument with her brother before getting physical with her.

[Watch the interview here]

“I said, ‘Dez, this is wrong. This is what everybody’s been talking about. This outside stuff is gonna get you in trouble.’” Angela told police. “He goes, ‘I don’t care!’ and grabs my shirt. I pulled away from him and he grabbed it again and tears my shirt.

“Then, he started threatening me, talking about how he’s gonna knock me out. … I kept telling him to leave.”
