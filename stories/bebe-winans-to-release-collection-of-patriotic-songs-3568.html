BeBe Winans to Release Collection of Patriotic Songs

February 4, 2012 Uncategorized

Award winning singer and songwriter BeBe Winans will be releasing a new collection of patriotic songs that he hopes will inspire unity and restore pride to the nation. America America, Winans seventh solo release, will contain three new original songs and the remainder will be works that are in the public comain.
The three original songs are titled “America America,” “We’re the United States of America” and the “Ultimate Sacrifice.” The latter is a moving tribute to our troops, acknowledging and thanking them for the personal sacrifices they make each day to protect our freedom. A video for the single, shot in New York City and Washington D.C., will be released soon.
Other songs on the CD include all-American classics “America the Beautiful,” “Lift Every Voice,” “The Battle Hymn of the Republic,” and our national anthem, “The Star Spangled Banner.”
“I have seen the power of music,” says Winans. “If we all come together, we all win. When our country is united, we find the path to success.”
