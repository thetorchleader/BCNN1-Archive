Natalie Grant and Bernie Herms Celebrate 13th Wedding Anniversary

August 28, 2012 Uncategorized

Contemporary singer/songwriter Natalie Grant and her husband, producer Bernie Herms, celebrate their 13th wedding anniversary.
Natalie Grant exclaimed today via her social network, “13 yrs ago, I said “I do” to him. He makes me better, stronger, AND still gives me butterflies.”
During their married life, the couple from Nashville, TN, gave birth to the three daughters, twins Grace Ana and Isabella Noelle, and Sadie Rose. The couple continues be an example to follow by many married couples in the music industry.
