For Every Age and All People, Books Are the Perfect Last-Minute or Planned Christmas Gift

December 23, 2014 Books

Certainly there are people still shopping. Plenty of folks relish the challenge of last-minute shopping, and to them I say: Buy a book.

To the grandchildren not sure of what to get their elders: Buy a book. It’s way better than the stationery or nightgown you were considering. To the office workers who need to buy something but don’t want it to be too personal: Buy a book. A gag calendar is annoying and do not even consider a nightgown.

To anyone buying for a teenager: The world does not need another electronic item that beeps: Buy a book. If you are buying for a child: Buy a book. A plastic toy that will be broken by Monday or anything that requires batteries is nowhere as engaging and totally unnecessary.

What the world does need are more literate people.
