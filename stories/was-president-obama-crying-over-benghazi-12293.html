Was President Obama Crying Over Benghazi?

May 13, 2013 Uncategorized

An aggravated President Obama on Monday dismissed criticism of his handling of the attacks in Benghazi, Libya, as a political “sideshow.” He said a Republican charge of a coverup “defies logic.”
Pictured: Obama appears to tear Monday while discussing the attack on the U.S. consulate in Benghazi, Libya, on Sept. 11. Republicans say the administration tried to play down an act of terror that might reflect poorly on the President before the election.
Although Obama sounded more irritated than emotional, a Reuters photo showed what the news agency described as a “tear” running down his face.
The image wasn’t seen in Associated Press photos.
Obama has teared up in public at least twice since November — while thanking his campaign staff and after the Newtown massacre.
The U.S. ambassador and three other Americans died in Benghazi last Sept. 11.
Republicans claim the administration misled America immediately after the attack, trying to play down an act of terrorism that would reflect poorly on Obama before the election.
Obama said “Nobody understood exactly what was taking place during the course of those first few days” but that it soon became clear the attack was terrorism.
SOURCE: JOSEPH STRAW
NEW YORK DAILY NEWS
