Tasha Cobbs Set to Record Live Album at The Redemption Center In South Carolina

October 13, 2014 Entertainment

Artist Tasha Cobbs speaks at the45th annual GMA Dove Awards presentation announcements at the Hall of Fame in the Allen Arena at Lipscomb University on Wednesday Aug. 13, 2014. (Photo: John Partipilo / The Tennessean)

Tasha Cobbs is arguably one of the most successful Gospel artists in recent years. With a debut album that has been Top-10 since its 2013 release; a hit single that was #1 at Gospel radio for 8 weeks; a Grammy, three Stellar Awards and three Dove Awards under her belt – Tasha Cobbs’ achievements are extraordinary, and she’s only recorded one nationally released project!

A powerful and highly in demand worshipper, Tasha Cobbs’ music ministry is a gift to all who experience it – and on October 13 she will unleash a new body of work at the live recording for her as-yet-untitled 2015 sophomore album release. The recording will take place at The Redemption Center in Greenville, South Carolina, and is destined to be a life changing event.

“We all have heard the saying that you ‘appreciate something more when you invest in it’. If you’ve sacrificed and invested in order to get there, you’re not coming as a spectator, you’re coming to actively participate in the worship experience,” says Cobbs.

“I’ve noticed that God always sends me places that require a sacrifice in order for one to attend,” she says about recording in South Carolina. “I had the opportunity to minister at Redemption Center a few months ago and I knew immediately that I was going to record there. Apostle Carpenter and the Redemption Church have been very gracious to open their church doors to us.”
