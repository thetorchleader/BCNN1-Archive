Youth Evangelist and “The Basement” Founder, Matt Pitt, Released from Jail

April 14, 2014 Christian

Matt Pitt (photo courtesy of the Shelby County Sheriff’s Office)

Youth Evangelist Matt Pitt was released this morning from Shelby County Jail after being behind bars since he was arrested Aug. 20 on a second charge of impersonating a law enforcement officer.

Pitt had his probation revoked in September and was ordered to serve his one-year suspended sentence from his first conviction in 2012, but he is being released early based on good behavior and credit for good time served as calculated by the Shelby County Jail staff.

Pitt was released just after 6 a.m. and met by his family and followers of The Basement youth ministry he founded.

“I hope he’s learned his lesson and does better,” said Shelby County Sheriff’s Department Capt. Ken Burchfield.

Before his arrests, Pitt had been drawing crowds of 4,000 to 7,000 preaching at Linn Park, Boutwell Auditorium, Bartow Arena and other venues. His followers have been anxiously awaiting his release, campaigning with “Free Pitt” T-shirts, concerts and messages on Twitter.
