President Obama to Visit Detroit on November 1 Before Election Day

October 24, 2014 National

President Obama (Photo: Getty Images/Pool/Kristoffer Tripplaar )

President Barack Obama will visit metro Detroit on the Saturday before Election Day to rally Democrats for the party’s nominees for governor and U.S. Senate.

The White House and the state Democratic Party announced Thursday that Obama would appear at a campaign event Nov. 1 for Democratic U.S. Senate nominee Gary Peters and gubernatorial nominee Mark Schauer.

The president’s visit had been announced earlier, but not the date. The state Democratic Party said it did not have details about when and where on Nov. 1 the event would be held.

“We are honored to have President Obama come to Michigan and highlight just how much is at stake this November,” state Michigan Democratic Party Chairman Lon Johnson.

Obama has been stepping up his campaign appearances lately, despite poor job approval ratings. The Real Clear Politics aggregate average of recent polls shows Obama’s job approval rating at 42% compared to a disapproval rating of 54%.
