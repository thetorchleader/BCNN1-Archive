Root Magazine Celebrating 5 Years With Anniversary Concert

August 11, 2014 Entertainment

Root Magazine is celebrating its 5th year in grand fashion with the concert of the year! Our pre-show consists of the best Indie artists around and will be guaranteed to make dreamers into believers. Our main event will feature the best that Gospel music has to offer. Myron Butler, Anita Wilson, Kierra Sheard and many others will be helping us celebrate with some more of our musical friends. Please come and get ROOTED with us at the celebration of the year! #ROOT5THANNIVERSARY
