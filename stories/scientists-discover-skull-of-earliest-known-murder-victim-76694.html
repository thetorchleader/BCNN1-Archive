Scientists Discover Skull of ‘Earliest Known’ Murder Victim

May 30, 2015 Science

ITALY – CIRCA 2002: Cain and Abel, Titian (ca 1490-1576). Church of the Salute, Venice.

According to the Bible, Abel was the world’s first murder victim, killed at the hands of his brother Cain.

But now archaeologists have discovered a skull dating from 430000 years ago that shows distinct evidence of foul play.

The scientists were able to determine that the victim was probably killed by two blows to the head before being tossed down a shaft in a cave.

If so, the find represents the earliest known murder in human history.

The truth of what occurred at the Sima de los Huesos (Pit of Bones) site in the Atapuerca Mountains, northern Spain, was revealed after researchers pieced together 52 bone fragments to reconstruct a near-complete skull.
