Vanilla Ice, Who Should be Now Known as Melted Ice, Arrested for Home Burglary

February 18, 2015 Entertainment

Vanilla Ice at the Thanksgiving’s Day parade on Nov. 27.
(Photo: Paul Warner, Getty Images)

Talk about a reality show twist.

TMZ and Variety report that Vanilla Ice (real name:Robert Van Winkle) has been arrested and charged with home burglary for allegedly stealing furniture, a pool heater and other items from a home in Lantana, Florida.

In a Hollywood twist, the police department told media the theft is connected to the rapper’s ongoing DIY Network TV show, The Vanilla Ice Project, in which he and a crew renovate and flip homes.
