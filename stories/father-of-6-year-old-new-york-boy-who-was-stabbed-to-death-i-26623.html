Father of 6-Year-Old New York Boy Who Was Stabbed to Death in Elevator Feels Heartache on Father’s Day

June 15, 2014 Black

Nicholas Avitto, father of stabbing victim P.J., says his heart is ‘really, really broken.’

Nicholas Avitto was still reeling Saturday from the savage June 1 stabbing death of his 6-year-old son P.J. Mikayla Capers, the 7-year-old who was also attacked, is still recovering. 

Father’s Day, once a source of unmatched joy to Nicholas Avitto, now brings only the deepest of heartache.

The distraught dad was still reeling Saturday from the savage June 1 stabbing death of his 6-year-old son P.J.

“My heart is really, really broken and I’m devastated beyond words,” said Avitto, joined by the boy’s mom, Aricka McClinton, on the eve of his first Father’s Day without P.J.’s smiling face.

The news was better about little Mikayla Capers, the 7-year-old who barely escaped with her life after watching a knife-wielding attacker murder her playmate inside a Brooklyn elevator.

“She’s very fragile right now,” said the little girl’s great-grandmother, Regenia Trevathan. “Very fragile — she’s gone through so much. Sometimes I just have to wrap my arms around her.”

Asked how the girl was doing physically, the 62-year family matriarch replied with a question of her own.

“Well, have you ever been stabbed 16 times?” asked Trevathan. “Answer that, and you’ll have your answer.”

Trevathan said Mikayla’s recovery was a day-to-day process. The little girl sleeps with all the lights on in their home, and wakes up at 4 a.m.
