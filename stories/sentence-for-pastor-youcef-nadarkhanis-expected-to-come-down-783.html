Sentence for Pastor Youcef Nadarkhani Expected to Come Down Today

October 11, 2011 Christian

The plight of Iranian Pastor Youcef Nadarkhani could come any minute. Nadarkhani was charged and convicted on apostasy charges and could face the death penalty. Some have called Nadarkhani a member of a cult.
“Clearly, in the eyes of the Iranian government, this is a case where they’re attacking someone, persecuting someone for being a Christian, and the repercussions of that could sort of filter out into other Christian groups as well,” says Todd Nettleton of Voice of the Martyrs says. “Secular media organizations have covered it. U.S. government officials–including the White House–have spoken out about it. Other officials in other countries have spoken out on his behalf. And the Iranian government has responding by hedging on what the charges actually are against him.”
First, Nadarkhani was charged with apostasy. However, now the press is talking about him being a rapist and a Zionist, which Nettleton says appears to be in response to the international pressure. Nettleton says this could come to an end soon.
“We really expect some kind of a verdict from the local court within the next couple of days,” he says. “The reality is: It could happen at any moment.”
