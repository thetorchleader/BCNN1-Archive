Two Days After his Release for Murdering a Neighbor 30 Years Ago, Convicted Killer Charged with Murdering his Mother

October 14, 2014 Black

Steven Pratt
TWITTER

Steven Pratt, 45, had just completed serving a 30 year sentence for the murder of his neighbor in 1984, two days later he was charged with killing his own mother, Gwendolyn Pratt. A New Jersey man is back behind bars after allegedly killing his own mother, two days after completing a 30-year prison sentence for another murder, NJ.com reports.

According to the report, Steven Pratt, 45, was taken into custody on Sunday, not even 48 hours after he was released from Bayside State Prison for the 1984 murder of his neighbor, Michael Anderson, in which he was tried as an adult.

Records of that incident detail an argument between Pratt and Anderson, after Pratt and some friends refused to evacuate an apartment hallway where they were making noise. A scuffle with a lead pipe ensued. Anderson took the pipe away from Pratt and hit Pratt in the face. Pratt then left and borrowed a firearm, shooting Anderson.
