Sarah Goldberg, ‘7th Heaven’ Actress, Dies at 40

October 8, 2014 Uncategorized

Sarah Goldberg, who starred in TV’s 7th Heaven from 2002 to 2006, died on Sept. 27 of natural causes, according to the Chicago Sun-Times. She was 40.

“She went to sleep and didn’t wake up,” her mother told the Sun-Times.

Goldberg played Sarah Glass Camden on 7th Heaven. Her character was the daughter of a rabbi (Richard Lewis) who falls in love and eventually marries Matt Camden (Barry Watson), the son of Rev. Eric Camden (Stephen Collins). Their inter-religious relationship was a popular story line for fans.

Collins made headlines on Tuesday when it was revealed that he is under investigation by police in New York over allegations that he molested a 14-year-old girl in 1972. TMZ also revealed on Tuesday that it had obtained a tape recording that features Collins, 67, discussing three instances of child molestation.
