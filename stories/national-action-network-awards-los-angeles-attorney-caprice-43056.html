National Action Network Awards Los Angeles Attorney Caprice Collins with the Vanguard Award

November 2, 2014 Black

Attorney Caprice Collins of the Collins Law Group

The Los Angeles Chapter of the National Action Network (NAN) presented its Vanguard Award to Attorney Caprice Collins of the Collins Law Group on Thursday, October 16 at the California African American Museum.  With civil rights activist Reverend Al Sharpton as the Keynote speaker, the awards ceremony featured some of the brightest and most talented philanthropists and activists from multiple industries.  Selected by an acclaimed list of African-American academics, artists, civic organizations and leaders, Attorney Collins has a longstanding history of educating the community on how to protect their families, themselves, and their hard earned legacies from loss and/or court processes.

“Honored, humbled, and grateful only scratch the surface in describing my reaction to receiving such a notable honor,” said Attorney Collins.  “Throughout my career, I have aggressively worked to educate my community as to the power to protect themselves, family, and legacy by thoughtful Estate Planning.”

In addition to helping thousands of families avoid Probate, Attorney Collins has also raised millions for faith-based and non-profit organizations as part of her belief that the community’s progress is a collective responsibility.  A graduate of Harvard Law School, Attorney Collins serves as a trusted expert on television and radio shows while maintaining her commitment to giving a voice to those who often feel silenced.
