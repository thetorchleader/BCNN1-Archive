1 Dead, 6 Injured in Chicago Shootings

April 12, 2015 Black

© Marcus DiPaola/NurPhoto/REX The Chicago Police Department Homan Square building, Chicago, America.

One man was killed and six men were injured in separate shootings on the South, Southwest and Northwest sides since Friday afternoon, police said.

A 39-year-old man was fatally shot in a “domestic incident” inside a residence in the 11300 block of South Green Street in Morgan Park about 10:35 p.m., said Officer Hector Alfaro, a police spokesman.

The victim was arguing with another male who shot him in the chest, Alfaro said. The victim was taken in critical condition to Advocate Christ Medical Center in Oak Lawn, where he was later pronounced dead. He was identified as David E. Finney of the same address where he was killed, according to the Cook County medical examiner’s office.

Early Saturday, a person was in custody for the shooting, but police said the investigation is ongoing.

Two men were wounded in a drive-by shooting in the Humboldt Park neighborhood.

The men, ages 31 and 33, were leaving a home in the 800 block of North Mozart Street when someone inside a passing dark-colored Jeep yelled gang slogans and opened fire, police said.

The 31-year-old man was shot in his stomach, and the 33-year-old man was hit in his upper right arm. Both men were taken to Norwegian American Hospital in good condition. The two were being transferred to John H. Stroger Hospital for further treatment Saturday morning, police said.

About 2:30 a.m., a 27-year-old man walked into Northwestern Memorial Hospital after he was shot in the South Loop early Saturday.

The man was sitting in a parked SUV in the 2100 block of South Wabash Avenue when he heard gunfire and felt pain, police said.

Friends drove the man to Northwestern Memorial Hospital where he was treated for a gunshot wound to the left side of his chest. Police said the man’s condition had stabilized early Saturday.

About an hour earlier, a man was shot in Ashburn neighborhood on the Southwest Side, police said.
