Two Nicki Minaj Crew Members Stabbed to Death; One Dead, One in Critical Condition

February 18, 2015 Entertainment

Nicki Minaj and Meek Mill at 2015 NBA All-Star Game at Madison Square Garden on Feb.15, in New York.
(Photo: Elsa, Getty Images)

Nicki Minaj’s crew has been struck by tragedy: Two members of her stage team were stabbed in Philadelphia early Wednesday.

One man died and another is in the hospital in critical condition.

Minaj announced the news on Twitter and on her Instagram page Wednesday.

@NICKIMINAJ
Two members of my team were stabbed last night in Philly. One was killed. They had only been there for two days rehearsing for the tour.
5,991 RETWEETS 5,826 FAVORITES

“these guys flew into Philly for my tour rehearsals 2 days ago and were both stabbed last night. #RIPDAYDAY condolences to your family. Unbelievable. Eric (on the right) is recovering. Another senseless act of violence that took the life of a great guy. So sad,” she posted next to a picture of the two men.
