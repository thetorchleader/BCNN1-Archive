RG3 Leads Redskins to Playoff Land

December 30, 2012 Uncategorized

The NFC East belongs to Robert Griffin III and the Washington Redskins. It may just stay that way for a while.

On a night where the quarterbacks didn’t exactly light up the box score, Alfred Morris rushed for 200 yards and three touchdowns in a 28-18 victory by the Redskins over the Dallas Cowboys. The win gives Washington the NFC East title and sends Tony Romo and the Cowboys home early once again with a bitter season-ending defeat.
The Cowboys, who continued to lose starter after starter to injury during the game, got the ball back in Romo’s hands with under four minutes to go only down three points. Romo thought Redskins linebacker Rob Jackson was coming on a blitz and floated an interception right into Jackson’s waiting hands. It was Romo’s third interception on a nightmarish night that will kick off a very long offseason in Dallas.
Washington, meanwhile, can get ready for the playoffs. The Redskins offense didn’t look quite as dynamic as usual because Robert Griffin III was clearly not 100%. He threw for 100 yards on 18 attempts, and amazingly rushed for 63 yards and a score despite missing his usual burst.
The Redskins were once 3-6. It turns out they needed to win seven straight games to make the playoffs and that’s exactly what they did. They are the first 3-6 teams since the 1996 Jacksonville Jaguars to make the playoffs. The Redskins defense has holes, but defensive coordinator Jim Haslett schemed and blitzed quarterbacks like Romo into submission. The offense varied little from week-to-week, but defenses could never catch up with Griffin’s playfakes, accuracy, and read option throws.
The Giants and Cowboys both had chances to put the Redskins away. They failed. The future arrived in the NFC East faster than anyone imagined.
And now the Redskins will face the Seattle Seahawks and their phenom rookie quarterback Russell Wilson next Sunday. The NFL’s future is in great hands.

SOURCE: NFL – Gregg Rosenthal
