WATCH: Mike Huckabee says Radical Islamists Want to “Kill Every Last One of Us in the Name of God”

May 1, 2015 National

Former Arkansas governor Mike Huckabee speaks at the National Rifle Association convention in Nashville. (Justin Sullivan, Getty Images)

Mike Huckabee, who is expected to announce his presidential plans next Tuesday, sat down with The Brody File in Iowa this past weekend and made clear that as president he’ll be ready to handle radical Islamic terrorists. “When we look at something that is inherently evil like radical jihadism, let’s quit kidding ourselves and pretending we’re going to have some negotiated settlement because we’re not. They believe that they’re here on earth to kill every last one of us in the name of God.”

The Brody File sees Huckabee as a candidate that very well could be one of the last ones standing when all is said and done in the GOP primaries. His likeability numbers are through the roof which gives him an opening. His communication skills are second to none. Now, he just needs lots of money. Ask Jeb Bush. He’ll have some.

http://www.cbn.com/media/player/embedplayer.aspx?bcid=4197449338001&width=320&height=180

David Brody, “Foreign policy. You talk about cutting the head off of that rattlesnake. The last time around when you ran in 2008 there were some folks that had some concerns about how tough you would be on national security. What about this time around?”

Mike Huckabee, “If they had those concerns, it’s because they didn’t know me very well. If you’re a principle person and you understand the nature of evil and you realize there’s a difference between good and evil. Some things aren’t negotiable. You also understand that if there is something that is truly evil that you can’t negotiate with it. That’s the whole point of the analogy I used with the snake. Is that the snake knows its nature. It knows what it is going to do and if you have any brains at all you know what the snake’s nature is and you know what it’s going to try to do. You can either pretend that you can make nice with the snake and pet the snake and feed the snake, reason with the snake or you can do what a smart person would do and that’s see that snake is going to bite me unless I take it our first. And when we look at something that is inherently evil like radical jihadism, let’s quit kidding ourselves and pretending we’re going to have some negotiated settlement because we’re not. They believe that they’re here on earth to kill every last one of us in the name of God. And when you’re dealing with someone who is doing what they’re doing out of religious fanaticism, believing that it is pleasing to God to murder people, then you better understand that the only way you’re going to keep from being murdered is to be able to stop them in their tracks before they kill your children.”

SOURCE: The Brody File, CBN
