Ohio Groom Has Been Killed, Bride Injured in Wedding Day Crash

October 14, 2014 National

A single-vehicle crash in Upper Sandusky, Ohio, on Oct. 12, 2014, claimed the life of just-married groom, Andrew M. Bloomfield, and a friend, Elizabeth M. Shelton. Bloomfield’s wife, and another friend were seriously injured.
(Photo: Wyandot County Sheriff’s Office)

What likely began as the happiest time in a couple’s life ended in tragedy Sunday when the groom was killed in a single-vehicle crash on their wedding day.

Andrew M. Bloomfield, 27, died just hours after saying his vows with Ruth D. Driskill Bloomfield, who was seriously injured in the crash that happened at 4:49 p.m. Sunday in Antrim Township.

Elizabeth M. Shelton, 26, was pronounced dead at the scene by Wyandot County Coroner Joseph Sberna.

Driskill Bloomfield, 37, was flown by medical helicopter to St. Rita’s Hospital in Lima, Ohio, where her new husband was pronounced dead. The driver of the 2014 Dodge Ram quad cab pickup, Timothy N. Tebbe, was flown to St. Vincent’s Medical Center in Toledo. Both were listed in critical condition Monday.

According to Wyandot County Sheriff Michael Hetzel, the preliminary investigation shows that Tebbe lost control of the pickup. The truck traveled off the west side of the road and hit an embankment, which caused the truck to begin to roll. The truck then hit a utility pole and rolled several more times, coming to rest in a cornfield.
