Former Wisconsin Pastor Charged With Having Sex With Teenage Girls

December 24, 2014 Christian

Jerry E. Fletcher Jr.
(Photo: File)

A former Black Creek minister faces additional sexual assault-related charges in two more Brown County incidents involving teenage girls.

During a court hearing on Monday, Jerry E. Fletcher Jr., 34 of Neenah, pleaded not guilty to charges of repeated sexual assault of a child, second-degree sexual assault of a child and three counts of child enticement.

In these charges, which were filed in November, prosecutors say Fletcher met a 15-year-old girl online in the summer of 2005 and had sex with her several times in Green Bay, according to a criminal complaint filed in Brown County Circuit Court.

In another instance, a woman, now 20, told investigators she met Fletcher at St. Mark Lutheran Church in De Pere when she was 14 or 15. The two messaged each other through Facebook, meeting face to face when the girl was 16, according to court documents

Fletcher had sex with the girl in Green Bay in 2011 and 2012, when the girl was 16 and 17, and again when she was 18, according to the complaint. He pleaded not guilty to those charges on Monday.

Those charges came after a separate case filed against Fletcher in September. At that time, he was charged with repeated sexual assault of a child; child enticement; and sexual exploitation of a child, filming. The then 14-year-old victim in that case told investigators she knew Fletcher through church and had been a babysitter for him, according to a criminal complaint.

The girl told investigators she and Fletcher had sexual contact on a number of occasions and between 2012 and 2014.
