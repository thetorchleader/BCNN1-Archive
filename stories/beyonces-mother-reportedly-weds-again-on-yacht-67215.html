Beyonce’s Mother Reportedly Weds Again on Yacht

April 13, 2015 Entertainment

Tina Knowles reportedly married Richard Lawson in a white wedding on a yacht on Sunday Credit: Cliff Watts/Beyonce.com

UPDATE: Beyonce herself shared a photo of the bride and groom on her website. In the pic, taken by Cliff Watts, Tina dons an elegant white Romona Keveza gown with a gold belt and stands next to her husband, who looks dapper in an all-white tux.

Beyonce’s mom Tina Knowles has married Richard Lawson on board a yacht in California, in an all-white wedding ceremony attended by Beyonce, Solange, Jay Z, and Blue Ivy, TMZ reports.

The outlet shared a host of pictures from Tina’s uber-romantic big evening, on Sunday, April 12, showing around 300 guests gathering on a superyacht called “Eternity” for the celebrations. Jay Z is seen exiting a black SUV looking dapper in a white suit, while Queen Bey is snapped wearing a pretty white lace dress with matching headband, and little Blue Ivy is seen in the distance, also dressed in white.

It’s the second marriage for Tina, 61, who divorced husband Mathew Knowles in December 2011. Her new husband, Lawson, 67, is an actor, best known for his work on the soap All My Children. He also starred in the 1982 horror movie Poltergeist.
