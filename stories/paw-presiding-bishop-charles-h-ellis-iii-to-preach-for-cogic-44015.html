PAW Presiding Bishop Charles H. Ellis III to Preach for COGIC’s 107th Annual Convocation Tonight

November 7, 2014 Christian

Current PAW Presiding Bishop Charles H. Ellis III will preach at the 107th COGIC Convention this Friday in St. Louis.

Tomorrow evening (November 7, 2014), leaders of two of the world’s largest Pentecostal churches renew a historic fellowship that has often gone unattended, in recent years.

PAW Presiding Bishop Charles H. Ellis III will preach for the 107th Annual Convocation of the Church of God in Christ (COGIC) at the America’s Center in St. Louis, on Friday night. A contingent of PAW Bishops and the Midwestern District Council will join the gathering, in support of Bishop Ellis.

The Presider’s visit to COGIC should not be shocking. It is “not” a first-ever encounter between these two organizations. In fact, there is a longstanding historical fellowship/relationship that exists between the leadership of these two behemoth Pentecostal organizations . . . dating back to the Azusa Street Revival in 1906 Los Angeles.

Accompanying the Presider to the COGIC Convocation on Friday are 1st Assistant Presiding Bishop Richard Young, 2nd Assistant Presiding Bishop Theodore Brooks, Midwest District Bishop Larry Jones, Canadian District Bishop Mark Tolbert, Tri-States Bishop Lance Foster, Southern California Bishop Robert Douglas, Hawaii Bishop A. Glenn Brady, Global Missions Bishop James Gaiters, South African Bishop J.D. Guthrie, and PAW General Secretary Donald Golder.

Bishop Larry Jones put the Midwestern District Council on hold for the evening. The district will attend the event in support of the Presiding Bishop, at the America’s Center. The MDC is meeting in St. Louis this week.

Although COGIC was founded in 1897 in Mississippi, it was originally a “holiness” organization that did not teach the Pentecostal experience of speaking in tongues. When Elder Charles Harrison Mason visited Azusa Street, he received the gift of the Holy Ghost (speaking in tongues).

When Mason returned home and began teaching the gift of tongues, his COGIC co-founder C.P. Jones rejected it and parted ways with Mason. From there, Mason chartered COGIC in Memphis as a “Pentecostal” church and was elected its Bishop. Shortly thereafter, C.P. Jones founded the Church of Christ (Holiness) in Mississippi.

Meanwhile, the PAW was established in 1906 in Los Angeles by participants in the Azusa Street Revival. It began organizing missionaries and ministers to evangelize the world with the new Pentecostal message.

Given that most of the ministers saved at Azusa Street were from the Baptist or Methodist churches, they were disfellowshipped from their organizations, when they returned teaching tongues. The result was that the PAW and the COGIC were the only two “Pentecostal” churches that were offering official licenses to Pentecostal ministers, immediately following Azusa Street.

By historical comparison, Gaston B. Cashwell (an Azusa Street alum) did not convert the A.J. Tomlinson-led Church of God (Cleveland, TN) to Pentecostalism until after a 1908 revival in Tennessee, when Tomlinson fell to the floor and spoke in tongues. The Church of God had been founded in 1886 as “The Christian Union,” but was a non-Pentecostal “holiness” church.
