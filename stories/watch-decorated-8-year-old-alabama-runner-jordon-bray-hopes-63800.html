WATCH: Decorated 8-Year-Old Alabama Runner, Jordon Bray, Hopes to One Day Go to the Olympics

March 26, 2015 Black

Jordon Bray was born to run.

“Once I’m on the track I’m not nice anymore and I’m all business,” Bray said.

And once more, she’s fast.

“It feels good, great, awesome,” She explained.

Bray is 8 years old and is rated No. 1 in the state of Alabama for her age group in the 100, 200 and 400 meter distances.

“In the 400, that’s all the way around the track, and you’ll get like very tired and you feel like you want to pass out, but somehow I don’t,” Bray explained

Bray is coached by her father Jason and as it stands now, we may be looking at a future Olympic hopeful.

“I dream about myself at the Olympics running the 100 and I win the race and I get like this big medal and it’s heavy and it’s real gold,” Bray said.

She has the medals to prove it. To fully appreciate her talent, you’ve got to see her in action, or just tag along for a run.
