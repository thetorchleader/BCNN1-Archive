LISTEN: Roland Martin and Michelle Nunn Talk About Georgia’s Senatorial Race

November 4, 2014 Black

Roland Martin talks to Michelle Nunn about the U.S. Senators race in Georgia, in light of election day, November 4th.

“We have a lot at stake in this race. We need to lift up the minimum wage so that families can be self sufficent We also need to pass the paycheck fairness act. We’re excited abut the momentum we see in Georgia,” Nunn said.
