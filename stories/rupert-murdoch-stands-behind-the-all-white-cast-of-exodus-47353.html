Rupert Murdoch Stands Behind the All White Cast of ‘Exodus’

November 29, 2014 Media

© Provided by TheWrap
Rupert Murdoch Defends ‘Exodus’ All-White Cast: ‘Since When Are Egyptians Not White?’

TV, newspaper and media mogul. And now, Rupert Murdoch-casting director?

21st Century Fox CEO Rupert Murdoch waded up to his neck into the casting decisions of his studio’s upcoming biblical thriller “Exodus,” which has been slammed by critics for having an all-white cast.

Murdoch took to Twitter to ask what all the fuss was about.

Moses film attacked on Twitter for all white cast. Since when are Egyptians not white? All I know are.

– Rupert Murdoch (@rupertmurdoch) November 29, 2014

Everybody-attacks last tweet. Of course Egyptians are Middle Eastern, but far from black. They treated blacks as slaves.

– Rupert Murdoch (@rupertmurdoch) November 29, 2014
