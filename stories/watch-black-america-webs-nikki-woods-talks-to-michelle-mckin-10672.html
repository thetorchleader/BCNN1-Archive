WATCH: Black America Web's Nikki Woods Talks to Michelle McKinney Hammond About Singlehood and Marriage

February 7, 2013 Uncategorized

As a bestselling author, speaker, singer and television co-host, Michelle has authored over 35 books (selling over two million copies worldwide), including best-selling titles “The Diva Principle”, “Sassy, Single and Satisfied”, “101 Ways to Get and Keep His Attention”, and “Secrets of an Irresistible Woman”. She has been more than a champion for the single and satisfied, saying that “you have to proactively decide that you want to be happy single or not,” says Michelle. “Every single should decide that they want that because they’re not going to be a happy married person unless they are a happy single person.”

You have to be a whole person on your own first. It helps you make better choices. It helps you clarify what you want and what you don’t want, and it helps you not have unrealistic expectations once you do find your mate. Get a life .. literally. Too many single people put their lives on hold waiting for their spouse and idolizing the institution of marriage. The real truth is that there are just as many miserable people married as there are single. So marriage is not the end all. And then the conversation really took off!
