Pray for Our Super-Bowl Winning Christian Brother Russell Wilson: Seahawks Quarterback Makes ‘Difficult Decision’ to File for Divorce

April 24, 2014 Sports

Russell Wilson is going through a tough time.
(Mark J. Rebilas-USA TODAY Sports)

Russell Wilson and his wife, Ashton, have filed for divorce.

The Seahawks quarterback released a statement through the team on Wednesday.

“I have made the difficult decision to file for divorce,” Wilson said. “Clearly, decisions like these don’t come easy. Ashton and I respectfully ask for prayers, understanding and privacy during this difficult time. Moving forward, I will have no further comments on this personal matter.”

Wilson is active on Twitter and Wednesday retweeted this:

Bible Scripture @Scripture_Truth
God never promises the absence of storms. He just promises to walk with us through them.

Russell and Ashton were married in January 2012.
