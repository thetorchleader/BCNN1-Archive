Jeremy Meeks, Felon Whose Mugshot Went Viral, Sentenced to 2 Years in Federal Prison

February 6, 2015 Black

Jeremy Meeks’ police booking photo after his arrest on felony weapon charges June 18, 2014 in Stockton, California.
STOCKTON POLICE DEPARTMENT/GETTY IMAGES

California felon Jeremy Meeks, whose mughshot photo broke the Internet long before Kim Kardashian’s nudes, was sentenced to 27 months in federal prison.

According to the New York Daily News, Meeks, 30, “was arrested during a gang sweep June 18 for gun possession in Stockton, Calif.” Having already served time in 2002 for grand theft, this most recent charge could have landed the model prisoner up to 10 years in the pokey.

On Thursday a federal judge sentenced Meeks to more than two years in prison and ordered that the he take part in substance abuse program.

Meeks was in prison when his mug shot photo, which was posted to the Stockton Police Department’s Facebook page, went viral. According to the Daily News, Meeks’ photo garnered some “100,000 likes, 12,800 shares and nearly 27,000 comments.”
