Disgraced Former House Speaker Dennis Hastert Expected to Enter Plea Deal in Court

June 2, 2015 National, zTransfer-UCN

Former Speaker of the House Dennis Hastert, R-Ill., is in the Illinois delegation at the 2012 Republican National Convention at the Tampa Bay Times Forum. (PHOTO CREDIT: Chris Maddalon/Getty Images)

Former U.S. House Speaker Dennis Hastert is expected to make his first court appearance Thursday on charges that he paid $3.5 million in hush money to cover up sexual misconduct.

Hastert, 73, an Illinois Republican who was once second in line to the U.S. presidency, will likely enter a plea before a U.S. District Judge Thomas Durkin.

The judge once donated $1,500 to Hastert’s reelection campaign.

The former pol was indicted Thursday on charges he lied to FBI agents to conceal payments he allegedly made to a former male student to keep quiet about sexual abuse.

If convicted, Hastert faces a maximum penalty of 10 years in prison and a $500,000 fine.
