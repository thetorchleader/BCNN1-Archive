In “America”, Dr. Tony Evans Says ‘It’s Time for the Church to Speak Up, to Lead Out’

January 7, 2015 Christian

Tony Evans

A noted author and pastor contends the root of America’s social, political and economic unrest is that the nation has turned its back on God.

Dr. Tony Evans, pastor of Oak Cliff Bible Fellowship and author of America: Turning a Nation to God, writes that the impetus behind the problems in the U.S. are spiritual – even though the symptoms reveal themselves as physical, social, financial and racial.

“The burden on my heart is the reclaiming of God as the centerpiece of the culture through the leadership of the Church,” the pastor tells OneNewsNow. “I think the Church has been the tail, not the head; we have been reacting, not leading – and it’s time for the Church to speak up, to lead out…

“And so we argue in America: Turning a Nation to God that unless the Church leads out, there is no hope for America.”

Evans believes the time has come to re-invite God back into the culture.

“… What we are saying is let’s get churches together – without compromising any of the fundamentals – across racial, cultural, class and denominational lines to re-invite God back into the centerpiece of the culture,” he shares, “because if [God] can work to us, then He can work through us to impact the culture from us.”

Evans’ prayer is that the plan revealed in his new book will lead the Church to impact the nation’s leaders.
