Global Day of Worship at Regent University on November 11

November 7, 2011 Uncategorized

The first annual Global Day of Worship (GDW) is scheduled for November 11, 2011. This may turn out to be one of the most dramatic and powerful days in the history of our world, as countless worshipers will focus their attention on loving and lifting high the name of Jesus. 

The GDW strives to unify the body of Christ while embracing and celebrating differences in expressions of worship. Worshipers of Jesus Christ from various churches, denominations, organizations and geographic locations with a common vision to see Jesus lifted up and celebrated around the world will participate.

Like the coming of a new year, for one hour in each time zone (7-8 p.m.), a wave of worship and adoration across the nations will unite the Church as one body. Beginning at GMT 7:00 p.m., worship will traverse westward across the globe. Every hour a new time zone’s participants will provide a live stream of their worship to central servers until the entire earth has been part of an event unlike anything experienced before. From individual expressions to large groups, from solemn liturgical assemblies or family gatherings in home churches to loud and exuberant expressions, the globe will worship the Living God on 11.11.11. Believers can worship Jesus through reflective expressions of love or loud, joyful celebration. They can sing hymns and choruses or engage in free-flowing passionate worship.
Those expressions, from myriad tribes and tongues, will be streamed live to an Internet Protocol Television site hosted in Virginia Beach, VA by Regent University and the Christian Broadcasting Network – and made available to viewers around the world. Viewers will be able to watch and participate with fellow believers’ worship via the worldwide web or by logging on to the IPTV site. CBN and Regent University are serving as the hosts and producers of the event. CBN viewers will be able to watch six hours of worship on CBN.com from 6:00 p.m until midnight EST. All 24 hours of worship will be streamed live on the GDW Web site beginning at 1:00 a.m. EST.
