Drew Brees and Family Go to See “Noah” Movie Dressed in Full Biblical Costume

March 24, 2014 Sports

Drew Brees tweeted this photo with the message: “Saw @NoahMovie the other night at Cinepolis. Opens 3/28. Very intense! #thefloodiscoming” (Credit: Twitter, @drewbrees)

Saints quarterback Drew Brees and his wife, Brittany, saw the movie “Noah” last week.

The film, which opens in theaters this Friday, stars Russell Crowe trying to save the world from a flood.

The couple decided to dress up for the occasion, complete with aluminum foil ax.
