Barbecue Season Is Here! Here Are Some Delicious Recipes to Try

May 25, 2015 From Around the Block

© Rogers Media Inc. 2015

Yay! Barbecue season is here, so get ready to cook up some delicious meals your family will love.

THE ULTIMATE TURKEY BURGER
Turkey can sometimes taste dry, but the bacon and cheese in this burger guarantee it’s one of the juiciest and most flavourful you’ll ever eat!

FOIL PACKETS OF SAUSAGE, PEPPERS AND POTATOES
This one-package meal means practically no cleanup, especially if you serve everyone their packets in the foil. Oh, and it’s delicious, too.

JAMAICAN JERK CHICKEN
There may be no chilies here, but this chicken delivers on the original taste of jerk seasoning, without the heat.

GREEK PORK KEBABS
Kids love meals on a stick, since they’re so easy for them to eat.

GRILLED SHRIMP SALAD
Looking for a lighter supper salad that will keep your family satisfied? Then look no further!

BEST-EVER RIBS AND CORN
What could be better than ribs and corn? And this recipe includes homemade barbecue sauce, so you control the ingredients.

MARMALADE GLAZED SALMON ON A PLANK
Super-healthy salmon gets a touch of sweetness from the marmalade in the glaze.

GRILLED CHICKPEA BURGER
Tired of your regular beef burger? Here’s a fresh, vegetarian alternative.

GRILLED FISH ‘N’ CHIPS
Deep fried? Forget about it! Barbecuing makes for a healthy, modern version of this combo.

THAI GRILLED CHICKEN SALAD
This Thai grilled chicken recipe is a healthy and gluten-free dinner sure to please your family.
