Approximately 1000 People Braved the Cold for Premiere of ‘Preachers of Detroit’

February 27, 2015 Black

Pastors of Detroit’s, Evangelist Dorinda Clark Cole

Last Friday the Change Agent Consortium and Mix 92.3 presented the “Preachers of Detroit” Premiere Party, powered by Superstar Media Group.

The “Preachers of Detroit” premiere was held at the Garden Theatre in Detroit, MI and despite record breaking- low temperatures there were over 1,000 attendees at the red carpet premiere.

Following the show, attendees had an opportunity to watch a panel discussion of hot topics involving Preachers of Detroit cast members, led by Mix 92.3 Detroit Radio Personality- Tracey McCaskill.
