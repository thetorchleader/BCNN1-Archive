It Is Just Now Being Revealed that Josh Duggar Was Investigated In Underage Sex Probe in 2005

May 21, 2015 Entertainment

© AMI Editors josh-duggar-sex-scandal-family-lockdown-pp

Just one day after a bombshell report revealed that Josh Duggar was accused of committing a sex crime in his teens, the typically chatty 19 Kids And Counting family is keeping quiet, RadarOnline.com can reveal. 

When asked for a comment about the shocking story, Josh’s bubbly cousin Amy Duggar told Radar she had “nothing to say at all about that.”

Josh’s sister-in-law Susanna Keller also shut down.

“That’s personal, I’m not talking about that,” she said to Radar.

With the sex scandal blowing up online, the reality stars took to social media in an apparent attempt to distract fans.

Just hours after the news hit, soon-to-be father of four Josh, 27, tweeted about filming with his fervent Christian clan and Today weekend co-host Erica Hill.
