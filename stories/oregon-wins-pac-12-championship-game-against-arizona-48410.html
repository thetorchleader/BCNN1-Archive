Oregon Wins Pac 12 Championship Game Against Arizona

December 6, 2014 Sports

Oregon quarterback Marcus Mariota scores on a 7-yard touchdown run.
(Photo: Kirby Lee, USA TODAY Sports)

Analyzing No. 2 (in playoff ranking) Oregon’s 51-13 victory against No. 7 Arizona in the Pac-12 Championship Game:

THE BIG PICTURE: The Ducks (12-1) rolled to the Pac-12 title — and from there, into the College Football Playoff field — with a dominant performance in avenging their only loss of the season. Oregon’s offense started slowly, but the Ducks pulled away. Marcus Mariota was inconsistent but occasionally sensational in likely wrapping up a bid for the Heisman Trophy, but the Ducks’ defense was the story. Oregon led 23-0 at halftime, when Arizona (10-3) had 25 total yards (minus-9 rushing) and two first downs — both on its first possession. The Wildcats’ final six possessions of the first half went three-and-out. It was a marked difference from Arizona’s offensive performances in two consecutive wins against Oregon.

WHAT WE’LL BE TALKING ABOUT: It developed slowly, but Oregon made an emphatic statement. There wasn’t any question that with a win the Ducks would be headed for the Playoff. But their performance — especially defensively — against a good Arizona team that had beaten them in early October was a strong closing argument. It might depend on what happens elsewhere — how Alabama performs against Missouri, for example, in the SEC Championship — but the Ducks might wind up as the four-team tournament’s top seed.
