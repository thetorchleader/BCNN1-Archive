Steve Harvey Says ‘God Is Good!’ After Winning Two Daytime Emmy Awards

June 23, 2014 Entertainment

Steve Harvey

Steve Harvey is a testament to what is truly possible with faith, perseverance and undying belief in your dreams. The once homeless comedian is having the time of his life and is giving all glory to God.

“God is good! Thank you to all of my fans for supporting me,” tweeted the 57-year-old, following news that he had won two Daytime Emmy Awards.

The “Steve Harvey” show was honored as outstanding informative talk show, while Harvey won as best game show host for “Family Feud.”

Though Harvey did not attend the live 41st annual ceremony in Beverly Hills that streamed over the Internet, he is thrilled by the honor and recognition.
