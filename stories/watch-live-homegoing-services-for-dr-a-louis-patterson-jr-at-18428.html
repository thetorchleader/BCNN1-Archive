WATCH LIVE: Homegoing Services for Dr. A. Louis Patterson, Jr. at 11:00 am CST

April 17, 2014 Christian

Click here to watch live on BCNN3.TV

Dr. and Mrs. A. L. Patterson at the National Baptist Congress of Christian Education
Tribute to Dr. Patterson, St. Louis, MO, June 19, 2013

Dr. Albert Louis Patterson, Jr., pastor of the Mount Corinth Missionary Baptist Church of Houston, TX was called home to glory today, April 9, 2014.  

To READ Dr. Patterson’s obituary online, click here.
To PRINT Dr. Patterson’s obituary, click here

Funeral Arrangements

A viewing will be held Wednesday April 16, 2014 from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm at Mount Corinth Missionary Baptist Church 4901 Providence Street. Services will be held Thursday April 17, 2014 at Mount Corinth Baptist Church. Visitation will begin at 8:30 am with funeral starting at 11:00 am. Interment will follow at Brookside Memorial Park 13747 East Texas Freeway, Houston, TX.

——————————————–

Message from President Scruggs:

A valiant soldier and preacher/pastor of the National Baptist Convention, USA, Inc. and a valuable member of the body of Christ has been called home by our Lord.  We will greatly miss him.

Our prayers are being lifted to God for his wife and family and for the family of the Mount  Corinth Baptist Church.

President Julius R. Scruggs
