WATCH: Kirk Franklin Looks to Bring Some 'Flavor' to The American Bible Challenge

February 14, 2013 Uncategorized

NBC Rock Center’s Harry Smith sat down with gospel singer Kirk Franklin to discuss his new role as a cast member on popular faith-based game show, “The American Bible Challenge.” Franklin says he wants to “bring a little more sauce to it” and “make it a little more fun.” WATCH!
The new season of The American Bible Challenge begins March 21.
