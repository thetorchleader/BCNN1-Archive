Shonda Rhimes and Viola Davis Team Up for “How to Get Away With Murder” In September

August 8, 2014 Entertainment

A new show is catching the attention of critics, called ‘How To Get Away With Murder’. CCTV Hollywood reporter, Jonathan Shupack, sits down with the show’s star, Viola Davis, to find out what it’s all about.

‘How To Get Away With Murder’ has two things immediately going for it. It is produced by Shonda Rhimes and it stars Viola Davis. Sitting down with her, Ms. Davis explained the show and her character.

“It is a show that centers on this criminal defense attorney who is also a criminal law professor at a fictitious Ivy League school. It is about her and a murder mystery involving her students that will eventually reveal something very sinister – and that’s all I can say,” she said.

A Tony Award-winning actress, Viola Davis has enjoyed success on film as well as the stage, appearing in such movies as The Help and Doubt each garnering an Academy award nomination.

“We’ve seen this trend where lot’s of a-list movie actors are making their way to television. What are your comments on this trend? What are your personal reasons for doing this?” reporter asked.

“The material. The material. Nobody is writing like this for me in movies. Not like this in a way that’s sexual and intriguing and very vulnerable and you go wherever the material is whether it’s TV, film, or even the stage,” she said.

How To Get Away With Murder is produced by Shonda Rhimes, the prolific producer that has won a combined 36 awards for herself and her actors.

Reporter: “Shonda Rhimes has surprised us with great shows like Scandal and Grey’s Anatomy. What do you think audiences can expect from this particular show?”
