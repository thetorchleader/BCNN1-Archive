Here are 10 Perks of Costco or Sam’s Club Membership

April 21, 2015 Lifestyle

1. TECHNICAL SUPPORT
Members who have questions about setting up or using a TV, home theater, digital music player, camera, or computer purchased at a warehouse club can rely on free tech support by phone. It’s available 24/7 at Sam’s Club and 5 a.m. to 10 p.m., excluding holidays, at Costco.

2. HEALTH SCREENINGS
Sam’s Club offers free health screenings at its pharmacies once a month. In April the focus is allergy relief and screenings include glucose, cholesterol, and blood pressure testing. Costco schedules osteoporosis, heart, and lung screenings at select locations throughout the month.

3. TIRE SERVICES
Both wholesale clubs rotate, balance, and repair tires at no charge. At Sam’s Club, the free flat repair applies even for tires bought somewhere else, and wiper-blade installation and car battery testing is also free.

4. DRUG PRICES THAT UNDERCUT YOUR COPAY
A 2013 Consumer Reports survey found that Costco has such low prices on generic medications that customers with insurance coverage are better off paying cash for certain prescription drugs. Five generics are free (in most states) with a premium Sam’s Plus membership.

5. TRAVEL DEALS
Just as you might ask about an AAA or AARP discount, ask if there’s a reduced rate for Sam’s Club or Costco members when booking travel. Both clubs have online travel portals and booking by phone for vacation packages, car rental, hotels, cruises, and more.

6. SATISFACTION GUARANTEES
Costco and Sam’s Club both promise to refund your membership fee if you’re not satisfied. They set no time limit for returns of most merchandise, with the exception of electronics. Sam’s Club’s Freshness Guarantee allows for a full refund plus replacement of fresh produce, meat, and baked goods, or double your money back.
