It Is Reported that Christian NBA Star Kevin Durant May Be Represented by Jay-Z’s Roc Nation

June 8, 2013 Uncategorized

Oklahoma City Thunder star Kevin Durant has decided to cut ties with agent Rob Pelinka and go without representation, a person with knowledge of the situation told USA TODAY Sports.
The person spoke to USA TODAY Sports on the condition of anonymity because of the sensitivity of the situation.
Pelinka, who heads Landmark Sports Agency, which represents the Los Angeles Lakers’ Kobe Bryant and Houston Rockets’ James Harden, confirmed the decision in a statement.
“We are honored and blessed to have worked on behalf of Kevin, for a brief period of time,” Pelinka said. “He is an amazing person and athlete, and we are proud of our work and service towards advancing his brand in a manner that reflects the mutual qualities we share with Kevin. We wish him nothing but success in his future endeavors.”
If Durant follows through on what he has been telling family members is his plan, he will look to partner with the Roc Nation sports agency that is headed by music mogul Jay-Z. Yet it appears his interest is only in the marketing opportunities that could come from the partnership and that he does not have a desire to be represented by the agent who works with Roc Nation, Leon Rose of the Creative Artists Agency.
SportsBusiness Journal reported the decision first.
