Two Tuscaloosa, Alabama Home Invasion Teen Suspects Appear in Court

March 26, 2015 Black

Marquanda Anderson. Source: WBRC video

Two of six Tuscaloosa-area home invasion suspects appeared in court on Tuesday morning.

Tuscaloosa County District Judge Joel Chandler found probable cause for 17-year-old Marquanda Anderson and 18-year-old Khali Jones to remain in jail on their current charges and bond amounts.

Prosecutors accuse them of taking part in two home invasions in Tuscaloosa and one in Northport in January and February.

John Fisher, an attorney for Anderson, says she was in wrong place at the wrong time.

“It’s an unfortunate situation. You have a 17-year-old child, literally who got caught up with a bunch of hardened individuals, I’m going to say criminals. That what these boys were, and they took advantage of Marquanda and the entire situation and now she’s got to really fight to get herself out it,” Fisher said.

He says Anderson was an acquaintance of Tyler Davis, one of the other suspects charged in the case.

They grew up together and reconnected again shortly before the crime spree started.

During the hearing, an investigator testified one victim heard a female voice in the home invasion crew say, “I’ll kill you, grandma,” as they tried to cash her certified check.

Investigators believe Marquanda Anderson made that threat.

None of the victims could identify the suspects.

An investigator testified they covered their faces or put something over the victims heads before ransacking homes and stealing electronics.

They were charged after Birmingham police arrested four of them in a car stolen from Tuscaloosa that crashed while they were investigating a home invasion there.

Some of them claimed Anderson and Tyler Davis were also involved in the Tuscaloosa area home invasions.

But Fisher said Tyler Davis forced Anderson to participate in the crimes.

“It’s hard to get out of a situation like that where you have real life criminals with guns and things are going on. Basically she was biding her time till they would permissibly allow her to leave,” Fisher said.

Gregory Stewart, Cassius Lanier Jr. and Jonathan Davis are also charged with kidnapping burglary and robbery in the cases.

The other four suspects are being held in Birmingham on similar home invasion charges.
