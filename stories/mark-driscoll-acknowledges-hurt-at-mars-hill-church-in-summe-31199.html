Mark Driscoll Acknowledges “Hurt” at Mars Hill Church In Summer Video/Letter to Megachurch Members

July 23, 2014 Christian

(The Resurgence)

The senior pastor at Mars Hill Church, Mark Driscoll, has delivered a long summer video/letter to mega-church members in which he admits to conflict with “a group of largely anonymous former leaders,” acknowledges “hurt” and speaks vaguely of reconciling with critics.

The half-hour message is far less apologetic than a mea culpa delivered by Driscoll in March, when he lamented for hiring a firm to game sales of his book “Real Marriage,” took himself off the internet, and pledged to back off from being a celebrity.

Still, Driscoll displays a far more humble mien than is normally the case, telling followers:

“Thank you for allowing me to be your pastor. I love you with a deep affection. And I am sorry for the hurting that some of you are feeling, and by God’s grade we will love one another as a family . . ..”

As noted by Warren Throckmorton in Patheos, “Consistent with the dominant narrative in recent years, Driscoll omitted Lief Moi and Mike Gunn who co-founded Mars Hill Church.”

Driscoll has seemed, in recent years, on top of the world. Mars Hill has opened churches as far distant as Orange County, California, and has claimed as many as 14,000 members. Driscoll was subject of a profile in the New York Times Magazine, depicting a strictly disciplined church that preaches male primacy, condemns homosexuality and has shunned some former members.

In recent months, however, he has been bedeviled by charges he has lifted the writings of others, the use of Mars Hill money to get his book on the New York Times bestseller list, and anguished defections of former pastors at the mega-church.

“If I’m real honest with you, at first it was just a little overwhelming and a bit confusing,” Driscoll says in his latest communication. “We, and I, were not exactly sure what was happening so it took a little while to sort it out.”
