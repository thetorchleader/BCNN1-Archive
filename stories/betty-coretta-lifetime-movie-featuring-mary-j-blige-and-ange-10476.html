'Betty & Coretta' Lifetime Movie Featuring Mary J Blige and Angela Bassett Set for February Premiere

January 30, 2013 Uncategorized

“Betty & Coretta,” the made for television movie centered around the
lives of Dr. Betty Shabazz and Coretta Scott King, has been given a
premiere date. Lifetime broke the news confirming the series will
premiere in February.

Starring Angela Bassett as Coretta Scott King and Mary J. Blige as Dr. Betty Shabazz, “Betty & Coretta” tells the dual “real-life stories” of both women following the tragic assassinations of their husbands (Dr. Martin Luther King and Malcolm X).

Also starring Malik Yoba (King) and Lindsay Owen Pierre (Malcolm X), “Betty & Coretta” kicks off Lifetime’s celebration of Black History Month.
