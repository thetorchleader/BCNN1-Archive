Building Donated to Church of Deliverance United Holy Church in West Virginia Whose Building Was Burned Down by Arsonist

January 28, 2015 Black

KENNY KEMP | Sunday Gazette-Mail

St. Albans United Methodist Church has donated the vacant St. Luke United Methodist Church to the Church of Deliverance United Holy Church for temporary use. The Church of Deliverance building was destroyed last week by an arsonist.

A Kanawha County church that was set on fire last week has been ruled a total loss, but another congregation has loaned members a vacant church to use for services.

The predominantly black Church of Deliverance United Holy Church was intentionally set on fire on Martin Luther King Jr. Day, the State Fire Marshal’s Office has determined.

Elder Howard Nappier, the assistant pastor at the church, said he won’t jump to concluding that racism might have been involved, “but it’s awful funny that it happened on that day.”

“God doesn’t look at color,” he said. “He loves us all. I don’t want to say that.”

He said he and his wife, Elder Estella Nappier, are turning to their faith to heal them in the aftermath of the fire, but the stress is taking its toll. He said his wife’s cellulitis, a bacterial skin infection that can turn life-threatening if left untreated, flared up Thursday night. He had to rush her to the emergency room.

“A lot of it’s the stress with the church being destroyed,” he said. “The doctor feels the same way.”

Earlier last week, Nappier and his wife, the church’s pastor, were thinking about trying to rebuild on the same site. He said Friday they had changed their minds.

“We’re not going to rebuild there,” he said. “We’re thinking about moving. We’ve had so much trouble there. We don’t know what is wrong.”

Last year, around Christmas time, someone broke into the church and stole thousands of dollars worth of items — from musical instruments to kitchen supplies, and even Bibles.

“We’ve had cars vandalized while we were in there having services,” he said. “We’ve had rocks thrown that broke the windows. I don’t know what the problem is.”
