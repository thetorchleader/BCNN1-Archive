‘Think Like a Man’ Hung In There at #2

May 7, 2012 Uncategorized

By now, you’ve already heard, either from friends or news reports, the Hulk — alongside Iron Man and the rest of “The Avengers” — smashed this weekend’s box office, doing numbers so big, it broke records.

In addition to Avengers, last weekend’s #1 film, “Think Like A Man”, fell to #2 with $8 million; “The Hunger Games” came in at #3 with $5.7 million; “The Lucky One” held at #4 with $5.5 million; and “The Pirates! Band of Misfits” closes out the top 5 with $5.4 million.
