Jazz and Gospel Music Reign at 9th Annual Taste of Soul

October 26, 2014 Black

Linda Broadous-Miles (photo by Nell Cunningham)

The 9th Annual Taste of Soul was filled with an array of culinary, visual and musical delights. Many of the musical delights were found on the Brenda Marsh-Mitchell Gospel Stage, which was dedicated to the late Taste of Soul president. The theme of the stage was ‘God loves you, period.’

The Holman Quartet with Linda Broadous-Miles opened with jazz-oriented praise. They began with a cover of ‘Blessed Assurance,’ which had the crowd moving, especially when Linda scatted.

They shined with the blues-flavored jazz, ‘The Lord Will Make A Way,’ which had the crowd rocking in their seats, waving their hands and shouting. Saxophonist Terry took over the lead on the jamming instrumental version, ‘Lord, I Lift Your Name On High,’ which had this reporter rocking in my seat. They ended with the crowd pleasing cover of Fred Hammond’s, ‘We’re Blessed.’ Everyone in the crowd was singing along to the chorus.

Next up was May West and part of the KJLH Gospel Choir, who also sang ‘We’re Blessed’ with the band and comedian host, J-Red, provided the rap that got the crowd hyped up.

FAME Unity Choir raised the praise with the song, ‘Are You Ready (Let The Praise Begin)’ and the song also set the crowd on fire.  They ushered in the Holy Spirit with the song, ‘Ye Shall Have Eternal Life,’ which was led by Amber.  The crowd was very pleased with the singing and dancing on the songs, ‘Great God’ and ‘They That Wait.’
