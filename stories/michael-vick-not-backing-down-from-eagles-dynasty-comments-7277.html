Michael Vick Not Backing Down from Eagles “Dynasty” Comments

July 23, 2012 Uncategorized

If you thought Michael Vick would back down from the “dynasty” comment he made, dream on.


Last week, the Eagles starting quarterback said he felt Philadelphia had the chance to become a dynasty, much like 49ers of the 1980s and Cowboys of the 1990s.
On Sunday, he stood firmly behind that proclamation.
“That’s just me speaking out of confidence, and I think if you ask my teammates they’ll tell you the same thing, that they feel the same way,” Vick told CSNPhilly.com shortly after arriving in Bethlehem, Pa., for the start of training camp. “As a quarterback, that’s just how I feel. You’ve got to speak it into existence. You’ve got to aim to shoot for the stars, and if you miss at least you’ll be amongst them, and … I think that’s gotta be our mindset.”
Last year, Vince Young created a firestorm — not to mention lofty expectations — when he called the Eagles a “Dream Team” after bringing aboard a bunch of high-profile free agents. To Vick, though, there’s a distinction between his words and what Young said last year.
