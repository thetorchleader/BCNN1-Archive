WATCH: Regent University Announces New Program to Reach African-American and Latino Students; Partnering with The Potter’s House and Other Churches

April 16, 2013 LCNN1, Uncategorized

Regent University is launching a new program to help African American and Latino students complete a degree in higher education.

Regent’s president Dr. Carlos Campo announced the “Take 2 Through College” program Monday.
The university will partner with churches, like The Potter’s House in Dallas, and other groups to sponsor and mentor students at Regent.
Each partner provides up to 50 percent tuition and fees for two undergraduate students and Regent matches the funds.
Dr. Campo said the initiative is aimed at attracting more minorities to Christian education.
“Christian colleges and universities have not done that well recruiting students of color. So we’re partnering with churches who have a very high commitment or maybe even a demographic that’s reflected in a Hispanic or African American community because African American and Hispanic males in particular have dropped out of higher education,” he said.
