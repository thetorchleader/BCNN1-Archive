Christian Broadcasting Network Honored With Seven 2015 Media Awards from the National Religious Broadcasters

December 19, 2014 Media

The National Religious Broadcasters 2015 Media Awards have honored the Christian Broadcasting Network with seven programs or series receiving recognition.

NRB named “The 700 Club” as Best Television Talk Show, “700 Club Interactive” as Best Television Teaching Program, and “Jerusalem Dateline” as Best Creative Television Programming.

CBN News Director Rob Allman said he’s especially pleased to see recognition for Jerusalem Dateline, a weekly program started three years ago by the CBN News Jerusalem Bureau.

“We’re thrilled to be recognized for work on such an important topic, especially in these days,” Allman said.

Jerusalem Dateline reports on events in Jerusalem, Israel, and the Middle East from a biblical perspective.
