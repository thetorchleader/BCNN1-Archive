Serena Williams says She Was Not Drunk, on Drugs, or Pregnant during Wimbledon Meltdown

August 26, 2014 Sports

(Getty Images)

In her first in-depth comments on the subject, Serena Williams told S.L. Price of Sports Illustrated that she wasn’t drinking, on drugs or pregnant during a surreal incident at Wimbledon when she appeared disoriented and withdrew from a doubles match with her sister, Venus.

Price, who profiled Serena ahead of this week’s U.S. Open, said the world No. 1 had heard all the rumors about what caused her to hit serves into the ground and rendered her unable to catch a bouncing ball.

“I have nothing to hide,” she says. “No, I didn’t take anything. If you want to ask me if I took drugs, I didn’t take drugs. I’m not on drugs. I’ve heard it all. I’m not pregnant; I wasn’t pregnant. Although I think a baby would be great, but there’s a time and place for everything. But no, I don’t do drugs. Never did ’em. I’m scared of ’em. I’m not on that stuff.”

Word had gone around that some in the women’s locker room that day, watching her distress live on TV, were pantomiming Serena drinking. But, Williams says, she wasn’t drunk, either. “God, no,” she says, laughing. “I wish.”

She later explains that she was dehydrated, dizzy and feverish after her shock third-round loss to Alize Cornet. When she tried to play in the doubles match three days later, she was no better. The “viral illness” excuse had famously been rejected by Martina Navratilova, who said “I think it’s clear that’s not the case” in the days after the match.

Serena told Price she was drug tested the day after the incident and didn’t receive any notification of a positive result.
