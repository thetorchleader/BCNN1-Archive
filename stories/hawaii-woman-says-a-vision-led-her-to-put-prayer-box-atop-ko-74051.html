Hawaii Woman Says a Vision Led Her to Put Prayer Box Atop Koko Head Trail

May 17, 2015 National

The prayer box on Koko Crater Trail. (Facebook)

Lots of people look up when they’re searching for inspiration, but one Hawaii woman climbs there.

Angela Tomiye keeps a prayer box at the top of the Koko Head trail, a vision that came to her one day along with a powerful message.

“I heard him say very clear to me, ‘Angela, everybody needs prayer, but not everyone is willing to walk into a church to receive it,’” Tomiye said.

And so Tomiye brought the church to one of Hawaii’s toughest hikes in a form of her grandpa’s old tool box turned into a prayer box.

“I didn’t tell anyone about it because even in my own mind, I was like, this is kind of crazy!” said Tomiye. “Hiked up here, put the box up there, and just left it. And I didn’t know what would come.”

In the year since, each and every week, she finds dozens of messages left by people from around the world, all different but with one common theme.

“The most common prayer has been happiness,” said Tomiye, “that they find happiness.”

When paper runs out, people get creative and write on bottle wrappers, bandaids and hotel stationary.

“It fills my heart,” said Tomiye.”It feels like Christmas.”

Recently, Tomiye found a message from a husband to his late wife on a scrap of cardboard that said, “Dena, I love you and miss you. The kids are okay. Love John.”

“It is a big responsibility,” said Tomiye. “I feel incredibly fortunate and I feel fortunate that we get to read this and we get to pray over John and his family.”
