Cory “CoCo Brother” Condrey Interviews First Lady Michelle Obama

November 1, 2012 Uncategorized

Earlier this year, First Lady Michelle Obama sat down for a chat with Cory “CoCo Brother” Condrey, host of BET’s Lift Every Voice during the second anniversary celebration of Let’s Move!, her nationwide campaign to end childhood obesity. Now, with just 5 days to go before Election Day, Mrs. O is headed back to BET for a one-hour exclusive set to air tonight at 8:30 PM.
In her previous interview with Condrey, the first lady touched on motherhood, her spiritual life, and of course, physical health.  
While speaking about the impact of prayer on her family, she said, “It is the thing that sustains us. I’m always blessed that when we go places, there are people who come up and they just give you hug and a squeeze, and they say they’re praying for us. And I tell ’em keep it going, don’t stop, because we feel that. There is an unspoken energy that comes from the millions of people who are lifting us up in prayer.”
