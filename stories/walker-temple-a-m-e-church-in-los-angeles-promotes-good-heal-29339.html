Walker Temple A.M.E. Church in Los Angeles Promotes Good Health

Date not recorded in the original archive Black

Residents of all ethnicities filled the parking lot at Walker Temple A.M.E. Church to take advantage of free screenings, cooking demonstrations and social services assistance available at the church’s Community Health Fair on June 28. 

Participants included (above, from left) Kay Silva and Jena Song of Yonsei Medical Clinic, Los Angeles Councilman Curren Price, Pastor Rosalynn K. Brookins and Lillian Witt.

Below, Pastor Brookins and Councilman Price greet neighborhood youth who enjoyed face-painting and other fun activities at the event.

Thirty-one vendors participated in the fair. Also, Walker Temple’s Feeding Ministry distributed two bags of groceries to more than 200 individuals and families.
