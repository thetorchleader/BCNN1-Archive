WATCH: Mother of Tamir Rice says Police Have Not Apologized for Killing her Son

March 3, 2015 Black

Lawyer Benjamin Crump, left, stands beside Samaria Rice, whose son was killed Nov. 22, 2014, by police as he carried a pellet gun.
(Photo: WKYC-TV, Cleveland)

The mother of a 12-year-old whom police shot in November as he was holding a pellet gun said Tuesday that city officials have been very hurtful in their conduct since the killing.

In a response filed last week to a federal lawsuit that Tamir Rice’s family filed because of his death, the city’s lawyers blamed the child for bringing on his own death.

“The city’s answer was very disrespectful to my son, Tamir,” Samaria Rice said at a press conference at Olivet Institutional Baptist Church. “I have not yet received an apology from the police department or the city of Cleveland in regards to the killing of my son, and it hurts.”

On Nov. 22, an officer responding to a call about someone with a firearm shot the boy twice in the torso as he walked in the playground area of a city recreation center. Tamir’s weapon was an air-soft gun, but authorities have said it was indistinguishable from a real gun because its orange tip was removed.
