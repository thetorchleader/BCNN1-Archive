WATCH LIVE: Essence Festival 2014

Date not recorded in the original archive Entertainment

If you can’t make it to the 20th anniversary ESSENCE Festival in New Orleans this weekend, don’t fret! We’re bringing the experience right to you here on ESSENCE.com. From the concerts to the ESSENCE Empowerment Experience seminars, we are livestreaming the best content for your Fourth of July weekend.

Here’s the full schedule of the 2014 ESSENCE Festival livestreams: (all times are EST)

Thursday
11:50pm:Nas

Friday
1:10-2pm: Robin Roberts “Sisters in the Name of Love”
3-4pm: Soledad O’Brien’s “Empowering Your Voice on Youtube”
9:20pm: Alice Smith concert

Saturday
12:15pm: The Mayor’s Panel: “Our Cities, Our Solutions”
9:30pm: Marsha Ambrosius

Sunday
3-7pm: Praising Together: All Star Gospel Tribute to Yolanda Adams
8:30pm: Sevyn Streeter concert

You can watch these events live at the times above on Essence’s YouTube channel
