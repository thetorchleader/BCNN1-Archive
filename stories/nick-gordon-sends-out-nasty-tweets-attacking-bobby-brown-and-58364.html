Nick Gordon Sends Out Nasty Tweets Attacking Bobby Brown and Other Family Members

February 24, 2015 Entertainment

The public nastiness between Bobbi Kristina Brown’s family and her under-suspicion boyfriend, Nick Gordon, escalated again on Monday with scores of accusatory tweets from Gordon.

Barred by her father, Bobby Brown, from her Atlanta hospital room where she has been comatose since Jan. 31, Gordon lashed out at him, accusing him of neglecting Bobbi Kristina for years, of wanting to get his hands on her money, and of issuing death threats against Gordon.

Gordon tweeted he regretted not getting to her in time to perform CPR after she was found unresponsive in a bathtub in their Roswell, Ga., home. He said he’s talked to the Roswell police about their ongoing investigation of what happened, and that he visited her bedside along with family friend Tyler Perry.

Meanwhile, The Wrap reported that Gordon also texted Bobby Brown that, “Your daughter is going to hate you when she wakes up.”

“Man to man, I love your daughter,” Gordon wrote in a screenshot he posted to Twitter that later disappeared. “I don’t know why you won’t let me see her. Grow up.
