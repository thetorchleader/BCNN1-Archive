WATCH: New Unfortunate Video of Ray and Janay Rice Right After Elevator Incident

December 19, 2014 Sports

http://abcnews.go.com/video/embed?id=27726761
More ABC News Videos | ABC World News

ABC News obtained 45 minutes of surveillance footage from now-defunct Revel Casino where Ray Rice knocked out his then-fiancee, now-wife Janay Palmer.

You can watch a two-minute summary of the footage, which shows the two going through a very wide range of emotions.

Rice is warded off by Casino security, Palmer shoves Rice away as he approaches and seems to attempt to apologize, she cries as security gives her first aid. As the two are escorted onto the same elevator, Rice knocks his head on the wall in what looks like dejection while Palmer, who is also handcuffed at this point (as Palmer was also issued a charge of assault that was later dismissed) speaks to him.

They embrace each other and even appear to kiss before they’re loaded into separate police cars.

(Thanks to ABC News for sharing.)
