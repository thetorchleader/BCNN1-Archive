Will the Church Stand for God, the Lord Jesus Christ, and the Bible, and March on Washington for Religious Freedom and Free Speech and Against Homosexual Marriage and the Homosexual Agenda?

March 26, 2015 Uncategorized

Is God giving the church the opportunity of the centuries to show her love for Him?

If there ever was a time to march against something that is a real issue in our society, that time is now. The issue is religious freedom and free speech in light of homosexual marriage and the homosexual agenda. The church needs to stand for God and truth in our society. Not since the Civil Rights Movement, which was a righteous cause, has the church had such an important cause to march on Washington. If we love God, easily 7 million Christians should march on Washington along with people of goodwill. It should be the largest march in the history of the world. If you really want racial reconciliation, nothing should bring black, white, red, and yellow Christians and people of goodwill together like this issue. Homosexual marriage has nothing to do with the Civil Rights Movement of the 60s. Martin Luther King Jr. did not die for homosexual marriage. Who will be the Martin Luther King Jr. of our time?

BCNN1 is calling on Christian leaders to lead a mass march some time within the next few weeks to the steps of the Supreme Court to let the Supreme Court, President Obama, the Justice Department, and this nation know that there are Christians in this country who oppose the legalization of same-sex marriage. If the court chooses to legalize same-sex marriage, then we demand that, as a part of their ruling, they put provisions in place that protect churches, Christians, Christian-owned businesses, and other religious-based institutions from being sued, forced to participate in, or otherwise facilitate homosexual weddings. BCNN1 is urging the following Christian leaders to get together and make this happen: Tony Evans, T.D. Jakes, Ronnie Floyd, Rick Warren, John Hagee, Russell Moore, Al Mohler, Fred Luter, Dwight McKissic, Frank Page, Randy Vaughn, Franklin Graham, Charles Stanley, and others. Who will be the Martin Luther King Jr. of our time?

–BCNN1
