Chris Brown Reportedly Has a 9-Month-Old Daughter With Former Model

March 4, 2015 Entertainment

Surprise! Chris Brown is allegedly a father.

The “Look at Me Now” singer is reportedly a dad to a 9-month-old baby girl with a former model by the name of Nia, according toTMZ.

Brown and the 31-year-old baby mama are allegedly not romantically involved, although they are on very good terms, a source told TMZ.

It is being reported that Brown is happy about being a dad and there is no formal child support order in place.

The singer is on tour and will be performing his next concert Friday in San Jose, Calif.

Reps for Brown have yet to respond to the Daily News’ request for comment.

SOURCE: NY Daily News – Elizabeth Vanmetre
