Bridge Washes Out in West Virginia Stranding 100 Residents

March 5, 2015 National

Robert Caldwell, standing in his doorway, found himself on the wrong side of the Guyandotte River as he and his 5-month-old child were stranded in their Madison Creek home after the temporary bridge that crosses the river was washed out in Logan County on Wednesday morning. (F. BRIAN FERGUSON | Gazette photos)

Robert Caldwell had just parked his truck on the Neibert Bottom side of the Guyandotte River on Wednesday and walked across a temporary bridge to his mobile home on the Madison Creek side of the river when the causeway broke loose and washed downstream. It was about 9:40 a.m.

The washed-out bridge left Caldwell on the wrong side of the river with a 5-month-old baby and no way out of the hollow. He was one of about 110 people on the west bank of the river stranded by flooding caused by Wednesday’s heavy rains.

“What can we do?” Caldwell said in a telephone call to a house across the river on the Neibert side, as cascades of water poured down the mountainside high above his mobile home.

Caldwell said the temporary bridge was built in the first place because access to the other side of the river was disrupted by construction of a new section of W.Va. 10 on the mountain high above. With the temporary bridge gone, construction crews were trying desperately to reopen the old road and provide residents with a way in and out.

“The workers are doing a great job,” Caldwell said, but he didn’t have high hopes a road would be cleared soon.

He said construction crews hoped to have an emergency route cleared by 2 p.m. but, by early Wednesday afternoon, were making little progress. “You can watch them go 200 feet and it will cave in behind them,” he said. “I’m thinking it’s going to keep sliding.”

Roger Bryant, director of the Logan County Office of Emergency Management, said Wednesday night that an emergency route had been cleared.

“Crews are going to continue working through the night and hope to have one lane open tomorrow,” he said.
