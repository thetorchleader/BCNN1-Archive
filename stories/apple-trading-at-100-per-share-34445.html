Apple Trading at $100 Per Share

August 19, 2014 Technology

AP

Apple (AAPL) stock finally pulled its head over the $100-a-share market for the first time on a split-adjusted basis. But savvy investors know this is a dangerous price.

Doing a bit of math explains why. Don’t be fooled by the $100 a share price. Remember that the stock was split 7-for-1 earlier this year. Backing out that stock split, the shares are trading for more than $700 a share.

Adjusting for the split, the last time the stock traded for more than $100 a share was just before a peak that turned into a vicious crash. Shares traded for $702.10 on Sept. 19, 2012 (on a non-split adjusted basis). Adjusted for the split, that means shares peaked last time at $100.30, which is about where the stock is now. Shares of Apple are up $1.21, or 1.2%, a share to $100.38.

The problem is, Apple shares collapsed after hitting $100 a share (split adjusted) before. The stock fell 44% from the peak through June 2013. Ouch! Perhaps that explains why Apple today set a record for the most trades in a second (5,094) according to Nanex.
