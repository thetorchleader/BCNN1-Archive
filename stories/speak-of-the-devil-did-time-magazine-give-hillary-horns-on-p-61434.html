Speak of the Devil! Did TIME Magazine Give Hillary Horns on Purpose?

March 12, 2015 Media

The latest issue of Time featuring former secretary of State Hillary Rodham Clinton on the cover has caught the notice of some on Twitter.

On close examination, the placement of Clinton’s silhouette under the ‘M’ in Time appears to give the likely presidential candidate horns.

Reading @jimgeraghty email re Hillary Clinton’s Time cover story…I can’t be only 1 to notice they gave her horns? pic.twitter.com/dnlvbZAkyd

— Sarah Rumpf (@rumpfshaker) March 12, 2015

@jimgeraghty re. Hillary Clinton’s @TIME cover story…are those horns we see? pic.twitter.com/mLoFovDGxj

— tradylady (@oledi45) March 12, 2015

Time‘s Zeke Miller noted that this isn’t the first time this has happened with the magazine — everyone from popes to presidents have gotten horns over the years.
