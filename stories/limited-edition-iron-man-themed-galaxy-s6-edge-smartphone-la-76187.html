Limited Edition Iron Man-Themed Galaxy S6 Edge Smartphone Launches in Asia

May 27, 2015 Technology

Avengers fans, assemble! Samsung today announced the limited-edition Iron Man Galaxy S6 Edge smartphone.

Samsung tweeted a photo of the upcoming handset’s box earlier this month. Now, more details have been revealed—like the red-and-gold metal shell and matching arc reactor-like wireless charger, 64GB of storage, and clear back cover.

But don’t go cracking open your piggy bank any time soon: The limited-edition superhero-themed smartphone, created in collaboration with Marvel, will only be available in Korea (starting tomorrow), as well as China and Hong Kong in June.

Prices were not announced, and vary by country and carrier.

“The limited-edition Iron Man Galaxy S6 Edge embodies the power of the iconic Avenger with a dual-curved edge design and advanced features that will provide Marvel fans with the ultimate entertainment experience,” Younghee Lee, executive vice president of Samsung’s mobile division, said in a statement.

Samsung and Marvel last summer announced a global brand partnership as part of the unveiling of the new Galaxy Tab S tablet lineup. But until now, all it has yielded is a few months’ access to the Marvel Unlimited mobile comics app.
