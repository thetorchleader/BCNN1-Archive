Cars Broken Into During Church Services In Reidsville, North Carolina

January 30, 2015 Black

Deputies with the Rockingham County Sheriff’s Office say they are looking for information about two cars that were broken into during church services Sunday.

Investigators say the break-ins happened in church parking lots during the 11 a.m. services on Sunday, January 25. One car was broken into at Evangelical Methodist Church located at 413 McCoy Road in Reidsville.

Another car was also broken into at Fairview Baptist Church located at 2531 Flat Rock Road in Reidsville. Police say the churches are within a short distance of each other and both cars broken into had a window busted out and a purse stolen. Church was in service at both locations when the break-ins happened.

Anyone with information is asked to call Reidsville Police at (336) 349-1011, the Rockingham County Sheriff’s Office at (336) 634-3232 or Rockingham County Crime Stoppers at (336) 349-9683. Crime Stoppers pays cash tips about crimes and all callers remain anonymous.

Source: WFMY
