Get Ready: September 21 Is National Back to Church Sunday

August 24, 2014 Christian

Sept. 21 is the sixth annual National Back to Church Sunday. Over 30,000 churches of all denominations are expected to participate across the nation.

National Back to Church Sunday (NBTCS) is the largest annual community-based church outreach effort in the nation. The campaign empowers churches and church members with the tools they need to welcome their neighbors, friends and loved ones back to church, while providing an easy way for everyone in the community to find a welcoming church.

In 2013, more than 21,000 churches in all 50 states (over 120 denominations) participated, according to NBTCS figures. In a survey of participants in 2013, 96 percent of responding churches said they’d participate again. Participating churches also reported an average 31 percent increase in their attendance on Back to Church Sunday. In addition, 84 percent of responding churches said that having a special day motivated their members to invite family and friends.
