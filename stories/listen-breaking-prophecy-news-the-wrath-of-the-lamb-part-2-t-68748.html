The Bible says in John 5:28-29: “Marvel not at this: for the hour is coming, in the which all that are in the graves shall hear his voice, And shall come forth; they that have done good, unto the resurrection of life; and they that have done evil, unto the resurrection of damnation.”

PROPHECY BOOTCAMP: Our topic for today is titled “The Wrath of the Lamb” (Part 2) from Dr. Ed Hindson’s book “Revelation: Unlocking the Future.”

The basic question we must ask regarding Revelation is: How does one read the succession of events predicted in the Apocalypse? Some view these prophecies as a strict succession of events: Seals, then Trumpets, then Bowls.

Others view them as happening somewhat simultaneously. Various schemes have been proposed, but the most common seems to be: The Seal judgments happen first, and then the Trumpet and Bowl judgments happen at the same time.

The trumpet judgments and the bowl judgments have several obvious similarities. Merrill Tenney has observed, “The seven bowls are a closely knit series following each other in rapid succession. They parallel the trumpets in their spheres of action, but they are more intense.” The similarities in the parallels are as follows: While there are some differences in the two accounts (wormwood, locusts, kings of the east, Armageddon), there are far more similarities.
