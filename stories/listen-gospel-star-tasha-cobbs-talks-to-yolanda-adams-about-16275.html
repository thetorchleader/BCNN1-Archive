LISTEN: Gospel Star Tasha Cobbs Talks to Yolanda Adams About How Losing her Father Helped her Spiritual Life

April 1, 2014 Uncategorized

Earlier this year, Tasha Cobbs won three Stellar Awards and a Grammy in the same week her father passed away. She talked to ”The Yolanda Adams Morning Show” live at the Inspiration & Music Conference about that life changing week. Listen to this exclusive interview to hear Tasha explain how losing her father was great for her spiritual life and her thoughts on achieving a career high at one of the most difficult times in her life.
