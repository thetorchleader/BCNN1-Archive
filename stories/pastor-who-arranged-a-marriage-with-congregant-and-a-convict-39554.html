Pastor Who Arranged a Marriage with Congregant and a Convicted Child Molester Is Accused of ‘Genital Exams’

October 14, 2014 Christian

Rev. Ernest Angley listens during a interview in his office on Sept. 3 in Cuyahoga Falls, Ohio.
(Photo: Phil Masturzo, Akron Beacon Journal.)

Former church members have accused an Ohio pastor of performing genital “examinations,” encouraging abortions and vasectomies, and even arranging a marriage between a congregant and a convicted child molester, according to The Akron Beacon Journal.

The week-long series by The Journal, which was the product of a two-month investigation, details decades of alleged abuse and coverups by televangelist Ernest Angley, 93, and his Grace Cathedral Church in Cuyahoga Falls, Ohio.

Reporter Bob Dyer interviewed 21 former church members “who insist that Angley has been running a cult, not a church,” Dyer writes in the series.

Dyer, who’s been with the paper for 30 years, told USA TODAY Network he had heard rumors about Angley for a long time. But it wasn’t until July when Dyer got a tip — a recording of a church service in which Angley admits to examining the private parts of males —that he began looking into the reports.

“Sure, I’d have them uncover themselves, but I did not handle them at all,” Angley said in the recording, according to The Journal.
