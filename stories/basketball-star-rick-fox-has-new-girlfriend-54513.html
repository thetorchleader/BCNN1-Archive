Basketball Star Rick Fox has New Girlfriend

January 31, 2015 Sports

AnnaLynne McCord and Rick Fox have reportedly put their love on display at a pre-Super Bowl event in Scottsdale, Ariz. Thursday night, Jan. 29. (Photo : Getty Images/Noel Vasquez)

AnnaLynne McCord and Rick Fox are reportedly dating!

The 27-year-old “90210”alum and the 45-year-old retired professional basketball player, who last played for the NBA’s Los Angeles Lakers in 2004, have reportedly put their love on display at a pre-Super Bowl event in Scottsdale, Ariz. Thursday night, Jan. 29.

Canadian-born Fox was at the Hennessy V.S Super Bowl lounge with a few friends when McCord reportedly came later in the night.

“As soon as she arrived they were together for the rest of the evening,” an eyewitness told People. “They were really cute, holding hands, and even while fans asked Rick to take pics he held her hand while he took pics with them.”

And it seems that their relationship is serious, as McCord was reportedly overheard later that evening referring to Fox as her “boyfriend.”

In the wee hours Friday morning, Fox – who also played for Boston Celtics from 1991 to 2004 – even posted an Instagram photo of himself with his new rumored girlfriend and NBA TV’s Lance Fresh.
