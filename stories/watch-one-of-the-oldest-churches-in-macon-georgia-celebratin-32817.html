WATCH: One of the Oldest Churches in Macon, Georgia Celebrating Its 151st Anniversary and Homecoming On Sunday

August 7, 2014 Christian

One of the oldest churches in Macon is celebrating its 151st anniversary this weekend and is having a homecoming as well. The theme is “No Place Like Home,” and the community is invited to the festivities. Thaddeus West from Bethel CME Church joined 41 Today with the information.

At this event will be card games and dance contests, plus Nintendo Wii games and a bounce house for kids. Hamburgers and hot dogs will be made on the grill. Families can get together to play Family Feud.
