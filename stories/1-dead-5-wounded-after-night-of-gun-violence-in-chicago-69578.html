1 Dead, 5 Wounded After Night of Gun Violence in Chicago

April 24, 2015 Black

Chicago police investigate around a damaged vehicle displaying a Chicago Water Management tag on the windshield in the 3000 block of South Komensky Avenue on April 23, 2015 after a man was shot twice. (José M. Osorio, Chicago Tribune)

Shootings since about midday Thursday have left a 16-year-old boy dead and at least five other people wounded on the city’s South and West sides, according to police.The most recent shooting, in the 7300 block of South Dante Avenue in the Grand Crossing neighborhood, left a 16-year-old boy dead. He was shot about 6 p.m. Police said he was found shot in the neck and chest and pronounced dead on the scene.

Family members identified the teen as Amari Sutton and said he was fatally shot while waiting on a ride from a mentor outside his grandmother’s home.

Most recently, a 26-year-old man was wounded in a driveby shooting in the Ausin neighborhood on the West Side.

The man was standing with a group in the 5700 block of West Ohio Avenue about 10:55 p.m. when someone inside a passing light-colored SUV fired shots, police said. The 26-year-old was shot in his abdomen. He was driven to West Suburban Medical Center and then later transported John H. Stroger Hospital where his condition stabilized.

Another man was shot in Austin hours earlier.

A 20-year-old man walked into West Suburban Medical Center about 6:20 p.m. after he had been shot in his side and stomach, police said.

The 20-year-old was involved in a large fight in the 1200 block of North Parkside Avenue when someone pulled out a gun and shot him, police said. A friend then drove him to West Suburban, where he was listed in serious condition. The man was later transferred to John H. Stroger Hospital, police said.

Earlier, a 40-year-old man working for the Department of Water Management was shot in the Little Village neighborhood Thursday morning, according to authorities.
