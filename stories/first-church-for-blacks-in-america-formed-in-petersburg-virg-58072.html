First Church for Blacks in America Formed in Petersburg, Virginia

February 22, 2015 Black

Formed before there even was a United States, one of the nation’s first Black churches is right here in Central Virginia. The First Baptist Church was formed in 1756 in Prince George County before moving to Petersburg in 1774.

No matter what city or state you visit you’ll probably find a church called First Baptist, but this one in Petersburg claims to be just that – the first for Black Baptists in the nation.

Behind the riveting gospel tunes heard every Sunday is a story of pride and purpose.

“People just flocked here for good preaching, good teaching, and community involvement,” said Betty Jackson.

Jackson recalls her church’s pivotal role in the Civil Rights Movement and the time Dr. Martin Luther King visited the congregation.

“I remember as a young girl my parents would not allow me to attend because they were afraid of the violence that may have happened but my father attended. He was not on the inside but he stood on the corner of Liberty and Harrison just to be a part of that movement, that opportunity,” she said.

A movement well after the church first formed as the first Black church in the nation. The early church’s minutes have been preserved through the years.

“September 25th, 1876….Prayers were offered by Brother Lewis Robinson,” Jackson reads from the collection.

“When I read this I see freedom. Freedom that we were able to write and to read and to govern our history,” she said.
