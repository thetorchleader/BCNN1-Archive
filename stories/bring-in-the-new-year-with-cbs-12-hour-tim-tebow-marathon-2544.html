Bring In the New Year with CBS’ 12-Hour Tim Tebow Marathon

December 30, 2011 Uncategorized

For Florida fans looking for plans for New Year’s Eve, CBS Sports Network has an idea for you – 12 hours of Tim Tebow to ring in 2012. The network will run a marathon of classic Florida games featuring the former Heisman Trophy winner.
In addition, CBS Sports Network is re-broadcasting parts of the top 20 games of the 2011 SEC season as part of “SEC Week Presented by Dr. Pepper.” The one-hour broadcasts started today at 4 p.m. and run until Saturday at 6 p.m. with a look at LSU’s overtime win over Alabama earlier this season.
Tim Tebow Programming
Florida at Kentucky (Oct. 20, 2007) – Airs Saturday, 9 p.m.
During his Heisman season, Tebow out-duels Kentucky quarterback Andre Woodson, throwing for four touchdowns and running for one as the Gators top the Wildcats, 45-37.
Florida vs. Alabama (SEC Championship Dec. 6, 2008) – Airs Sunday, Midnight
Tebow leads No. 2-ranked Florida over No. 1-ranked Alabama, 31-20, en route to the National Championship. Tebow throws for 216 yards and three touchdowns and rushes for 57 yards.
Georgia vs. Florida (Oct. 31, 2009) – Airs Sunday, 3 a.m.
The dual-threat quarterback throws for two touchdowns and runs for two touchdowns and 85 yards as Florida pounds Georgia, 45-17.
Arkansas at Florida (Oct. 17, 2009) – Airs Sunday, 6 a.m.
Tebow leads a game-winning drive in the final minutes as Florida downs Arkansas, 23-20.  He throws for 255 yards and one touchdown and rushes for 69 yards.
