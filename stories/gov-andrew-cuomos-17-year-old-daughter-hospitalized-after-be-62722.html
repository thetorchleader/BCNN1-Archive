Gov. Andrew Cuomo’s 17-Year-Old Daughter Hospitalized After Being Found Unconscious In Home

March 20, 2015 National

Michaela Kennedy Cuomo and Andrew Cuomo
SPENCER PLATT/GETTY

New York Governor Andrew Cuomo’s youngest daughter, Michaela Kennedy Cuomo, was reportedly hospitalized on Wednesday after being found unconscious in her Westchester home.

News 12 Westchester reports that Michaela, 17, was taken to Westchester Medical Center, where she was treated and released. At the time, she was at the Bedford, New York, home she shared with mother Kerry Kennedy, the daughter of the late Robert F. Kennedy.

“I can verify we responded to a residence on Chestnut Ridge Road on March 18 at approximately 4:46 p.m. and transported one female to Westchester Medical Center by Bedford Fire Volunteer Ambulance,” the Bedford Police Department tells PEOPLE.
