Mary Tyler Moore’s ‘LOU’, Ed Asner (85), Filed for Divorce After 8 Years of Separation

May 16, 2015 Entertainment

Ladies, Ed Asner is officially back on the market.

The 85-year-old filed for divorce from his wife Cindy, eight years after they first separated. TMZ has the report that Ed and Cindy, who first split in 2007, actually reached a financial settlement in 2009. But it’s taken until now for one of them to actually make the split formal with a divorce filing.

Asner and Cindy were wed in 1998. It’s long forgotten now, but their split in 2007 was highly contentious. Asner tried to cut off her spousal support, claiming that she wasn’t making enough of an effort to become independent and support herself.

It’s unclear why Asner decided to file for divorce now, years after the split was all about settled financially. Asner’s representatives gave TMZ no comment.

Ed Asner has a long and storied career in Hollywood, but he is perhaps best known for his role as Lou Grant on The Mary Tyler Moore Show. In 2009, he starred as the voice of Carl Fredricksen in Pixar’s Up.
