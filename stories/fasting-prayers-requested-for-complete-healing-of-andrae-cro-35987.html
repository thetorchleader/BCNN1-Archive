Fasting, Prayers Requested for Complete Healing of Andrae Crouch

August 28, 2014 Christian

Andrae’ Crouch

via Facebook:

Thanks for the many many prayers! We invite Pastor Andrae’s greater fan base to join in with the Church that Andrae Crouch Pastors, New Christ Memorial Church, and fast for the completion of his Healing. You can choose the type of fast that the Lord leads you to do. Below is what the church leadership and membership agreed to as a minimum:

Fasting every Tuesday night 12:00 midnight – Wednesday afternoon at 12:00 noon while praying for Pastor Andrae’s complete healing. We are praying the scriptures Isaiah 26:3; Psalm 107:20 and Isaiah 53:5. Any type of fast your health permits. Dry fast, water, juice only or social media.
