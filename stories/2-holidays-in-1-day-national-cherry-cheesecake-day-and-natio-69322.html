2 Holidays In 1 Day: National Cherry Cheesecake Day and National Picnic Day!

April 23, 2015 God & Food

Today we celebrate two great holidays: National Cherry Cheesecake Day and National Picnic Day! Take advantage of the spring weather for an outdoor get together. Grab the blankets, sandwiches and fruit… and don’t forget dessert! We’re thinking a Sara Lee® Original Cream Cherry Cheesecake is a good choice!

Try this Dessert: Cheesecake with Strawberries

Ingredients

1 package Sara Lee® Original Cream Cheesecake thawed
2 cups strawberries sliced

Directions

1 Remove cheesecake from pan according to package directions and set on a plate.

2 Top with strawberries (or berries of your choosing), slice and serve.
