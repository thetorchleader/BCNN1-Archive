TLC to Air Docuseries Based on Life of Family of Christian Ministers and Their Experience Starting a Church

Date not recorded in the original archive Christian

The Speegle Family: Lori Adgate, Seth Adgate, Cash Adgate, Janice Speegle, Legend Adgate, Abi Windham, Allen Speegle, Walker Windham, Rodrick Windham, Jill Windham. Not pictured is London Adgate, 4 months. The Speegle family will star in a TLC reality show airing this fall, which documents their efforts to start a church in Madison, Ala. (Courtesy of TLC)

On June 2, 2013, Allen and Janice Speegle held a family meeting with their two daughters and their families.

Allen and Janice had been pastoring a church near Orlando, Fla. for more than 20 years. They’d been mentoring their oldest daughter, Jill, and her husband Rod to eventually take their place at the church, which served a congregation of about 1,000 people.

“I knew my season of pastoring was nearly over,” said Allen, who also runs an online ministry called Safe Harbor International “Net” Work. “I said, ‘Do you guys – Rod and Jill – see yourself pastoring this church in a few years?’ And they said no, but they would like to move back to Alabama and start a church.”

Allen and Janice were Cullman County natives who had pastored in North Alabama before moving to Florida in 1993. Allen told his daughter and her husband he thought they ought to consider Madison, Ala., for their new church.

“Their eyes got big and they said, ‘That’s exactly where we’re talking about going,'” said Allen. “Four days later, a producer out of New York emailed Janice, wanting to talk to us about a reality TV show.”

And the rest, as they say, is history.

A family gets discovered

The Speegle family just wrapped up filming of the first season of their reality show, tentatively titled “The Speegle Life.” It’s a docuseries about the ups and downs of starting and growing a church. The series is set to air on TLC later this fall and is produced by Ryan Seacrest Productions.

The Speegles are an outgoing family of ministers that include Allen and Janice Speegle; their daughter Jill Windham and her husband Rod; the Windhams’ two young children; Allen and Janice’s daughter Lori Adgate and her husband Seth; and the Adgates’ three young children.

The Speegle family was originally “discovered” as contestants on the Family Feud television game show a few years ago. In one funny episode, they correctly name every place couples like to make love other than the bed.

“I gotta go to this church!” host Steve Harvey jokes in the segment.

“While we’re serious about what we do,” said Allen, “we’re very fun, very lighthearted. It’s really been interesting how this thing has progressed so fast.”

After being contacted by the television producer about a reality series, the Speegles vetted him and he shot a promotional “sizzle reel.” Four of the five major production companies that were contacted wanted the show, and the family eventually decided to go with Ryan Seacrest Productions. That company thought it would best fit TLC – home to popular reality series such as 19 Kids and Counting, The Little Couple and Sister Wives.

TLC ordered six one-hour episodes. “I don’t know anything about TV, but the producers told me, ‘You don’t understand how big a deal this is. Usually you’ll get one or two 30-minute pilots, but you guys are going straight to series.'”
