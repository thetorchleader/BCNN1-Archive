WATCH: First Look at Alfre Woodard as President Constance Payton In NBC’s ‘State of Affairs’

October 29, 2014 Entertainment

President Constance Payton

ESSENCE’s October cover girl Alfre Woodard returns to the small screen to star in the NBC political drama State of Affairs.

Woodard plays commander-in-chief in the drama being described as “Scandal meets The West Wing.” Former Grey’s Anatomy star Katherine Heigl leads as a CIA officer who works closely with Woodard’s President Constance Payton.

State of Affairs premieres Monday, November 17 at 10pm ET on NBC.
