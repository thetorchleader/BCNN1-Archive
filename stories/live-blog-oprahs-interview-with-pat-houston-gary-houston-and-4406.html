Live Blog: Oprah’s Interview With Pat Houston, Gary Houston and Bobbi Kristina

March 12, 2012 Uncategorized

Oprah Winfrey sat down with Whitney Houston’s longtime manager and sister-in-law, Patricia Houston; her older brother, Gary; and her daughter, Bobbi Kristina Brown, 19, in a 90-minute Oprah’s Next Chapter special on OWN on Sunday night. The family opened up for the first time since Houston’s Feb. 11 death. There were tears and emotional moments for the woman both Pat and Bobbi Kristina called “an angel.”

Here’s how it unfolded:
10:27 p.m. Gary is singing I Look To You. But he breaks down crying and has to stop. The show closes with a clip of Whitney singing the song.
I look to you
I look to you 
And when melodies are gone 
In you I hear a song, I look to you
10: 19 p.m. Oprah asks Gary what happened at the funeral regarding Bobby Brown. Is it true the family didn’t want him there?
“No, he was supposed to be there,” said Gary.
“He reached out to me a couple of days after the funeral,” said Pat. He was checking on Bobbi Kristina. Pat said Bobby and Bobbi “have a relationship.” But Pat wasn’t sure if Whitney encouraged Bobbi to have a relationship with Bobby. Gary says he “loved” Bobby, but he tried to stay out of his marriage to Whitney.
Oprah asks Gary about his best memories of Whitney and he says he misses singing with her.
Gary says their mother used to say, “She’s not going to be with us too long. She’s an angel. She’s a gift.”
Oprah asks if Gary felt he had closure with Whitney and he says yes. Did Pat? “Yeah, I did,” says Pat. “She trusted me with her life. And it means a great deal to me. I will miss her, but I know she’s with me.”
10:11 p.m. This segment opens with a clip of Whitney singing with her brother, Gary. Gary has now joined the conversation with Pat and Oprah. He starts by talking about how he was very much the older brother, looking out for Whitney.
As time went on, he says, “The stakes got higher.”
Oprah asks if he feared he would one day get a call, like he got on Feb. 11. “Not this soon,” says Gary. “I never thought it would end up like this, really.”
Gary says he always could tell Whitney the truth, and so would their mother, Cissy Houston.
And how is Cissy? “It’s rough for her,” says Gary.
Oprah says she invited Cissy to speak on the show, but Cissy said she’s not ready to talk right now. She did tell Oprah that she wants the world to know she’s not angry at the funeral home about the photo of Whitney in the casket that wound up on the cover of a tabloid.
