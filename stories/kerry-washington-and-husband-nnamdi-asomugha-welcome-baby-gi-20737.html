Kerry Washington and Husband, Nnamdi Asomugha, Welcome Baby Girl

May 2, 2014 Entertainment

Scandal actress Kerry Washington has given birth to her first child with husband Nnamdi Asomugha, Us Weekly confirms — get the details
Credit: Axelle/Bauer-Griffin/FilmMagic

Another gladiator has arrived! Kerry Washington secretly welcomed a baby girl on April 21 in L.A., TMZ reports. A source also tells Us Weekly, “Kerry recently had her baby.” It’s the first child for the Scandal actress and husband Nnamdi Asomugha. Washington’s rep does not comment on her client’s personal life, but the Emmy nominee’s due date was a few weeks ago.

Us exclusively broke news in late October that Washington, 36, and the former NFL player were expecting. Three months later, the ABC star finally opened up about her pregnancy as she showed off her growing baby bump at the 2014 Golden Globe Awards on Jan. 12.

“I feel like I have the best date of the night,” Washington, clad in a white Balenciaga gown, gushed on the red carpet at the Beverly Hilton Hotel. “My little accessory tonight.” Asomugha later joined his pregnant wife at an after party, which marked a rare public appearance for the pair.
