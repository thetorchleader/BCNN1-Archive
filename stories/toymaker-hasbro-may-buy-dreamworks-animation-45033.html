Toymaker Hasbro May Buy Dreamworks Animation

November 13, 2014 Business

DreamWorks Animation (DWA) stock soared more than 15% in morning trading on reports that toymaker Hasbro (HAS) is in talks to acquire the film studio.

The studio is known for such animated movie franchises as Shrek and Madagascar.

The New York Times, citing anonymous sources briefed on the talks, reported that Hasbro is in “advanced talks” with DreamWorks about a potential deal financed with a combination of cash and stock. A price has not been determined.

According to Deadline.com, “DreamWorks Animation is deeply engaged in two potentially game changing negotiations that could transform the company — including one with Hasbro to forge a family entertainment powerhouse to be called DreamWorks-Hasbro. The other talks are with Hearst to turn DWA’s AwesomenessTV into a joint venture.”
