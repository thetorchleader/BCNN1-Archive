Rev. Alyn E. Waller, Enon Tabernacle Baptist Church Pastor, Keynote Speaker at Philadelphia Tribune Black History Awards Luncheon

February 20, 2015 Black

The Rev. Alyn E. Waller, of Enon Tabernacle Baptist Church, keynote speaker at The Philadelphia Tribune Black History Awards Luncheon on Thursday.

The fifth annual Philadelphia Tribune’s Christopher J. Perry/Carter G. Woodson Black History Awards Luncheon served as both a notable learning experience and networking opportunity for more than 400 guests Thursday at the venerable Union League of Philadelphia.

Three living history makers where honored: Trudy Haynes, the first African-American female television news personality in the Delaware Valley and the first African-American weather reporter in the United States; Herb Douglas, the oldest living African-American Olympic medalist (1948 London Games) and Sydney Gibson King, owner of the legendary Sydney School of Dance where she trained well-known African-American dance professionals, such as Joan Myers Brown, Lola Falana and Arthur Hall.

“While we are not here to ignore the achievements of others, we think that it is important to acknowledge the many contributions African Americans have made to the greatness of this nation,” Robert W. Bogle, president and CEO of The Philadelphia Tribune said. “Bear in mind, it wasn’t done by one, it was done by many, and African Americans played an initial and important role in the greatness of this nation.”

The Philadelphia Tribune was established in 1884 by Christopher J. Perry (1854-1920), a pioneering Black businessman who championed racial equality. Dr. Carter Godwin Woodson (1875–1950) launched Negro History Week in 1926 as an initiative to bring national attention to the contributions of Black people throughout American history.

“These men documented the African American experience, detailing the broad strokes of history,” Bogle said. “This year marks the 130th anniversary of The Philadelphia Tribune. This newspaper has evolved from a one-page, one-man operation to a multi-media commodity that encompasses print, web, mobile and our newest venture, we hope, with your support, TribTV.”

With that announcement, the room erupted into applause, as the guests would do several times over the next 90 minutes.
