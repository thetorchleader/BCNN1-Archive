WATCH: North Carolina Abortionist Refers to the Children He Aborts as “Ugly Black Babies”

August 7, 2012 Uncategorized

One pro-lifer is appalled that a Charlotte, North Carolina abortionist refers to the children he aborts as “ugly black babies.”
Abortionist Ashutosh Ron Virmani was recently caught on camera telling a group of pro-lifers, “Let me see one of you adopt one of these ugly black babies.” Day Gardner of the National Black Pro-Life Union says that statement demonstrates that Planned Parenthood and other abortionists are targeting the black community.
“They think that they’re just … worthless, they’re unplanned, unwanted, and maybe even in this abortionist’s case, uglier than other children,” she comments. “I just think that it’s appalling. It’s despicable, and it turns my stomach. It makes me sick.”
Virmani is also heard in the video warning that if the children he aborts were allowed to live, they could grow up to be like James Holmes, the shooter at Colorado movie theater.
