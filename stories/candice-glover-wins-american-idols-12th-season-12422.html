Candice Glover Wins American Idol’s 12th Season

May 17, 2013 Uncategorized

Candice Glover is the queen of American Idol’s 12th season as the R&B performer became the first woman in six years to win the Fox singing competition.
The 23-year-old from St. Helena Island, S.C., won the viewer vote over country singer Kree Harrison, 22, in what was Idol’s second all-female finale and the first since Season 3.
Glover, who had auditioned twice before, in Seasons 9 and 11, said afterward, “I’m happy I came back, and that I was determined enough and it finally paid off.”
She said she was “speechless. … Part of me is so happy that it happened, and part of me is relieved that I don’t have to sing for votes and worry about making it to the next round, because that’s such a stressful process. But I know the work is only going to get bigger from here on out.”
Harrison said she felt as if “Candice and I, we were almost too calm, because we already feel like we’ve made it. I’m so proud of her, and so proud of myself.” And she said she told Glover, “‘If you win, you’d better sing your butt off,’ and she did. Singing when you’re crying is probably the hardest thing in the world to do, and she killed it.”
Glover, who performed her first single, I Am Beautiful, for the coronation, said: “I love it. I connect with it because of the words, and that’s part of the reason I was crying.”
When the two of them were awaiting the decision, Glover says, “the whole time we were up there, I was telling her I love her no matter what. I was rooting for Kree, and she was rooting for me, so we were just happy either way.”
