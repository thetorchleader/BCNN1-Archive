Arnold Schwarzenegger Addresses His Affair In Memoir

September 24, 2012 Uncategorized

Just when fans may have forgotten about his scandalous affair with his housekeeper, Arnold Schwarzenegger dredged the whole drama up again while promoting his new memoir, Total Recall, this week.


In an excerpt from the book obtained by ABC News, the action star and former governor of California says his ex-wife, Maria Shriver, confronted him last January about fathering a child with their housekeeper in 1996.
He confessed, but says the years of lying had already taken their toll. “Instead of doing the right thing, I’d just put the truth in a mental compartment and locked it up where I didn’t deal with it every day,” he writes.
Amazingly, Shriver extracted the truth from him just one day after he ended his term as governor. He writes that she set up a couples counseling session on Jan. 4, 2011, and that’s when the fireworks began.
