Adrian Peterson Got Married This Summer

August 5, 2014 Sports

Courtesy: Starkey Foundation
Vikings star Adrian Peterson showed up at the annual Starkey Hearing Foundation Gala on Sunday with an apparent wedding ring on his finger.

Turns out there’s apparently a Mrs. Adrian Peterson.

The star Vikings running back showed up at the annual Starkey Hearing Foundation Gala on Sunday with a ring on his finger and the former Ashley Brown on his arm. The woman’s Twitter profile now lists her name as Ashley Peterson (@MsAshPeterson), and she describes herself as “Wife. Mother. God 1st.”

The couple already had a 2-year-old son. Now, it would appear as if they both have wedding rings.

With Peterson choosing to keep his personal life personal when possible, it’s unknown how long the two have been married.
