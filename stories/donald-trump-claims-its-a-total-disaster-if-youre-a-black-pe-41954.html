Donald Trump Claims ‘It’s a Total Disaster’ if You’re a Black Person in Obama’s America

October 27, 2014 Black

Businessman Donald Trump noted the falling family income under President Obama during his weekly appearance on “Fox and Friends,” saying in particular that Obama had done “nothing” for African-Americans.

Mexico is taking our jobs and so many other countries are taking our jobs. Interestingly, family income is much lower, the family as a group, much lower now than it was when President Obama took office. People are having a much lower income right now than when he took office. I mean, that to me is a really terrible statistic and if you happen to be African-American, it’s a total disaster. So what has President Obama done for African-Americans? Nothing.

SOURCE: ALEX GRISWOLD
