Indyspotlight Celebrates Black Music Month

June 24, 2014 Entertainment

MELROSE PARK, PA (June 24, 2014) – In conjunction with Black Music Month in June, Indyspotlight is celebrating gospel music in the African Diaspora. Indyspotlight is a new website dedicated to promoting independent Christian artists, comedians, filmmakers, animators, etc. 

“I’ve have always wanted to be supportive of the Christian creative community but was not sure how to do it effectively. The Lord gave me the idea of having one place where many talented believers would have the opportunity to showcase their ministerial crafts,” says Noelle Sewell, Founder and CEO.
Ms. Sewell’s vision has helped many artists reach a platform of new listeners.  Indyspotlight is committed to promoting diversity in the global Christian creative community. Ms. Sewell is a member of Redeemed Christian Church of God New Song Chapel.
Celebrate Black Music month and support the arts and view some of the artists on the Indyspotlight website at www.indyspotlight.net.
