NY Jets’ Tim Tebow Staying Prepared, Even Though He Says He Is Not Expecting to Start Over Mark Sanchez Soon

October 6, 2012 Uncategorized

The most scrutinzed backup quarterback/H-back/slot receiver/personal punt protector in NFL history says he has said nothing to anyone about being unhappy with his role with Gang Green.
Tim Tebow toed the company line on Thursday, conveying the same team-first mantra that he has expressed since the moment he arrived from Denver in March: He’s willing to help out any way possible.
There have been recent rumblings that the most scrutinized backup quarterback/H-Back/slot wide receiver/personal punt protector in league history has grown frustrated with his limited role. That talk begs the question of why the struggling Jets have not used him more.
Tebow denied that he was concerned with his role – “I haven’t talked to anybody or said anything” – or that the team made promises they haven’t kept.
“They didn’t tell me specifically anything,” Tebow said.
GM Mike Tannenbaum maintained that owner Woody Johnson hasn’t put pressure on Rex Ryan or him to expand the popular Tebow’s role.
“Play time is always driven by Rex,” Tannenbaum said on ESPN radio on Thursday. “Rex is in charge of who plays.”
The calls for Tebow grew louder during Sunday’s 34-0 loss to San Francisco. As Mark Sanchez struggled, the fans at MetLife Stadium chanted Tebow’s name.
“Yeah, that’s something I heard, but I wasn’t paying attention to it or focusing on it,” Tebow said. “You worry about [what] you can control and what matters at the time not what the fans are saying… Focusing on what they’re chanting isn’t the right thing to be doing.”
After leading the Broncos to the playoffs last season, Tebow has been a nonfactor with Gang Green. The lefthander has played in 12% of the offensive snaps (31 of a possible 257) through the first four games. Tebow was in for only seven offensive plays on Sunday.
“Right now, if you said, ‘OK, hey, I need you to line up and play defensive tackle,’ Tim would be like, ‘No problem. Tell me where to line up and let’s go,’??” Ryan said. “That’s the type of mentality he has.”
Maybe so, but Tebow admitted on Thursday, “My dream since I was a boy was to play quarterback.”
