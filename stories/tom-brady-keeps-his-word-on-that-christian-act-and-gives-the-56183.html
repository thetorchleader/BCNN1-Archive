Tom Brady Keeps his Word on that ‘Christian Act’ and Gives the Truck he Won for Being Super Bowl MVP to Malcolm Butler

February 10, 2015 Sports

Super Bowl hero Malcolm Butler is getting his truck.

New England Patriots quarterback Tom Brady on Tuesday is handing over the keys to the red 2015 Chevy Colorado he was awarded for being named Super Bowl MVP to Butler, the undrafted rookie cornerback who made the game-clinching interception in the Patriots’ win against the Seattle Seahawks.

Brady had said on his Boston radio show last week that he wanted Butler to get the vehicle, they just had some logistics to work through. Butler’s agent, Derek Simpson, told USA TODAY Sports on Tuesday that those details had been worked out, and Butler will be the one paying taxes on the truck.

“Tom Brady was so gracious with the whole thing,” Simpson said.
