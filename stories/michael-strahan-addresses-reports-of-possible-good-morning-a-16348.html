Michael Strahan Addresses Reports of Possible ‘Good Morning America’ Job Offer

April 2, 2014 Media

Kelly Ripa and Michael Strahan (Photo: Charles Sykes, Invision/AP)

Michael Strahan addressed those reports that he’s heading to Good Morning America on Wednesday when his Live with Kelly & Michael partner, Kelly Ripa, asked him about the big news.

“Surprise!” she said.

“Surprise!” he said.

Strahan said he saw his “name in the paper” and “was surprised.”

So what happened?

“The big news (is) that I’m going to be on GMA now. But it’s somewhat surprising,” said Strahan. “We had a meeting and talked about the possibility of doing something there.” Then he said on Tuesday night he went to see a screening of the new Captain America movie, came out, looked at his phone and found out he was going to be onGood Morning America.

“What’s going on here?” he said, explaining, “We talked and we’ll see.”

So is he going to do it? Ripa asked.

“I don’t know,” said Strahan. But he quickly added, “This is what I do. This is me. This is my home. No matter what. I go nowhere. This is my love. You are my love. This is my job. So don’t worry, anybody, I’m not going anywhere.”
