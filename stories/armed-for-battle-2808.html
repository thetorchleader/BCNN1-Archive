Ready to Fight

November 21, 2011 Uncategorized

by Corey L. Brown
January 9, 2012
Click here to read previous articles by Corey L. Brown

“Tomorrow I will stand on top of the hill with the staff of
God in my hands” (Exodus 17:9).

It’s a good idea to take your history with you into battle. When
you’re facing an attack by the enemy, you have to remember some
events that occurred in the past. One way to ensure that you’ll
make it through your current situation is to recall what the Lord has
already done for you.

Moses recognized the importance of using his history in battle. When
the Israelites encountered an attack from the Amalekites, Moses took
the staff of God in his hands in order to come out victorious. This is
so important, because this was the same staff that was used to open up
the Red Sea and to obtain water from a rock. When the Israelites found
themselves in desperate situations the staff was used to address their
condition.

Your history, perhaps, is the most lethal weapon that you can use to
counter the enemy’s attack. You need to recognize that you have
some weapons of mass destruction at your disposal. You can use the many
things that God has done for you to defeat the enemy. Whenever the
enemy attacks you, you have to remind yourself of what you’ve
already overcome.

When you understand the context of the text it will make you shout. The
Israelites are on their way to the Promised Land. They are on their way
to their blessing. You need to understand that one of the reasons the
enemy attacks you is because he knows where you’re headed. If
you’re currently under attack you’re in a terrific place
because it only means that you’re headed for what God has for
your life.

For further study, please read Exodus 17:8-13.
