Solange Knowles Talks About Elevator Fight with Jay Z in Interview

Date not recorded in the original archive Entertainment

Solange Knowles Breaks Her Silence on Jay Z Elevator Fight (ABC News)

Solange Knowles has finally commented on that fight with Jay Z in May following the Met Gala.

In an interview with Lucky Magazine, she referred to the altercation with her brother-in-law as “that thing.”

“What’s important is that my family and I are all good,” she told the magazine. “What we had to say collectively was in the statement that we put out, and we all feel at peace with that.”

Beyonce’s sister, 28, added her parents instilled a true family bond within her and her megastar sibling.

“We’ve always held each other down no matter what,” she said.

Video of the fight broke a week after the May 5 incident allegedly took place, showing Solange kicking and punching her rapper brother-in-law.

Later that month, Jay Z, Solange and Beyone released a statement to the Associated Press, which read, “As a result of the public release of the elevator security footage from Monday, May 5th, there has been a great deal of speculation about what triggered the unfortunate incident. But the most important thing is that our family has worked through it. Jay and Solange each assume their share of responsibility for what has occurred. They both acknowledge their role in this private matter that has played out in the public. They both have apologized to each other and we have moved forward as a united family.”
