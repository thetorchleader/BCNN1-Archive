WATCH: ‘Hardball’ Host Chris Matthews Defends Having Tony Perkins of Family Research Council on His Show Against Homosexuals

April 9, 2012 Uncategorized

When approached at a recent book signing, an MSNBC program host wouldn’t allow homosexual activists to silence him or a frequent guest on his show.
The activists showed up in Framingham, Massachusetts, on March 24 as Hardball host Chris Matthews signed books. In a video obtained by Americans for Truth About Homosexuality (AFTAH), the activists from a group called Faithful America challenged Matthews for having Tony Perkins of the Family Research Council on his show. Matthews defended his guest:
Matthews: I watched them deal with Buchanan [referring to MSNBC’s recent firing of conservative commentator Pat Buchanan] … I don’t believe in fatwas. I don’t believe in silencing people. I don’t believe in it, okay? I want Rush Limbaugh on the air. I want him on every day. I want people to hear, I want everybody to hear all the arguments. It’s a free country. You want to silence people. You want to silence people.
Activist: We want a progressive … to counter him.
Matthews: What channel do you want Tony Perkins on?
Activist: I’d prefer that he’s not.
Matthews: See? You want to silence him.
Activist: It’d be great, if he is going to be on, it’d be nice to have a progressive on.
 
Matthews: He doesn’t pull that homophobic stuff on my show.
