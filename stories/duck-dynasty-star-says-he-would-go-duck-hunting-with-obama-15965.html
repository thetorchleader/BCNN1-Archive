“Duck Dynasty” Star says he Would Go Duck Hunting with Obama

March 29, 2014 National

Willie Robertson of the reality television show “Duck Dynasty” speak at the Walmart Stores, U.S. associates meeting in Fayetteville, Ark., June 5, 2013. (PHOTO: REUTERS/RICK WILKING)

Duck Dynasty star Willie Robertson dropped by Laura Ingraham‘s radio show Monday, and Ingraham asked him what Democratic politician he would like to do duck hunting with. Robertson’s answer: none other than President Obama himself.

He explained, “I’d like to talk and debate and find out what’s going on in his brain a little more.” Robertson said he likes listening to people he disagrees with and “see if you can move to maybe change their thinking in some ways.”

Robertson, who said he briefly spoke with Obama at this year’s State of the Union, told Ingraham, “I’d like to get him out there with some camouflage and a gun and we could sit out there and talk.”

It’s probably never going to happen, but if the president did go on Duck Dynasty, guaranteed, everyone’s brain would explode.
