Scott Walker and Jeb Bush Rise to the Top of the Heap

March 5, 2015 Politics

Former Florida governor Jeb Bush, left, and Wisconsin Gov. Scott Walker. (Getty Images)

It’s still very early, but a new poll shows Scott Walker and Jeb Bush leading among prospective Republican presidential candidates.

The Quinnipiac University Poll gives Walker, the governor of Wisconsin, 18%, and Bush, the former governor of Florida, is second at 16%.

New Jersey Gov. Chris Christie and former Arkansas governor Mike Huckabee are tied for third at 8%, said the Quinnipiac survey.

Further back: retired physician Ben Carson (7%),  Sens. Rand Paul of Kentucky and Ted Cruz of Texas (6% each), and Sen. Marco Rubio of Florida (5%).

“No other Republican contender tops 2%,” the poll said. “Another 17% are undecided.”

Another poll also brings encouragement to Walker, who again is neck-and-neck with Bush … in Florida.

A Gravis Insights robocall poll of registered Republicans shows Bush slightly ahead with 23%, to Walker’s 22%. Another 12% are undecided, while Rubio polled at 11%.
