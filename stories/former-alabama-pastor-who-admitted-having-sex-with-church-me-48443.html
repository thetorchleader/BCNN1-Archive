Former Alabama Pastor Who Admitted Having Sex With Church Members While HIV-positive Is Permanently Barred from Church

December 6, 2014 Christian

A supporter tries to hide Pastor Juan McFarland from cameras as he leaves the Montgomery County Courthouse during his October trial.
(Photo: Mickey Welsh / Advertiser)

A Montgomery Circuit Court judge on Friday permanently barred from his former church a pastor who admitted from the pulpit to having sex in the church with church members while HIV-positive.

Rev. Juan McFarland, the now-former pastor of Shiloh Missionary Baptist Church in Montgomery, isn’t allowed to return to the church, must return all Shiloh property and must report to several banks that he is no longer an authorized signer on church accounts, Judge Charles Price ruled.

While the deacon board and trustees of Shiloh, who filed the injunction against McFarland when the pastor refused to relinquish his position, were satisfied with the outcome, they were not celebrating the win.

“It’s not a joyous occasion for them by any stretch,” said attorney Kenneth Shinbaum, who represented the deacons and trustees. “They obviously wanted this outcome, but they hate that it came to this. They would’ve much preferred for him to bow out gracefully after admitting to everything and allow the church to deal with this privately.”

Attempts to reach McFarland or his attorney, Dwayne Brown, were unsuccessful Friday afternoon.

According to Price’s ruling, McFarland also admitted to church members that he used illegal drugs, misappropriated church funds and carried a loaded firearm inside the church.

However, McFarland argued that the church had provided him a lifetime contract to carry out the duties of pastor and he therefore could not be terminated, no matter his actions.
