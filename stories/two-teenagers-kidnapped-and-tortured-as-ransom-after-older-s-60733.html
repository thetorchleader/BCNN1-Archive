Two Teenagers Kidnapped and Tortured as Ransom After Older Sister Steals Money from her Drug Dealing Boyfriend

March 9, 2015 Black

Aaron Blackwell, 16; Emma Blackwell, 13
FOX 59 SCREENSHOT

Two Indianapolis teens were blindfolded, kidnapped and tortured after their older sister reportedly stole $120,000, some 4,000 OxyContin pills and a kilogram of cocaine from her alleged drug-dealing boyfriend.

According to the Indy Star, on March 2 around 2:30 a.m., three men with pistols kicked in the family’s door demanding the loot, valued at $250,000, that 27-year-old, exotic dancer Whitney Blackwell reportedly stole from the her boyfriend, John ‘Jay’ Thomas.

An affidavit obtained by MLive alleges that Thomas, 38, sent the men out to retrieve his stolen goods.

When Blackwell’s mother told the intruders that she didn’t know of any money or drugs they took Blackwell’s sister, 13-year-old Emma Blackwell and her brother Aaron, 16 as ransom.

According to the Indy Star, the teens were zip-tied, blindfolded and thrown in the back of two separate SUVs. The men reportedly stole the mother’s phone and then drove off with the children who were held captive and tortured for 24 hours before police rescued them.

Aaron, who was wearing only a bathrobe when he was kidnapped, told investigators that he was asked several questions that he didn’t know the answers to and each time he failed to give the men the answer they were looking for, they cut his fingers. Aaron added that the men called his older brother trying to find Whitney and threatened to cut off his fingers.
