Does Obama Favor Homosexuals?

March 20, 2012 Uncategorized

A California attorney says President Barack Obama’s nomination of The Golden State’s first openly “gay” federal judge just adds to the evidence that the chief executive favors homosexuals.
Los Angeles attorney Michael Fitzgerald has won confirmation as California’s first openly homosexual federal judge, and he will serve on the U.S. District Court in his county. Fitzgerald, a former federal prosecutor, joins a handful of homosexual judges nominated by the president, including Paul Oetken and Alison Nathan, to the federal bench. Judge Deborah Batts, a lesbian, of New York is the only other homosexual serving as a federal judge. She is an appointee of former President Bill Clinton.
Brad Dacus, president of the Sacramento-based Pacific Justice Institute, believes these judges’ professional positions have little to do with their qualifications.
