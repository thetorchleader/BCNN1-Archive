Tim Tebow and Camilla Belle Promise to Save Each Other for Marriage, Report Says

November 29, 2012 Uncategorized

Tim Tebow’s squeeze Camilla Belle might just be his perfect match since the Brazilian actress, like Tebow, is supposedly saving herself for marriage.
According to the National Enquirer, a source who knows the couple says the two have “been dating secretly since the summer” and “can’t hide their love any longer.”
“Tim is looking for a woman so he can have a proper engagement and church wedding, and he’s told friends that Camilla fits the bill,” the source added.
“Tim calls her ‘the woman of his dreams,” the source continued. “He’s anxious to make Camilla part of his close-knit family.”
Back in October Tebow and Belle were spotted together for the first time in Jacksonville, Fla. in a group outing with friends.
