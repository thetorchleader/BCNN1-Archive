Sprint and Boingo Team Up for Free Airport Wi-Fi

May 1, 2015 Technology

When you’re sitting around at the airport waiting to board your flight, what do you do to pass the time? Play around on your smartphone, right?

Playing games and checking social media can be an awesome distraction, but it can really cut into your smartphone data plan — especially if your fight is delayed for hours on end. Sprint wants to change that.

The carrier on Thursday announced a new partnership with Boingo to offer its customers free Wi-Fi in 35 major airports across the country. Sprint says the deal will make connecting to Wi-Fi at airports as “easy and safe” as cellular. Thanks to the new partnership, Sprint devices within proximity of a Boingo hotspot can automatically connect to the Wi-Fi network, so you won’t have to manually search for Wi-Fi and log in.
