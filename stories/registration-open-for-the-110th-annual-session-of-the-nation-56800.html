Registration Open for the 110th Annual Session of the National Baptist Congress of Christian Education

February 14, 2015 Black

Pre-registration is now open for the 110th Annual Session of the National Baptist Congress of Christian Education. With over 280 Classes, special events, preaching, banquets and more, this is one Congress Annual Session you won’t want to miss.  The city of Detroit will host fireworks to welcome us on Monday, June 22, 2015. Won’t you join us for Faith-building, Fellowship and Fireworks??
