Oldest Black Baptist Church In Jackson, Tennessee Celebrates Its 145th Anniversary

April 20, 2013 Uncategorized

First Baptist Church at 433 Dr. Martin Luther King Drive is commemorating 145 years of service to the community April 28. The oldest black Baptist church in Jackson plans to do so in a number of ways during the 10:45 a.m. worship service, including honoring four women at the church who have celebrated more than 100 birthdays.
Those four members are Mary Swink, 106; and Mattie Robertson Roberson, Lurline Brooks and Beatrice Owens, 100.
Dennis Blaylock, pastor of New St. Luke Baptist Church, will deliver the message at the 2 p.m. service. Music will be provided by the New St. Luke Mass Choir.
In 2012, First Baptist Church worshiped with First Baptist Church located on North Highland Avenue for the first time since its existence. First Baptist on Dr. Martin Luther King Drive originated from the First Baptist on North Highland in 1868.
Prior to the Civil War, First Baptist Church was one integrated congregation due to slavery. A few years after the conclusion of the war, at least 60 former slaves were recorded as members of First Baptist Church. In November 1867, the blacks decided to withdraw from the church most of them were baptized in to form their own church.
On April 22, 1868, land was purchased and the First Baptist Church began. The church has survived tornadoes, relocations, economic depressions and the civil rights movement.
