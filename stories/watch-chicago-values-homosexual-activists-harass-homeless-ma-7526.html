WATCH: Chicago Values? Homosexual Activists Harass Homeless Man Who Was Reading his Bible at Chick-Fil-A

August 6, 2012 Uncategorized

On Friday, a group of gay activists surrounded, harassed and “chalked” an elderly African-American homeless man who was reading his Bible outside a Chicago Chick-fil-A.

Pictured: Gay activist harasses African-American Christian at Chick-fil-A.
Legal Insurrection’s Anne Sorock attended the gay “kiss-in” protest at the Chicago restaurant and captured the incident on video.
“I asked many of the protesters whether they agreed with Alderman Moreno’s actions,” she wrote, adding that “the reactions were mixed. Some agreed — one woman told me she took a ‘European view’ of our Constitutional Rights — while others felt he was in the wrong despite agreeing with him in a general sense.”
Alderman Moreno has said he would block a second Chick-fil-A restaurant because he doesn’t agree with the religious views of Dan Cathy, president of the fast food chain.
Sorock observed a group of activists that “formed around an elderly African-American homeless man, who was reading his Bible while seated along a fence rail off to the side of the protest.”
She noted that while some in the group debated him as he read his bible, others mocked him.
Toward the end, someone scribbled in chalk, “He’s really gay deep down,” with an arrow pointing to where the man sat.
Last week, Chicago Mayor Rahm Emmanuel said the values of fast food chain “are not Chicago values.”
“They disrespect our fellow neighbors and residents,” he added.
But it seems that harassing and mocking an African-American Christian are Chicago values.
No doubt, if the roles had been reversed, the incident would be called proof of bigotry and racism.
