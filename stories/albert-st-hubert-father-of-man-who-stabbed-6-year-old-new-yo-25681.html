Albert St. Hubert, Father of Man Who Stabbed 6-Year-Old New York Boy, Speaks Out and says his Son Was ‘Sick in the Brain’

June 7, 2014 Black

JOSHUA PREZANT FOR NEW YORK DAILY NEWS
Albert St. Hubert, 69, father of Daniel St. Hubert, at his home in Fort Lauderdale, Fla., on Friday.

Albert St. Hubert tells the Daily News that his son was a good kid, but appeared to be ‘sick in the brain’ as he grew older. Daniel St. Hubert is charged with killing Prince Joshua (P.J.) Avitto, 6, with a steak knife and wounding his playmate in a Brooklyn elevator.

The father of the accused Brooklyn butcher knew his son was troubled — but had no clue he would grow into a violent psychopath.

“I didn’t want him to be an animal,” Albert St. Hubert said of his son, who was charged with fatally stabbing a 6-year-old Brooklyn boy. “He was a good kid when he was a child,” the dad told the Daily News on Friday. “When he came home from jail just a few days ago, I spoke with him, I see he is not okay. The mental is not good.”

Daniel St. Hubert, 27, is charged with killing Prince Joshua (P.J.) Avitto with a steak knife and injuring his playmate in the elevator of a Brooklyn housing project. The attacks came eight days after his May 23 release from prison, where he did five years for attempted murder and assault.

Told that his son was also a possible suspect in the killing of Tanaya Copeland, an 18-year-old Long Island University freshman, his father started sobbing.

“Why? Why? Why?” he asked. “Why did this happen?”

Albert St. Hubert, who is 69 and lives in Fort Lauderdale, said Daniel was 3 when he split up with his mother and moved from Brooklyn to Florida.

The anguished dad said he remembers teaching his boy table manners and “how to eat like a civilized person.”
