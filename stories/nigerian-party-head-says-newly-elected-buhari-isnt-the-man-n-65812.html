Nigerian Party Head says Newly-Elected Buhari isn’t the Man Nigeria Needs Right Now

April 7, 2015 Eye on Africa

Muhammadu Buhari

A chieftain of the Labour Party, Hon Chibuzom Aghalibe, has said that the president-elect, General Muhammadu Buhari, cannot take Nigeria to the Promised Land, as he cannot transform into the modern president the country deserves.

Aghalibe, who said this in a chat with newsmen in Port Harcourt, the Rivers State capital, said Buhari, who ran for the Presidency on the platform of the All Progressives Congress (APC), has not changed a bit from what he used to be when he took over as military head of state in 1983.

He said, “I have to be frank with you; I don’t see General Muhammadu Buhari taking Nigeria to the Promised Land. I don’t see that. I have watched him; I was in the secondary school when he came on board the first time and I have grown to see that Muhammadu Buhari has not changed. He is still the Muhammadu Buhari we knew in 1983. I am yet to see how he is going to transform into the modern president Nigeria deserves at this point of our development.”

Aghalibe, who is contesting the Mbaitoli Constituency seat in the Imo State House of Assembly on the platform of LP, stated that although majority of Nigerians expected President Goodluck Jonathan to complete his eight years in office, the outcome of the Presidential election, which saw the emergence of Buhari may have been the wish of all Nigerians.
