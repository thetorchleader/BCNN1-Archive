Great Tips for Cyber Monday

November 30, 2014 Lifestyle

Check out some tips for finding the best deals this Cyber Monday.
(Photo: Daniel Allan, Getty Images)

This Monday (Dec. 1) is the premier day for online shopping. Some advice:

Keep credit information safe. “Make sure your browser is up to date with the latest version because that will be the most secure,” says Robert Siciliano, McAfee online security expert. “Whenever you’re using your credit card online, in the (website) address bar, you want it to say ‘https’; that means it’s a secure page.”

Go big. There are so many retailers online that you never know who you’re dealing with, according to Siciliano. If you’re worried about legitimacy, stick to big names, such as Amazon, Best Buy and so on.
