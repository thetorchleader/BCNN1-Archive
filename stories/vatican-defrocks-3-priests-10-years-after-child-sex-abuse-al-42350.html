Vatican Defrocks 3 Priests 10 Years After Child Sex Abuse Allegations

October 29, 2014 National

The Vatican defrocked three Greater Cincinnati priests Tuesday for sexual misconduct involving kids. Here, Pope Francis delivers an address at the Vatican.
(Photo: AP)

The Vatican defrocked three Cincinnati-area priests because of sexual misconduct with children that occurred more than a decade ago.

The decision Tuesday permanently removes the men from the priesthood and is the final step in the Vatican’s slow, methodical process of evaluating abuse cases and handing out discipline.

All three priests — Ronald Cooper, Thomas Feldhaus and Thomas Kuhn — had been on administrative leave for years and had been barred from saying Mass and performing any priestly acts. The Vatican’s move to dismiss them “from the clerical state” means they can never again present themselves as priests.

“I hope that this resolution will bring some measure of closure and healing to anyone harmed by these priests,” Cincinnati Archbishop Dennis Schnurr said.

Schnurr said the decision Tuesday the last of the clergy-abuse cases from this area pending before the Vatican’s Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith. The cases came to light a decade ago after the clergy abuse scandal exploded nationwide.
