Christians Discuss Implications of Ferguson Outcome and How to Move Forward with #Evangelicals4Justice, #BlackLivesMatter Hashtags

November 25, 2014 Christian

The internet erupted with opinions, condemnations, and critique as the Ferguson grand jury decision to not indict Officer Darren Wilson for the slaying of Michael Brown was announced on yesterday evening. Much of the discussion on Twitter centered around hashtags, #Evangelicals4Justice and #BlackLivesMatter.

#Evangelicals4Justice was launched by “an eclectic network of evangelical thought leaders and activists dedicated to making broad cultural impact through prophetic witness to the whole church.” The ongoing tweet stream from this conversation are below.
