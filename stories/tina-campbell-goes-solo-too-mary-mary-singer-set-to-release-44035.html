Tina Campbell Goes Solo Too: Mary Mary Singer Set to Release New Music, Book

November 7, 2014 Entertainment

Tina Campbell

Fabulous red-haired gospel singer, Tina Campbell, 40, is going solo. The member of GRAMMY® winning sister-duo Mary Mary confirmed the news via Twitter.

After sparking speculation of an individual musical career launch in May after a Twitter chat, the WE tv reality star is set to unveil new music this month.

On November 22, during an event dubbed “An Evening with Tina Campbell,” the gifted vocalist and songwriter promises to share, for the first time, excerpts of her upcoming book in addition to cuts from the forthcoming solo album.

Tina, who has openly aired her marital struggles with her professional drummer husband Teddy, has put the infidelity drama behind her and is stepping into a new season.

“New solo music, new book, new me,” wrote Tina in a Nov. 2 tweet, while promoting the event to be held in Woodland Hills, California.
