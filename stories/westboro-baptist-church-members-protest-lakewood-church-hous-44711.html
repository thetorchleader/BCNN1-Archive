Westboro Baptist Church Members Protest Lakewood Church, Houston Mayor Annise Parker

November 11, 2014 Christian

Pastors from Christ Fellowship in North Carolina spouted their message. Westboro Baptist Church followers held their highly charged and offensive signs nearby, too.
(Photo: KHOU)

It was day two for Westboro Baptist Church members protesting in Houston.

They did so in front of Lakewood Church Sunday morning and in front of City Hall Monday morning.

Monday, they spoke out against Mayor Annise Parker’s equal rights ordinance and her now recently reversed decision to subpoena Houston pastors.

“We are gonna say our part. That is a filthy abomination to be a lesbian and to promote that type of activity,” said Marc Hill, a Westboro Baptist Church member. “Jesus Christ said you will be hated of all men for my namesake. So when they disagree with this message that tells us we are doing the right thing.”

Directly across the street there were counter protesters gathered too, preaching a more peaceful, hate-free message.
