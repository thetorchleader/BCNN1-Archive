Anthony Evans Receives Highest Billboard Debut With “Real Life/Real Worship” Album Release

April 18, 2014 Entertainment

Last week, pop and R&B artist Anthony Evans unveiled his latest studio album Real Life/Real Worship in which he recieved the highest Billboard debut of his career. Real Life/Real Worship debuted at No. 2 on Billboard’s Gospel Albums, No. 4 on the Christian Albums and No. 5 on Top Christian/Gospel Albums Chart. Additionally, Evans’ reached No. 2 on iTunes ® Christian & Gospel Albums Chart.

Distributed by Fair Trade Services, the pop/worship album delivered to critical acclaim from the likes of NewReleaseTuesday, CCM and many others. To coincide with the release of the project, the former Voice contestant has been opening up about his faith journey and real life experiences that inspired Evans’ seventh studio album with various media platforms including FaithVillage.com, Breathecast.com, Beliefnet, CCM and most recently appeared and performed on Daystar’s Marcus & Joni.

With upcoming appearances on Life Today with James Robison and numerous concerts in the pipeline, Evans is also working with celebrated director Rock Jacobs as they prepare to unveil a brand new music video for Evans’ new inspirational single “Never Fail.” The contagious worship anthem, which is currently impacting radio across the nation, reminds listeners that God will do in His children what He has promised, in spite of their unfaithfulness.

For more information on Anthony Evans and Real Life/Real Worship, please go to www.anthony-evans.com.
