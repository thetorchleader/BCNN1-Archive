Is Jesus Using the Political Changes from the Arab Spring to Awaken the Muslim World?

October 13, 2012 Uncategorized
While many are concerned about the political changes sweeping the Islamic world, some Christians are seeing another kind of revolution shaking the region.
Tom Doyle, vice-president of e3 Partners ministry, has spent years working in the Middle East. He’s just written a book called, Dreams and Visions: Is Jesus Awakening the Muslim World?
