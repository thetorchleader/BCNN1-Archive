Oprah Winfrey Attends Church at The Potter’s House Online

June 2, 2015 Christian

Credit: OWN

Ain’t nothing like a Bishop T.D. Jakes sermon. In fact, the one the mega pastor preached Sunday, just about sent media mogul Oprah Winfrey into a Holy Ghost fit.

The message, “Faith Is A Perspective,” struck a chord with the 61-year-old, who shared its impact on Twitter.

“So worth the visit,” said Winfrey of her e-church experience at The Potter’s House of Dallas, adding, “Having church in my screening room right now.”

Though the OWN CEO felt the joy of the Lord and had a mighty good time at home, she pointed out, “But nothing like being in The Potters House.”
