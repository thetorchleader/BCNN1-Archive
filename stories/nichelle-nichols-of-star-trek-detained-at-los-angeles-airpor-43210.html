Nichelle Nichols of Star Trek Detained at Los Angeles Airport After Drugs Found

November 3, 2014 Entertainment

Nichelle Nichols, who played Lt. Uhura in the Star Trek series in the 1960s, appears at the Sci-Fi and Fantasy Creators Convention June 27, 2003 in New York City.
CHRIS HONDROS/GETTY IMAGES

The male companion of the 81-year-old actress, who played Lt. Uhura on the TV series, was arrested for drug possession, while she was allowed to board her flight.A “cloaking device” would have come in handy last week for a traveling companion of Star Trek’s Nichelle Nichols, who played Lt. Uhura on the popular, classic TV series.

The 81-year-old actress was stopped at the Los Angeles airport Friday after her 20-something male companion was discovered carrying drugs, the Daily News reports, citing reports at TMZ.

Investigators told TMZ that Nichols and her male companion were at the American Airlines terminal when the man’s suitcase fell open, revealing a bag of meth. See, a Star Trek “cloaking device” would have allowed him to escape the bad situation.

Nichols reportedly told police she had no knowledge of the drugs and was allowed to board her flight. Her companion, however, was arrested for drug possession. The star’s rep told TMZ that the man was her assistant, and that it was an e-cigarette, not meth, that was discovered in his luggage.
