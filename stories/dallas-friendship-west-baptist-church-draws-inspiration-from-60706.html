Dallas’ Friendship West Baptist Church Draws Inspiration from History on the 50th Anniversary of “Bloody Sunday”

March 9, 2015 Christian

Friendship-West Baptist Church / Facebook

Sunday morning service at Friendship West Baptist Church took on a special message this week; drawing inspiration from history on the 50th anniversary of “Bloody Sunday.”

On March 7, 1965, what was supposed to be a peaceful march for voter rights and racial equality turned violent as marchers were turned away and beaten back by Alabama state police at the Edmund Pettus Bridge in Selma.

Pastor Frederick Haynes told the congregation to remember the sacrifice of those protesters but also look to them as inspiration to continue the fight for civil equality in America.

“It says, ‘we remember that bridge because we still have some more bridges to cross,’” said Haynes.

“Make sure that liberty and justice is for all and not just a few.”
