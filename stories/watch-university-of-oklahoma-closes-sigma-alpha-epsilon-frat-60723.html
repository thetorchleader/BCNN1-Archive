WATCH: University of Oklahoma Closes Sigma Alpha Epsilon Fraternity Chapter After Video of Members Singing Racist Chant

March 9, 2015 Black, Education

Students at the University of Oklahoma have planned a protest against Sigma Alpha Epsilon.

The demonstration is expected to take place at 7:30 Monday morning and was planned after a video surfaced on Sunday reportedly showing members of the fraternity singing a racist chant.

Sunday night, the national SAE organization closed the chapter, effective immediately.

“In addition, all of the members have been suspended, and those members who are responsible for the incident may have their membership privileges revoked permanently,” the national organization said in a statement. “We apologize for the unacceptable and racist behavior of the individuals in the video, and we are disgusted that any member would act in such a way. Furthermore, we are embarrassed by this video and offer our empathy not only to anyone outside the organization who is offended but also to our brothers who come from a wide range of backgrounds, cultures and ethnicities.”

OU President David Boren said the university was investigating the video.

“I have just been informed of the video, which purports to show students engaging in a racist chant. We are investigating to determine if the video involved OU students. If OU students are involved, this behavior will not be tolerated and will be addressed very quickly. This behavior is contrary to all of our values,” Boren said in a statement.
