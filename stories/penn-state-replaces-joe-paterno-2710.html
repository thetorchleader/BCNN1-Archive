Penn State Replaces Joe Paterno

January 7, 2012 Uncategorized

The Penn State assistant coaches walked in and out of the front doors of the Lasch Football Building hundreds of thousands of times before.

Beyond those doors are offices, meeting rooms,
trophies and, in recent months, a sanctuary away from the university’s
child sexual abuse scandal. In one corner of the building, there used to
be Joe Paterno‘s
office lined with pictures of his family, awards and a big, cozy desk
chair. But now, many of the staff members are taking those belongings,
all the memories, packing them up and boxing them away.

Penn State officially announced the hiring of New England Patriots offensive coordinator Bill O’Brien late Friday night, nearly 20 hours after ESPN
first reported he’d become Paterno’s permanent successor. O’Brien is
scheduled to meet with the media Saturday at 11:30 a.m. ET on campus at
the Nittany Lion Inn.

The
hiring came with much disapproval from fans, alumni and former
letterman who felt the university didn’t act quickly enough to name a
successor. Former interim head coach Tom Bradley remains a staff member for the time being, though the ultimate decision for all the assistants will be made by O’Brien.
