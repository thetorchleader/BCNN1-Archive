LISTEN: Tavis Smiley, C. NaTasha Richburg, and Gary McCants on BC Book Review Show #256

January 18, 2015 Books

The authors that we are featuring today are: Tavis Smiley, author of “Death of a King: The Real Story of Dr. Martin Luther King Jr.’s Final Year”; C. NaTasha Richburg, author of “Outside the Inside Circle”; and Gary McCants, author of “Hope Always”.

The purpose of this show is to highlight wholesome Christian books, promote them to our audience, encourage you to buy these books for yourself and for others, and most importantly, to see people come to Christ and lives changed for the glory of God.

MUSICAL SELECTION: “I Am Free” by Newsboys, “You’re an Awesome God” by the Chicago Mass Choir, and “Surrender” by Flame (ft. V Rose)
