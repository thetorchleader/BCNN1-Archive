Church in Trappe, Maryland, Show Appreciation for Law Enforcement Officials

February 23, 2015 Black

Snow and icy conditions did not stop a group of Scotts United Methodist Church’s parishioners from gathering and giving thanks to the law enforcement agencies of Talbot County, Sunday, Feb. 22.

“We knew the law enforcement would be out in the snow and ice and cold weather, so we wanted to be here for them,” Diane Thomas, chairman of the outreach ministry committee at Scotts, said.

Although the church’s official service was canceled due to icy roads caused by sleet Saturday night, some from the church went on with the event.

After a brief description of each agency in Talbot County, representatives from Easton Police Department, St. Michaels Police Department, Oxford Police Department and Trappe Police Department were awarded certificates of appreciation from the church.

Representatives from Maryland State Police Easton barracks were not able to attend.

“We want to let you know that we appreciate you and want to give you some encouragement. Everyone needs encouragement sometimes,” Thomas said as she began the service.

Rev. Lucille Nichols said that each year the outreach committee puts together a service that highlights a certain group in the community, from small businesses to teachers to senior citizens, and then gives them thanks.
