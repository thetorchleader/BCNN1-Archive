Husband of Former Christian Minister, Porsha Williams, Says “Real Housewives of Atlanta” Star Allowed Fame to Lure Her Away from God

May 2, 2014 Christian

Porsha Williams

Lights, camera, action: they can be intoxicating and it appears the Real Housewives of Atlanta star Porsha Williams, former Christian minister preaching the gospel, has gotten drunk with the toxic trio.

Somewhere along the way, the allure fame, notoriety and materialism lured her away from God, leading to a life out of control—something her ex-husband Kordell Stewart noted.

In response to Williams’ claims of physical abuse against her NFL star ex during a recent Access Hollywood interview, he spoke out in his own defense to The Atlanta Journal Constitution, noting that she has, in fact, lost her way.

“I married my wife because she was a Godly woman who had accepted her calling as a minister. The bright lights made Porsha lose her way. I no longer know the person she has become,” Stewart said.

Though in his full AJC statement he did not directly deny the abuse claim, in a different statement to Access Hollywood, Stewart definitively refuted it.

“It’s not true. I’ve never abused her. Never put my hands on her. Never,” he said—words read on air by the hosts.

“I hope and pray she can turn her life around soon. I loved my wife, supported her and invested in her success,” he told AJC.
