Family, Friends Mourn Tragic Death of Mother, Boyfriend, and Children Killed in Georgia Murder-Suicide

February 9, 2015 Black

LaToya Andrews, her ex-husband Cedric Prather and their children.

On Sunday, police identified Cedric G. Prather, 33, as the one who pulled the trigger in a mass shooting in Douglas County Saturday. He died of a self-inflicted gunshot wound. The other adult victims were identified as Latoya A. Andrews, 33, and Joseph Terry Brown also 33.

“I just talked to her every day and it felt like she was telling me goodbye without actually telling me bye,” said Andrews’ best friend Terri Adams. “It’s shocking because there was a time when Cedric truly loved his kids. He loved his wife.”

She showed us pictures of the couple happily married with their children. Two of them, 7-year-old London and 9-year-old Jeremiah died with their mother in the shooting.

“I didn’t think it would be her because she was so strong and if anything she would’ve fought,” said Adams.
