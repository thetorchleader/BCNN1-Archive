National Baptist Convention USA President Jerry Young Honored by the Mississippi Senate

February 14, 2015 Black

The Reverend Dr. Jerry Young, Pastor of New Hope Baptist Church in Jackson, (7th from left), the first Mississippi pastor chosen as President of the National Baptist Convention USA, was honored by the Mississippi Senate on Tuesday, February 3, 2105, with presentation of Senate Concurrent Resolution 529 that commemorated the historic occasion.  Standing with Young is his wife Helen.Standing from left to right are: Senator Bill Stone, Senator Hillman T. Frazier, Senator Albert Butler, Senator Kelvin E. Butler, Senator Sollie B. Norwood, Young’s granddaughter Linzee Nelson, Senator John Horhn, primary author of the resolution, Young’s daughter Dr. Jerlen Young Nelson, (Linzee’s mother), Senator Robert L. Jackson, Senator Kenneth Wayne Jones, Senator Willie Simmons and Senator David Blount.

Source: Arnold Lindsay, Press Secretary, Mississippi Senate
