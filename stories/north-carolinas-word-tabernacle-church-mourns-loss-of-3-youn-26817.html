North Carolina’s Word Tabernacle Church Mourns Loss of 3 Young Members – Including Pastor’s Oldest Son

June 16, 2014 Christian

Pastor James Gailliard

Members of a Rocky Mount church are in mourning this week after three of their members – including the pastor’s oldest son – were killed in a fiery car crash early Friday.

Among the dead was 27-year-old Kyol Gailliard, the son of Word Tabernacle Church Senior Pastor James Gailliard. Also killed were Dennis Battle and Jason Britt, both listed as being in their 20s.

Battle and Britt were keyboard players at the church. Gailliard operated the church’s sound system. According to news reports, their car, driven by Gailliard, skidded on a wet street, stuck a brick building and burst into flames.

On Sunday, less than 53 hours after the crash, Pastor Gailliard took to the pulpit, as he does every Sunday, to preach all four worship services.

“I serve a lot of people here, and they’re going to lose their jobs, have children to die and have spouses to leave – they’re going to go through a lot of stuff in life – and when that happens, they just have to be faithful, so I think it was important for me to model that behavior,“ he told television station WRAL.
