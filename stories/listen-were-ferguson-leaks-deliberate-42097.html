LISTEN: Were Ferguson Leaks Deliberate?

October 28, 2014 Black

The official autopsy of Michael Brown was leaked earlier this week sparking more protests in Ferguson and caused the Justice Department to condemn the release of the information. According to reports, Attorney General Eric Holder is “exasperated” over the “selective leaks” in the Brown case. Brown family attorney Benjamin Crump believes the leaks might be a deliberate move by prosecutor Bob McCulloch.
