Bobbi Kristina’s Family Are Reportedly Arguing About Funeral Arrangements; Hospital Room on Lock Down

February 8, 2015 Entertainment

In this Aug. 16, 2012, file photo, Bobbi Kristina Brown attends the Los Angeles premiere of “Sparkle” at Grauman’s Chinese Theatre in Los Angeles. (PHOTO CREDIT: Jordan Strauss/Invision/AP)

Bobbi Kristina Brown has been in a medically-induced coma since being found unconscious in a bath tub last week. Now, her family is arguing over funeral arrangements, according to TMZ.

The Brown side of the family tells TMZ that they do not want to feel the same way they did when Bobbi’s mother, Whitney Houston, died years ago. Brown was reportedly told from Houston’s family that he could not sit with his own daughter at Whitney’s funeral.

Sources say that the hospital room Bobbi Kristina has been staying in has been on lock down and several members from the Brown family were not allowed access to see her.

Bobbi Kristina has been in a coma for a few days after coming to the hospital and her family has come to visit as police continue to investigate what caused Bobbi to end up unconscious in her own home.

Source: Fox 8
