New Jersey Man, Jaysen Williams, Who Has Been Arrested 40 Times, Gets Arrested Again for Stealing Suitcases

May 30, 2014 Black

PORT AUTHORITY POLICE
Jaysen P. Williams, 38, was arrested Wednesday for allegedly stealing luggage at Newark Liberty International Airport. He has 40 prior arrests.

Jaysen Williams, 38, allegedly snatched two bags from the Terminal C luggage carousel at Newark Liberty International Airport on Wednesday. It’s his second arrest in three months on charges of stealing luggage, police said.

A New Jersey man with more than 40 arrests to his name was collared again Wednesday as he tried to make off with two suitcases he allegedly stole from a luggage carousel at Newark Liberty International Airport.

Plainclothes officers watched as Jaysen P. Williams, 38, snatched two bags from Terminal C around 8:30 p.m. before he tried to board a bus outside, the Newark Star-Ledger reported.

Among the sticky-fingered Newark man’s 40 arrests is his most recent, from February, when he was also nabbed for stealing suitcases, Port Authority spokesman Joe Pentangelo told the newspaper.

Court records show he was convicted in 2008 of theft by unlawful taking in an unrelated incident.
