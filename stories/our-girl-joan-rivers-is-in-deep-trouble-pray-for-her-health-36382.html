Our Girl, Joan Rivers, Is In Deep Trouble – Pray for her Health and her Salvation

August 31, 2014 Entertainment

© WENN / Joan Rivers

Joan Rivers has been put on life support, TMZ reports. 

According to sources, the comedian’s family maintains faith in Rivers’ ability to bounce back even if they decide to turn off the machines. They still have a few days to make a decision whether or not they will take Rivers off life support.

Rivers was rushed to the hospital on Thursday after she stopped breathing while undergoing a procedure on her vocal chords. She was initially in critical condition, but was upgraded to stable later that day. On Friday, her daughter Melissa released a statement saying Rivers was “resting comfortably and is with our family.”
