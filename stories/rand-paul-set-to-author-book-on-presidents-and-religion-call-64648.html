Rand Paul Set to Author Book on Presidents and Religion Called “Our Presidents & Their Prayers”

March 31, 2015 Books

There’s multi-tasking, then there’s Rand Paul-style multi-tasking.

Apparently intent on running for both the presidency and his Senate seat in 2016, Kentucky’s junior senator is going to produce not one, but two books this year. That’s according to the website of his publisher, Center Street Books.

We already knew Paul was slated to pen Taking a Stand: Moving Beyond Partisan Politics to Unite America, due in bookstores on May 26. And we now can show you two versions of the book’s cover. One looks more GQ-presidential than the other, but what do you think?

And the second book? Well, this was a surprise add-on to what promises to be a very busy year for Paul.

Center Street’s site says the senator will author Our Presidents & Their Prayers: Proclamations of Faith by America’s Leaders. That book is due out on Sept. 8.

In the 144-page book, “Rand Paul reveals the practices of each President of the United States and sheds light on how religion played a part in their governing and personal lives,” the publisher’s description says.

“From George Washington to Abraham Lincoln to Barack Obama, and every President in between, prayer and religion made its way into their public speeches, private journals and official proclamations,” the blurb goes on. “With excepts taken from speeches, journals and other public records, this book will feature prayers from every President the U.S. has ever had. Illustrated with photos and paintings, the book will also give background histories of each President and reveal their personal feelings on religion, prayer, and our Creator.”
