WATCH: Man Who Shot Video of Eric Garner Calls Grand Jury Decision a ‘Slap in the Face’

December 5, 2014 Black

Eric Garner

A New York woman who in July filmed a black man being put into an apparent “chokehold” by a police officer calls the decision not to prosecute a “slap in the face”.

The man, Eric Garner, died but a grand jury decided on Wednesday that there would be no charges.

A neighbour of Mr Garner said: “Nothing’s going to happen, it’ll just be the same things”.
