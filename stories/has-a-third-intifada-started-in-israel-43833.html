Has a Third Intifada Started In Israel?

November 6, 2014 Second Coming Watch

Jerusalem has been beset with protests over the past few weeks [EPA]

U.S.-Israel relations have reached a new low under the Obama administration.

Its pressured Israel to negotiate with the Palestinians based on the pre-1967 borders.

During the recent war between Israel and Hamas in Gaza, the administration gave the terrorist group legitimacy by negotiating directly with its leaders.

U.S. officials have even antagonized Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu by referring to him in derogatory terms in the press.

While there is hope the recent GOP takeover of the Senate will strengthen Israel’s position, others are concerned the administration will continue its policies and even weaken Israel by speeding up its negotiations with Iran over its nuclear program.
