Can Finding Happiness Change the Way We Look?

October 23, 2014 Lifestyle

Renee Zellweger arrives at ‘Elle’s Women In Hollywood event.
(Photo: Frazer Harrison, Getty Images)

Renee Zellweger, who has been at the center of a swirling pop culture debate over her new look, is chalking it up to “a different, happy, more fulfilling life.”

Social media has been flooded with celebwatchers and fans pondering what exactly she did to herself, while others have been shaming everyone else for unseemly gawking.

Tweeted singer Iggy Azalea, 24: “It’s sort of awful to see everyone have such horrible thing to say about Renee Zellweger. I don’t think she really needs our opinion, world.”

But that’s the way it is in our celeb world. And she’s not the first star to come under fire, as she joins a list that includes Mickey Rourke, Jennifer Grey, Lara Flynn Boyle, Melanie Griffith and Meg Ryan.

The conversation reached such a crescendo that Zellweger, 45, issued a response early Tuesday morning. While calling all the chatter “silly,” she also offered an explanation.
