New Report says Bobbi Kristina Brown’s Organs Are Shutting Down

February 14, 2015 Entertainment

REUTERS/Fred Prouser
Bobbi Kristina Brown, daughter of the late singer Whitney Houston, poses at the premiere of “Sparkle” in Hollywood, California August 16, 2012.

Bobbi Kristina Brown’s condition has reportedly taken a serious turn for the worse.

The 21-year-old, who has been hospitalized since she was discovered unresponsive in her bathtub at her Georgia home two weeks ago, is experiencing organ failure and is dying, TMZ reports.

According to family sources, because several of Bobbi Kristina’s organs are shutting down, it does not matter if her father Bobby Brown withdraws life support.

At a candlelight vigil on Monday, Bobbi Kristina’s aunt Tina Brown said that her niece’s condition was“progressing” and that she was “getting better.” Another aunt, Keolah Brown, told Fox 5 Atlanta that the young woman was “moving her eyes” and that her doctors said “she is improving.”

While she was said to have experienced an “improvement in brain function” at one point, TMZ reports that her condition has since declined.

Due to his religious views, Brown has reportedly maintained that he will “do what it takes” to keep his daughter alive, even if that means if she remains on life support with little possibility of recovery. While some family members do not agree with his stance, they will respect his decision, according to the site.
