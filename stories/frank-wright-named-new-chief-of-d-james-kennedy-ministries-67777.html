Frank Wright Named New Chief of D. James Kennedy Ministries

April 16, 2015 Christian

D. James Kennedy

Dr. Frank Wright, the former head of the National Religious Broadcasters and Salem Communications, will take the helm of the global ministry founded by his long-time friend, the late D. James Kennedy.

The Ft. Lauderdale, Florida-based ministry, formerly known as Coral Ridge Ministries, produces a weekly nationally syndicated TV and radio broadcast, called “Truth That Transforms” and “Kennedy Classics.”

Wright previously served as president at Salem Communications, a major Christian radio broadcaster, internet content provider and media publisher.

He also spent 10 years leading the National Religious Broadcasters.
