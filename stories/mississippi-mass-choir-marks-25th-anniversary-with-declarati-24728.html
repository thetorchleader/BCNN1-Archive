Mississippi Mass Choir Marks 25th Anniversary With “Declaration of Dependence” Album

May 31, 2014 Entertainment

The Mississippi Mass Choir is arguably one of the most influential choirs of our generation. For years this choir with over 250 voices has topped Billboard charts, receiving numerous recognitions and awards while performing worldwide. This year marks its 25th anniversary, and the group will celebrate with the release of a new album entitled “Declaration of Dependence.”

The project was produced by Mississippi natives Stan Jones, Jerry Smith, David R. Curry Jr. and Jerry Mannery. “God’s On Your Side” featuring the powerful vocals of Le’Andria Johnson, is the first single to release off the highly anticipated album. The song is something both old and new fans can appreciate.

“Declaration of Dependence” will be released June 3rd, but click here to listen to the album now!
