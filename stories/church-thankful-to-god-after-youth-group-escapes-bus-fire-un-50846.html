Church Thankful to God After Youth Group Escapes Bus Fire Uninjured

January 3, 2015 Christian

A church bus carrying about 30 children was evacuated after a fire on Friday in Green Bay.
(Photo: Courtesy of Jake Whalen)

About 30 youths headed to a religious conference in Green Bay got a scare but are safe after the church bus they were on caught fire.

No injuries were reported during the Friday incident on the city’s west side. The bus was from the Immanuel Baptist Church in Wausau, and its passengers were members of the church’s youth group.

The bus is a total loss, and many passengers lost their luggage in the fire, according to the youth group’s Facebook page.

Authorities responded to the fire just before 5 p.m. and cleared the scene around 6 p.m., according to the Green Bay Metro Fire Department.

Jake Whalen, a Wausau West High School senior on board at the time, said the 30-plus passengers were headed to the FLD Districts Youth Conference at the Hyatt on Main in Green Bay when it appeared that the vehicle’s accelerator stuck and the engine overheated.

“So blessed to have it made it out safe, though,” Whalen, a first-team all-state football player, wrote in a text message. “It could have been a huge disaster.”

His friend and Wausau West classmate, Brady Boehm, captured video footage showing the bus driver directing passengers out the door and shouting “Get out!” just as flames burst from the engine compartment outside the vehicle.

“It was pretty intense,” Boehm wrote in a text message. “Most people stayed calm. We hurried off as it was on fire, and we thank God that we all made it off safe and unharmed.”
