Head of Messianic Jewish Ministry Says We Are Beginning to See ‘White House Unleash Unholy Hell on Nation of Israel’

December 9, 2014 Second Coming Watch

Markell, Jan (Olive Tree Ministries)

The head of a Messianic Jewish ministry based in Minnesota isn’t surprised Barack Obama is considering sanctions against Israel.

Last week the Israeli newspaper Haaretz reported that President Obama’s administration is considering taking action against the construction of Jewish homes in the West Bank and East Jerusalem, rather than making do with issuing denunciatory statements.

Jan Markell, founder and director of Olive Tree Ministries, says she is not at all surprised by this development.

“I have said for the last [several] years – just wait until all the elections are over, including Midterm 2014,” she recalls. “And once these elections are over, the White House is going to unleash unholy hell on the nation of Israel – and we are beginning to see it.”

Markell admits she’s concerned the White House’s actions could bring the wrath of God upon the U.S. “My concern is the repercussions against America for cursing the nation of Israel,” shares the ministry leader. “According to Zechariah 12 … all nations – not all nations except one – but all nations will come against her in the last days. And so we see it.”
