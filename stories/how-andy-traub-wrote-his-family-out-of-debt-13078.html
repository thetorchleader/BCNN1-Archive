How Andy Traub Wrote His Family Out of Debt

June 19, 2013 Uncategorized

In 2012, a mountain of debt towered over Andy Traub. He and his wife Sara had accumulated roughly $15,000 in bills, trying to cover the many expenses that come with raising three young children and building a life together.
Andy Traub and his family. (Photo courtesy of Andy Traub)
By the end of each month, he and his wife paid their bills, tithed to their local church and put food on the table. But as the sole breadwinner earning $45,000 producing podcasts for inspirational authors and personalities, Traub and his family didn’t have much left over. For two years, he took public transportation to work. His family even cut out cable television. There was little left to trim from their budget.
Inspired by the success and advice of authors such as Dan Miller, a Nashville-based career coach, and Jon Acuff, who wrote the recent New York Times bestselling book “Start,” Traub sketched out a plan. He decided to take a series of emails he wrote about how to wake up early and turn them into an ebook.
Last fall, Traub begin giving away free copies of the book, “Early to Rise: Learn to Rise Early in 30 Days,” to several hundred of his Twitter followers, in exchange for their honest feedback. After revisions, he launched the book on Amazon on January 25.
The results? In the first 100 days, he earned approximately $21,000, selling more than 3,000 copies and erasing $15,000 worth of debt in April.
