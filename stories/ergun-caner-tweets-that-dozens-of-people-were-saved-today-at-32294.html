Ergun Caner Tweets that Dozens of People Were Saved Today at Funeral for his Son, Braxton Caner; Little Brother, Drake, Speaks at Funeral

August 2, 2014 Christian

Braxton Caner

Funeral services were held today, August 2nd, for Braxton Caner, the 15-year-old son of Baptist professor and author, Dr. Ergun Caner. Tweets from the funeral attendees show that in spite of the pain and grief, the Gospel was presented and dozens of people accepted Jesus Christ as their Lord and Saviour.

 

RT @ScottColter: Dozens saved today @ Brax’s funeral. We wanted the Gospel given. Pic: His football team after. pic.twitter.com/bxtoeJA9u1

— Ergun Mehmet Caner (@erguncaner) August 3, 2014

 

RT @AsaNWalker: Drake Caner, 9-years-old, spoke @ his brother Braxton’s funeral. So strong. Not a dry eye here. He’s my hero today//Mine 2

— Ergun Mehmet Caner (@erguncaner) August 2, 2014

-BCNN1
