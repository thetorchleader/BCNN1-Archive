Helen Mirren Wants to Star in a “Fast & Furious” Film

April 3, 2015 Entertainment

Helen Mirren attends the “Woman In Gold” New York premiere at The Museum of Modern Art, March 30, 2015, in New York. (Andrew Toth/Getty Images)

While “Furious 7” already has an amazing ensemble, there’s always been one glaring omission from the cast: Dame Helen Mirren.

And as the legendary 69-year-old actress told ET on Monday, she’s wanted to star in a “Fast and Furious”film for years, and has been waiting for a call from franchise star Vin Diesel.

“Vin, get your act together!” Mirren said at the premiere of her new film, “Woman in Gold,” on Monday.

ET spoke with Diesel at the star-studded Hollywood premiere of “Furious 7,” where we played Mirren’s message for the star.

“I respect that. That’s what you need to do in this business,” Diesel said, reacting to her statement. “I respect that she’s coming out and saying, ‘I don’t care what anyone thinks. I know the deal. I know I want to work with this dude, and I know I want to work with this franchise.'”
