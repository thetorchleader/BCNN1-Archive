Keke Palmer Will Make History as Broadway’s First Black Cinderella

August 4, 2014 Black

(Chris Pizzello/Invision/AP, File)

Newly minted youngest talk show host in TV history Keke Palmer can add another record-breaking line to her resume. She’ll star as Cinderella on Broadway this fall, marking the first time a black actress has taken on the role.

The 21-year-old mini mogul, who has guested on Masters of Sex in addition to hosting her daily BET talk show, will make her Broadway debut with the role on Sept. 9 and she couldn’t be more excited.
