Police Are Searching for Missing 11-Year-Old Lamont Smith

January 30, 2015 Black

Lamont Smith, 11. (Chicago Police Department)

Police are searching for an 11-year-old boy last seen Thursday afternoon.

Lamont Smith went missing from his home in the 6700 block of South Rhodes Avenue around 3:30 p.m., according to the Chicago Police Department’s Office of News Affairs.

Lamont is 5-foot-1 and weighs around 100 pounds, according to the statement. He has black hair, brown eyes, a small build and dark complexion.

He was last seen wearing a white shirt, black jacket, navy blue pants and black boots.

Anybody with information about Lamont’s location should call Area Central detectives at 312-747-8380.

Source: Chicago Tribune
