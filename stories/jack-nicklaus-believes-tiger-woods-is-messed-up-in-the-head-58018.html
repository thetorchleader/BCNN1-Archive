Jack Nicklaus Believes Tiger Woods is Messed Up in the Head More Than Any Place Else

February 22, 2015 Sports

Jack Nicklaus and Tiger Woods in 2012.
Photo: Reuters

Tiger Woods’ problems aren’t in his swing, according to no less a golf authority than Jack Nicklaus.

“Tiger’s struggling, I don’t think there’s any question about that,” Nicklaus said Friday on the Golf Channel. “I think he’s struggling more between his ears than he is anyplace else.’’

Nicklaus is a golf Hall of Famer with a record 18 major championships, so his opinion carries as much weight as Michael Jordan critiquing an NBA scorer or Joe Montana talking quarterbacks. And when he looks at Woods’ deteriorating game, he doesn’t like what he sees.

“He’s struggled with the driver most of his life, really, but he’s always been able to find the golf ball and get it somewhere back around the green,’’ Nicklaus said of Woods, who is on an indefinite leave from the PGA Tour. “And now he’s having trouble with the short game. That is not a good combination, to drive it poorly and have a bad short game.’’

But he isn’t ready to throw dirt on the 14-time major champion just yet, despite Woods sinking to 66th in the world rankings, not even eligible for the upcoming WGC-Cadillac Championship at Doral.
