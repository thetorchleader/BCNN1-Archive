66-Year-Old Woman in New Jersey Freezes to Death Near her Home

February 17, 2015 Black

Olivia Benito
FACEBOOK

66-year-old Olivia Benito stopped past her neighbor’s house for a nightcap on Saturday night and in the morning she was found dead, face down and covered in snow, as police believe she froze to death just steps from her home.

According to NJ.com, the Lakewood, New Jersey resident had a few drinks during the day and a few more while at her neighbor’s house. Al Della Fave of the Ocean County prosecutor’s office told the newspaper that Benito left her neighbor’s house around midnight and was found around 7 a.m. when the same neighbor she had been drinking with walked outside to clear snow from her car.

The neighbor called 911 and tried to revive her friend but ambulance personnel pronounced her dead at the scene.

The newspaper notes that Benito’s home was just two doors from her neighbor’s. Benito’s husband, Louis told police that he believed his wife had decided to stay at their neighbor’s when it got late and temperatures dropped to just 10 degrees, NJ.com reports.
