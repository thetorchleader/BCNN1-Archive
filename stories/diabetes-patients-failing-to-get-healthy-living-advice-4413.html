Diabetes Patients ‘Failing to Get Healthy Living Advice’

March 12, 2012 Uncategorized

Less than half of overweight people with diabetes say that a doctor has recommended boosting their activity during the past year
Doctors think they are giving people with diabetes much more advice about adopting healthier lifestyles than the patients think they are getting, according to new research.
It found that nearly all clinicians said they routinely talked about the importance of physical activity with overweight people who have diabetes, yet less than half of these people could recall being given such advice in the previous 12 months.
The study, led by Newcastle University, was funded by the charity Diabetes UK which said it was concerned by the findings.
