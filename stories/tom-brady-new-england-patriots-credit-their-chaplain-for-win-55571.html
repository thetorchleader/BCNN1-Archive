Tom Brady & New England Patriots Credit Their Chaplain for Wins

February 6, 2015 Sports

After you see this report from ESPN, if you weren’t already a Tom Brady & New England Patriots fan… you will be now.

Watch (below) as he and the Patriots explain the key to their success: Chaplain Jack Easterby

Most teams have a volunteer Chaplain, but Easterby is a full-time hire. The players consider him a ‘moral coach’ and look to him for wisdom through Biblical scripture.

“He just wants to love you,” Wide receiver Matthew Slater says. “He just wants to be your friend. How can you not love a guy like that?”
