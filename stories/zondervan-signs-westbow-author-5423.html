Zondervan Signs WestBow Author

April 23, 2012 Uncategorized

WestBow Press, the Christian self-publishing division of Thomas Nelson, announced that author Annie F. Downs’ debut nonfiction work, From Head to Foot has been acquired by Zondervan. An updated and revised version of the book will be available this August under a new title, Perfectly Unique: Praising God from Head to Foot.
The news comes a few months after HarperCollins, parent company of Zondervan, agreed to acquire Thomas Nelson for $200 million although the purchase has not yet been finalized.
SOURCE: Publishers Weekly
