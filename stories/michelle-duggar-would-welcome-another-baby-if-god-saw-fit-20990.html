Michelle Duggar Would Welcome Another Baby ‘If God Saw Fit’

May 4, 2014 God & Family

Michelle Duggar, mom of 19 (Courtesy The Duggars)

Michelle Duggar has a house full of children and an unwavering faith in God so the reality star is hopeful that she might expand her brood to include even more babies.

The 19 Kids and Counting star and her husband Jim Bob Duggar have made no secret of their desire to have more children, despite her last two difficult pregnancies – one that was five months premature and one that ended in a miscarriage.

And the reality show star doesn’t see her maternal instincts slowing down any time soon.

“I think when we have moments like this it really makes me appreciate the time I get with my grandchildren,” the 47-year-old mother said in the recent episode of her show, revealing that she is relishing in being a grandmother to son Josh and daughter-in-law Anna’s three children, but she yearns for more of her own.

“I realize that I don’t have those little tiny babies in my home anymore. We would welcome another baby if God saw fit to give us another one.”

19 Kids and Counting airs on TLC Tuesdays at 9/8c.
