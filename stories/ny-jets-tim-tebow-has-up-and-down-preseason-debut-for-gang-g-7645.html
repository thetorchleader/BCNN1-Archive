NY Jets’ Tim Tebow Has Up-and-Down Preseason Debut for Gang Green vs. Bengals

August 12, 2012 Uncategorized

Quarterback runs four times bringing Paul Brown Stadium to its feet, but he’s trying to become a little more setted as a passer.
A razzle-dazzle quarterback is getting used to being a little more boring.
Even in Cincinnati, with a sparse crowd on Friday night, Paul Brown Stadium awoke each time Tim Tebow ran with the ball.
Four times he ran. Twice, he managed to escape what appeared to be an impossible situation. And while Tebow enjoys running — he’ll let you know that — he mostly spoke about an increased sense of ease in the pocket.
Tebow said Friday night, following the Jets’ 17-6 loss to the Bengals, that he was most pleased with “my rhythm and my feet from my drops from under center. The footwork from the drops and still being in balance and rhythm and timing. All that leads to your throw and accuracy.”
Tebow looked good at times, spotty at others.
For the Florida alum who has spent most of his career out of the shotgun, this is part of the slow transition to becoming more of a pocket passer. Though he ran successfully on Friday, he did so against a Bengals team that played in a lot of man coverage, making it easier to run off bigger gains once he gets past the line of scrimmage. In a regular season game, Tebow won’t be so lucky. That’s why the QB was so encouraged by his comfort level as a traditional passer.
“Tonight, I felt good in the pocket,” he said. “From the first play, checking to the slant. Going hot to Joe (McKnight), to the one to Stephen (Hill). It felt pretty good.”
