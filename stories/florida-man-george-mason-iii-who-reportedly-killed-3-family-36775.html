Florida Man, George Mason III, Who Reportedly Killed 3 Family Members Gets Hit by Truck While Running From Scene of Crime

September 3, 2014 Black

George Mason III
10 NEWS SCREENSHOT

George Mason III allegedly shot and killed three family members and seriously wounded a fourth before he was hit by a truck while running away from the shooting.  

A Florida man, who allegedly went on a shooting rampage Friday killing three people, may have killed more had he not been struck by a truck accidentally, police told 10 News.

According to witnesses who spoke with the news station, George Mason III reportedly shot and killed Jannie Taylor, 81, Ralph Peyton, 29, and 37-year-old Tarasha Yata Townsend. It remains unclear as to what sparked the shooting, but police told News 10 that Mason had had a gun trained on his half brother, Gabriel Taylor, and even chased him around the outside of the house where the shooting began. Police would later find Taylor shot several times lying in a roadway.

“He didn’t have to kill them people like that, he didn’t have to kill that lady, he didn’t have to kill none of them. They’re all family,” Linda Stanley told 10 News. Stanley said the victims and the alleged shooter all lived in the same home.

Brooksville Police Chief George Turner told the news station that while running away from the crime scene, Mason was hit by a truck.
