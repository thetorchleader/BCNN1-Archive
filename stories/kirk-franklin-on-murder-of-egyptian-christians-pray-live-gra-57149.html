Kirk Franklin on Murder of Egyptian Christians: Pray, Live Grateful

February 17, 2015 Christian

GETTY

Believers in Christ around the world were mortified to learn that, over the weekend, a terrorist group with links to the Islamic State (ISIL) beheaded 21 Egyptian Coptic Christians in Libya.

GRAMMY® Award-winning gospel artist Kirk Franklin, 45, believes this occurrence is a perspective-shifter.

“Christians being executed in Egypt puts what we go through daily in a different light,” he wrote on Twitter. He then added hastags, “#pray” and “#livegrateful.”
