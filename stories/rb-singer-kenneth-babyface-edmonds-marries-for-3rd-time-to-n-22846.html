R&B Singer Kenneth “Babyface” Edmonds Marries for 3rd Time to Nikki Pantenberg

May 19, 2014 Entertainment

Kenneth “Babyface” Edmonds and Nicole Pantenburg (JASON LAVERIS/FILMMAGIC)

Congrats are in order for R&B crooner Kenneth “Babyface” Edmonds. The producer/singer said “I Do” to longtime girlfriend Nikki Pantenberg on Saturday in Southern California.

Singer Carole B. Sager posted a video of their nuptials on Instagram, which she has since deleted. The couple exchanged vows in front of celebrity guests including Oprah Winfrey.

They have been dating since 2007 and are parents to daughter Peyton Nicole, 5. Edmonds announced their engagement in February.

This is the third marriage for Edmonds, who was previously married to Tracy Edmonds. They have two sons together.

Babyface’s rep did not immediately return calls for comment.
