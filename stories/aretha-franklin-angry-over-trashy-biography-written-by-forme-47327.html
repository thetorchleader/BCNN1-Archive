Aretha Franklin Angry Over “Trashy Biography” Written by Former Collaborator David Ritz

November 29, 2014 Books

Aretha Franklin photographed in Detroit, MI April 7th, 2014 Photo: Matthew Jordan Smith

Aretha Franklin has launched a blistering attack on a new unauthorised biography about her, claiming it is “a trashy book full of lies”.

Franklin, the 72-year-old singer from Memphis, who has had 20 No1 hits including Respect, Chain of Fools and Spanish Harlem, is angry over David Ritz’s book Respect: The Life of Aretha Franklin.

In the biography, Ritz writes about Franklin’s children (she had her first child at 14), her parents’ separation and her problems with alcohol. What complicates matter is that the two have a history of collaborating: Franklin and award-winning Ritz worked together on her 1999 biography, Aretha: From These Roots.

Ritz also won a Grammy Award for best album notes for his work on Franklin’s 1992 box set, Queen of Soul: The Atlantic Recordings.

But Franklin has alleged that the latest book is distorted. In a statement, Franklin said: “As many of you are aware, there is a very trashy book out there full of lies and more lies about me. … (The writer’s) actions are obviously vindictive because I edited out some crazy statements he had the gall to try and put in my book written 15 years ago. Evidently, he has been carrying this hatred ever since.”
