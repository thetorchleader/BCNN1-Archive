CBS to Air Interview with President Obama in Rome

March 28, 2014 National

President Obama arrives for a meeting and a working lunch with Italian President Giorgio Napolitano at the Quirinale presidential palace in Rome on March 27. (Photo: Andrew Medichini, AP)

A late addition to President Obama’s schedule in Rome: an interview with CBS News.

CBS anchor Scott Pelley will speak with Obama on Friday, before the president departs for Saudi Arabia on the final leg of his weeklong trip.

The network will air portions of the interview Friday on CBS This Morning and the CBS Evening News.

Obama’s Thursday schedule in the Eternal City included an audience with Pope Francis, meetings with Italian officials and a tour of the Colosseum.

SOURCE: David Jackson
USA TODAY
