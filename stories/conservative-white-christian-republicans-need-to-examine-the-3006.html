Conservative White Christian Republicans Need to Examine Their Hearts and Be Very Wise and Spiritual About Supporting Romney Lest They Alienate Black, Latino Conservative Christians and Destroy the Republican Party’s Chances for the Next 50 to 100 Years

January 14, 2012 LCNN1, Uncategorized

Mitt Romney

If conservative, white, Christian Republicans turn into little Judases and go against their Christian faith, which should be most dear to them, and throw their support behind the false Mormon minister, Mitt Romney, to so call “take our nation back”, whether they do it publicly or privately, will appear as racist to many Blacks and Latinos.

Whether it is justified or not, the Republican Party will certainly appear to be a racist party by many of them for years to come. If Blacks and Latinos get the perception that the Republican party is a racist party, Republicans will lose the chance of a lifetime to get many Blacks and Latinos to join with them to take America into a more Godly direction. If this happens, conservative Republicans will lose the chance of winning the presidency or Congress, for that matter, and we will see the country destroyed faster than it is going now with liberal and anti-God policies.

Now the truth of the matter is, Mitt Romney could not be hired as a janitor at Samaritan’s Purse or Bob Jones University, not only because he is a Mormon, but more importantly, because he is a master liar and a flip-flopper on major issues such as abortion and homosexual marriage, which makes him doubly untrustworthy. Yet, the head of Samaritan’s Purse (which, by the way, I financially support, and will continue to support), Franklin Graham, and Bob Jones III, who already struggles with a terrible racist image, are not only supporting him for the most powerful position in the world – the Presidency of the United States–but are encouraging other Christians to do so as well. This is truly a sad day in America. Talk about the apostasy of the church before the second coming of Christ, well there you go.

IT IS NOT THE ECONOMY, STUPID, IT IS THE SPIRITUAL CONDITION OF THE NATION

I thought we all, as Christians, agreed that the real problem in America is spiritual, not economical. I thought we understood from biblical history that if we get our spiritual house in order, that the economy and everything else would take care of itself. Please tell me, how can we get our spiritual house in order if we elect a false Mormon prophet who believes that Jesus Christ and the devil are brothers and who is a master liar and flip-flopper on such key issues as abortion and homosexual marriage? We all know that the best indicator of what someone is going to do in the future is what they did in the past. And, by the way, whatever happened to the verses that we all used to quote below:

Proverbs 29:2: When the righteous are in authority, the people rejoice: but when the wicked beareth rule, the people mourn.

Proverbs 14:34: Righteousness exalteth a nation: but sin is a reproach to any people.

Recently, Robert Jeffress called Mitt Romney the “lesser of two evils” when matched up against Barack Obama. Well, my friend, you had better examine your heart and make sure that Mitt is not the “lesser of two evils” simply because he is white and Barack Obama is black, because most blacks and most Latinos see Barack Obama as the “lesser of two evils.” Frankly, I see both Romney and Obama as evil as hell itself, simply because of their awful track record on the subjects of homosexual marriage and abortion.

Therefore, my conservative, white, Christian Republican brothers, there is only one solution if you want to be faithful and consistent, and that is we pick one of the other candidates who claim to be Christians and who has a positive track record on homosexual marriage and abortion, and then pray and trust God for the best whether they win or lose.

Let me remind you that the late Jerry Falwell, Sr., always led from the front; he never led from the rear. Falwell led Christians and those of other faiths, including Mormons, in the direction of what was morally right for the nation. If you try to lead from the rear by trying to put this ungodly, unprincipled, wicked, Mormon minister in the White House, it will be very sad for America for many years to come.
