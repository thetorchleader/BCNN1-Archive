Oprah Takes Part In ALS #IceBucketChallenge

August 17, 2014 Entertainment

For the past two weeks, you’ve probably seen people dumping buckets of ice water over the heads for the ALS #IceBucketChallenge. According to the ALS Association, the challenge has not only helped spread awareness about the disease but nearly quadrupled donations to the organization for research and care.

Oprah is the latest participant to take on the challenge. After being called out by Modern Family’s Jesse Tyler Ferguson, Tyler Perry, Entertainment Tonight’s Nancy O’Dell, she agreed to dump water over her head with the assistance of BFF Gayle King.

Oprah’s screams will tell that this wasn’t exactly an enjoyable experience. But what feels better than helping someone in need?
