Assault Charges Filed by Daughter Against Pastor Creflo Dollar Dropped

January 25, 2013 Uncategorized

A simple assault charge filed against 50-year-old mega-church founder Creflo Dollar Jr. has been dropped.
Dollar’s then 15-year-old daughter filed the charges against him in June.
Fayette County State Court officials said Dollar is required to enroll in an anger management program, report to probation and pay $1,072 in court fees.
Dollar has consistently denied the allegations.
Dollar is the founder and pastor of College Park-based World Changers Church International.
SOURCE: The Associated Press
