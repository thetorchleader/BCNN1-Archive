Oklahoma Pastor Says Recent Storms ‘Bordering on Absurd’

May 29, 2015 Christian

Storm damage in Texas. (Texas Baptist Disaster Recovery photo)

Oklahoma Pastor Mitch Randall views the tornados, hailstorms and floods that have hammered the region as almost surreal.

“It’s been one crazy May,” said Randall, pastor of NorthHaven Church, a Baptist General Convention of Texas-affiliated congregation in Norman, Okla.

A tornado hit NorthHaven in early May, causing minor damage. An additional 24 inches of rain followed, and it caused flooding in Norman homes and businesses.
