Tebow Is Jets Backup QB

August 20, 2012 Uncategorized

The Jets probably wish the media were sworn to secrecy when it comes to Tim Tebow’s performance on Saturday.
The Jets’ prized pickup of the offseason struggled mightily in the second half of the 26-3 loss to the Giants. He did not have much help, playing with the second team, but it was ugly after some initial success.
One league executive who watched the game laughed about the idea of a quarterback controversy.
“They brought in Tebow to compete with and push Sanchez, but I’m not so sure that’s working,” the league executive said. “If I’m Sanchez and watched Tebow throw [Saturday], I’m not too worried about my job as a passer.”
Tebow has completed just 9 of 22 passes (40.9 percent) for 96 yards and an interception over the first two games. In his defense, he was running for his life in both games. He was sacked four times by the Giants.
The Jets have yet to show any of the special schemes they have designed for Tebow in a preseason game.
“I don’t think you can get frustrated,” Tebow said. “We haven’t even played a real gameWhen the regular season gets here, that’s when it’s for real.We’ve got to continue to show improvement and get better every day. There’s no reason to get frustrated, we just have to go and get better.”
