National Pound Cake Day: 8 Sweet Recipes to Try

March 4, 2015 God & Food

Barbara Bakes

We’re going beyond the plain pound cake and diving headfirst into 8 unique and delicious pound cake recipes just in time for March 4th, National Pound Cake Day! From pound cakes made with Greek yogurt to the perfect chocolate pound cake treat, there is definitely a pound cake for everyone in this collection!

Blood Orange Pound Cake
Not only is this blood orange pound cake gorgeous but it’s also delicious. Finished with a blood orange glaze and filled with zest, it’s loaded with flavor! Get the recipe here!

Chocolate Pound Cake
This chocolate pound cake is a cinch to make! It’s made with a healthy twist too so it’s definitely one you won’t want to miss! Get the recipe here!

Orange Pound Cake
Refreshing and full of citrus flavor, this Orange Pound Cake is full of delicious flavor from zest, glaze, and a sauce! This is a must-try recipe! Get the recipe here!

Nutella Swirl Pound Cake
What’s better than a delicious and moist pound cake? Well, a pound cake swirled with Nutella! This is definitely a must-try! Get the recipe here!

Lemon Lavender Pound Cake
Made with Greek yogurt, this lemon lavender pound cake is definitely worth a try! I love the unique lavender addition to a traditional lemon pound cake. Get the recipe here!

Lemon Blueberry Pound Cake
This melt-in-your-mouth pound cake will instantly become a family favorite. It’s perfect for celebrations or just a weekend breakfast treat! Get the recipe here!

Coconut Lime Pound Cake
Put a tropical twist on your pound cake with this coconut lime loaf pound cake that will have you dreaming of palm trees and Bahama breezes! Get the recipe here!

Butter Pecan Pound Cake
The flavors of butter and pecan are perfect additions to a moist pound cake making this treat perfect for dessert… or breakfast! Get the recipe here!
