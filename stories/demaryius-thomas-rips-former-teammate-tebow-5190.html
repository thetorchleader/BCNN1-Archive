Demaryius Thomas Rips Former Teammate Tebow

April 13, 2012 Uncategorized

Demaryius Thomas had enough “Tebow Time.”
Now he’s ready for Peyton Manning time.
Thomas caught the famous overtime pass from Tim Tebow in the Broncos’ wild-card playoff win over the Steelers. But the wide receiver was perfectly OK with the “business decision” the Broncos made to acquire Manning before trading Tebow to the Jets.
“I ain’t going to say I was sad because the only thing they remember is that pass,” Thomas said in a radio interview with 790 The Zone in Atlanta about Tebow leaving Denver. “You gotta go back and look at the rest of the games. I wasn’t getting no balls and you had to make some of these plays where some players were open and he is not making the throws, but I don’t want to talk bad about Tim, but hey I am happy we got Peyton (Manning).”
