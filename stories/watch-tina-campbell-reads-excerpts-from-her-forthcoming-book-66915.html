WATCH: Tina Campbell Reads Excerpts from Her Forthcoming Book, “I Need a Day to Pray”

April 12, 2015 Books

Source: WE TV Press / We TV

Tina explains that the challenges she’s gone through from learning about Teddy’s infidelity and losing her father, to career changes, financial woes, and experiencing postpartum depression, her answer was to turn to prayer. In the excerpt, Campbell reads from a chapter based on truths that she needed to to turn things around for herself. The gospel singer turned author recently released some excerpts from her forthcoming book, “I Need a Day to Pray.” She says her prayer journal allowed her to live the life she prayed and spoke about it.
