19-Year-Old Son of Dallas Ebola Patient Asks for Continued Prayers for His Family

October 8, 2014 National

Eric Duncan, the 19-year-old son of the Ebola patient Thomas Eric Duncan, speaks to reporters in Dallas on Oct. 7, 2014. (PHOTO CREDIT: WFAA-TV, Dallas)

The son of the man who is critically ill with Ebola and being treated at a Dallas hospital asks for continued prayers for his mother and the father he hasn’t seen since he was 3 years old.

“I just came by here because I feel like God was calling me to come see my dad,” said Karsiah Duncan, 19, speaking at Wilshire Baptist Church.

His father, Thomas Eric Duncan, 42, remains in critical condition at Texas Health Presbyterian Hospital in Dallas after falling ill with the Ebola virus.

Karsiah said he has not seen his father since he was 3 years old, after his mother, Louise Troh, fled with him from civil war in Africa. The senior Duncan flew to the United States last month to rekindle his relationship with Karsiah and Troh.

On Tuesday — 16 years after their last visit — Karsiah drove to Presbyterian Hospital hoping to peer through a glass panel inside the isolation unit where his father is undergoing treatment.

But the young man was not able to see his father, said Dr. George Mason, senior pastor at Wilshire Baptist Church.

Duncan remains in critical condition at Texas Health Presbyterian Hospital Dallas. He is on a ventilator and dialysis. He began taking an experimental medication over the weekend, and his condition has stabilized, the hospital said.

Troh has not developed symptoms, but remains quarantined at an undisclosed residence in the Dallas area.

“I want to thank my mom for all the strength she has,” Karsiah said. “Be strong, even though it’s hard being in the house for 21 days.”

According to news accounts, Troh and Duncan met years ago at a refugee camp on the Ivory Coast. She came to the United States about a decade ago with their small son. He remained behind in their impoverished homeland.
