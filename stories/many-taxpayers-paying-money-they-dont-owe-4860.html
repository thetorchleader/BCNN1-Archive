Many Taxpayers Paying Money They Don’t Owe

March 27, 2012 Uncategorized

More than 25 million taxpayers are faced with the terrifying prospect of audits and assessments during tax season by the Internal Revenue Service.
But is there a way to file without fear? Tax expert Daniel Pilla says, “Yes.”
As Americans scramble to file before the April 17 tax deadline, millions will inevitably pay an untold billions of dollars back to the government.
And Pilla claims this is money that, surprisingly, many taxpayers may not even owe.
Pilla said the federal government often looks to the IRS as a first line of attack in balancing the budget.
“You take all 20,000 pages of the tax code and you can boil it down to three words: get the money,” he told CBN News.
